# Perfect Swimmer

*Reference Book: [[Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

You are among the best of your kind when swimming and can navigate even the most difficult waters.

**Prerequisite:** [[Gungan]]

**Effect:** Whenever you reroll a [[Swim]] check, you always keep the better result, even if you have multiple reroll abilities.

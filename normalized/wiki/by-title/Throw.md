# Throw

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

You can Throw a creature that you've [[Grappled]].

**Prerequisites:** [[Base_Attack_Bonus]] +1, [[Trip]]

**Effect:** If you successfully [[Trip]] an opponent with a [[Grapple]] attack, the opponent falls [[Prone]] in any unoccupied space you desire up to 1 square beyond your [[Reach]] and take Bludgeoning damage equal to your [[Unarmed]] attack damage. A Thrown opponent is no longer considered [[Grappled]].

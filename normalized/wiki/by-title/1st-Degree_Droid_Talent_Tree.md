# 1st-Degree Droid Talent Tree

*Reference Book: [[Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide]]*

[[1st-Degree_Droids]] are usually Medical, Analytical, or Scientific Droids. Only they can select Talents from this Droid Talent Tree.

## Core Talents

### Dull the Pain

*Prerequisite:* **Medical Programming**

As a [[Full-Round_Action]], you can make a DC 15 [[Treat_Injury]] check on an adjacent living creature to move it +1 step along the [[Condition_Track]].

### Interrogator

You can create an aura of unliving, emotionless, menace that no biological creature can match, and combine it with the cruel application of medical knowledge. You can use your [[Treat_Injury]] modifier on a [[Persuasion]] check made to change a creature's [[Attitude]], or to [[Intimidate]]. You must be adjacent to your target to use this ability, in addition to the normal requirements for these uses of the [[Persuasion]] skill.

### Medical Programming

When you use a [[Medpac]] to perform [[First_Aid]] with the [[Treat_Injury]] skill, the creature gains 2 [[Hit_Points]] for every point by which your check exceeds the DC (rather than the normal 1 [[Hit_Point]]).

## Additional Talents

### Known Vulnerability

*Reference Book: [[Scavenger's_Guide_to_Droids]]*

*Prerequisite:* [[Trained]] in [[Knowledge_(Life_Sciences)]]

You know the vulnerable spots to hit on most [[Species]]. As a [[Free_Action]], you make a DC 15 [[Knowledge_(Life_Sciences)]] check (DC 25 for rare [[Species]], DC 35 for unknown [[Species]], both as determined by the Gamemaster). If the check is successful, until the end of the encounter, whenever you make a successful [[Melee_Attack]] that deals damage against a target of that [[Species]], your target takes a -2 penalty to attack rolls until the end of your next turn.

### Medical Analyzer

*Reference Book: [[Scavenger's_Guide_to_Droids]]*

*Prerequisites:* **Medical Programming**, [[Trained]] in [[Knowledge_(Life_Sciences)]]

You use your medical knowledge and advanced processor to improve your treatment of medical conditions. When making a [[Treat_Injury]] check to [[Treat_Disease]], [[Treat_Poison]], or [[Treat_Radiation]], you can also add your [[Intelligence]] modifier to your roll.

### Science Analyzer

*Reference Book: [[Scavenger's_Guide_to_Droids]]*

*Prerequisites:* [[Trained]] in [[Knowledge_(Life_Sciences)]] and [[Knowledge_(Physical_Sciences)]]

You use your extensive databanks to better analyze scientific data. You can add double your [[Intelligence]] modifier to your [[Knowledge_(Life_Sciences)]] and [[Knowledge_(Physical_Sciences)]] skill checks.

### Triage Scan

*Reference Book: [[Scavenger's_Guide_to_Droids]]*

*Prerequisites:* **Medical Programming**, [[Trained]] in [[Treat_Injury]]

As a [[Standard_Action]], you can make a DC 20 [[Treat_Injury]] check. If the check succeeds, you know if the organic characters within 6 squares and within your [[Line_of_Sight]] are below one half of their [[Hit_Points]] and at what step they are along the [[Condition_Track]].

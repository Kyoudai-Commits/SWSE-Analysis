# Category : Double Weapons

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

You can attack with both ends of a Double Weapon as a [[Full-Round_Action]], but both attack rolls take a -10 penalty (although certain [[Feats]] and [[Talents]] can reduce these penalties).

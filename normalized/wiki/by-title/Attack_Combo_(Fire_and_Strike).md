# Attack Combo (Fire and Strike)

*Reference Book: [[Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

You can make deadly ranged and melee attacks in rapid succession.

**Prerequisites:** [[Base_Attack_Bonus]] +9, [[Attack_Combo_(Melee)]], [[Attack_Combo_(Ranged)]]

**Effect:** If you hit a single target with two consecutive [[Ranged_Attacks]], [[Melee_Attacks]], and/or [[Unarmed_Attacks]] during the same turn, any additional [[Ranged_Attacks]], [[Melee_Attacks]], or [[Unarmed_Attacks]] you make until the end of your next turn (including [[Attacks_of_Opportunity]] and attacks made as [[Reactions]]) deal +1 die of damage on a hit. This extra damage stacks with extra damage granted by other [[Feats]] or [[Talents]].

# Galactic Alliance Military Training

*Reference Book: [[Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

Service in [[The_Galactic_Alliance]] has honed your toughness.

**Effect:** You do not move down the [[Condition_Track]] the first time an attack exceeds your [[Damage_Threshold]] in an encounter.

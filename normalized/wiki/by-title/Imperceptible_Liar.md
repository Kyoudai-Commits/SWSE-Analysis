# Imperceptible Liar

*Reference Book: [[Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

You have worked hard to eliminate your behavioral tells, making it almost impossible to discern when you are lying.

**Prerequisite:** [[Twi'lek]]

**Effect:** Whenever you spend a [[Force_Point]] to add to a [[Deception]] check, increase the die type by two steps (from d6 to d10, or from d8 to d12).

# Armor Proficiency (Light)

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

You are proficient with [[Light_Armor]], and can wear it without impediment.

**Effect:** When you wear [[Light_Armor]], you take no [[Armor_Check_Penalty]] on attack rolls or [[Skill_Checks]]. Additionally, you benefit from all of the armor's special [[Equipment]] bonuses (if any).

**Normal:** A character who wears [[Light_Armor]] with which they are not proficient takes a -2 [[Armor_Check_Penalty]] on attack rolls as well as [[Skill_Checks]] made using the following [[Skills]]: [[Acrobatics]], [[Climb]], [[Endurance]], [[Initiative]], [[Jump]], [[Stealth]], and [[Swim]]. Additionally, the character gains none of the armor's special [[Equipment]] bonuses.

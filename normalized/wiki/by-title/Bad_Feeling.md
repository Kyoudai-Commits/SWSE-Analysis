# Bad Feeling

*Reference Book: [[Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide]]*

You have developed an innate ability to sense when things are going badly, and thus can prepare yourself for the worst.

**Effect:** You can always take a [[Move_Action]] during a [[Surprise_Round]], even if you are [[Surprised]]. If you are not [[Surprised]], you can take this [[Move_Action]] in addition to any other Actions you are normally allowed to take in the [[Surprise_Round]].

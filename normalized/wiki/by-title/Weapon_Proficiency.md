# Weapon Proficiency

*Reference Books: [[Core_Rulebook]], [[Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide]]*

You are proficient with a particular kind of weaponry.

**Effect:** Choose one [[Weapon_Group]]. You are proficient with all weapons of the selected group. [[Weapon_Groups]] include the following:

- [[Advanced_Melee_Weapons]]
- [[Heavy_Weapons]] (including both [[Heavy_Weapons]] and [[Vehicle_Weapons]])
- [[Lightsabers]]
- [[Pistols]]
- [[Rifles]]
- Simple Weapons (including [[Simple_Weapons_(Melee)]], [[Simple_Weapons_(Ranged)]], and [[Grenades]])

**Normal:** If you wield a weapon with which you are not proficient, you take a -5 penalty to your attack rolls.

**Special:** You can gain this feat multiple times. Each time you take the feat, it applies to a different [[Weapon_Group]]. You cannot select [[Exotic_Weapons]] for this feat; instead you must select the [[Exotic_Weapon_Proficiency]] feat to gain proficiency with a specific [[Exotic_Weapon]].

**[[Combined_Feat]]:** If you have the [[Quick_Draw]] feat, you can draw and ignite [[Lightsabers]] as a single [[Swift_Action]].

# Read the Winds

*Reference Book: [[Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

You are so attuned to the wind patterns of your world that you can detect subtle shifts in air pressure around you.

**Prerequisite:** [[Kel_Dor]]

**Effect:** You ignore [[Concealment]] and [[Cover]] for the purposes of [[Perception]] checks against targets within 10 squares of you.

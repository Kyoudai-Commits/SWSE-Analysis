# Squad Leadership Talent Tree

|  |
| --- |
| ***This section was titled Squad Leader Talent Tree in its official source, but has been renamed to distinguish it from the similarly named "[[Squad_Leader_Talent_Tree]]."*** |

*Reference Book: [[Galaxy_at_War]]*

You are an expert at small-unit tactics, leading a small group of allies into battle as a cohesive unit.

All Talents in the Squad Leadership Talent Tree apply to other characters in a squad you command. A squad includes yourself plus a number of characters equal to or less than the sum of your [[Elite_Trooper]] Class Level plus your [[Charisma]] modifier (minimum of one other character). These characters must be on foot, must have [[Line_of_Sight]] to you, and must be able to communicate with you to gain the benefit of your Squad Leadership Talents. As a [[Free_Action]], you designate which characters are in your squad at the beginning of your first turn.

Each squad can benefit from the Talents of only a single squad leader, designated when an encounter begins. A character without a squad leader can join an existing squad if both character and squad leader are willing and if the squad leader has not reached his or her maximum squad size.

## Core Talents

### Fall Back

*Prerequisite:* [[Charisma]] 13

As a [[Move_Action]], you can enable each member of your squad to immediately [[Move]] 2 squares. This movement does not provoke an [[Attack_of_Opportunity]].

### Form Up

*Prerequisite:* [[Charisma]] 13

As a [[Move_Action]], you give all squad members a +2 morale bonus to their [[Reflex_Defense]] until the end of your next turn, as long as they are within 6 squares of another squad member.

### Full Advance

*Prerequisite:* [[Charisma]] 13

As a [[Move_Action]], you give all squad members a +2 morale bonus to damage rolls until the end of your next turn.

### Hold Steady

*Prerequisite:* [[Charisma]] 13

Once per encounter, as a [[Standard_Action]], you move all members of your squad +1 step on the [[Condition_Track]].

### Search and Destroy

*Prerequisite:* [[Charisma]] 13

As a [[Move_Action]], you give all squad members a +2 morale bonus to [[Perception]] checks until the end of your next turn.

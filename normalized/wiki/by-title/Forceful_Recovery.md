# Forceful Recovery

*Reference Book: [[Galaxy_of_Intrigue]]*

[[The_Force]] is strong with you, and it gives you focus and vitality.

**Prerequisites:** [[Force_Sensitivity]], [[Force_Training]]

**Effect:** Whenever you catch a [[Second_Wind]], choose one expended [[Force_Power]] and return that power to your [[Force_Power_Suite]].

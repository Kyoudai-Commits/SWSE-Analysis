# Grab Back

*Reference Book: [[Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide]]*

You are able to counter [[Grab]] and [[Grapple]] attacks, redirecting them back on your attacker.

**Prerequisite:** [[Dexterity]] 13

**Effect:** You gain a +2 bonus to your [[Reflex_Defense]] against [[Grab]] and [[Grapple]] attacks. If an enemy misses you when making a [[Grab]] or [[Grapple]] attack, you can immediately make your own [[Grab]] attack against the same enemy as a [[Reaction]].

**Special:** If you have a [[Feat]] that enables you to make a [[Grapple]] attack (such as [[Pin]] or [[Trip]]), you can make a [[Grapple]] attack instead.

# Scoundrel Bonus Feats

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

At each even-numbered level (2nd, 4th, 6th, etc.), a [[Scoundrel]] gains a Bonus [[Feat]]. This [[Feat]] must be selected from the following Scoundrel Bonus Feats lists, and a [[Scoundrel]] must meet any prerequisites for that [[Feat]].

## Core Bonus Feats

| FEAT | PREREQUISITES | BENEFITS |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[Deadeye]] | [[Base_Attack_Bonus]] +4, [[Point-Blank_Shot]], [[Precise_Shot]] | If you [[Aim]], deal extra damage. |
| [[Dodge]] | [[Dexterity]] 13 | Gain a +1 dodge bonus to [[Reflex_Defense]] against a selected target. |
| [[Melee_Defense]] | [[Intelligence]] 13 | Trade attack bonus on [[Melee_Attacks]] for a dodge bonus to [[Reflex_Defense]]. |
| [[Mobility]] | [[Dexterity]] 13, [[Dodge]] | Gain +5 dodge bonus to [[Reflex_Defense]] against some [[Attacks_of_Opportunity]]. |
| [[Point-Blank_Shot]] | None | +1 bonus on [[Ranged_Attacks]] and damage rolls against foes within Point-Blank [[Range]]. |
| [[Precise_Shot]] | [[Point-Blank_Shot]] | No -5 penalty for [[Attacking_into_a_Melee]]. |
| [[Quick_Draw]] | [[Base_Attack_Bonus]] +1 | Draw weapon as a [[Swift_Action]]. |
| [[Rapid_Shot]] | [[Base_Attack_Bonus]] +1 | Take a -2 penalty on a ranged attack roll to deal +1 die of damage. |
| [[Running_Attack]] | [[Dexterity]] 13 | Move before and after making an attack. |

# Wicked Attack

|  |
| --- |
| ***This section was titled Wicked Strike in its official source, but has been renamed to distinguish it from the similarly named "[[Wicked_Strike]]."*** |

*Reference Book: [[Scum_and_Villainy]]*

Your melee attacks can catch bystanders.

**Prerequisite:** [[Rapid_Strike]]

**Effect:** When you damage a target with a single, non-[[Area_Attack]] using the [[Rapid_Strike]] feat, once per turn on your turn, you can immediately make a second attack at a -2 penalty against a second target within your [[Reach]]. If this attack hits, you deal half of the original attack's damage to that target.

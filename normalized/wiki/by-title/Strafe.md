# Strafe

*Reference Book: [[Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide]]*

You can make a [[Strafing_Attack]] without a [[Vehicle]].

**Prerequisite:** [[Base_Attack_Bonus]] +1

**Effect:** When you make an [[Autofire]] attack, instead of attacking a 2-square-by-2-square area, you may attack a line 1 square wide and 4 squares long.

**Special:** If you are using a [[Jet_Pack]], you can use the Strafe feat to make an [[Autofire]] attack against all squares you fly over.

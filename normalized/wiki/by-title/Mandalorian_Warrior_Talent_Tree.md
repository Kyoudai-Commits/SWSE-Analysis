# Mandalorian Warrior Talent Tree

*Reference Book: [[Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide]]*

[[The_Mandalorians]] are talented and ferocious warriors. Their skillful training, warrior culture, and advanced combat abilities make them formidable foes in any era. The following Talents are particularly suited to [[The_Old_Republic_Era]].

## Core Talents

### Armored Mandalorian

*Prerequisites:* [[Dexterity]] 13, **Mandalorian Glory**, Proficient with Armor

Mandalorians wear armor constantly and learn to adjust to take an impact on the strongest section of their armor. You add your armor's [[Fortitude_Defense]] bonus as an [[Equipment]] bonus to your Elite Trooper [[Damage_Reduction]] (with a maximum bonus equal to your base [[Elite_Trooper]] DR). Additionally, if [[Lightsabers]] do not ignore the DR of the armor you are wearing (such as [[Cortosis_Weave/Phrik_Alloy_General_Template]]), [[Lightsabers]] do not ignore your [[Elite_Trooper]] Damage Reduction.

### Mandalorian Advance

Veteran Mandalorians know how to move on the battlefield. Once per encounter, on your turn, you can [[Move]] up to your [[Speed]] as a [[Free_Action]] before any other Action.

### Mandalorian Ferocity

*Prerequisites:* [[Dexterity]] 13

Mandalorians can be ferocious fighters. Select one [[Weapon_Group]] or [[Exotic_Weapon]] you are proficient with. Once per encounter, when making more than one attack in a round, you can add one damage die to each successful hit with the selected [[Weapon_Group]] or [[Exotic_Weapon]]. You can take this Talent more than once, selecting a different [[Exotic_Weapon]] or [[Weapon_Group]] each time.

### Mandalorian Glory

Above everything else, Mandalorians fight for glory in battle. Once per encounter, when you reduce an opponent's [[Hit_Points]] to 0, you gain a +5 attack bonus with your next attack roll during the same encounter.

### Mandalorian Martial Arts Mastery

*Mandalorian Martial Arts Mastery Talent created by Miraheze user JuFroSamurai.*

Whereas other martial disciplines train in light clothing that doesn't restrict movement, Mandalorian warriors live and train in their Beskar'gam until it feels like a second skin. Once per round, as a swift action, until the end of your next turn you may treat yourself as wearing light or no armor strictly for the purposes of activating or gaining the benefits of [[Martial_Arts_Feats]] or [[Martial_Arts_Forms_Talents]] that would otherwise require the user to be wearing light or no armor.

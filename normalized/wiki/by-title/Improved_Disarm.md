# Improved Disarm

*Reference Books: [[Core_Rulebook]], [[Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide]]*

You are skilled at [[Disarming]] opponents in melee combat.

**Prerequisite:** [[Intelligence]] 13, [[Melee_Defense]]

**Effect:** You gain a +5 bonus on any melee attack roll made to [[Disarm]] an opponent. In addition, if you fail to [[Disarm]] an opponent, they don't get to make a free attack against you.

**[[Combined_Feat]]:** If you have the [[Force_Training]] feat, when you use the *[[Force_Disarm]]* Force Power, you gain the +5 bonus from Improved Disarm on your [[Use_the_Force]] check as though you were making an attack roll.

# Crossfire

*Reference Book: [[Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide]]*

You are skilled at aiming your shots so that they have a chance of hitting more than one target.

**Prerequisites:** [[Base_Attack_Bonus]] +6, [[Point-Blank_Shot]], [[Precise_Shot]]

**Effect:** If you miss when making a [[Ranged_Attack]] against a target that has "soft" [[Cover]] (that is, [[Cover]] provided by another character, creature, or [[Droid]]), you can immediately make an attack roll (with the same weapon and at the same attack bonus) against the target that is providing the "soft" [[Cover]]. You can only use this feat once per round.

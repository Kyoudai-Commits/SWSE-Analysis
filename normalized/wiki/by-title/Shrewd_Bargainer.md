# Shrewd Bargainer

*Reference Book: [[Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

You know how to break down the defenses of your opponents, ensuring that you gain the upper hand in any negotiation.

**Prerequisite:** [[Quarren]]

**Effect:** Whenever you make [[Persuasion]] checks against a target's [[Will_Defense]], the target does not receive the benefit of insight or morale bonuses to its [[Will_Defense]].

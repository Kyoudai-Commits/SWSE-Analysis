# Hold Together

*Reference Book: [[Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide]]*

Sometimes [[Vehicles]] seem to hold together by your force of will.

**Prerequisite:** [[Trained]] in [[Mechanics]]

**Effect:** As a [[Reaction]], you can spend a [[Force_Point]] to delay the effect of damage dealt to a [[Vehicle]] you are riding in or piloting until the end of the round. The [[Vehicle]] must be of Colossal size or smaller.

# Covert Operatives

*Reference Book: [[Galaxy_at_War]]*

You and your allies specialize in undercover missions in which stealth and subtlety are paramount.

**Prerequisite:** [[Trained]] in [[Stealth]]

**Effect:** You gain a +3 competence bonus on [[Stealth]] checks. At the time you make a check, for each ally within 12 squares of you who also has this feat, the competence bonus increases by +1 (to a maximum of +7).

Additionally, when you move more than your [[Speed]] or more than twice you [[Speed]], reduce the penalties to [[Stealth]] checks by 2.

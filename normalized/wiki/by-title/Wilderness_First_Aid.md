# Wilderness First Aid

*Reference Book: [[Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide]]*

You know how to treat wounds in the wilderness, using only the natural materials around you.

**Prerequisite:** [[Trained]] in [[Survival]].

**Effect:** Once per day, you can make a DC 20 [[Survival]] check. If the check is successful, when you use the [[Basic_Survival]] application of the [[Survival]] skill, you are considered to have a [[Medpac]] for any [[Treat_Injury]] checks made until the end of the day.

**Special:** The Gamemaster can adjust the DC for the current environment, raising it for harsh or desert climates or lowering it for diverse locales.

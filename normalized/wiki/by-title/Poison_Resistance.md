# Poison Resistance

*Reference Book: [[Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide]]*

You are naturally more resistant to the effects of [[Poison]].

**Prerequisite:** [[Constitution]] 13

**Effect:** You gain a +5 bonus to your [[Fortitude_Defense]] when attacked by [[Poison]]. If the attack succeeds, you take only half damage.

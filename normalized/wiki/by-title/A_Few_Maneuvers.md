# A Few Maneuvers

*Reference Book: [[Threats_of_the_Galaxy]]*

You can weave, juke, and roll to avoid enemy fire in the thick of combat.

**Prerequisites:** [[Dodge]], [[Vehicular_Combat]]

**Effect:** When piloting a [[Vehicle]] of Colossal size or smaller, you grant a +2 dodge bonus to your Vehicle's [[Reflex_Defense]]. Furthermore, if you are the target of a [[Missile_or_Torpedo]] and the attack roll misses by 5 or more, the projectile self-destructs harmlessly.

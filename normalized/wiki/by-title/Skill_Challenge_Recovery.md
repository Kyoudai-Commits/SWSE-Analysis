# Skill Challenge: Recovery

*Reference Book: [[Galaxy_of_Intrigue]]*

You can snatch victory from the jaws of defeat when involved in a [[Skill_Challenge]].

**Effect:** Once per [[Skill_Challenge]], you treat the [[Skill_Challenge]] as if it had the [[Recovery]] effect, even if it does not have that effect.

**Special:** This Feat is allowed only in campaigns that use [[Skill_Challenges]].

# Artillery Shot

*Reference Book: [[Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide]]*

You have learned to use the angle and distance between you and your target to devastating effect.

**Effect:** When you make an attack with a [[Burst]] or [[Splash]] weapon with which you are proficient against a target at greater than Point-Blank [[Range]], you can affect two additional squares adjacent to the normal [[Burst]] radius or [[Splash]] area.

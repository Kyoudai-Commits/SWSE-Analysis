# Category : Bellow

As a [[Standard_Action]], a [Species] can make a special attack roll (1d20 + its Character Level) and compares the result to the [[Fortitude_Defense]] of all creatures and unattended objects in a 6-Square [[Cone]]. A successful hit deals 3d6 points of [[Sonic]] damage; if the attack misses, the target takes half damage instead.

- Each use of this ability moves the [Species] -1 step along the [[Condition_Track]].
- A [Species] can choose to add more dice (d6s) to the damage it deals with its Bellow, but each additional 1d6 of damage moves the [Species] another -1 step along the [[Condition_Track]].

# Jet Pack

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

**Cost:** 300

**Weight:** 30 Kilograms

A Jet Pack is a propulsion system that a character can strap on, allowing flight over short distances. Arm and wrist controls are employed for maneuvering. Activating a Jet Pack is a [[Swift_Action]], and you gain a Fly Speed of 6 squares until the end of your turn. A Jet Pack has 10 charges and can be run continuously; no [[Swift_Action]] is required to activate the Jet Pack on subsequent rounds of use.

Routine maneuvers do not require a [[Pilot]] check, but you must make a DC 20 [[Pilot]] check if you land after moving more than 12 squares during the same turn; on a failure you fall [[Prone]]. A Jet Pack can lift up to 180 kg while flying. Replacement fuel cells cost 100 credits.

# Force Disarm

*Reference Books: [[Core_Rulebook]], [[Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide]]*

You [[Disarm]] an opponent by using [[The_Force]] to pull the weapon from their grasp.

**[*[[Telekinetic]]*]**

**Time:** [[Standard_Action]]

**Targets:** One target within 12 squares, and within [[Line_of_Sight]].

**Make a [[Use_the_Force]] check.** Use this check in place of your attack roll when attempting to [[Disarm]] the target. If your [[Disarm]] attempt succeeds, you may choose to let the item drop to the ground in the target's fighting space or have the item fly into your hand (in which case you must have a free hand to catch it).

**Special:** You can spend a [[Force_Point]] to damage or destroy the target weapon instead. If your [[Disarm]] attempt succeeds, the weapon takes damage equal to your [[Use_the_Force]] check result. You must declare that you are using this option before making your [[Disarm]] attempt. If you have the [[Improved_Disarm]] feat, you gain the +5 bonus on your [[Use_the_Force]] check to activate *Force Disarm* as though you were making an attack roll

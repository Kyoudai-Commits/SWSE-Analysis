# Republic Military Training

*Reference Book: [[Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide]]*

You are trained in Republic military fighting techniques, giving you an advantage in ranged combat. This training is generally given to members of the Republic military forces.

**Effect:** Once per encounter, as a [[Reaction]], you gain [[Damage_Reduction]] 10 against an incoming attack if you have [[Cover]] from the attacker. You can gain the [[Damage_Reduction]] as long as you have [[Cover]], even if your opponent uses [[Aim]] to ignore your [[Cover]] bonus to [[Reflex_Defense]].

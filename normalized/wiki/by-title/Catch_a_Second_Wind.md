# Catch a Second Wind

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

If you are reduced to one-half your maximum [[Hit_Points]] or less, you can Catch a Second Wind as a [[Swift_Action]]. You can only Catch a Second Wind once per day. Only characters with Heroic Levels can Catch a Second Wind; [[Vehicles]], [[Beasts]], [[Nonheroic]] characters, objects, and devices cannot. This Action heals one-quarter of your full [[Hit_Point]] total (rounded down) or a number of points equal to your [[Constitution]] score, whichever is greater. You can Catch a Second Wind only once per day.

Certain [[Feats]] or [[Talents]] may allow you to Catch a Second Wind more often, but never more than once in a single encounter. If you have the [[Extra_Second_Wind]] feat, you can Catch a Second Wind an additional time per day (but never more than once in a single encounter). A [[Nonheroic]] character that takes the [[Extra_Second_Wind]] feat can Catch a Second Wind once per day.

## Multiple Second Winds

*Reference Book: [[Web_Enhancements]] ([[Jedi_Counseling]])*

Instead of having one Second Wind per day, every heroic character can Catch a Second Wind a number of times per day equal to 1 + (Class Bonus to [[Fortitude_Defense]]) + ([[Constitution]] Modifier), with a minimum of 1. Any ability that would add an extra Second Wind (such as the [[Extra_Second_Wind]] feat or the **[[Tough_as_Nails]]** Talent) instead adds a number of Second Winds equal to this base number. As always, you can Catch a Second Wind no more than once in a single encounter.

## The Edge of Exhaustion

*Reference Book: [[Web_Enhancements]] ([[Jedi_Counseling]])*

If you have already used your Second Wind(s) for the day, you can catch another Second Wind by voluntarily moving -1 [[Persistent]] step down the [[Condition_Track]]. The [[Persistent_Condition]] remains until you've had [[Surgery]] performed on you or until you get eight consecutive, uninterrupted hours of rest.

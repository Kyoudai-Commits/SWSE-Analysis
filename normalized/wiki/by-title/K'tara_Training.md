# K'tara Training

*Reference Book: [[Galaxy_at_War]]*

You are trained in the K'tara style of unarmed fighting, which enables you to neutralize enemies before they even know that you are present.

**Prerequisites:** [[Trained]] in [[Stealth]], [[Martial_Arts_I]]

**Effect:** You roll an extra die of damage against a [[Flat-Footed]] enemy on one [[Unarmed_Attack]] during your turn.

Once per encounter, when you deal damage with an [[Unarmed_Attack]] to a target that has been denied its [[Dexterity]] bonus to [[Reflex_Defense]], make a second [[Unarmed_Attack]] against the target's [[Fortitude_Defense]] as a [[Free_Action]]. If successful, you render the target incapable of speech until the end of your next turn. This is a [[Stunning]] effect.

**Special:** If you have the **[[K'tara_Expertise]]** Talent, you can spend two [[Swift_Actions]] to cause a single adjacent enemy to be [[Flat-Footed]] against your first attack during the same round.

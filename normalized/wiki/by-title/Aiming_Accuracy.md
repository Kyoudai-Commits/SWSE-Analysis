# Aiming Accuracy

*Reference Book: [[Scavenger's_Guide_to_Droids]]*

When you have time to analyze your shot, your shooting accuracy has a precision that only a machine can achieve.

**Prerequisites:** [[Droid]], [[Point-Blank_Shot]], [[Precise_Shot]]

**Effect:** If you [[Aim]] at a target as a [[Full-Round_Action]] (instead of two [[Swift_Actions]]), you gain a +5 bonus to your next attack made with a weapon with which you are proficient in the following round against that target. The target must remain in your [[Line_of_Sight]].

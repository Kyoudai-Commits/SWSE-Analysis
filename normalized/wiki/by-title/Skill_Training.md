# Skill Training

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

You are considered [[Trained]] in a new [[Skill]].

**Effect:** Choose one [[Untrained_Skill]] from your list of [[Class_Skills]]. You become [[Trained]] in that [[Skill]].

**Special:** This feat may be selected multiple times. Each time you take this feat, it applies to a different [[Class_Skill]].

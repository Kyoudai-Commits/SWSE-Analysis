# Vornskr's Ferocity

*Reference Book: [[Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual]]*

**[*[[Lightsaber_Form]]*, *[[Dark_Side]]*]**

You walk the thin line between darkness and light as you ferociously attack your foe.

**Time:** [[Standard_Action]]

**Targets:** One enemy within [[Reach]].

**Make a [[Use_the_Force]] check.** The result of the check determines the effect, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 20 | You make a single [[Melee_Attack]] with a [[Lightsaber]] against the target, dealing +1 die of damage if you hit. |
| 25 | You make a single [[Melee_Attack]] with a [[Lightsaber]] against the target, dealing +2 dice of damage if you hit. |
| 30 | You make a single [[Melee_Attack]] with a [[Lightsaber]] against the target, dealing +3 dice of damage if you hit. |
| 35 | You make a single [[Melee_Attack]] with a [[Lightsaber]] against the target, dealing +4 dice of damage if you hit. |

**Lightsaber Form ([[Juyo]]):** If you have the **[[Juyo]]** Talent, this Form Power does not have the [*[[Dark_Side]]*] descriptor for you.

**Special:** You can spend a [[Force_Point]] to use this Form Power at the end of a [[Charge]].

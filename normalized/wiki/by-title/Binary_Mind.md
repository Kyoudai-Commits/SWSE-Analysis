# Binary Mind

*Reference Book: [[Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

The dual nature of your mind makes it difficult for others to influence you.

**Prerequisite:** [[Cerean]]

**Effect:** Whenever an enemy tries to use a Mind-Affecting effect against you, the enemy must roll twice and take the lower result.

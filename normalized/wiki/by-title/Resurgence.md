# Resurgence

*Reference Book: [[Scum_and_Villainy]]*

Catching your [[Second_Wind]] allows you to seize the advantage in combat.

**Prerequisite:** [[Trained]] in [[Endurance]]

**Effect:** When you catch your [[Second_Wind]], you immediately gain a [[Move_Action]] to be used immediately.

# Category : Simple Weapons

Simple Weapons include weapons that require no special training to use (including [[Grenades]], [[Simple_Weapons_(Ranged)]], and [[Simple_Weapons_(Melee)]]). [[Unarmed_Attacks]] and Natural Weapons are considered Light Simple Weapons in which all characters are proficient. [[Weapon_Proficiency_(Simple_Weapons)]] is a Starting Feat for all [[Heroic_Classes]].

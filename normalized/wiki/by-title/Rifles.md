# Rifles

| DISTANCE | RANGE | PENALTY |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-30 Squares | Point-Blank | No penalty |
| 31-60 Squares | Short | -2 to attack rolls |
| 61-150 Squares | Medium | -5 to attack rolls |
| 151-300 Squares | Long | -10 to attack rolls |

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

Standard military weapons, used with both hands for precise and accurate shooting, Rifles deal more damage and have a greater [[Range]] than [[Pistols]]. You take a -5 penalty on attack rolls if you wield a Rifle with one hand (regardless of the weapon's size relative to you). [[Weapon_Proficiency_(Rifles)]] is a Starting Feat for [[Scouts]] and [[Soldiers]], and is a Class Bonus Feat for [[Nobles]].

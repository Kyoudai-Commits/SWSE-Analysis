# Channel Rage

*Reference Book: [[Galaxy_of_Intrigue]]*

You have learned to turn your [[Rage]] into pure will and focus.

**Prerequisite:** [[Rage]]

**Effect:** Once per day, instead of entering into a [[Rage]], you gain a +5 bonus to your [[Will_Defense]] until the end of the encounter. This bonus counts as using your [[Rage]] ability for that day.

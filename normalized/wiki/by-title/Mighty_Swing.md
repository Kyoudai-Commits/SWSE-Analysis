# Mighty Swing

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

You are capable of delivering jarring [[Melee_Attacks]].

**Prerequisite:** [[Strength]] 13

**Effect:** You can spend two [[Swift_Actions]] in the same round to deal +1 die of damage on your next [[Melee_Attack]] in the same round.

**Special:** The effects of this feat do not stack with the extra damage provided by the [[Rapid_Strike]] feat.

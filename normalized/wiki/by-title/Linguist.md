# Linguist

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

You pick up [[Languages]] quickly and easily.

**Prerequisite:** [[Intelligence]] 13

**Effect:** You gain a number of additional [[Languages]] equal to 1 + your [[Intelligence]] modifier (minimum 1).

**Special:** You can take this feat more than once. Each time you take this feat, you gain a number of additional [[Languages]] equal to 1 + your [[Intelligence]] modifier (minimum 1). If your [[Intelligence]] modifier increases so does the number of [[Languages]] granted by this feat.

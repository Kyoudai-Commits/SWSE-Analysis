# Friends in Low Places

*Reference Book: [[Scum_and_Villainy]]*

Given enough time, you can dig up the right tool for the job.

**Prerequisite:** [[Trained]] in [[Gather_Information]]

**Effect:** Whenever you would acquire a license for a [[Restricted]] or [[Military]] object, you can substitute a [[Gather_Information]] check for a [[Knowledge_(Bureaucracy)]] check. Reduce the [[Black_Market]] cost multiplier of such items by 1.

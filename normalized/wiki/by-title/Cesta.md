# Cesta

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

Another [[Gungan]] weapon, the Cesta is a flexible pole used to hurl small [[Energy_Balls]]. It can also be used as a stafflike melee weapon. A Cesta is a melee weapon with a [[Reach]] of 2 squares. Although the Cesta is considered an [[Exotic_Weapon]], [[Gungans]] are treated as proficient if they have the [[Weapon_Proficiency_(Simple_Weapons)]] feat.

**Weapon Type:** [[Exotic_Weapons_(Melee)]]

**Size:** Large

**Cost:** 100

**Damage:** 2d4

**[[Stun]] Setting:** NO

**Weight:** 1.8 Kilograms

**Type:** Bludgeoning

**Availability:** [[Rare]]

**Special:** Can be used to hurl [[Energy_Balls]]

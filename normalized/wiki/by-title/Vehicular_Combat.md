# Vehicular Combat

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

You can avoid attacks made against your [[Vehicle]].

**Prerequisite:** [[Trained]] in [[Pilot]]

**Effect:** Once per round (as a [[Reaction]]), when you are [[Piloting]] a [[Vehicle]] or [[Starship]], you may negate a weapon hit by making a successful [[Pilot]] check. The DC of the [[Skill_Check]] is equal to the result of the attack roll you wish to negate. In addition, while you are [[Piloting]] a [[Vehicle]], you are considered proficient with Pilot-operated [[Vehicle_Weapons]].

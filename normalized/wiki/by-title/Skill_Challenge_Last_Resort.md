# Skill Challenge: Last Resort

*Reference Book: [[Galaxy_of_Intrigue]]*

When failure threatens, you throw everything you have at the [[Skill_Challenge]] to prevent disaster.

**Effect:** Once per [[Skill_Challenge]], when you or an ally accrues a third Failure (which would normally end the [[Skill_Challenge]]), you or the ally can reroll the attempt, keeping the better result.

**Special:** This Feat is allowed only in campaigns that use [[Skill_Challenges]].

# Grand Army of the Republic Training

*Reference Book: [[Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide]]*

You have received basic training from the Grand Army of the Republic and know how to turn even bulky armor into an asset.

**Prerequisite:** [[Armor_Proficiency_(Light)]]

**Effect:** If you wear [[Armor]] that provides an [[Equipment]] bonus to your [[Fortitude_Defense]], and you are proficient with that [[Armor]], you also apply the Armor's [[Equipment]] bonus to your [[Will_Defense]].

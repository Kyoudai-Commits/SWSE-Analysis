# Jedi Bonus Feats

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

At each even-numbered level (2nd, 4th, 6th, etc.), a [[Jedi]] gains a Bonus [[Feat]]. This [[Feat]] must be selected from the following Jedi Bonus Feats lists, and a [[Jedi]] must meet any prerequisites for that [[Feat]].

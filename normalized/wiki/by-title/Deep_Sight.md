# Deep Sight

*Reference Book: [[Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

You have spent so much time in the deepest parts of your home planet that you can see even in total darkness.

**Prerequisite:** [[Quarren]]

**Effect:** You gain [[Darkvision]], ignoring [[Concealment]] (including [[Total_Concealment]]) from darkness. You cannot perceive colors in total darkness.

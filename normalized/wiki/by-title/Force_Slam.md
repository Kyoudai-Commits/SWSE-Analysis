# Force Slam

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

You pound one or more creatures with [[The_Force]].

**[*[[Telekinetic]]*]**

**Time:** [[Standard_Action]]

**Targets:** All targets within a 6-Square [[Cone]] and within [[Line_of_Sight]].

**Make a [[Use_the_Force]] check.** Make one roll and compare the result to each target's [[Fortitude_Defense]]. If the result equals or exceeds a target's [[Fortitude_Defense]], it takes 4d6 points of [[Force]] damage and is knocked [[Prone]]. If the result is less than a target's [[Fortitude_Defense]], it takes half damage and is not knocked [[Prone]]. Targets larger than Medium add a size modifier to [[Fortitude_Defense]] against this [[Force_Power]]: Colossal +50, Gargantuan +20, Huge +10, Large +5. This is an [[Area_Attack]].

**Special:** When you use this [[Force_Power]], you can spend a [[Force_Point]] to deal an additional 2d6 points of damage to targets in the area.

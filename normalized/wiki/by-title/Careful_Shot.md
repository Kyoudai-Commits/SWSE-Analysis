# Careful Shot

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

You are particularly skilled at [[Aiming]] your attacks.

**Prerequisites:** [[Base_Attack_Bonus]] +2, [[Point-Blank_Shot]]

**Effect:** If you [[Aim]] before making a [[Ranged_Attack]], you gain a +1 bonus on your attack roll.

**Special:** A [[Vehicle_Pilot]] can use this feat, but since they must spend at least one [[Move_Action]] operating their [[Vehicles]], they must split their [[Aim]] (two [[Swift_Actions]]) and attack ([[Standard_Action]]) into two separate turns, making it more likely that the target will break line of sight or avoid you.

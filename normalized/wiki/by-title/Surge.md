# Surge

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

[[The_Force]] enables you to jump great heights and distances as well as move quickly.

**Time:** [[Free_Action]]

**Targets:** Self

**Make a [[Use_the_Force]] check.** The result of the check determines the effect, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 10 | You gain a +10 [[Force]] bonus on [[Jump]] checks and your [[Speed]] increases by 2 squares until the start of your next turn. The [[Force]] bonus on [[Jump]] checks includes the adjustment for increased speed. |
| 15 | You gain a +20 [[Force]] bonus on [[Jump]] checks and your [[Speed]] increases by 4 squares until the start of your next turn. The [[Force]] bonus on [[Jump]] checks includes the adjustment for increased speed. |
| 20 | You gain a +30 [[Force]] bonus on [[Jump]] checks and your [[Speed]] increases by 6 squares until the start of your next turn. The [[Force]] bonus on [[Jump]] checks includes the adjustment for increased speed. |

**Special:** Using the *Surge* Force Power counts as a [[Running_Start]] for determining a [[Jump]] DC. You can spend a [[Force_Point]] to increase the Force Power's bonus on [[Jump]] checks by 10 and increase your [[Speed]] by an additional 2 squares. You may spend a [[Destiny_Point]] to gain an additional 4 squares of movement; when you do so, you may also use any and all movement for the round as a part of a [[Jump]] (no [[Jump]] check required).

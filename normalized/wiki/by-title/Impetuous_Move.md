# Impetuous Move

*Reference Book: [[Scum_and_Villainy]]*

You have an uncanny ability to call upon your reserves when you most need them.

**Prerequisite:** [[Constitution]] 13

**Effect:** When you catch a [[Second_Wind]], you can choose to regain only half of the [[Hit_Points]] you normally would with a [[Second_Wind]] and immediately [[Move]] up to half of your [[Speed]]. This movement does not provoke [[Attacks_of_Opportunity]].

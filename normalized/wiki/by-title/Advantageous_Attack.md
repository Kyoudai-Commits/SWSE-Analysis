# Advantageous Attack

*Reference Book: [[Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide]]*

You know how to take advantage of slow-moving enemies in combat.

**Prerequisite:** [[Base_Attack_Bonus]] +1

**Effect:** When you make a successful attack against an enemy who has not yet acted in combat, you add your full Heroic Level to damage rolls.

**Normal:** Typically you add only one-half your Heroic Level to damage rolls.

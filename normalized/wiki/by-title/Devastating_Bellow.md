# Devastating Bellow

*Reference Book: [[Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

Your bellow produces greater sonic energy than do the bellows of other members of your Species.

**Prerequisite:** [[Ithorian]]

**Effect:** Your [[Bellow]] attack deals 4d6 points of damage on a hit and half damage on a miss.

**Normal:** Without this feat, your [[Bellow]] attack deals 3d6 points of damage on a hit.

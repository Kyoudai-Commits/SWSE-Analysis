# Desperate Gambit

*Reference Book: [[Scum_and_Villainy]]*

You can draw upon your inner reserves to make a shot or strike count when it matters most.

**Effect:** Once per turn, when you miss on an attack roll, you reroll the attack, but you take a -2 penalty to [[Reflex_Defense]] until the end of your next turn. If your first attack missed because you rolled a Natural 1, you can still use this feat, but you take a -5 penalty to [[Reflex_Defense]] instead. You must accept the result of the second roll, even if it's worse than the first.

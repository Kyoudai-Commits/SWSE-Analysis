# Staggering Attack

Scum and VillainyGalaxy at War

*Reference Book: [[Scum_and_Villainy]]*

You can deliver nasty attacks that leave your opponents gasping.

**Prerequisite:** **[[Sneak_Attack]]** or [[Rapid_Shot]] or [[Rapid_Strike]]

**Effect:** Any time you would deal additional damage from a [[Feat]] or [[Talent]] that grants one or more extra dice of damage, you can forgo the extra damage to move the target 2 squares per extra die sacrificed. This movement does not provoke [[Attacks_of_Opportunity]].

*Reference Book: [[Galaxy_at_War]]*

You can deliver punishing blows to your enemies, distracting them and keeping them from focusing their efforts.

**Prerequisite:** [[Base_Attack_Bonus]] +1

**Effect:** When using a melee weapon with which you are proficient, you can make an attack that disrupts an enemy's concentration. You take a -2 penalty on your attack roll, and if your attack deals damage, the target takes a -2 penalty to [[Skill_Checks]] until the end of your next turn. Alternatively, you can take a -5 penalty to your attack roll, and if your attack deals damage, the target takes a -5 penalty to [[Skill_Checks]] until the end of your next turn.

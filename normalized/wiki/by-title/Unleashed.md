# Unleashed

*Reference Book: [[Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide]]*

You gain access to [[Unleashed_Abilities]]. If you have the [[Force_Sensitivity]] feat, you also gain access to [[Force_Unleashed_Abilities]].

**Prerequisites:** Have a [[Destiny]], Receive the Gamemaster's Approval

**Effect:** You can activate [[Unleashed_Abilities]] by spending a [[Destiny_Point]]. You must also have the [[Force_Sensitivity]] feat in order to activate [[Force_Unleashed_Abilities]] for [[Force_Powers]] and [[Force_Talents]].

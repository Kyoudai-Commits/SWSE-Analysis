# Medical Team

*Reference Book: [[Galaxy_at_War]]*

You and your allies know the value of keeping soldiers alive and perform as a cohesive healing unit.

**Prerequisite:** [[Trained]] in [[Treat_Injury]]

**Effect:** You gain a +3 competence bonus on [[Treat_Injury]] checks. At the time you make a check, for each ally within 12 squares of you who also has this feat, the competence bonus increases by +1 (to a maximum of +7).

Additionally, when you use the [[Aid_Another]] Action to assist an ally who has this feat with a [[Treat_Injury]] check to restore [[Hit_Points]], your ally restores an extra 4 [[Hit_Points]] to the target.

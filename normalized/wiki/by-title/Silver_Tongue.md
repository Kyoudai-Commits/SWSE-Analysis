# Silver Tongue

*Reference Book: [[Galaxy_of_Intrigue]]*

You are adept at quickly changing a creature's [[Attitude]] toward you.

**Prerequisite:** [[Trained]] in [[Persuasion]]

**Effect:** You can [[Intimidate]] or [[Change_Attitude]] as a [[Standard_Action]].

**Normal:** To [[Intimidate]] or [[Change_Attitude]] requires a [[Full-Round_Action]].

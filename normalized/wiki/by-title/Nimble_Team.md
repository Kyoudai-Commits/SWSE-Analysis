# Nimble Team

*Reference Book: [[Galaxy_at_War]]*

You and your allies train extensively to avoid hazards and to slip through tight spaces, making it difficult to pin your team down.

**Prerequisite:** [[Trained]] in [[Acrobatics]]

**Effect:** You gain a +3 competence bonus on [[Acrobatics]] checks. At the time you make a check, for each ally within 12 squares of you who also has this feat, the competence bonus increases by +1 (to a maximum of +7).

Additionally, when you use the [[Acrobatics]] skill to [[Tumble]], you can [[Tumble]] 1 extra square.

# Phase

*Reference Book: [[Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide]]*

*See also: [[Force_Powers]]*

You can pass through solid objects, such as walls and doors.

**Time:** [[Move_Action]]

**Targets:** Self

**Make a [[Use_the_Force]] check.** When moving using your Phasing Speed, you can move through the spaces of your enemies, as well as through [[Vehicles]], walls, large objects, and other obstructions, but must end your movement in a legal, unoccupied space. You retain this Phasing Speed until the beginning of your next turn. You may move up to your Phasing Speed as a part of the activation of this Force Power. The result of the check determines the effect, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 25 | You gain a Phasing Speed of 2 squares; your Phasing Speed can never exceed your own base [[Speed]]. |
| 30 | You gain a Phasing Speed of 4 squares; your Phasing Speed can never exceed your own base [[Speed]]. |
| 35 | You gain a Phasing Speed of 6 squares; your Phasing Speed can never exceed your own base [[Speed]]. |
| 40 | You gain a Phasing Speed of 8 squares; your Phasing Speed can never exceed your own base [[Speed]]. |

**Special:** You can spend a [[Force_Point]] to increase your Phasing Speed by 2 squares.

You can maintain *Phase* from round to round, extending the normal duration. Maintaining the *Phase* Force Power is a [[Move_Action]], and whenever you maintain this [[Force_Power]] you can move up to your Phasing Speed. If you take damage while maintaining *Phase*, you must succeed on a [[Use_the_Force]] check (DC = 15 + damage taken) to continue maintaining the *Phase*.

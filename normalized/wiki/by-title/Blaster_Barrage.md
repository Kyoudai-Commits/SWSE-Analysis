# Blaster Barrage

*Reference Book: [[Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide]]*

You and your allies engulf an area with dense blaster fire.

**Prerequisite:** [[Coordinated_Attack]]

**Effect:** When you make an attack with a weapon set on [[Autofire]] that deals damage to at least one target within the designated area, you grant a +2 circumstance bonus on any of your allies' [[Autofire]] attacks made against that same target until the beginning of your next turn.

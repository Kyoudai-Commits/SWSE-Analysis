# Clawed Subspecies

*Reference Book: [[Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

You come from a [[Quarren]] subspecies that has sharp claws at the tips of its fingers.

**Prerequisite:** [[Quarren]]

**Effect:** You have Natural Weapons in the form of claws. When you make an [[Unarmed_Attack]], you can use your claws, dealing 1d6 points of Slashing damage with that attack instead of normal [[Unarmed]] damage. You are always considered armed with your Natural Weapons.

# Awareness Talent Tree

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

You are exceptionally good at noticing things and avoiding perilous situations.

## Core Talents

### Acute Senses

You may reroll any [[Perception]] check, but the result of the reroll must be accepted, even if it is worse. This Talent applies to [[Use_Computer]] checks made to [[Use_Sensors]].

### Expert Tracker

*Prerequisite:* **Acute Senses**

You take no penalty on [[Survival]] checks made to [[Track]] while moving your normal [[Speed]].

### Improved Initiative

*Prerequisite:* **Acute Senses**

You may choose to reroll any [[Initiative]] check, but the result of the reroll must be accepted, even if it is worse.

### Keen Shot

*Prerequisite:* **Acute Senses**

You take no penalty on your attack roll when attacking a target with [[Concealment]] (but not [[Total_Concealment]]).

### Uncanny Dodge I

*Prerequisite:* **Improved Initiative**

You retain your [[Dexterity]] bonus to your [[Reflex_Defense]] regardless of being caught [[Flat-Footed]] or struck by a hidden attacker. You still lose your [[Dexterity]] bonus to your [[Reflex_Defense]] if you are [[Immobilized]]. If you are the [[Vehicle_Pilot]], this Talent applies to your [[Vehicle]].

### Uncanny Dodge II

*Prerequisite:* **Uncanny Dodge I**

You cannot be [[Flanked]]. You can react to opponents on opposite sides of you as easily as you can react to a single attacker. If you are the [[Vehicle_Pilot]], this Talent applies to your [[Vehicle]].

## Additional Talents

### Reset Initiative

*Reference Book: [[Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide]]*

*Prerequisites:* **Improved Initiative**, [[Trained]] in [[Initiative]]

Once per encounter, at any time after the first round (that is, the first full round after the [[Surprise_Round]], if one occurs), you can set your [[Initiative_Count]] to your current Initiative +5.

### Weak Point

*Reference Book: [[Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide]]*

*Prerequisite:* **Keen Shot**

Once per encounter, you can use a [[Swift_Action]] to ignore the [[Damage_Reduction]] of a single target within your line of sight for the rest of your turn.

# Discblade

*Reference Book: [[Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual]]*

The Discblade, the preferred weapon of [[The_Zeison_Sha]], is a small metal ring with handles on the top and bottom of the disc and four slender blades that tightly curve back as they extend from the sides. When thrown, the Discblade flies straight due to its aerodynamic design, but a trained Zeison Sha Warrior can use [[The_Force]] to return the weapon to their hand after it has been thrown. A Discblade is treated as a [[Thrown_Weapon]] for the purpose of determining [[Range]].

**Weapon Type:** [[Exotic_Weapons_(Ranged)]]

**Size:** Small

**Cost:** 2,000

**Damage:** 2d8

**[[Stun]] Setting:** NO

**Rate of Fire:** Single-Shot

**Weight:** 1.25 Kilograms

**Type:** Slashing

**Availability:** [[Rare]]

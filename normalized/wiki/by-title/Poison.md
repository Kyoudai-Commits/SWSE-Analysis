# Category : Poison

Poisons are [[Hazards]] that are harmful or fatal to creatures. They always attack the [[Fortitude_Defense]] of a target, ignoring [[Equipment]] bonuses to [[Fortitude_Defense]], [[Damage_Reduction]], and [[Shield_Rating]]. If a Poison moves a target down the [[Condition_Track]], it likely imposes a [[Persistent_Condition]] as well, which cannot be removed until the Poison is cured or until it fails an attack roll against the target.

# Strong in the Force

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

You have a particularly strong connection to [[The_Force]].

**Effect:** When you spend a [[Force_Point]] to adjust the result of an attack roll, [[Skill_Check]], or [[Ability_Check]], you roll d8s instead of d6s.

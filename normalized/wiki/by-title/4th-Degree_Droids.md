# 4th-Degree Droids

*Reference Books: [[Core_Rulebook]], [[Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide]]*

Security and Battle 4th-Degree [[Droids]] have the following traits:

- **Ability Modifiers:** All 4th-Degree Droids receive a +2 bonus to their [[Dexterity]], but suffer -2 penalties to both their [[Intelligence]] and [[Charisma]].
- **Behavioral Inhibitors:** 4th-Degree Droids firstly obey orders from their designated owner (whether that be an actual owner or themselves).
- **Droid Traits:** All 4th-Degree Droids posses [[Droid_Traits]].
- **Droid Talents:** All 4th-Degree Droids can select Droid Talents from the [[4th-Degree_Droid_Talent_Tree]].

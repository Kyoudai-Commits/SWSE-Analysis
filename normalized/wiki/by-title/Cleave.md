# Cleave

*Reference Books: [[Core_Rulebook]], [[Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide]]*

You can follow through with a powerful [[Melee_Attack]].

**Prerequisite:** [[Strength]] 13, [[Power_Attack]]

**Effect:** If you deal an opponent enough damage to reduce its [[Hit_Points]] to 0, you get an immediate extra [[Melee_Attack]] against another opponent within your [[Reach]]. The extra attack is with the same weapon and at the same attack bonus as the attack that dropped the previous opponent. You can use this ability once per round.

**Combined Feat:** If you have the [[Follow_Through]] feat, you can move up to your [[Speed]] before making the extra [[Melee_Attack]] granted by the Cleave feat.

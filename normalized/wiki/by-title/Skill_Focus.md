# Skill Focus

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

One of your [[Skills]] is particularly well honed.

**Effect:** Choose one [[Skill]] in which you are [[Trained]]. You gain a +5 competence bonus on all [[Skill_Checks]] made with that [[Skill]].

**Special:** This feat may be selected multiple times. Its effects do not stack. Each time you take this feat, it applies to a different [[Trained_Skill]].

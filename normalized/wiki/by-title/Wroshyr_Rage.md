# Wroshyr Rage

*Reference Book: [[Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

You have learnt that in the dangerous jungles of [[Kashyyyk]], your mighty [[Rage]] must be tempered by wariness so that predators don't get the drop on you.

**Prerequisite:** [[Wookiee]]

**Effect:** When you first enter your [[Rage]], you gain a number of [[Bonus_Hit_Points]] equal to 10 + one-half your level. Damage is subtracted from [[Bonus_Hit_Points]] first, and any [[Bonus_Hit_Points]] remaining at the end of the encounter are lost. [[Bonus_Hit_Points]] do not stack.

# Opportunistic Trickery

*Reference Book: [[Galaxy_of_Intrigue]]*

When your opponent lets down its guard, you know how to set it off balance for several moments.

**Prerequisites:** **[[Sneak_Attack]]**, [[Combat_Reflexes]]

**Effect:** Whenever an opponent provokes an [[Attack_of_Opportunity]] from you, once per turn you can sacrifice that attack to reduce the target's [[Reflex_Defense]] by 2 for the following round.

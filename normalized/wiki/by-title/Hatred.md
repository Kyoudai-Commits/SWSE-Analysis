# Hatred

*Reference Book: [[Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual]]*

You give yourself over to [[The_Dark_Side]], letting your hate radiate out from your body in palpable waves.

**[*[[Dark_Side]]*]**

**Time:** [[Standard_Action]]

**Targets:** All creatures within 6 squares of you.

**Make a [[Use_the_Force]] check.** The result of the check determines the effects, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 15 | Until the end of your next turn, any creature that starts its turn within 6 squares of you takes 1d6 points of [[Force]] damage, and takes a -1 penalty to all its [[Defenses]]. |
| 20 | Until the end of your next turn, any creature that starts its turn within 6 squares of you takes 2d6 points of [[Force]] damage, and takes a -1 penalty to all its [[Defenses]]. |
| 25 | Until the end of your next turn, any creature that starts its turn within 6 squares of you takes 3d6 points of [[Force]] damage, and takes a -1 penalty to all its [[Defenses]]. |
| 30 | Until the end of your next turn, any creature that starts its turn within 6 squares of you takes 4d6 points of [[Force]] damage, and takes a -1 penalty to all its [[Defenses]]. |

**Special:** You can spend a [[Force_Point]] to increase the damage dealt by an additional +1d6 points of [[Force]] damage.

You can maintain *Hatred* from round to round, extending the normal duration. Maintaining the *Hatred* [[Force_Power]] is a [[Swift_Action]], and you must make a new [[Use_the_Force]] check each round. If you take damage while maintaining *Hatred*, you must succeed on a [[Use_the_Force]] check (DC = 15 + damage taken) to continue maintaining the *Hatred*.

# Stagger

*Reference Book: [[Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

You use [[The_Force]] to lash out at a nearby enemy, causing it to stumble.

**[*[[Telekinetic]]*]**

**Time:** [[Swift_Action]]

**Targets:** One target adjacent to you.

**Make a [[Use_the_Force]] check.** Compare the result to the target's [[Fortitude_Defense]]. If the check result equals or exceeds the target's [[Fortitude_Defense]], the target takes 2d6 points of [[Force]] damage and is pushed 1 square away from you. This movement does not provoke [[Attacks_of_Opportunity]].

**Special:** You can spend a [[Force_Point]] when you activate this Force Power to affect all enemies adjacent to you, in which case compare your [[Use_the_Force]] check result to each target's [[Fortitude_Defense]].

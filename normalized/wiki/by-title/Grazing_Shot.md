# Grazing Shot

*Reference Book: [[Galaxy_of_Intrigue]]*

Using precise aim, you can hit two targets with a single shot.

**Prerequisite:** [[Point-Blank_Shot]]

**Effect:** When you successfully make a [[Ranged_Attack]] against a single target, you can make a second attack roll against an additional target that is in direct [[Line_of_Sight]], and no farther than 6 squares from the original target. If the second attack roll succeeds, make a single damage roll, and divide the damage equally between the two targets. If the second attack roll fails, you deal no damage to either target.

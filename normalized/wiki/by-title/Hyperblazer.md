# Hyperblazer

*Reference Book: [[Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide]]*

You are familiar with blazing new routes and navigating the tangled web of hyperspace in [[The_Unknown_Regions]].

**Prerequisite:** [[Trained]] in [[Use_Computer]]

**Effect:** When traversing the Hyperspace Tangle, you reduce by half the calculation time and penalties on [[Use_Computer]] checks to [[Astrogate]] (see [[Hyperspace_Navigation_Challenges]]). You also reduce hyperspace mapping time by half when mapping new hyperspace routes.

# Confident Success

*Reference Book: [[Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

You have confidence in your ability to learn information that others consider secret, and in doing so, boost your confidence in other areas.

**Prerequisite:** [[Bothan]]

**Effect:** Whenever you successfully use the [[Learn_Secret_Information]] application of the [[Gather_Information]] skill, you gain 1 [[Force_Point]]. You cannot gain more than 3 [[Force_Points]] each level, and you cannot have more [[Force_Points]] than you gained when you reached your current level.

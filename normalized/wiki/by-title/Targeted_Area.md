# Targeted Area

*Reference Book: [[Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide]]*

Your [[Area_Attacks]] deal more damage to one target within the area of effect.

**Prerequisite:** [[Base_Attack_Bonus]] +5

**Effect:** When you make a successful [[Area_Attack]] with a weapon with which you are proficient, select a target hit by the attack. That target takes an additional 5 points of damage from the attack, before applying the effect of the **[[Evasion]]** Talent.

# Powerful Rage

*Reference Book: [[Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide]]*

Your Rage produces feats of extreme strength.

**Prerequisite:** [[Rage]]

**Effect:** You gain a +4 bonus to [[Strength]] checks and [[Strength]]-based [[Skill_Checks]] when Raging.

# Trample

*Reference Book: [[Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide]]*

When charging, your living mount can flatten smaller targets between you and your target.

**Prerequisite:** [[Trained]] in [[Ride]]

**Effect:** When you use the [[Mounted_Charge]] Action, your mount can make a single [[Melee_Attack]] as a [[Free_Action]]. While [[Charging]], compare the result to the [[Reflex_Defense]] of each enemy whose square you [[Mounted_Charge]] through. Each enemy hit by the attack takes Claw, Gore, or Slam damage from the mount's Natural Weapons plus the mount's [[Strength]] modifier. You or your mount still can make a regular attack at the end of the [[Mounted_Charge]].

**Special:** You can use this feat if either you or your mount has taken it. Mounts with this feat do not require the prerequisite.

# Pitiless Warrior

*Reference Book: [[Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

Whenever you fell an opponent, the victory stokes your bloodlust and prepares you for more carnage.

**Prerequisite:** [[Trandoshan]]

**Effect:** Whenever you reduce a target to 0 [[Hit_Points]], you gain a number of [[Bonus_Hit_Points]] equal to 5 + one-half your Character Level. Damage is subtracted from [[Bonus_Hit_Points]] first, and any [[Bonus_Hit_Points]] remaining at the end of the encounter are lost. [[Bonus_Hit_Points]] do not stack.

# Force of Personality

*Reference Book: [[Galaxy_at_War]]*

As a result of your self-confidence and conviction, your will is strong.

**Prerequisite:** [[Charisma]] 13

**Effect:** You can use either your [[Wisdom]] modifier or your [[Charisma]] modifier to determine your [[Will_Defense]].

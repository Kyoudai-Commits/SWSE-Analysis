# Impersonate

*Reference Book: [[Scum_and_Villainy]]*

You can alter your appearance to resemble a specific person.

**Prerequisites:** [[Shapeshift]], [[Skill_Focus_(Deception)]]

**Effect:** You can make a [[Deception]] check to alter your features to that of a specific person. This feat also allows you to change your voice to match the target's. You always treat impersonating a person as a Moderate [[Deception]].

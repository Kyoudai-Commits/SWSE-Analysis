# Master Tracker

*Reference Book: [[Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

You can follow a shadow on a moonless night and survive in the wilderness with nothing but your wits to protect you.

**Prerequisite:** [[Rodian]]

**Effect:** Whenever you spend a [[Force_Point]] to add to a [[Survival]] check, increase the die type by two steps (from d6 to d10, or from d8 to d12).

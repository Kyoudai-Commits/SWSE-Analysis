# Mounted Defense

*Reference Book: [[Threats_of_the_Galaxy]]*

You are able to react to incoming attacks, using your mount or [[Speeder_Bike]] to absorb attacks against you.

**Prerequisite:** [[Trained]] in [[Pilot]] or [[Ride]]

**Effect:** When riding a [[Beast]] as a mount or on a [[Speeder_Bike]] (as a passenger or a [[Vehicle_Pilot]]), once per encounter you can redirect any attack made against you at your [[Beast]] or [[Vehicle]] instead. The decision to redirect the attack is made after the result of the attack roll is determined but before damage or other effects are resolved.

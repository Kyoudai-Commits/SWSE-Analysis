# Power Pack

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

**Cost:** 25

**Weight:** 0.1 Kilograms

A Power Pack is a compact rectangular battery that fits into the grip or barrel of an energy weapon, such as a blaster, to provide the power needed to fire it. A Power Pack can be recharged with a [[Power_Recharger]].

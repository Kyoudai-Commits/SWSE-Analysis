# Darkness Dweller

*Reference Book: [[Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

You have lived underground for so long that you can spot sneaky enemies easily and make it hard for them to slip past your allies.

**Prerequisite:** [[Sullustan]]

**Effect:** Any enemy that makes a [[Stealth]] check within 10 squares of you takes a -2 penalty to the check. This penalty does not stack with the same penalty imposed by other characters with this feat.

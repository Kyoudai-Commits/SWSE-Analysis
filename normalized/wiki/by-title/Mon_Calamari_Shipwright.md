# Mon Calamari Shipwright

*Reference Book: [[Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

You have intimate knowledge of the workings of [[Starships]] and other [[Vehicles]] and can keep them running no matter what.

**Prerequisite:** [[Mon_Calamari]]

**Effect:** You need spend only two [[Swift_Actions]] to move a [[Vehicle]] you occupy +1 step on the [[Condition_Track]]. In addition, you automatically succeed on [[Mechanics]] checks to [[Reroute_Power]].

**Normal:** It takes three [[Swift_Actions]] to use the [[Reroute_Power]] Action.

# Bantha Rush

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

You can shove your opponents around the battlefield to gain a tactical advantage.

**Prerequisites:** [[Strength]] 13, [[Base_Attack_Bonus]] +1

**Effect:** After making a successful [[Melee_Attack]] against an opponent up to one size category larger than you, you can choose to move that opponent 1 square in any direction as a [[Free_Action]]. You can't Bantha Rush an opponent that's being [[Grabbed]] or [[Grappled]], and you can't Bantha Rush your opponent into a solid object or another creature's [[Fighting_Space]].

# Instinctive Defense

*Reference Book: [[Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide]]*

You naturally know how to avoid attacks and defend your mind and body when [[The_Force]] is with you.

**Prerequisite:** Cannot be a [[Droid]]

**Effect:** On your turn, as a [[Free_Action]], you can spend a [[Force_Point]] to increase all of your [[Defenses]] by 2 until the start of your next turn.

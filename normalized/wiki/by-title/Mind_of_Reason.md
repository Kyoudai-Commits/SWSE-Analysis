# Mind of Reason

*Reference Book: [[Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

You have learnt to consider all sides of an issue carefully before making a decision, allowing you to reason out information that may not be readily apparent.

**Prerequisite:** [[Cerean]]

**Effect:** You may use your [[Wisdom]] bonus instead of your [[Intelligence]] bonus for all [[Intelligence]]-based [[Skill_Checks]].

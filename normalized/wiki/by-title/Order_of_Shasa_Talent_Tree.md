# Order of Shasa Talent Tree

*Reference Book: [[Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide]]*

[[The_Order_of_Shasa]] is still developing training techniques and abilities, taking most of their influence from Manaan's oceans. Only Force-users that are part of [[The_Order_of_Shasa]] may select [[Talents]] from this tree.

## Core Talents

### Deception Awareness

Realizing how devastating the consequences would have been if [[The_Sith]] had successfully deceived her and her companions during the Jedi Civil War, Shasa has developed a technique for using [[The_Force]] to detect deceptions. You gain +5 to your [[Will_Defense]] against uses of the [[Deception]] skill.

Additionally, you can make a [[Use_the_Force]] check instead of a [[Perception]] check to [[Sense_Deception]] and [[Sense_Influence]]. You are considered [[Trained]] in [[Perception]] for the purposes of using this Talent. If you are entitled to a [[Perception]] check reroll, you may reroll your [[Use_the_Force]] check instead (subject to the same circumstances and limitations).

### Greater Weapon Focus (Fira)

*Prerequisite:* [[Weapon_Focus_(Fira)]]

You have mastered the chosen weapon of [[The_Order_of_Shasa]]. You gain a +1 bonus on [[Melee_Attack]] rolls with a [[Fira]]. This bonus stacks with the bonus granted by the [[Weapon_Focus_(Fira)]] feat.

### Progenitor's Call

You have learned to sense the call of your ancestors and wield it through [[The_Force]]. Once per encounter, you can telepathically disrupt an opponent by making a [[Use_the_Force]] check against the target's [[Will_Defense]]. If you succeed, the target is confused by the call, moving -1 [[Persistent]] step down the [[Condition_Track]] and losing its [[Standard_Action]] on its next turn.

A creature can only be affected by this power once per encounter, and the [[Persistent_Condition]] can be removed with a DC 15 [[Treat_Injury]] check or by resting for 8 hours. This is a Mind-Affecting effect.

### Waveform

Taking cues from Manaan's oceans, you know how to emulate wave action using [[The_Force]]. As a [[Swift_Action]], when you use a [[Telekinetic]] Force Power (that is, a [[Force_Power]] with the [*[[Telekinetic]]*] descriptor), you manipulate the particles of the environment around you to add to the force of the impact, allowing you to add your [[Charisma]] modifier to any damage dealt (minimum +1).

### Fira Mastery

*Fira Mastery Talent created by Miraheze user Chellewalker.*

You treat a [[Fira]] as a [[Simple_Weapon_(Melee)]] instead of as an [[Exotic_Weapon_(Melee)]]. Additionally, you gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls with a [[Fira]]. This Talent counts as the [[Weapon_Focus_(Fira)]] Feat for the purpose of satisfying prerequisites. If you also have the [[Weapon_Focus_(Simple_Weapons)]] Feat, the attack bonus provided by this Talent does not stack with the attack bonus provided by [[Weapon_Focus_(Simple_Weapons)]].

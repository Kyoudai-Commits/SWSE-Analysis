# Fringe Benefits

*Reference Book: [[Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

You are a well-known member of criminal society and can get the best deal possible on illicit goods.

**Prerequisite:** [[Rodian]]

**Effect:** Whenever you buy goods on the [[Black_Market]], reduce the cost multiplier by 2 (for example, from x5 to x3), to a minimum of x1.

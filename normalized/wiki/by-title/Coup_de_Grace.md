# Coup de Grace

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

As a [[Full-Round_Action]], you can use a melee weapon to deliver a Coup de Grace to a [[Helpless]] target. You can also use a ranged weapon, provided you are adjacent to the target. You automatically score a Critical Hit, dealing double damage. A defender reduced to 0 [[Hit_Points]] by a Coup de Grace dies instantly, or, in the case of a [[Droid]], is destroyed. An [[Unconscious]] or [[Disabled]] defender hit by a Coup de Grace also dies or is destroyed instantly.

You can't deliver a Coup de Grace against a [[Vehicle]] or object.

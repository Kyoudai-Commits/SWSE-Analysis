# Unwavering Focus

*Reference Book: [[Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

Others might try to sway you, but once you focus on an objective, you cannot be stopped.

**Prerequisite:** [[Zabrak]]

**Effect:** Whenever you are targeted by a Mind-Affecting effect that requires a [[Skill_Check]] against your [[Will_Defense]], as a [[Reaction]] you can impose a -2 penalty to that [[Skill_Check]].

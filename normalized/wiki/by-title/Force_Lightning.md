# Force Lightning

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

You blast an enemy with deadly arcs of [[Force]] energy.

**[*[[Dark_Side]]*]**

**Time:** [[Standard_Action]]

**Targets:** One target within 6 squares and within [[Line_of_Sight]].

**Make a [[Use_the_Force]] check.** Make one roll and compare the result to the target's [[Reflex_Defense]]. If your [[Use_the_Force]] check equals or exceeds the target's [[Reflex_Defense]], the target takes 8d6 points of [[Force]] damage and moves -1 step along the [[Condition_Track]]. Otherwise, the target takes half damage and does not move along the [[Condition_Track]].

**Special:** You can spend a [[Force_Point]] to move a target an additional -1 step along the [[Condition_Track]] when you successfully hit it with *Force Lightning*.

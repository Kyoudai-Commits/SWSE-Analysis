# Reroute Power

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

By spending 3 [[Swift_Actions]] during the same turn, or on consecutive turns, an [[Engineer]] can make a DC 20 [[Mechanics]] check to move their [[Vehicle]] +1 step on the [[Condition_Track]].

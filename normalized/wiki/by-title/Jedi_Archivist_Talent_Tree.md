# Jedi Archivist Talent Tree

*Reference Book: [[Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide]]*

You have dedicated your life as a Jedi to preserving knowledge and applying it in times when it is needed.

## Core Talents

### Direct

As a [[Standard_Action]], you can return one spent [[Force_Power]] to the [[Force_Power_Suite]] of any ally within 6 squares of you and in your [[Line_of_Sight]]. The [[Force_Power]] must have been spent by the ally you designate.

### Impart Knowledge

*Prerequisite:* **[[Skilled_Advisor]]**

You can [[Aid_Another]] on the [[Knowledge]] checks of an ally within 6 squares of you as a [[Reaction]] for [[Knowledge]] skills you are [[Trained]] in.

### Insight of the Force

You can make a [[Use_the_Force]] check in place of a [[Knowledge]] check for any [[Knowledge]] skill you are not [[Trained]] in. You are considered [[Trained]] in that [[Knowledge]] skill for the purposes of using this Talent. If you are entitled to a [[Knowledge]] check reroll, you can reroll your [[Use_the_Force]] check instead (subject to the same circumstances and limitations).

### Master Advisor

*Prerequisite:* **[[Skilled_Advisor]]**

When you use the **[[Skilled_Advisor]]** Talent, the ally you aid gains one temporary [[Force_Point]] at the end of their next turn. If the [[Force_Point]] is not spent before the end of the encounter, it is lost.

### Scholarly Knowledge

As a [[Swift_Action]], you can reroll a [[Knowledge]] check and keep the better of the two results. This can be used with any [[Knowledge]] skill you are [[Trained]] in.

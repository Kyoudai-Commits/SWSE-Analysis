# Improvised Weapon Mastery

*Reference Book: [[Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide]]*

You use [[Improvised_Weapons]] with true skill.

**Prerequisite:** [[Weapon_Proficiency_(Simple_Weapons)]]

**Effect:** You treat all [[Improvised_Weapons]] as [[Simple_Weapons]], and you can use them without penalty and with [[Talents]] and [[Feats]] designed for use with [[Simple_Weapons]]. You also deal an additional 1d6 points of damage on a successful hit.

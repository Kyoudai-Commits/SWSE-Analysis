# Returning Bug

*Reference Book: [[Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

You have mastered the art of using Yuuzhan Vong [[Thrown_Weapons]] and can cause them to return to your hand.

**Prerequisite:** [[Weapon_Proficiency_(Simple_Weapons)]]

**Effect:** When you miss with a [[Razor_Bug]] or a [[Thud_Bug]], the weapon returns to your hand immediately.

# Savage Attack

*Reference Book: [[Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide]]*

You are well trained in attacking multiple times in a round.

**Prerequisite:** [[Double_Attack]] (Chosen Weapon)

**Effect:** Choose one [[Weapon_Group]] or [[Exotic_Weapon]] you have selected for the [[Double_Attack]] feat. When you use a [[Full_Attack]] Action and successfully hit the target with your first attack, you deal +1 die of damage on each successful attack remaining in the [[Full_Attack]] made against that target.

# Stand Tall

*Reference Book: [[Galaxy_of_Intrigue]]*

Your courage under fire inspires those around you to acts of bravery.

**Effect:** Once per encounter when you take damage, all allies who are within 6 squares of you and within your [[Line_of_Sight]] can, as a [[Reaction]], make a single attack on the target that damaged you.

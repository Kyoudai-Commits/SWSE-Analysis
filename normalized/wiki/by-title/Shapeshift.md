# Category : Shapeshift

[Species] are shapechangers and can alter their appearance at will (see the [[Deception]] skill). While Shapeshifted, a [Species] gains a +10 [[Species]] bonus on [[Deception]] checks made to disguise appearance. Additionally, a [Species] can disguise its appearance as a [[Full-Round_Action]] at no penalty.

- [Species] cannot alter their size without the [[Metamorph]] feat.

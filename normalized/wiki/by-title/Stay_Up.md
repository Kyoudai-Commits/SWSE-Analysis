# Stay Up

*Reference Book: [[Scum_and_Villainy]]*

You can soldier on in spite of damage dealt to you.

**Prerequisite:** [[Trained]] in [[Endurance]]

**Effect:** Once per encounter, when you would normally take damage from an attack, you can instead choose to take half damage and move -1 step along the [[Condition_Track]].

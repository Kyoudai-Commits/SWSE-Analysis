# Fortifying Recovery

*Reference Book: [[Galaxy_at_War]]*

Even as you reel from attacks, you can get yourself back into the fight and make a difference.

**Prerequisite:** [[Constitution]] 13

**Effect:** When you take the [[Recover]] Action, you also gain a number of [[Bonus_Hit_Points]] equal to 2 x your [[Constitution]] bonus (minimum 2). Damage is subtracted from the [[Bonus_Hit_Points]] first, and any [[Bonus_Hit_Points]] remaining at the end of encounter are lost. [[Bonus_Hit_Points]] do not stack.

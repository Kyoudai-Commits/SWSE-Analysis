# Simple Weapons (Melee)

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

Simple Weapons include weapons that require no special training to use. [[Unarmed_Attacks]] and Natural Weapons are considered Light Simple Weapons in which all characters are proficient. [[Weapon_Proficiency_(Simple_Weapons)]] is a Starting Feat for all [[Heroic_Classes]] (which is also applicable to [[Grenades]] and [[Simple_Weapons_(Ranged)]]).

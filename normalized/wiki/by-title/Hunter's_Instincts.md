# Hunter's Instincts

*Reference Book: [[Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

You have a sixth sense that allows you to spot your prey under almost any circumstances.

**Prerequisite:** [[Rodian]]

**Effect:** Whenever you reroll a [[Perception]] check, you always keep the better result, even if you have multiple reroll abilities.

# Survivor Talent Tree

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

As an explorer of dangerous places, you are trained to react to danger swiftly and adroitly, as well as navigate [[Difficult_Terrain]], and reduce damage.

## Core Talents

### Evasion

If you are hit by an [[Area_Attack]], you take half damage if the attack hits you. If the area attack misses you, you take no damage. If you are the [[Vehicle_Pilot]], this talent applies to your [[Vehicle]].

### Extreme Effort

You can spend two [[Swift_Actions]] to gain a +5 bonus on a single [[Strength]] check or [[Strength]]-based [[Skill_Check]] made during the same round.

### Sprint

When you use the [[Run]] Action, you can move up to five times your [[Speed]] (instead of up to four times).

### Surefooted

Your [[Speed]] is not reduced by [[Difficult_Terrain]].

### Wilderness Medic

*Wilderness Medic Talent created by Miraheze user Chellewalker.*

You can use your [[Survival]] check modifier instead of your [[Treat_Injury]] modifier when making [[Treat_Injury]] checks. If you are entitled to an [[Treat_Injury]] check reroll, you can reroll your [[Survival]] check instead (subject to the same circumstances and limitations). You are considered to be [[Trained]] in [[Treat_Injury]].

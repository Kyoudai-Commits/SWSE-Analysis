# Cornered

*Reference Book: [[Scum_and_Villainy]]*

You are vicious when cornered.

**Effect:** Whenever you are [[Threatened]] by an opponent and unable to take the [[Withdraw]] Action, you gain a +2 bonus on attack rolls against opponents that [[Threaten]] you.

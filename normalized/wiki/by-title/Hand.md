# Hand

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

**Cost:** 50 x [[Cost_Factor]]

**Weight:** (5 x [[Cost_Factor]]) Kilograms

A Droid is considered to have a true Hand if its gripping Appendage includes at least three digits, one of which is opposable. Factory-model [[3PO-Series_Protocol_Droids]] and [[B1-Series_Battle_Droids]] come equipped with Hands.

Hands deal damage when used for [[Unarmed_Attacks]] as determined by the Droid's size:

| DROID SIZE | FINE | DIMINUTIVE | TINY | SMALL | MEDIUM | LARGE | HUGE | GARGANTUAN | COLOSSAL |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| UNARMED DAMAGE | - | - | 1 | 1d2 | 1d3 | 1d4 | 1d6 | 1d8 | 2d6 |

# Detonate

*Reference Book: [[Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

You can perceive points of weakness within an object and use [[The_Force]] to telekinetically press on one of those points, shattering the object.

**[*[[Telekinetic]]*]**

**Time:** [[Standard_Action]]

**Targets:** One unattended object within 12 squares of you and in your [[Line_of_Sight]].

**Make a [[Use_the_Force]] check.** The result of the check determines the result, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 20 | The object takes 4d6 points of damage. |
| 25 | The object takes 6d6 points of damage. |
| 30 | The object takes 8d6 points of damage. |
| 35 | The object takes 10d6 points of damage. |

**Special:** You may spend a [[Force_Point]] when you activate this Force Power to increase the damage dealt to the object by +2d6.

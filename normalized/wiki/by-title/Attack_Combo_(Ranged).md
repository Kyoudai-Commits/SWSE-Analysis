# Attack Combo (Ranged)

*Reference Book: [[Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

Your damage-dealing ability improves after hitting a single target with successive [[Ranged_Attacks]].

**Prerequisite:** [[Base_Attack_Bonus]] +3

**Effect:** If you hit a single target with two consecutive [[Ranged_Attacks]] during the same turn, any additional [[Ranged_Attacks]] you make until the end of your next turn (including [[Attacks_of_Opportunity]] and attacks made as [[Reactions]]) deal +1 die of damage on a hit. This extra damage stacks with extra damage granted by other [[Feats]] or [[Talents]].

# Whirlwind Attack

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

You can strike nearby opponents in an amazing, spinning [[Melee_Attack]].

**Prerequisites:** [[Dexterity]] 13, [[Base_Attack_Bonus]] +4, [[Melee_Defense]]

**Effect:** As a [[Full-Round_Action]], you can make an [[Area_Attack]] with your melee weapon, striking every target within your [[Reach]]. This Whirlwind Attack uses the [[Area_Attack]] rules; you make one attack roll and apply the result to every target in range.

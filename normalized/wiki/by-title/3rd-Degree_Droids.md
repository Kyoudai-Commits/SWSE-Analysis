# 3rd-Degree Droids

*Reference Books: [[Core_Rulebook]], [[Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide]]*

Protocol and Domestic 3rd-Degree [[Droids]] have the following traits:

- **Ability Modifiers:** All 3rd-Degree Droids receive +2 bonuses to both their [[Wisdom]] and [[Charisma]], but suffer a -2 penalty to their [[Strength]].
- **Behavioral Inhibitors:** 3rd-Degree Droids firstly cannot knowingly harm a sentient creature, unless another creature would be harmed due to inaction. 3rd-Degree Droids secondly obey orders from their designated owner (whether that be an actual owner or themselves).
- **Droid Traits:** All 3rd-Degree Droids posses [[Droid_Traits]].
- **Droid Talents:** All 3rd-Degree Droids can select Droid Talents from the [[3rd-Degree_Droid_Talent_Tree]].

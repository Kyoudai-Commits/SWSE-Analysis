# Mechanical Martial Arts

*Reference Book: [[Scavenger's_Guide_to_Droids]]*

You know how to inflict pain using your metal appendages when fighting in [[Unarmed]] combat against organic enemies.

**Prerequisites:** [[Droid]], [[Base_Attack_Bonus]] +1, [[Martial_Arts_I]]

**Effect:** When you successfully damage an enemy with an [[Unarmed_Attack]], the enemy takes a -5 penalty to all [[Melee_Attack]] rolls and damage rolls until the start of your next turn.

**Special:** If you strike an organic enemy while using this feat during an [[Attack_of_Opportunity]], the penalty lasts until the start of the enemy's next turn.

# Attack Combo (Melee)

*Reference Book: [[Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

After landing successive blows against a single target, your [[Melee_Attacks]] and [[Unarmed_Attacks]] become more devastating.

**Prerequisite:** [[Base_Attack_Bonus]] +3

**Effect:** If you hit a single target with two consecutive [[Melee_Attacks]] and/or [[Unarmed_Attacks]] during the same turn, any additional [[Melee_Attacks]] or [[Unarmed_Attacks]] you make until the end of your next turn (including [[Attacks_of_Opportunity]] and attacks made as [[Reactions]]) deal +1 die of damage on a hit. This extra damage stacks with extra damage granted by other [[Feats]] or [[Talents]].

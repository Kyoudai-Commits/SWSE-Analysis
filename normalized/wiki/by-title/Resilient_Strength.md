# Resilient Strength

*Reference Book: [[Galaxy_at_War]]*

Your brute strength and physical conditioning allow you to shrug off effects that would debilitate others.

**Prerequisite:** [[Strength]] 13

**Effect:** You can use either your [[Strength]] modifier or your [[Constitution]] modifier to determine your [[Fortitude_Defense]].

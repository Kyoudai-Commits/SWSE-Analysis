# Opportunistic Shooter

*Reference Book: [[Galaxy_at_War]]*

You are quick to react when nearby enemies expose themselves to your attacks.

**Effect:** You gain a +2 bonus on [[Attacks_of_Opportunity]] with ranged weapons.

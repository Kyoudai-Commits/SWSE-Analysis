# Slow

*Reference Book: [[Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide]]*

[[The_Force]] enables you to slow your targets as if they are encumbered by an extremely heavy load, making it difficult for them to move.

**[*[[Telekinetic]]*]**

**Time:** [[Standard_Action]]

**Targets:** One creature or [[Droid]] within 12 squares, and within [[Line_of_Sight]].

**Make a [[Use_the_Force]] check.** If your result equals or exceeds your target's [[Fortitude_Defense]], they take the following effects as determined by your check result:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 15 | Target's [[Speed]] is reduced by 1 square until the beginning of your next turn. The target is also [[Encumbered]], as though carrying a [[Heavy_Load]], taking a -10 penalty on [[Acrobatics]], [[Climb]], [[Endurance]], [[Initiative]], [[Jump]], [[Stealth]], and [[Swim]] checks. |
| 20 | Target's [[Speed]] is reduced by 2 squares until the beginning of your next turn. The target is also [[Encumbered]], as though carrying a [[Heavy_Load]], taking a -10 penalty on [[Acrobatics]], [[Climb]], [[Endurance]], [[Initiative]], [[Jump]], [[Stealth]], and [[Swim]] checks. |
| 25 | Target's [[Speed]] is reduced by 3 squares until the beginning of your next turn. The target is also [[Encumbered]], as though carrying a [[Heavy_Load]], taking a -10 penalty on [[Acrobatics]], [[Climb]], [[Endurance]], [[Initiative]], [[Jump]], [[Stealth]], and [[Swim]] checks. |
| 30 | Target's [[Speed]] is reduced by 4 squares until the beginning of your next turn. The target is also [[Encumbered]], as though carrying a [[Heavy_Load]], taking a -10 penalty on [[Acrobatics]], [[Climb]], [[Endurance]], [[Initiative]], [[Jump]], [[Stealth]], and [[Swim]] checks. |

The target adds a size modifier to its [[Fortitude_Defense]] for purposes of this [[Force_Power]]: Colossal, +50; Gargantuan, +20; Huge, +10; Large, +5; Medium or smaller, +0.

**Special:** You can spend a [[Force_Point]] to impose a -5 penalty to your target's [[Fortitude_Defense]] against the use of this [[Force_Power]].

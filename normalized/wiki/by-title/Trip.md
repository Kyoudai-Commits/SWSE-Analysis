# Trip

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

You are skilled at tripping [[Grappled]] foes.

**Prerequisite:** [[Base_Attack_Bonus]] +1

**Effect:** If you succeed on a [[Grappling]] attack and your opponent fails the opposed [[Grapple]] check, it falls [[Prone]] in its space and is no longer considered [[Grappled]]. A [[Prone]] opponent takes a -5 penalty on [[Melee_Attack]] rolls. [[Melee_Attacks]] made against a [[Prone]] target gain a +5 bonus, while [[Ranged_Attack]] made against a [[Prone]] target take a -5 penalty.

**Special:** You cannot use the [[Pin]] and Trip feats in the same round.

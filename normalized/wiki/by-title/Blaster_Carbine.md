# Blaster Carbine

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

This small Blaster Carbine has a short barrel and compact two-handed grip, making it look more like a long pistol than a rifle. Some Blaster Carbines, such as the E-5 used by [[B1-Series_Battle_Droids]], have a [[Retractable_Stock]]. A Blaster Carbine without a [[Retractable_Stock]] costs 850 credits (instead of 900 credits). Because of its compact design, a Blaster Carbine can always be used to make an [[Attack_of_Opportunity]], even if its stock is not folded. A Blaster Carbine requires a [[Power_Pack]] to operate. After 50 shots, the [[Power_Pack]] must be replaced.

**Weapon Type:** [[Rifles]]

**Size:** Medium

**Cost:** 900

**Damage:** 3d8

**[[Stun]] Setting:** YES

**Rate of Fire:** Single-Shot, [[Autofire]]

**Weight:** 2.2 Kilograms

**Type:** Energy

**Availability:** [[Restricted]]

**Inaccurate:** This weapon cannot fire at targets at Long [[Range]].

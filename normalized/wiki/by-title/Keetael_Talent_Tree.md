# Keetael Talent Tree

*Reference Book: [[Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide]]*

Keetael learn to efficiently and secretly manipulate [[The_Force]] in combat. Because of their long lives, they gain certain advantages when attempting to view the past through [[The_Force]]. Only Force-users that are part of [[The_Keetael]] may select [[Talents]] from this tree.

## Core Talents

### Conceal Force Use

You have learned to use [[The_Force]] without telltale gestures, reducing the disturbances created in the process. Whenever you make a [[Use_the_Force]] check, as a [[Swift_Action]] you can make a [[Deception]] check to convey [[Deceptive_Appearances]] in order to conceal the effects of your [[Use_the_Force]] use. Normal modifiers for the Deception's complexity still apply.

### Force Direction

You can use [[The_Force]] to guide your [[Ranged_Attacks]] to their target. Whenever you spend a [[Force_Point]] to add to a [[Ranged_Attack]] roll, you can always add +3 (or +4, if you roll d8s for [[Force_Points]]) instead of rolling the die.

### Force Momentum

You use [[The_Force]] to add to the impact of your melee weapon, increasing your damage. Whenever you spend a [[Force_Point]] to add to a [[Melee_Attack]] roll, if the attack hits you can also add the Force Point's result to the damage roll.

### Past Visions

*Prerequisites:* **[[Visions]]**

The long-lived [[Draethos]] are particularly adept at searching and understanding the past. When using *[[Farseeing]]* to look into the past, reduce your DC numbers by half. Also, you are able to see everything within 6 squares of your target clearly without spending a [[Force_Point]].

# Recovering Surge

*Reference Book: [[Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

When you get back into the fight, you do not let your wounds hamper you.

**Effect:** When you catch a [[Second_Wind]], you move +1 step on the [[Condition_Track]].

# Light Armor

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

Light Armor is by far the most widespread [[Armor]] in the *Star Wars* galaxy, used by hunters, bounty hunters, bouncers, and anyone in between. All Light Armor shares the following characteristics unless otherwise specified:

- Light Armor inflicts a -2 [[Armor_Check_Penalty]] (negated by [[Armor_Proficiency_(Light)]])
- Light Armor does not affect a character's [[Speed]]

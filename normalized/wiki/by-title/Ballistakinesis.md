# Ballistakinesis

*Reference Book: [[Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

You use [[The_Force]] to spray an area with dangerous debris.

**[*[[Telekinetic]]*]**

**Time:** [[Standard_Action]]

**Targets:** All targets within a 2x2 square area (the entire area must be within 12 squares of you and in your [[Line_of_Sight]]).

**Make a [[Use_the_Force]] check.** Compare the result to the [[Reflex_Defense]] of each target in the affected area. If the check result equals or exceeds a target's [[Reflex_Defense]], the target takes 3d8 points of Bludgeoning, Piercing, or Slashing damage (your choice) and takes a -2 penalty on all attack rolls until the start of your next turn. This is an [[Area_Attack]].

**Special:** You may spend a [[Force_Point]] when you activate this Force Power, in which case targets whose [[Reflex_Defense]] you fail to exceed with your [[Use_the_Force]] check take half damage.

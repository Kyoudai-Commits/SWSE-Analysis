# Medium Armor

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

Considered the standard other [[Armor]] compare themselves to, Medium Armor is typically used by military troopers, and various militia. All Medium Armor shares the following characteristics unless otherwise specified:

- Medium Armor inflicts a -5 [[Armor_Check_Penalty]] (negated by [[Armor_Proficiency_(Medium)]])
- If a character has a base [[Speed]] of 6 Squares, Medium Armor reduces it to 4 Squares
- If a character has a base [[Speed]] of 4 Squares, Medium Armor reduces it to 3 Squares

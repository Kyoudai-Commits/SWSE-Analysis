# Keen Scent

*Reference Book: [[Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

You can smell trouble coming from a much greater distance.

**Prerequisite:** [[Ewok]]

**Effect:** The range of your [[Scent]] ability increases to 20 squares.

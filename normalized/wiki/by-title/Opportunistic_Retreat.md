# Opportunistic Retreat

*Reference Book: [[Scum_and_Villainy]]*

When your opponent lets down his guard, you can better position yourself to press your advantage.

**Prerequisite:** [[Combat_Reflexes]]

**Effect:** Whenever an opponent provokes an [[Attack_of_Opportunity]] from you, once per turn you can sacrifice the attack to [[Move]] a number of squares equal to one-half your [[Speed]]. Moving in this way does not provoke [[Attacks_of_Opportunity]].

# Prime Shot

*Reference Book: [[Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

You know how to handle an enemy when none of your allies is closer.

**Prerequisite:** [[Point-Blank_Shot]]

**Effect:** If none of your allies are closer to your target than you are when you make a [[Ranged_Attack]], you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to the attack roll. Your target must be at Short [[Range]] or closer to gain this bonus.

# Shake It Off

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

You have learned to shake off debilitating conditions.

**Prerequisites:** [[Constitution]] 13, [[Trained]] in [[Endurance]]

**Effect:** You can spend two [[Swift_Actions]] instead of three to move +1 step along the [[Condition_Track]].

**Normal:** It takes three [[Swift_Actions]] to move +1 step up along the [[Condition_Track]].

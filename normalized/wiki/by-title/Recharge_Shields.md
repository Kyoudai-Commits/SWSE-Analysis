# Recharge Shields

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

By spending 3 [[Swift_Actions]] during the same turn, or on consecutive turns, a [[System_Operator]] can make a DC 20 [[Mechanics]] check to increase their Vehicle's current [[Shield_Rating]] by 5, up to its normal maximum.

# Burst of Speed

*Reference Book: [[Scum_and_Villainy]]*

You are capable of incredible bursts of speed.

**Prerequisite:** [[Trained]] in [[Endurance]]

**Effect:** As a [[Move_Action]], you can [[Move]] up to twice your [[Speed]]. At the end of your movement, you move -1 step along the [[Condition_Track]].

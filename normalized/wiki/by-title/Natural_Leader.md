# Natural Leader

*Reference Book: [[Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide]]*

You are a natural leader, and you founded your own [[Organization]].

**Prerequisite:** [[Charisma]] 13

**Effect:** You become the leader of an [[Organization]] of your design. The [[Organization]] has a scale equal to one-half your Heroic Level plus your [[Charisma]] bonus and continues to grow in scale as you gain levels. You automatically begin with a +10 bonus to your [[Organization_Score]] for your new [[Organization]].

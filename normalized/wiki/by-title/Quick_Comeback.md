# Quick Comeback

*Reference Book: [[Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

When you take damage that sends you reeling, you can recover quickly and get back in the fight.

**Prerequisite:** [[Gamorrean]]

**Effect:** Whenever you are moved down the [[Condition_Track]] by an attack that deals damage that equals or exceeds your [[Damage_Threshold]], until the end of your next turn you can move +1 step on the [[Condition_Track]] as a single [[Swift_Action]]. You can use this feat only once per attack that moves you down the [[Condition_Track]].

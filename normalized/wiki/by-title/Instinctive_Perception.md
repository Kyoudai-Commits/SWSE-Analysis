# Instinctive Perception

*Reference Book: [[Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

You can sense the presence of danger naturally, giving you and edge even when you fail to spot trouble the first time.

**Prerequisite:** [[Zabrak]]

**Effect:** Whenever you reroll a [[Perception]] check and take the second result, if your second result is lower than your first result, you gain a temporary [[Force_Point]]. This [[Force_Point]] can be spent only to add to the result of a [[Perception]] check, and if it is not spent before the end of the encounter, it is lost.

# Wary Sentries

*Reference Book: [[Galaxy_at_War]]*

You and your allies specialize in keeping tabs on your surroundings, making you excellent guards and advance scouts.

**Prerequisite:** [[Trained]] in [[Perception]]

**Effect:** You gain a +3 competence bonus on [[Perception]] checks. At the time you make a check, for each ally within 12 squares of you who also has this feat, the competence bonus increases by +1 (to a maximum of +7).

Additionally, you can [[Take_10]] on [[Perception]] checks, even when threatened or rushed.

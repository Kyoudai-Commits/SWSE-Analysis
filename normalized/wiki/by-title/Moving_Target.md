# Moving Target

*Reference Book: [[Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

You know how to stay mobile, making it harder for your enemies to hit you with their attacks.

**Prerequisite:** [[Dodge]]

**Effect:** If you end your turn at least 3 squares away from where you started, you gain a +1 dodge bonus to your [[Reflex_Defense]] until the start of your next turn.

# Overwhelming Attack

*Reference Book: [[Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide]]*

You know how to get your attacks past enemy defenses, making sure they strike true.

**Effect:** You must spend two [[Swift_Actions]] in the same round to activate this feat. If your target attempts to use a [[Talent]], [[Feat]], or other ability to negate your attack (such as **[[Block]]**, **[[Deflect]]**, or [[Vehicular_Combat]]), the target takes a -5 penalty to any attack rolls or [[Skill_Checks]] it makes to negate the attack. This effect applies to the next attack you make before the end of the same round.

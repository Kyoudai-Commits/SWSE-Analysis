# Angled Throw

*Reference Book: [[Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide]]*

Your amazing throws bounce [[Grenades]] off walls into open hatches or through congested battle zones.

**Prerequisite:** [[Dexterity]] 13

**Effect:** When throwing a [[Grenade]] or grenadelike weapon, you can attempt to bounce it off a wall or other surface close to your target. If you attack roll exceeds a [[Reflex_Defense]] of 15, you ignore [[Cover]] and [[Improved_Cover]] (but not [[Total_Cover]]) with your attack.

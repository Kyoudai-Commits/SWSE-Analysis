# Wookiee Grip

*Reference Book: [[Galaxy_of_Intrigue]]*

Your strength is such that you need only one hand to use weapons that normally require two hands.

**Prerequisite:** [[Strength]] 13

**Effect:** When using a weapon in which you are proficient and that normally requires two hands, you can wield that weapon with a single hand. You take a -2 penalty to attack rolls with the weapon when it is used in this manner.

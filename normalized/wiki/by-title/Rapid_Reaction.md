# Rapid Reaction

*Reference Book: [[Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

Your lightning reflexes allow you to complete complex actions in response to the actions of others.

**Effect:** Once per encounter, you can use two different [[Reactions]] in response to the same trigger. For example, if you use the **[[Deflect]]** Talent and fail to negate the attack, you can also use *[[Negate_Energy]]* as a response to the same attack.

**Normal:** Without this feat, you can use only one [[Reaction]] in response to a particular trigger.

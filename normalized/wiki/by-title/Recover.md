# Recover

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

You can spend three [[Swift_Actions]] in the same round or across consecutive rounds to move +1 step on the [[Condition_Track]]. You cannot use the Recover Action while afflicted by a [[Persistent_Condition]].

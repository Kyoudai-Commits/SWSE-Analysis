# Tactical Advantage

*Reference Book: [[Scum_and_Villainy]]*

You can shift your position to take advantage of openings your opponent offers you.

**Prerequisite:** [[Combat_Reflexes]]

**Effect:** When you successfully damage an opponent using an [[Attack_of_Opportunity]], you can immediately move 1 square in any direction. This movement does not provoke [[Attacks_of_Opportunity]].

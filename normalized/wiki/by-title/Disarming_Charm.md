# Disarming Charm

*Reference Book: [[Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

Like many among your Species, you know how to blend in with galactic society, using your natural gregarious disposition to keep others off guard.

**Prerequisite:** [[Sullustan]]

**Effect:** Whenever you successfully use the [[Change_Attitude]] application of the [[Persuasion]] skill to change a target's [[Attitude]], you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to all [[Deception]] or [[Persuasion]] checks against that target for the next 24 hours.

# Saber Swarm

*Reference Book: [[Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual]]*

**[*[[Lightsaber_Form]]*]**

You slash at your opponent rapidly, aiming many short strikes at the target's body.

**Time:** [[Standard_Action]]

**Targets:** One enemy within [[Reach]].

**Make a [[Use_the_Force]] check.** The result of the check determines the effect, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 20 | You can make two [[Lightsaber]] melee attacks against a single target. Each of these attacks is made at a -5 penalty. |
| 25 | You can make two [[Lightsaber]] melee attacks against a single target. Each of these attacks is made at a -2 penalty. |
| 30 | You can make two [[Lightsaber]] melee attacks against a single target. Each of these attacks is made at a -1 penalty. |
| 35 | You can make two [[Lightsaber]] melee attacks against a single target. You suffer no additional penalties for these attacks. |

**Lightsaber Form ([[Ataru]]):** If you have the **[[Ataru]]** Talent, your opponent takes a -5 penalty on all [[Use_the_Force]] checks made to block your attacks with the **[[Block]]** Talent or the *[[Makashi_Riposte]]* Form Power.

**Special:** You can spend a [[Force_Point]] to [[Move]] up to 2 squares before or after you use this Form Power. This movement does not provoke [[Attacks_of_Opportunity]].

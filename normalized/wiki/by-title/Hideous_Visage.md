# Hideous Visage

*Reference Book: [[Scum_and_Villainy]]*

You can twist your features into a startling mask.

**Prerequisite:** [[Shapeshift]]

**Effect:** Once per encounter, as a [[Swift_Action]], you can make a [[Deception]] check against one opponent that can see you. If the check equals or exceeds the opponent's [[Will_Defense]], you move the target 1 square away from you and it takes a -1 penalty on all attacks until the start of your next turn. This is a Mind-Affecting Fear effect.

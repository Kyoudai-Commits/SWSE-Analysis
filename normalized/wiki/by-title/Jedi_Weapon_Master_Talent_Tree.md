# Jedi Weapon Master Talent Tree

*Reference Book: [[Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual]]*

You have learned to use [[The_Force]] to master a variety of weapons, and have expanded your martial prowess to cover more than just [[Lightsabers]].

## Core Talents

### Combat Trance

*Prerequisite:* *[[Battle_Strike]]*

Whenever you use the *[[Battle_Strike]]* Force Power, you gain the Force Power's bonus on attack rolls on your first [[Melee_Attack]] made each round until the end of the encounter. If you do not attack in a round, this effect ends.

### Improvised Weapon Expertise

|  |
| --- |
| ***This section was titled Improvised Weapon Mastery in its official source, but has been renamed to distinguish it from the similarly named "[[Improvised_Weapon_Mastery]]."*** |

You take no penalty on attack rolls made with [[Improvised_Weapons]].

### Twin Weapon Style

As a [[Standard_Action]], whenever you are wielding two weapons (or a [[Double_Weapon]]), you can make one attack with each weapon (or each end of a [[Double_Weapon]]). Each attack must be against a different target.

### Twin Weapon Mastery

*Prerequisite:* **Twin Weapon Style**

Whenever you use the **Twin Weapon Style** Talent, you can [[Move]] 2 squares between each attack. This movement does not provoke [[Attacks_of_Opportunity]].

### Shoto Pin

*Prerequisite:* **[[Block]]**

Whenever you are wielding a [[Light]] Lightsaber (typically a [[Shoto_Lightsaber]]) and successfully use the **[[Block]]** Talent to negate a [[Melee_Attack]], the attacker can make no further [[Melee_Attacks]] until the start of its next turn or until you are no longer adjacent to it.

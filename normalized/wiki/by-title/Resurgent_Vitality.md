# Resurgent Vitality

*Reference Book: [[Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

Though your Species heals quickly over time, you can push through your pain in the short term and return to fighting form.

**Prerequisite:** [[Wookiee]]

**Effect:** Whenever you catch a [[Second_Wind]], you regain an additional number of [[Hit_Points]] equal to twice your [[Constitution]] bonus (minimum +2).

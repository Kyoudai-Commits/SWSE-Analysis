# Unified Squadron

*Reference Book: [[Galaxy_at_War]]*

You and your allies are excellent pilots whose skills shine when you fly together, making you better as a unit than as individuals.

**Prerequisite:** [[Trained]] in [[Pilot]]

**Effect:** You gain a +3 competence bonus on [[Pilot]] checks. At the time you make a check, for each ally within 12 squares of you who also has this feat, the competence bonus increases by +1 (to a maximum of +7).

Additionally, you always succeed when trying to [[Avoid_Collision]] with [[Vehicles]] piloted by allies who also have this feat (no [[Collisions]] occur, even on [[Character_Scale]]).

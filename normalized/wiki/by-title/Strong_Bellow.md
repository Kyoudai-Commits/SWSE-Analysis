# Strong Bellow

*Reference Book: [[Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

You can use your lungs to [[Bellow]] without growing fatigued.

**Prerequisite:** [[Ithorian]]

**Effect:** Once per encounter when you use your [[Bellow]] ability, you move one less step down the [[Condition_Track]].

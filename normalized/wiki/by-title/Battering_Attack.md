# Battering Attack

*Reference Book: [[Galaxy_at_War]]*

You know how to use your strength to batter an opponent to the ground, knocking him or her [[Prone]].

**Prerequisites:** [[Bantha_Rush]], [[Trip]]

**Effect:** Whenever you successfully move a creature with the [[Bantha_Rush]] feat, you also knock that target [[Prone]].

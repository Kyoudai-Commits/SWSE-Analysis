# Unstoppable Force

*Reference Book: [[Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide]]*

Your constant exposure to Force-users has taught you methods of physically resisting their effect on you in battle.

**Effect:** You gain a +5 insight bonus to [[Fortitude_Defense]] and [[Will_Defense]] against any attack or effect requiring a [[Use_the_Force]] check.

# Withdrawal Strike

*Reference Book: [[Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide]]*

Your enemies do not escape easily.

**Prerequisite:** [[Base_Attack_Bonus]] +5

**Effect:** When taking this feat, select a single [[Exotic_Weapon_(Melee)]] or melee [[Weapon_Group]] with which you are proficient. When wielding that chosen [[Exotic_Weapon_(Melee)]] or melee weapon from the chosen [[Weapon_Group]], adjacent opponents may not [[Withdraw]] from squares you [[Threaten]]. They may still make [[Acrobatics]] checks to [[Tumble]] as normal.

# Damage Conversion

*Reference Book: [[Scavenger's_Guide_to_Droids]]*

You can make last-minute moves to take hits on more durable portions of your chassis and/or armor, altering the damage dealt to you.

**Prerequisites:** [[Droid]], [[Dexterity]] 13

**Effect:** When you take damage from an attack against you (other than an [[Area_Attack]], or an attack that deals [[Ion]] or [[Force]] damage) and that damage equals or exceeds your [[Damage_Threshold]], you can take an additional 10 points of damage instead of moving down the [[Condition_Track]]. Each subsequent time you use this feat in a single encounter, the additional damage you take increases by 5 points.

# Force Readiness

*Reference Book: [[Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide]]*

[[The_Force]] is always with you, and you have a knack for succeeding even when the odds are against you.

**Effect:** You can spend [[Force_Points]] as a [[Free_Action]], even if it is not your turn. All other restrictions to [[Force_Point]] use still apply.

**Normal:** Unless otherwise specified, you can spend [[Force_Points]] only on your turn, preventing you from enhancing attacks and [[Skill_Checks]] made as [[Reactions]].

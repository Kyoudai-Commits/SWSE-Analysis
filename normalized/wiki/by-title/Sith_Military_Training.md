# Sith Military Training

*Reference Book: [[Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide]]*

You are trained in Sith military fighting techniques, giving you an advantage in melee combat. This training is generally given to rank and file military members of [[The_Sith_Empire]] regardless of their Force training.

**Effect:** Once per encounter, as a [[Reaction]] when you reduce a target to 0 [[Hit_Points]] or deal damage that exceeds the target's [[Damage_Threshold]], you can activate this feat to cause all enemies within 6 squares of that target to take a -2 penalty to all [[Defenses]] until the end of your next turn. This is a Mind-Affecting effect.

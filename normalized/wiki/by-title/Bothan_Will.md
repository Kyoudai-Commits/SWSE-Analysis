# Bothan Will

*Reference Book: [[Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

You epitomize the legendary willpower and resolve of the [[Bothan]] people.

**Prerequisite:** [[Bothan]]

**Effect:** Whenever an attack or [[Skill_Check]] targets your [[Will_Defense]] and fails to equal or exceed it, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to your [[Will_Defense]] until the start of your next turn.

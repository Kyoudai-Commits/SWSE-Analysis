# Thick Skin

*Reference Book: [[Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

Your thick hide protects you from damage and fends off toxins and other hazards.

**Prerequisite:** [[Trandoshan]]

**Effect:** You gain a +2 [[Species]] bonus to your [[Fortitude_Defense]].

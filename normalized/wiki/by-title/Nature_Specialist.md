# Nature Specialist

*Reference Book: [[Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

You know even the most obscure information about the natural world.

**Prerequisite:** [[Ithorian]]

**Effect:** Whenever you spend a [[Force_Point]] to add to a [[Knowledge_(Life_Sciences)]] check, increase the die type by two steps (from d6 to d10, or from d8 to d12).

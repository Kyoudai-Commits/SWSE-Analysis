# Improved Opportunistic Trickery

*Reference Book: [[Galaxy_of_Intrigue]]*

When your opponent lets down its guard, you open it up to additional attacks.

**Prerequisites:** [[Combat_Reflexes]], [[Opportunistic_Trickery]]

**Effect:** Whenever an opponent provokes an [[Attack_of_Opportunity]] from you, once per turn you can sacrifice the attack to reduce the target's [[Reflex_Defense]] by 5 until the end of the target's next turn.

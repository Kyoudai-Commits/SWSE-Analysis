# 2nd-Degree Droids

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]], [[Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide]]*

Mechanical and Technical 2nd-Degree [[Droids]] have the following traits:

- **Ability Modifiers:** All 2nd-Degree Droids receive a +2 bonus to their [[Intelligence]], but suffer a -2 penalty to their [[Charisma]].
- **Behavioral Inhibitors:** 2nd-Degree Droids firstly cannot knowingly harm a sentient creature, unless another creature would be harmed due to inaction. 2nd-Degree Droids secondly obey orders from their designated owner (whether that be an actual owner or themselves).
- **Droid Traits:** All 2nd-Degree Droids posses [[Droid_Traits]].
- **Droid Talents:** All 2nd-Degree Droids can select Droid Talents from the [[2nd-Degree_Droid_Talent_Tree]].

# Anointed Hunter

*Reference Book: [[Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide]]*

You have been anointed as a hunter for your tribe and possess skills few other [[Nelvaanians]] do.

**Prerequisite:** [[Nelvaanian]]

**Effect:** If you end your [[Move]] at least 2 squares from where you started, you gain a +1 competence bonus on attacks made with [[Thrown_Weapons]] until the end of your turn.

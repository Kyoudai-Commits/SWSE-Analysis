# 5th-Degree Droids

*Reference Books: [[Core_Rulebook]], [[Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide]]*

Labor and Utility 5th-Degree [[Droids]] have the following traits:

- **Ability Modifiers:** All 5th-Degree Droids receive a +4 bonus to their [[Strength]], but suffer -4 penalties to both their [[Intelligence]] and [[Charisma]].
- **Behavioral Inhibitors:** 5th-Degree Droids firstly cannot knowingly harm a sentient creature, unless another creature would be harmed due to inaction. 5th-Degree Droids secondly obey orders from their designated owner (whether that be an actual owner or themselves).
- **Droid Traits:** All 5th-Degree Droids posses [[Droid_Traits]].
- **Droid Talents:** All 5th-Degree Droids can select Droid Talents from the [[5th-Degree_Droid_Talent_Tree]].

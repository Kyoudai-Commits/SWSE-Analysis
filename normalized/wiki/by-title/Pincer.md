# Pincer

*Reference Book: [[Scavenger's_Guide_to_Droids]]*

You use your mechanical strength to restrain and damage enemies.

**Prerequisites:** [[Droid]], [[Claw]] or [[Hand]] Appendage, [[Base_Attack_Bonus]] +1, [[Pin]], [[Crush]]

**Effect:** When you successfully [[Pin]] an enemy, you can maintain the [[Pin]] beyond 1 round and can make subsequent [[Grapple]] checks against the [[Pinned]] enemy as a [[Swift_Action]]. You can apply the [[Crush]] feat whenever your subsequent checks are successful.

**Normal:** [[Grapple]] checks are a [[Standard_Action]]. The [[Pin]] feat must be rerolled each round as a new [[Grapple]] check.

**Special:** You must have a [[Claw]] or [[Hand]] Appendage to use this Feat.

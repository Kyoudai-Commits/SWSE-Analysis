# Veteran Spacer

*Reference Book: [[Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

Your people are among the oldest explorers to chart the stars, and you benefit from eons of collected knowledge about space travel.

**Prerequisite:** [[Duros]]

**Effect:** You gain a +5 [[Species]] bonus to [[Use_Computer]] checks made to [[Astrogate]] aboard a [[Starship]].

# Hasty Modification

*Reference Book: [[Scum_and_Villainy]]*

You can tweak and adapt equipment on the fly.

**Prerequisite:** [[Tech_Specialist]]

**Effect:** You can exchange a Trait you have applied to a piece of [[Equipment]] or [[Droid]] for another one by spending 1 minute to make a DC 20 [[Mechanics]] check. If you succeed, the [[Equipment]] loses the old Trait and gains a new Trait until the end of the encounter, at which point the device loses all Traits previously acquired through use of the [[Tech_Specialist]] feat.

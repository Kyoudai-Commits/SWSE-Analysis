# Wheeled

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

**Base Speed:** Small (6 squares); Medium (8 squares); Large or bigger (10 squares)

**Cost:** 5 x [[Cost_Factor]] x (Base Speed Squared)

Wheeled Droids use one or more powered wheels to move and are generally designed to traverse smooth surfaces. Wheeled Droids can't use the [[Climb]] skill, and the penalties of moving through [[Difficult_Terrain]] are doubled.

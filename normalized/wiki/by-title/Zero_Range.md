# Zero Range

*Reference Book: [[Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

You are lethal when using ranged weapons against adjacent targets.

**Prerequisite:** [[Point-Blank_Shot]]

**Effect:** When firing a ranged weapon at a target within or adjacent to your [[Fighting_Space]], you gain a +1 bonus on the attack roll and deal +1 die of damage on a hit.

**Special:** The effects of this feat do not stack with the extra damage provided by the [[Burst_Fire]] or [[Rapid_Shot]] feats. This feat does not apply to [[Heavy_Weapons]] or [[Vehicle_Weapons]], or in [[Vehicle_Combat]].

# Momentum Strike

*Reference Book: [[Threats_of_the_Galaxy]]*

You know how to put the full weight of your momentum into attacks while riding a mount or a [[Speeder_Bike]].

**Prerequisite:** [[Trained]] in [[Pilot]] or [[Ride]]

**Effect:** When riding a [[Beast]] as a mount or on a [[Speeder_Bike]] (as a passenger or a [[Vehicle_Pilot]]), you add +1 die of damage to any [[Melee_Attacks]] if your [[Beast]] or [[Vehicle]] has already moved at least its [[Speed]] this turn.

# Category : Rage

Once per day, a [Species] can fly into a Rage as a [[Swift_Action]]. While Raging, the [Species] temporarily gains a +2 Rage bonus on melee attack rolls and melee damage rolls, but cannot use [[Skills]] that require patience and concentration, such as [[Mechanics]], [[Stealth]], or [[Use_the_Force]]. A fit of Rage lasts for a number of rounds equal to 5 + the [Species]'s [[Constitution]] modifier.

- At the end of its Rage, the [Species] moves -1 [[Persistent]] step along the [[Condition_Track]]. The penalties imposed by this [[Persistent_Condition]] persist until the [Species] takes at least 10 minutes to recuperate, during which time the [Species] can't engage in any strenuous activity.

# Razor Bug

*Reference Book: [[Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

Razor Bugs are small, disc-shaped insects engineered by the [[Yuuzhan_Vong]] to serve as ranged weapons. Though they are thrown by hand, Razor Bugs are shaped in such a way that they fly with great speed, distance, and accuracy, making them an effective replacement for longer-ranged weapons. A thrown Razor Bug is treated as a [[Simple_Weapon_(Ranged)]], not a [[Thrown_Weapon]], for purposes of determining [[Range]].

**Weapon Type:** [[Simple_Weapons_(Ranged)]]

**Size:** Small

**Cost:** 800

**Damage:** 2d8

**Rate of Fire:** Single-Shot

**Weight:** 0.5 Kilograms

**Type:** Slashing

**Availability:** [[Illegal]], [[Rare]]

**Accurate:** This weapon takes no penalty when firing at targets at Short [[Range]].

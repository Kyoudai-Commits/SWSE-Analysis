# Blockade Runner Talent Tree

*Reference Book: [[Scum_and_Villainy]]*

One of the most valued skills a pilot in the Fringe can have is the ability to blast past the authorities and not get caught. This Talent Tree covers abilities common to smugglers and blockade runners throughout the galaxy.

## Core Talents

### Close Cover

*Prerequisite:* **Watch This**

If you occupy the same space as a [[Vehicle]] that is larger than the [[Vehicle]] you are [[Piloting]], your [[Vehicle]] gains a +5 [[Cover]] bonus from the larger [[Vehicle]].

### Outrun

Whenever you use the [[All-Out_Movement]] Action as the [[Vehicle_Pilot]] of a [[Vehicle]], your [[Vehicle]] gains a +2 dodge bonus to its [[Reflex_Defense]].

### Punch Through

If you are the [[Vehicle_Pilot]] of a [[Vehicle]], smaller [[Vehicles]] that attempt to engage you in a [[Dogfight]] take a -10 penalty on their [[Pilot]] checks, instead of the normal -5.

### Small Target

When you are the [[Vehicle_Pilot]] of a Colossal or smaller [[Vehicle]], [[Capital_Ship]] weapons that take a -20 penalty on attack rolls against your [[Vehicle]] (such as [[Turbolasers]]) do not automatically score a [[Critical_Hit]] on your [[Vehicle]] on a Natural 20. The attack is only a [[Critical_Hit]] if the total attack roll (20 + the weapon's attack bonus) would normally hit your [[Vehicle]]. Otherwise, the attack deals normal damage.

### Watch This

You can move into or through a space occupied by a [[Vehicle]] of Colossal (Frigate) size or larger without causing a [[Collision]]. Additionally, if you are the [[Vehicle_Pilot]] of a Colossal or smaller [[Vehicle]], you can occupy the same space as a [[Vehicle]] of Colossal (Frigate) size or larger.

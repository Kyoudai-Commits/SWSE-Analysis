# Trench Warrior

*Reference Book: [[Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide]]*

You have experience fighting in the trenches, and you know how to use [[Cover]] to your attack advantage.

**Effect:** When you are adjacent to a wall or object that provides you with [[Cover]] from your target's [[Ranged_Attacks]], you gain a +1 circumstance bonus on your attack rolls against that target.

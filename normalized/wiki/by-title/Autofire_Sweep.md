# Autofire Sweep

*Reference Book: [[Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

Your can sweep an area with your [[Autofire]] weapon, spraying fire in a 180-degree arc in front of you.

**Prerequisite:** [[Weapon_Focus]] (Chosen Weapon)

**Effect:** When making an [[Autofire]] attack with a weapon with which you possess the [[Weapon_Focus]] feat, you can attack all targets in a 6-Square [[Cone]]. The origin square of the cone can be any square in your [[Line_of_Sight]] and within Point-Blank [[Range]].

**Special:** This feat cannot be used with the [[Autofire_Barrage]] feat or the [[Burst_Fire]] feat. It can be used with the **[[Improved_Suppression_Fire]]** Talent.

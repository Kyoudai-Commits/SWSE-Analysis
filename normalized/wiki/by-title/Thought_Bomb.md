# Thought Bomb

*Reference Book: [[Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual]]*

You use [[The_Force]] to radiate out harmful waves of telepathy, damaging the minds of nearby foes.

**[*[[Mind-Affecting]]*]**

**Time:** [[Standard_Action]]

**Targets:** All opponents within 2 squares of you.

**Make a [[Use_the_Force]] check.** Compare the result of the [[Use_the_Force]] check to each target's [[Will_Defense]]. If the result equals or exceeds the target's [[Will_Defense]], the target takes 2d6 points of [[Force]] damage, and loses its [[Swift_Action]] on its next turn. This is an area effect.

**Special:** You can spend a [[Force_Point]] to increase the damage dealt by this [[Force_Power]] by an additional +2d6 points of [[Force]] damage.

# Imperial Military Training

*Reference Book: [[Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

You have been instilled with the sense of discipline and loyalty demanded by the Empire.

**Effect:** Once per encounter, as a [[Free_Action]] on your turn, you can negate any one Mind-Affecting effect targeting or currently affecting you.

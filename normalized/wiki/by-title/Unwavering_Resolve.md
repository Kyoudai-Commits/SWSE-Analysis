# Unwavering Resolve

*Reference Book: [[Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide]]*

You are not easily swayed by the words of others, and can see through deceptions thanks to your keen mind.

**Prerequisite:** [[Trained]] in [[Perception]]

**Effect:** You gain a +5 insight bonus to [[Will_Defense]] against [[Deception]] and [[Persuasion]] checks.

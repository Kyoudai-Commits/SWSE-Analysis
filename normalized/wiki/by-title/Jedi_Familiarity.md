# Jedi Familiarity

*Reference Book: [[Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide]]*

You have worked alongside Jedi or other Force-users and have developed an excellent rapport.

**Effect:** Once per encounter, when you are targeted or affected by a [[Force_Power]] or [[Force_Talent]] originating from an ally, you gain one temporary [[Force_Point]], which must be spent before the end of the encounter or it goes away. If the [[Force_Power]] or [[Force_Talent]] damages you or moves you down the [[Condition_Track]], you do not gain the benefit of this Feat.

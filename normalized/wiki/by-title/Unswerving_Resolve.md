# Unswerving Resolve

*Reference Book: [[Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual]]*

You stand your ground in the face of terrifying or domineering opposition.

**Prerequisite:** [[Base_Attack_Bonus]] +2

**Effect:** Whenever you are targeted by a Fear effect or Mind-Affecting effect that fails to affect you, you gain a temporary [[Force_Point]]. If this [[Force_Point]] is not used before the end of your next turn, it goes away. If you negate the contingent effect in any way, you do not gain the benefit of this feat.

# Ion Shielding

*Reference Book: [[Scavenger's_Guide_to_Droids]]*

Your systems are insulated to improve your resistance to [[Ion]] damage.

**Prerequisites:** ([[Droid]] and [[Strength]] 13) or ([[Cyborg_Hybrid]] and [[Constitution]] 13)

**Effect:** If the [[Ion]] damage (before being halved) equals or exceeds your [[Damage_Threshold]], you move only -1 step on the [[Condition_Track]].

**Normal:** If the [[Ion]] damage (before being halved) equals or exceeds your [[Damage_Threshold]], you normally move -2 steps on the [[Condition_Track]].

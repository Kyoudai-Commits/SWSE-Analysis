# Resolute Stance

*Reference Book: [[Galaxy_of_Intrigue]]*

By standing your ground, you focus your mind to repel any assault.

**Prerequisite:** [[Base_Attack_Bonus]] +1

**Effect:** When you [[Fight_Defensively]], you gain a +2 morale bonus to your [[Will_Defense]]. If you make no attacks until your next turn, you gain a +5 morale bonus to your [[Will_Defense]] until the start of your next turn.

**Normal:** When you [[Fight_Defensively]] you only get a dodge bonus to your [[Reflex_Defense]].

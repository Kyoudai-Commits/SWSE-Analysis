# Tech Specialist

*Reference Books: [[Starships_of_the_Galaxy]], [[Scum_and_Villainy]]*

You can make custom modifications to [[Armor]], [[Weapons]], [[Vehicles]], [[Droids]], and devices.

**Prerequisite:** [[Trained]] in [[Mechanics]]

**Effect:** You can modify a device, suit of [[Armor]], [[Weapon]], [[Droid]], or [[Vehicle]] so that it gains a special trait. Specific traits are given on the table below. You may perform only one modification at a time. Unless noted otherwise, you cannot grant more than one benefit to a single device, suit of [[Armor]], [[Weapon]], [[Droid]], or [[Vehicle]], and you cannot apply the same benefit more than once.

Before beginning the modification, you must pay one-tenth the cost of the item you wish to modify or 1,000 credits, whichever is more. Completing the modification requires 1 day per 1,000 credits of the modification's cost. At the end of this time, make a DC 20 [[Mechanics]] check; you can't [[Take_10]] or [[Take_20]] on this check. If the check succeeds, the modification is completed successfully, and the object gains the desired trait. If the check fails, you lose all credits spent making the modification, and the object doesn't gain the desired trait. However, you may start over if you wish.

Other characters [[Trained]] in the [[Mechanics]] skill can assist you, reducing proportionately the time needed to complete the modification (for example, 3 people working together on a 1,000 credit modification would take 1/3 of one day). At the end of the modification process, they can make a [[Mechanics]] check to aid your check. The market value of a modified item is equal to the base cost of the item + double the cost of the modifications made to it (not including credits wasted on failed modification attempts).

The bonuses provided by the Tech Specialist feat and the [[Superior_Tech]] feat always stack with those provided by [[Equipment_Upgrades]]; however, upgrades and other gear still follow the normal rules for stacking with one another. Additional clarifications are outlined below.

- **Device Traits:** Vehicles, weapons, and armor are also devices, so they are eligible for all of these modifications. ([[Droids]] are characters, not devices.) In addition to the listed modifications, it is possible to install an additional [[Upgrade_Slot]] on any device using the Tech Specialist feat. This modification can be made only once.
- **Droid Traits:** Any equipment with the [[Droidification]] upgrade is technically a [[Droid]], so it is eligible for these modifications.
- **Armor Traits:** If a device or [[Vehicle]] has an Armor bonus to its [[Reflex_Defense]] (either normally or because of an upgrade), it can benefit from the Protective Armor modification.
- **Weapon Traits:** If an [[Autofire]]-only weapon has the Selective Fire modification, it is still treated as an [[Autofire]]-only weapon for purposes of [[Bracing]]. An [[Autofire]]-only weapon with a [[Sniper_Switch]] does not gain this benefit.

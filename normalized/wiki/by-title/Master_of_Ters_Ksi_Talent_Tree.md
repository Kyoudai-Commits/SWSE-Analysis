# Master of Teräs Käsi Talent Tree

*Reference Book: [[Threats_of_the_Galaxy]]*

Teräs Käsi is a powerful and deadly martial arts form that enables its wielder to deal devastating amounts of [[Unarmed]] damage.

## Core Talents

### Teräs Käsi Basics

*Prerequisite:* [[Martial_Arts_I]]

You deal an additional die of damage with your [[Unarmed_Attacks]].

### Ignore Damage Reduction

*Prerequisites:* **Teräs Käsi Basics**, [[Martial_Arts_I]]

When you make an [[Unarmed_Attack]] against a target that has [[Damage_Reduction]], and you deal more damage than the target's DR, you ignore the target's DR completely.

### Teräs Käsi Mastery

*Prerequisites:* **Teräs Käsi Basics**, [[Martial_Arts_I]], [[Martial_Arts_II]], [[Martial_Arts_III]]

If you make only [[Unarmed_Attacks]] during a [[Full_Attack]] Action, you can take the [[Full_Attack]] Action as a [[Standard_Action]], instead of a [[Full-Round_Action]].

Jedi CounselingJedi Counseling

*Reference Book: [[Web_Enhancements]] ([[Jedi_Counseling]])*

**Q:** Can a character who has the **Teräs Käsi Mastery** Talent and the [[Double_Attack]] Feat combine these with the [[Mighty_Swing]] Feat? Specifically, could I use two [[Swift_Actions]] to get the benefit of [[Mighty_Swing]] and then use my [[Standard_Action]] to make a [[Double_Attack]]? If so, would the extra die of damage from [[Mighty_Swing]] apply to both attacks from the [[Double_Attack]] or only to the first attack?

**A:** Yes, you can use **Teräs Käsi Mastery**, [[Double_Attack]], and [[Mighty_Swing]] at the same time, using two [[Swift_Actions]] for [[Mighty_Swing]] and then your [[Standard_Action]] for an unarmed [[Double_Attack]].

However, [[Mighty_Swing]] specifies that its extra damage applies only to your *next* attack in the same round, so the second attack from [[Double_Attack]] (or any other source of extra attacks) would not gain this benefit.

### Unarmed Counterstrike

*Prerequisites:* **Teräs Käsi Basics**, **Unarmed Parry**, [[Martial_Arts_I]], [[Martial_Arts_II]]

When you successfully parry a [[Melee_Attack]] with the **Unarmed Parry** Talent, you can immediately make an [[Unarmed_Attack]] as a [[Reaction]] against that target.

### Unarmed Parry

*Prerequisites:* **Teräs Käsi Basics**, [[Martial_Arts_I]], [[Martial_Arts_II]]

When you [[Fight_Defensively]], as a [[Reaction]] you can negate a [[Melee_Attack]] by making a successful [[Unarmed]] attack roll. If your attack roll equals or exceeds the attack roll of the incoming [[Melee_Attack]], the attack is negated. You must be aware of the attack, and not be [[Flat-Footed]], and you take a cumulative -2 penalty to all attack rolls for each attack roll made since the beginning of your last turn.

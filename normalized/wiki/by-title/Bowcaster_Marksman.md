# Bowcaster Marksman

*Reference Book: [[Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

You are an excellent shot with the [[Bowcaster]], and even your most difficult shots are solid hits.

**Prerequisite:** [[Wookiee]]

**Effect:** Whenever you spend a [[Force_Point]] to add to an attack with a [[Bowcaster]], if you hit the target, you gain a competence bonus to your damage roll equal to the amount added to the attack.

# Medpac

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

**Cost:** 100

**Weight:** 1 Kilogram

Medpacs are compact packages designed to both equip a trained medic for work in the field and allow untrained individuals to apply [[First_Aid]] in emergencies. A Medpac contains bandages, Bacta, synthetic flesh, coagulants, stimulants, and other medicines designed to help an injured patient recover quickly.

Once you use a Medpac, its contents are expended even if your [[Treat_Injury]] check is not successful. Any given creature can only benefit from the use of a Medpac once in a 24-hour period.

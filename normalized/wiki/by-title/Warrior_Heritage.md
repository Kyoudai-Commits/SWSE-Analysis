# Warrior Heritage

*Reference Book: [[Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

You come from a proud warrior culture, and your pride fills you with self-assurance.

**Prerequisite:** [[Gungan]]

**Effect:** You gain a +2 morale bonus to your [[Will_Defense]] when you wield an [[Atlatl]] or a [[Cesta]].

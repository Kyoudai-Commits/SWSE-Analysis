# Separatist Military Training

*Reference Book: [[Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide]]*

You have received extensive training from the military of [[The_Confederacy_of_Independent_Systems]] and are familiar with its practices.

**Effect:** While you are adjacent to at least one of your allies, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus on any one attack roll you make on your turn.

# Wary Defender

*Reference Book: [[Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide]]*

Even in the heat of battle you can bolster yourself against nearly anything that comes your way.

**Effect:** When you use the [[Fight_Defensively]] Action, you gain a +2 competence bonus to your [[Fortitude_Defense]] and [[Will_Defense]] until the beginning of your next turn.

# Recurring Success

*Reference Book: [[Galaxy_of_Intrigue]]*

You know how to make your enemies fall for the same trick twice.

**Effect:** When you select this feat, choose one [[Talent]] or [[Feat]] that can normally be used once per encounter. You can use that [[Talent]] or [[Feat]] one additional time per encounter.

**Special:** You can select this feat multiple times. Each time you do so, you must choose a different [[Talent]] or [[Feat]] to gain the benefits of this feat.

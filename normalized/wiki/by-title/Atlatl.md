# Atlatl

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

A [[Gungan]] weapon, the Atlatl adds speed and power as an extension of a throwing arm, allowing the user to hurl [[Energy_Balls]] farther than with just an unaided toss. If forced into close combat, the wielder can use the Atlatl as a club-like melee weapon. Although the Atlatl is considered an [[Exotic_Weapon]], [[Gungans]] are treated as proficient if they have the [[Weapon_Proficiency_(Simple_Weapons)]] feat.

**Weapon Type:** [[Exotic_Weapons_(Melee)]]

**Size:** Medium

**Cost:** 50

**Damage:** 2d4

**[[Stun]] Setting:** NO

**Weight:** 1.5 Kilograms

**Type:** Bludgeoning

**Availability:** [[Rare]]

**Special:** Can be used to hurl [[Energy_Balls]]

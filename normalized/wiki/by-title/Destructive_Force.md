# Destructive Force

*Reference Book: [[Galaxy_at_War]]*

You cause so much devastation to [[Vehicles]] and objects that you also deal damage to others around those targets.

**Effect:** When you deal damage to an object or [[Vehicle]] that equals or exceeds the target's [[Damage_Threshold]] and reduces the target to 0 [[Hit_Points]], you deal 1 die of damage (of the same type as the damage used in the attack) to all targets, including allies, that are adjacent to the [[Vehicle]] or object.

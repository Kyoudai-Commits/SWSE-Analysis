# Pinpoint Accuracy

*Reference Book: [[Scavenger's_Guide_to_Droids]]*

This improved version of the [[Aiming_Accuracy]] feat moves your enemy -1 step on the [[Condition_Track]].

**Prerequisite:** [[Droid]], [[Aiming_Accuracy]], [[Point-Blank_Shot]], [[Precise_Shot]]

**Effect:** When using the [[Aiming_Accuracy]] feat, a target you damage cannot take the [[Recover]] Action until the end of its next turn.

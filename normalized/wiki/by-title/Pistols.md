# Pistols

| DISTANCE | RANGE | PENALTY |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-20 Squares | Point-Blank | No penalty |
| 21-40 Squares | Short | -2 to attack rolls |
| 41-60 Squares | Medium | -5 to attack rolls |
| 61-80 Squares | Long | -10 to attack rolls |

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

Hand-held ranged weapons, favored for self-defense, and are easily concealed, Pistols are ranged weapons that can be fired with one hand. [[Weapon_Proficiency_(Pistols)]] is a Starting Feat for [[Nobles]], [[Scoundrels]], [[Scouts]], and [[Soldiers]].

A [[Rifle]] with a folded [[Retractable_Stock]] is also treated as a Pistol.

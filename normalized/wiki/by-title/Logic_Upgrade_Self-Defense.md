# Logic Upgrade: Self-Defense

*Reference Book: [[Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide]]*

You can use your acquired knowledge and experiences to increase your [[Defenses]].

**Prerequisite:** [[Droid]]

**Effect:** Once per encounter, as a [[Reaction]], you can grant yourself a +2 morale bonus to the [[Defense_Score]] of your choice until the end of your next turn.

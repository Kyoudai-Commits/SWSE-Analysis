# Vehicle Systems Expertise

*Reference Book: [[Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

You may use your technical expertise to manipulate a Vehicle's systems more quickly than usual.

**Prerequisites:** [[Trained]] in [[Mechanics]], [[Tech_Specialist]]

**Effect:** You may use the [[Recharge_Shields]] or [[Reroute_Power]] Actions by spending two [[Swift_Actions]]. Also, once per encounter, you may attempt to use either of these Actions as a single [[Swift_Action]] by making a DC 30 [[Mechanics]] check.

**Normal:** The [[Recharge_Shields]] and [[Reroute_Power]] Actions normally requires you to spend three [[Swift_Actions]].

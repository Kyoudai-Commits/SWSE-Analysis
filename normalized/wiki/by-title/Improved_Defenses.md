# Improved Defenses

*Reference Books: [[Core_Rulebook]], [[Starships_of_the_Galaxy]]*

You are skilled at fending off attacks of many forms.

**Effect:** You gain a +1 bonus to your [[Reflex_Defense]], [[Fortitude_Defense]], and [[Will_Defense]].

**Special:** This feat does not apply to the [[Defenses]] of a [[Vehicle]], even if you are [[Piloting]] or [[Commanding]] it. This is one reason many [[Pilots]] prefer the [[Dodge]] feat to Improved Defenses.

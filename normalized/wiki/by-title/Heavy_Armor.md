# Heavy Armor

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

Heavy Armor is the top of the line [[Armor]], primarily used by military specialists, and high-spec mercenaries. All Heavy Armor shares the following characteristics unless otherwise specified:

- Heavy Armor inflicts a -10 [[Armor_Check_Penalty]] (negated by [[Armor_Proficiency_(Heavy)]])
- If a character has a base [[Speed]] of 6 Squares, Heavy Armor reduces it to 4 Squares
- If a character has a base [[Speed]] of 4 Squares, Heavy Armor reduces it to 3 Squares
- When [[Running]] in Heavy Armor, you can only move up to three times your [[Speed]] (instead of four times)

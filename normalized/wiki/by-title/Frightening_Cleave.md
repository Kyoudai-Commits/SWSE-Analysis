# Frightening Cleave

*Reference Book: [[Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide]]*

Enemies that see you successfully use the [[Cleave]] feat become fearful.

**Prerequisites:** [[Strength]] 13, [[Base_Attack_Bonus]] +4, [[Cleave]], [[Power_Attack]]

**Effect:** Immediately after you use the [[Cleave]] feat, each enemy within 6 squares of you and within your [[Line_of_Sight]] takes a -1 penalty to its [[Reflex_Defense]], [[Skill_Checks]], and attack rolls against you until the end of the encounter. This penalty stacks with itself to a maximum of -5. This is a Mind-Affecting effect.

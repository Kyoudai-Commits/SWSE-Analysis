# Lightning Draw

*Reference Book: [[Scum_and_Villainy]]*

You can draw and fire in one smooth action.

**Prerequisite:** [[Quick_Draw]]

**Effect:** Once per encounter, you can draw a holstered weapon and attack as a single [[Standard_Action]].

**Normal:** With the [[Quick_Draw]] feat, drawing or holstering a weapon is a [[Swift_Action]].

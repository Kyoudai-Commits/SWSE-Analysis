# Coordinated Barrage

*Reference Book: [[Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide]]*

When you combine fire with an ally, your barrage can have devastating results on the attacker.

**Prerequisites:** [[Base_Attack_Bonus]] +5, [[Coordinated_Attack]]

**Effect:** When you [[Aid_Another]] ally's attack, for every 3 points that the ally's attack roll beats the target's [[Reflex_Defense]], the attack deals +1 die of damage. The maximum number of bonus damage dice the attack receives is equal to the number of allies with this feat that aided on the attack roll or +5 dice, whichever is lower.

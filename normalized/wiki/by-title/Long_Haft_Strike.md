# Long Haft Strike

*Reference Book: [[Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual]]*

You have learned to take advantage of oversized [[Lightsabers]], turning them into [[Double_Weapons]].

**Effect:** When you use a [[Lightsaber_Pike]] or a [[Long-Handle_Lightsaber]], you can attack with both ends of the weapon, treating it as a [[Double_Weapon]].

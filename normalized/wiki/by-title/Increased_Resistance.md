# Increased Resistance

*Reference Book: [[Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

You remain resilient against attacks that target your vitality, making it difficult for enemies to sap your strength.

**Prerequisite:** [[Gamorrean]]

**Effect:** Whenever an attack or [[Skill_Check]] targets your [[Fortitude_Defense]] and fails to equal or exceed it, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to your [[Fortitude_Defense]] until the start of your next turn.

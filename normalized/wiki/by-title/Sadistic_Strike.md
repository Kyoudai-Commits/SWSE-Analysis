# Sadistic Strike

*Reference Book: [[Galaxy_of_Intrigue]]*

By dispatching a [[Helpless]] creature, you terrify opponents around you.

**Effect:** When you deliver a [[Coup_de_Grace]] to a [[Helpless]] creature, all opponents within [[Line_of_Sight]] move -1 step on the [[Condition_Track]] until the end of encounter.

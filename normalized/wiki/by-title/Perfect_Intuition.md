# Perfect Intuition

*Reference Book: [[Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

Your insights serve you well, giving you some of the fastest reaction times among members of your Species.

**Prerequisite:** [[Cerean]]

**Effect:** Whenever you reroll an [[Initiative]] check, you always keep the better result, even if you have multiple reroll abilities.

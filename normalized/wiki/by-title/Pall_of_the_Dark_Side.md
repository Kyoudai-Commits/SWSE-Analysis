# Pall of the Dark Side

*Reference Book: [[Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide]]*

You are adept at hiding yourself from Force-users, thanks to the haze of [[The_Dark_Side]].

**Prerequisite:** [[Dark_Side_Score]] 1+

**Effect:** You can add one-half your [[Dark_Side_Score]] (minimum 1) to any [[Use_the_Force]] checks made to resist attempts to detect you with the [[Sense_Force]] application of the [[Use_the_Force]] skill.

# Matukai Adept Talent Tree

*Reference Book: [[Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual]]*

All Matukai strive to achieve harmony between the physical and the spiritual aspects of their lives. As a result, they find ways to use [[The_Force]] in improve their bodies. Only Force-users that are part of [[The_Matukai]] may select [[Talents]] from this tree.

## Core Talents

### Body Control

You can add your [[Charisma]] modifier instead of your [[Constitution]] modifier to your [[Fortitude_Defense]]. Additionally, you can spend a [[Force_Point]] as a [[Swift_Action]] to become immune to [[Poison]], [[Radiation]], and [[Disease]] until the end of the encounter.

### Physical Surge

Whenever you roll an [[Initiative]] check at the start of combat, you can spend a [[Swift_Action]] immediately, regardless of whether or not you are [[Surprised]].

### Soft to Solid

As a [[Reaction]] when you are damaged by an attack, you can spend a [[Force_Point]] to increase the rigidity of your skin, gaining [[Damage_Reduction]] 10 until the end of your next turn.

### Wan-Shen Defense

*Prerequisite:* [[Weapon_Proficiency_(Simple_Weapons)]]

As a [[Swift_Action]], you can use your [[Wan-Shen]] to parry your opponents' attacks, gaining a +1 deflection bonus to your [[Reflex_Defense]] against [[Melee_Attacks]] until the start of your next turn. You must have your [[Wan-Shen]] in hand to use this Talent, and you do not gain the deflection bonus if you are [[Flat-Footed]] or otherwise unaware of the incoming attack. You can take this Talent multiple times; each time you take this Talent, the deflection bonus increases by +1 (to a maximum of +3).

### Wan-Shen Kata

*Prerequisite:* [[Weapon_Proficiency_(Simple_Weapons)]]

You treat the [[Wan-Shen]] as a Medium weapon instead of a Large weapon. You can use the [[Pin]] and [[Trip]] feats with a [[Wan-Shen]], substituting your attack bonus with the [[Wan-Shen]] for your [[Grapple]] check. You must have your [[Wan-Shen]] in hand to use this Talent. In addition, when you use the [[Wan-Shen]] as a [[Double_Weapon]], you reduce all attack penalties for attacking with both ends of the weapon by 1.

### Wan-Shen Mastery

*Prerequisites:* [[Base_Attack_Bonus]] +5, **Wan-Shen Kata**, [[Weapon_Proficiency_(Simple_Weapons)]]

As a [[Standard_Action]], you make two attacks with your [[Wan-Shen]], each one against a different target within your [[Reach]]. You must have your [[Wan-Shen]] in hand to use this Talent.

# Close Combat Escape

*Reference Book: [[Scum_and_Villainy]]*

When you slip free from your opponent, you can deliver a parting attack.

**Prerequisite:** [[Trained]] in [[Acrobatics]]

**Effect:** When you successfully use [[Acrobatics]] to escape a [[Grapple]], you can spend a [[Swift_Action]] to make a single attack with a melee weapon or an [[Unarmed_Attack]] against the opponent that had [[Grappled]] you. If the attack hits, it deals normal damage, and your opponent is considered [[Flat-Footed]] until the start of its next turn.

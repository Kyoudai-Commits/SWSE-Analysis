# Deceptive Drop

*Reference Book: [[Scum_and_Villainy]]*

When you get the drop on opponents, you dazzle them with your speed and ferocity.

**Prerequisite:** [[Trained]] in [[Initiative]]

**Effect:** During the [[Surprise_Round]], if you damage a [[Flat-Footed]] target, that target is also knocked [[Prone]] if your attack roll also exceeds the target's [[Fortitude_Defense]]. The target adds its size modifier to its [[Fortitude_Defense]]:

- Medium and smaller, +0;
- Large, +5;
- Huge, +10;
- Gargantuan, +20;
- Colossal, +50.

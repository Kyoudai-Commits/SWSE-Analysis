# Hovering

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

**Base Speed:** 6 squares

**Cost:** 100 x [[Cost_Factor]] x (Base Speed Squared)

Hovering Droids use repulsorlift technology to float slowly above the ground (within 3 meters). They ignore the penalties of [[Difficult_Terrain]].

# Survivor of Ryloth

*Reference Book: [[Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

You know how to survive on both the night and day sides of your homeworld, allowing you and your allies to weather the harsh environments of the galaxy.

**Prerequisite:** [[Twi'lek]]

**Effect:** Once per hour when you and your allies are in an environment of [[Extreme_Heat_or_Cold]], you can make a [[Survival]] check. You and up to 10 allies can use the results of the [[Survival]] check in place of your [[Fortitude_Defense]] when the [[Extreme_Heat_or_Cold]] makes its hourly attack roll against your [[Fortitude_Defenses]].

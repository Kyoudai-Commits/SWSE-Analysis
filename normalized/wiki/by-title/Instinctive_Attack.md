# Instinctive Attack

*Reference Book: [[Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide]]*

Your accuracy improves when you act on instinct. Though you might call it luck, [[The_Force]] is with you.

**Prerequisite:** Cannot be a [[Droid]]

**Effect:** When you use a [[Force_Point]] when making an attack with a weapon with which you are proficient, you can reroll the attack and take the better result. Apply the results of the die from the [[Force_Point]] to the better result.

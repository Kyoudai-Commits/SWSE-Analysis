# Swarm Attack

|  |
| --- |
| ***This section was titled Swarm in its official source, but has been renamed to distinguish it from the similarly named "[[Swarm]]."*** |

*Reference Book: [[Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide]]*

You are trained to fight with allies en masse.

**Prerequisite:** [[Coordinated_Attack]]

**Effect:** You gain a +1 circumstance bonus on [[Melee_Attack]] rolls for each allied character adjacent to your target.

# Grapple Resistance

*Reference Book: [[Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

You make it difficult for enemies to [[Grab]] or [[Grapple]] you, or attack objects in your possession.

**Effect:** You get a +5 bonus to your [[Reflex_Defense]] when an enemy makes a [[Grab]] or [[Grapple]] attack against you, and you get a +5 bonus to all opposed [[Grapple]] checks. All objects that you are holding or carrying gain a +5 bonus to their [[Reflex_Defense]] when attacked (see [[Attack_an_Object]]).

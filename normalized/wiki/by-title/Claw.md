# Claw

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

**Cost:** 20 x [[Cost_Factor]]

**Weight:** (5 x [[Cost_Factor]]) Kilograms

Claws are an intermediate step between [[Tool_Mounts]] and [[Hands]]. They are useful for grabbing onto objects to be moved, but aren't very good for tasks that require fine manipulation. For example, while a Droid could easily carry a blaster in its Claw, it would have difficulty firing it. A Droid using a Claw to perform a task that normally requires a true hand must make a DC 15 [[Dexterity]] check to succeed at the task. If the check fails, the Droid drops the object it is attempting to manipulate.

Claws deal damage when used for [[Unarmed_Attacks]] as determined by the Droid's size:

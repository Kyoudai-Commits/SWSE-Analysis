# Surgical Expertise

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

You can perform skillful surgical procedures quickly.

**Prerequisite:** [[Trained]] in [[Treat_Injury]]

**Effect:** You can perform [[Surgery]] in 10 minutes.

**Normal:** Performing [[Surgery]] typically takes 1 hour.

# Master of Disguise

*Reference Book: [[Galaxy_of_Intrigue]]*

You are adept at creating convincing disguises in remarkably short periods of time.

**Prerequisites:** [[Charisma]] 13, [[Trained]] in [[Deception]]

**Effect:** You gain a +5 insight bonus to your [[Deception]] check when creating a [[Deceptive_Appearance]]. In addition, you can rush the process by taking a -2 penalty to the [[Deception]] check.

**Normal:** When you rush the creation of a [[Deceptive_Appearance]], you take a -10 penalty to your [[Deception]] check.

# Precise Shot

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

You are skilled at timing your [[Ranged_Attacks]] so that you don't hit your ally by mistake.

**Prerequisite:** [[Point-Blank_Shot]]

**Effect:** You can shoot or throw a ranged weapon at an opponent engaged in melee combat with one or more of your allies without taking the standard -5 penalty for [[Attacking_into_a_Melee]].

**Special:** If another [[Vehicle]] fires into a [[Dogfight]], it takes a -5 penalty on its attack roll, unless the [[Gunner]] has the Precise Shot feat.

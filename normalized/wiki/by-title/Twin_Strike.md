# Twin Strike

*Reference Book: [[Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual]]*

**[*[[Lightsaber_Form]]*]**

You swing both of your [[Lightsabers]] in an arc toward the target, slamming them home with great force.

**Time:** [[Standard_Action]]

**Targets:** One enemy within [[Reach]].

**Make a [[Use_the_Force]] check.** The result of the check determines the effect, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 15 | You make a single [[Melee_Attack]] with a [[Lightsaber]] against your target. If this attack hits, add the base damage of your other [[Lightsaber]] to the damage roll. |
| 25 | You make a single [[Melee_Attack]] with a [[Lightsaber]] against your target, with a +1 [[Force]] bonus to the attack roll. If this attack hits, add the base damage of your other [[Lightsaber]] to the damage roll. |
| 30 | You make a single [[Melee_Attack]] with a [[Lightsaber]] against your target, with a +2 [[Force]] bonus to the attack roll. If this attack hits, add the base damage of your other [[Lightsaber]] to the damage roll. |

**Lightsaber Form ([[Jar'Kai]]):** If you have the **[[Jar'Kai]]** Talent, you triple your deflection bonus from the **[[Lightsaber_Defense]]** Talent instead of doubling it until the end of your next turn.

**Special:** You must be wielding two [[Lightsabers]] to use this Form Power. You can spend a [[Force_Point]] to add +1 die of damage to the damage from your [[Lightsaber]] attack.

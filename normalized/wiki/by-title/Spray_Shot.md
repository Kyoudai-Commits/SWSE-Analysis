# Spray Shot

*Reference Book: [[Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide]]*

You can fire a short burst at an enemy while protecting nearby allies from errant shots.

**Effect:** When you have a weapon set on [[Autofire]], you can reduce the area targeted by your [[Autofire]] attack to 1 square.

# Bone Crusher

*Reference Book: [[Galaxy_of_Intrigue]]*

You are skilled at dealing lasting damage to [[Grappled]] opponents.

**Prerequisites:** [[Crush]], [[Pin]]

**Effect:** When you deal damage to a [[Grappled]] opponent, the opponent also moves -1 step along the [[Condition_Track]].

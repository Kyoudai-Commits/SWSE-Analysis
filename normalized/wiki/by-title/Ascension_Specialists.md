# Ascension Specialists

*Reference Book: [[Galaxy_at_War]]*

You and your allies train on the sheer cliffs of [[Teth]] and the sinkholes of [[Utapau]] as a matter of course.

**Prerequisite:** [[Trained]] in [[Climb]]

**Effect:** You gain a +3 competence bonus on [[Climb]] checks. At the time you make a check, for each ally within 12 squares of you who also has this feat, the competence bonus increases by +1 (to a maximum of +7).

Additionally, when you climb, you climb at half your [[Speed]] as a [[Move_Action]] (or at your normal speed as a [[Full-Round_Action]]).

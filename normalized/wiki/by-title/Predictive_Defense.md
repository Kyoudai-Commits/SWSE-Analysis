# Predictive Defense

*Reference Book: [[Galaxy_at_War]]*

The sharpness of your mind, not the speed of your reactions, allows you to avoid incoming attacks.

**Prerequisite:** [[Intelligence]] 13

**Effect:** You can use either your [[Dexterity]] modifier or your [[Intelligence]] modifier to determine your [[Reflex_Defense]].

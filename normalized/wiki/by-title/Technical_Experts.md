# Technical Experts

*Reference Book: [[Galaxy_at_War]]*

You and your allies are just as comfortable in the vehicle pool as on the battlefield and are capable of maintaining heavy equipment.

**Prerequisite:** [[Trained]] in [[Mechanics]]

**Effect:** You gain a +3 competence bonus on [[Mechanics]] checks. At the time you make a check, for each ally within 12 squares of you who also has this feat, the competence bonus increases by +1 (to a maximum of +7).

Additionally, when you use the [[Aid_Another]] Action to assist an ally who has this feat with a [[Mechanics]] check, you provide a bonus of +4 instead of +2.

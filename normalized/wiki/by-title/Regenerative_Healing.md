# Regenerative Healing

*Reference Book: [[Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

Your natural regeneration can help you recuperate from wounds more quickly.

**Prerequisite:** [[Trandoshan]]

**Effect:** Once per day when you catch a [[Second_Wind]], you can regain no [[Hit_Points]] immediately and instead regain 5 [[Hit_Points]] at the end of each of your turns until you reach full [[Hit_Points]] or until the end of the encounter (whichever comes first).

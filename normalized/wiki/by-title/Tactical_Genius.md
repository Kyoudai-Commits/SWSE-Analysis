# Tactical Genius

*Reference Book: [[Starships_of_the_Galaxy]]*

You are a master at using multiple [[Starship_Maneuvers]] over the course of long space combats.

**Prerequisites:** [[Trained]] in [[Pilot]], [[Starship_Tactics]], [[Vehicular_Combat]]

**Effect:** You regain all spent [[Starship_Maneuvers]] at the end of any round in which you roll a Natural 20 on an attack roll.

**Normal:** Only a Natural 20 rolled on a [[Pilot]] check to activate a [[Starship_Maneuver]] restores all [[Starship_Maneuvers]].

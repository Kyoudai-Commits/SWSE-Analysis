# Heavy Hitter

*Reference Book: [[Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide]]*

You know how to pound targets when firing heavy-weapons emplacements and vehicle weaponry.

**Prerequisite:** [[Weapon_Focus_(Heavy_Weapons)]]

**Effect:** When making an attack roll using a [[Weapon_Emplacement]] or a [[Weapon_System]], you add an additional 1 point of damage for every 5 points you roll above the target's [[Reflex_Defense]]. If you exceed the target's [[Damage_Threshold]], then on its next turn the target cannot attack and its [[Speed]] is reduced by 2 squares (1 square [[Starship_Scale]]).

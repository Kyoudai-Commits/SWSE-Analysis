# Resist Force

*Reference Book: [[Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide]]*

You use [[The_Force]] to protect yourself from an opponent's [[Force_Powers]].

**Time:** [[Standard_Action]]

**Targets:** Self.

**Make a [[Use_the_Force]] check.** The result of the check determines the effects, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 15 | You gain +1 to the [[Defense]] score of your choice against [[Force_Powers]] used against you, until the beginning of your next turn. |
| 20 | You gain +2 to the [[Defense]] score of your choice against [[Force_Powers]] used against you, until the beginning of your next turn. |
| 25 | You gain +3 to the [[Defense]] score of your choice against [[Force_Powers]] used against you, until the beginning of your next turn. |

**Special:** You can spend a [[Force_Point]] to apply the bonus provided by this [[Force_Power]] to any two [[Defense]] scores of your choice.

You can maintain *Resist Force* from round to round, extending the normal duration. Maintaining the *Resist Force* [[Force_Power]] is a [[Swift_Action]]. If you take damage while maintaining *Resist Force*, you must succeed on a [[Use_the_Force]] check (DC = 15 + damage taken) to continue maintaining the *Resist Force*. You may have multiple instances of *Resist Force* active at once, but maintaining each one requires a [[Swift_Action]] each round, and each one must be activated by a separate use of *Resist Force*.

# Demoralizing Strike

*Reference Book: [[Galaxy_of_Intrigue]]*

You know how to dishearten your opponents when they least expect it.

**Prerequisite:** [[Charisma]] 13

**Effect:** When you successfully deal damage to an opponent with an [[Attack_of_Opportunity]], you can immediately make a [[Persuasion]] check to [[Intimidate]] the opponent as a [[Free_Action]].

# Deadly Sniper

*Reference Book: [[Scum_and_Villainy]]*

You are adept at staying out of sight when attacking from hidden positions.

**Prerequisites:** [[Base_Attack_Bonus]] +9, [[Trained]] in [[Stealth]], [[Sniper]]

**Effect:** When you make a [[Ranged_Attack]] against a target that is unaware of you, you gain a +2 bonus on your attack roll and deal +1 die of damage on the first attack each turn.

# Steadying Position

*Reference Book: [[Galaxy_at_War]]*

You know how to fire from the optimal position, using the ground to steady your aim.

**Prerequisite:** [[Careful_Shot]]

**Effect:** When you are [[Prone]] and [[Aim]] before making a [[Ranged_Attack]], your target does not benefit from its [[Dexterity]] bonus to its [[Reflex_Defense]].

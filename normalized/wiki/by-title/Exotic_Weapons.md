# Category : Exotic Weapons

Exotic Weapons are unusual or unique weapons that require specific training to properly utilize (including both [[Exotic_Weapons_(Ranged)]] and [[Exotic_Weapons_(Melee)]]). Unlike other [[Weapon_Groups]], you must select the [[Exotic_Weapon_Proficiency]] feat each time you want to learn how to use a different Exotic Weapon. [[Exotic_Weapon_Proficiency]] is a Class Bonus Feat for [[Nobles]] and [[Soldiers]].

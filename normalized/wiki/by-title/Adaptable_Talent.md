# Adaptable Talent

*Reference Book: [[Galaxy_of_Intrigue]]*

You're a multitalented being, capable of pulling all kinds of tricks out of your sleeve.

**Effect:** Choose one [[Talent]] that you meet the prerequisites for, from a [[Class]] you possess. Once per day, after at least 6 hours of rest, you can choose to swap out one of your current [[Talents]] for the [[Talent]] you chose for this feat. The [[Talent]] you swap out cannot be the prerequisite for any other [[Talent]] you have. You can swap back to your original [[Talent]] after at least 6 hours of rest.

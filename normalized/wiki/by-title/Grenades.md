# Grenades

| DISTANCE | RANGE | PENALTY |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-6 Squares | Point-Blank | No penalty |
| 7-8 Squares | Short | -2 to attack rolls |
| 9-10 Squares | Medium | -5 to attack rolls |
| 11-12 Squares | Long | -10 to attack rolls |

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

Highly volatile explosives, Grenades are highly dangerous, used only by galactic armies, or professional mercenaries. Unless stated otherwise, Grenades are both [[Area_Attack]] and [[Thrown_Weapons]] for the purpose of determining [[Range]], with a [[Burst]] radius determining their attack size. [[Weapon_Proficiency_(Simple_Weapons)]] is a Starting Feat for all [[Heroic_Classes]] (which is also applicable to [[Simple_Weapons_(Melee)]] and [[Simple_Weapons_(Ranged)]]).

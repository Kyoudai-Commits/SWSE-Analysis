# Combat Trickery

*Reference Book: [[Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide]]*

You excel at deceiving others with your tricks and masterful [[Feint]] in combat.

**Prerequisite:** [[Trained]] in [[Deception]]

**Effect:** You can spend two successive [[Swift_Actions]] on the same turn to make a [[Deception]] check against a target's [[Will_Defense]]. If the check is successful, the target is [[Flat-Footed]] against your next attack made before the end of your next turn. Additionally, you can spend a [[Force_Point]] to extend your target's penalties until the end of encounter.

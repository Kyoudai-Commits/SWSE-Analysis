# Great Cleave

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

You can wield a melee weapon with such power that you can strike multiple times when you drop your opponents.

**Prerequisites:** [[Strength]] 13, [[Base_Attack_Bonus]] +4, [[Power_Attack]], [[Cleave]]

**Effect:** As the [[Cleave]] feat, except that you have no limit to the number of times you can use it per round.

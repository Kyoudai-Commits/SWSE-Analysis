# Sniper

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

You are particularly adept at hitting the right target in a crowd.

**Prerequisites:** [[Base_Attack_Bonus]] +4, [[Point-Blank_Shot]], [[Precise_Shot]]

**Effect:** You always ignore "soft" [[Cover]] (that is, [[Cover]] provided by a character, creature, or [[Droid]]) when you make a [[Ranged_Attack]].

**Normal:** You can only ignore [[Cover]] if you [[Aim]] before making a [[Ranged_Attack]].

# Collateral Damage

*Reference Book: [[Scum_and_Villainy]]*

Your hail of fire can catch secondary targets in your firing arc.

**Prerequisites:** [[Base_Attack_Bonus]] +6, [[Rapid_Shot]]

**Effect:** When you deal damage with a single, non-[[Area_Attack]] using the [[Rapid_Shot]] feat, once per turn on your turn, you can immediately make a second attack at a -2 penalty against a second target within 2 squares of the first. If this attack hits, you deal half the original attack's damage to that target.

# Unstoppable Combatant

*Reference Book: [[Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

You can keep coming back for more punishment, no matter what your enemies throw at you.

**Prerequisite:** [[Extra_Second_Wind]]

**Effect:** You can catch more than one [[Second_Wind]] per encounter.

**Normal:** Even if you can catch multiple [[Second_Winds]] per day, without this feat you are limited to one [[Second_Wind]] per encounter.

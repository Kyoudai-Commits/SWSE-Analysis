# Acrobatic Dodge

*Reference Book: [[Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide]]*

When you react to an attack, your acrobatics training improves your mobility.

**Prerequisites:** [[Dexterity]] 13, [[Dodge]], [[Mobility]], [[Skill_Focus_(Acrobatics)]]

**Effect:** Once per encounter, as a [[Reaction]] if a [[Melee_Attack]] misses you, you can immediately move into an adjacent square without provoking an [[Attack_of_Opportunity]].

**Special:** You must be aware of the attacker to benefit from this feat. You can use this feat a second time during the encounter by spending a [[Force_Point]].

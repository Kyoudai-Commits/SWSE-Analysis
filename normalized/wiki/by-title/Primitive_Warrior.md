# Primitive Warrior

*Reference Book: [[Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

You have learned to make the most of primitive weapons and can wield them with deadly results.

**Prerequisite:** [[Gamorrean]]

**Effect:** You deal +1 die of damage with [[Simple_Weapons_(Melee)]].

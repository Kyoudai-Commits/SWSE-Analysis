# Indomitable Personality

*Reference Book: [[Galaxy_of_Intrigue]]*

Your powerful personality helps you cope with attacks that assault your will.

**Prerequisite:** [[Charisma]] 13

**Effect:** Once per encounter, you can add your [[Charisma]] modifier to your [[Will_Defense]] as a [[Reaction]]; this benefit lasts until the end of your next turn.

# Informer

*Reference Book: [[Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide]]*

You are adept at prying into the affairs of others and dealing with those who are most interested in gaining that knowledge.

**Prerequisite:** [[Trained]] in [[Perception]]

**Effect:** You can use your [[Perception]] modifier instead of your [[Gather_Information]] modifier when making [[Gather_Information]] checks. You are considered [[Trained]] in the [[Gather_Information]] skill. If you are entitled to a [[Gather_Information]] check reroll, you can reroll your [[Perception]] check instead (subject to the same circumstances and limitations).

Additionally, when you have favorable conditions for your [[Gather_Information]] check (such as from operating on your home planet), you reduce by half the time it takes to make a [[Gather_Information]] check.

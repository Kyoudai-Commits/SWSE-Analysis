# Tracked

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

**Base Speed:** Small (4 squares); Medium (6 squares); Large or bigger (8 squares)

**Cost:** 20 x [[Cost_Factor]] x (Base Speed Squared)

Tracked Droids are an improvement on the [[Wheeled]] Droid, having rigid treads that give them more traction. Tracked Droids ignore the penalties of [[Difficult_Terrain]], but they take a -5 penalty on all [[Climb]] checks.

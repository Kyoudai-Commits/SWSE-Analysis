# Cunning Attack

*Reference Book: [[Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide]]*

You are skilled at attacking unprepared opponents.

**Effect:** You gain a +2 bonus on attack rolls against a [[Flat-Footed]] enemy or one who is denied its [[Dexterity]] bonus to its [[Reflex_Defense]].

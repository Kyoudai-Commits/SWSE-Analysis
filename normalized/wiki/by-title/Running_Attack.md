# Running Attack

*Reference Books: [[Core_Rulebook]], [[Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide]]*

You can move as you attack.

**Prerequisite:** [[Dexterity]] 13

**Effect:** When making an attack with a melee or ranged weapon, you can [[Move]] both before and after the attack, provided that the total distance moved is not greater than your [[Speed]].

**[[Combined_Feat]]:** If you have the [[Dodge]] feat, you can add your dodge bonus to your [[Reflex_Defense]] against anyone who makes an [[Attack_of_Opportunity]] against you while using the Running Attack feat.

# Gungan Weapon Master

*Reference Book: [[Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

You have learned to master the ancestral weapons of your people and can penetrate even the staunchest defenses.

**Prerequisite:** [[Gungan]]

**Effect:** Whenever you spend a [[Force_Point]] to add to an attack roll with an [[Atlatl]] or a [[Cesta]], increase the die type by one step (from d6 to d8, or from d8 to d10).

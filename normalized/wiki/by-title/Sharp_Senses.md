# Sharp Senses

*Reference Book: [[Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

Your senses have been honed to the point where you can spot things that others could never see.

**Prerequisite:** [[Mon_Calamari]]

**Effect:** Whenever you spend a [[Force_Point]] to add to a [[Perception]] check, increase the die type by two steps (from d6 to d10, or from d8 to d12).

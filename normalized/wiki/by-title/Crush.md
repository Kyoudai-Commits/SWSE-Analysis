# Crush

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

You can deal damage to a target that you've [[Grappled]].

**Prerequisites:** [[Base_Attack_Bonus]] +1, [[Pin]]

**Effect:** If you successfully [[Pin]] an opponent with a [[Grapple]] attack, you can immediately deal Bludgeoning damage to it equal to your [[Unarmed]] damage or claw damage, whichever is greater.

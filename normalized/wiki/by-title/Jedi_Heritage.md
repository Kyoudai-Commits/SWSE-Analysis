# Jedi Heritage

*Reference Book: [[Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

You come from a clan that has produced many Jedi, and you have overcome your Species' impulsiveness by becoming a master of [[The_Force]].

**Prerequisites:** [[Twi'lek]], [[Force_Sensitivity]]

**Effect:** Your [[Wisdom]] score is considered to be 4 points higher for the purpose of determining how many [[Force_Powers]] you gain from the [[Force_Training]] feat, granting you two extra [[Force_Powers]] for each [[Force_Training]] feat.

# Meat Shield

*Reference Book: [[Galaxy_of_Intrigue]]*

You are adept at using an opponent's defense to your own advantage.

**Prerequisites:** [[Base_Attack_Bonus]] +4, [[Point-Blank_Shot]], [[Precise_Shot]]

**Effect:** Whenever an opponent attacks you while in [[Cover]] provided by another character, creature, or [[Droid]], you are treated as being in [[Cover]] from attacks by that opponent.

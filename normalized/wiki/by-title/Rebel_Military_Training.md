# Rebel Military Training

*Reference Book: [[Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

You are well versed in the hit-and-run tactics used by [[The_Rebel_Alliance]] and know how to use your mobility to avoid retribution.

**Prerequisite:** [[Running_Attack]]

**Effect:** Whenever you use the [[Running_Attack]] feat to move both before and after making an attack, you gain a +2 dodge bonus to your [[Reflex_Defense]] until the start of your next turn.

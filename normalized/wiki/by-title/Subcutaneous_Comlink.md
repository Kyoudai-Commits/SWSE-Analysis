# Subcutaneous Comlink

*Reference Book: [[Galaxy_at_War]]*

**Cost:** 4,000

**Installation Cost:** 3,000

**Rejection Attack Bonus:** +3

**Availability:** [[Restricted]]

A Subcutaneous Comlink features special sensors and transmitters attached to the vocal and auditory organs of the host. This allows the creature with this [[Advanced_Cybernetic]] to hear [[Comlink]] transmissions and to send his or her own by making only slight vocal sounds. When a being with a Subcutaneous Comlink wishes, he or she can communicate through this Subcutaneous Comlink without making a sound loud enough for others to hear.

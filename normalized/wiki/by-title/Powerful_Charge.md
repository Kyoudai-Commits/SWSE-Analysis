# Powerful Charge

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]]*

You can [[Charge]] with extra force.

**Prerequisites:** Medium or larger size, [[Base_Attack_Bonus]] +1

**Effect:** When you [[Charge]], you gain an additional +2 bonus on your melee attack roll. If your [[Melee_Attack]] hits, you deal additional damage equal to one-half your level.

# Skill Challenge: Catastrophic Avoidance

*Reference Book: [[Galaxy_of_Intrigue]]*

Your luck is such that catastrophe occurs less frequently for you when you are engaged in stressful situations.

**Effect:** Once per [[Skill_Challenge]] that has a chance of [[Catastrophic_Failure]], this Failure occurs only if you fail a [[Skill_Check]] by 15 or more. In addition, when a [[Catastrophic_Failure]] occurs, you accrue one Failure in the [[Skill_Challenge]], not two.

**Special:** This Feat is allowed only in campaigns that use [[Skill_Challenges]].

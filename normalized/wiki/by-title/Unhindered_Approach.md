# Unhindered Approach

*Reference Book: [[Galaxy_at_War]]*

You and your allies have trained on worlds where the terrain is unstable, and your team also specializes in vertical insertion into combat zones.

**Prerequisite:** [[Trained]] in [[Jump]]

**Effect:** You gain a +3 competence bonus on [[Jump]] checks. At the time you make a check, for each ally within 12 squares of you who also has this feat, the competence bonus increases by +1 (to a maximum of +7).

Additionally, when you use the [[Jump]] skill, add 1 square to your total jump distance.

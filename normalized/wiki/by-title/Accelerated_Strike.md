# Accelerated Strike

*Reference Book: [[Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide]]*

Your melee attack is exceptionally quick.

**Prerequisite:** [[Base_Attack_Bonus]] +6

**Effect:** Once per encounter, when using only weapons with which you are proficient, you can take a [[Full_Attack]] Action as a [[Standard_Action]].

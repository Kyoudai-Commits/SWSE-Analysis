# Inborn Resilience

*Reference Book: [[Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide]]*

You come from a hardy Species, but some of your people are better than others at avoiding certain kinds of harm.

**Prerequisite:** [[Zabrak]]

**Effect:** You can reduce your [[Species]] bonus to any one [[Defense]] to +0 and increase your [[Species]] bonus to another [[Defense]] to +2. Once you change your [[Species]] bonuses in this way, they cannot be changed back.

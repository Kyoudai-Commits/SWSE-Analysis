# Force Secrets

Skilled Force-users can learn to manipulate their [[Force_Powers]] in intriguing ways. Force Secrets represent a sublime connection of The Force and are usually available only to powerful Force-users such as [[Force_Disciples]], [[Jedi_Masters]], and [[Sith_Lords]]. Activating a Force Secret cost either a Force Point or a [[Destiny_Point]] (as noted in its description), and the normal limits on spending Force Points and [[Destiny_Points]] during a round apply.

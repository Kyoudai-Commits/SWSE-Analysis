# Criteria Tables

Becoming a member of the Revenants involves finding them and proving that one has the skills necessary to become a pirate. The Revenants are theoretically willing to accept anyone who can pull his or her weight, but Imperial sympathizers are always unwelcome. Additionally, any members who can bring unique resources to the group, such as new ships for the fleet or new technology to make piracy easier, will quickly find a home among the Revenants.

# Criteria Tables

Becoming a member of The Bounty Hunters' Guild is a relatively simple process. Typically, a bounty hunter need only contact a guild representative for membership, pay an initial guild membership fee of 500 credits, and pledge to follow the code and rules set forth by the guild. A member of The Bounty Hunters' Guild receives guild membership identification and secure comm codes to contact guild representatives in a variety of star systems.

Additionally, guild members must pay dues equal to 10% of any bounty to the guild. Failure to pay this tithe will result from expulsion from the guild, and usually violent reprisals from guild representatives. Each house within the guild has its own dues and regulations, and members are subject to the rules laid down by the head of the house.

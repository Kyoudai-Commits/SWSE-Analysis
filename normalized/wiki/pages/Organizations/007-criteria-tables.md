# Criteria Tables

Becoming a member of The Bothan SpyNet in any meaningful capacity usually involves earning the trust of an existing member. Sending relevant information along the SpyNet's channels is the only way to become trusted; informing the SpyNet that a group of travelers was attacked by wildlife on [[Felucia]] likely isn't relevant unless one of those travelers is a major personality.

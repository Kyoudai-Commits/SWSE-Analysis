Apart from the Republic Fleet Systems consortium in which it holds an interest, the Republic Navy prefers to purchase all its craft from private manufacturers, thus contributing to the galactic economy. Many are commissioned under contracts in which [[The_Old_Republic]] controls the patents, limiting the ability of manufacturers to sell their craft to other navies.

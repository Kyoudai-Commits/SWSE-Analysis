# Speeder Bikes

[[Speeder_Bikes]] spike in popularity following [[The_Great_Sith_War]] as [[Lhosan_Industries]] introduces two new models- the [[Daggerstar_Swoop_Racer]] for racing, and a more common [[AeroChaser_Speeder_Bike]] for simple transport. Both of these designs spark a revolution in Speeder Bike design. Only the Aratech [[Urban_Navigator_Speeder_Bike]] comes close to the Lhosan bikes' popularity. The Speeder Bikes are based on military [[Speeders]] used by [[The_Old_Republic]] just prior to [[The_Great_Sith_War]].

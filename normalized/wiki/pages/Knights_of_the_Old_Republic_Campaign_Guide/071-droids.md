# Droids

The [[Basilisk_War_Droid]], one of the most unique weapons of war in the galaxy, is frequently seen in skirmishes throughout [[The_Great_Sith_War]] and [[The_Mandalorian_Wars]]

Mandalorians are technological mimics, able to replicate and improve upon the technologies of conquered peoples. Mandalorian ship designs are based on pirated vessels, augmented beyond recognition by Mandalorians looking for an extra technological edge.

The ships of traditional Mandalorians are therefore, as highly personalized as their suits of armor. Most known "Types" are vague categorizations, named by Republic observers for their convenience. (Use the [[Junker_Vehicle_Template]] on any of the ships below to represent modified designs from before [[The_Mandalorian_Wars]].) Under the Neo-Crusaders, more designs are routed to War Forges for mass production, creating somewhat more recognizable classes of [[Vehicles]].

Manufacturing during [[The_Mandalorian_Wars]] is all for the Crusade, without such Republic niceties as patents or corporate entities. In later times, with [[The_Mandalorians]] scattered, their technologies resurface in more traditional corporate bodies such as MandalMotors.

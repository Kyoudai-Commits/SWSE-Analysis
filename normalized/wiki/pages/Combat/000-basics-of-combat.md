# Basics of Combat

All forms of Combat in the *Saga Edition* system use "rounds" to simulate time in a mechanical sense, with "actions" representing the things done in those rounds. The following are the core mechanics of an form of Combat heroes might encounter.

- **[[Combat_Sequence]]:** Combat takes place through a series of rounds, allowing every character involved in the encounter a chance to perform their turn in the [[Initiative_Order]]. Certain events may also affect just how an encounter begins.
- **[[Actions]]:** Every round, on your character's turn, you may take a [[Standard_Action]], a [[Move_Action]], and a [[Swift_Action]] (in any order). These Actions represent everything a character can do in Combat, with [[Bonuses_and_Penalties]] applied to tasks that a given situation might be easier or harder to accomplish.
- **[[Line_of_Sight]]:** A character can target an opponent that they see, which is to say, any opponent within their Line of Sight. If a line can be drawn from any point in the attacker's [[Fighting_Space]] to any point in the target's [[Fighting_Space]], they have Line of Sight.
- **[[Attacks_of_Opportunity]]:** If an opponent moves out of a square adjacent to you, or performs an action that forces them to let down their guard, you can make a single, immediate attack against that opponent (even if you've already acted during the round).

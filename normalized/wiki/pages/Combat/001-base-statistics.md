# Base Statistics

When determining an individual character, creature, or any other entity acting in Combat, several key statistics determine their capabilities.

- **[[Attacks]]:** Attacking (whether it be [[Melee_Attacks]] or [[Ranged_Attacks]]) is how characters primarily inflict damage on their opponents. If their roll of a 1d20 and their appropriate modifiers is high enough, the Attack can harm their enemies in Combat.
- **[[Defenses]]:** Your Defenses represent your ability to avoid taking [[Damage]] and overcome attacks to the body and mind. You have three Defense Scores; Reflex (how hard you are to hit in combat), Fortitude (your ability to resist debilitating effects), and Will (your willpower and ability to resist mental attacks).
- **[[Speed]]:** Your Speed tells you how far you can [[Move]] with a single [[Move_Action]]. Sometimes you might have special types of movement (a Climb Speed, Fly Speed, Swim Speed, etc) that are also part of your Speed.
- **[[Hit_Points]]:** Hit Points (abbreviated "HP") represent two things in the game world: the ability to take physical punishment and keep going, and the ability to turn a serious blow into a graze or near miss.

# Mass Combat

*Reference Book: [[Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide]]*

[[The_Clone_Wars]] are all about the massive battles that transform the galaxy for decades- even centuries- to come. No conflict on the scale of [[The_Clone_Wars]] is complete without sweeping battles that cover entire planets. In most cases, these battles serve as the backdrop for adventures, with either predetermined outcomes planned in advance by the Gamemaster or outcomes based on the results of smaller encounters, as described in the guidelines for [[Large-Scale_Conflict]]. However, sometimes the GM might want the players to act as commanders and tacticians on a higher level. In these cases, Jedi Generals can truly be generals in command of entire battalions, and all the heroes can participate in these large-scale battles. [[The_Clone_Wars]] feature battles deep in the jungles of [[Felucia]], on the ash-choked streets of [[Mygeeto]], and deep in the sinkholes of [[Utapau]]. Picking an exciting setting for a Mass Combat battle is an important step- just as important as choosing the two (or more) opposing forces.

The following rules govern massive ground combat, like that seen in the *Star Wars* movies and *The Clone Wars* animated series. In many ways, these rules mirror Vehicle Combat- and for good reason. [[Battalions]]- the basic organizational name for a large number of troops- function like Vehicles in that heroes can join the Battalion and take Actions to control and manipulate that Battalion. These Mass Combat rules represent the chance to put thousands of soldiers into play during a single encounter. This section also includes rules for integrating both individual Vehicles as well as groups of smaller Vehicles, allowing Gamemasters more diversity than just having two armies firing at one another across an open plain. Like designing interesting character-scale and Vehicle encounters, designing interesting Mass Combat battles is a matter of choosing compelling opponents, providing an engaging setting, and adding existing [[Hazards]] and challenges.

Everything in a Mass Combat encounter takes place at Starship Scale. As in Starship Scale, each square is abstract, representing however much terrain the Gamemaster needs it to. In a densely packed battle, the squares might be relatively small, forcing the members of each [[Battalion]] to crowd together (as in the Battle of [[Geonosis]]). In other cases, the battle might be spread out over several kilometers. As a result, the actual time taken up by the combat round is also abstracted, and to a certain extent, so are the Actions of each [[Battalion]].

There are two options for determining [[Initiative_Order]] in Mass Combat. First, each character can make separate [[Initiative]] checks. This is probably the best method if most or all characters are part of the same [[Battalion]], but it can result in excessive [[Delayed]] or [[Readied]] Actions as [[Attack_Leaders]] wait for [[Field_Commanders]] to move [[Battalions]] into position. An alternative is to make an [[Initiative]] check for each [[Battalion]], using the Battalion's [[Initiative]] check modifier. This is particularly appropriate when the characters are parts of different [[Battalions]], since it allows everyone in the same [[Battalion]] to act more or less simultaneously.

**Special:** If a [[Battalion]] or a Battalion's [[Field_Commander]] is [[Trained]] in [[Knowledge_(Tactics)]], you can choose to make a [[Knowledge_(Tactics)]] check instead of an [[Initiative]] check to determine that Battalion's place in the [[Initiative_Order]].

Resolving Mass Combat with OrganizationsResolving Mass Combat with Organizations

*Reference Book: [[Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide]]*

You can use the Organization [[Power_Check]] rules to resolve Mass Combat. Though the Gamemaster might have an outcome of battle in mind when Mass Combat takes place, the [[Power_Check]] rules make it easy to resolve large-scale battles without having to script them entirely.

When the heroes participate in a large battle, such as the Battle of [[Naboo]] or the Battle of [[Endor]], they have the chance to make a difference and turn the tide of combat. Typically, a massive fight will take place in the background while the heroes engage in a small number of more focused encounters. If you want to use this system to resolve Mass Combat, design adventures using massive battles as the backdrop for a number of encounters.

When a [[Battalion]] is reduced to 0 [[Hit_Points]], or when the [[Field_Commander]] of the [[Battalion]] orders it so, the Battalion Disbands. Any heroes in a Battalion that Disbands are no longer part of the [[Battalion]], and they are then considered individual targets. Two or more adjacent Disbanding Battalions can recombine into a single [[Battalion]]. The newly formed Battalion is of the same type as the lowest CL Disbanding Battalion; thus, a CL 8 Battalion and a CL 10 Battalion can Disband and reform as a [[Battalion]] of the same type as the CL 8 Battalion.

The [[Hit_Points]] of the Disbanding Battalions are added together, and become the new Hit Point total of the [[Battalion]] (up to the Battalion's maximum [[Hit_Points]]). If the CLs of the Disbanding Battalions have a difference of 4 or more, the Gamemaster can give the new Battalion an Advantaged [[Battalion_Template]]. However, this is solely at the Gamemaster's discretion, and might be affected by the circumstances surrounding the formation of the new [[Battalion]]. A Disbanding Battalion that does not reform with another Disbanding Battalion leaves the battlefield.

A character in a [[Battalion]] fills one of several possible roles, determining not only the Actions a character can take but also how the [[Battalion]] behaves in Mass Combat. Mass Combat allows several distinct roles, but a single character can fill multiple roles at the same time. You can change roles from round to round, but you can fill a particular role only if no other character has filled that role since the end of your last turn. Characters within [[Battalions]] cannot be specifically targeted by effects or attacks, just as one cannot target specific vehicle systems in most circumstances.

Heroes contribute to the [[Battalion]] by their leadership and ability to affect the whole [[Battalion]], not their individual combat prowess. A single creature or character within a [[Battalion]] cannot be targeted; all attacks target the [[Battalion]], effectively providing all heroes in a [[Battalion]] with [[Total_Cover]] from outside attacks. Unless noted otherwise, joining a [[Battalion]] is a [[Move_Action]].

- **[[Field_Commander]]:** A Field Commander controls the Battalion's movement and can also fill every other Battalion Position not currently filled by another character. A [[Battalion]] can have only one Field Commander at a time.
- **[[First_Officer]]:** A First Officer can perform all the same Actions that the [[Field_Commander]] hasn't performed since the end of the First Officer's last turn. A [[Battalion]] can have only one First Officer at a time.
- **[[Attack_Leader]]:** The Attack Leader coordinates all the Battalion's attacks. An Attack Leader can order attacks and use special tactics. A [[Battalion]] can have only one Attack Leader at a time.
- **[[Communications_Officer]]:** The Communications Officer is responsible for coordinating all the orders within the [[Battalion]] and coordinating with other [[Battalions]]. A [[Battalion]] can have only one Communications Officer at a time.
- **[[Medical_Officer]]:** The Medical Officer coordinates a team of combat physicians who oversee the general welfare of the [[Battalion]]. A [[Battalion]] can have only one Medical Officer at a time.

Characters filling the various roles in a [[Battalion]] might have [[Talents]], [[Feats]], [[Force_Powers]], and other special abilities that they can use in Mass Combat. As a general rule, special abilities that function in Character Combat have too small an effect to be noticeable in Mass Combat. However, some abilities, especially those used by [[Officers]] and [[Nobles]], can give [[Battalions]] a big advantage through superior leadership. [[Talents]], [[Feats]], and special abilities can be used in Mass Combat only if they meet certain criteria.

- **All Targets who can See, Hear, and Understand You:** Abilities that require your targets to be able to see, hear, and understand you function only for targets within your same square in Mass Combat.
- **All Targets in [[Line_of_Sight]]:** Abilities that affect enemies, allies, or targets in [[Line_of_Sight]] function in Mass Combat. They affect all targets of the appropriate type within your same square.
- **Single Target, Limited Number of Targets, or Nearby Targets:** Abilities that affect a single target, a limited number of targets, or targets within a certain number of squares of you have no effect during Mass Combat.

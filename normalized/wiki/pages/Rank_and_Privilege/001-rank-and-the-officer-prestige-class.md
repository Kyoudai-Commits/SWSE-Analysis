# Rank and the Officer Prestige Class

When using the Rank and Privilege system, a character can take the [[Officer]] Prestige Class without having a sufficient Organization Score to put him or her in the officer rank category (Score 12; Second Lieutenant). Such a character might have the knowledge, experience, and command ability to place him or her in the ranks of officer at a later date. Many noncommissioned officers, such as Warrant Officers, are capable of commanding more troops than their rank implies, but choose to remain in the field with their soldiers, thriving on the challenges that come with being in close proximity with the troops that they lead or taking on specialized tasks without the burden of command.

Alternatively, heroes who take levels in the [[Officer]] Prestige Class can be automatically raised to the rank of Second Lieutenant and can be assumed to have the required Organization Score.

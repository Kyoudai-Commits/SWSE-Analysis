# Resources

The heroes need locations that can provide them with resources. A starport or transportation hub could offer the means to travel between operational areas or bases. An urban center could contain a market plaza where they might find supplies and allies willing to covertly support their activities. Specific locales in a city- a residential neighborhood, an industrial sector, an alien enclave, the starport, an entertainment district, or a commercial zone- could be the location of specific businesses and other establishments in which action could take place.

# Space Transports

The Empire's move to [[Capital_Ships]] that were unable to land easily on planetary surfaces makes [[Space_Transports]] a critical part of any fleet. Transports are used to move troops to groundside battles, shuttle cargo from supply vessels to Star Destroyers, and move crew and commanders among ships of a fleet. Though often armed, such [[Starships]] are much more valuable as convoy vessels than as warships.

# Additional Talent Trees

| [[Aing-Tii_Monk_Talent_Tree]] | [[Tradition_Talent_Tree]] ([[The_Aing-Tii_Monks]]) | [[The_Aing-Tii_Monks]] have an understanding of [[The_Force]] that is different from [[The_Jedi]] and most other [[Force_Traditions]]. [[The_Force]] has many more aspects than just a Light Side and a Dark Side, and it is most useful for facilitating freedom of travel. |

| [[Iron_Knight_Talent_Tree]] | [[Tradition_Talent_Tree]] ([[The_Iron_Knights]]) | [[The_Iron_Knights]] are truly unique, in that they are silicon-based creatures that use [[Droids]] as their physical bodies to interact with the galaxy. | [[Matukai_Adept_Talent_Tree]] | [[Tradition_Talent_Tree]] ([[The_Matukai]]) | All Matukai strive to achieve harmony between the physical and the spiritual aspects of their lives. As a result, they find ways to use [[The_Force]] in improve their bodies. |
| [[Mystic_Talent_Tree]] | [[Force_Adept]] | You have studied [[The_Force]] in a radically different way from other Force-users, relying on rituals and mystic lore to harness its power. |
| [[Seyugi_Dervish_Talent_Tree]] | [[Tradition_Talent_Tree]] ([[The_Seyugi_Dervishes]]) | Seyugi Dervishes are masters of [[Unarmed]] combat augmented by their control over [[The_Force]], and they traditionally hire themselves out as contract assassins. | [[Shaper_of_Kro_Var_Talent_Tree]] | [[Tradition_Talent_Tree]] ([[The_Shapers_of_Kro_Var]]) | [[The_Shapers_of_Kro_Var]] are deeply in touch with the four classic elements of air, earth, fire, and water. They use [[The_Force]] to manipulate these elements at a fundamental level, creating impressive effects, such as ripping stone, walls of solid wind, and gouts of flame. The Talents listed here are a small fraction of the "spells" that these primitive Force-users are capable of using. |
| [[Sith_Alchemy_Talent_Tree]] | [[Sith_Apprentice]] | You have learned the alchemical arts of the ancient [[Sith_Lords]] and can fill objects and creatures with the power of [[The_Dark_Side]]. |
| [[Telepath_Talent_Tree]] | [[Force_Adept]] | You have mastered the art of mental manipulation, using [[The_Force]] to touch, read, and affect the minds of others with ease. |
| [[Tyia_Adept_Talent_Tree]] | [[Tradition_Talent_Tree]] ([[The_Tyia]]) | The masters of [[The_Tyia]] seek out Harmony while eschewing Discord. A Tyia Adept connects to [[The_Force]] in search of unity, and attempts to align their body and spirit with the galaxy. |
| [[Warden_of_the_Sky_Talent_Tree]] | [[Tradition_Talent_Tree]] ([[The_Wardens_of_the_Sky]]) | Wardens of the Sky use a combination of martial arts and [[Force_Powers]] to help keep the space lanes safe. As a Warden of the Sky, you rely on nothing more than your own body to serve as a weapon against predators. |
| [[White_Current_Adept_Talent_Tree]] | [[Tradition_Talent_Tree]] ([[The_Fallanassi]]) | The White Current guides [[The_Fallanassi]] to use its power to benefit everything and everyone around them, as well as to protect themselves. Those who succeed in channeling the White Current often display powers with surprising effects. |
| [[Zeison_Sha_Warrior_Talent_Tree]] | [[Tradition_Talent_Tree]] ([[The_Zeison_Sha]]) | Zeison Sha talented enough to become Warriors focus their powers on manipulating the objects around them. They are also masters of their signature weapon: the [[Discblade]]. |

*Reference Book: [[Core_Rulebook]], [[Web_Enhancements]]*

The Gamemaster is the guiding force of the game. If the game is fun, it will be to your credit. If it isn't you'll need to make some adjustments. But don't worry- running a *Star Wars* game is nowhere near as daunting as it might seem at first. Described below are the different duties of the GM. As with any hobby, focus on what you enjoy the most, but remember that the other duties are also important.

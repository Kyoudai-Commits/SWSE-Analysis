# Animal Companions and Gaining Experience Points

If the Gamemaster decides to award an animal companion an equal share of the group's earned [[Experience_Points]], eventually the creature will gain enough [[Experience_Points]] to advance in level. Even though it is a player-controlled NPC, the animal companion still follows the advancement rules (see [[Beast]]). Since heroes are exceptional individuals, their animal companions would likely become similarly exceptional.

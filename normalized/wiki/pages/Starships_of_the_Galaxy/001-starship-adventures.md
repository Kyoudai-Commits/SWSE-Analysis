# Starship Adventures

The very title of the *Star Wars* setting makes it clear- most adventures are going to take place at least partially in space. Whether they're running past an enemy blockade of a planet, escaping [[Star_Destroyers]] through an asteroid field, or fighting epic space battles to decide the fate of the galaxy, *Star Wars* heroes are going to end up in space. This chapter provides advice on adding [[Starship_Scale]] encounters to a game and rules for heroes to go beyond being part-time pilots and fighter jockeys.

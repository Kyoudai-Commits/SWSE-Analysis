# Starship Codex

This chapter features new [[Starships]] from a variety of eras and locales. Each Starship has its own entry, with a complete stat block and image of the [[Starship]]. Many entries describe variants or famous individual ships, to further expand the usefulness of entries. Additionally, some [[Space_Transports]] have deckplans included in their entries. These ships are among the most suitable vessels for heroes to use as a base of operations and means of transport, and the deckplans are designed to let Gamemasters use the ships as an adventuring locale as well as a means of transport.

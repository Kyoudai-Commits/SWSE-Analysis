# Politics is Personal

Politics plays a major part in [[Intrigue_Campaigns]], with politicians using the power of a legislature, a royal court, or even a tribal council to shape policy and influence the will of the people. Corporations use their influence and their lobbyists to ease restrictions on trade. Interest groups of all kinds use the political arena to make their voices heard and draw attention to their messages. Politics touches every aspect of life in the galaxy, even in the most far-flung, backwater planets.

As a case in point, the conflict in *Star Wars:* Episode I *The Phantom Menace* begins innocuously enough as a trade dispute. Over the course of that and the subsequent movies, however, the galaxy is rocked as the dispute quickly progresses through a blockade, an armed conflict, the removal of the Supreme Chancellor, the rise of Senator Palpatine, and eventually the full-scale war of [[The_Clone_Wars]] in *Star Wars:* Episode III *Revenge of the Sith*.

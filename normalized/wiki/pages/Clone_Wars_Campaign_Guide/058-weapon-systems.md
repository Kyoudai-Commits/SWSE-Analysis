# Weapon Systems

[[Vehicle]] Weapon Systems share many of the same design characteristics as [[Starship]] Weapon Systems, and the two are often interchangeable. This allows [[Vehicle_Weapons]] to be employed against [[Starships]]- as seen at the Battle of [[Geonosis]]- and, of course, lets weapons factories crank out weapons without the need for wholesale retooling of the assembly lines.

Below are a few weapons used by the Republic during [[The_Clone_Wars]]. Some of these are later discontinued but might be available as "military surplus" in later eras.

# The Council of Reassignment

The Council of Reassignment, stationed in the northeast tower of the Jedi Temple on [[Coruscant]], organizes work for the younglings and other Jedi students not chosen to become Padawans. Members of the Council work with the Jedi Service Corps to ensure the fair distribution of members. In addition, the Council reassigns Jedi to other positions and responsibilities as warranted.

The Council of Reassignment is made up of five Jedi Masters: two permanent members, and three rotating members (usually members of the High Council) who serve five-year terms.

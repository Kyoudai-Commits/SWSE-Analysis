# Iridonia

Although it figured prominently in past wars, Iridonia plays only a small part in [[The_Clone_Wars]]. A loyal member of the Republic following [[The_Mandalorian_Wars]], Iridonia as a whole maintains its place in the Senate throughout this turbulent period. Though Iridonia itself is not subject to Separatist invasion, a number of its colonies suffer insurrections and political upheavals inspired by the Confederacy.

Several months after the start of [[The_Clone_Wars]], one of the eight Iridonian colonies petitions to secede from [[The_Galactic_Republic]] and join the Confederacy. This starts what many [[Zabrak]] refer to as "The Great Debate," which eventually ends with Iridonia's continued support of [[The_Galactic_Republic]].

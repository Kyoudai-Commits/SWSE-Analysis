# Species

[[The_Clone_Wars]] are a time of turmoil and strife throughout the galaxy. The number of alien [[Species]] affected by the war number in the thousands. In addition to those systems and [[Species]] that side with either the Republic or the Confederacy, a number of neutral Species, such as the [[Nelvaanians]], are unwittingly drawn into the war.

This chapter presents several new [[Species]] that can be played as heroes in *Star Wars* campaigns, although they are particularly appropriate for [[Clone_Wars_Campaigns]].

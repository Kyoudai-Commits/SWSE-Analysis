# Droid Colors

Unlike the Grand Army's color-coded ranking system, the Separatist's Droid Army only has two rank distinctions: commanders and troops. However, specialized Battle Droids are often painted in other colors so that organic personnel can easily tell them apart. This practice is mainly confined to [[OOM-Series_Battle_Droids]] and [[B1-Series_Battle_Droids]]. Other Droids- such as the [[Droideka_Destroyer_Droid]], [[IG-Series_Lancer_Droid]], and even the [[B2-Series_Super_Battle_Droid]]- do not use the same conventions for insignia.

- **Droid Officer:** Yellow
- **Security Droid:** Red
- **Pilot/Technician Droid:** Blue
- **Battle Droid:** None

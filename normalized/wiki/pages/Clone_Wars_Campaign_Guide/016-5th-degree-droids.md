# 5th-Degree Droids

5th-Degree Droids are Labor and Utility Droids, performing heavy lifting and low-level, repetitive grunt work that many beings in the galaxy prefer to avoid. The main difference between these and other domestic [[Droids]] is that 5th-Degree Droids are not programmed to provide companionship. Instead, they perform the ordered task and report back when it is completed.

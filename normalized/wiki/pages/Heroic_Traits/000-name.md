# Name

Invent or choose a name that fits your character's [[Species]] and [[Heroic_Class]]. A Species' information page typically has some examples of alien names. A name is great way for you to start thinking about your character's background. For instance, a [[Rodian]] [[Scout]] might be named after a great [[Rodian]] hunter of the past, and the [[Rodian]] may be striving to live up to that heritage. Alternatively, the name could be that of an infamous traitor, and the hero could be bent on proving the he or she is not like their namesake.

A name can also tell a lot about a character and help establish an image in your mind and the minds of the other players. It doesn't have to be descriptive, but you want it to fit the type of character you're going to play. Use the sample names to help you make up a name that has the appropriate *Star Wars* feel.

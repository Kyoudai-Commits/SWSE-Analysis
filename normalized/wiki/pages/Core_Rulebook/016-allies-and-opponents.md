# Allies and Opponents

The *Star Wars* universe is home to a dizzying array of sapient [[Species]]. Despite the fact that [[Humans]] have been traveling the stars for tens of thousands of years, and many aliens have been doing the same for an equal amount of time, scouts discover new inhabited worlds all the time. This chapter presents various creatures and characters that GMs can use as potential allies or adversaries for the heroes, from rampaging [[Rancors]] to [[Clone_Troopers]]. Each ally or opponent comes with ready-to-play statistics to make the GM's job a little easier.

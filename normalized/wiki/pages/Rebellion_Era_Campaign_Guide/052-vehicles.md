[[The_Corporate_Sector]] is a section of space in the Tingel Arm, formed just under 500 years ago from a group of several hundred systems lacking intelligent life. [[The_Galactic_Republic]] closely supervised corporate exploitation of the resources found in the sector. Under the control of [[Emperor_Palpatine]], [[The_Corporate_Sector]] was expanded to its current 30,000 star systems, some of which contained native, sentient life. [[The_Corporate_Sector_Authority]] (CSA) was established to manage the sector's business. The Empire has exercised less oversight of [[The_Corporate_Sector]] than did the Republic, and allows the CSA's military and police forces free reign in dealing with the sector's native populations. This freedom has allowed the CSA to maximize profits at the expense of ecosystems and personal rights.

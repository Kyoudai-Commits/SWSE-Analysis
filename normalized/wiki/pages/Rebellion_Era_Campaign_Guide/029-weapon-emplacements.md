# Weapon Emplacements

The Rebel Alliance relies on artillery emplacements to bolster the defenses of major bases and outposts. Fixed emplacements take time to install, and only permanent bases or those most in need of defense are granted this scarce resource. Weapon Emplacements are treated as [[Vehicles]] for statistical purposes, but they are usually immobile and do not require a [[Vehicle_Pilot]] to function. Instead, [[Gunners]] control all the functions of a Weapon Emplacement.

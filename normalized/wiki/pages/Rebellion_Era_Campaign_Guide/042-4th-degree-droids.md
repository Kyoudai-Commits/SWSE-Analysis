# 4th-Degree Droids

The primary 4th-Degree Droids of [[The_Rebellion_Era]] are the Dark Troopers created by General [[Rom_Mohc]] around the time of the Battle of [[Yavin]] and the variant of that model. The original [[Phase_I_Dark_Trooper]] uses Droids rather than living beings wearing powered armor. However, [[Phase_II_Dark_Trooper]] and [[Phase_III_Dark_Trooper]] models are designed to function both as independent [[Droids]] and as wearable suits of armor.

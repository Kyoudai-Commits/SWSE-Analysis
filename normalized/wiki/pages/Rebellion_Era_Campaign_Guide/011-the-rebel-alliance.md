# The Rebel Alliance

DescriptionHistoryOrganizationAllied CommandsAlliance OperativesAlliance PersonnelAlliance PersonalitiesTechnology and Vehicles

The Rebel Alliance is a ray of hope in the blackness of [[The_Dark_Times]]. Outmanned and outgunned, the fighters of the Alliance risk their lives in the effort to free the galaxy from Emperor Palpatine's iron grip. Some Rebels fight for their homeworlds, some fight for their families, and some fight for ideals, but all fight the evils of tyranny. In many ways, The Rebel Alliance defines the *Star Wars* universe. It represents the ultimate battle between good and evil, light and dark.

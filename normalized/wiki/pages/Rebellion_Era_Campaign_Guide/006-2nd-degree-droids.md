# 2nd-Degree Droids

2nd-Degree Droids were the focus of the most impressive technological advances during [[The_Rebellion_Era]], in large part because of the need to maintain older equipment coupled with the increasing importance of [[Starfighter]] combat. Astromech and Repair Droids are strong sellers for all companies that manufacture them, as are kits to repair, upgrade, or modify existing Droids. Many of the most successful 2nd-Degree Droids incorporate simple designs that empathize durability and flexibility.

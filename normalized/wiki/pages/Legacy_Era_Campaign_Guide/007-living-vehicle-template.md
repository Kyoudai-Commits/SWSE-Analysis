# Living Vehicle Template

Used to represent everything from [[Yuuzhan_Vong]] Vehicles to the spacecraft of [[Zonama_Sekot]], the Living Vehicle Template allows you to create statistics for biological analogies of existing ships. Though the template won't present the fine mechanical tuning of designing a [[Vehicle]] from scratch, if you just need a basic Living Vehicle comparable in firepower to an [[X-Wing_Starfighter]], the Living Vehicle Template should suffice.

# For the Gamemaster

This book is an invaluable reference for Gamemasters. GMs can pull statistics straight out of the book for use at the game table without having to write up the Characters themselves. Likewise, GMs can use the Creatures as monstrous foes and minions to pit against their players, while the Droids chapter is an excellent expansion to the [[Droids]] described in the *[[Core_Rulebook]]*.

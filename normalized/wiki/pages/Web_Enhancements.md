During the publication of *Saga Edition*, *Wizards of the Coast* released a number of web articles online that provided additional characters, species, planets, and Gamemaster resources. For convenience sake, these Web Enhancements have been compiled together. Additionally, in collaboration with the *Star Wars Miniatures*, some additional Heroes, Villains, and specialized Equipment were also made available.

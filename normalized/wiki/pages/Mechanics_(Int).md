*Reference Books: [[Core_Rulebook]], [[Scum_and_Villainy]]*

Mechanics is a **Trained Only** skill.

You can bypass locks and traps, set and disarm explosives, fix malfunctioning devices, and modify and repair damaged [[Droids]].

**Special:** You can [[Take_10]] or [[Take_20]] on a Mechanics check. When making a Mechanics check to [[Jury-Rig]], you can't [[Take_20]]. Characters who are untrained in the Mechanics skill can still use the [[Aid_Another]] action to assist on Mechanics checks.

**Retry:** You can usually retry a Mechanics check. In some specific cases, however, a failed Mechanics check has negative ramifications that prevent repeated check (see Disable Device below, for example).

# Aiding Another

The basics of the [[Aid_Another]] Action remain the same in the new version of the rules. Characters still make checks against a DC of 10 to provide a +2 bonus on another character's action. However, two changes to the [[Aid_Another]] rules encourage cooperation in combat.

Characters can now use [[Aid_Another]] to grant a +2 bonus to an ally's attack roll against a target as long as they both have [[Line_of_Sight]] to the target. The target no longer needs to be engaged in melee combat with the ally.

Instead of using [[Aid_Another]] to grant a bonus to an individual ally's [[Defense_Scores]], you can use it to lay down [[Suppressing_Fire]] against an opponent. As a result, the target takes a -2 penalty on attack rolls for 1 round.

These changes make the [[Aid_Another]] Action easier to use in combat, and they provide more legitimate options for characters that have difficulty damaging a target.

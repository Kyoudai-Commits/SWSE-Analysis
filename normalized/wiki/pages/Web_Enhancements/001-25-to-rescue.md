# 25 to Rescue

*By Sterling Hershey*

In the midst of [[The_Clone_Wars]], Separatist invaders lay siege to the planet [[Christophsis]], pounding Republic defenders throughout the planet's crystalline cities. During the fighting, clone forces learn that the enemy has captured 25 technicians and other civilians. In a hastily arranged rescue mission, nearby heroes must rush to save as many prisoners as possible before a [[Starship]] arrives to transport them offworld. With the majority of clone troops and Jedi engaged elsewhere, it's up to the heroes to carry the day on their own.

# Act III

This act is for building to the ultimate point of the story and for wrapping up loose ends. The Heroes should experience increased tribulations, to the point at which they are not sure how they are supposed to survive or succeed, leading up to the climax- the most important or exciting part of your story. From the climax, they move into the denouement, which should tie up all loose ends and answer most, if not all, questions. In general, everything after the climax should be quick.

Now that the Hero has been transformed by their journey, there may a couple of reasons why they wouldn't want to return. They may be excited by their adventure in the special world that they do not want to go back to the ordinary world. Alternatively, they may wonder if they should bring their hard-won ultimate boon back to the ordinary world, who may not be ready for or deserve it.

In *Star Wars*, this typically manifests a little differently. The Hero usually does not have to go back to the ordinary world, but instead make a choice to return to a version of the special world that will become their new ordinary world. What was is gone, but there will be routine in what is to come.

- Luke Skywalker joins [[The_Rebel_Alliance]] as a fighter pilot. This is a far cry from the farm boy he recently was, but he is also no longer a special hero as he was on [[The_Death_Star]].
- Obi-Wan is no longer just the Padawan, nor is the intrepid adventurer that has travelled with Qui-Gon. He is now being relied upon to behave as a Jedi Knight and a full-blown warrior.
- Revan is no longer an amnesiac soldier, nor are they the heroic adventurer. They are now a Jedi Knight, being relied upon by the Jedi Council to destroy everything they built as a Dark Lord.

Oftentimes, the journey to return with the boon is as perilous is the journey to get it. Sometimes the Hero has stirred up the hornet's nest by stealing the ultimate boon and the forces guarding it chase them to get it back. This is often the part of the story where an exciting life-or-death chase occurs.

- Luke and his friends flee [[The_Death_Star]], but they are chased by [[TIE_Fighters]]. They must fend off the [[TIE_Fighters]] until they can get clear and plot a Hyperspace route.
- Obi-Wan and his friends must break through the Trade Federation blockade in order to get to [[Naboo]].
- Revan and their companions must break through the Sith lines guarding [[The_Star_Forge]] and the planet [[Lehon]].

When the Hero began their journey, they needed the guidance of a Mentor in order to prepare them properly to survive and succeed. Sometimes the Hero needs powerful guidance or the help of rescuers to get back from their journey. The forces that oppose them may be too great, now that they have stirred up the hornet's nest, or they may have been weakened by their experience in the special world.

- Strangely, Darth Vader is actually the thing that rescues Luke and his friends. He lets them go, for he has planted a tracking device on [[The_Millennium_Falcon|The Millennium Falcon]] that will lead him to the Rebel's base.
- The [[Gungans]] are Obi-Wan's rescue. In order for the small band of [[Naboo]] security volunteers and the Jedi to infiltrate the palace and capture Nute Gunray, they need the Gungan Army to draw out the Trade Federation's army.
- Revan receives their rescue from the [[Rakatans]] that still hold the old ways. They help Revan disarm the field that prevents ships from reaching [[The_Star_Forge]].

Just as they crossed a threshold to get to the special world, the Hero must also cross a threshold to get back. This can often be difficult for the Hero, as they are returning to the ordinary world and must accept it as the place they belong.

Again, in *Star Wars*, this often manifests a little differently. The Hero is returning to a new ordinary world, distinct from the world in which they began, and they must accept their place in it- which may not be as prominent as the place they occupied in the special world.

Similar to the Refusal of the Call, Luke Skywalker becomes a run-of-the-mill fighter pilot for [[The_Rebel_Alliance]], Obi-Wan becomes an agent of the Jedi Council, and Revan becomes a Jedi Knight in service to the Council, not even a general.

The Hero ultimately triumphs because they can master both the world inside of them and the world outside of them, the special world and the ordinary world, the material world and the spiritual world. During the climax, they reach a new understanding of themselves and the things around them.

- Luke begins to use [[The_Force]] in the trenches of [[The_Death_Star]].
- Obi-Wan masters his fear while facing Darth Maul.
- Revan masters themselves, balancing [[The_Light_Side]] they are with [[The_Dark_Side]] they were.

The Hero's new understanding and mastery leads them to freedom. They now have the freedom to live as they are, no longer afraid of failure or death. Instead of being the Hero of what once was, they become the Hero of now and what is to come.

- Luke uses [[The_Force]] to fire the proton torpedo that destroys [[The_Death_Star]]. By doing so, he also begins to resurrect [[The_Jedi_Order]].
- Obi-Wan conquers his fear and baits Darth Maul into thinking he is defeated- he then kills his assailant. In doing so, he cripples the Sith for the moment and becomes the first guardian against their return.
- Revan rejects their old ways to destroy Darth Malak and [[The_Star_Forge]]. They bring an end to [[The_Sith_Empire]], the very thing they built.

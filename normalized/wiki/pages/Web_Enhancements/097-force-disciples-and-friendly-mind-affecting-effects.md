# Force Disciples and Friendly Mind-Affecting Effects

**Q:** Does a Force Disciple's immunity to Mind-Affecting effects also apply to friendly effects, such as many of the [[Officer_Talents]]?

**A:** A [[Force_Disciple]] is immune to such effects, even if the effects would be helpful. Conceptually, think of [[Force_Disciples]] as having minds that exist on such a transcendental plane of thought that mundane influences simply don't move them enough to make a difference. That said, I don't think it would be unbalancing to allow a [[Force_Disciple]] to raise or lower this immunity (probably a [[Swift_Action]], if you decide to use such a variant as a house rule).

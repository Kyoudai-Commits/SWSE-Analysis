# Far Shot, Accurate Weapon, and Targeting Scope

**Q:** Let's say I have a sniper with the [[Far_Shot]] Feat who [[Aims]] before making a single attack with an [[Accurate]] weapon that has a [[Targeting_Scope]]. Do any of these effects stack with one another?

**A:** [[Far_Shot]], [[Aiming]] with a [[Targeting_Scope]], and using an [[Accurate]] weapon all stack, providing progressively better attacks against distant targets. I can see how the precise interaction might be a bit confusing, so here are the [[Range]] penalties (Point-Blank, Short, Medium, and Long) for each combination:

**Non-[[Accurate]] weapon (such as a [[Blaster_Rifle]]):**

- With no other modifiers: Point-Blank +0, Short -2, Medium -5, Long -10
- With [[Far_Shot]] OR [[Aim]] with a [[Targeting_Scope]]: Point-Blank +0, Short +0, Medium -2, Long -5
- With [[Far_Shot]] AND [[Aim]] with a [[Targeting_Scope]]: Point-Blank +0, Short +0, Medium +0, Long -2

**[[Accurate]] weapon (such as a [[Sporting_Blaster_Rifle]]):**

- With no other modifiers: Point-Blank +0, Short +0, Medium -5, Long -10
- With [[Far_Shot]] OR [[Aim]] with a [[Targeting_Scope]]: Point-Blank +0, Short +0, Medium +0, Long -5
- With [[Far_Shot]] AND [[Aim]] with a [[Targeting_Scope]]: Point-Blank +0, Short +0, Medium +0, Long +0

Thus, if a trained sniper with the [[Far_Shot]] Feat uses a good sniping weapon (anything with the [[Accurate]] quality plus a mounted [[Targeting_Scope]]) and takes the time to [[Aim]], that sniper can attack a target at Long [[Range]] without taking a penalty on the attack roll.

# Armor

Armor works differently in *Saga Edition*, providing a bonus to [[Reflex_Defense]] (and sometimes [[Fortitude_Defense]]) instead of Damage Reduction.

The Armor bonus to [[Reflex_Defense]] is equal to the armor's previous Damage Reduction. Add +1 to the Armor bonus if the suit had a DR of 3 or more, add +2 to the Armor bonus if the suit had a DR of 5 or more, and add +3 to the Armor bonus if the suit had a DR of 7 or more.

Most full-body armor (that is, covering the whole body, including the head and face) provides an [[Equipment]] bonus to [[Fortitude_Defense]]. This bonus is +1 for an Armor bonus of +4 or less, +2 for an Armor bonus of +5 to +8, +3 for an Armor bonus of +9, and +4 for an Armor bonus of +10.

Most armor has a Maximum [[Dexterity]] Bonus equal to (12 - Armor bonus)/2, rounded down.

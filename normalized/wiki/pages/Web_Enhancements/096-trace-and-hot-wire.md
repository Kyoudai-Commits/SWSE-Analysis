# Trace and Hot Wire

**Q:** If my [[Scoundrel]] takes both **[[Trace]]** and **[[Hot_Wire]]**, can they make a [[Mechanics]] check in place of [[Gather_Information]] as long as they have access to a computer terminal that they can hot-wire?

**A:** No. **[[Hot_Wire]]** works only when [[Improving_Access]] to a computer (that is, when you're trying to improve the computer's [[Use_Computer|Attitude]] toward you). Making [[Use_Computer]] checks in place of [[Gather_Information]] checks is a completely separate use of the Skill, so [[Mechanics]] can't be substituted in that case.

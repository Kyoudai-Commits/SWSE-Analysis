# Equilibrium and Persistent Conditions

**Q:** Does the **[[Equilibrium]]** Talent move you to the top of the [[Condition_Track]], or does it remove [[Persistent_Conditions]] (such as from [[Poison]], [[Disease]], or serious injury)?

**A:** **[[Equilibrium]]** does both of these, removing all [[Persistent_Conditions]] *and* moving you to the top of the [[Condition_Track]].

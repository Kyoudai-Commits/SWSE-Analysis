# Unarmed Weapon Group

**Q:** What [[Weapon_Group]] includes [[Unarmed_Attacks]]? How about Natural Weapons? In most places, it seems like they're [[Simple_Weapons]], but [[Weapon_Focus]] refers to [[Unarmed_Attacks]] as if they are separate.

**A:** [[Unarmed_Attacks]] and Natural Weapons are considered [[Simple_Weapons]] in which all characters are proficient. The reference to [[Unarmed_Attacks]] in the [[Weapon_Focus]] Feat is an error.

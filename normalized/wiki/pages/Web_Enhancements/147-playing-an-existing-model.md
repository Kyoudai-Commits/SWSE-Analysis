# Playing an Existing Model

Players who want to run [[Droid_Heroes]] might wish to choose an iconic [[Droid]] type. The [[Droids]] chapter of the *[[Core_Rulebook]]* presents [[R2-Series_Astromech_Droids]], [[3PO-Series_Protocol_Droids]], and a number of other standard [[Droid_Models]]. Though not all [[Droids]] in the book are suitable for heroic play, a great many of them are.

In terms of game mechanics, this option is very similar to the rules in previous editions. The specific [[Droid_Model]] you choose determines your [[Ability_Scores]], size, class, and level. If you choose a [[Droid_Model]] that has 1 or 2 levels in the [[Nonheroic]] class, you can add 1 level of a [[Heroic_Class]] of your choice. Otherwise, you can play a [[Droid]] with 3 [[Nonheroic]] levels or 1 Heroic level, and character generation proceeds as normal.

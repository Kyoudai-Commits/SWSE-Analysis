# Playing a New Model

In *Saga Edition*, you can also create a new custom [[Droid_Model]], choosing its [[Ability_Scores]], [[Heroic_Classes]], [[Skills]], [[Feats]], [[Talents]], and [[Droid_Systems]]. This option allows you to set the Droid Model's [[Ability_Scores]], select its "degree" (a designation based on its role), and give it weapons and equipment that define the equivalent of a new stock model of that [[Droid]].

For example, let's say you want to play a combat-oriented [[Droid]]. You could make use of the statistics for the [[B1-Series_Battle_Droid]] or [[B2-Series_Super_Battle_Droid]], but that might not fit your vision. Instead, you can create a new [[Droid_Model]], assign [[Ability_Scores]] that are geared more closely to your desired role (in this case, you'd probably give the [[Droid]] high [[Strength]] and [[Dexterity]] scores), pick a standard class (such as [[Soldier]]), and continue with character generation as normal. The end result- the 1st-level character that you create- becomes a new stock model of [[Droid]], and your particular character becomes a heroic individual [[Droid]] that has a role to play in galactic events.

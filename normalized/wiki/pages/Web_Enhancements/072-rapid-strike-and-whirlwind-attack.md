# Rapid Strike and Whirlwind Attack

**Q:** Reading the rules, it seems that you can use [[Rapid_Strike]] in conjunction with [[Whirlwind_Attack]], gaining an extra die of damage against all targets. Is this correct?

**A:** No, they don't work together, nor will [[Rapid_Shot]] and [[Autofire]]. The text of both [[Rapid_Strike]] and [[Rapid_Shot]] mention that they work with a single attack roll against a single target. [[Area_Attacks]] (such as [[Whirlwind_Attack]]) deal with multiple targets, so they are not eligible.

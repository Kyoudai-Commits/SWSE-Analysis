# Atonement and Force Point Recovery

**Q:** If you have [[Force_Point_Recovery]] and you atone to reduce your [[Dark_Side_Score]], do you get the [[Force_Point]] back?

**A:** No. [[Force_Points]] that you sacrifice for [[Atonement]] are permanently lost and cannot be recovered by any means.

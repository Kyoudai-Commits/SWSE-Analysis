# Knights of the Old Republic: Creatures of KOTOR

*By Eric Cagle*

[[The_Old_Republic_Era]] is a time of heroic deeds, powerful villains, and intricate plots. However, these are not the only threats that heroes of the era face. Every planet is home to a bewildering number of dangerous creatures- so many, in fact, that there wasn't room to put them in the *[[Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide]]*. "Creatures of KOTOR" is a new series of articles presenting new creatures and beasts from that era to include in your *Star Wars Roleplaying Game Saga Edition* adventures.

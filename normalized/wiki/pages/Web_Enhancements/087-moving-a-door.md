# Moving a Door

**Q:** What happens if you use *[[Move_Object]]* against a door? How about *[[Force_Thrust]]*?

**A:** If you use *[[Move_Object]]* against a door, you deal normal damage to it as the door strains against its frame. (Don't forget to apply the effects of the door's [[Damage_Reduction]].) If the damage is sufficient to reduce the door to 0 [[Hit_Points]], it becomes [[Disabled]] and the door is pulled free, making the doorway passable.

If you use *[[Force_Thrust]]* against a door, compare your [[Use_the_Force]] check result to the door's [[Break_DC]]. If successful, you have forced the door open. If not, the door remains intact and functional.

For those who are interested, an object's [[Break_DC]] is calculated as if it were a character resisting a [[Bantha_Rush]] or *[[Force_Thrust]]*. Take its [[Strength]] bonus, add 10 (the average result on a roll of 1d20, rounded down), and- in most cases- add 5 more for being exceptionally stable. (Some particularly fragile objects may deviate on this last detail, but that is fairly rare.)

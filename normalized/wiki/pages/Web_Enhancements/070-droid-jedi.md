# Droid Jedi

**Q:** The rules for [[Droids]] say that you can't select [[Jedi]] as your class at 1st level. Given the ruling above, could a [[Droid]] Multiclass and take levels in [[Jedi]] after 1st level?

**A:** Yes. In fact, in *Shadows of the Empire*, [[Darth_Vader]] had some customized [[ASP_Labor_Droids]] that were modified to serve as [[Lightsaber]]-sparring partners. Such [[Droids]] could be represented in the game by having at least some levels in the [[Jedi]] class to pick up [[Lightsaber_Combat_Talents]].

# Recovering Temporary Force Points

**Q:** If you gain a temporary [[Force_Point]] (such as with the **[[Fringe_Savant]]** Talent) and you also have the [[Force_Point_Recovery]] Force Technique, can you gain a [[Force_Point]] by "recovering" the temporary [[Force_Point]] that you spent?

**A:** No. Temporary [[Force_Points]] are just that- temporary- and they cannot be recovered by any means after the end of the encounter.

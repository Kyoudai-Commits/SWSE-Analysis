# Double and Rapid Attacks

**Q:** It is possible to use [[Double_Attack]] with [[Rapid_Strike]]?

**A:** Yes. [[Rapid_Strike]], [[Rapid_Shot]], and [[Burst_Fire]] all can be used when making multiple attacks, regardless of their source ([[Double_Attack]], [[Triple_Attack]], or fighting with multiple weapons).

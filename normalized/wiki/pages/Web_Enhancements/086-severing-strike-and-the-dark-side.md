# Severing Strike and the Dark Side

**Q:** Would **[[Severing_Strike]]** be a [[Major_Transgression]]? It seems that you'd never learn this [[Talent]] unless you intended to maim others.

**A:** No, **[[Severing_Strike]]**, in itself, is not a [[Dark_Side_Transgression]] because it is designed to protect your target from an otherwise lethal blow. The same would be true for a doctor who has to perform a medical amputation. Although the act is "maiming" in the strictest sense, it is only to protect the target from a more dangerous medical problem.

This doesn't mean that **[[Severing_Strike]]** can't be used in a blatantly evil way, of course. Cutting off the limbs of an enemy who is at your mercy would still be tantamount to torture no matter how you do it (by [[Lightsaber]], scalpel, or angry [[Wookiee]]).

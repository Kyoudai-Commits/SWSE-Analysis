# Behind the Threat: The Sith

Part 1: PortrayalPart 2: The BecomingPart 3: DesignPart 4: The Teeth in the DarknessPart 5: Antiheroes

*By Matthew Grau*

[[The_Sith]]. The very name has haunted [[The_Jedi_Order]] for millennia. This cult of Force practitioners has known both absolute power and unconscionable self-destruction. Indeed, they are something of an enigma- nightmares to all, but to themselves, a natural expression of [[The_Force]].

It is no surprise that many players are drawn to the idea of exploring [[The_Dark_Side]] and creating Sith characters. Although *[[Threats_of_the_Galaxy]]* examines [[The_Sith]] as enemies, this series of articles will explore the different aspects involved in developing, portraying, and using compelling and unique Sith characters in five eras of the *Star Wars* saga.

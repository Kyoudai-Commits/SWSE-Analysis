# Cargo Pods and Emplacements Points

**Q:** Can you gain [[Emplacement_Points]] by converting the cargo space provided by a [[Cargo_Pod]]?

**A:** No. A [[Starship]] can only convert its own internal cargo space into [[Emplacement_Points]].

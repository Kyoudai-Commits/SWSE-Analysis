# Tinkering With Your Droid

The *Saga Edition* rules make it simpler and more logical to tinker with [[Droids]]. To modify a Droid's hardware, characters (including [[Droids]] themselves) use the [[Mechanics]] Skill. To reprogram a Droid's software, they turn to the [[Use_Computer]] Skill. In keeping with the goal of making [[Droids]] more suitable for heroic use, [[Reprogramming]] a [[Droid]] requires a character to overcome the Droid's [[Will_Defense]]- a sign that you have penetrated the Droid's software defenses. This ensures that [[Droid_Heroes]] are more resistant to [[Reprogramming]] than stock models.

*By Rodney Thompson*

Now that you've seen previews of basic and advanced combat, let's put it all together to see how a scene might unfold. This preview breaks down the skirmish at the Great Pit of Carkoon from *Return of the Jedi*, when [[Luke_Skywalker]] rescued his friends from the clutches of the vile gangster [[Jabba_the_Hutt]].

This combat example focuses on the events taking place outside during the battle. The interior struggles, including [[Princess_Leia]] strangling the bloated [[Hutt]], are taking place at the same time, but to keep this example simple, we left them out (though Leia does enter the scene in round 12). Additionally, all of Jabba the Hutt's thugs use the generic [[Thug]] statistics from the *[[Core_Rulebook]]* and don't differentiate between the [[Species]].

# Aiming

*Saga Edition* includes the ability to [[Aim]]. When [[Aiming]] with a ranged weapon, you can take two consecutive [[Swift_Actions]] in a round to line up the shot carefully. On your next attack, you ignore all [[Cover]] bonuses to your target's [[Reflex_Defense]], provided that you don't lose [[Line_of_Sight]] to your target or take any other [[Actions]] before making your attack. Furthermore, you can enhance your attacks with the [[Careful_Shot]] Feat and the [[Deadeye]] Feat, which provide benefits such as increased attack bonuses and extra damage.

# Jedi

- **Starting HP:** Jedi begin play at 1st level with a number of Hit Points equal to 30 + Constitution modifier. At each level after 1st, Jedi gain 1d10 Hit Points + their Constitution modifier. · **Trained skills:** 2+Int
- **Defenses:** Fort +1; Ref +1; Will +1
- **Starting feats:** Force Sensitivity, Weapon Proficiency (Lightsabers), Weapon Proficiency (Simple Weapons); Force Points: Jedi gain a number of Force Points equal to 5 + one-half their Character Level (rounded down) at 1st level, and every time they gain a new level in this Heroic Class. Any Force Points left over from the previous levels are lost.; Lightsaber: A Jedi begins play with a Lightsaber provided by their Master. Later, they can build their own (see Lightsaber Construction).; Credits: A 1st-level Jedi starts play with 3d4 x 100 credits
- **Talent trees:** Jedi Consular; Jedi Guardian; Jedi Sentinel; Lightsaber Combat
- **Class skills:** Acrobatics; Endurance; Initiative; Jump; Knowledge (all skills, taken individually); Mechanics; Perception; Pilot; Use the Force (Force Sensitive)

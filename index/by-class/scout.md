# Scout

- **Starting HP:** Scouts begin play at 1st level with a number of Hit Points equal to 24 + Constitution modifier. At each level after 1st, Scouts gain 1d8 Hit Points + their Constitution modifier. · **Trained skills:** 5+Int
- **Defenses:** Fort +1; Ref +2
- **Starting feats:** Shake It Off (must have the prerequisite Constitution of 13 and be Trained in Endurance), Weapon Proficiency (Pistols), Weapon Proficiency (Rifles), Weapon Proficiency (Simple Weapons); Force Points: Scouts gain a number of Force Points equal to 5 + one-half their Character Level (rounded down) at 1st level, and every time they gain a new level in this Heroic Class. Any Force Points left over from the previous levels are lost.; Credits: A 1st-level Scout starts play with 3d4 x 250 credits
- **Talent trees:** Awareness; Camouflage; Fringer; Survivor; Hyperspace Explorer; Spy; Reconnaissance; Surveillance; Versatility; Unpredictable; Advance Patrol; Espionage; Master Scout; Mobile Scout
- **Class skills:** Climb, Endurance, Initiative, Jump, Knowledge (all; taken individually), Mechanics, Perception, Pilot, Ride, Stealth, Survival, Swim

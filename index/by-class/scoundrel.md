# Scoundrel

- **Starting HP:** Scoundrels begin play at 1st level with a number of Hit Points equal to 18 + Constitution modifier. At each level after 1st, Scoundrels gain 1d6 Hit Points + their Constitution modifier. · **Trained skills:** 4+Int
- **Defenses:** Ref +2; Will +1
- **Starting feats:** Point-Blank Shot, Weapon Proficiency (Pistols), Weapon Proficiency (Simple Weapons); Force Points: Scoundrels gain a number of Force Points equal to 5 + one-half their Character Level (rounded down) at 1st level, and every time they gain a new level in this Heroic Class. Any Force Points left over from the previous levels are lost.; Credits: A 1st-level Scoundrel starts play with 3d4 x 250 credits
- **Talent trees:** Fortune; Misfortune; Slicer; Spacer; Outlaw Tech; Malkite Poisoner; Run and Gun; Smuggling; Opportunist; Recklessness; Brigand; Revolutionary; Outsider
- **Class skills:** Acrobatics, Deception, Gather Information, Initiative, Knowledge (all; taken individually), Mechanics, Perception, Persuasion, Pilot, Stealth, Use Computer

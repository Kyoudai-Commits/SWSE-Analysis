# Noble

- **Starting HP:** Nobles begin play at 1st level with a number of Hit Points equal to 18 + Constitution modifier. At each level after 1st, Nobles gain 1d6 Hit Points + their Constitution modifier. · **Trained skills:** 6+Int
- **Defenses:** Ref +1; Will +2
- **Starting feats:** Linguist (must have the prerequisite Intelligence of 13), Weapon Proficiency (Pistols), Weapon Proficiency (Simple Weapons); Force Points: Nobles gain a number of Force Points equal to 5 + one-half their Character Level (rounded down) at 1st level, and every time they gain a new level in this Heroic Class. Any Force Points left over from the previous levels are lost.; Credits: A 1st-level Noble starts play with 3d4 x 400 credits
- **Talent trees:** Influence; Inspiration; Leadership; Lineage; Fencing; Ideologue; Disgrace; Collaborator; Loyal Protector; Provocateur; Gambling Leader; Anticipation; Master of Intrigue; Skill Challenge; Superior Skills; Exile
- **Class skills:** Deception, Gather Information, Initiative, Knowledge (all; taken individually), Perception, Persuasion, Pilot, Ride, Treat Injury, Use Computer

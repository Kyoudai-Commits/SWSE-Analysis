# The Order of Shasa

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Order_of_Shasa](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Order_of_Shasa) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:04:18Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

The Order of Shasa is a new [Force Tradition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Tradition "Force Tradition"), formed just after the end of [The Jedi Civil War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Civil_War "The Jedi Civil War"). Named for its founder, the Order is exclusive to the planet [Manaan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Manaan "Manaan") and its native Species, the [Selkath](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Selkath "Selkath"). Toward the end of [The Jedi Civil War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Civil_War "The Jedi Civil War"), [Shasa](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shasa "Shasa") and a number of other young [Selkath](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Selkath "Selkath") are lured to the Sith Embassy, where they receive Sith training. Their training is short-lived. [Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Revan "Revan") intervenes, proving to [Shasa](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shasa "Shasa") and the others that [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") are distorting the truth and setting them up to serve as a puppet government under Sith control.

After the war ends, [Shasa](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shasa "Shasa") realizes that if [Manaan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Manaan "Manaan") is to remain truly neutral in the coming years, the [Selkath](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Selkath "Selkath") must not rely on [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") anymore than they rely on the corrupt Sith. However, Force-sensitive [Selkath](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Selkath "Selkath") still need training and guidance to develop their powers and avoid [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"). [Shasa](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shasa "Shasa") and the other Sith-trained [Selkath](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Selkath "Selkath") develop a doctrine of peace and defense, creating an organization initially known as The Order of Shasa. With few members formally training in the ways of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), the Order rapidly discovers that Dark Side temptations are a constant threat. Those with Sith training have difficulty disregarding their earlier lessons, especially when they were so powerful, useful, and tempting.

The Order of Shasa struggles to resolve the conflict between ideals derived from [The Light Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Light_Side "The Light Side") and techniques based on [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"), seeking a path of moderation between the light and dark. [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") deception fresh in her mind, [Shasa](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shasa "Shasa") has avoided contacting [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") or any other [Force Tradition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Tradition "Force Tradition") for aid. However, if Dark Side proponents make significant advances or begin to influence new members of the Order, she will not hesitate to seek advice from a Jedi she trusts, or from another [Force Tradition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Tradition "Force Tradition") with a good reputation, such as [The Jal Shey](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jal_Shey "The Jal Shey").

Because the Order is so new, initiates undergo little formal training; those teaching have little more experience than those learning. However, [Shasa](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shasa "Shasa") has instituted one formal initiation requirement- the Rite of Fira. Students deemed ready to become full members of the Order are sent on a special quest that serves as a final test of their abilities, reminding them of the reason the Order was founded and providing them with a weapon for personal defense. The Rite of Fira requires the initiate to swim to the wreck of the kolto harvester destroyed by [Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Revan "Revan") in the Hrakert Rift. After removing a piece of metal from its cortosis-laden hull plating, the initiate must then use the heat from volcanic vents on the ocean floor to craft the cortosis into a [Fira](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fira "Fira")- a curved, [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber")-resistant sword, which members of the Order can use to defend themselves against possible Sith- or even Jedi- threats.

Members of The Order of Shasa can select [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") from the [Order of Shasa Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Order_of_Shasa_Talent_Tree "Order of Shasa Talent Tree").

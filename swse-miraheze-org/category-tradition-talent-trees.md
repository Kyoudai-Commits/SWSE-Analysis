# Category : Tradition Talent Trees

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Tradition_Talent_Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Tradition_Talent_Trees) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:03:48Z.

When a character who has the [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity") feat would normally gain a [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") (such as from advancing to an odd-numbered level in a Heroic Class), he or she can instead select a [Force Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Talent "Force Talent") from among those from a Force Talent Tree. A Force Talent Tree is any [Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent_Tree "Talent Tree") that isn't limited to a particular [Heroic Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Class "Heroic Class") or [Prestige Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Class "Prestige Class") and require the [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity") feat to access its Talents.

If a character who has the [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity") feat is a member of a [Force Tradition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Tradition "Force Tradition"), he or she can instead select a Force Talent from that tradition's individual Force Talent Tree. The [Force Disciple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Disciple "Force Disciple"), [Jedi Master](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Master "Jedi Master"), and [Sith Lord](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Lord "Sith Lord") Prestige Classes all state that they can select [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") from any Tradition Talent Tree. Talent Trees belonging to the various [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions") are considered Force Talent Trees, as they are non-class specific but require [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity") to access.

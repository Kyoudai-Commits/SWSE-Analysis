# Cesta

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cesta](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cesta) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:07:44Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

Another [Gungan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gungan "Gungan") weapon, the Cesta is a flexible pole used to hurl small [Energy Balls](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Energy_Balls "Energy Balls"). It can also be used as a stafflike melee weapon. A Cesta is a melee weapon with a [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach") of 2 squares. Although the Cesta is considered an [Exotic Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon "Exotic Weapon"), [Gungans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gungans "Gungans") are treated as proficient if they have the [Weapon Proficiency (Simple Weapons)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Simple_Weapons) "Weapon Proficiency (Simple Weapons)") feat.

**Weapon Type:** [Exotic Weapons (Melee)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapons_(Melee) "Exotic Weapons (Melee)")

**Size:** Large

**Cost:** 100

**Damage:** 2d4

**[Stun](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stun "Stun") Setting:** NO

**Weight:** 1.8 Kilograms

**Type:** Bludgeoning

**Availability:** [Rare](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rare "Rare")

**Special:** Can be used to hurl [Energy Balls](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Energy_Balls "Energy Balls")

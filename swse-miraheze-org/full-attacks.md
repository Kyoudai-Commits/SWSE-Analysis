# Full Attacks

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full_Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full_Attacks) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:05:58Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

As a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"), you can make more than one [Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack "Attack"). To gain extra attacks, you must be wielding two weapons, wielding a [Double Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Weapon "Double Weapon"), or using a special ability that grants extra attacks. When making multiple attacks, you may resolve your attacks in any order desired, declaring the target of each attack immediately before making the attack roll.

Extra attacks granted from different sources are cumulative. Any penalties associated with gaining an extra attack apply to all attacks that the character makes until the start of their next turn.

A [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot") who spends at least a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action") every round moving their [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") cannot make a Full Attack Action, unless the Vehicle has already been brought to a [Full Stop](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full_Stop "Full Stop").

**Q:** It seems as if the number of attacks available to most characters is very low, especially for [Gunslingers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunslingers "Gunslingers") or other heroes who charge into battle with blasters blazing. In the *Star Wars* movies, most characters seem to fire multiple shots per second, but in *Saga Edition*, most characters make only one shot every six seconds. Even a high level [Gunslinger](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunslinger "Gunslinger") using [Triple Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Triple_Attack "Triple Attack"), [Rapid Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapid_Shot "Rapid Shot"), and two [Blaster Pistols](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Blaster_Pistols "Blaster Pistols") is really making only eight shots (one every 0.75 seconds), which still seems to fall short of the cinematic pace seen in the movies. Was this intentional?

**A:** This is one of those places where you have to separate the cinematic description of a blaster fight from the more abstract game mechanics used to resolve it. For example, in a melee fight, each combatant is assumed to be making multiple swings over the course of a round even though each rolls only one attack. Similarly, two gunslingers in a blaster fight might be pulling the trigger two to three times per second even though they roll only one attack (or more, depending on their [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"), [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents"), and so on).

This might seem straightforward enough, but many players are thrown off by the fact that ranged weapons such as blasters usually have a limited number of shots of ammunition. If the gunslingers are "really" pulling the trigger so many times, why don't they use extra shots?

The answer is that you should think of a ranged weapon's ammunition count as representing the number of attacks it allows before reloading, not the number of times a character physically pulls the trigger. In other words, it's an abstract game mechanic that applies to another abstract game mechanic, not the cinematic interpretation of the fight itself. Either way, the net amount of power used is the same whether you make a flurry of low-powered shots or a single concentrated blast, whichever is appropriate for that character's style and the weapon's design.

Thus, a sniper like the one described earlier would almost certainly make carefully aimed single shots wherein each blast is at full strength. On the other hand, an infantry soldier might make short, controlled bursts with their [Blaster Rifle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Blaster_Rifle "Blaster Rifle") on a single trigger pull. A gunslinger might fill the air with multiple shots as they repeatedly pulls the trigger over a few seconds. You should always feel free to describe your character's style of attack in whatever way seems most appropriate, cinematic, and fun.

## Dual Weapon Mastery

The [Dual Weapon Mastery I](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dual_Weapon_Mastery_I "Dual Weapon Mastery I"), [Dual Weapon Mastery II](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dual_Weapon_Mastery_II "Dual Weapon Mastery II"), and [Dual Weapon Mastery III](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dual_Weapon_Mastery_III "Dual Weapon Mastery III") feats reduce the -10 penalty on attack rolls when fighting with two weapons or both ends of a [Double Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Weapon "Double Weapon"). (The penalty is respectively reduced to a -5 penalty, a -2 penalty, and no penalty.)

### Attacking with Two Weapons

As a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"), a character armed with two weapons can attack once with each weapon, but the character takes a -10 penalty on all attacks for the Round. This penalty assumes that the character is proficient with the weapon in hand; apply an additional -5 penalty on the attack roll if the character is not proficient with the weapon.

A character armed with three or more weapons still only gains one extra attack, but that character may choose which weapon it wishes to use for this extra attack each round.

### Attacking with a Double Weapon

As a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"), a character armed with a [Double Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Weapon "Double Weapon") (such as a [Double-Bladed Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double-Bladed_Lightsaber "Double-Bladed Lightsaber")) can attack once with each end of the weapon, but the character takes a -10 penalty penalty on all attacks for the Round. This penalty assumes that the character is proficient with the weapon in hand; apply an additional -5 penalty on the attack roll if the character is not proficient with the weapon. (A character who chooses to attack with only one end of a double weapon can do so as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action").)

## Double Attack and Triple Attack

The [Double Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Attack "Double Attack") feat allows a character to make one extra attack during a Full Attack, but the character takes a -5 penalty on all attacks until the start of their next turn. The [Triple Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Triple_Attack "Triple Attack") feat allows a character to make one extra attack in addition to that granted by the [Double Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Attack "Double Attack") feat, giving the character an additional -5 penalty (for a total -10 penalty) on all attacks until the start of their next turn.

If the character is armed with more than one weapon (or uses a [Double Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Weapon "Double Weapon")), they may choose which weapon (or weapons) they will choose to make each extra attack.

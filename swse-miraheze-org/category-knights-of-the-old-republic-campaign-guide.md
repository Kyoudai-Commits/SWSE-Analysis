Journey back nearly four thousand years before the Battle of Yavin to a time when the Republic struggled for survival against the threats of the Sith and the Mandalorians. The *Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide* gives you everything you need to play in the time of the Mandalorian Wars and the Jedi Civil War. Play a noble Jedi struggling against the Sith, or a Republic soldier fighting to keep the Mandalorian hordes from sweeping across the galaxy like a storm.

## [The Old Republic Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic_Era "The Old Republic Era")

**4,000 to 3,951 Years before *A New Hope***

During the era of [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic"), Jedi battle against Sith Lords, Mandalorian warriors raid Outer Rim worlds, and the Republic struggles to fight off enemies at every turn. Smugglers dodge Sith warships and bluff their way past Republic customs agents, while bounty hunters and mercenaries play both sides of every conflict for the greatest profit. Soldiers fight on distant battlefields and defend planets close to home. Vast empires rise, fall, and are reborn. This is the time of The Old Republic Era, and it is one of the most exciting periods in all *Star Wars* continuity.

In these years of conflict, your heroes have limitless opportunities to become part of the galaxy's salvation- or it's destruction. It is a time of constant warfare, when peace has become little more than a memory and survival is threatened by armies in all parts of the galaxy. It is a time when [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") are in abundance, fighting on all fronts, only to be whittled down to near extinction over the course of several conflicts. It is a time when civilization is under siege; warlords and Force-users struggle to unmake the Republic and sow the seeds of chaos. It is a time of mysterious enemies and sudden betrayals, when allies turn to foes, and you never see the [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") until it is buried in your back. It is a time when new powers are on the rise, and anyone can ascend to greatness.

An [Old Republic Era Campaign](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Old_Republic_Era_Campaign "Old Republic Era Campaign") calls for heroes. A hero might be a Jedi Knight, sworn to protect the Republic but reeling from losses in previous wars. Another hero might be a Republic soldier on the front lines of battle, fighting with every ounce of effort to hold the line against Mandalorian invaders. One hero might be an arms dealer smuggling weapons to resistance cells on Sith-controlled worlds, while another might be a Hyperspace explorer that stumbles across a secret rallying point for the forces of the Republic. A hero might be a corporate agent overseeing the construction of vital defense systems, or a Jedi Shadow working behind the scenes to bring down a sinister villain. Whatever your hero's history and speciality, an [Old Republic Era Campaign](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Old_Republic_Era_Campaign "Old Republic Era Campaign") is rife with opportunities to send ripples of influence throughout the galaxy.

## Species

In the days of [The Old Republic Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic_Era "The Old Republic Era"), as in all *Star Wars* eras, a variety of [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") thrive throughout the galaxy. Even though [Humans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Humans "Humans") dominate the galactic governments and corporations, many other [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") hold prominent positions of power. The colonization of the galaxy expands during this era, however the number of inhabited worlds is comparatively fewer than in the classic era. Less common [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") live on Republic worlds, but they are likely to be fewer in number, and reside in their own enclaves.

Relations between [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") are more varied than during the classic era. Some worlds have few conflicts, with each [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") considering others more or less their equal. On other planets, [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") in the minority might be restricted to certain areas or have fewer rights than the dominant [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species"). This might be true even on planets that consider themselves to be among the most civilized in the galaxy. Non-Human [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") on [Taris](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Taris "Taris"), for instance, are not welcome in the Upper City during certain periods of this era. Characters might find their efforts and adventures hampered by such attitudes, but players might find such conflicts useful points for their character backgrounds.

Any of the common [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") available during [The Rise of the Empire Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Rise_of_the_Empire_Era "The Rise of the Empire Era") are suitable for an [Old Republic Era Campaign](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Old_Republic_Era_Campaign "Old Republic Era Campaign"). However, primitive Species such as [Gungans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gungans "Gungans") and [Ewoks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ewoks "Ewoks") leave their homeworlds only in rare and unusual circumstances. The remaining [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") presented in the *[Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*, along with the [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") presented in this chapter, are all appropriate for an [Old Republic Era Campaign](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Old_Republic_Era_Campaign "Old Republic Era Campaign").

## [Old Republic Era Heroes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Old_Republic_Era_Heroes "Old Republic Era Heroes")

[The Old Republic Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic_Era "The Old Republic Era") provides exciting story opportunities for all character types, especially Jedi and other Force-users. This section contains information for creating heroes tailored to this era, including story considerations, new ideas and talents for the standard [Heroic Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Classes "Heroic Classes"), plus new [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") and expanded [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills") usable by all [Heroic Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Classes "Heroic Classes"). These volatile times- when it is difficult to distinguish friend from foe - also call for new heroes to fulfill their destinies.

In the *Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide*, characters are assumed to be good, fighting against the forces of evil. It is a time of wide-ranging open warfare, with shifting fronts and loyalties. Heroes can face off against Mandalorian or Sith invaders. Other enemies might not be so clear-cut, especially if the campaign takes place during [The Jedi Civil War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Civil_War "The Jedi Civil War"). Although it is possible to play darker characters using these rules, the forces of good remain the focus of this book.

## Prestige Classes

[Prestige Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Classes "Prestige Classes") enable characters to specialize by gaining new [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") and abilities unavailable to other [Heroic Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Classes "Heroic Classes"). [Prestige Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Classes "Prestige Classes") allow players to develop their characters around specific concepts, roles, or character types. [Prestige Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Classes "Prestige Classes") are optional, and the Gamemaster has the final say on which, if any, are allowed in the campaign.

All [Prestige Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Classes "Prestige Classes") are suitable for player or Gamemaster use. To qualify for a [Prestige Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Class "Prestige Class"), a character must meet all prerequisites, including story factors and statistical requirements. Players and Gamemasters should work together to generate adventures and storylines that allow a player to take advantage of these [Prestige Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Classes "Prestige Classes").

New Talents for Existing Prestige ClassesNew Prestige Classes

This chapter features three new [Prestige Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Classes "Prestige Classes") suitable for an [Old Republic Era Campaign](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Old_Republic_Era_Campaign "Old Republic Era Campaign") and also includes several new [Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent_Trees "Talent Trees") for existing [Prestige Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Classes "Prestige Classes"). Like those new [Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent_Trees "Talent Trees") for [Heroic Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Classes "Heroic Classes") ([Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi"), [Noble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble "Noble"), [Scoundrel](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrel "Scoundrel"), [Scout](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scout "Scout"), [Soldier](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier "Soldier")) in [Old Republic Era Heroes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Old_Republic_Era_Heroes "Old Republic Era Heroes"), any character taking a level in the designated [Prestige Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Class "Prestige Class") that would normally grant the Talent can take one of these Talents. Even though a [Prestige Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Class "Prestige Class") description might not mention these [Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent_Trees "Talent Trees") specifically, they are considered to be a part of the list of suitable [Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent_Trees "Talent Trees") for that [Prestige Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Class "Prestige Class").

For example, in the description of Talents granted to the [Elite Trooper](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Elite_Trooper "Elite Trooper") Prestige Class that appear in the *[Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*, the text states that a character can choose [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") from the [Weapon Master Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Master_Talent_Tree "Weapon Master Talent Tree"), the [Commando Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Commando_Talent_Tree "Commando Talent Tree"), or the [Camouflage Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Camouflage_Talent_Tree "Camouflage Talent Tree"). This chapter presents the [Mandalorian Warrior Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mandalorian_Warrior_Talent_Tree "Mandalorian Warrior Talent Tree") for the [Elite Trooper](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Elite_Trooper "Elite Trooper"), which is considered to be on the list of [Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent_Trees "Talent Trees") for the [Elite Trooper](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Elite_Trooper "Elite Trooper") just like the other three. Any [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") that relies on a character's Class Level considers the levels of all [Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Classes "Classes") that have access to that Talent cumulative.

However, none of the new [Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent_Trees "Talent Trees") granted to [Heroic Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Classes "Heroic Classes") in [Old Republic Era Heroes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Old_Republic_Era_Heroes "Old Republic Era Heroes") are added to the list of approved [Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent_Trees "Talent Trees") for that [Prestige Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Class "Prestige Class") unless specifically noted in that Talent Tree's description. Though the [Elite Trooper](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Elite_Trooper "Elite Trooper") has access to the [Commando Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Commando_Talent_Tree "Commando Talent Tree") of the [Soldier](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier "Soldier") Heroic Class, they do not gain access to the [Rocket Jumper Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rocket_Jumper_Talent_Tree "Rocket Jumper Talent Tree") even though it is considered a [Soldier](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier "Soldier") Talent Tree. New [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") for existing [Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent_Trees "Talent Trees") are treated no differently from any other [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") in that Tree.

## The Force

[The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") and its practitioners have an enormous impact on the galaxy during this era. Jedi travel throughout the galaxy pursuing their own missions and those of [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic"). Non-Jedi traditions are active in many societies, both primitive and technologically advanced. The resurgence of [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") and the dangers of [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") become all too apparent during [The Jedi Civil War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Civil_War "The Jedi Civil War").

Most beings have never personally seen a Jedi, but they are aware of their exploits and have opinions about the nature of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). In fact, especially during [The Jedi Civil War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Civil_War "The Jedi Civil War"), many regular folks do not know or understand the differences between the Sith and Jedi philosophies, despite Jedi efforts to educate them. However, Republic citizens regard [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") as a good and helpful force, if unpredictable and occasionally misguided.

This chapter presents new [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers"), [Force Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Talents "Force Talents"), [Force Techniques](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Techniques "Force Techniques"), and [Force Secrets](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Secrets "Force Secrets") used specifically in this era. Some of these powers are available to characters in other [Eras of Play](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Eras_of_Play "Eras of Play"), at the Gamemaster's discretion. Many of these powers are powerful compared with some of the powers used in the classic era, so the Gamemaster should carefully consider which ones to allow.

This chapter also includes new [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions") active during this time. These traditions can be restricted to specific cultures or certain planets, but they might be extremely influential in these areas. Additionally, [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") have been known to add to their own body of knowledge by studying other traditions, and even accepting members of other traditions into the Jedi ranks.

## Academies and Enclaves

Throughout this era, Jedi training continues to be largely decentralized. For many, training takes place at academies and enclaves scattered throughout the galaxy. Even though these organizations recognize the direction of the Jedi High Council on [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant"), they operate independently and promote their own philosophical views on [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). The terms academy and enclave are sometimes used interchangeably. However, academies are educational facilities, whereas enclaves provide living quarters and support for fully trained Jedi. An enclave might house its own Jedi Council, and might have an attached academy. In either case, academies and enclaves are communal facilities, providing living quarters, meeting space, and safe haven to members.

**The Jedi Temple on Coruscant:** The most prominent Jedi Academy is located at the Jedi Temple on [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant"), under the auspices of the Jedi High Council. This academy is usually the largest in the Order, but its size and overall influence fluctuates with political and military events that impact [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant"). Training methods vary, but the Master and Apprentice model is most often used.

**The Jedi Enclave on Dantooine:** The Jedi Enclave on [Dantooine](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dantooine "Dantooine") is one of the oldest enclaves and academies in the Order. A satellite Council here manages Jedi affairs on nearby worlds in [The Outer Rim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Outer_Rim?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Outer Rim (page does not exist)"), and the chambers serve as a meeting place for the [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant") High Council when the Council is in the area. Many prominent Jedi students in the era study here. The enclave is completely destroyed by [Darth Malak](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Malak "Darth Malak") during [The Jedi Civil War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Civil_War "The Jedi Civil War"). The site is abandoned and in ruins for many years after the Sith attack, but it is eventually rebuilt.

**The Taris Academy:** The Taris Academy, housed in the Jedi Tower on the planet [Taris](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Taris "Taris"), is an example of a smaller training facility. Early classes vary in size, but in the years immediately before [The Mandalorian Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorian_Wars "The Mandalorian Wars"), it is home to five Masters with a single student each. In addition to teaching, the Masters there contribute to planetary security, with their Padawans keeping justice as part of their training. [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order") and local officials do not know that the five Masters are also the First WatchCircle of Krynda's Covenant, sent there to investigate possible Sith threats to the galaxy. When these Masters perceive such a threat from their own students, they murder them and blame the lone surviving student, [Zayne Carrick](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Zayne_Carrick "Zayne Carrick"). The academy is closed, and the Jedi are recalled to [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant"). The building is later used as a headquarters for the Mandalorian invaders until its destruction by the planetary resistance.

### Using Academies and Enclaves

The decentralized and wide variations in the Jedi teaching techniques and organizations during this era provide Gamemasters with a lot of flexibility in their own games. The Gamemaster might place the campaign in a familiar setting, such as the Jedi Enclave on [Dantooine](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dantooine "Dantooine") or Jedi Temple on [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant"), and find plenty of material in this article to support it. The Gamemaster can also create a Jedi training facility populated with characters and backstories of their own devising, giving the Gamemaster the advantage of full control over the characters' fate and interaction with the player characters. Examples include:

- **The Solo Master:** A single Jedi Master provides training for one or more apprentices, often on an isolated planet. The Master might be a Jedi Watchman for the planet, or a freethinking Jedi, promoting their own view of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") without disruptions from the Jedi Council. Their views are not likely to be radically different from other Jedi, such as promoting [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"), but might be thought of as subversive and dangerous to the Council itself.
- **The Satellite Academy:** A small teaching facility run by several Jedi Masters and Knights with a small number of students, A satellite academy is attached to a larger or more prominent facility, such as the Jedi Temple on [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant") or the Jedi Enclave on [Dantooine](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dantooine "Dantooine"). Students might be training at this smaller facility for a limited time, or they might be permanent residents, making short trips to the main academy for additional training. These satellites might also provide other Jedi services. The Jedi Tower on [Taris](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Taris "Taris") is an example of a satellite academy.
- **The Regional Enclave:** A moderately sized Jedi enclave that serves as a regional center for Jedi personnel and major contact point for area governments and other organizations to work directly with [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order"). The enclave might not have a Jedi Council of its own, but it will at least have one Jedi Master who is recognized as the leader. The enclave might promote its own view of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), or might have considerable influence in the area, ranging from a local presence to planetary or sectorwide influence.

## [The Jedi Covenant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Covenant "The Jedi Covenant")

An example of the limited reach and effectiveness of the Jedi High Council, The Jedi Covenant is a secret organization established within [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order"). The Jedi High Council on [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant") is unaware of its existence. The Covenant is wholly dedicated to preventing the return of [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith"). Covenant members depend heavily on **[Visions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Visions "Visions")** and *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")* for guidance. Their fanatical dedication to this task leads them down paths that most Jedi would never find acceptable. However, they have been able to operate and grow without significant interference for twenty years before the Mandalorian onslaught.

[Krynda Draay](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Krynda_Draay?action=edit&redlink=1 "Krynda Draay (page does not exist)") establishes The Jedi Covenant after her perceived failure to recognize the threat [Exar Kun](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exar_Kun "Exar Kun") represented to [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") and the galaxy. Funded by the assets of her dead husband's fortune, she dedicates herself and her new organization to defeating [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith"). To achieve this goal, the Covenant recruits Jedi of many traits and talents. At its core, the Covenant depends on Jedi Consular vision techniques developed by Krynda. She trains other Consulars, organizing them into WatchCircles. They pass significant information on to Jedi Guardians and Jedi Shadows within the Covenant, enabling them to carry out any tasks necessary to support or prevent the Consular's visions from coming true.

At least, that is the theory. In reality, the situation is more complex and fluid. Those involved in the Covenant are on constant guard to keep the greater Jedi Order from discovering their activities. Many also have influential connections that they wish to protect or wield, in addition to their own ambitions within [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order"). As such, the flow of information is not as direct as planned, and those acting upon the information are not always those intended in the structure of the Covenant. In some cases, a WatchCircle revealed to [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order") or of the general public can be severed from the organization, framed as a single group of fanatics or wayward Jedi.

### Covenant Hierarchy

**[Krynda Draay](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Krynda_Draay?action=edit&redlink=1 "Krynda Draay (page does not exist)") and [Haazen](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Haazen?action=edit&redlink=1 "Haazen (page does not exist)"):** [Krynda Draay](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Krynda_Draay?action=edit&redlink=1 "Krynda Draay (page does not exist)") has the power, finances, and will to build the Covenant. She works with [Haazen](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Haazen?action=edit&redlink=1 "Haazen (page does not exist)") to establish the details and organization. In the years before [The Mandalorian Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorian_Wars "The Mandalorian Wars"), [Haazen](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Haazen?action=edit&redlink=1 "Haazen (page does not exist)") directs the day-to-day operations of the Covenant, leaving Krynda to concentrate on visions and WatchCircle training.

**WatchCircles:** WatchCircle cells are groups of three to eight Consulars called Seers of the Circle. They typically use *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")*, **[Visions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Visions "Visions")**, and Krynda's group techniques to foresee the future. Over time, WatchCircles are quietly installed across the galaxy, particularly near locations where Sith threats have been foreseen. Some are devoted to early warnings on various aspects of galactic life, such as the Economic WatchCircle. The WatchCircle of [Lucien Draay](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lucien_Draay "Lucien Draay") is known as the First WatchCircle. Other WatchCircles are named for fallen Jedi heroes of [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War"), such as WatchCircle Vodo for [Vodo-Siosk Baas](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vodo-Siosk_Baas "Vodo-Siosk Baas").

**Executors:** Each WatchCircle has a single Jedi Guardian who acts as an Executor, the facilitator who manages logistical details so the WatchCircles can do their job. The Executor is also responsible for reporting the WatchCircle's findings and activities back to [Haazen](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Haazen?action=edit&redlink=1 "Haazen (page does not exist)"). Executors also operate individually, providing general security for the Covenant. In all cases, these individuals are selected for their inability to use *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")* or other significant precognitive abilities, so that they do not taint or influence the findings of their WatchCircle.

**Covenant Shadows:** The Covenant Shadows operate at the direction of [Haazen](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Haazen?action=edit&redlink=1 "Haazen (page does not exist)"), at the request of an Executor, to carry out assignments and missions that a WatchCircle or Executor should not be associated with. When undertaking a mission, the Shadow operates under the Executor's authority, but the Shadow is detached from the Executor's group once the task is complete. Missions include investigating possible Sith-influenced individuals, as well as locating and acquiring Sith artifacts. They operate alone in most cases. The Shadows are removed from the rolls of [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order"), either through bureaucratic subterfuge, or by being falsely reported dead. They give up their former lives in their dedication to the eradication of [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith"). The only full list of Shadows is kept at the Draay Estate and is maintained by [Haazen](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Haazen?action=edit&redlink=1 "Haazen (page does not exist)"), [Lucien Draay](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lucien_Draay "Lucien Draay"), and a small number of other Guardians involved in logistics.

The mysteries and manipulations of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") have never been solely the province of [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") and [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith"). These two groups dominate the galactic stage for thousands of years, but many worlds and cultures establish their own traditions based on their own cultures and understanding of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). Unlike the Jedi and Sith, these [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions") rarely seek dominance of all Force teaching.

In [The Old Republic Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic_Era "The Old Republic Era"), most Jedi view non-Jedi traditions as misguided at best, or downright threatening if the tradition embraces [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"). As always, Dark Side threats are sought out and eliminated where possible. Other [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions") are tolerated, even embraced, especially when [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") believe they can learn something new. However, [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") still attempt to convert practitioners of these other [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions") to [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order").

[The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith"), on the other hand, attempt to dominate all other [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions"). Exactly how they view a specific [Force Tradition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Tradition "Force Tradition") depends on the strength of [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith"). When [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") number in the thousands, such as during [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War"), they attempt to eradicate all other [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions"). They corrupt and convert those open to their teachings, and eliminate all others. When [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") numbers are reduced, or are operating under Darth Bane's Rule of Two, they rarely take on another [Force Tradition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Tradition "Force Tradition") directly. However, they might still attempt to recruit apprentices from these Force Traditions.

In [The Old Republic Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic_Era "The Old Republic Era"), many more [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions") are operating throughout the galaxy than in the classic era. Practitioners of these [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions") are most likely found on their homeworlds, or within their home cultures. However, individual practitioners might travel to other worlds on occasion.

## Equipment and Droids

An [Old Republic Era Campaign](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Old_Republic_Era_Campaign "Old Republic Era Campaign") takes place during a time of technological innovation. New weapons and armor appear on the galactic market every day, and the constant warfare requires arms makers to keep abreast of the latest technological trends. Despite the rapidly advancing pace of technology, relics of older eras remain in active use. As some planets race toward the cutting edge of technology, others are blasted back to the past by widespread devastation.

This chapter presents new [Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapons "Weapons"), [Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor "Armor"), [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment"), and [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") that can be used to populate your campaign. In this chapter you will find both advanced and archaic technologies that can add diversity to the equipment found in your campaign. These weapons and equipment are designed to supplement (but not replace) those found in the *[Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*. Heroes still wield blasters and [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers"), and those weapons' statistics change little through the ages.

The [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") in this chapter, on the other hand, are meant to replace the [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") from the *[Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")* in an [Old Republic Era Campaign](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Old_Republic_Era_Campaign "Old Republic Era Campaign"). For this reason, the chapter presents a wide variety of [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") from all degrees, making it possible to use only the [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") in this book (and those you create yourself) throughout your campaign. Players already familiar with the setting of an [Old Republic Era Campaign](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Old_Republic_Era_Campaign "Old Republic Era Campaign") might recognize some of these [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids"), helping to reinforce the feel of the setting for those players.

During the days of [The Old Republic Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic_Era "The Old Republic Era"), Jedi experiment with different kinds of crystals as key components to their [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers"). In later years [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") use crystals from the caves at [Ilum](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ilum?action=edit&redlink=1 "Ilum (page does not exist)") because of their stability and their connection to [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). However, during the tumultuous period following [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War"), Jedi and Sith alike experiment with different kinds of Lightsaber Crystals.

[Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") heroes can use alternative Lightsaber Crystals in the construction of their [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers"), gaining certain benefits, depending on the crystal. However, a Jedi using an alternative Lightsaber Crystal instead of a standard Lightsaber Crystal does not gain the usual +1 bonus on attack rolls for a personally constructed [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber"). Essentially, the new benefit of the alternative crystal replaces the normal +1 bonus on attack rolls. [Lightsaber Construction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Construction "Lightsaber Construction") is handled as normal, even with an alternative crystal, though any crystal that provides any kind of [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus does so only for the person building the [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber").

Alternative Lightsaber Crystals are rare, and finding one could be the focus of a small adventure or even a special prize for a [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") character completing a difficult quest. These crystals are not for sale on any market, and have no purchase value. Only [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") who specifically seek out or earn them can use one of these crystals for [Lightsaber Construction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Construction "Lightsaber Construction").

### [Gear Templates](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gear_Templates "Gear Templates")

In an [Old Republic Era Campaign](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Old_Republic_Era_Campaign "Old Republic Era Campaign"), a hero might have access to a wide variety of exotic gear with special properties not found in more common types of [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment"). Rather than list individual pieces of [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") from dozens of different worlds in the galaxy- such as a Bothan Heavy Blaster Pistol or a suit of Arkanian Battle Armor- Gear Templates can be applied to normal [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") statistics to produce an unusual version of an item.

To create an item using a Gear Template, choose a basic piece of [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") (such as a [Blaster Rifle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Blaster_Rifle "Blaster Rifle")), then choose one of the Gear Templates from the sections below. Apply the changes to the Equipment's statistics and effect as described in the Template's description. Some Gear Templates have restrictions, either because of the Gear Template type (for example, Weapon Templates can only be applied to [Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapons "Weapons")) or because of specific prerequisites (for example, only weapons that deal [Stun](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stun "Stun") damage can have the [Bothan Weapon Template](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bothan_Weapon_Template "Bothan Weapon Template") applied).

Gamemasters might rule that some weapons cannot be altered due to cultural or logical prerequisites; for example, since a [Bowcaster](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bowcaster "Bowcaster") is a weapon unique to [Wookiee](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wookiee "Wookiee") culture, it makes little sense for there to be an Echani Bowcaster.

Introducing Gear TemplatesIntroducing Gear Templates

Introducing Gear Templates into your campaign takes a major step away from the simplicity of [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") design, purchase, and use that the *[Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")* provides. Gear Templates are balanced in that the benefit of a Gear Template also contains certain drawbacks, so heroes still have a reason to pick up a standard [Heavy Blaster Pistol](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Blaster_Pistol "Heavy Blaster Pistol") rather than an Iridonian Heavy Blaster Pistol. Items that have had a Gear Template applied represent rare samples that are found only once in a great while. All Templated items have the [Rare](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rare "Rare") tag applied to their Availability, since they are found only on one planet or in the possession of one particular group.

Gamemasters should be cautious when introducing Gear Templates into the game. Items with Gear Templates applied should not be included in the list of items a character can buy; rather, they must be found or earned. Items with Gear Templates applied make great rewards for completing missions or defeating important villains, but if they can be found in every shop or purchased off a computer shopping network then they are no longer special.

Even when these items are available for purchase, such as buying a suit of Eriadun Flex Armor on [Eriadu](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Eriadu "Eriadu"), only one such item will be available, since they are above and beyond the weapons and armor found commonly throughout the galaxy. Unlike the heroes in the *Knights of the Old Republic* video games, your heroes probably should not walk around with unique item on every part of their bodies. Instead, treat these special items as exceptional rewards or prized finds.

## Vehicles and Starships

The troubled times of an [Old Republic Era Campaign](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Old_Republic_Era_Campaign "Old Republic Era Campaign") is the dawn of a new age for the engineering and manufacturing of [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") and [Starships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships "Starships"). The presence of conflict creates a breeding ground for new ideas and new technologies. The [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") and [Starships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships "Starships") produced by manufacturers during times of war are, out of necessity, on the cutting edge of technology. Factions seeking advantage in war commission new designs, or manufacturers benefit from designs handed down from government engineers to the private sector.

The [Starships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships "Starships") and [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") presented in this chapter represent a cross-section of vessels that find their way into the hands of heroes and their allies over the course of an [Old Republic Era Campaign](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Old_Republic_Era_Campaign "Old Republic Era Campaign"). Vessels such as the [578-R Space Transport](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/578-R_Space_Transport "578-R Space Transport") or the [G-Type Light Shuttle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/G-Type_Light_Shuttle "G-Type Light Shuttle") can serve as mobile bases of operations for a group of heroes (Gamemasters should also consider the [*Dynamic*-Class Freighter](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dynamic-Class_Freighter "Dynamic-Class Freighter") for this role). The [Ground Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ground_Vehicles "Ground Vehicles"), including [Speeder Bikes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speeder_Bikes "Speeder Bikes") and [Landspeeders](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Landspeeders "Landspeeders"), represent contemporary civilian [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") in common use throughout the galaxy during this time period. Heroes are as familiar with these [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") and [Starships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships "Starships") as heroes during the Galactic Civil War are familiar with [Corellian YT-1300 Transports](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Corellian_YT-1300_Transports "Corellian YT-1300 Transports") or [T-16 Skyhoppers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/T-16_Skyhoppers?action=edit&redlink=1 "T-16 Skyhoppers (page does not exist)").

Additionally, some of the [Starships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships "Starships") in this chapter also list an [Emplacement Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Emplacement_Point "Emplacement Point") value in their statistics, enabling compatibility with the [Starship Modification](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Modification "Starship Modification") rules. These [Starships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships "Starships") follow all the normal rules for [Starship Modification](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Modification "Starship Modification") as presented in *[Starships of the Galaxy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships_of_the_Galaxy "Starships of the Galaxy")*.

The [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") and [Starships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships "Starships") described in this chapter are those that are available and commonly found in the ownership of individuals and organizations other than [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"), [The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians"), [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic"), or [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire"). For [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") and [Starships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships "Starships") for those factions, see their respective chapters later in the book.

## [Old Republic Era Campaigns](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Old_Republic_Era_Campaigns "Old Republic Era Campaigns")

The galaxy is torn by war. [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") is fighting for its survival, and everywhere the enemies of civilization- [The Krath](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Krath "The Krath"), [The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians"), [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith")- threaten to shatter what tenuous hold the Republic has on the galaxy. [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers") clash on nearly every planet, and few worlds remain untouched by the violence of the times. This is the galaxy into which heroes in a Old Republic Era Campaign must venture forth, and it is a galaxy that teems with adventure possibilities.

An Old Republic Era Campaign gives Gamemasters opportunities that they do not have in other [Eras of Play](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Eras_of_Play "Eras of Play"), allowing the freedom to create unique stories that let the heroes influence galactic events. An Old Republic Era Campaign gives the Gamemaster *carte blanche* to create epic campaigns that put the heroes (and their [Destinies](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destinies "Destinies")) in the forefront of the setting, with only a few sources of existing continuity to deal with.

Old Republic Era Campaigns take place against a backdrop that captures everything that makes *Star Wars* unique. Even more so than in [The Clone Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Clone_Wars "The Clone Wars"), these are the days of [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") in their prime. They do battle with the evil Sith, and defend the galaxy against the Mandalorian hordes. Heroes rise to prominence and then fall into darkness, and villains who seem beyond saving find redemption and reject [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"). Soldiers leave the planets of their birth to head off to war on distant battlefields, only to be drawn to their homeworlds as the war comes quickly to a new front. Criminals thrive on the chaos of war, and nobles and corporations struggle to wrest the reins of power away from their adversaries, and perhaps even from the Republic. Yet, [The Old Republic Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic_Era "The Old Republic Era") has a feel all its own, and this section should help Gamemasters develop a sense for the qualities that make a campaign in this era unique, explaining how to capture that feeling and use it to create epic adventures.

Familiar planets undergo significant changes over the centuries between these troubled times and the Galactic Civil War. Some of these planets are quite different in [Old Republic Era Campaigns](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Old_Republic_Era_Campaigns "Old Republic Era Campaigns"), and the updates in this section should provide Gamemasters with enough information to use these planets in this era.

## [Bespin](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bespin "Bespin")

The gas giant Bespin has not yet been colonized during this time period, and will not be until 1,989 BBY when Cloud City is first commissioned. However, hyperspace scouts discover Bespin years before the Galactic Civil War, and a number of corporations try- and fail- to establish Tibanna Gas mining operations above the world. With the decline of pulse-wave weapons and the ascension of blaster weapons, the undeveloped world of Bespin is rising in importance to the galactic economy. A number of corporations continue to lobby the Galactic Senate for mining rights to Bespin, though corporate lobbies keep the Senate tied up in procedures as each company jockeys for position.

The carbonite-rich planet [Empress Teta](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Empress_Teta?action=edit&redlink=1 "Empress Teta (page does not exist)") stakes an extremely strong claim to Bespin, because explorers from [The Hyperspace Navigator's Guild](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Hyperspace_Navigator%27s_Guild "The Hyperspace Navigator's Guild")- whose guildhouse is on [Empress Teta](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Empress_Teta?action=edit&redlink=1 "Empress Teta (page does not exist)")- discovered the world. No permanent outposts are established above Bespin, and Tetan mining operations consist mainly of large repulsorcraft dipping into Bespin's atmosphere to scoop up gas where they can. During [The Mandalorian Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorian_Wars "The Mandalorian Wars"), a fleet of Mandalorian warships destroys most of the Tetan mining infrastructure, hoping to break up the Tibanna Gas supply chain for the Republic. Following this attack, few companies have the time or resources to attempt to mine Bespin, and the planet remains nearly unheard of until the founding of Cloud City.

## [Bothawui](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bothawui "Bothawui")

The [Bothans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bothans "Bothans") have long been members of [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic"), and in an [Old Republic Era Campaign](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Old_Republic_Era_Campaign "Old Republic Era Campaign") Bothawui is one of the most recognizable and heavily trafficked planets in the Republic. In the millennia preceding [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War"), rapid expansion of hyperspace lanes opens up new worlds to exploration and investigation, and Bothawui's influence in the Galactic Senate is considerable, and many of the Republic's bureaucratic committees meet on Bothawui to discuss matters of government.

More important, [The Bothan SpyNet](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Bothan_SpyNet "The Bothan SpyNet") is beginning to grow at an exponential rate. Each new hyperspace route quickly has dozens of [Bothans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bothans "Bothans") traveling across it to reach new worlds. As the [Bothans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bothans "Bothans") send agents to join the inhabitants of dozens of newly discovered worlds, information begins to flow through Bothawui back to the Republic about the expanding known galaxy. Moreover, Bothawui becomes a hub of not only information but trade as well. [Bothan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bothan "Bothan") spies, returning from stints on distant worlds, bring back exotic items to sell on Bothawui's open market. Collectors of rare items flock back to Bothawui, and many [Bothan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bothan "Bothan") explorers become wealthy overnight. Bothawui becomes a world not only of abundant information but also of abundant opulence. The [Bothan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bothan "Bothan") homeworld is more prominent and more envied than any other time in galactic history.

## [Cerea](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cerea "Cerea")

The [Cerean](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cerean "Cerean") homeworld has not yet established a formal connection with the galaxy at large, nor with [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic"). Such contact does not come for several millennia yet, though the planet does appear in [The Hyperspace Navigator's Guild](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Hyperspace_Navigator%27s_Guild "The Hyperspace Navigator's Guild") database as Planet 36-AFE-2C, with a note that the scouts encountered no intelligent life on the world nor any valuable resources. Despite this entry, Cerea has had contact with the outside galaxy, and it has not been pleasant. Shortly after the planet was discovered, a secret expedition is sent to the world by [Adascorp](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Adascorp "Adascorp"), the [Arkanian](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Arkanian "Arkanian") corporation operated by Lord Adasca. After encountering resistance from the natives, the [Adascorp](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Adascorp "Adascorp") agents kidnap an entire village of [Cereans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cereans "Cereans") and flee the world, leaving behind several damaged and destroyed [Starships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships "Starships"), which leak fuel and pollute the area around the landing site.

Since the [Adascorp](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Adascorp "Adascorp") mission to Cerea, few ships have even chanced by the planet. Well away from most common hyperspace routes, Cerea sees visits from the outside only once every decade or so, and those [Starships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships "Starships") rarely land because they are merely Navigators Guild survey vessels updating their records. The [Cerean](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cerean "Cerean") people are wary of visitors after the violent encounter with [Adascorp](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Adascorp "Adascorp") agents, and they have turned against technology of any kind.

## [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant")

Already the most important and prominent world in the galaxy, Coruscant is a bustling planet where decisions affecting the entire galaxy are made. Though [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") fights to defend itself against threats from all sides, Coruscant is a shining gem at the center of [The Core](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Core?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Core (page does not exist)"). It serves as a bastion of civilization in a darkening galaxy. Coruscant changes little in the time between [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War") and [The Clone Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Clone_Wars "The Clone Wars"), and most of the features of Coruscant that are seen in later years are already in place by this time.

Coruscant has seen its share of battles, however, and continues to do so throughout the wars that plague the galaxy during this time. The defenses around Coruscant are tougher than they will be during the fall of the Republic a few millennia later, and security procedures during this time more closely resemble those that will be in place during the reign of [The Galactic Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Galactic_Empire "The Galactic Empire"). Corporations volunteer their security firms to help police and protect Coruscant, which the Senate allows as a part of the rebuilding process following [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War"). As a result, visitors are just as likely to encounter corporate enforcers as Republic military personnel when arriving on Coruscant, a fact that gives citizens of the Republic pause.

## [Dac](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dac "Dac")

Only recently discovered by explorers from [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic"), Dac is a new and mysterious planet whose inhabitants are largely unknown to the majority of Republic citizens. Little is known about Dac during this time because its discovery just prior to [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War") is not widely publicized. Though the natives of Dac are technologically advanced and possess a seemingly inherent aptitude for [Starship](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship "Starship") construction and design, most other Republic worlds are reluctant to trade with Dac since its two native [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") appear to be constantly on the brink of war with one another. Dac has begun to deal tentatively with [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic"), but relations are far from solid. Dac is far from becoming the staunch supporter of the Republic it will be in the future.

Dac is an isolated world with few outsiders during this time. The [Quarren](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Quarren "Quarren") natives are occasionally defensive- even violent- when confronted by outsiders, and the [Mon Calamari](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mon_Calamari "Mon Calamari") are reluctant to trust anyone from off-world. Dac has not yet become a prominent [Starship](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship "Starship") manufacturer, though the planet does possess early hyperspace technology, and its inhabitants have been exploring nearby star systems for several years. Cities on Dac have not yet been adapted for visitors from other worlds, and the settlements on the planet are largely underwater, making a visit to the planet difficult for outsiders.

## [Dorin](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dorin "Dorin")

Already a member and staunch supporter of [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") before [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War"), the home planet of the [Kel Dor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kel_Dor "Kel Dor") is rarely visited because of natural hyperspace hazards nearby. Though Dorin sees little traffic, it plays an important role in galactic politics, particularly in the affairs of [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"). The [Kel Dor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kel_Dor "Kel Dor") have sent as many Padawans to train with [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order") as any other [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") except for [Humans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Humans "Humans"). So many Force-sensitive [Kel Dor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kel_Dor "Kel Dor") have been discovered that the Republic funded a Jedi Enclave on Dorin. Though only [Kel Dor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kel_Dor "Kel Dor") students can study at the enclave regularly because of the planet's unique atmosphere, Jedi of other [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") sometimes visit the world (using environment-filtering [Breath Masks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Breath_Masks "Breath Masks")) to study the exceptional Force abilities taught by [Kel Dor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kel_Dor "Kel Dor") Jedi Masters.

The enclave on Dorin continues to send new Jedi to help defend the Republic, but the planet provides far more to the galaxy. Following each of the wars of this era, teams of [Kel Dor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kel_Dor "Kel Dor") travel to various planets to aid in reconstruction efforts. The [Kel Dor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kel_Dor "Kel Dor") knack for helping environmentally ravaged worlds earns them a positive reputation, making [Kel Dor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kel_Dor "Kel Dor") welcome on nearly every world. Dorin remains untouched during the major conflicts of this time because it is too difficult for most space fleets to reach. However, after [The Jedi Civil War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Civil_War "The Jedi Civil War"), Sith assassins infiltrate the Jedi Enclave on Dorin and manage to exterminate nearly every Jedi present.

## [Duro](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Duro "Duro")

The world of Duro is one of the earliest members of [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic"), and like [Bothawui](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bothawui "Bothawui") it has risen to prominence thanks to that association. The [Duros](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Duros "Duros") are some of the galaxy's foremost hyperspace explorers, and their knowledge of space lanes exceeds that of nearly any other group in the galaxy. As a result, the world of Duro is a prominent travel hub during the years leading up to and following [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War"). Not only is Duro a [Core World](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_World?action=edit&redlink=1 "Core World (page does not exist)") that acts as a gateway to [The Colonies](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Colonies?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Colonies (page does not exist)"), [Starships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships "Starships") that pass through Duro are more likely to obtain the most accurate hyperspace navigation data available- rivaled only by data obtained on [Empress Teta](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Empress_Teta?action=edit&redlink=1 "Empress Teta (page does not exist)"). Thousands of [Starships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships "Starships") pass through the space above Duro every day, and hyperspace explorers consider it the best launching point for expeditions.

Unfortunately for Duro, the planet's reputation as a hub of hyperspace travel comes back to haunt it during these war-torn years. [The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians") devastate Duro, reducing its infrastructure to rubble and destroying most of its orbital space platforms in an attempt to cut off a major hyperspace lane for [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic"). Later, [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") stages its invasion of [The Core](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Core?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Core (page does not exist)") from Duro.

## [Gamorr](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gamorr "Gamorr")

The primitive world of Gamorr has already been visited many times by the time of [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War"), and [Gamorreans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gamorreans "Gamorreans") have spread throughout the galaxy. [The Hutt Kajidics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Hutt_Kajidics "The Hutt Kajidics") have been rounding up [Gamorrean](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gamorrean "Gamorrean") slaves and mercenaries for years, depositing them on distant worlds to protect their interests. During these war-torn years, [Gamorreans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gamorreans "Gamorreans") see unprecedented expansion into the galaxy, because of their warrior culture. The undeveloped planet produces some of the most violent and ravenous mercenaries in the galaxy, a fact some factions use to their advantage.

Prior to obtaining the services of [The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians"), the combined Krath and Sith armies seek to bolster their ranks by scooping up thousands of [Gamorrean](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gamorrean "Gamorrean") mercenaries to serve as cannon fodder against [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") in [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War"). Krath and Sith ships arrive daily to obtain more [Gamorrean](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gamorrean "Gamorrean") warriors, and Gamorr is considered to be under Sith occupation. When the Republic drives the Sith forces away from Gamorr, the world returns to a state of primitive isolation that lasts until after [The Jedi Civil War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Civil_War "The Jedi Civil War"). Though both [The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians") and [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") avoid the planet, several Sith warlords press [Gamorrean](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gamorrean "Gamorrean") mercenaries into their service during [The Dark Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Wars "The Dark Wars"), and the planet changes hands among nearly a dozen Sith warlords during this time.

## [Iridonia](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Iridonia "Iridonia")

Home of the [Zabrak](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Zabrak "Zabrak"), Iridonia is frequently among the first planets to be pulled into war. A Mid Rim world situated near important hyperspace lanes, Iridonia is one of the gateway planets that connects [The Outer Rim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Outer_Rim?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Outer Rim (page does not exist)") to [The Inner Rim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Inner_Rim?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Inner Rim (page does not exist)"). At the start of [The Mandalorian Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorian_Wars "The Mandalorian Wars"), Iridonia is one of the first planets to be invaded. It suffers heavy losses in the initial wave of attacks because the planet's defenders fight aggressively and recklessly to repel the invaders. Though [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") eventually liberates Iridonia and the planet subsequently joins the Republic's war against [The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians"), many of the planet's soldiers are recruited and indoctrinated by [The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians"), joining the enemy cause before the Republic can free their homeworld.

Iridonia is the site of several battles throughout these dangerous years. The First Battle of Iridonia is a turning point in [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War"). Jedi-led Republic forces rout the combined armies of [The Krath](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Krath "The Krath") and [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith"), driving them back and gaining significant momentum. During [The Jedi Civil War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Civil_War "The Jedi Civil War"), the planet changes hands several times as the Sith and Republic clash over the planet, which is used as a staging point for both militaries. The [Zabrak](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Zabrak "Zabrak") suffer heavy losses during [The Jedi Civil War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Civil_War "The Jedi Civil War"), though they never stop fighting the Sith invaders. After [The Jedi Civil War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Civil_War "The Jedi Civil War"), Iridonia's eight colonies became independent, but cooperative, worlds, because the central government on Iridonia cannot maintain control of the colonies when the home planet is occupied.

## [Ithor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ithor "Ithor")

One of the oldest members of [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic"), Ithor can be an extremely important world in an [Old Republic Era Campaign](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Old_Republic_Era_Campaign "Old Republic Era Campaign"). The [Ithorian](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ithorian "Ithorian") aptitude for biology, botany, and other life sciences sends the planet's natives across the galaxy, and the nearly untouched ecosystem of Ithor becomes a valuable asset to the Republic throughout the various wars of the time. [Ithorian](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ithorian "Ithorian") scientists- working side by side with [Selkath](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Selkath "Selkath") marine biologists- first discover the methods of using Kolto to produce exceptionally powerful healing salves. [Ithorian](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ithorian "Ithorian") efforts to produce new strains of Kolto that grow in Ithor's jungles meet with mixed results. But the planet is still a primary source of agricultural and medical supplies during the wars and rebuilding periods following each of them.

Realizing the military value of Ithor early in [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War"), [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") stations a massive planetary defense fleet around the world. The fleet is increased during [The Mandalorian Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorian_Wars "The Mandalorian Wars") and [The Jedi Civil War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Civil_War "The Jedi Civil War"). Though the Republic presence is strong, enemy forces attempt to infiltrate the world in indirect ways. Sith saboteurs manage to disable at least two [Ithorian](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ithorian "Ithorian") herdships, sending them crashing into their jungles and causing the native [Ithorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ithorians "Ithorians") to partially withdraw from the war effort. Though [Ithorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ithorians "Ithorians") are a common and welcome sight throughout the galaxy, the Republic presence at Ithor- ostensibly there for the planet's protection- takes a firm and arguably forceful hand in the planet's defense, instituting strict policies that border on martial law.

## [Kashyyyk](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kashyyyk "Kashyyyk")

During the period following [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War"), Kashyyyk is an occupied world, oppressed and controlled by [The Czerka Corporation](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Czerka_Corporation "The Czerka Corporation"). Because of Czerka's investment in the Republic's rebuilding efforts, the Senate grants Czerka domain over Kashyyyk, citing the corporation's discovery of the world as justification for the control of the planet. Believing the world to possess no sentient life (though this attitude is likely one born more of prejudice than scientific research), [The Czerka Corporation](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Czerka_Corporation "The Czerka Corporation") ignored the native Wookiees' designation for the world and renamed it Edean. Czerka took control of the world against the wishes of the [Wookiees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wookiees "Wookiees") and began allowing slavers to have free rein over the planet's native inhabitants.

Kashyyyk remains under Czerka control until after [The Jedi Civil War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Civil_War "The Jedi Civil War"), when [Wookiee](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wookiee "Wookiee") natives rise up against their corporate occupiers to liberate their home world. Prior to that, Kashyyyk largely escapes the conflicts of [The Mandalorian Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorian_Wars "The Mandalorian Wars") and [The Jedi Civil War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Civil_War "The Jedi Civil War"), as both sides of each conflict see that Kashyyyk offers little value but would be difficult to capture. With its dangerous and unstable terrain, Kashyyyk is valued only by [The Czerka Corporation](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Czerka_Corporation "The Czerka Corporation"), which treats the [Wookiees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wookiees "Wookiees") as less than sentient and exploits the planet's natural resources and native population for its own profit.

## [Naboo](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Naboo "Naboo")

One of many planets first colonized during this time, Naboo is an untamed world inhabited only by the native [Gungans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gungans "Gungans"). The first [Humans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Humans "Humans") settle Naboo shortly after [The Jedi Civil War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Civil_War "The Jedi Civil War"), and the [Gungans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gungans "Gungans") live in underwater cities, seemingly unaware of the events of the galaxy at large. Prior to [The Jedi Civil War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Civil_War "The Jedi Civil War"), no records of visits to Naboo by Republic explorers exist. An explorer named [Kwilaan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kwilaan?action=edit&redlink=1 "Kwilaan (page does not exist)") supposedly discovers the planet after the end of [The Dark Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Wars "The Dark Wars"). However, [Kwilaan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kwilaan?action=edit&redlink=1 "Kwilaan (page does not exist)") might not have been the first person to visit the planet, because the [Gungan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gungan "Gungan") natives seem unperturbed by the arrival of Kwilaan's expedition, as though they had encountered [Humans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Humans "Humans") before. Most likely, previous visits to the world were not recorded, though the explorers find no trace of such landings.

Naboo is a pristine world, untouched by technology except for what little the [Gungans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gungans "Gungans") possess. No civilization exists above the water line, and the planet's rolling plains and beautiful landscapes are untainted by cities or other settlements. Despite its beauty, Naboo is a dangerous world, with a number of large predators both on land and in the seas, and traveling to Naboo can be nearly as dangerous as visiting the jungles of [Kashyyyk](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kashyyyk "Kashyyyk").

## [Nar Shaddaa](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nar_Shaddaa "Nar Shaddaa")

By [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War"), Nar Shaddaa is already a dangerous place infested with criminals of all stripes. Nar Shaddaa sees a massive influx of refugees from hundreds of worlds passing through the Smuggler's Moon on their way to safer worlds. Though the planet is a dangerous place, huge numbers of refugees put down roots in Nar Shaddaa, forging a new life on a world where they have little to fear from governments or wars.

Nar Shaddaa falls under the control of [The Exchange](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Exchange "The Exchange") during these years, becoming a strong base of operations for the vast criminal organization. [The Exchange](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Exchange "The Exchange") acts much like a government on the largely lawless world of Nar Shaddaa, imposing its own form of order wherever it sees fit. Most visitors to Nar Shaddaa have a brush with [The Exchange](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Exchange "The Exchange"), usually encountering low-ranking lackeys. Though [The Hutt Kajidics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Hutt_Kajidics "The Hutt Kajidics") continue to fight [The Exchange](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Exchange "The Exchange") for control of the world, their power has waned, and Nar Shaddaa is, at least temporarily, firmly in the domain of the crime syndicate.

## [Rodia](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rodia "Rodia")

The [Rodians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rodians "Rodians") are, like [Ithorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ithorians "Ithorians") and [Twi'leks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Twi%27leks "Twi'leks"), among the most common non-[Human](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Human "Human") Species seen throughout the galaxy. During this time, Rodia has a unique and valuable resource waiting to be exploited by all parts of the galaxy: [Rodian](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rodian "Rodian") hunters. [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic"), [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith"), [The Exchange](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Exchange "The Exchange"), and other factions continually vie for the favor of Rodia, hoping to woo the skilled hunters to their side in nearly every conflict. Rodians play these factions off of one another, building up wealth while never committing fully to one side or another.

Rodia remains largely neutral throughout most of this time- with one notable exception. During [The Jedi Civil War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Civil_War "The Jedi Civil War"), [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") attempt to destroy the planet using the massive firepower of [The Star Forge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Star_Forge "The Star Forge") fleet, hoping to deny [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") of one of its greatest sources of scouts and explorers. Before this plot can succeed, however, [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") uncover it and signal the Republic Fleet, which comes to Rodia's rescue. The Battle of Rodia is a pivotal battle in [The Jedi Civil War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Civil_War "The Jedi Civil War"), and after the Republic repels the Sith fleet, Rodia officially joins the war on the side of the Republic. Individual [Rodians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rodians "Rodians") still work for [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") and other factions, but Rodia does not forget its debt to the Republic for the remainder of the war.

## [Ryloth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ryloth "Ryloth")

A longstanding member of [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic"), Ryloth is of little intrinsic value during the wars of this time, and it is largely ignored by the various militaries. Though [Twi'leks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Twi%27leks "Twi'leks") join the fight on various sides during the wars of the period, officially Ryloth is a Republic world. The planet's allegiance holds little sway over its citizens, however. Important and influential [Twi'leks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Twi%27leks "Twi'leks") join the ranks of [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic"), [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"), [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith"), [The Exchange](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Exchange "The Exchange"), [The GenoHaradan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_GenoHaradan "The GenoHaradan"), and dozens of other organizations. This leads to significant strife on Ryloth, since the government's unwillingness to rein in its own citizens leads to conflicts among the [Twi'leks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Twi%27leks "Twi'leks"). One family might support the Republic, but another might have a son studying at the Sith Academy on [Korriban](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Korriban "Korriban"), creating individual loyalties within communities.

Ryloth is on the brink of civil war throughout this time. Though disagreements between individuals, families, and settlements rarely come to violence, skirmishes break out from time to time. The [Twi'lek](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Twi%27lek "Twi'lek") clans are hard pressed to keep the peace. The inhabitants of Ryloth seethe as ideological differences between neighbors create an environment of dislike, distrust, and occasionally hatred that leads to violent outbursts. Riots are not uncommon on Ryloth during this time, though the clans manage to stop them before they do too much harm.

## [Sullust](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sullust "Sullust")

A Republic world since before [The Great Hyperspace War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Hyperspace_War?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Great Hyperspace War (page does not exist)"), Sullust is at the center of many conflicts during this time. Sullust is tactically important as a hub of trade along the Rimma Trade Route. With varying degrees of success, several factions attempt to blockade Sullust and disrupt commerce along the Rimma Trade Route. Though the space above Sullust is frequently filled with military vessels, either blockading or defending the world, the planet's volatile environment is as unappealing to [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") and [The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians") as it is to most of [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic"). Few skirmishes take place on the planet's surface.

Since Sullust's infrastructure is rarely affected by the various wars, the SoroSuub Corporation continues to produce [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles"), [Starships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships "Starships"), and other technological marvels unimpeded by galactic strife. In fact, the few skirmishes on Sullust involve enemy forces trying to sabotage SoroSuub installations, which provide military technology to [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic"). Occupying forces are rarely in place for long, because the Republic assiduously defends the planet against outside invaders, keeping SoroSuub technology flowing to the Republic war effort.

## [Tatooine](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tatooine "Tatooine")

Out of the way and of little interest to military forces, Tatooine is under the control of [The Czerka Corporation](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Czerka_Corporation "The Czerka Corporation") during this time. Anchorhead is the only active settlement on the planet. Anchorhead is a modest outpost for Czerka operatives and a few other entrepreneurs who have made their way to the distant Outer Rim world hoping to find their fortune mining the desolate world. The two indigenous peoples of Tatooine- [Tusken Raiders](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tusken_Raiders "Tusken Raiders") and [Jawas](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jawas "Jawas")- have made contact with the settlers; the [Tusken Raiders](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tusken_Raiders "Tusken Raiders") violently oppose Czerka operations, and the [Jawas](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jawas "Jawas") seize the opportunity to make a hefty profit from the ill-prepared newcomers.

Tatooine sees little military action during this time. [The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians") avoid the planet entirely, and [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") visit the planet individually only when [Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Revan "Revan") and [Malak](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Malak "Malak") seek out ancient [Rakatan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rakatan "Rakatan") Star Maps to help find [The Star Forge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Star_Forge "The Star Forge"). Realizing that the planet is of little interest, yet possessing the basics of civilization, criminals flock to Tatooine as a short-term hiding place from the long arm of the law. [The Hutt Kajidics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Hutt_Kajidics "The Hutt Kajidics") show a small amount of interest in the planet, and both [The Exchange](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Exchange "The Exchange") and [The GenoHaradan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_GenoHaradan "The GenoHaradan") have influence over the Anchorhead colony.

## [Trandosha](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trandosha "Trandosha")

By the time of [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War"), Trandosha has been a part of [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") for several centuries, though it has not integrated itself into the Republic as thoroughly as other planets. In fact, though the world has representation in the Galactic Senate, Trandosha itself has little Republic presence. The recent discovery of [Kashyyyk](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kashyyyk "Kashyyyk"), however, opens up new avenues to galactic prominence for Trandosha. The planet negotiates with [The Czerka Corporation](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Czerka_Corporation "The Czerka Corporation") to gain slaving rights to [Wookiees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wookiees "Wookiees"), beginning a millennia-long struggle between the [Wookiees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wookiees "Wookiees") and the [Trandoshans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trandoshans "Trandoshans").

## [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi")

DescriptionKnights of the Galactic RepublicGuardians of Peace and JusticeStarships

Eons ago, The Jedi Order was whole and in pursuit of a deeper understanding of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). However, some were not content with the Jedi path and turned to [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). They abandoned their pasts and were driven to the farthest reaches of the galaxy by the remaining Jedi. For thousands of years, The Jedi thought their long-lost traitorous comrades gone and forgotten. But when hyperspace explorers discover an empire ruled with an iron fist by the descendants of those fallen Jedi, the Order realizes that the exiles had found and enslaved a people called [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith"), taking the name as their own. When the whole of this Sith Empire turns its terrifying might against [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") in [The Great Hyperspace War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Hyperspace_War?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Great Hyperspace War (page does not exist)"), The Jedi fully grasp the consequences of that nearly forgotten decision to banish their fallen comrades.

Some Jedi denounce the Order's zeal to extinguish every trace of [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") in the war's aftermath, and many think [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") extinct. Then the mortal enemy of The Jedi returns in force. First the Jedi Knights [Exar Kun](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exar_Kun "Exar Kun") and [Ulic Qel-Droma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ulic_Qel-Droma "Ulic Qel-Droma") turn to [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") and ally themselves with [The Krath](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Krath "The Krath"), a Dark Side cult borne of ancient Sith tradition. As the newly revived Sith Empire thrusts the galaxy into the conflict that would be called [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War"), [Ulic Qel-Droma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ulic_Qel-Droma "Ulic Qel-Droma") cements his power by defeating the Mandalore and binding [The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians") to his cause. The Jedi face not only fanatical soldiers and skilled warriors but also their own comrades; Jedi flee the Order to join the Sith cause, and [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") begin to regain the numbers they once had during [The Great Hyperspace War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Hyperspace_War?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Great Hyperspace War (page does not exist)").

During this time, The Jedi Order is at the summit of its power and self-confidence. The Jedi High Council is a largely ceremonial body with no real authority over Jedi. Jedi Masters and Watchmen are entrusted to maintain peace and train the new generations of Jedi, allowing remarkable diversity and freedom of ideas. Though a dichotomy of "Light" and "Dark" remains firmly entrenched, schools of thought abound about how to best serve [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). Jedi Padawans are regularly accepted in their teens and twenties, and even though Jedi Knights rarely take more than two apprentices, a single Jedi Master might have dozens. Unfortunately, The Jedi suffer heavy losses during this war, and by the time The Jedi defeat Qel-Droma and the rest of [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") their numbers are drastically diminished.

## [The Restoration Period](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Restoration_Period "The Restoration Period")

**3,995-3,966 BBY**

Humbled by [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War"), The Jedi undertake major reform. The Jedi Council seeks to hold Jedi Masters and Watchmen to a higher standard of accountability and asserts more uniform practices. But as [The Jedi Code](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Code "The Jedi Code") indicates, Jedi do not rule over others, and the Council faces a long, uphill battle toward claiming unquestioned authority.

Tension is thick as differing points of view polarize the Order, and the Council seeks a reasonable median. [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") views the Jedi Council's newfound sternness as positive, but Jedi accustomed to a looser hierarchy see the Council as oppressive in its influence. Conversely, some Jedi are enthusiastic about implementing more stringent policies. [The Jedi Covenant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Covenant "The Jedi Covenant") is one example of a reactionary group that Jedi heroes might oppose.

Though few galaxy-spanning threats are apparent during this time, The Jedi must still deal with remnants of [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith"), though this is a lower priority given their need to rebuild the Order. The Jedi expunge [The Mecrosa Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mecrosa_Order "The Mecrosa Order") from the decadent Tapani Sector, and the Great Hunt turns Jedi into exterminators, slaying [Terentateks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Terentateks "Terentateks") and other Sithspawn left over from [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War"). However, the Order returns to some semblance of normal operation, with Jedi running down pirates, breaking up crime rings, adjudicating planetary disputes, and fighting pockets of Mandalorian marauders on distant worlds.

## [The Mandalorian Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorian_Wars "The Mandalorian Wars")

**3,965-3,960 BBY**

Once again the galaxy plunges into war as [The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians"), thought disorganized after their defeat in [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War"), begin their onslaught of Republic worlds at the command of [Mandalore the Ultimate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mandalore_the_Ultimate "Mandalore the Ultimate"). Even though Jedi numbers have recuperated, their confidence and solidarity are not. This first interstellar crisis since [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War") highlights the rift in Jedi ideology, with rogue Jedi pressing the Council to help [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") and the Jedi Council taking a stance of nonviolence. Once the charismatic Jedi Knight [Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Revan "Revan") defiantly joins The Mandalorian Wars, other Jedi must choose a side. [The Revanchists](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Revanchists "The Revanchists"), as those Jedi who choose to defy the Council and join the war are called, are heralded as heroes by the Republic and its citizens.

Jedi fighting [The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians") experience regular interaction with grateful Republic soldiers as they share the mess halls, battleships, and front lines. Reluctant Jedi are drawn into the war, but some of Revan's followers abandon the fight once they see how thoughtlessly their leader approaches [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"). Some of Revan's recruits are spies, reporting back to the Council, but few are prepared for Revan's betrayal. At [Malachor V](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Malachor_V "Malachor V"), Revan confronts [Mandalore the Ultimate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mandalore_the_Ultimate "Mandalore the Ultimate"), drawing him into a trap and activating a superweapon known as [The Mass Shadow Generator](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mass_Shadow_Generator "The Mass Shadow Generator"). [The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians") are destroyed, but celebrations are cut short as [Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Revan "Revan"), already steeped in [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") from their dealings with Sith remnants, flees to [The Unknown Regions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Unknown_Regions "The Unknown Regions"), taking a large piece of the Republic military with them.

## [The Jedi Civil War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Civil_War "The Jedi Civil War")

**3,959-3,956 BBY**

When [Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Revan "Revan") and [Malak](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Malak "Malak") return from [The Unknown Regions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Unknown_Regions "The Unknown Regions") at the head of a revitalized Sith Empire, The Jedi are inevitably drawn into the conflict as [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") fights to defend itself against the might of [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire"). Though The Jedi Civil War is precipitated by a betrayal from within their own ranks, The Jedi have been preparing for battle since [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War"). Witnessing the Sith corruption of Revan's army, The Jedi are again filled with conviction, fighting with impunity beside loyal Republic soldiers. Popular opinion of The Jedi, however, has declined steeply. Failing to comprehend the convoluted causes of the invasion of [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire"), the galactic citizenry understands only that Jedi they once championed against [The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians") have now betrayed them; hence, they refer to the conflict as The Jedi Civil War.

The Jedi Civil War rages for several years until the Council decides to deal [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") a major blow. A Jedi strike team, led by legendary Jedi Padawan [Bastila Shan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bastila_Shan "Bastila Shan"), lands on Darth Revan's flagship, captures them, and brings them back to the Republic. The Jedi convert [Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Revan "Revan") back to [The Light Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Light_Side "The Light Side") of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), purging them of the corruption of [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith"). With Revan's help The Jedi locate [The Star Forge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Star_Forge "The Star Forge")- the source of the Sith military's resurgence- and defeat [Darth Malak](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Malak "Darth Malak"). Having defeated the Sith leaders, The Jedi continue to struggle against the remnants of [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire"), who retreat behind their borders to recuperate.

## [The Dark Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Wars "The Dark Wars")

**3,955-3,951 BBY**

With their leaders defeated, [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") crumbles, and The Jedi face not a single, united Sith front, but instead a dozen or more Sith warlords who fight against [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic"), The Jedi, and each other. Unfortunately for The Jedi, a small cadre of Sith warlords manage to band together long enough to deal a staggering blow to the Order, using assassins to decimate The Jedi, leaving but a handful of survivors. This is a dire time for The Jedi. Reeling from crippling losses sustained during The Jedi Civil War, the Order hobbles on the brink of extinction. The Jedi Council's authority has completely broken down and satellite academies are near-empty. The surviving Jedi are on a desperate mission to find the source of the killings.

After The Sith Triumvirate is discovered and eliminated by a surviving Jedi, the few remaining Jedi resurrect the Order from the ashes of its defeat.

## [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith")

DescriptionUsing the SithThe Legions of the SithAgents of EvilStarshipsDroids

*Peace is a lie, there is only passion.*

*Through passion, I gain strength.*

*Through strength, I gain power.*

*Though power, I gain victory.*

*Through victory, my chains are broken.*

*[The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") shall free me.*

*-The Code of The Sith*

The story of The Sith goes back one hundred thousand years, eons before even the rise of [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") or [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi").

The Sith were a people who called the world [Korriban](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Korriban "Korriban") home. But around the year 28,000 BBY, the archetypal model of ambition rises to power among them. This inflexible Sith ruler unites his world into a single kingdom, and claims the titles of both monarch and god of his people. History calls him King [Adas](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Adas?action=edit&redlink=1 "Adas (page does not exist)"), but according to legend, [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") bestows on him a different name: *Sith'ari*- the perfect being.

Three thousand years before the founding of the Republic, Adas' hegemony is invaded by [The Infinite Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Infinite_Empire "The Infinite Empire"), an immensely powerful interstellar nation. Here, later Sith would say, they prove their right to rule the galaxy. Outsmarting the technologically superior invaders, [Adas](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Adas?action=edit&redlink=1 "Adas (page does not exist)") conquers the conquerors, sacrificing his life in the process. With the aliens' technology, The Sith prosper, colonizing nearby worlds and even banishing a group of pureblooded Sith as far away as [Tund](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tund?action=edit&redlink=1 "Tund (page does not exist)"). But there is a greater gift [The Infinite Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Infinite_Empire "The Infinite Empire") bequeathed to The Sith- [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force").

Eventually, The Sith are again visited by aliens, this time by Dark Jedi exiled in [The Hundred-Year Darkness](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Hundred-Year_Darkness?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Hundred-Year Darkness (page does not exist)"). These exiles, initially called *Jen'jidai* in the Sith tongue, easily conquer the reigning Sith overlord, earning their leader [Ajunta Pall](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ajunta_Pall "Ajunta Pall") the title *Jen'ari* or "Dark Lord" of the Sith. Though a number of these Sith conquerors follow their leaders back to Republic Space for revenge against [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"), many stay to interbreed with the Sith by alchemical means. For the next two thousand years, Jedi blood mixes with Sith, breeding out feeble characteristics of each [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species")- such as superstition, sympathy, and loyalty- while sharpening desirable qualities such as ambition, cunning, and raw power in [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). At last, around the year 5,000 BBY, The Sith, led by the Dark Lord [Naga Sadow](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Naga_Sadow?action=edit&redlink=1 "Naga Sadow (page does not exist)"), is ready and invades Republic Space.

Because of treason, the Sith campaign fails, and special enforcers called [Jedi Shadows](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Shadows "Jedi Shadows") zealously eradicate every last vestige of The Sith they find. But in a twist of irony, a few short centuries later, the Jedi Knight [Freedon Nadd](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Freedon_Nadd "Freedon Nadd") finds two Sith relics the Shadows missed. Taking instruction from both King Adas' Holocron and Sadow's disembodied spirit, Nadd becomes a Dark Lord. In turn, he inculcates the Jedi [Exar Kun](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exar_Kun "Exar Kun") into The Sith creed, consequently bringing [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") to its knees as Kun's Brotherhood of the Sith instigates [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War").

Kun's Brotherhood is defeated, and in another blow, [The Mecrosa Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mecrosa_Order "The Mecrosa Order"), a long-time Sith bastion, overplays its hand in [The Tapani Sector](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Tapani_Sector?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Tapani Sector (page does not exist)") and is completely annihilated in [The Cleansing of the Nine Houses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Cleansing_of_the_Nine_Houses?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Cleansing of the Nine Houses (page does not exist)"). Still, The Sith need never fear extinction again. From the seed of Kun's conversion comes a host of Jedi followers, and after his death, his acolytes scatter throughout the galaxy with the teachings of The Sith ancients.

## [The Restoration Period](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Restoration_Period "The Restoration Period")/[The Mandalorian Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorian_Wars "The Mandalorian Wars")

**3,995-3,960 BBY**

Though the forces of [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") and [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") manage to throw back the combined forces of The Sith and [The Krath](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Krath "The Krath") during [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War"), many followers of The Sith remain loyal to their ways. As the Republic rebuilds and subsequently engages [The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians") in a bloody war of attrition, remnants of The Sith, some on worlds completely unknown to the Republic, continue to practice their dark arts in secret. Though they remain scattered and unable to mount a serious effort to regroup, Sith loyalists, fallen Jedi, and the descendants of powerful leaders in Exar Kun's Sith Empire await their chance to rebuild the mighty Sith Empire.

## [The Jedi Civil War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Civil_War "The Jedi Civil War")

**3,959-3,956 BBY**

They do not have to wait long, for soon [Darth Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Revan "Darth Revan") and [Darth Malak](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Malak "Darth Malak") appear, resurrecting [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") in a Jedi Civil War. Returning from [The Unknown Regions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Unknown_Regions "The Unknown Regions") following [The Mandalorian Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorian_Wars "The Mandalorian Wars"), the two Sith Lords engage in a lightning-fast campaign to reestablish [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") and reclaim conquered worlds. Among the first planets retaken by The Sith is [Korriban](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Korriban "Korriban"), which becomes one of the most important locations in [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") and houses the academy where new Sith leaders are trained. With [The Star Forge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Star_Forge "The Star Forge") producing huge amounts of [Starships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships "Starships"), [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles"), and weapons at all times, The Sith are able to resurrect their Empire in a matter of months.

At the height of The Jedi Civil War, [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") covers nearly one-third of the known galaxy, with many worlds hotly contested between [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") and [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire"). Some worlds join [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") willingly, while others are taken by force. Dozens if not hundreds of Sith apprentices learn the ways of [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), and a few even rise to the rank of Sith Master, training students of their own. With legions of armored troops spreading throughout the galaxy, all led by masters of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") seems unstoppable.

Betrayal spells the undoing of Revan's glorious, if short-lived, Sith Empire. At the moment [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") capture them, Darth Revan's own apprentice attempts to seize control, firing on Revan's flagship. Though [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") continues to grow for some months under the control of the traitorous [Darth Malak](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Malak "Darth Malak"), eventually the redeemed [Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Revan "Revan") slays their former apprentice and shatters the backbone of the reborn Sith Empire. The Jedi Civil War comes to an end as the once-unified Sith Empire fragments into dozens of smaller territories.

## [The Dark Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Wars "The Dark Wars")

**3,955-3,951 BBY**

[The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") crumbles, and within months former Sith Lords have declared themselves the masters of much smaller domains. As The Sith squabble among themselves, fighting over the scraps of [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire"), [Darth Sion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Sion "Darth Sion") and [Darth Nihilus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Nihilus "Darth Nihilus") engage in a campaign to wipe out [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"), with great success. Thanks to the foolish ambitions of the Jedi [Atris](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Atris "Atris"), Nihilus is able to wipe out almost all of the remaining Jedi, leaving only a small handful alive.

The skirmishes between the Sith warlords continue for nearly four years until [Darth Sion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Sion "Darth Sion") and [Darth Nihilus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Nihilus "Darth Nihilus") attempt to reunite The Sith under a single banner of strong leadership. Any hope for resurrecting [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") is undone by a lone surviving Jedi, and when [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order") is reborn The Sith fade back into the shadows, not to be seen again in force for many years.

One quality that has allowed The Sith to survive after so many millennia is their ability to adapt. This era in particular presents a gamut of Sith incarnations. The Sith operating at this time fall into the following general categories: Imperialists, Traditionalists, Secret Societies, and Remnants. Each offers unique gaming opportunities.

### [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire")

The reinvented Sith Empire of [Darth Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Revan "Darth Revan") and [Darth Malak](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Malak "Darth Malak") is a massive galaxy-threatening organization on par with [The Galactic Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Galactic_Empire "The Galactic Empire") of the original *Star Wars* trilogy. This version of The Sith has it all, from huge battleships and [Starfighters](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starfighters "Starfighters") to countless shocktroopers of both the "normal" and Force-using variety. They even have a Death Star-like superweapon, in the form of [The Star Forge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Star_Forge "The Star Forge"). Technology and military engagements naturally play a major role for these Sith, since a show of overwhelming force is a prized strategy. Nonetheless, this revised Sith Empire has not abandoned its supernatural roots, training an army of acolytes in an academy on the ancient Sith homeworld [Korriban](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Korriban "Korriban").

At the height of its power, The Sith Empire is much like [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") in that it is highly organized and has the ability to wage an effective war against its enemies. Despite the inherently traitorous nature of The Sith, The Sith Empire's hierarchy is surprisingly rigid, and has a rank structure that ensures that all war efforts do not suffer from infighting.

At the top of the command structure are the two most prominent Sith Lords, [Darth Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Revan "Darth Revan") and [Darth Malak](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Malak "Darth Malak") (though Malak assumes sole control of The Sith Empire after betraying his master). A number of [Sith Lords](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Lords "Sith Lords") serve under the two most prominent masters, including [Darth Bandon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Bandon "Darth Bandon"). Each being that achieves the full title of Dark Lord of the Sith is given control over some military or civilian assets; many [Sith Lords](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Lords "Sith Lords") are placed in charge of entire fleets or battalions, while others serve as the rulers of captured worlds. In many respects, the [Sith Lords](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Lords "Sith Lords") are above and beyond the normal rank structure, and they have the final authority over admirals, commanders, and entire sector governments. In many ways, the [Sith Lords](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Lords "Sith Lords") are comparable to the Moffs of [The Galactic Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Galactic_Empire "The Galactic Empire").

The Sith Navy consists of all of the military [Starships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships "Starships") under the control of The Sith Empire. The Sith Navy is further subdivided into fleets, some of which are large enough to patrol an entire sector effectively. Each fleet is under the control of an admiral, like [Saul Karath](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Saul_Karath "Saul Karath"), though these admirals in turn answer to a [Sith Lord](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Lord "Sith Lord"). Fleets are further subdivided into battle groups, which usually consist of five to ten [Capital Ships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Capital_Ships "Capital Ships") and various support craft. The descending order of ranks among the Sith Navy are admiral, vice admiral, commodore, captain, major, and lieutenant. All nonofficer personnel aboard [Starships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships "Starships") in the Sith Navy are [Sith Troopers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Troopers "Sith Troopers") or specially assigned crewmembers, such as students from the Sith Academy on [Korriban](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Korriban "Korriban") joining a crew for a special assignment.

Though there is no formal Sith Army, the ground forces of The Sith Empire are an entity all on their own. Used not only in ground warfare but also in the occupation, policing, and pacification of captured worlds, [Sith Troopers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Troopers "Sith Troopers") and their officers fall directly under the command of the [Sith Lords](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Lords "Sith Lords") they serve. While aboard ships of the Sith Navy they are under the command of the captain of the ship they serve on, but once planetside they report either to the [Sith Lord](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Lord "Sith Lord") ruling that planet or to the [Sith Lord](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Lord "Sith Lord") in command of the military efforts in the region. The highest-ranking Sith officers are commanders, who outrank colonels and lieutenants.

Among the [Sith Troopers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Troopers "Sith Troopers"), there are truly only two designations: troopers and commandos, though neither truly outranks the other. Lieutenants and colonels in command of squads handle the front-line efforts of [Sith Trooper](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Trooper "Sith Trooper") squads, reporting up the chain of command to colonels who apprise [Sith Lords](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Lords "Sith Lords") of ongoing situations and ensure the Sith Lords' commands are carried out.

### Traditionalists

The Brotherhood of the Sith of [Exar Kun](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exar_Kun "Exar Kun") and [Ulic Qel-Droma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ulic_Qel-Droma "Ulic Qel-Droma") and [The Sorcerers of Tund](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sorcerers_of_Tund "The Sorcerers of Tund") are both Sith traditions that rely significantly on the more esoteric and mystical practices of their ancient order. Adventures involving these groups carry a greater "fantasy" flavor, with arcane Dark Side powers and talismans, Sith swords, and alchemically created monsters at every turn. In contrast to the faceless empire of [Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Revan "Revan") and [Malak](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Malak "Malak"), the Brotherhood of the Sith is represented by a handful of tightly knit darksiders with an egalitarian methodology. The Brotherhood uses technological and military elements, but these are deemphasized in favor of [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") and [Sith Talismans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Talismans "Sith Talismans").

### Secret Societies

The Sith Triumvirate and [The Mecrosa Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mecrosa_Order "The Mecrosa Order") are two examples of Sith secret societies, similar to The Sith in the *Star Wars* prequels. The most prevalent tools of this class of Sith are secrecy, deception, and patience. Like a disease, they conquer from the inside and work from the small scale to the grand design. They reveal themselves only after calculated planning, in the meantime using their resources to quietly dismantle their enemies and sow chaos with precision strikes. The Sith Triumvirate carries out the genocide of [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order") through subterfuge and assassination, but [The Mecrosa Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mecrosa_Order "The Mecrosa Order") uses its Sith sparingly to intimidate, persuade, and murder key rivals until such time as they can establish their total domination.

### Remnants

The Sith fall into diasporas at the immediate conclusion of both [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War") and [The Jedi Civil War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Civil_War "The Jedi Civil War"). These Sith scatter to the cosmic winds, setting up their own petty kingdoms on distant worlds or fighting one another to claim the title of new Dark Lord. Still, even a small-time Sith can be big-time trouble for characters on a mission to eliminate the Sith remnants one Dark Side follower at a time. These Sith are violent, opportunistic, and protective of whatever power they have been able to consolidate. Some even have dreams of returning The Sith to their former glory, recruiting under the guise of disenchanted and charismatic Jedi Knights.

[The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") (and all affiliated groups) make use of a large number of agents, both specialized and general. ## Sith Paraphernalia

- **[Sith Tremor Sword](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Tremor_Sword "Sith Tremor Sword"):** A Sith Tremor Sword is a deadly throwback to the blades wielded by the ancient Sith Lords of [Ziost](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ziost?action=edit&redlink=1 "Ziost (page does not exist)"). Sith Tremor Swords are [Dire Vibroblades](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dire_Vibroblades "Dire Vibroblades") with the [Cortosis Weave General Template](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cortosis_Weave_General_Template "Cortosis Weave General Template").

## [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic")

DescriptionUsing the Old RepublicRepublic PersonnelRepublic PersonalitiesEquipmentStarships

The Old Republic has known times of prosperity, of great upheaval, of hopelessness, and of renewal. But the half century following [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War") is a favorite of historians for a good reason: it has all of those things- often at the same time!

The Republic prior to [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War") retains trappings of the past, both in fashion and in behavior. Archaic wardrobe is in vogue during a brief revival that spans much of the Republic. Wealth coalesces in the hands of the few, including [Tapani Nobility](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tapani_Nobility "Tapani Nobility") and families with such names as Adasca, Zentine, Vanjervalis, and Draay. And the Republic sees [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order") as a lifeline to the past, as a force for good.

[The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War") changes everything. This "internal squabble over Jedi beliefs," as reporters first characterized it, takes untold lives and damages over 25% of the Republic's infrastructure. Though [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War") begins seemingly as an internal conflict within the Empress Teta System, the chaos spreads outward from [The Deep Core](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Deep_Core?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Deep Core (page does not exist)") at an incredible rate. When [The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians") join the war, the Republic nearly finds itself overwhelmed, and attacks on [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant") and other important worlds drive the real danger of this war home. While the Republic lets [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") deal with their traitorous comrades, the Republic military battles Mandalorians on one front and Krath warriors on another, trying to keep from being torn apart. After years of grueling conflict where worlds are leveled and the Republic is placed on the brink of total destruction, the tide turns, and eventually, with the help of [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"), the Republic wins the day.

The results of [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War") are devastating. Entire worlds have been blasted back to pre-hyperspace technology levels. Entire sectors vanish from communications channels, and go for months at a time without any news of their survival reaching the central systems of the Republic. Trade routes blazed over millennia require redevelopment at every stop. Piracy is rampant. The Republic, just as much a financial compact as a deliberate institution, fails to deliver on the basic premises of its creation.

## [The Restoration Period](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Restoration_Period "The Restoration Period")

**3,995-3,966 BBY**

A decade-long postwar depression ends when business- the driver of much in the Republic- comes to its rescue. With both rich and poor suffering, the Senate has to act- and it does. The brainchild of economist [Herron Morvis](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Herron_Morvis?action=edit&redlink=1 "Herron Morvis (page does not exist)"), the Coruscant Financial Exchange Establishment Act (the first of the so-called Commercial Protective Acts) restores the capital markets by making a simple bargain. Corporations listing their shares on the Coruscant Financial Exchange receive guarantees of protection on all trade routes within the Republic. A rejuvenated Republic navy, raised on investment taxes, provides that protection. The navy of [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War") had been weak, eclipsed in some sectors by private space forces. The new force would have the mission- and the power- to keep the goods flowing.

Firms are quick to sign on, and even dynastic families float their holdings on the exchange. Corporations shed their security budgets altogether, relying on the Republic to defend spacelanes with its [*Hammerhead*-Class Cruisers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hammerhead-Class_Cruisers "Hammerhead-Class Cruisers") and state-of-the-art [*Inexpugnable*-Class Tactical Command Vessels](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Inexpugnable-Class_Tactical_Command_Vessels "Inexpugnable-Class Tactical Command Vessels"). The Restoration Period is a rewarding time for many.

Efforts to play the system for profit weaken the Republic strategically. The accession of [Taris](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Taris "Taris") into the Republic is the final, fatal straw. When Mandalorian attacks on nonaligned worlds increase, executives with operations on [Taris](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Taris "Taris") bribe Senators to bring the planet into the Republic. Even though it is improved, the navy is too weak to protect such a distant world. Some, such as rising star [Saul Karath](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Saul_Karath "Saul Karath"), see the dangers, but the admiralty, delighted with its additional funding, pays no heed. So when the [Zayne Carrick](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Zayne_Carrick "Zayne Carrick") affair causes civil order on [Taris](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Taris "Taris") to collapse, [The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians") see their chance and launch a surprise invasion of the Republic. Defenses on [The Outer Rim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Outer_Rim?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Outer Rim (page does not exist)") crumble.

## [The Mandalorian Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorian_Wars "The Mandalorian Wars")

**3,965-3,960 BBY**

Finger-pointing at home coincides with desperate (and unsuccessful) moves on the frontier, including the tactic of positioning military units near cities. Mandalorian forces push into [The Outer Rim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Outer_Rim?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Outer Rim (page does not exist)") and then further inward, reaching critical worlds along hyperspace routes and either seizing them or making holding those worlds a huge drain on Republic resources. As the Republic scrambles to deal with a continued Mandalorian threat, many in the Republic see echoes of [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War") in current events. Though the Republic is pushed back during the early campaigns, losing [Taris](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Taris "Taris"), [Serroco](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Serroco?action=edit&redlink=1 "Serroco (page does not exist)"), and other worlds almost immediately, the fighting intensifies and the Republic wins important battles at [Iridonia](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Iridonia "Iridonia") and [Ithor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ithor "Ithor"), bolstering their own supply lines as well as improving their troops' morale.

A solid advantage in [The Mandalorian Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorian_Wars "The Mandalorian Wars") arrives only when a Jedi Knight calling themselves [Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Revan "Revan") comes to the Republic's aid. [Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Revan "Revan") is so successful that the Republic yields operational control of its forces to them. With Revan's final defeat of [The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians") at [Malachor V](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Malachor_V "Malachor V"), many in the Republic had cause to hope that the postwar lessons from [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War") might restore prosperity faster than before. The Republic is stunned when two of their greatest Jedi leaders take the ships under their command and vanish into [The Unknown Regions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Unknown_Regions "The Unknown Regions") under the pretense of hunting down the last of [The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians"). Though surprised, the Republic begins to regroup and rebuild quickly, and as curiosity over their missing fleet turns to concern there is an ever-palpable tension nearly everywhere in the Republic.

## [The Jedi Civil War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Civil_War "The Jedi Civil War")

**3,959-3,956 BBY**

[Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Revan "Revan"), having fallen to [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"), returns to the Republic as an invader at the head of a massive Sith armada. The Republic, barely having recovered from [The Mandalorian Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorian_Wars "The Mandalorian Wars"), loses several battles in rapid succession as [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") carves out a large chunk of the galaxy for its own. Other Republic worlds, sensing a shift in power and dissatisfied with the performance of the Republic in the last two wars, secede and join [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") voluntarily. Treachery is not limited to [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"). For many, the worst comes when [Admiral Saul Karath](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Admiral_Saul_Karath "Admiral Saul Karath") turns traitor, rupturing the navy between loyal and Sith factions.

For several years [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") grows, pitting the Republic against its former allies and comrades in every battle. Unlike fights against the nomadic Mandalorians, the Republic cannot force [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") into retreat, as many of its members fight to defend their homeworlds with no intention of leaving. [The Jedi Civil War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Civil_War "The Jedi Civil War") is very much a battle between two well-established and entrenched forces, though fortunately for the Republic its Jedi allies enter the war. After a Jedi strike force captures the enemy commander, [Darth Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Revan "Darth Revan"), and then sets them on a path of redemption, [Darth Malak](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Malak "Darth Malak") takes control of [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire"). His willingness to destroy the Republic's infrastructure is unlike the care [Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Revan "Revan") showed for preserving the worlds they conquered, and many battles mirror the mutually assured destruction so often seen in [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War").

Revan's redemption breaks the Sith domination, but the citizens of the Republic see only hardships ahead. With Sith survivors warring with the shattered Jedi Order, Republic worlds struggle to restore their civilization for the second time in half a century. This era teaches the lesson that the protection of [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order") is no free ride for the Republic.

## [The Dark Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Wars "The Dark Wars")

**3,955-3,951 BBY**

When [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") shatters into many smaller domains, the Republic finally has a chance to rebuild and take the offensive. While its enemies squabble among themselves, the Republic puts shipyards into service, quickly rebuilding the navy in a matter of just over a year. At last able to police and patrol outlying sectors, the Republic can finally make the galaxy safe for its citizens once more. Skirmishes with Sith warlords continue, but thanks to excellent strategic planning and leadership the Republic topples one Sith dictator after another, reclaiming lost territories piecemeal. By the time the last of [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") are wiped out, the Republic is once again at nearly its full strength.

The wartime periods of [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War"), [The Mandalorian Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorian_Wars "The Mandalorian Wars"), and [The Jedi Civil War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Civil_War "The Jedi Civil War") allow ample opportunities for military campaigns ranging from limited engagements to operations spanning multiple worlds at once. Combined operations using the Republic Navy and Army provide for interesting interactions between members of those services- as do joint efforts with Jedi. A campaign using The Old Republic does not have to be about major wars, but there are plenty to choose from.

Following [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War") and [The Jedi Civil War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Civil_War "The Jedi Civil War"), the Republic is eager to reestablish a credible role in galactic affairs. The military works to keep order, [Nobles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nobles "Nobles") seek to extend their influence, and [Bounty Hunters](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bounty_Hunters "Bounty Hunters") and [Scoundrels](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrels "Scoundrels") are out to make a quick credit. These postwar eras are ideal for Gamemasters looking to craft campaigns with less Jedi and Sith involvement.

Many adventures can be found by exploring the two major facets of the Republic: the military, and the body politic.

## The Republic Navy

When the Commercial Protectorate Acts directly ties its fortunes to the trade of the galaxy, the Republic Navy finally has a worthy mandate and abundant resources. In a few decades, the Navy goes from being an antiqued, irrelevant institution to an organizing feature of Republic life.

The direct linkage between commerce and the Naval budget chafes the old-timers; Sith War veteran [Orley Vanicus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Orley_Vanicus?action=edit&redlink=1 "Orley Vanicus (page does not exist)") likens it to "running a protection racket." This is particularly true; protecting more trade routes means more tax revenues, which means more ships to protect more trade routes. It becomes a recipe for disaster during [The Mandalorian Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorian_Wars "The Mandalorian Wars"). But in the meantime, more ships to command means more career opportunities- making the Navy a respectable calling indeed. Even Admiralty Plaza on [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant"), once considered a retirement home for spacers, becomes a popular posting sought by many.

Gamemasters can promote the spirit of the Navy in these times by giving player opportunities to work with or join the Navy. With the Rendili Yards turning out a [*Hammerhead*-Class Cruiser](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hammerhead-Class_Cruiser "Hammerhead-Class Cruiser") every ten days during the years before [The Mandalorian Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorian_Wars "The Mandalorian Wars"), postings become available faster than they can be filled. Given the tougher life of ground-pounders, it should not be uncommon to find players initially aiming for [Elite Trooper](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Elite_Trooper "Elite Trooper") turning toward [Ace Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ace_Pilot "Ace Pilot"), instead. Money- and advancement- are in the stars!

### Joining the Navy

Characters join the Republic Navy through one of three paths: as commissioned officers, as crew recruits, or through provisional rating.

Officers come from the Naval Academy at [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant") and from the satellite schools focusing on particular specialties. The Corellian Academy, for example, produces many of the Navy's shipwrights and engineers, and a Duro orbital facility specializes in tactical networking.

Most beings aboard the Navy's ships and working its shipyards are noncommissioned crew members. Most join through local recruiting centers. Signing bonuses are awarded in times of great need, but the desirability of the Navy is such that compulsory naval service is not needed, even when the Navy is under Sith control.

Rarely, in times of danger, merchant space captains are granted provisional ratings to serve on Republic bridges. However, such ratings can be revoked in peacetime- a practice that causes other spacers to hold the service in disdain.

### Life in the Navy

With the exception of ground crews and station personnel ("where careers go to die," the saying goes), spacers for the peacetime Republic lead privileged lives. The pay is far higher than that which soldiers receive. Given the newness of the vessels, living conditions are practically antiseptic, and technology has removed many of the challenges interstellar combat used to pose. Ships of the *Inexpugnable* line slave the tactical systems of whole fleets to a single command- creating occasions when bridge crews are "along for the ride."

For those who serve as jacks-of-all-trades, such as [Carth Onasi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Carth_Onasi "Carth Onasi"), the Navy offers a variety of experiences, from running spacelift missions for the army to flying stealth [Starfighter](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starfighter "Starfighter") patrols to trap pirates. As the recruitment ad says, "Joining the Navy is the way to see the galaxy."

The portion of the Navy that defects to [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") during [The Jedi Civil War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Civil_War "The Jedi Civil War") provides an interesting contrast. Spacers join the Sith causes as a means of preserving their status- and, indeed, the quality of life aboard Sith vessels is better than in other places under Sith rule. But [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") stoke ambition- the lifeblood of the Republic Navy during peacetime- to unhealthy levels, pitting ship against ship, officer against officer, and crewman against crewman. Martial punishments are many and harsh. For this reason, the Republic Navy under [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") is an attractive path for Dark Side players.

### Naval Organization

Few enterprises pay more respect to formal command structures than the Navy. With millions of beings moving trillions of tons of deadly force across the galaxy, Admiralty Plaza must be sure its directives reach everyone, down to the serving-Droid repair technicians in the ship's mess.

Most beings working in the Navy's ships and shipyards are noncommissioned. The rating of "Able Crew Member" (usually addressed as "Crewman") encompasses a variety of titles, from Captain's Steward to Medic Assistant to Machinist. All report to a Shipmaster, the highest noncommissioned posting on each ship. In many ways, the Shipmaster is the most powerful being abroad. Bridge crews come and go, but the Shipmaster and crew stay, passing knowledge to the next senior staff they have to break in. The Shipmaster is responsible for crew discipline; if they fail to act, tradition regards them as culpable for the crew members' crimes. Therefore, most run tight ships.

Crew members can receive commissions on the recommendations of the Shipmaster or the ship's Captain. Usually the result of years of service, this sometimes happens because of a crew member's act of heroism. [Admiral Saul Karath](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Admiral_Saul_Karath "Admiral Saul Karath"), who started as Vanicus's steward, followed that path. Later, during [The Jedi Civil War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Civil_War "The Jedi Civil War"), and affinity for [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") can help a new recruit stand out.

The list of commissioned officer ranks is larger.

**Admiral of the Fleet:** A single officer holds this position for a ten-year term, chosen from the pool of full Admirals by the Defense Ministry. A political appointment for a political job, it is one of two ranks not awarded by a Merit Board.

**Admirals, Vice Admirals, Rear Admirals:** Managing sectors of battlefront and commanding heavy vessels, Admirals make the real strategic decisions. The three levels are largely a functional matter; a Merit Board (or court martial) for a Rear Admiral would consist of Vice Admirals, for example. The conduct of full Admirals is subject to the arbitrary discretion of the Admiral of the Fleet. Admirals in combat during [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War") are sometimes informally known as "Fleet Captains."

**Captains:** The workhorses of the Navy, Captains are on the spot across the galaxy, commanding cruisers and sometimes heavier vessels. For example, [Admiral Saul Karath](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Admiral_Saul_Karath "Admiral Saul Karath") was Captain of the [*Inexpugnable*-Class Tactical Command Vessel](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Inexpugnable-Class_Tactical_Command_Vessel "Inexpugnable-Class Tactical Command Vessel") *Courageous* while waiting for his Merit Board to meet. The term "Captain" can be misleading, though, as it is used in an honorary sense on smaller ships when Commanders or Lieutenants are at the helm. Panels of Captains also handle all promotions and court martials for lesser officers.

**Lieutenants:** Most pilots of [Starfighters](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starfighters "Starfighters") and spacelift vessels are Lieutenants, as are most of the tactical bridge crew on heavy ships. On lighter ships such as blockade runners, Midships fill those roles.

**Midships:** Students are rated as provisional Midships on entering the Academy and are fully rated for service on graduating. An "Ensign" rank is not used.

Dates of service are used among Admirals to determine seniority; at Captain and below, all within the same rank are considered equal- though the one with the most connections usually wins.

In the Navy, "Justice is swift; promotions are slow." Merit Boards can take anywhere from weeks to years to meet, but courts martial meet as soon as enough officers can be found. Honor dictates that a board can be convened even in matters when a conviction is not sought. Customarily, the accused resigns their commission at the onset, "winning" their position back in the course of the inquiry. [Saul Karath](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Saul_Karath "Saul Karath") lost the *Courageous* after the Battle of [Serroco](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Serroco?action=edit&redlink=1 "Serroco (page does not exist)") and resigned immediately on return to [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant"); the Navy, mindful of his role in the encounter with Mandalore on [The *Arkanian Legacy*](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Arkanian_Legacy "The Arkanian Legacy"), rejected his resignation and restored his command.

## The Republic Armies

Unlike the "Grand Army of the Republic," the Republic Armies- plural- of this period are a chaotic agglomeration of planetary militias and mercenary forces united by Republic funding and an institutional inferiority complex.

Until [Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Revan "Revan"), no single Field Marshal commands all ground forces. Rather, all Generals take direction from the Defense Ministry on [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant"). The lack of local coordination frequently puts the true command of surface forces in the hands of the navy, which provides spacelift. During wartime, more soldiers are in transit aboard ships than are on ground.

The result is a parochial body that bears signs of its piecemeal origins. Units are known not by uniform designations, but by their original nomenclatures- and not all units of the same type are created equal. The Ralltiir Corps contains brigade-sized groups such as the Telerath 2nd and the Serroco Colonial- but it also contains smaller units such as Graayson's Company, which reports directly to Corps Command because of the agreement under which it entered Republic service.

Unsurprisingly, the institution exerts little political influence, but the navy has expressed little desire to take control of the ground forces as marines. Beyond the culture clash, which often manifests itself violently, the biggest expense of the Army- the [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") hauled on ships- would be added to the navy budget. "We'd rather rent than own," Admiral [Jimas Veltraa](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jimas_Veltraa?action=edit&redlink=1 "Jimas Veltraa (page does not exist)") once said.

### Joining the Armies

With pay and conditions far worse than in the Republic Navy, surface units suffer from recruiting problems. The Republic addresses this by contracting with existing local militias and mercenary groups, buying some out entirely. It also offers healthy signing bonuses for dangerous duties. Service is not compulsory- outside the areas [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") take over in [The Jedi Civil War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Civil_War "The Jedi Civil War")- but some irregular units use "recruiting" methods that the Defense Ministry would rather not know about.

### Army Organization

Army structures can have local and historical eccentricity, but some elements are common.

**Squad:** Teams of 8 to 12 soldiers lead by a Sergeant and a Corporal, Squads are the basic building block of Republic Army operations. Regardless of speciality or duration of service, Squad members are known as troopers and regarded as equal in rank.

**Battalion:** Groups of 25 to 40 Squads organized under a major, Battalions are the level at which specialization occurs. A 400-meter laser artillery Battalion travels with its own assigned [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") and [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles"), as well as a 20-member headquarters units. Specialist Battalions are frequently reassigned to other corps, leaving them with no true home.

**Brigade:** Brigades combine four to nine Battalions with a headquarters Battalion run by a Brigadier, resulting in a force ranging between 1,000 and 5,000 strong. The typical force for raids and occupation, Brigades work together in larger landing operations, with command and control handled, as often as not, by the navy that transported them.

**Corps:** Corps comprise all forces within an area and are named for the planets where their headquarters are based. A General leads each Corps, although the office is weak relative to that of other eras. Infrequently at the front, Generals relay commands from the Defense Ministry to the Brigadiers on the scene. Direct superiors of irregular units (former mercenary groups and single-[Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") militias) in their command, they lose time to bureaucratic and provisioning headaches. Therefore, promotion past Brigadier is rarely sought. No ranks of Lieutenant, Captain, and Colonel exist- partially to prevent confusion with the navy- but they can be found vestigially in the irregular native units, as well as in such strange titles as Commandant, Gray Chieftain, and Grand Xenog.

## The Galactic Senate

The Republic of this era provides [Noble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble "Noble") heroes with a wide variety of places to polish their skills and find adventure. Local leadership takes many interesting forms on the planets of the Republic, but the greatest politicians aspire to serve in one place: the Galactic Senate.

The Galactic Senate undergoes many changes after [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War"). It had met for years in ancient stonework chambers, but after [Exar Kun](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exar_Kun "Exar Kun") kills [Vodo-Siosk Baas](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vodo-Siosk_Baas "Vodo-Siosk Baas") on the Senate floor, Senators order the construction of a secure high-tech facility with no reminders of that fateful day. Attitudes modernize as well, with Senators taking more interest in trade with the Commercial Protectorate Acts. The "Age of the Staffer" begins, with Senators cultivating large entourages of young educated beings who deal with the pleas of corporate lobbyists.

In this era, the length of Senators' terms and the methods of choosing them are set by individual systems. Some are lifetime appointments chosen by local potentates; others, such as [Senator Graw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Senator_Graw?action=edit&redlink=1 "Senator Graw (page does not exist)") of [Ithor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ithor "Ithor"), face frequent trips home to stand for reelection. Thus, staffing for a Senator is not just a lucrative and educational experience for a young noble; it can also involve extensive travel. Senators from planets occupied during [The Mandalorian Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorian_Wars "The Mandalorian Wars") keep their seats, and some, such as [Haydel Goravvus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Haydel_Goravvus "Haydel Goravvus") of [Taris](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Taris "Taris"), actively organize resistance movements.

As bruising as the battles for committee assignments are, nothing in the Senate tests a noble like the campaign for Chancellor. Once elected to a four-year term by their colleagues, a Chancellor of this era must manage (or attempt to manage) a bureaucracy of ministries devoted to defense, internal security, economic development, and jurisprudence. The Chancellor frequently calls upon Jedi to fulfill assignments of state and to perform investigations on behalf of oversight committees.

## [The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians")

DescriptionUsing the MandaloriansMandalorian PersonnelMandalorian PersonalitiesEquipmentStarships

The Mandalorians are not a race, but a movement. They are a society of warriors who embrace a rough, honor-driven, and nomadic lifestyle. Conflict with The Mandalorians is a defining characteristic of the half century that began with [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War"). For The Mandalorians, the period encompasses their movement's greatest triumph and its near-total end. For centuries, [Mandalorian Crusaders](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mandalorian_Crusaders "Mandalorian Crusaders") roam the galaxy, seeking new battles and challenges. During [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War"), this movement of free agents becomes embroiled in the politics of the galaxy, setting off a chain of events that will include the near-extinction of their own movement.

Sensing an opportunity in the conflict between [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") and [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic"), [Mandalore the Indomitable](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mandalore_the_Indomitable "Mandalore the Indomitable") directs his forces against the Empress Teta System, expecting to make an easy conquest. But fallen Jedi [Ulic Qel-Droma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ulic_Qel-Droma "Ulic Qel-Droma") bests the leader in single combat, an act which has immediate and long-term repercussions. As a term of the duel, the Mandalorian Clans are obliged to swear allegiance to [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith"), joining them in their war against [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic").

[Mandalore the Indomitable](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mandalore_the_Indomitable "Mandalore the Indomitable") falls in battle and [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") eventually prevail, but for The Mandalorians, the consequences linger long after [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War") ends. Warriors are scattered across the galaxy, endangering the continuation of their traditions. Worse, many warriors are embarrassed and feel betrayed. Uninterested in the differences between Jedi and Sith, they see only their people in servitude to beings with powers beyond their understanding. Whenever clans gather, there is talk of retribution against [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") and against [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") they protected.

## [The Restoration Period](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Restoration_Period "The Restoration Period")

**3,995-3,965 BBY**

Soon, another leader rises with the name and mask of Mandalore. He calls upon the talents of advisors whose knowledge goes beyond the battlefield- [Cassus Fett](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cassus_Fett "Cassus Fett"), [Demagol](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Demagol "Demagol"), and others- to help him reshape Mandalorian identity. When an extremist cult calls for the reestablishment of the Crusader tradition, this Mandalore, the "Ultimate," uses that group as the foundation to reinvent the Mandalorian movement as a more disciplined, organized fighting force.

The spread of the "Neo-Crusader" subculture bears fruit in The Mandalorians' successful conquests of nonaligned worlds. By the time The Mandalorians launch the "onslaught"- their full-scale attack on the Republic- a system has been established to assimilate the new conquests. Factories that might have been pillaged before are now made to serve as War Forges. Captured ships are quickly refitted for immediate service. Any many of the conquered peoples are efficiently transformed into Mandalorians, undergoing speedy indoctrination by [Neo-Crusader Rally Masters](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Neo-Crusader_Rally_Masters "Neo-Crusader Rally Masters") and receiving Neo-Crusader Armor.

Some see the cultivation of the "Neo-Crusaders" as a crass ploy, sacrificing Mandalorian tenets in the name of a larger goal. But dissidents are effectively silenced. When [Rohlan Dyre](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rohlan_Dyre "Rohlan Dyre") risks his life to question the movement, [Mandalore the Ultimate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mandalore_the_Ultimate "Mandalore the Ultimate") uses the warrior's fate as an example for not bucking authority. So it is that The Mandalorians who nearly conquer the galaxy bear little resemblance to the Crusaders of just a generation before.

## [The Mandalorian Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorian_Wars "The Mandalorian Wars")

**3,965-3,960 BBY**

After decades of rebuilding, The Mandalorians are ready to strike. For some years they test the Republic's defenses, engaging in small skirmishes along [The Outer Rim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Outer_Rim?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Outer Rim (page does not exist)"), seeing how strongly the damaged Republic can resist their efforts. When at last The Mandalorians have amassed large enough resources and numbers, they launch an all-out invasion of Republic worlds, seeking to claim those planets for their own. Under the direction of [Mandalore the Ultimate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mandalore_the_Ultimate "Mandalore the Ultimate") with the aid of [Cassus Fett](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cassus_Fett "Cassus Fett"), The Mandalorians are successful in seizing countless worlds before a real defense can be mounted.

For years The Mandalorians engage [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") in a devastating war of attrition. Each world they conquer provides The Mandalorians with thousands of new troops, while the Republic's resources begin to feel strained. With only a few Jedi engaged in the opposition, The Mandalorians are able to take advantage of the bureaucratic weakness displayed by the Republic military and their superior warrior's training earns them victory in many battles. When they finally fall to the Jedi [Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Revan "Revan") and their Republic forces at [Malachor V](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Malachor_V "Malachor V"), Mandalorians feel a new kind of shame. The hurt of being defeated and dispersed again pales before the knowledge that, in their eagerness to defeat the Republic, they had given up the traits that had made them Mandalorians.

## [The Jedi Civil War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Civil_War "The Jedi Civil War")/[The Dark Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Wars "The Dark Wars")

**3,959-3,951 BBY**

The remaining Mandalorians, scattered and lacking in identity, face [The Jedi Civil War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Civil_War "The Jedi Civil War") and the tough years to follow. Some turn to crime. Others, such as [Canderous Ordo](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Canderous_Ordo "Canderous Ordo"), attempt to restore the Mandalorian movement to its original principles. [The Mandalorian Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorian_Wars "The Mandalorian Wars") are over, but the story of the Mandalorian people will go on. Many Mandalorians become mercenaries, particularly those who bought into the Neo-Crusader movement, as they quickly find themselves falling out of favor with the remaining Mandalorian traditionalists. Honorable Mandalorians continue to carry on their traditions throughout the galaxy, awaiting a leader to reunite them and help restore the clans to their former glory. While the scattered Mandalorians weather [The Jedi Civil War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Civil_War "The Jedi Civil War") and [The Dark Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Wars "The Dark Wars") that follow, they cease to be a major faction in the galactic scene following two devastating defeats in [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War") and [The Mandalorian Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorian_Wars "The Mandalorian Wars").

Yet all hope in not lost for The Mandalorians. Near the end of [The Dark Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Wars "The Dark Wars"), an honorable Mandalorian warrior named [Canderous Ordo](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Canderous_Ordo "Canderous Ordo") takes up the mantle of Mandalore and reunites the Mandalorian clans into one people again. Under his leadership, the clans restore much of their honor in helping defeat the last remaining Sith and begin establishing their place in the galaxy once more.

Even though The Mandalorians' belief system has existed for centuries before [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War"), the years leading up to and including [The Mandalorian Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorian_Wars "The Mandalorian Wars") bring major changes to Mandalorian organization and behavior. The two distinct varieties- the Crusader, or traditional culture, and the Neo-Crusader movement- coexist uneasily in the years between [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War") and the defeat at [Malachor V](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Malachor_V "Malachor V"). The numbers of each vary depending on the time. Early on, Neo-Crusaders are few, but during the heat of the war with [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic"), when their ranks have ballooned from conquered peoples, the majority of Mandalorians are Neo-Crusaders.

Mandalorians from the period after [The Mandalorian Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorian_Wars "The Mandalorian Wars") are a mix of former members of the two groups.

## The Crusaders

Following the traditions of old, Mandalorian Crusaders are a community of individuals who adopt and respect the same warrior tradition. They value abilities more than possessions, understanding that, even though they do settle in places from time to time, there will always be another call to battle beyond the horizon.

Traditional Mandalorians are unafraid of technology, using what they learn to help augment their personal arsenals. Thus, warriors' armor and weaponry can be very different. Many wear personal [Jet Packs](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jet_Packs "Jet Packs") as tools for opening new avenues of attack rather than for escape, because they are honor-bound to stand and fight. Mandalorian armors also have internal environmental systems. In addition to allowing warriors to wear them for long durations, they provide protection against poison gas and permit activity in the vacuum of space.

### Joining the Crusaders

The way to become a Mandalorian is simple. You are a Mandalorian because you choose to be a Mandalorian and because you act like a Mandalorian. Of course, it helps to have other Mandalorians- known collectively in their language as the *Mando'ade*- around, because much of acting like a Mandalorian involves the community. Mandalorian society has no written laws and few norms, but the few that exist are sacred. Chief among these are the *Resol'Nare*, or the Six Actions:

***Ba'jur, beskar'gam,***

***Ara'nov, aliit,***

***Mando'a bal Mand'alor-***

***An vencuyan mhi.***

*Education and armor,*

*Self-defense, out tribe,*

*Our language, our leader-*

*All help us survive.*

A few words, for a people of few words. Raising younglings as Mandalorians; wearing armor; defending one's self and family; helping the clan flourish; speaking *Mando'a*; and rallying to Mandalore's side when needed. It is a simple creed that translates well, given the variety of non-Human [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") represented among The Mandalorians. It is vital to keep these tenets, because someone ignorant of Mandalorian culture is regarded as *dar'manda*- seen as the equivalent as living without a soul. Just as no individual authority figure declares you a Mandalorian, no one figure decides when someone has been unworthy. In both cases, that decision comes organically from the community, accepting or shunning the newcomer.

The traditional Crusaders do not proselytize; rather, they attract other to their cause through the examples they set. Veterans see the later Neo-Crusader movement, which actively converts outsiders in its hurry to conquer the galaxy, as a perversion.

### Life in the Clans

A harsh life awaits newcomers to the Mandalorian ways- particularly those who have spent their lives in the Republic, which The Mandalorians see as soft and decadent. Even though Mandalorians help each other- directing a promising convert to old or spare armor and weapon for an initial outfit- self-sufficiency is a necessity. Meals, weapons, and Starship fuel: all are where you find them.

Fortunately, traditional Mandalorians are not unduly burdened with a respect for the property rights of outsiders. Craftspeople within the Crusader Clans use industrial skills as necessary, but no static infrastructure for formal manufacturing exists for long anywhere other than the planet [Mandalore](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mandalore?action=edit&redlink=1 "Mandalore (page does not exist)"). Thus, much of The Mandalorians' arsenal- from the [Basilisk War Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Basilisk_War_Droids "Basilisk War Droids") they ride to the ships they fly- has traditionally come from somewhere else. The galaxy's greatest flouters of the Republic patent laws, Mandalorians happily adopt technologies found elsewhere for their own purposes.

Mandalorians place great emphasis on the family, prizing chastity before marriage and fidelity thereafter. Children are trained from a young age, and all members of the family share an equal role in its preservation against enemies.

Trade within Mandalorian communities is based on barter, with jewelry and precious metals used as portable commodities alongside weapons and armor. However, Mandalorians understand the currencies used in the galaxy around them, and many have been known to amass fortunes for reinvestment in their gear or ships.

### Crusader Organization

Throughout most of their history, "Mandalorian" and "organization" are contradictory terms. Such is the case with the Crusaders of [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War"). They recognize no official ranks; they do not divide into formal units; they use no command structure of any kind. Respect for Mandalore- their sole leader- and filial obligation to their clans are The Mandalorians' only responsibilities to others.

Mandalorian forces- a term more accurate than "armies"- are amorphous, with information spreading through the warrior mass by sight and sound. Keeping one eye on their comrades enables Mandalorians to respond quickly when the direction of battle changes. Mass movements, too, appear to follow a sort of hive behavior. When respected warriors head to another ridge- or star system- others follow.

## The Neo-Crusaders

The *Resol'Nare* have nothing to say when it comes to bureaucracy, payrolls, or other features found in regular militaries. Of course, nothing in the creed forbids those features from developing, either. Thus, the first Neo-Crusaders are able to argue for a change in the ideal Mandalorian without seeming to offend Mandalorian ideals.

The Neo-Crusader movement springs from Sith War veterans who are discontent about how the Crusaders performed against the Republic and its Jedi. They are excellent at pillaging and destroying Republic installations, but The Mandalorians are not efficient enough at exploiting their conquests. With the Crusader ethic focused on fighting the next battle, the brief Sith War provides The Mandalorians with little time to digest their gains. As such, huge conquered industrial complexes sit idle rather than rearming the *Mando'ade*, and untold millions of beings who might have been brought into the fold are not. The Crusaders are talented at invading but not at occupying or, for that matter, at creating new Crusaders- at least, not at a pace necessary to hold a galaxy.

Ascribing this deficit to the lack of order arising from their nomadic lifestyle, a small group of zealots calling themselves Neo-Crusaders take residence on the planet [Mandalore](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mandalore?action=edit&redlink=1 "Mandalore (page does not exist)"). They wear identical suits of armor, a practice others see as bizarre. Further, they adopt their own rudimentary chain of command and division of labor- again, a feature not found in the traditional culture. Many Mandalorians find this freakish, but a few take interest in what the Neo-Crusaders have to say. And when The Mandalorians again reach out to raid nonaligned worlds, the Neo-Crusaders- with their discipline and willingness to rely more on their helmet transceivers- are winning more than their share of battles.

Sensing an opportunity, Mandalore and aide-de-camp [Cassus Fett](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cassus_Fett "Cassus Fett") encourages the movement. Cassus refines the one-time cult's organizational strategies for use on a larger scale- the big time, as it were. And it becomes big, indeed. The Mandalorians who first tested the Republic in the so-called "False War" find that they are at a distinct advantage with Neo-Crusaders as their vanguard. By the time the wider Mandalorian Wars with the Republic break out, Mandalore has Neo-Crusaders present in every unit, boosting morale and spreading the new ideals of conformity and discipline.

When The Mandalorians take Republic worlds, they immediately locate likely recruits. Shipped to training centers (another concept alien to Mandalorians), the new recruits are taught *by* Neo-Crusaders to *become* Neo-Crusaders. Traditional Mandalorians are encouraged to adopt the system, too- although some are distressed by the changes. The Mandalorians of the past are voluntarily committed to the cause, but Neo-Crusaders increasingly include warriors of poor quality who join to escape occupation or to share in the pillaging.

Having transformed from a grassroots movement to a weapon that nearly conquers a galaxy, the Neo-Crusaders dream ends with their final defeat in [The Mandalorian Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorian_Wars "The Mandalorian Wars").

### Joining the Neo-Crusaders

During the early years of the movement the Neo-Crusaders are ideologically pure. Coming from the ranks of existing Mandalorians, members tend to be young, often children of Crusaders lost in [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War"). Later, after victories against nonaligned worlds, the Neo-Crusaders gain official blessing and begin targeting existing Mandalorian warriors for conversion. Intellectual agreement with the Neo-Crusader approach remains important, and new members who buck authority or show excessive individualism risk being cast out.

Finally, during [The Mandalorian Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorian_Wars "The Mandalorian Wars") when the entire Mandalorian movement is organized according along Neo-Crusader lines, standards quickly begin to collapse. On [Taris](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Taris "Taris"), as on other worlds, [Cassus Fett](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cassus_Fett "Cassus Fett") gives citizens a choice: join The Mandalorians as warriors, or sit quietly and work as manufacturers for the clans as *de facto* slaves. Many- including a host of criminals from the prisons The Mandalorians empty- go with the recruiters to planetary staging centers. There they receive whirlwind indoctrination in the *Resol'Nare* from [Neo-Crusader Rally Masters](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Neo-Crusader_Rally_Masters "Neo-Crusader Rally Masters") before being issued blue Neo-Crusader armor, fresh from the on-site War Forges.

As the size of the conquered population explodes during [The Mandalorian Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorian_Wars "The Mandalorian Wars"), Neo-Crusader training devolves to "put on a helmet and go," as one dissident puts it. The average Neo-Crusader warrior in the Battle of [Malachor V](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Malachor_V "Malachor V") is "average," indeed.

### Life as a Neo-Crusader

In the beginning, Neo-Crusaders are looked upon as weird cultists. Their uniforms and structured approach are as much an offense to Mandalorian senses of style as anything. Many warriors laugh at or avoid them.

When they gain Mandalore's imprimatur in the years leading up to the war on the Republic, they rise quickly in standing. They fashion themselves as "Model Mandalorians," setting examples to be followed by others. Neo-Crusaders initially receive preferential treatment when it comes to receiving armor and weaponry from the new War Forges. Eventually, all new equipment is designed for Neo-Crusaders, leaving the holdouts to fend for themselves.

Neo-Crusaders use their helmet comm systems constantly, and warriors are expected to keep them on to hear any orders coming through.

### Neo-Crusader Organization

Under the structure decreed by Mandalore the Ultimate and implemented by Cassus Fett, the Neo-Crusader recognize four levels of authority. The gulf between ranks is wide and the ratios of underling to superior soon grow huge, but this organization is far more structured than traditional Mandalorians are accustomed to.

**[Mandalorian Neo-Crusader](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mandalorian_Neo-Crusader "Mandalorian Neo-Crusader"):** The basic warrior, Mandalorian Neo-Crusaders wear identical blue armor. The color-coding, [Cassus Fett](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cassus_Fett "Cassus Fett") contends, makes it easier for new recruits to recognize authority even when they do not speak *Mando'a*. A transponder signal is later implemented to help color-blind [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") in this regard.

**[Neo-Crusader Rally Master](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Neo-Crusader_Rally_Master "Neo-Crusader Rally Master"):** Where the Crusaders had formed up around whatever warriors seemed to be most lethal- Rohlan was one- the Rally Masters are Mandalore the Ultimate's trusted lieutenants on the ground. They are called upon for many duties: part drill sergeant, part political officer, part battlefield commander. Wearing crimson Neo-Crusader armor, they are instantly recognizable in the field- and blue Neo-Crusaders are expected to find and heed them. Formal units do not exist at the outbreak of [The Mandalorian Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorian_Wars "The Mandalorian Wars"), so warriors are expected to obey the Rally Master nearest them. Since they all presumably speak with Mandalore's voice, this division of command is enough.

**[Neo-Crusader Marshal](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Neo-Crusader_Marshal "Neo-Crusader Marshal"):** [Cassus Fett](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cassus_Fett "Cassus Fett") is among the first of Mandalore's top advisors to accept the gold-colored Neo-Crusader armor as an example that even those at the top should give up individualism. But the gold armor's value as a status symbol quickly deteriorates. Some, such as science advisor [Demagol](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Demagol "Demagol"), refuse to take on the new appearance- and [Mandalore the Ultimate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mandalore_the_Ultimate "Mandalore the Ultimate") soon distributes the golden armor as rewards to everyone from his personal vanguard to anyone who desires differentiation from the Rally Masters- all the way down to Demagol's own aids. Admitting defeat on this one point, Fett later argues for unique armors for the truly special- including himself. But on the battlefield, the shining armor continues to designate the operation's field marshal.

As the population of Neo-Crusaders increase, [Mandalore the Ultimate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mandalore_the_Ultimate "Mandalore the Ultimate") approves new armor packages for special purposes. [Neo-Crusader Shock Troopers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Neo-Crusader_Shock_Troopers "Neo-Crusader Shock Troopers") are the first of several kinds of Neo-Crusader, all with look-alike armor geared toward their tactical specialty.

## The Scattering

During the years following [The Mandalorian Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorian_Wars "The Mandalorian Wars"), The Mandalorians scatter to the galactic winds. Many of those remaining in the Republic stop wearing armor and blend in with the crowd. Others, in more remote areas, attempt to keep quietly to their traditions, but the going is difficult. Many turn to smuggling, bounty hunting, and piracy, plying their battle-hardened skills as best they can. However, not everyone using Mandalorian weapons or armor is Mandalorian. After the wars, Mandalorian artifacts are so plentiful that they are frequently used in other contexts.

[The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians") are a diverse group with members of many [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") represented throughout its forces. ## Allies and Opponents

The galaxy is filled with organizations other than large governments such as [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") and [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire"), or powerful invaders such as [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") or [The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians"). Though they vary in scale and complexity, these other organizations fulfill functions much more vital to the day-to-day needs of most galactic citizens. Some might be employees of interstellar corporations, but others find their niche within underworld criminal operations.

This chapter covers a wide range of entities, corporations, and other groups in the galaxy. It highlights influential- if not prominent- individuals.

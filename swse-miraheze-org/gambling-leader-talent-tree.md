# Gambling Leader Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gambling_Leader_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gambling_Leader_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:05:08Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

You aren't afraid to take chances when leading your allies, and you risk defeat in exchange for a shot at even the most unlikely victories. You put yourself on the front lines to encourage your allies to overcome long odds.

## Core Talents

### Assault Gambit

Once per turn, as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can designate one ally and one enemy that have [Line of Effect](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Effect "Line of Effect") to each other. The ally and the enemy make opposed [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative") checks, and the winner can make a single immediate [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") against the loser. No character can benefit from this Talent more than once per round.

### Direct Fire

*Prerequisite:* **Assault Gambit**

Once per encounter, as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can designate one ally and one target that does not have [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") from you. Until the start of your next turn, the ally you designate ignores the target's [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") bonuses to [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense").

### Face the Foe

If you do not have [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") from a target, you gain a +1 morale bonus to attack rolls against that target.

### Lead From the Front

*Prerequisite:* **Face the Foe**

If you do not have [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") from a target that you damaged with a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack"), all your allies gain a +2 morale bonus to attack rolls against that target and a +5 circumstance bonus to opposed [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative") checks against that target until the start of your next turn.

### Luck Favors the Bold

*Prerequisite:* **Face the Foe**

If at least one enemy within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") is aware of you and you do not have [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") against that enemy, at the start of your turn if you are conscious you gain a number of [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") equal to 5 + one-half your Heroic Level. Damage is subtracted from [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") first, and any [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") remaining at the end of the encounter are lost. [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") do not stack.

# Scoundrel Bonus Feats

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrel_Bonus_Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrel_Bonus_Feats) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:05:35Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

At each even-numbered level (2nd, 4th, 6th, etc.), a [Scoundrel](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrel "Scoundrel") gains a Bonus [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat"). This [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat") must be selected from the following Scoundrel Bonus Feats lists, and a [Scoundrel](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrel "Scoundrel") must meet any prerequisites for that [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat").

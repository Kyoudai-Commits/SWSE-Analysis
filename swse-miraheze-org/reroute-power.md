# Reroute Power

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reroute_Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reroute_Power) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:07:32Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

By spending 3 [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions") during the same turn, or on consecutive turns, an [Engineer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Engineer "Engineer") can make a DC 20 [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") check to move their [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") +1 step on the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track").

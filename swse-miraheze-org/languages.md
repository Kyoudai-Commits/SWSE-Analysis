# Languages

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Languages](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Languages) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:03:08Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

The primary Language used throughout known space is called Basic. Most characters can speak Basic, and all characters understand it even if they can't speak it. Nonhuman characters can also speak, read, and write the Language associated with their [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species"); for example, [Bothans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bothans "Bothans") speak, read, and write Bothese as well as Basic. Characters with an [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") bonus know how to speak, read, and write other Languages as well. For each point of [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") bonus, a character can speak, read, and write one additional Language of their choice.

Some [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") ([Gamorreans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gamorreans "Gamorreans") and [Wookiees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wookiees "Wookiees"), for example) are incapable of speaking any other Language other than their native tongue, but they can learn to read and write other Languages just fine.

The *Star Wars* galaxy is home to millions of Languages but picking Languages for your character doesn't need to be daunting. A few of the most common languages are listed below. Among these, Basic is the most widely spoken. Other prevalent languages include Huttese (particularly common in [The Outer Rim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Outer_Rim?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Outer Rim (page does not exist)")), [Binary](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Binary "Binary") (used by [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") and programmers), and Bocce (a trade language that can be spoken by almost any [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species")).

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

In addition to the languages described in Common Languages, Arkanian and Snivvian are considered common Languages in the galaxy during [The Old Republic Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic_Era "The Old Republic Era"). Other Languages might be more common in specific regions of the galaxy. For instance, [The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians") bring Mando'a to the worlds they conquer. Except for the earliest period of the original Sith Empire, [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") of this era rely on Basic and their own native languages rather than any common Sith tongue.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

- Old Corellian is the original language spoken by the people of [Corellia](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Corellia "Corellia") before that world joined the Republic and adopted Basic as the standard language. Though few actually speak Old Corellian these days, the Language is far from dead, and many Corellians still pepper their speech with Old Corellian phrases.
- Socorran is the language spoken by the natives of the volcanic desert world of [Socorro](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Socorro "Socorro"). Like Old Corellian, Socorran has largely been supplanted by Basic, but many pirates, smugglers, and other criminals use it as a secret language known only to those who have spent time on the seedy world.
- Sy Bisti is a trade language spoken on [The Outer Rim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Outer_Rim?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Outer Rim (page does not exist)") and also in parts of [The Unknown Regions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Unknown_Regions "The Unknown Regions"). Though the language is not as widespread as Bocce, many inhabitants of worlds on the edge of [The Unknown Regions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Unknown_Regions "The Unknown Regions") and [Wild Space](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wild_Space?action=edit&redlink=1 "Wild Space (page does not exist)") use Sy Bisti as a common language between [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") that have not learned Basic.

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

Although [The Unknown Regions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Unknown_Regions "The Unknown Regions") have no widespread common language such as Basic, several Languages are regularly used among specific nomadic groups and within some systems and regions. Trade languages are commonly used to bridge the formidable language gap.

# The Witches of Dathomir

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Witches_of_Dathomir](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Witches_of_Dathomir) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:03:56Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

The Witches of Dathomir are part of a primitive culture with a strong yet rudimentary grasp of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). Though much of their own knowledge is derived from [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") arts, over the centuries their lifestyle and philosophies have shaped their knowledge of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to fit their own needs. The Witches of Dathomir train and ride fearsome [Dathomiri Rancors](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dathomiri_Rancors "Dathomiri Rancors"), using [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to communicate with them. Though their tribal organization makes them seem primitive, they are sophisticated and knowledgeable Force-users who, despite their rustic lifestyle, have evolved Force powers seemingly unheard of in other sects.

Native to the planet of [Dathomir](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dathomir?action=edit&redlink=1 "Dathomir (page does not exist)"), The Witches of Dathomir were the first non-Jedi Force-users encountered by [Luke Skywalker](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Luke_Skywalker "Luke Skywalker") during his quest to rebuild [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order"). Some 600 years before [The Clone Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Clone_Wars "The Clone Wars"), [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order") banished a Jedi named [Allya](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Allya?action=edit&redlink=1 "Allya (page does not exist)") to the primitive planet of [Dathomir](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dathomir?action=edit&redlink=1 "Dathomir (page does not exist)"). In exile, she founded her own sect of Force adepts, selectively breeding with a group of exiled arms dealers, and within decades had created a Force-using society to rule. Though ruthless, [Allya](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Allya?action=edit&redlink=1 "Allya (page does not exist)") maintained a strict rule of law (even authoring a tome called the Book of Law for her followers to use as an example). After Allya's death, the society fragmented into six clans, and despite Allya's command to never concede to evil, some of the witches she trained fell to [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"), becoming the Nightsisters.

**Membership:** Only female natives of [Dathomir](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dathomir?action=edit&redlink=1 "Dathomir (page does not exist)") (from [The Rise of the Empire Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Rise_of_the_Empire_Era "The Rise of the Empire Era") onward) with the [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity") feat can be members of The Witches of Dathomir. Members of The Witches of Dathomir can select [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") from the [Dathomiri Witch Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dathomiri_Witch_Talent_Tree "Dathomiri Witch Talent Tree").

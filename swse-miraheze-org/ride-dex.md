# Ride (Dex)

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ride](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ride) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:06:32Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

Ride skill checks are affected by an **[Armor Check Penalty](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor_Check_Penalty "Armor Check Penalty")** only when attempting to Fast Mount or Fast Dismount.

Use this skill to ride any kind of mount, such as a [Tauntaun](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tauntaun "Tauntaun"), [Dewback](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dewback "Dewback"), or [Bantha](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bantha "Bantha").

**Special:** You can [Take 10](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_10 "Take 10") when making a Ride check, but you can't [Take 20](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_20 "Take 20").

**Time:** Ride is a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action"), except when otherwise noted.

## Applications

The following are the primary applications of the Ride skill.

### Ride Mount

Typical riding actions don't require checks. You can saddle, mount, ride, and dismount without a problem. Mounting or dismounting an animal is a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action"). Some tasks, such as those undertaken in combat or other extreme circumstances, require checks. In addition, attempting trick riding or convincing the animal to do something unusual also requires a check.

| RIDING TASK | DC |
| --- | --- |
| Guide with knees | 10 |
| Stay in saddle | 10 |
| Cover | 15 |
| Soft Fall | 15 |
| Leap | 15 |
| Control mount in battle | 20 |
| Fast Mount or Dismount | 20\* |

*\*[Armor Check Penalty](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor_Check_Penalty "Armor Check Penalty") applies.*

### Control Mount in Battle

As a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action"), you can attempt to control a mount while in combat. If you fail, you can do nothing else that round. You do not need to roll for riding animals specifically trained for battle.

### Fast Mount or Dismount

You can Mount or Dismount as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"). If you fail the check, Mounting or Dismounting is a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action"). (You can't attempt a Fast Mount or Dismount unless you can perform the Mount or Dismount as a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action") this round, should the check fail.)

### Guide with Knees

You can react instantly to guide your mount with your knees so that you can use both hands in combat or to perform some other action. Make the check at the start of your turn. If you fail, you can only use one hand this round because you need the other to control your mount.

### Leap

You can get your mount to Leap obstacles as part of its movement. Use your Ride skill modifier or the mount's [Jump](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jump "Jump") skill modifier, whichever is lower, to see how far the mount can [Jump](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jump "Jump"). A DC 15 Ride check is required to stay on the mount when it leaps.

### Soft Fall

You react instantly when you fall off a mount, such as when it is killed or when it falls, to avoid taking damage. If you fail, you take 1d6 points of [Falling Damage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Falling_Damage "Falling Damage").

### Stay in Saddle

You can react instantly to try to avoid falling when your mount rears or bolts unexpectedly or when you take damage.

### Use Mount as Cover

As a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") to an attack against you, you can drop down and hang alongside your mount, using it as one-half [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover"). You can't attack while using your mount as [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover"). If you fail, you don't get the [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") benefit.

## Mounted Combat

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

The Ride skill, along with associated [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") and [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") found in this and other *Star Wars Roleplaying Game Saga Edition* supplements, covers fighting from a living mount. The following combat actions can also be used with mounts, by characters [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in the Ride skill.

Riding a mount in combat is much like riding a [Speeder Bike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speeder_Bike "Speeder Bike") or another [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle"). The [Beast](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Beast "Beast") uses the rider's Actions, such as a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action") to move up to its Speed, or a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") to use its natural weapons, or 3 [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions") to allow the mount to move up the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") with the [Recover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Recover "Recover") Action.

For the most part, the mount is treated as being under the rider's control, especially if the creature has been trained for battle. Obviously, being [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in Ride helps maximize the benefit of using a live mount in battle. Typically, a mount has better speed than its rider, or it possesses a means of travel that its rider does not, such as exceptional climbing ability or flight.

When an enemy provokes an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity"), both you and your mount can each make a single attack once per round. You can elect for both you and your mount to take the [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity") simultaneously in response to the same provoking Action, or you can save one or both attacks for a future [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity") in the same round.

Having the [Combat Reflexes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Combat_Reflexes "Combat Reflexes") Feat allows you or your mount to make additional attacks, but you cannot share extra attacks with your mount, or vice versa.

You can [Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge "Charge") while riding a mount. As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can move your mount's [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") (minimum 4 squares) in a straight line, and then you or your mount can make a melee attack at the end of movement. You or your mount gain a +2 competence bonus to the attack roll and take a -2 penalty to [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") until the start of your next turn.

You can [Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge "Charge") through squares containing [Low Objects](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Low_Objects "Low Objects") and [Difficult Terrain](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Difficult_Terrain "Difficult Terrain"), but doing so costs twice as much per square as normal. You can [Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge "Charge") through squares occupied by enemies smaller than the size of your mount, but not squares occupied by enemies of the same size as or larger than your mount.

After you resolve a Mounted Charge attack, you can take no further [Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Actions "Actions") this turn. You can use [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") that modify the [Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge "Charge") Action when making a Mounted Charge.

A mount that has a [Heroic Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Class "Heroic Class") or the [Extra Second Wind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Extra_Second_Wind "Extra Second Wind") feat can [Catch a Second Wind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Catch_a_Second_Wind "Catch a Second Wind") once per day as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"). This Action heals one-quarter of the mount's full [Hit Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Point "Hit Point") total (rounded down) or a number of points equal to its [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") score, whichever is greater.

A mount voluntarily falling into a [Prone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prone "Prone") position requires a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"). If the mount is [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in the [Acrobatics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatics "Acrobatics") skill, it can fall [Prone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prone "Prone") as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action") with a successful DC 15 [Acrobatics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatics "Acrobatics") check.

Standing a mount up from a [Prone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prone "Prone") position requires a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action"). A mount [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in the [Acrobatics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatics "Acrobatics") skill can stand up as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") with a successful DC 15 [Acrobatics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatics "Acrobatics") check.

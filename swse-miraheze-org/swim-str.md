# Swim (Str)

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swim) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:07:45Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

Swim skill checks are affected by an **[Armor Check Penalty](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor_Check_Penalty "Armor Check Penalty")**.

Using this skill, a land-based creature can swim, dive, navigate underwater obstacles, and so on.

**Special:** You can [Take 10](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_10 "Take 10") when making a Swim check, but you can't [Take 20](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_20 "Take 20").

**Retry:** A new check is allowed the round after a check is failed.

## Applications

The following is the primary application of the Swim skill.

### Swim

A successful Swim check allows you to swim one-quarter your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") as a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action") or one-half your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") as a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"). Roll once per round. If you fail, you make no progress through the water. If you fail by 5 or more, you go underwater and must hold your breath with an [Endurance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Endurance "Endurance") check until you can reach the surface by succeeding on a Swim check.

The DC for the Swim check depends on the situation:

| SITUATION |  | DC |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Calm Water |  | 10 |
| Rough Water |  | 15 |
| Stormy Water |  | 20 |

# Ambusher Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ambusher_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ambusher_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:05:07Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

You specialize in springing ambushes on your enemies.

## Core Talents

### Ambush Specialist

If you are not [Surprised](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surprised "Surprised") on the first round of combat in an encounter, you can treat the first round of combat as if it were the [Surprise Round](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surprise_Round "Surprise Round") for the purposes of [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") and [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") that trigger only during the [Surprise Round](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surprise_Round "Surprise Round"). Additionally, during the [Surprise Round](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surprise_Round "Surprise Round") as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action") you can designate a target as your Prime Target. You gain a +2 morale bonus to attack rolls against your Prime Target until the end of the encounter.

### Destructive Ambusher

*Prerequisite:* **Ambush Specialist**

After you designate a Prime Target, you deal +1 die of damage on attacks against the Prime Target until the end of the encounter.

### Keep It Going

*Prerequisite:* **Ambush Specialist**

If you reduce your Prime Target to 0 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points"), as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action") you can designate another target within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") as your new Prime Target. This new target remains your Prime Target until the end of the encounter.

### Keep Them Reeling

*Prerequisite:* **Ambush Specialist**

Once per turn as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can make an [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative") check, opposed by the [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative") check of your Prime Target. If your check result equals or exceeds your Prime Target's check result, your target is [Flat-Footed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flat-Footed "Flat-Footed") against all attacks you make before the end of your turn.

### Perceptive Ambusher

*Prerequisite:* **Ambush Specialist**

You gain a +5 circumstance bonus to [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") checks against your Prime Target until the end of the encounter.

### Spring the Trap

If you and all your allies roll higher [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative") checks to start combat than do all your opponents, you automatically gain a [Surprise Round](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surprise_Round "Surprise Round"), even if the opponents are aware of you when combat begins.

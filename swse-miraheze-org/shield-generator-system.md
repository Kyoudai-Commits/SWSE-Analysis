# Shield Generator System

A Droid can be fitted with a deflector Shield Generator- the same type mounted on [Starships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships "Starships"). Whenever the Droid would take damage, reduce the damage by the Droid's [Shield Rating](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shield_Rating "Shield Rating") (SR). If the damage is equal to or greater than the Droid's [Shield Rating](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shield_Rating "Shield Rating"), the Droid's SR is reduced by 5. By spending three [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions") on the same or consecutive rounds, the Droid may make a DC 20 [Endurance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Endurance "Endurance") check to restore lost shield power. If the check succeeds, the Droid's [Shield Rating](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shield_Rating "Shield Rating") increases by 5 points (up to its normal SR).

Due to the size and energy requirements of Shield Generator Systems, only Droids of Small size or larger can be equipped with a SR 10 Generator. Only Droids of Medium size or larger can be equipped with a SR 15 generator. Only Droids of Large or bigger size can be equipped with a SR 20 generator.

# Wheeled

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wheeled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wheeled) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:08:23Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

**Base Speed:** Small (6 squares); Medium (8 squares); Large or bigger (10 squares)

**Cost:** 5 x [Cost Factor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cost_Factor "Cost Factor") x (Base Speed Squared)

Wheeled Droids use one or more powered wheels to move and are generally designed to traverse smooth surfaces. Wheeled Droids can't use the [Climb](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Climb "Climb") skill, and the penalties of moving through [Difficult Terrain](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Difficult_Terrain "Difficult Terrain") are doubled.

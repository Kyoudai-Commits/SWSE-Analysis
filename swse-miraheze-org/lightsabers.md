# Lightsabers

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:04:56Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

This group includes the standard [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") (a handgrip hilt that projects a single blade of energy) and all its variants, including the [Double-Bladed Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double-Bladed_Lightsaber "Double-Bladed Lightsaber") (as wielded by Darth Maul in *Episode I: the Phantom Menace*). Lightsabers ignore [Damage Reduction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Reduction "Damage Reduction") unless noted otherwise. However, a target's [Shield Rating](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shield_Rating "Shield Rating") reduces a Lightsaber's damage normally. [Weapon Proficiency (Lightsabers)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Lightsabers) "Weapon Proficiency (Lightsabers)") is a Starting Feat for [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi").

**Q:** There are a lot of custom Lightsabers in *Star Wars*, but my group can't decide if they all fall under the Lightsabers Weapon Group. For example, would Lumiya's [Lightwhip](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightwhip "Lightwhip") or the [Lightsaber Tonfa](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Tonfa "Lightsaber Tonfa") used in *The Force Unleashed* video game be considered Lightsabers or [Exotic Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapons "Exotic Weapons")?

**A:** Both the [Lightwhip](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightwhip "Lightwhip") and the [Guard Shoto](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Guard_Shoto "Guard Shoto"), also known as the [Lightsaber Tonfa](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Tonfa "Lightsaber Tonfa"), are part of the Lightsabers Weapon Group.

[Weapon Groups](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Groups "Weapon Groups") are pretty broad; for example, "[Simple Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Simple_Weapons "Simple Weapons")" includes [Nets](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nets "Nets"), [Slings](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Slings "Slings"), [Clubs](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clubs "Clubs"), [Knives](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knives "Knives"), and many others. We usually don't separate a weapon and call it exotic unless it's particularly bizarre or unusual in design. A [Flamethrower](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flamethrower "Flamethrower"), for example, operates by shooting flaming liquid. As a result, you have to arc your shots and anticipate the liquid's splash to get the most out of the weapon, and this is very different from the simple point-and-shoot of a blaster or even a [Missile Launcher](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Missile_Launcher "Missile Launcher").

Similarly, a weapon whose design is sufficiently atypical may earn the exotic designation, and this combined with the Weapon Familiarity trait can provide a little local flavor. [Wookiees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wookiees "Wookiees") can use [Bowcasters](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bowcasters "Bowcasters") and [Gungans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gungans "Gungans") can use [Atlatls](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Atlatls "Atlatls") because they were raised around them, but offworld characters need to put in a little extra training to effectively operate these unfamiliar weapons.

### Training Lightsabers

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

There are two effective ways to build a less lethal Lightsaber, sometimes known as a Training Lightsaber: using either a [Bondar Crystal](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bondar_Crystal "Bondar Crystal") or a [Kathracite Crystal](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kathracite_Crystal "Kathracite Crystal").

A [Bondar Crystal](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bondar_Crystal "Bondar Crystal") is used in the creation of the safest Lightsabers, given to younglings for basic weapon training in the Jedi Temple. [Bondar Crystals](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bondar_Crystals "Bondar Crystals") create wholly nonlethal Lightsabers that merely shock the target and do not actually cut into it. Typically, Lightsabers with [Bondar Crystals](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bondar_Crystals "Bondar Crystals") see use during sparring sessions between Padawans, as they allow the Jedi to actually strike the target with what would be a serious blow without permanent harm. [Kathracite Crystals](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kathracite_Crystals?action=edit&redlink=1 "Kathracite Crystals (page does not exist)"), on the other hand, produces a more traditional Lightsaber blade, albeit a weaker one that is less likely to cause serious harm in case of an accident.

By the time an adventuring [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") begins their career, they have likely abandoned Training Lightsabers entirely and uses a full-strength [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber"), albeit not one of their own creation. Lightsabers with a [Bondar Crystal](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bondar_Crystal "Bondar Crystal") or [Kathracite Crystal](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kathracite_Crystal "Kathracite Crystal") usually appear in the hands of younglings, or are kept safely in reserve by the Jedi Temples and Academies for when new students come of age.

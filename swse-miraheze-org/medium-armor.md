# Medium Armor

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Medium_Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Medium_Armor) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:05:43Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

Considered the standard other [Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor "Armor") compare themselves to, Medium Armor is typically used by military troopers, and various militia. All Medium Armor shares the following characteristics unless otherwise specified:

- Medium Armor inflicts a -5 [Armor Check Penalty](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor_Check_Penalty "Armor Check Penalty") (negated by [Armor Proficiency (Medium)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor_Proficiency_(Medium) "Armor Proficiency (Medium)"))
- If a character has a base [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") of 6 Squares, Medium Armor reduces it to 4 Squares
- If a character has a base [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") of 4 Squares, Medium Armor reduces it to 3 Squares

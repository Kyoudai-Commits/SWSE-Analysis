# Unpredictable Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unpredictable_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unpredictable_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:05:18Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

You make it difficult for enemies to harm you because you're never in the place where they expect you to be. When they do manage to hurt you, you make them pay for it with sudden retribution attacks.

## Core Talents

### Aggressive Surge

Once per encounter when you catch a [Second Wind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Second_Wind "Second Wind"), you can make a [Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge "Charge") attack as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action"), provided that you can make a [Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge "Charge") attack against a legal target at the time you catch a [Second Wind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Second_Wind "Second Wind").

### Blast Back

Once per round when you are damaged by an enemy's [Area Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attack "Area Attack"), as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") you can make an immediate [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") against the source of the [Area Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attack "Area Attack"), provided that you have [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") to the attacker and the target is within your melee or ranged reach.

### Fade Away

Once per turn when you are damaged by an enemy's attack, as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") you can [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") up to half your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed"). This movement does not provoke [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity").

### Second Strike

*Prerequisite:* **Blast Back**

Once per encounter when you miss a target with a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack"), as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action") you can [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") up to half your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") and make a second attack of the same type against a different target. This movement doesn't provoke [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity"). If you have the [Combat Reflexes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Combat_Reflexes "Combat Reflexes") feat, you may use this Talent a number of times per encounter equal to your [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus (minimum 1). You may still only use this Talent once per round.

### Swerve

*Prerequisite:* **Fade Away**

Once per encounter when an enemy makes an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity") against you, as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") you can automatically negate the attack and immediately [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") up to half your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed"). This movement does not provoke [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity"). If you have the [Combat Reflexes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Combat_Reflexes "Combat Reflexes") feat, you may use this Talent a number of times per encounter equal to your [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus (minimum 1). You may still only use this Talent once per round.

# Pistols

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pistols](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pistols) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:04:35Z.

| DISTANCE | RANGE | PENALTY |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-20 Squares | Point-Blank | No penalty |
| 21-40 Squares | Short | -2 to attack rolls |
| 41-60 Squares | Medium | -5 to attack rolls |
| 61-80 Squares | Long | -10 to attack rolls |

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

Hand-held ranged weapons, favored for self-defense, and are easily concealed, Pistols are ranged weapons that can be fired with one hand. [Weapon Proficiency (Pistols)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Pistols) "Weapon Proficiency (Pistols)") is a Starting Feat for [Nobles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nobles "Nobles"), [Scoundrels](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrels "Scoundrels"), [Scouts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scouts "Scouts"), and [Soldiers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldiers "Soldiers").

A [Rifle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rifle "Rifle") with a folded [Retractable Stock](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Retractable_Stock "Retractable Stock") is also treated as a Pistol.

# Ranged Attacks

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attacks) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:05:54Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

With a ranged weapon as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can throw or shoot at any target within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") if there are no obstructions (including other characters) between you and the target. The maximum Range of a ranged weapon depends on the weapon used.

**Ranged Attack: 1d20 + [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") + [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") Modifier + Range Modifier**

- *[Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus"):* Your [Heroic Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Class "Heroic Class") and level determine your [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus").
- *[Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") Modifier:* Since [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") measures coordination and steadiness, your [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") modifier applies to attacks with ranged weapons.
- *Range Modifier:* A ranged weapon can attack a target at Point-Blank, Short, Medium, or Long Range. If you make a ranged attack against a target within the weapon's Point-Blank Range, you take no penalty on the attack roll; your penalty on attack rolls increases to
  - -2 at Short Range,
  - -5 at Medium Range,
  - and -10 at Long Range.

**Ranged Damage: Weapon Dice + One-Half Heroic Level (Rounded Down)**

- *Weapon Dice:* A hit always deals at least 1 point of damage, even if penalties to damage bring the damage result below 1.
- *One-Half Heroic Level:* Weapons are simply more dangerous in the hands of powerful heroes (and villains).
  - Beasts add half their [Beast](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Beast "Beast") Level (rounded down) to damage rolls made with Natural Weapons.
- *Special:* When you hit with a Thrown Weapon, you add your [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") modifier to damage.

Vehicle CombatMass Combat

With a ranged weapon, you can throw or shoot at any target within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"), as long as your [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") does not grant you [Total Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Total_Cover "Total Cover") ([Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range") modifiers apply normally for your weapon). You cannot make a personal ranged attack in [Starship Scale](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Scale "Starship Scale"), except within the confines of the [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") you occupy.

For example, [Clone Troopers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Troopers "Clone Troopers") can fire their blasters at targets outside a [LAAT/i Gunship](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/LAAT/i_Gunship "LAAT/i Gunship"), as long as the ship's doors are open.

All [Battalions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalions "Battalions") can make [Melee Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attacks "Melee Attacks") and Ranged Attacks if they possess the appropriate weaponry. Apply the bonuses to the attack roll and compare the result to the [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") of the target, as normal. A Battalion's attack bonuses are listed in its statistics blocks.

A [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion") can make Ranged Attacks against [Battalions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalions "Battalions") within its weapons' Ranges.

## Range

All ranged weapons apply the wielder's [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") modifier to the attack roll. Ranged attacks made at Point-Blank Range suffer no penalty. However, Ranged Attacks made at Short, Medium, or Long Range take a penalty on the attack roll, as presented in the below table.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Point-Blank Shot, Rangefinders, and Targeting ScopesPoint-Blank Shot, Rangefinders, and Targeting Scopes

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

Under most circumstances, a character with the [Point-Blank Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Point-Blank_Shot "Point-Blank Shot") Feat receives a bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls only when the target is within his or her weapon's Point-Blank [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range").

The use of a [Targeting Scope](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Targeting_Scope "Targeting Scope") does not change how far your target is from you, so taking an [Aim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aim "Aim") Action with a [Targeting Scope](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Targeting_Scope "Targeting Scope") on a [Blaster Rifle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Blaster_Rifle "Blaster Rifle") to attack a target that is 50 squares away does not give you the +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls that you would receive if the target were within 30 squares. This is the same for the [Rangefinder](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rangefinder "Rangefinder") upgrade, which negates the penalty for attacking a target that is at Short [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range").

## Attacking into a Melee

If you shoot a ranged weapon or throw a weapon at an opponent that is adjacent to one or more of your allies, you take a -5 penalty on your attack roll. This penalty accounts for the fact that you're trying not to hit your allies.

If you have the [Precise Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Precise_Shot "Precise Shot") feat, you don't take this penalty.

# The Baran Do Sages

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Baran_Do_Sages](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Baran_Do_Sages) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:04:51Z.

DescriptionHistoryLocationPhilosophyBuilding a Baran Do Sage

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

The Baran Do Sages are an obscure group of [Kel Dor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kel_Dor "Kel Dor") seers and mystics who engage in deep contemplation with [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to look into the future and assist their people. They empty their minds to allow [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to provide it's wisdom and secrets. These quiet masters of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") sit in silence, listening to the ebb and flow of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), before leaping into action. Although humble and introspective, The Baran Do Sages are full of confidence, relying on [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to guide their deeds.

**Membership:** Any [Kel Dor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kel_Dor "Kel Dor") with the [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity") Feat is eligible to join The Baran Do Sages. Members of The Baran Do Sages can select [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") from the [Baran Do Sage Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Baran_Do_Sage_Talent_Tree "Baran Do Sage Talent Tree").

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

This [Kel Dor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kel_Dor "Kel Dor") Force Tradition has lasted for thousands of years. The Baran Do Sages serve as seers and advisors to leaders, using their talents to discern the best path to take for the good of the [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species"). They are called upon to aso help investigate crimes, reveal secrets that only [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") could uncover, and even help predict the weather so farmers can better raise their crops. Time and time again, the insights of The Baran Do Sages help avert disaster for the [Kel Dor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kel_Dor "Kel Dor").

Upon entry into the Galactic Republic, increasing numbers of Baran Do Sages and Force-sensitive [Kel Dor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kel_Dor "Kel Dor") younglings are indoctrinated into the Jedi Order. Although The Baran Do Sages never entirely go away, their numbers dwindle to such a degree that eventually the group becomes little more than a remnant in [Kel Dor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kel_Dor "Kel Dor") consciousness, remembered only for being mystics and sorcerers. Despite this, the teachings of The Baran Do Sages made a huge impact on the study and understanding of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") by [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"), who incorporate various Baran Do techniques into their own teachings. Faded into obscurity, The Baran Do Sages manage to survive the Great Jedi Purge by [Darth Vader](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Vader "Darth Vader"), and their numbers slowly increase again during the time of [The New Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_New_Republic "The New Republic"), once more providing wisdom and insight for the good of the [Kel Dor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kel_Dor "Kel Dor") people.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

The Baran Do Sages are found almost exclusively on on [Dorin](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dorin "Dorin"), the homeworld of the [Kel Dor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kel_Dor "Kel Dor"), contemplating the nature of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") and advising their people on important matters. However, because of their ability to discern the future, the sages sometimes depart from their home planet, following the will of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). In this way, they can be found almost anywhere in the galaxy. Consummate investigators, these traveling sages find themselves on bustling worlds and backwater planets alike, seeking knowledge or following slender threads of information provided by [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). Although capable fighters, they often travel with well-armed bodyguards or native guides to ensure their safety. Heroes might be asked to protect a Baran Do Sage on a mission or to help return them safely to [Dorin](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dorin "Dorin") with their vital information.

Heroes might be drawn to the Temple of The Baran Do Sages on [Dorin](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dorin "Dorin"), searching for specific information or advice on the best way to proceed. The Baran Do Sages maintain good relations with [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") and willingly provide assistance to them or their allies as needed. During the time of the Great Jedi Purge, renegade Jedi could also find safe haven at the temple, although getting there is fraught with danger.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

The Baran Do Sages emphasize contemplation and insight over brash impulse. Every thought, deed, and action of a Baran Do Sage is carefully deliberated, using [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to help guide them. A Baran Do Sage seeks peace and stillness, for a quiet mind is when [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") speaks the clearest. Powerful Baran Do Sages use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to look into the past, the present, and the future for answers and receive visions about individuals to which they have no other connection. They are also used to investigate "unsolvable" crimes using [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), which lets them see the perpetrator in the act or to locate missing evidence.

The [Kel Dor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kel_Dor "Kel Dor") believe that children born with silver irises are unusually strong in [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). The Baran Do Sages seek out these younglings, along with any other children who show potential. Raised in the peaceful surroundings of the Baran Do Temple, young recruits are taught patience and stillness, including different meditation techniques and exercises to expand the mind. Sensory deprivation is a common technique, forcing a pupil to rely on other senses (Or none at all), or sensory overload, where the younglings must solve puzzles or deduce patterns while bombarded with loud noises, jostling crowds, or an overpowering stench. The training is neither rigorous nor physically taxing, but encourages introspection and thoughtfulness at all times. The Baran Do Sages are not adverse to opening their doors to any [Kel Dor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kel_Dor "Kel Dor") with [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity") and a willingness to learn and achieve calmness.

Baran Do Sages never rush to judgement or action, weighing every consequence with equal care. Outsiders may misconstrue this inaction as indecision, fear, or even laziness, but nothing is further from the truth. When a Baran Do Sage acts, they do so with utter confidence and purpose- days of silent meditation replaced by a flurry of activity and movement. Baran Do Sages are constantly on the hunt for solid facts to back up their visions and might range far into the galaxy to seek answers.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

True Baran Do Sages are members of the [Kel Dor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kel_Dor "Kel Dor") Species, so to build a Baran Do Sage you will want to make your hero a [Kel Dor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kel_Dor "Kel Dor"). That being true, it is possible that your hero is an exception to the rule, and that somehow you convinced the [Kel Dor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kel_Dor "Kel Dor") to train you despite being a member of another [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species"). (If you choose this background, you'll need your Gamemaster's permission, and you should probably find a way to explain how you survived in the poisonous atmosphere of [Dorin](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dorin "Dorin")). There are some questions you might want to answer when coming up with your hero's background. Why were you trained as a Baran Do Sage instead of a Jedi? Did you seek out training, or did the Sages choose you? What [Destiny](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny "Destiny") have you seen for yourself in the future? What knowledge of the future and the past do you now possess, and how might that motivate you?

Since The Baran Do Sages focus many of their efforts on attuning themselves to the flow of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), when building your hero you will likely want to choose [Force Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Talents "Force Talents") from the [Sense Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sense_Talent_Tree "Sense Talent Tree"). Chief among those is **[Visions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Visions "Visions")**, which in turn requires the *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")* Force Power. Likewise, The Baran Do Sage ability to see into the future is well represented by the **[Foresight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Foresight "Foresight")** Talent, as well as the **[Motion of the Future](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Motion_of_the_Future "Motion of the Future")** Talent. Given that The Baran Do Sages use esoteric techniques, the [Mystic Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mystic_Talent_Tree "Mystic Talent Tree") for the [Force Adept](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Adept "Force Adept") could reflect the methods your hero uses to tap into [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). Lastly, The Baran Do Sages are masters of introspection and meditation; many of their techniques are suitably represented by the standard [Force Regimens](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Regimens "Force Regimens"), and these can reflect your hero's dedication to the techniques taught by Baran Do Sage masters.

When building a hero that is a Baran Do Sage, you should consider your [Heroic Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Class "Heroic Class") and [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") selection carefully. The Baran Do Sages are the consummate [Force Adepts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Adepts "Force Adepts"), so levels in the [Force Adept](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Adept "Force Adept") Prestige Class are practically a necessity. Before going into a [Prestige Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Class "Prestige Class"), your hero is likely to take levels in the [Noble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble "Noble") Class (reflecting the hero's dedication to skill over combat technique) or the [Scout](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scout "Scout") Class (reflecting the hero's ability to survive on the harsh world of [Dorin](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dorin "Dorin")). Your hero is more likely to take [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") that enhance [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill") use or provide durability and protection, such as [Improved Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improved_Defenses "Improved Defenses") or [Toughness](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Toughness "Toughness"), as opposed to more offense-oriented [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") such as [Weapon Focus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus "Weapon Focus").

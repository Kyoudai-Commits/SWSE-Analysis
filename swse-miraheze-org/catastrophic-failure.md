# Catastrophic Failure

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Catastrophic_Failure](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Catastrophic_Failure) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:08:34Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy of Intrigue](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_of_Intrigue "Galaxy of Intrigue")*

The Catastrophic Failure effect represents the fact that the heroes' plight is so delicate that a significant Failure causes the situation to worsen rapidly. When a hero attempts an action and fails by a large margin, that Failure moves the characters much closer to ultimate defeat. In a [Skill Challenge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Challenge "Skill Challenge") with the Catastrophic Failure effect, if a participant fails a [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") by 10 or more, the heroes accrue two Failures instead of one. For example, you might use this effect when the heroes are navigating an extremely dangerous asteroid field or trying to maintain a low profile in the seat of their enemy's power.

**Use the Catastrophic Failure Effect when:**

- Dire failure has a chance of producing dire consequences.
- You want to increase tension in challenges with lower CLs.
- You want to encourage heroes to aid the [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks") of their allies instead of making their own checks.

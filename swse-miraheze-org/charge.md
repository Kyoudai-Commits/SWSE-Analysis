# Charge

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:05:50Z.

*Reference Books: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook"), [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide"), [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") you can move your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") (minimum 2 squares) in a straight line, and then make a melee attack at the end of your movement. You gain a +2 competence bonus on your attack roll, but take a -2 penalty to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") until the start of your next turn.

You cannot Charge through low objects or squares occupied by opponents (allies do not hinder your Charge), and Charging through [Difficult Terrain](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Difficult_Terrain "Difficult Terrain") costs twice as much per square as normal. You cannot [Tumble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tumble "Tumble") during your movement as a part of a Charge. After you resolve a Charge, you can take no further [Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Actions "Actions") this turn (although other [Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Actions "Actions") can be taken prior to the Charge).

If you are [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Acrobatics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatics "Acrobatics"), you can make a DC 25 [Acrobatics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatics "Acrobatics") check to Charge through [Low Objects](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Low_Objects "Low Objects"). If you fail this check, you cannot attack at the end of your movement. You can combine this with the [Cross Difficult Terrain](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cross_Difficult_Terrain "Cross Difficult Terrain") application; however, the DC increases to 35.

## Mass Combat

The [Field Commander](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Field_Commander "Field Commander") can order the [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion") to Charge, moving toward an enemy and making an attack in one Action. As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), the [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion") moves up to its [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") and then makes a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") against a target [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion") or [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") in the same square or a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") against a [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion") within weapon range.

The Charging [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion") gains a +2 bonus on its attack roll but takes a -2 penalty to its [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") until the start of its next turn. If a [Field Commander](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Field_Commander "Field Commander") orders a [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion") to Charge, the [Attack Leader](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_Leader "Attack Leader") cannot order the [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion") to attack until the beginning of the Field Commander's next turn.

# Biotech General Template

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Biotech_General_Template](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Biotech_General_Template) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:04:49Z.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

The term "Biotech" is a new phrase used to describe the biological technology introduced by the [Yuuzhan Vong](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong "Yuuzhan Vong"). The term Biotech can be applied to devices, weapons, armor, and even [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles"), and it acts as a descriptor that indicates certain new rules mechanics. A Biotech object is defined as any object of distinct [Yuuzhan Vong](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong "Yuuzhan Vong") origin, such as [Amphistaffs](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Amphistaffs "Amphistaffs"), [Razor Bugs](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Razor_Bugs "Razor Bugs"), [Thud Bugs](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Thud_Bugs "Thud Bugs"), and [Vonduun Crabshell](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vonduun_Crabshell "Vonduun Crabshell"); additionally, any object with the Biotech General Template, any [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") with the [Living Vehicle Template](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Living_Vehicle_Template "Living Vehicle Template"), and [Bio-Implants](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bio-Implants "Bio-Implants") are also always considered to be Biotech devices.

**Cost:** A Biotech device costs four times as much as its technological equivalent. (For example, a Biotech Breath Mask costs 800 credits, whereas a normal [Breath Mask](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Breath_Mask "Breath Mask") costs 200 credits.) Biotech devices are considered [Rare](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rare "Rare") across most of the galaxy.

**Applicable To:** Equipment (excluding [Explosives](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Explosives "Explosives")); Armor; Weapons (excluding [Exotic Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapons "Exotic Weapons") and [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers"))

The term "Biotech" is a new phrase used to describe the biological technology introduced by the [Yuuzhan Vong](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong "Yuuzhan Vong"). The term Biotech can be applied to [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment"), [Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapons "Weapons"), [Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor "Armor"), and even [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") (see the [Living Vehicle Template](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Living_Vehicle_Template "Living Vehicle Template")), and it acts as a descriptor that indicates certain new rules mechanics. A Biotech object is defined as any object of distinct [Yuuzhan Vong](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong "Yuuzhan Vong") origin, such as [Amphistaffs](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Amphistaffs "Amphistaffs"), [Razor Bugs](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Razor_Bugs "Razor Bugs"), [Thud Bugs](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Thud_Bugs "Thud Bugs"), and [Vonduun Crabshell](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vonduun_Crabshell "Vonduun Crabshell"); additionally, any object with the Biotech General Template, any [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") with the [Living Vehicle Template](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Living_Vehicle_Template "Living Vehicle Template"), and [Bio-Implants](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bio-Implants "Bio-Implants") are also always considered to be Biotech devices.

Most Biotech devices created by the [Yuuzhan Vong](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong "Yuuzhan Vong") serve the same purposes as their mechanical or electronic counterparts. You can create new Biotech devices by applying the following template to standard technological [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment"), [Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor "Armor"), and [Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapons "Weapons"). Once the Template is applied, the new Biotech device may be used with the Biotech [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") and [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents"). Note that certain [Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapons "Weapons") and [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") have no Biotech equivalent, including most [Explosives](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Explosives "Explosives"), [Exotic Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapons "Exotic Weapons"), and [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers").

Biotech devices have the following special rules associated with them:

- They require the [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") skill (not the [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") skill) to [Biotech Repair](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Biotech_Repair "Biotech Repair").
- [Knowledge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge "Knowledge") checks concerning Biotech use [Knowledge (Life Sciences)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Life_Sciences) "Knowledge (Life Sciences)") instead of [Knowledge (Technology)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Technology) "Knowledge (Technology)").
- They are more difficult to sense and grasp with [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") (see [Sense Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sense_Force "Sense Force")).
- They may be modified using the [Biotech Specialist](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Biotech_Specialist "Biotech Specialist") Feat, but not the [Tech Specialist](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tech_Specialist "Tech Specialist") Feat.

**Armors:** [Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor "Armor") with the Biotech General Template has its weight reduced by one-half. This does not change the Armor's type ([Medium Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Medium_Armor "Medium Armor") remains [Medium Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Medium_Armor "Medium Armor"), [Heavy Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Armor "Heavy Armor") remains [Heavy Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Armor "Heavy Armor")). Repairing a suit of "Living Armor" requires the [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") skill (see [Biotech Repair](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Biotech_Repair "Biotech Repair")).

**Equipment:** Most [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") requires no special modifications. However, some Biotech devices may rely on natural power supplies or requires specialized training to use successfully. Such devices may be used without penalty by the [Yuuzhan Vong](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong "Yuuzhan Vong") and others with Biotech-related [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") and [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents"). Those without such knowledge must apply a -5 penalty to any [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks") or attacks made when using unfamiliar Biotech devices.

**Weapons:** A Biotech Weapon derived from a [Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon "Weapon") that deals Energy damage deals Bludgeoning, Piercing, or Slashing damage instead; the type of damage is selected when the Template is applied. Biotech Weapons are normally [Illegal](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Illegal "Illegal") in [The Legacy Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Legacy_Era "The Legacy Era").

Connecting Objects TogetherConnecting Objects Together

The [Biotech Adaptation](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Biotech_Adaptation "Biotech Adaptation") application allow you to connect Biotech Devices to non-Biotech Devices, effectively creating a hybrid device. For example, you could use the skill to connect [Recording Devices](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Recording_Devices "Recording Devices") to [Yuuzhan Vong](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong "Yuuzhan Vong") bugs like [Jariah Syn](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jariah_Syn "Jariah Syn") does before rescuing [Cade Skywalker](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cade_Skywalker "Cade Skywalker") from [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant"). However, Gamemasters should note that this application of the Skill probably shouldn't allow you to do something you couldn't do by connecting two non-Biotech Devices together.

Most of the time, you can just do things like connect a computer to a [Datapad](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Datapad "Datapad") without needing a [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check"). However, other combinations need to be more closely regulated; for example, connecting a recording rod to a [Thermal Detonator](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Thermal_Detonator "Thermal Detonator") to create an improvised, sound-based mine that might be exciting thing to do once, but if your players abuse that combination, it might be time to rethink how often you allow it. Feel free to ask for a [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") check, scaling the DC based on how common you want the modification to be.

Lastly, no equipment combination should be a better option than an existing piece of [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment"). Take a look at other gear and see if there is already a piece of [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") that does what your heroes want to do, and use that as a model for improvising the outcome of their creativity. Creative thinking is good, and you should encourage it among your players; just make sure it doesn't obviate existing options in the game.

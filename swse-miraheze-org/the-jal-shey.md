# The Jal Shey

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jal_Shey](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jal_Shey) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:04:11Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

The Jal Shey is one of the oldest known [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions"), and one of the most wide-ranging. Unlike other [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions"), the Jal Shey study [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") from an intellectual, scientific point of view, rather than a spiritual journey. The Jal Shey are regarded as effective and sincere diplomats, on par with the greatest Jedi negotiators. Unlike the Jedi, the Jal Shey spend less effort on physical pursuits, even when [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") is involved. But their understanding of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") is considerable, enabling them to create items that improve their abilities simply by wearing them. Force-users from other [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions") are able to imbue [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") into weapons for their own personal use, but the Jal Shey have perfected a method of extending their improvements to anyone who wields their items.

The origins of the Jal Shey remain lost to the mists of time. They are nomadic by nature and do not settle for long on any particular world. Instead, they seek out places that might improve their knowledge of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). They might research a [Dark Side Nexus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Nexus?action=edit&redlink=1 "Dark Side Nexus (page does not exist)"), or study another [Force Tradition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Tradition "Force Tradition"), or seek to learn the history of a particular [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") if it could lead to new insight. However, The Jal Shey are not only scholars and observers of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). They also participate in the society around them, using their knowledge to help improve the lives of others. They are especially adept at diplomacy, negotiation, and spirited debate. They act as intermediaries in disputes ranging from simple arguments, to negotiated settlements of great complexity- even wars.

The Jal Shey are not great in number. They fall into three categories: Neophytes, Advisors, and Mentors. Advisors' diplomatic missions make them likely to be encountered traveling throughout the galaxy. Neophytes typically study with Mentors until they are ready to accept investigative and diplomatic missions of their own. The Jal Shey recruit Force-users from many worlds and many [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species"), but hey are highly selective, choosing only those they believe will accept the Jal Shey way of life, and contribute to their scholarly and diplomatic efforts. Thus, those tainted with [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") and those who present an affinity for combat are not normally selected.

Many Jal Shey learn basic [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") techniques for defense, but they rarely specialize in them. Their defenses are not as strong as many Jedi techniques, hence their desire to create armor, belts, and other items to augment their abilities. It is believed that they achieve this through a combination of imbuing the item with [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), and applying technology that helps enhance [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") for the user. The nomadic Jal Shey have been creating such items for uncounted years, and many of their creations have found their way into shops and military stores scattered throughout the galaxy.

Members of The Jal Shey can select [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") from the [Jal Shey Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jal_Shey_Talent_Tree "Jal Shey Talent Tree").

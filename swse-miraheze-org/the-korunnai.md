# The Korunnai

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Korunnai](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Korunnai) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:04:39Z.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

The Korunnai are a nomadic "tribe" of [Humans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Humans "Humans") from the uplands of the planet [Haruun Kal](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Haruun_Kal?action=edit&redlink=1 "Haruun Kal (page does not exist)"), the sole planet of the Al'Har System. Not so much a formalized [Force Tradition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Tradition "Force Tradition") like [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"), The Korunnai live in the harsh jungles that grow above the layers of lethal gases that fill the planet's lowlands. They maintain their existence by following herd animals called [Grassers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grassers?action=edit&redlink=1 "Grassers (page does not exist)"), which provide them with sustenance and the materials they needed to survive. In addition to their keen survival skills, members of The Korunnai have a strong connection to [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force").

Life is not so simple for The Korunnai, as they also fight a bloody war against the planet's other sapient inhabitants- [The Balawai](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Balawai "The Balawai"). Originally offworlders, [The Balawai](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Balawai "The Balawai") invade the jungles The Korunnai call home and ransack the natural resources to sell in the galaxy's markets for profit, including spices and exotic woods. Their unsympathetic behavior quickly creates enmity between the two peoples that erupts into war lasting well into [The Clone Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Clone_Wars "The Clone Wars"). When the Separatist-backed Balawai are reinforced with updated weapons and technology to protect the planet's only spaceport, the Republic has too few troops to help in the conflict and sends only a single Jedi- [Depa Billaba](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Depa_Billaba?action=edit&redlink=1 "Depa Billaba (page does not exist)")- to back The Korunnai. The conflict between The Korunnai and [The Balawai](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Balawai "The Balawai") escalates to such an extent that the Jedi Master is mentally scarred from the experience.

The Korunnai refer to [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") as *Pelekotan*, interacting with what they believe to be the "jungle mind" as a way to survive the perils of their homeworld. They see *Pelekotan* as a dark force that rules the darkness of the jungles and challenges those that tap into its energy. Sometimes, *Pelekotan* presents its challenges in ways that cause physical ailments for its users, and at other times, those challenges can cause mental impairments. Jedi believe that users of *Pelekotan* equally use both [The Light Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Light_Side "The Light Side") and [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), but The Korunnai merely believe that they are all part of the same entity. Some Jedi feel that *Pelekotan* is just another name for [The Living Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Living_Force "The Living Force").

**Membership:** True members of The Korunnai are the natives of [Haruun Kal](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Haruun_Kal?action=edit&redlink=1 "Haruun Kal (page does not exist)") who roam the world's highlands. Others who join their tribe have the [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity") Feat and embrace the concept of *Pelekotan*. Members of The Korunnai gain access to the [Korunnai Adept Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Korunnai_Adept_Talent_Tree "Korunnai Adept Talent Tree").

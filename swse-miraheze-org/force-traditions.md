# Force Traditions

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:03:49Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

Even those who don't believe in [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") and aren't particularly attuned to its flow can call upon [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") without understanding exactly what they are doing. When a stroke of luck occurs, or fate seems to be on your side and helps you accomplish a difficult objective, it could be [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") coming to your aid. In game terms, the expenditure of [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") by non-Force-using characters represents this unconscious, tenuous connection to [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") that all living things share. When a Force-using character calls on [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") this way, he or she knows exactly what's happening.

The most prominent and well-known Force-users in the galaxy are [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order"). However, [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") are not the only ones who have learned to manipulate [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). Other Force-users sometimes attribute their abilities to sources other than [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), such as magic or gods, but they are all using the energy to manipulate the world around them. In game terms, anyone with the [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity") feat has the ability to master the [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") skill and learn [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers"), though many of these beings will never go down this particular path.

Other Force Traditions include Dark Side cults (such as [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith")), and obscure, isolated sects such as [The Witches of Dathomir](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Witches_of_Dathomir "The Witches of Dathomir") and [The Sorcerers of Tund](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sorcerers_of_Tund "The Sorcerers of Tund"). Some know [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") for what it is but approach its use in a different way from [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"). Others know [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") by a different name. Either way, it remains the mystic energy that binds the galaxy together and gives Force-users their powers.

**Membership:** The conditions for being considered a member of a given Force Tradition are outlined in the description. A character may be a member of more than one Force Tradition, but this is uncommon.

# Beast

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Beast](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Beast) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:04:52Z.

*Reference Books: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook"), [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

Beasts have an [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") of 1 or 2 (Beasts that show above average cunning usually have a good [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") score). They roll their other five [Ability Scores](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Scores "Ability Scores") as normal. Beasts increase only one [Ability Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Score "Ability Score") by one point every fourth level (instead of increasing two scores by one point each). However, they gain [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") normally as they advance in level. A beast that gains an [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") of 3 or higher as a result of an ability increase is eligible to [Multiclass](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Multiclass "Multiclass"). Beasts do not gain [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") or starting [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"), do not add their Beast Level to their [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses"), and do not gain [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") or [Destiny Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Points "Destiny Points").

**Examples of Beasts in Star Wars:** [Acklay](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acklay "Acklay"), [Dewback](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dewback "Dewback"), [Nexu](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nexu "Nexu"), [Rancor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rancor "Rancor"), [Reek](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reek "Reek"), [Tauntaun](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tauntaun "Tauntaun"), [Wampa](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wampa "Wampa").

### Class Features

- **Hit Points:** At each level, Beasts gain a number of [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") equal to 1d8 + their [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") modifier.
- **Class Skills:** 1st-level Beasts are [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in a number of [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills") equal to 1 + their [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") modifier (minimum of 1). They have the following [Class Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class_Skills "Class Skills"):
  - [Acrobatics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatics "Acrobatics"),
  - [Climb](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Climb "Climb"),
  - [Endurance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Endurance "Endurance"),
  - [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative"),
  - [Jump](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jump "Jump"),
  - [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception"),
  - [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth"),
  - [Survival](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Survival "Survival"),
  - [Swim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swim "Swim")
- **Force Points:** Beasts do not gain [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points").
- **Size Modifiers:** Beasts come in a multitude of sizes. See below for details.
- **Special Qualities** Beasts gain special features unique to themselves. See below.
- **Multiclassing** A Beast with an [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") of 3 or higher can [Multiclass](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Multiclass "Multiclass") into any [Heroic Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Class "Heroic Class"). Beasts with an [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") of 1 or 2 cannot [Multiclass](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Multiclass "Multiclass").

### Special Qualities

Special qualities give creatures abilities and features that differentiate them from other Beasts. Although special qualities are not required, most creatures have at least one, and some have up to three. A Beast must have the appropriate natural ability to have a special quality from one of those described below. For example, a Beast must be able to make two Claw attacks in the same turn if it is utilizing the Rend special quality.

Natural ArmorNatural WeaponsDefensive QualitiesOffensive QualitiesEnvironmental Types

Many Beasts have thick hides or scales that grant a Natural Armor bonus to their [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"). The amount of Natural Armor varies from creature to creature. A Beast's Natural Armor bonus is never higher than its Beast Level.

A Beast has one or more Natural Weapon attacks. It applies its [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") bonus on [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") rolls made with Natural Weapons, and its [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus on [Ranged Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attacks "Ranged Attacks") made with Natural Weapons. If a Beast has two or more Natural Weapons, it may make attacks with all of them at no penalty when using the [Full Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full_Attack "Full Attack") Action. A Beast gains a bonus on damage rolls made with its Natural Weapons equal to one-half its Beast Level, rounded down. A Beast is proficient with its own Natural Weapons, but not with any other weapon group.

Some Beasts have adapted to living in extreme environments, and gain special bonuses and traits, as summarized below:

- **Airborne:** May reroll [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative") checks, but must keep the reroll result, even if it is worse.
- **Aquatic:** Can't drown in water, and doesn't need to make [Swim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swim "Swim") checks; also possess [Low-Light Vision](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Low-Light_Vision "Low-Light Vision").
- **Arctic:** May reroll [Survival](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Survival "Survival") checks made to [Endure Extreme Cold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Endure_Extreme_Cold "Endure Extreme Cold"), keeping the better result.
- **Desert:** May reroll [Survival](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Survival "Survival") checks made to [Endure Extreme Heat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Endure_Extreme_Heat "Endure Extreme Heat"), keeping the better result.
- **Subterranean:** May reroll [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") checks, but must keep the reroll result, even if it is worse; also possess [Darkvision](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darkvision "Darkvision").

## Creating New Beasts

The galaxy teems with life, and most sentient beings encounter only a tiny fraction of the creatures of their own homeworld, let alone the galaxy at large. Heroes traveling [The Unknown Regions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Unknown_Regions "The Unknown Regions") should regularly discover, fight, and otherwise interact with creatures they have never seen before. In this section, you'll find a step-by-step guide through the Beast Creation process. If you need a simple Beast quickly, use the Creature Generator that follows this section. These tables focus on creating threats, rather than mounts. See [Explorer's Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Explorer%27s_Equipment "Explorer's Equipment") for a selection of mounts that the heroes can use. If you want to create a new mount, consider modifying one of the existing mounts with information from this section.

Before creating a Beast's statistics, decide on a theme or a concept for the beast. Start with a general physical description and a basic idea of how the beast behaves and attacks. These details can guide your choices through the rest of the process.

### Emphasizing Strange Creatures

In a galaxy already full of bizarre and fantastic creatures, creating a Beast that the characters perceive as unusual can be challenging. New creatures should add to the sense of strangeness of a newly discovered world in [The Unknown Regions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Unknown_Regions "The Unknown Regions"). A good combination of unusual creatures on a new world should make for a memorable adventure. The strangeness of a creature can be expressed through its physical description, a featured ability, or a behavior. Physical strangeness includes attributes such as outlandish color or markings, unexpected hide or hair texture, and irregular or asymmetrical body shapes or limbs. Behavioral strangeness includes unexpected actions, such as working with creatures not of the same species, intelligent communications, and unexpected planning, target selection, taunting, or movement in a skirmish.

When using a new creature in an encounter, play up the fact that the heroes have no idea what the creature might be capable of. Build the suspense. The heroes could run across unusual but threatening tracks or the remains of a carcass that is a recently finished meal. They might hear howls, screeches, or other bizarre noises that suggest that a dangerous beast might be nearby. Perhaps they catch only glimpses of the creature as it stealthily approaches or escapes. When the heroes eventually see it, emphasize the creature's most bizarre or unusual traits and actions. Its fighting style or decision making might be completely unforeseen and opposed to what the heroes expect, promoting confusion in their interaction with the creature.

### Creature Generator

This section builds on the Beast creation rules originally included in the *Saga Edition Core Rulebook*. The Beast Creation process is presented here in sequence, to aid in expediency. Beasts are not sentient, and have levels in the Beast class (see above).

1. Roll or select [Ability Scores](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Scores "Ability Scores"). Select an [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") score of 1 or 2. An advanced Beast species with [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") of 3 or above can [Multiclass](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Multiclass "Multiclass") into [Heroic Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Classes "Heroic Classes"), but must begin with its first level in the Beast class.
2. Increase one [Ability Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Score "Ability Score") by 1 point at every fourth level, if the Beast is above 1st level.
3. Select the Beast's size and apply the appropriate modifiers (see Size Modifiers, above).
4. Add [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") (1d8 + [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") modifier per level).
5. Beasts do not have [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points"). However, if a Beast has the [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity") feat, assign it a number of [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") equal to one-half its Beast Level (minimum 1).
6. Select a [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") for the creature (see Statistics, below). If the creature has other movement methods (such as Swim or Fly), select those speeds as well.
7. Determine the Beast's [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses") and [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold") (see Defenses, below). Apply Natural Armor, if any, adding a Natural Armor bonus to [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") equal to or lower than the creature's Beast Level. Some Natural Armor grants additional effects as a special quality (and counts as one of the Beast's special qualities).
8. Add [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") at the rate for normal characters (at 1st, 3rd, 6th, 9th, 12th, 15th, and 18th Level). The Beast class does not grant additional starting [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats").
9. Select a number of [Trained Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skills "Trained Skills") equal to 1 + [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") modifier (minimum 1). Apply the size modifier to [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth"), as well as modifiers to other [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills"), such as size or [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat") modifiers.
10. Select one to three Natural Weapons, and determine the Beast's [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") and damage with its Natural Weapons (see Attacks, below).
11. Determine the Beast's [Grapple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple "Grapple") modifier (see Attacks, below): **[Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") + [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") or [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") modifier (whichever is better) + size modifier.**
12. Select any special senses, such as [Low-Light Vision](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Low-Light_Vision "Low-Light Vision") or [Darkvision](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darkvision "Darkvision").
13. Select an Environmental Type, if desired (see above).
14. Select from zero to three special qualities. You can create them yourself, or select from those listed above.

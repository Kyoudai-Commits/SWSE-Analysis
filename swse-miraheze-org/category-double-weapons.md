# Category : Double Weapons

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Double_Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Double_Weapons) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:06:00Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You can attack with both ends of a Double Weapon as a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"), but both attack rolls take a -10 penalty (although certain [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") and [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") can reduce these penalties).

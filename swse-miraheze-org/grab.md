# Grab

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grab](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grab) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:07:26Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can make a Grab attack. A Grab attack is treated as an [Unarmed Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attack "Unarmed Attack") (thus provoking [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity") without the [Martial Arts I](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Martial_Arts_I "Martial Arts I") feat), except that it doesn't deal damage and you take a -5 penalty on the attack roll. You can only Grab an opponent up to one size category larger than yourself, and only one opponent at a time.

Until it breaks the Grab, a Grabbed creature takes a -2 penalty on attack rolls unless it uses a Natural Weapon or a [Light Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Weapon "Light Weapon"). Additionally, it cannot [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") until it breaks the Grab. Breaking the Grab is a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), and automatically clears one grabber per Character Level (the Grabbed creature chooses which grabbers it clears if there are any left over).

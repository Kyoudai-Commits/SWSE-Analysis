# Recharge Shields

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Recharge_Shields](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Recharge_Shields) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:07:31Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

By spending 3 [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions") during the same turn, or on consecutive turns, a [System Operator](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/System_Operator "System Operator") can make a DC 20 [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") check to increase their Vehicle's current [Shield Rating](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shield_Rating "Shield Rating") by 5, up to its normal maximum.

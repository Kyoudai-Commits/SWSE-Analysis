# Razor Bug

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Razor_Bug](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Razor_Bug) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:07:29Z.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

Razor Bugs are small, disc-shaped insects engineered by the [Yuuzhan Vong](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong "Yuuzhan Vong") to serve as ranged weapons. Though they are thrown by hand, Razor Bugs are shaped in such a way that they fly with great speed, distance, and accuracy, making them an effective replacement for longer-ranged weapons. A thrown Razor Bug is treated as a [Simple Weapon (Ranged)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Simple_Weapon_(Ranged) "Simple Weapon (Ranged)"), not a [Thrown Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Thrown_Weapon "Thrown Weapon"), for purposes of determining [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range").

**Weapon Type:** [Simple Weapons (Ranged)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Simple_Weapons_(Ranged) "Simple Weapons (Ranged)")

**Size:** Small

**Cost:** 800

**Damage:** 2d8

**Rate of Fire:** Single-Shot

**Weight:** 0.5 Kilograms

**Type:** Slashing

**Availability:** [Illegal](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Illegal "Illegal"), [Rare](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rare "Rare")

**Accurate:** This weapon takes no penalty when firing at targets at Short [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range").

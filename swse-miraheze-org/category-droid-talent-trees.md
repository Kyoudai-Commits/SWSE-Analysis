# Category : Droid Talent Trees

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Droid_Talent_Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Droid_Talent_Trees) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:04:20Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

At 1st level and every odd-numbered Heroic Level thereafter (3rd, 5th, 7th, etc.), a character gains a [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") from any one of the [Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent_Trees "Talent Trees") available to it. Any Talent in the Tree may be chosen, but the prerequisites (if any) must be met. No Talent can be chosen more than once unless expressly indicated. Droid Talents work the same as the Talents presented in primary Talents Trees; however, each Droid Talent Tree is available only to [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") of a particular Degree. Any time a Droid would normally gain a Talent (such as from gaining an odd-numbered level to a Heroic Class), the Droid can select a Droid Talent from among those present in the below Droid Talent Trees.

# The Disciples of Twilight

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Disciples_of_Twilight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Disciples_of_Twilight) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:04:44Z.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

The Disciples of Twilight have little influence beyond [Dyspeth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dyspeth?action=edit&redlink=1 "Dyspeth (page does not exist)"), a habitable moon that perpetually falls in the shadow of a remote and unremarkable Outer Rim planet.

Dyspeth's original colonists were [Humans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Humans "Humans") who sought to break away from [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") just over nine hundred years prior to the Battle of [Yavin](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yavin "Yavin"). The colonists, quickly forgotten, were rediscovered by a Republic survey team after five hundred years of peaceful settlement. Though wary of the Republic's motives, the people of [Dyspeth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dyspeth?action=edit&redlink=1 "Dyspeth (page does not exist)") reentered the galactic community, albeit as quiet merchants and traders.

The Disciples of Twilight formed shortly after [Dyspeth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dyspeth?action=edit&redlink=1 "Dyspeth (page does not exist)") was originally colonized. The founder, known only as Twilight, is rumored to have been a former Jedi who left [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order") in search of a new beginning. Using her Force abilities, Twilight sought to unite other Force-sensitive colonists in an effort to adapt to the darkness of their new home. Since then, The Disciples of Twilight have become defenders of Dyspeth's people, even though they hold no military or political power on the shadowy moon.

The Disciples of Twilight concern themselves primarily with using [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to manipulate light. When members of The Disciples of Twilight move out into the galaxy at large, they find themselves capable of so much more, since most worlds are brightly lit compared to their own. The Disciples of Twilight have learned to bend light using [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), a technique that has also been displayed by other Force Adept sects such as [The Fallanassi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Fallanassi "The Fallanassi"). They share similar powers but also posses new abilities unseen elsewhere in the galaxy.

Since Dyspeth's discovery by the Republic, The Disciples of Twilight have filtered out into the neighboring systems. Their motives are unclear, but they seem concerned with protecting the weak from the depredations of the strong. While their actions have yet to defy the Sith-controlled Empire, there have been rumblings within the organization that they are about to choose a side.

**Membership:** The membership of The Disciples of Twilight consist largely of natives of [Dyspeth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dyspeth?action=edit&redlink=1 "Dyspeth (page does not exist)"). Despite this, the movement has gained several new members since the moon's discovery by the Republic, and anyone with the [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity") Feat and a taste for shadowy justice is welcome to join. Members of The Disciples of Twilight gain access to the [Disciple of Twilight Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disciple_of_Twilight_Talent_Tree "Disciple of Twilight Talent Tree").

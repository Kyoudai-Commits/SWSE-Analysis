# Power Pack

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Power_Pack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Power_Pack) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:08:59Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

**Cost:** 25

**Weight:** 0.1 Kilograms

A Power Pack is a compact rectangular battery that fits into the grip or barrel of an energy weapon, such as a blaster, to provide the power needed to fire it. A Power Pack can be recharged with a [Power Recharger](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Power_Recharger "Power Recharger").

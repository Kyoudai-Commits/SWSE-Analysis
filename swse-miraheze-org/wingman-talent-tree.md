# Wingman Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wingman_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wingman_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:05:19Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

You are adept at flying in tight formations with your allies, working together with them to achieve your goals and using your [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") to shield others from harm. To use Talents from this Talent Tree, you must be [Piloting](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Piloting "Piloting") a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") of Colossal size or smaller.

## Core Talents

### Concentrate All Fire

When you use the [Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another") Action to aid an ally's attack roll with a [Vehicle Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Weapon "Vehicle Weapon"), if the attack hits, it deals +1 die of damage. Any ally can only benefit from this Talent once per attack roll, regardless of how many allies with this Talent aid on the attack.

### Escort Pilot

When a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") that you are [Piloting](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Piloting "Piloting") is adjacent to a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") of Colossal size or smaller that is [Piloted](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Piloted "Piloted") by an ally, both [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") gain a +10 bonus to their [Damage Thresholds](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Thresholds "Damage Thresholds").

### Lose Pursuit

When a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") that you are [Piloting](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Piloting "Piloting") is adjacent to a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") of Colossal size or smaller that is [Piloted](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Piloted "Piloted") by an ally, both you and your ally gain a +5 circumstance bonus to [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") checks to avoid being pulled into a [Dogfight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dogfight "Dogfight") as an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity").

### Run Interference

*Prerequisite:* **Escort Pilot**

As a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), you can use your [Vehicular Combat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicular_Combat "Vehicular Combat") feat to negate an attack against an adjacent [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") of Colossal size or smaller that is [Piloted](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Piloted "Piloted") by an ally. If you can use [Vehicular Combat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicular_Combat "Vehicular Combat") more than once per round, each use to negate an attack counts toward your limit of uses per round.

### Wingman Retribution

*Prerequisite:* **Escort Pilot**

When a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") of Colossal size or smaller that is [Piloted](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Piloted "Piloted") by an ally is damaged by an attack, once per round as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") you can make a [Vehicle Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Attack "Vehicle Attack") with a -5 penalty against your ally's attacker.

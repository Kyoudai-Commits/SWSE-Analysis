# Coup de Grace

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coup_de_Grace](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coup_de_Grace) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:07:25Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

As a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"), you can use a melee weapon to deliver a Coup de Grace to a [Helpless](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Helpless "Helpless") target. You can also use a ranged weapon, provided you are adjacent to the target. You automatically score a Critical Hit, dealing double damage. A defender reduced to 0 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") by a Coup de Grace dies instantly, or, in the case of a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid"), is destroyed. An [Unconscious](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unconscious "Unconscious") or [Disabled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disabled "Disabled") defender hit by a Coup de Grace also dies or is destroyed instantly.

You can't deliver a Coup de Grace against a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") or object.

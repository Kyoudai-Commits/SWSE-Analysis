# Category : Exotic Weapons

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Exotic_Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Exotic_Weapons) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:06:01Z.

Exotic Weapons are unusual or unique weapons that require specific training to properly utilize (including both [Exotic Weapons (Ranged)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapons_(Ranged) "Exotic Weapons (Ranged)") and [Exotic Weapons (Melee)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapons_(Melee) "Exotic Weapons (Melee)")). Unlike other [Weapon Groups](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Groups "Weapon Groups"), you must select the [Exotic Weapon Proficiency](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon_Proficiency "Exotic Weapon Proficiency") feat each time you want to learn how to use a different Exotic Weapon. [Exotic Weapon Proficiency](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon_Proficiency "Exotic Weapon Proficiency") is a Class Bonus Feat for [Nobles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nobles "Nobles") and [Soldiers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldiers "Soldiers").

# Conditions

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Conditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Conditions) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:06:10Z.

*Reference Books: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook"), [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

Certain debilitating attacks reduce one's combat effectiveness instead of one's [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points"). Examples include a [Stun Grenade](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stun_Grenade "Stun Grenade") blast, a [Forced March](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Forced_March "Forced March"), a paralyzing venom, or long-term exposure to extreme temperatures. Multiple Conditions have cumulative effects and can render a creature [Unconscious](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unconscious "Unconscious"), or a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid"), [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle"), or object [Disabled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disabled "Disabled"). Physically debilitating attacks are usually made against the target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"), while mentally deliberating attacks target one's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"). Either type of attack pushes the target along the same track.

## Damage Threshold

Attacks that deal massive amounts of damage can impair or incapacitate you regardless of how many [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") you have remaining. Your Damage Threshold determines how much [Damage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage "Damage") a single attack must deal to reduce your combat effectiveness or, in some cases, kill you. When a single attack made against you deals damage that equals or exceeds your Damage Threshold, but not enough to drop you to 0 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points"), you move -1 step along the Condition Track. If the [Damage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage "Damage") reduces you to 0 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points"), you are dead (see [Death](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Death "Death")).

#### Damage Threshold = [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") + Size Modifier

- **Size Modifier:** Creatures, [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids"), and [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") larger than Medium Size gain a Size bonus to their Damage Threshold. This Size bonus is:
  - +5 for Large,
  - +10 for Huge,
  - +20 for Gargantuan,
  - and +50 for Colossal.
- **[Improved Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improved_Damage_Threshold "Improved Damage Threshold"):** You can increase your Damage Threshold by taking the [Improved Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improved_Damage_Threshold "Improved Damage Threshold") feat.

#### Damage Threshold = [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") + Size Modifier

- **Size Modifier:** Apply the following Size Modifiers to a Vehicle's Damage Threshold:
  - Large, +5;
  - Huge, +10;
  - Gargantuan, +20;
  - Colossal, +50;
  - Colossal (Frigate), +100;
  - Colossal (Cruiser), +200;
  - Colossal (Station), +500.

#### Damage Threshold = [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") + 50 (Size Modifier)

- **[Improved Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improved_Damage_Threshold "Improved Damage Threshold"):** A Battalion can increase their Damage Threshold with the [Improved Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improved_Damage_Threshold "Improved Damage Threshold") feat.

If a [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion") takes damage from a single attack that equals or exceeds its Damage Threshold, the Battalion moves -1 Persistent step down the Condition Track. This represents the [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion") taking a staggering blow that greatly reduces its efficiency. A [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion") pushed to the bottom of the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") must [Disband](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disband "Disband") on its next turn as its only Action.

## The Condition Track

A creature, [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid"), object, or [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") not affected by any debilitating Conditions is assumed to be in a "Normal State," which represents one end of the Condition Track. Each debilitating effect to which it succumbs moves it one or more steps along the Condition Track. A creature pushed to the last step on the Condition Tack falls [Unconscious](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unconscious "Unconscious"). A [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid"), [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle"), or object pushed to the last step on the Condition Track is [Disabled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disabled "Disabled") until repaired using the [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") skill. When a device is pushed down the Condition Track, apply the indicated penalty on [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks") made with the device.

When a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") is pushed down the Condition Track, all of the Vehicle's occupants suffer the same penalties as the [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") itself until the [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") is either Disabled or [Repaired](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Repaired "Repaired"). Penalties imposed by multiple Condition Tracks are cumulative; in other words, a Vehicle's occupants suffer the effects of their own personal Condition Tracks in addition to the effects of the Vehicle's Condition Track.

**Q:** Condition Track penalties reduce all your [Defense Scores](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defense_Scores "Defense Scores"). Do they also reduce your Damage Threshold, since it is derived from your [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense")?

**A:** Yes. Although the effect is indirect, any modifiers that change your [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") also change your Damage Threshold by an equal amount because its underlying "starting value" has changed.

### Removing Conditions

You can improve your Condition by spending three [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions") to use the [Recover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Recover "Recover") Action, moving +1 step along the Condition Track. You can spend all Three [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions") in a single round, or spread them out across consecutive rounds.

For example, you could spend a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") at the end of one turn and two [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions") at the start of your next turn to move +1 step along the Condition Track. Certain situations, such as Persistent Conditions, may prevent you from spending [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions") to move toward a Normal State on the Condition Track.

Resting for eight consecutive, uninterrupted hours usually removes all debilitating Conditions afflicting a creature and returns it to its Normal State. However, some Persistent Conditions, such as [Poison](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Poison "Poison") and [Hunger](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hunger?action=edit&redlink=1 "Hunger (page does not exist)"), may prevent a creature from improving its Condition or returning to its Normal State until the cause of the debilitation is treated.

### Battalion Conditions

When a Battalion is moved down the Condition Track by attacks that equal or exceed its Damage Threshold, its movement down the Condition Track is always a Persistent Condition (this represents the Battalion taking a staggering blow that greatly reduces its efficiency). A Battalion pushed to the bottom of the Condition Track must [Disband](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disband "Disband") on its next turn as its only Action.

## Persistent Conditions

Some [Hazards](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hazards "Hazards") and attacks result in a Persistent Condition that cannot be removed except in certain circumstances. Any time a Condition is Persistent, you cannot use the [Recover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Recover "Recover") Action to move steps up the Condition Track, and you do not regain any [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") from [Natural Healing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Natural_Healing "Natural Healing").

However, once a Persistent Condition is removed by satisfying the requirements starting in its description, you can move up the Condition Track and heal normally.

Persistent Conditions do not prevent you from moving up the Condition Track by means other than the [Recover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Recover "Recover") Action or resting for eight hours. For example, an [Unconscious](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unconscious "Unconscious") creature that fails its first [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") check has a Persistent Condition from its injuries, but it still moves +1 step on the Condition Track when it makes a successful [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") check to regain consciousness.

### Multiple Persistent Conditions

Sometimes you are affected by more than one Persistent Condition. For example, you might be [Poisoned](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Poisoned "Poisoned") after already suffering the effects of a [Disease](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disease "Disease"). In this case, you must satisfy the requirements for removing all these Persistent Conditions before you can move up the Condition Track.

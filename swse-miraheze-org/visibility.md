# Visibility

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Visibility](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Visibility) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:07:50Z.

*Reference Books: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook"), [Scavenger's Guide to Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scavenger%27s_Guide_to_Droids "Scavenger's Guide to Droids")*

It's a rare mission that doesn't end up in the dark somewhere, and heroes need a way to see. The light sources table below indicates the duration of the more common light sources, as well as their light radius. A light source also provides shadowy illumination out to twice this distance; targets within shadowy illumination gain [Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Concealment "Concealment"), but they are visible. Without a light source, heroes are effectively Blinded (see below).

## Blindness

A Blinded creature can't see and takes a -2 penalty to their [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), loses its [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus to their [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") (if any), moves at half [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed"), and takes a -5 penalty on [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") checks. All opponents are considered to have [Total Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Total_Concealment "Total Concealment") to the Blinded creature. All checks and actions that rely on vision (such as reading) fail automatically.

## Low-Light Vision

A creature or [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") that has Low-Light Vision can see without penalty in shadowy illumination, ignoring [Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Concealment "Concealment") (but not [Total Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Total_Concealment "Total Concealment")) due to darkness. It retains the ability to distinguish color and detail under these conditions.

### Darkvision

A creature or [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") that has Darkvision can see in the dark, ignoring [Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Concealment "Concealment") and [Total Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Total_Concealment "Total Concealment") due to darkness. Darkvision is black and white only, so there must be at least some light to discern colors. It is otherwise like normal sight, and a creature with Darkvision can function with no light at all.

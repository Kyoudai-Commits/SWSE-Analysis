# 3rd-Degree Droids

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/3rd-Degree_Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/3rd-Degree_Droids) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:04:23Z.

*Reference Books: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook"), [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

Protocol and Domestic 3rd-Degree [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") have the following traits:

- **Ability Modifiers:** All 3rd-Degree Droids receive +2 bonuses to both their [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") and [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma"), but suffer a -2 penalty to their [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength").
- **Behavioral Inhibitors:** 3rd-Degree Droids firstly cannot knowingly harm a sentient creature, unless another creature would be harmed due to inaction. 3rd-Degree Droids secondly obey orders from their designated owner (whether that be an actual owner or themselves).
- **Droid Traits:** All 3rd-Degree Droids posses [Droid Traits](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Traits "Droid Traits").
- **Droid Talents:** All 3rd-Degree Droids can select Droid Talents from the [3rd-Degree Droid Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/3rd-Degree_Droid_Talent_Tree "3rd-Degree Droid Talent Tree").

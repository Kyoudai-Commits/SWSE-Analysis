# Noble Bonus Feats

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble_Bonus_Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble_Bonus_Feats) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:05:34Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

At each even-numbered level (2nd, 4th, 6th, etc.), a [Noble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble "Noble") gains a Bonus [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat"). This [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat") must be selected from the following Noble Bonus Feats lists, and a [Noble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble "Noble") must meet any prerequisites for that [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat").

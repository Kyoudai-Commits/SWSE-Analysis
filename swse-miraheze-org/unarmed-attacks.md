# Unarmed Attacks

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attacks) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:04:09Z.

*Reference Books: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook"), [Threats of the Galaxy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threats_of_the_Galaxy "Threats of the Galaxy")*

Striking for damage with punches, kicks, and headbutts is essentially a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a weapon. Unarmed Attacks deal normal Bludgeoning damage.

A Medium creature normally deals 1d4 points of Bludgeoning damage (plus [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") modifier) with a successful Unarmed Attack; a Small creature deals 1d3 points of Bludgeoning damage (plus [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") modifier); a Large creature deals 1d6 points of Bludgeoning damage (plus [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") modifier). Certain [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents"), [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"), or special abilities may increase the damage a character deals with their Unarmed Attacks.

For the purpose of [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") and [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") that allow you to select a weapon group for that ability to apply to, Unarmed Attacks are [Simple Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Simple_Weapons "Simple Weapons"). However, [Combat Gloves](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Combat_Gloves "Combat Gloves") provide a martial artist with a damage advantage that lets them make use of [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") such as **[Weapon Specialization (Simple Weapons)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Specialization_(Simple_Weapons) "Weapon Specialization (Simple Weapons)")**, further increasing their aptitude for Unarmed Attacks.

[Combat Gloves](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Combat_Gloves "Combat Gloves") are considered [Simple Weapons (Melee)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Simple_Weapons_(Melee) "Simple Weapons (Melee)"), but attacks with [Combat Gloves](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Combat_Gloves "Combat Gloves") are still considered to be Unarmed Attacks.

## Unarmed Weapon Group

**Q:** What [Weapon Group](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Group "Weapon Group") includes Unarmed Attacks? How about Natural Weapons? In most places, it seems like they're [Simple Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Simple_Weapons "Simple Weapons"), but [Weapon Focus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus "Weapon Focus") refers to Unarmed Attacks as if they are separate.

**A:** Unarmed Attacks and Natural Weapons are considered [Simple Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Simple_Weapons "Simple Weapons") in which all characters are proficient. The reference to Unarmed Attacks in the [Weapon Focus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus "Weapon Focus") Feat is an error.

## Unarmed Attacks and Weapon Finesse

**Q:** Do Unarmed Attacks and Natural Weapons benefit from the [Weapon Finesse](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Finesse "Weapon Finesse") Feat?

**A:** Yes. Unarmed Attacks and Natural Weapons are [Light Melee Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Melee_Weapons "Light Melee Weapons").

# Category : Jedi Academy Training Manual

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:03:01Z.

[![](//static.wikitide.net/swsewiki/thumb/1/17/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual.webp/250px-Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual.webp.png)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/File:Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual.webp)

Students come from all corners of the galaxy to study the ways of the Jedi. Under the guidance of talented Jedi Masters, these students gain knowledge of the Force and become the guardians of peace and justice. Yet the power of the Dark Side is ever-present, tempting reckless students to walk the path of evil. Your destiny is in your hands. Will you grow to become a powerful Jedi Knight, or succumb to the lure of the Dark Side?

## Introduction

You are about to take you first steps into a larger world. Following the Galactic Civil War, [Luke Skywalker](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Luke_Skywalker "Luke Skywalker"), the first of a new generation of Jedi Knights, reestablishes [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order") and begins training new students in the ways of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). The new Jedi Order starts out on [Yavin 4](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yavin_4 "Yavin 4"), and then eventually moves to other locales, including the Jedi Academy on [Ossus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ossus "Ossus"). Many years after Luke's time, his descendant, [Kol Skywalker](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kol_Skywalker "Kol Skywalker"), rises to the rank of Jedi Master and becomes one of the preeminent leaders of [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order").

It is from this perspective, that of students at the Jedi Academy on [Ossus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ossus "Ossus"), that this book is written. The *Jedi Academy Training Manual* is a guide to [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"), and [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith"). Inside this book, you will find a wealth of knowledge pertaining to all aspects of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") found in the *Star Wars Roleplaying Game*. This book provides many new options for players of [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") characters as well as other [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions"), designed to enhance your game experience. Though much of this content is geared toward players, Gamemasters should find this material just as useful in crafting NPCs.

There is more to being a Jedi than just [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") and [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers"). [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") tradition is steeped in history, and [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") are as much soldiers of an ideal as they are masters of incredible Force power. Likewise, [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") are far from two-dimensional villains; most every Sith Lord is cunning, devious, and complex. Throughout this book, players and Gamemasters alike are encouraged to look beyond the surface and see the motivations that make these characters memorable parts of the *Star Wars* saga.

This book has different kinds of thematic sidebars, as introduced below. These sidebars provide extra information, adventure hooks, and ideas to enhance the *Saga Edition* experience. Sometimes these sidebars contain new rules elements; other times they simply provide a bit of story or flavor to stimulate the imaginations of players and Gamemasters. Pay special attention to these sidebars, for they often enhance the text of the chapters in which they appear.

Courses of StudyCourses of Study

This book contains information for both Gamemasters and players. Below is a brief summary of the material contained herein.

- **Chapter 1: [Power of the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Power_of_the_Force "Power of the Force")** presents new [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents"), [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"), and [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") for heroes and villains. The bulk of the new mechanics in the book appear here, including the [Lightsaber Form Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Form_Powers "Lightsaber Form Powers"), [Force Regimens](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Regimens "Force Regimens"), and the new material for the [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") Heroic Class and for the [Force Adept](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Adept "Force Adept"), [Jedi Knight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Knight "Jedi Knight"), and [Sith Apprentice](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Apprentice "Sith Apprentice") Prestige Classes.
- **Chapter 2: [Philosophy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Philosophy "Philosophy")** discusses the philosophical side of apprenticeship. This in-depth look at the philosophy of [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") covers the basics of Jedi life as well as the dangers that all apprentices face, including temptation from [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"). This chapter also introduces several variant rules for how to use [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force").
- **Chapter 3: [Equipment & Artifacts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment_%26_Artifacts "Equipment & Artifacts")** includes an in-depth look at the physical objects that are key elements of [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") and [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") traditions. This chapter includes a comprehensive guide to [Lightsaber Construction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Construction "Lightsaber Construction"), lists many of the variant [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers") available for use, and provides a close look at [Holocrons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Holocrons "Holocrons"), including the [Holocron](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Holocron "Holocron") creation process (which is tied to the [Destiny](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny "Destiny") system).
- **Chapter 4: [Affiliated Programs](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Affiliated_Programs "Affiliated Programs")** takes a look at several [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions") that differ from [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"). These alternative [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions") can serve as a basis for the creation of characters who use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") but do not adhere to the tenets of [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") or [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith").
- **Chapter 5: [Instructors & Alumni](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Instructors_%26_Alumni "Instructors & Alumni")** looks at some of the most influential beings in the history of [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order"). This chapter covers many major figures stretching back as far as [The Great Hyperspace War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Hyperspace_War?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Great Hyperspace War (page does not exist)") and as far forward as the founder of the new Jedi Order, [Grand Master Luke Skywalker](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grand_Master_Luke_Skywalker "Grand Master Luke Skywalker").
- **Chapter 6: [Dangers of Study](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dangers_of_Study "Dangers of Study")** introduces some of the threats that a Jedi apprentice might face during his or her career. This chapter includes many new [Beasts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Beasts "Beasts") that threaten Jedi students, as well as a rogues gallery of those who have succumbed to [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). This chapter presents antagonists that Gamemasters can use to spice up their campaigns.
- **Chapter 7: [Studying Abroad](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Studying_Abroad "Studying Abroad")** covers the various sites and locations that are of historical significance to [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order"). Though only a few individual locales are of great significance to [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") or [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith"), this chapter takes a look at what makes those locations special, and how they can be used in adventures.

Though much of this book discusses Force-users in terms of Jedi and Sith, the book is equally useful to those who follow other [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions"). With an entire chapter devoted to new [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions"), players who don't want to play a traditional Jedi have much to draw upon for inspiration and character concepts. Additionally, this book features two new [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions") that have never appeared elsewhere. Both [The Shapers of Kro Var](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Shapers_of_Kro_Var "The Shapers of Kro Var") and [The Wardens of the Sky](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Wardens_of_the_Sky "The Wardens of the Sky") were designed from the ground-up to fill particular niches in the *Saga Edition Roleplaying Game* rules and include open-ended stories so that they can be dropped into any campaign, regardless of era. Several other traditions, including [The Baran Do Sages](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Baran_Do_Sages "The Baran Do Sages"), [The Matukai](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Matukai "The Matukai"), [The Tyia](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Tyia "The Tyia"), and [The Zeison Sha](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Zeison_Sha "The Zeison Sha"), originally appeared in previous editions of the game and are included in this book, as they make excellent choices for heroes looking for alternatives to [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi").

## Message from the Faculty

*Welcome to the Jedi Academy. As a new student, you will find the faculty and staff here interested in helping you adjust to your new environment. Periodically, new students will be issued memorandums and messages from faculty members, intended to guide you in your quest for knowledge. Each of the faculty members here at the academy has taken a personal interest in seeing that you receive the attention that you deserve, and the messages they send you will likely reveal essential information. Pay close attention to these messages, as they will make your apprenticeship much easier.*

This book introduces a number of new mechanics and mechanical subsystems. Most of these are contained in [Power of the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Power_of_the_Force "Power of the Force") and [Equipment & Artifacts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment_%26_Artifacts "Equipment & Artifacts"), with a few scattered appearances elsewhere in the book. For example, [Power of the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Power_of_the_Force "Power of the Force") introduces a new subsystem known as [Force Regimens](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Regimens "Force Regimens") that give Jedi characters something to do in their downtime. Likewise, [Equipment & Artifacts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment_%26_Artifacts "Equipment & Artifacts") introduces advanced rules for [Lightsaber Construction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Construction "Lightsaber Construction"), helping to tailor each weapon to the [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") who wields it. This book also describes how a character might come to possess a [Force Spirit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Spirit "Force Spirit") mentor (as [Obi-Wan Kenobi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Obi-Wan_Kenobi "Obi-Wan Kenobi") was to [Luke Skywalker](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Luke_Skywalker "Luke Skywalker")) or how one can devise monstrous [Beasts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Beasts "Beasts") created using forbidden [Sith Alchemy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Alchemy "Sith Alchemy").

[Power of the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Power_of_the_Force "Power of the Force") also introduces a new type of [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") that draws upon the various [Lightsaber Forms](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Forms "Lightsaber Forms") practiced by [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"), and serves not only to provide interesting new combat options but also to make [Lightsaber Forms](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Forms "Lightsaber Forms") a greater part of the character. Each [Lightsaber Form Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Form_Power "Lightsaber Form Power") is tied to a particular [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") combat technique, and possessing the correct Talent from the [Lightsaber Forms Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Forms_Talent_Tree "Lightsaber Forms Talent Tree") further enhances these [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers"). The [Lightsaber Form Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Form_Powers "Lightsaber Form Powers") also allow Jedi characters to make use of certain iconic abilities of the films and comics without having to invest precious resources such as [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") into such abilities.

## Holocron Lesson

*Greetings, student. I am the gatekeeper of this [Holocron](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Holocron "Holocron"), Jedi Master [Tedryn](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tedryn?action=edit&redlink=1 "Tedryn (page does not exist)"). I shall assume that you obtained this [Holocron](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Holocron "Holocron") from one of your instructors and did not simply steal it from the archives when none of your Jedi Masters were watching them. Within this [Holocron](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Holocron "Holocron") you will find a wealth of knowledge that even your own Jedi Masters may not have. Given the enduring nature of the [Holocron](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Holocron "Holocron"), I can provide you with complete historical records of events from my time, likely the distant past to you, and instruct you on the ways that we "ancient" Jedi went about things. However, be forewarned that not all of the knowledge I possess is for students just beginning down the path of enlightenment; many of the techniques within this [Holocron](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Holocron "Holocron") are intended only for advanced students. Please take the proper precautions when using any of the techniques that I share with you, as they can be quite dangerous.*

Though this book is intended to be used by heroes, much of the material herein covers mechanics and information more suitable for villains. Gamemasters should, of course, feel free to rule that any of the Sith or Dark Side mechanics are simply unavailable to heroes. However, this book also provides ample material for running campaigns in which the heroes are darksiders. Though these campaigns can be tricky, the information in this book should make it easier to create a variety of dark Force-users, just as this (and other books) have expanded upon the [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") to create a greater diversity of archetypes.

Additionally, Gamemasters should also be sure to peruse the first few chapters for new mechanics for their villains in heroic games. Many of the mechanics for Dark Side Force-users were designed to help facilitate more interesting encounters, and while some of the sample characters and creatures in [Dangers of Study](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dangers_of_Study "Dangers of Study") make use of these mechanics, there are far more combinations waiting to be explored that can be found in a single character or creature.

Did you know that [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") are not the only source of knowledge of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") at the Academy? It is true. These Jedi do not know that a powerful [Sith Spirit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Spirit "Sith Spirit") lurks in their midst, and, if you wish to seek out the true way to control the power of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), you would be wise to heed my words and keep my teachings to yourself.*

## [Power of the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Power_of_the_Force "Power of the Force")

[Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") heroes from all eras are as varied in their skills and abilities as any other kind of hero. Some Jedi are warriors, born to use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") on the battlefield to banish [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"). Others are born to be introspective seekers of knowledge, using meditation to peer into the secrets of the universe in search of truth. Regardless of what kind of Jedi your hero might be, this chapter presents many new [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents"), [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"), and [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") to help bring your Jedi's unique skills to the forefront. The mechanics presented in this chapter cover a variety of Force powers and methods, some meant for all and others meant only for those who embrace [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side").

This chapter also takes a look at many character archetypes within the ranks of [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") and [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith"). Among the Jedi Knights, many Jedi are classified by their specialities; there are Jedi Healers, Jedi Investigators, Jedi Battlemasters, Jedi Instructors, and so forth. These Jedi might be described by their archetype, but these archetypes do not necessarily define them. The nature of [Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent_Trees "Talent Trees"), the [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") Heroic Class, and the [Jedi Knight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Knight "Jedi Knight") Prestige Class gives each hero's player the choice of just how closely to follow the archetype. You can choose to make your Jedi a master of one aspect of Jedi Knighthood, embodying the archetype you have chosen in every [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") choice. Alternatively, you can dabble in two, three, or even more [Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent_Trees "Talent Trees"), creating a Jedi who chooses to be more cosmopolitan about their areas of study.

Additionally, many of the mechanics in this chapter can be used to flesh out [Force Adepts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Adepts "Force Adepts") of other [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions"), including those presented in [Affiliated Programs](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Affiliated_Programs "Affiliated Programs"). Some of the Force Powers in this chapter are specifically the domain of those [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions"), such as [The Aing-Tii Monks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Aing-Tii_Monks "The Aing-Tii Monks") and their *[Fold Space](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fold_Space "Fold Space")* Force Power, or the *[Intercept](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intercept "Intercept")* Force Power favored by [The Zeison Sha](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Zeison_Sha "The Zeison Sha") warriors. The [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") for the [Force Adept](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Adept "Force Adept") Prestige Class also create their own sub-archetypes, many of which may not be appropriate to certain [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions"). Players on non-Jedi Force-users should consult with their Gamemasters to determine exactly which [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") and [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") are appropriate for creating a character from a particular [Force Tradition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Tradition "Force Tradition").

### [Force Regimens](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Regimens "Force Regimens")

Force Regimens are training techniques used by [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"), [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith"), and other [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions") to hone their skills and enhance their sensitivity to The Force. Even Jedi Masters, who have spent years working to achieve a level of Force mastery that others cannot, still meditate and train on a regular basis. Force Regimens are ritualistic exercises that Force-users can perform during their downtime to keep themselves at peak performance.

Most Force Regimens are designed to focus on one particular aspect of Force training. The benefits of a Force Regimen last for 24 hours, and only one Force Regimen's effects can be active in a given 24-hour period. For story purposes, you can perform as many Force Regimens as you like; you only benefit from the first one performed, however. If you fail to complete a Force Regimen, you cannot gain the benefits of that Force Regimen for 24 hours, though you can attempt another Force Regimen you know.

You learn Force Regimens by taking the [Force Regimen Mastery](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Regimen_Mastery "Force Regimen Mastery") Feat. Once you learn a Force Regimen, you can use it any time you have enough spare time to do so. Most Force-users perform their Force Regimens in the morning each day, though there is no rule to say when a Force Regimen must be performed.

### [Lightsaber Form Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Form_Powers "Lightsaber Form Powers")

The following [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") are part of a new group of abilities called Lightsaber Form Powers. These Form Powers are tagged with the [*[Lightsaber Form](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Form "Lightsaber Form")*] descriptor. Each Form Power represents a single technique associated with one of the Jedi [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") combat forms. Each Form Power uses some [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") manipulation, and most also involve a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") attack; additionally, each power has a special added benefit that you gain as a result of having the appropriate [Lightsaber Form Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Forms_Talent_Tree "Lightsaber Forms Talent Tree") for that Form Power. You must be wielding an activated [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") to use any Form Power with the [*[Lightsaber Form](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Form "Lightsaber Form")*] descriptor. You cannot *[Rebuke](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebuke "Rebuke")* a Lightsaber Form Power, with two exceptions: *[Draw Closer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Draw_Closer "Draw Closer")* and *[Pushing Slash](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pushing_Slash "Pushing Slash")*.

Lightsaber Form Powers are intended to help Jedi (and Sith) characters make use of special attacks associated with the various Lightsaber Forms well before they are able to access the [Lightsaber Forms Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Forms_Talent_Tree "Lightsaber Forms Talent Tree"). They are also a good way for a character to say that he or she is a practitioner of a certain form without necessarily having spent a precious [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") on that form. Many famous Jedi are regarded as students of multiple forms, and with these Form Powers, your heroes can dabble in several different forms, even if they are only considered masters of a small few.

## [Philosophy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Philosophy "Philosophy")

Of all the elements present in the *Star Wars* saga, the one that brings everything together is [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). Without it, Palpatine would have no power to put his plans into motion as effectively as he did. Without it, the Sith-controlled Empire could not replace the Republic as the central government of the galaxy. Without it, neither [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") nor [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") would have any extraordinary abilities to help them accomplish their goals. Without [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), we would not have [Anakin Skywalker](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Anakin_Skywalker "Anakin Skywalker"), destined to fulfill an age-old prophecy.

Throughout the *Star Wars* saga, we have heard [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") referred to as an energy field created by all living things, the source of power for all Jedi, and that which unites all life in the galaxy. Although [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") is all that and more, we do not have very specific information about it. This chapter delves into the beliefs held by [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"), [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith"), and other [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions") in an attempt to answer pertinent questions about [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force").

## [Equipment & Artifacts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment_%26_Artifacts "Equipment & Artifacts")

Most Force-users, especially [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") and [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith"), need little in the way of special accoutrements beyond their robes and their [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers"). Still, over the millennia that [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") have roamed the galaxy, certain objects, weapons, and suits of armor have gained popularity among members of the Order and, in many cases, have subsequently faded into obscurity. However, one item has never gone out of style since its creation: the [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber"). This chapter provides an in-depth look at [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers"), including their construction, and includes information on a variety of other objects of significance to Force-users.

Not all items are available in all eras. In the days of [The Jedi Civil War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Civil_War "The Jedi Civil War"), the galaxy is filled with [Sith Artifacts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Artifacts "Sith Artifacts") and weapons of strange construction, as [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") and [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") try to outdo one another in a Force-assisted arms race. However, by the time of the Rebellion against the Empire, [Emperor Palpatine](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Emperor_Palpatine "Emperor Palpatine") had already confiscated and, in many cases, destroyed ancient Jedi and [Sith Artifacts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Artifacts "Sith Artifacts"), meaning that very few such objects survive in that time period and beyond. By the time that [Luke Skywalker](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Luke_Skywalker "Luke Skywalker") founds the new Jedi Academy, the search for Jedi artifacts unearths a few items of significance. A Gamemaster must decide which weapons and objects are available in his or her campaign, and should feel free to rule that some items simply no longer exist in the galaxy.

With that in mind, there are many ways to reintroduce lost objects and artifacts in games set farther forward in the *Star Wars* timeline. [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") are notorious for creating tombs and mausoleums for their dead, honoring great Sith Lords with temples of epic size and complexity. Within these tombs lie many undiscovered secrets, among them lost artifacts (or, perhaps, merely the secret lore of how to recreate them). Gamemasters who rule that some objects are not available for general purchase or construction might dole out those same objects by placing such relics in undisturbed tombs, in the remains of Sith and Jedi ships of war that crashed onto remote, unknown worlds long ago, or in vessels and [Space Stations](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Space_Stations "Space Stations") that have drifted derelict through space for thousands of years.

### [Advanced Lightsaber Construction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Advanced_Lightsaber_Construction "Advanced Lightsaber Construction")

For some Jedi and Sith, a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") is not just a weapon; it is a personalized emblem of one's life and philosophy. Similarly, some players may wish to customize their Jedi hero's Lightsaber to reflect the character's philosophy and fighting style, beyond the basic Lightsaber Construction rules found above. This following section includes a wealth of new options for players and Gamemasters looking to customize their [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers") to better fit the characters who wield them. The Advanced Lightsaber Construction process begins when a hero reaches 7th level.

1. First, the player or Gamemaster must decide what kind to [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") to build. The standard [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") is the most common [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") in the galaxy, and it is not only the easiest to build but also requires no special training to use effectively in combat (besides the standard [Weapon Proficiency (Lightsabers)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Lightsabers) "Weapon Proficiency (Lightsabers)") Feat).
2. Once a particular kind of [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") has been selected, the next step is to choose a Lightsaber Crystal. Any character can build a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") from an [Adegan Crystal](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Adegan_Crystal "Adegan Crystal"), [Ilum Crystal](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ilum_Crystal "Ilum Crystal"), or [Synthetic Crystal](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Synthetic_Crystal "Synthetic Crystal"); the default assumption is that [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") (and other [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber")-wielding [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions")) have ample access to these Lightsaber Crystals and need perform no special quest to obtain one. However, if you wish to use a Lightsaber Crystal of another kind, the character may need to undergo a special adventure to obtain the Lightsaber Crystal.
3. Once the desired Lightsaber Crystal has been chosen and obtained, the next step is to choose any special Lightsaber Accessories or Lightsaber Modifications to be made. With that in mind, the Advanced Lightsaber Construction can begin.
4. Special Lightsaber Crystals and Lightsaber Accessories can modify the DC to construct the [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber"). When Advanced Lightsaber Construction begins, using the standard rules above, applying these modifiers to the standard [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check DC before the check is made (the base DC is determined by the type of [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers") being constructed, per the table blow).
5. Once the Lightsaber is constructed, you may spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to attune it (a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action")). From that point on, the creator of this Self-Built Lightsaber gains the benefit of the used Lightsaber Crystal.

### [Holocrons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Holocrons "Holocrons")

There are few artifacts in the galaxy that are more highly valued and more unique than Holocrons. Holocrons are, essentially, special computers with built-in holoprojectors that contain vast knowledge of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). Each Holocron is a small, hand-held device (usually taking the form of some common geometric shape). Holocrons use ancient construction methods to combine powerful crystals with other technological elements to enhance its storage capabilities and durability. Though [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order") does not allow the study of Holocrons by outside technicians, most technological experts agree that the nature of the Holocron indicates that its inner workings are vastly more complex than most modern technologies, and that the components and workings of the Holocron follow few, if any, contemporary theories of how computers and other electronic machinery should work.

In truth, a Holocron is more of an artifact than a technological device. Each Holocron is imbued with at least one "Gatekeeper" personality, which serves as the user interface to the library of knowledge stored within. A [Holocron Gatekeeper](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Holocron_Gatekeeper "Holocron Gatekeeper") is far more than a simple voice-interface user interface; the Gatekeeper has its own personality and is usually based on an individual [Jedi Master](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Master "Jedi Master") (or [Sith Lord](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Lord "Sith Lord")) who was instrumental in creating the Holocron. Most Holocrons have only a single Gatekeeper, as the Holocron represents the sum total of the knowledge of its creator. Some rare Holocrons have multiple [Holocron Gatekeepers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Holocron_Gatekeepers "Holocron Gatekeepers"), each one acting as a means of obtaining subsets of knowledge. For example, a Jedi Holocron might have a Weapon Master Gatekeeper to impart knowledge of fighting styles, while also having an Investigator Gatekeeper responsible for all of the Holocron's knowledge of *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")* techniques.

The depth of a Holocron's knowledge varies, but suffice to say that every Holocron has within it a vast amount of information on [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers"), [Force Techniques](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Techniques "Force Techniques"), and [Force Secrets](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Secrets "Force Secrets"). Any given Holocron usually contains the sum total of all knowledge of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") gleaned by an individual [Sith Lord](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Lord "Sith Lord") or [Jedi Master](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Master "Jedi Master"), and in many cases it can contain more than that. Many [Jedi Masters](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Masters "Jedi Masters") of [The Old Republic Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic_Era "The Old Republic Era"), as well as [Sith Lords](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Lords "Sith Lords") from all [Eras of Play](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Eras_of_Play "Eras of Play"), see the creation of a Holocron as a crucial part of preserving their legacies. Though Sith sometimes linger as [Dark Side Spirits](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Spirits "Dark Side Spirits") and some Jedi remain can remain as [Force Spirits](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Spirits "Force Spirits"), a Holocron is a far more permanent representation of their legacies. Though the art of creating a Holocron is among the rarest secrets in the galaxy, the most powerful Sith Lords and Jedi Masters of old create Holocrons to spread their influence and preserve their knowledge for ages.

Sith HolocronsSith Holocrons

Unlike normal Holocrons, a Sith Holocron can have a corrupting influence on its possessor. Sith Holocrons contain forbidden knowledge of great power, and serve only to enhance the reputation of [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") for being the quick and easy path. A young student of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") in possession of a Sith Holocron can quickly come into possession of dark knowledge that is forbidden to most Jedi Knights.

A character with a Sith Holocron can be considered part of [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") Force Tradition for the purposes of the [Sith Apprentice](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Apprentice "Sith Apprentice") Prestige Class. Furthermore, a character that spends at least 1 hour studying under the tutelage of a Sith Holocron can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") to add one [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") with the [*[Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side "Dark Side")*] descriptor to his or her [Force Power Suite](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power_Suite "Force Power Suite"). That character can do this for one day; the following day, he or she must study for at least 1 hour with the Sith Holocron before gaining access to this ability again.

## [Affiliated Programs](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Affiliated_Programs "Affiliated Programs")

[The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") and [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") are just two of the many [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions") found throughout the galaxy. Every sentient [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") with the ability to utilize [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") has its own groups and philosophies, although only a tiny fraction have been documented by [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order"). Young students are taught about some of these groups, to show the various ways that [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") manifests itself in the galaxy and how it's expressed by different cultures.

The different programs described here are included to show some of the alternate beliefs, thoughts, and techniques practices by outlying traditions. Although many of these groups have similar agendas as [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"), most have unique requirements for entry that prevent large numbers of students from joining. For example, entry into [The Baran Do Sages](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Baran_Do_Sages "The Baran Do Sages") is limited to [Kel Dor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kel_Dor "Kel Dor") initiates with sensitivity to [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). Over the millennia, [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") have set up "exchange programs" with some of these groups, sending both students and Jedi Masters alike to study these alternate traditions and learn from them. [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") do their best to shield students from some of the more controversial beliefs, but realize that part of understanding [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), particularly [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"), is to ask questions and witness different beliefs firsthand.

## [Instructors & Alumni](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Instructors_%26_Alumni "Instructors & Alumni")

Throughout history, [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order") has worked hard to preserve life in all forms throughout the galaxy while expanding its knowledge of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). Although Jedi do not seek to place any great importance on a single person, some individuals are recognized by their peers as well as those in authority for furthering the cause of [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") and helping others become decent members of the Order. These individuals are the masters and teachers of [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") whose knowledge and wisdom have passed down through the generations and are revered for their contributions that have helped guide the Order in its mission to bring peace and order to the galaxy.

## [Dangers of Study](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dangers_of_Study "Dangers of Study")

The galaxy is vast, and even after thousands of years of study, only a tiny fraction of the planets, [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species"), and [Beasts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Beasts "Beasts") that it contains have been discovered, much less studied. Jedi Padawans are warned of the untold dangers that await them in the galactic vastness, which is not to say that [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") should destroy every dangerous beast they encounter. However, if one is to fulfill the role of galactic peacekeeper, one must be aware of the dangers that the galaxy abides.

The creatures and individuals described here are but a sample of the strange, exotic, and lethal dangers that a Jedi might encounter during an adventure. Some creatures are relegated to a single planet or even a single location, but others have managed to spread across the galaxy. In rare circumstances, [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") have deemed some of these creatures, notably those altered by [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"), as grave threats that must be eliminated to help bring harmony to the galaxy.

[The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") are interested in [Beasts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Beasts "Beasts") that have sensitivity to [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") and the ability to wield it in some manner. The Jedi Council has numerous ongoing programs in place to discover, catalog, and study Force-sensitive creatures, both to learn more about them and to gain a greater insight into their own assumptions on how [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") manifests itself. One thing is for certain- most creatures are dangerous in their own right, but those that can command [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") tend to be smarter, faster, tougher, or deadlier than normal.

## [Studying Abroad](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Studying_Abroad "Studying Abroad")

The *Star Wars* universe contains many locations that have some importance to the galaxy's various Force sects, especially [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"). Some of these locales are hubs of activity where The Jedi come together, while others are places immersed in [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") that tempt any Jedi lured into them. Regardless of their origins and histories, all of the following locations have been significant in one way or another to [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order") throughout its history.

**Places in the Light:** Areas where [The Light Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Light_Side "The Light Side") of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") holds prominence are held in high regard by [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") as well as most other religions and [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions") that believe in the preservation of life and balance in [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). Pilgrimages to these sites are frequent, and these locations hold within them the memories of conflict with those who sought the power of [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") and were defeated by defenders of [The Light Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Light_Side "The Light Side").

**Areas of Darkness:** Those who are consumed by [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") strive to gain power to fuel their own agendas, but they also know that there are individuals in the galaxy who will oppose them at every turn. For this reason alone, many darksiders choose places far away from the activities of [The Core Worlds](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Core_Worlds?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Core Worlds (page does not exist)") to pursue their covert studies and develop their secret plans. As these darksiders are found and defeated by [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") and other champions of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), the places where they lived become tainted by the dark powers they used while they lived and worked there. Although efforts are taken to reduce or eliminate the amount of influence [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") has over these locales, the dark power that thrived there for so long puts a permanent stain on the place that continues to affect everything around it for a long time. Because of this, [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") continue to search for ways to cleanse these areas and rid the galaxy of the evil that dwells there.

# Scout Bonus Feats

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scout_Bonus_Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scout_Bonus_Feats) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:05:36Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

At each even-numbered level (2nd, 4th, 6th, etc.), a [Scout](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scout "Scout") gains a Bonus [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat"). This [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat") must be selected from the following Scout Bonus Feats lists, and a [Scout](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scout "Scout") must meet any prerequisites for that [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat").

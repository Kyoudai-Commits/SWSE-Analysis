# Category : Shapeshift

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Shapeshift](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Shapeshift) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:07:03Z.

[Species] are shapechangers and can alter their appearance at will (see the [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") skill). While Shapeshifted, a [Species] gains a +10 [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") bonus on [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") checks made to disguise appearance. Additionally, a [Species] can disguise its appearance as a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action") at no penalty.

- [Species] cannot alter their size without the [Metamorph](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Metamorph "Metamorph") feat.

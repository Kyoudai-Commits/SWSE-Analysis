# The Inquisitorius

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Inquisitorius](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Inquisitorius) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:04:29Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

Imperial Inquisitors are the latest, secret addition to [Imperial Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Imperial_Intelligence "Imperial Intelligence") by [The Galactic Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Galactic_Empire "The Galactic Empire"). At the request of the Emperor, a special division has been created called The Inquisitorius, which specializes in scouring the galaxy for Force-users of all types and eliminating them. Led by a Grand Inquisitor, these Dark Side Force-users seek out surviving Jedi, adepts, primitive shamans, and all students of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") who are not aligned with the Emperor.

The Inquisitors are ruthless and effective even when not using [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"). Rumors of their methods and existence are slowly spreading throughout the intelligence services, making them among the most feared operatives. The Inquisitors work best through interrogation and investigation, with and without [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). Few can withstand an Inquisitor's interrogation session.

Some Inquisitors learn significant [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") skills, but most do not have the formal training of a Sith or a Jedi. Many Inquisitors learn only basic or specialized Force abilities best suited for interrogation. If they happen upon a Jedi or some other Force-user of significant skill, they might pass the word to [Darth Vader](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Vader "Darth Vader") that the matter requires his special attention.

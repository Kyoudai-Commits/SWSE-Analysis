# Gunner

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunner](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunner) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:04:01Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

Most military and some civilian [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") have built-in weapons. Any weapon not controlled by the [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot") or [Copilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Copilot "Copilot") requires a Gunner to operate. A [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") can have as many Gunners as it has Gunner positions.

The below Actions include all Actions that can be performed while functioning as a Gunner:

## [Full-Round Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Actions "Full-Round Actions")

Full Attack

As a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"), you can make more than one attack. To gain extra attacks, you must be wielding two weapons, wielding a [Double Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Weapon "Double Weapon"), or using a special ability that grants extra attacks. When making multiple attacks, you may resolve your attacks in any order desired, declaring the target of each attack immediately before making the attack roll.

Extra attacks granted from different sources are cumulative. Any penalties associated with gaining an extra attack apply to all attacks that the character makes until the start of their next turn.

## Double Attack and Triple Attack

The [Double Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Attack "Double Attack") feat allows a character to make one extra attack during a Full Attack, but the character takes a -5 penalty on all attacks until the start of their next turn. The [Triple Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Triple_Attack "Triple Attack") feat allows a character to make one extra attack in addition to that granted by the [Double Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Attack "Double Attack") feat, giving the character an additional -5 penalty (for a total -10 penalty) on all attacks until the start of their next turn.

If the character is armed with more than one weapon (or uses a [Double Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Weapon "Double Weapon")), they may choose which weapon (or weapons) they will choose to make each extra attack.

## [Standard Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Actions "Standard Actions")

Aid AnotherMelee AttacksRanged AttacksVehicle Attacks

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can aid an ally's next skill check or attack roll, or you can interfere with an enemy's attacks.

## Aiding a Skill Check

You can help another character achieve success on their [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") or [Ability Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Check "Ability Check") by making the same kind of [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") or [Ability Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Check "Ability Check") in a cooperative effort. If you roll a 10 or higher on your check, the character you are helping receives a +2 bonus on their own check. You can't [Take 10](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_10 "Take 10") on a [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") or [Ability Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Check "Ability Check") to Aid Another.

## Aiding an Attack Roll

In combat, you can Aid Another character's attack by forcing an opponent to avoid your own attacks, making it more difficult for them to avoid your ally. Select an opponent and make an attack against a [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") of 10. If you succeed, you grant a +2 bonus on a single ally's next attack roll against that opponent.

In [Vehicle Combat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Combat "Vehicle Combat"), you can Aid Another character's attack by providing improved targeting data, or by coordinating that Attack with other Vehicle Actions.

- A Gunner can grant a +2 bonus on another character's attack roll by making an attack against a [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") of 10.

## Suppressing an Enemy

In combat, you can distract or interfere with an opponent, making their attacks more difficult. Select an opponent and make an attack against a [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") of 10. If you succeed, that opponent takes a -2 penalty on its next attack roll.

With a melee weapon, you can strike any opponent within a square you [Threaten](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threaten "Threaten"). You can only [Threaten](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threaten "Threaten") squares within your [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach") from the Vehicle's space, and you don't [Threaten](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threaten "Threaten") spaces around the Vehicle at all if it grants [Total Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Total_Cover "Total Cover"). You can't make a melee attack in [Starship Scale](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Scale "Starship Scale"), except within the confines of the [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") you occupy.

For example, a Jedi on a Speeder Bike could attack targets adjacent to the bike with their [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber"), but the same Jedi couldn't do so while inside an [AT-AT](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/AT-AT "AT-AT").

With a ranged weapon, you can throw or shoot at any target within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"), as long as your [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") does not grant you [Total Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Total_Cover "Total Cover") ([Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range") modifiers apply normally for your weapon). You cannot make a personal ranged attack in [Starship Scale](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Scale "Starship Scale"), except within the confines of the [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") you occupy.

For example, [Clone Troopers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Troopers "Clone Troopers") can fire their blasters at targets outside a [LAAT/i Gunship](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/LAAT/i_Gunship "LAAT/i Gunship"), as long as the ship's doors are open.

Any Vehicle equipped with weapons can make attacks against opponents within [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") Weapon Range (see below). An attack roll with a Vehicle Weapon is calculated as follows:

**Vehicle Attack: 1d20 + [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") + Vehicle's [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") Modifier + Weapon Range Modifier**

- *[Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus"):* Use the [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") of the Gunner (which, in some cases, may be the [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot")).
  - If you are [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in the [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") skill, you gain a +2 bonus on all attacks made with any Vehicle Weapon identified as being crewed by the [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot").
- *[Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") Modifier:* A Vehicle's computer improves the accuracy of the Vehicle's [Weapon Systems](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Systems "Weapon Systems"), and the Vehicle's [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") score measures the quality of the computer.
- *Weapon Range Modifier:* A Vehicle Weapon can attack a target at Point-Blank, Short, Medium, or Long Range.
  - If you make a Vehicle Attack against a target within Point-Blank Range, you take no penalty on the attack roll; your penalty on attack rolls increases to
    - -2 at Short Range,
    - -5 at Medium Range,
    - and -10 at Long Range.
- **Critical Hits:** When you roll a Natural 20 on your attack, you automatically hit the target and deal double damage. When using a [Vehicle Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Weapon "Vehicle Weapon"), you cannot apply any effect that causes an automatic Natural 20 or automatic Critical Hit (such as spending a [Destiny Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Point "Destiny Point") or using the Jedi Master’s [Serenity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Serenity "Serenity") class feature), unless a rule specifies that it can be used with Vehicle Attacks.
- **Automatic Misses:** If you roll a Natural 1 on your attack, you automatically miss.

**Vehicle Damage: (Weapon Damage + 1/2 Pilot's Heroic Level + Miscellaneous Modifiers) x Damage Multiplier**

- *Damage Multiplier:* After rolling the weapon damage dice, multiply the result by the listed Damage Multiplier. For example, when you fire a [Vehicle Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Weapon "Vehicle Weapon") that deals 6d10x2 damage, roll 6d10 and multiply the result by 2.

## [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions")

Aim

Just as in Character Combat, you can Aim with two [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions") before making a ranged attack. When you do so, you ignore all [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") bonuses to your target's [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") on your next attack. You still must have [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") to the target, however. You lose the benefits of Aiming if you lose [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") to your target or if you take any other [Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Action "Action") before making your attack. Aiming provides no benefit when making an [Area Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attack "Area Attack").

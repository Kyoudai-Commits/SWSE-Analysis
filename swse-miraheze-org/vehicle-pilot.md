# Vehicle Pilot

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:06:28Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

The Pilot of the Vehicle controls its movement. Most [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") have only one position from where it can be Piloted. Piloting a Vehicle is, at a minimum, a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action") to [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") (a Pilot cannot reenter a square they just left), which means that the Vehicle Pilot can do other things with their [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") and [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"). A Vehicle can have only one Pilot at a time. The Vehicle Pilot adds the Vehicle's size modifier and [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") modifier on all [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative") and [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") checks.

The below Actions include all other Actions that can be performed while functioning as a Vehicle Pilot:

## [Full-Round Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Actions "Full-Round Actions")

All-Out MovementFull AttackRam

As a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"), a Vehicle Pilot can move up to four times their Vehicle's [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed"). All of this movement must be in a straight line, and you can't [Avoid Collisions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Avoid_Collisions "Avoid Collisions") while doing so. Your [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") loses its [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus to [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") until the beginning of your next turn. You can't use All-Out Movement unless you moved on your last turn. Because most power is diverted to the engines when using All-Out Movement, [Gunners](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunners "Gunners") onboard your [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") cannot attack until the start of your next turn.

## Maximum Velocity

When at [Character Scale](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Character_Scale "Character Scale"), if you use All-Out Movement on your turn, you may move up to your Vehicle's Maximum Velocity with All-Out Movement on your next turn. If you move your Vehicle's Maximum Velocity, you must either continue moving at the Vehicle's Maximum Velocity, or use All-Out Movement as your Action on the following turn.

As a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"), you can make more than one attack. To gain extra attacks, you must be wielding two weapons, wielding a [Double Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Weapon "Double Weapon"), or using a special ability that grants extra attacks. When making multiple attacks, you may resolve your attacks in any order desired, declaring the target of each attack immediately before making the attack roll.

Extra attacks granted from different sources are cumulative. Any penalties associated with gaining an extra attack apply to all attacks that the character makes until the start of their next turn.

A Vehicle Pilot who spends at least a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action") every round moving their [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") cannot make a Full Attack Action, unless the Vehicle has already been brought to a [Full Stop](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full_Stop "Full Stop").

## Double Attack and Triple Attack

The [Double Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Attack "Double Attack") feat allows a character to make one extra attack during a Full Attack, but the character takes a -5 penalty on all attacks until the start of their next turn. The [Triple Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Triple_Attack "Triple Attack") feat allows a character to make one extra attack in addition to that granted by the [Double Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Attack "Double Attack") feat, giving the character an additional -5 penalty (for a total -10 penalty) on all attacks until the start of their next turn.

If the character is armed with more than one weapon (or uses a [Double Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Weapon "Double Weapon")), they may choose which weapon (or weapons) they will choose to make each extra attack.

As a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"), a Vehicle Pilot can attempt to Ram a target by moving its [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") into the target's [Fighting Space](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fighting_Space "Fighting Space"). You must have sufficient movement to reach the target's [Fighting Space](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fighting_Space "Fighting Space"), and the Ram is treated as an [Area Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attack "Area Attack"). You can use your Vehicle to Ram just about anything, including another Vehicle, a creature, or a structure.

Make a [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") check at a -10 penalty against the target's [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"). If the check succeeds, your [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle"), the target, and all passengers aboard Vehicles involved in the [Collision](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Collision "Collision") (including you) take the amount of damage listed in [Collisions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Collisions "Collisions"). Assuming you're alive and conscious, you can continue moving through the target's [Fighting Space](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fighting_Space "Fighting Space") if your [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") has any movement left. Otherwise, your [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") is pushed out of the target's [Fighting Space](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fighting_Space "Fighting Space"), and into the nearest available square(s), and your turn ends. If your [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") check fails, your [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle"), the target, and all passengers aboard [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") involved in the [Collision](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Collision "Collision") (including you) take half damage. In addition, your [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") is pushed out of the target's [Fighting Space](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fighting_Space "Fighting Space"), and into the nearest available square(s), and your turn ends.

If you have the **[Vehicular Evasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicular_Evasion "Vehicular Evasion")** Talent, you can reduce or negate the damage your [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") and its passengers take when Rammed by another Vehicle.

## All-Out Movement

If the colliding Vehicle is performing an [All-Out Movement](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/All-Out_Movement "All-Out Movement") Action, or moving at Maximum Velocity, double the damage caused by the [Collision](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Collision "Collision").

## [Standard Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Actions "Standard Actions")

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can aid an ally's next skill check or attack roll, or you can interfere with an enemy's attacks.

## Aiding a Skill Check

You can help another character achieve success on their [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") or [Ability Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Check "Ability Check") by making the same kind of [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") or [Ability Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Check "Ability Check") in a cooperative effort. If you roll a 10 or higher on your check, the character you are helping receives a +2 bonus on their own check. You can't [Take 10](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_10 "Take 10") on a [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") or [Ability Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Check "Ability Check") to Aid Another.

## Aiding an Attack Roll

In combat, you can Aid Another character's attack by forcing an opponent to avoid your own attacks, making it more difficult for them to avoid your ally. Select an opponent and make an attack against a [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") of 10. If you succeed, you grant a +2 bonus on a single ally's next attack roll against that opponent.

In [Vehicle Combat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Combat "Vehicle Combat"), you can Aid Another character's attack by providing improved targeting data, or by coordinating that Attack with other Vehicle Actions.

## Suppressing an Enemy

In combat, you can distract or interfere with an opponent, making their attacks more difficult. Select an opponent and make an attack against a [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") of 10. If you succeed, that opponent takes a -2 penalty on its next attack roll.

With a melee weapon, you can strike any opponent within a square you [Threaten](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threaten "Threaten"). You can only [Threaten](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threaten "Threaten") squares within your [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach") from the Vehicle's space, and you don't [Threaten](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threaten "Threaten") spaces around the Vehicle at all if it grants [Total Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Total_Cover "Total Cover"). You can't make a melee attack in [Starship Scale](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Scale "Starship Scale"), except within the confines of the [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") you occupy.

For example, a Jedi on a Speeder Bike could attack targets adjacent to the bike with their [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber"), but the same Jedi couldn't do so while inside an [AT-AT](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/AT-AT "AT-AT").

With a ranged weapon, you can throw or shoot at any target within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"), as long as your [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") does not grant you [Total Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Total_Cover "Total Cover") ([Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range") modifiers apply normally for your weapon). You cannot make a personal ranged attack in [Starship Scale](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Scale "Starship Scale"), except within the confines of the [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") you occupy.

For example, [Clone Troopers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Troopers "Clone Troopers") can fire their blasters at targets outside a [LAAT/i Gunship](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/LAAT/i_Gunship "LAAT/i Gunship"), as long as the ship's doors are open.

Any Vehicle equipped with weapons can make attacks against opponents within [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") Weapon Range (see below). An attack roll with a Vehicle Weapon is calculated as follows:

**Vehicle Attack: 1d20 + [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") + Vehicle's [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") Modifier + Weapon Range Modifier**

- *[Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus"):* Use the [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") of the [Gunner](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunner "Gunner") (which, in some cases, may be the Vehicle Pilot).
  - If you are [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in the [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") skill, you gain a +2 bonus on all attacks made with any Vehicle Weapon identified as being crewed by the Vehicle Pilot.
- *[Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") Modifier:* A Vehicle's computer improves the accuracy of the Vehicle's [Weapon Systems](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Systems "Weapon Systems"), and the Vehicle's [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") score measures the quality of the computer.
- *Weapon Range Modifier:* A Vehicle Weapon can attack a target at Point-Blank, Short, Medium, or Long Range.
  - If you make a Vehicle Attack against a target within Point-Blank Range, you take no penalty on the attack roll; your penalty on attack rolls increases to
    - -2 at Short Range,
    - -5 at Medium Range,
    - and -10 at Long Range.
- **Critical Hits:** When you roll a Natural 20 on your attack, you automatically hit the target and deal double damage. When using a [Vehicle Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Weapon "Vehicle Weapon"), you cannot apply any effect that causes an automatic Natural 20 or automatic Critical Hit (such as spending a [Destiny Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Point "Destiny Point") or using the Jedi Master’s [Serenity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Serenity "Serenity") class feature), unless a rule specifies that it can be used with Vehicle Attacks.
- **Automatic Misses:** If you roll a Natural 1 on your attack, you automatically miss.

**Vehicle Damage: (Weapon Damage + 1/2 Pilot's Heroic Level + Miscellaneous Modifiers) x Damage Multiplier**

- *Damage Multiplier:* After rolling the weapon damage dice, multiply the result by the listed Damage Multiplier. For example, when you fire a [Vehicle Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Weapon "Vehicle Weapon") that deals 6d10x2 damage, roll 6d10 and multiply the result by 2.

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), a Vehicle Pilot can move up to their Vehicle's [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") (minimum 2 squares) in a straight line through unobstructed terrain directly toward their target, and then make a [Vehicle Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Attack "Vehicle Attack") at that target at the end of their movement. You gain a +2 bonus on your attack roll, and take a -2 penalty to your Vehicle's [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") until the start of your next turn.

In [Starship Scale](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Scale "Starship Scale"), you cannot make an Attack Run through squares occupied by opponents, but allies do not hinder your Attack Run.

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), the Vehicle Pilot of an [Airspeeder](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Airspeeder "Airspeeder") or [Starfighter](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starfighter "Starfighter") can initiate a Dogfight against an enemy [Airspeeder](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Airspeeder "Airspeeder") or [Starfighter](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starfighter "Starfighter") in an adjacent square ([Starship Scale](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Scale "Starship Scale")). Make a [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") check at a -5 penalty, opposed by the target's own [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") check. If an enemy [Starfighter](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starfighter "Starfighter") tries to move through your square, or an adjacent square, you may attempt to initiate a Dogfight as an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity"). If successful, the enemy Starfighter must stop moving.

## Consequences of Dogfighting

A [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") engaged in a Dogfight must select the Dogfight Action on every turn, and it cannot move out of its current square until it Disengages (see below). While engaged in a Dogfight, you cannot attack any target outside the Dogfight.

- **Attacking in a Dogfight:** Make an opposed [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") check as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), if you succeed on the opposed check, you may make a single [Vehicle Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Attack "Vehicle Attack") as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action").
  - If you fail, you cannot attack the target, and any [Gunners](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunners "Gunners") in your [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") take a -5 penalty on any attack rolls they make until your next turn.
- **Disengaging from a Dogfight:** Make an opposed [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") check as a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action"), if you succeed on the opposed check, you successfully Disengage from the Dogfight, and may move a number of squares equal to your Vehicle's [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed").
  - If you fail, you remain in the Dogfight, and any [Gunners](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunners "Gunners") in your [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") take a -5 penalty on any attack rolls they make until your next turn.
- **Firing into a Dogfight:** If another [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") fires into a Dogfight, it takes a -5 penalty on its attack roll, unless the [Gunner](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunner "Gunner") has the [Precise Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Precise_Shot "Precise Shot") feat.

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), a Vehicle Pilot can concentrate more on protecting their [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle"), than hurting their enemies. You and all [Gunners](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunners "Gunners") on your Vehicle take a -5 penalty on your attack rolls, and your [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") gains a +2 dodge bonus to its [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") until the start of your next turn. If you choose to make no attacks, your Vehicle instead gains a +5 dodge bonus to its [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), and all [Gunners](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunners "Gunners") take a -10 penalty on their attack rolls until the start of your next turn.

**Trained Pilot:** If you are [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in the [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") skill, your [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") instead gains a +5 dodge bonus to its [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), with you and all [Gunners](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunners "Gunners") on your Vehicle taking a -5 penalty on attack rolls; or a +10 dodge bonus if you choose to make no attacks on your turn.

You can make [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") checks to [Feint](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feint "Feint") while [Piloting](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Piloting "Piloting") a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle"). Add your Vehicle's size modifier (see below) and [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") modifier to your [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") check, and take a -5 penalty if you are not [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in the [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") skill. If you successfully [Feint](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feint "Feint") against a target, it is considered [Flat-Footed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flat-Footed "Flat-Footed") against only your first attack, not those of any [Gunners](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunners "Gunners") on your [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle").

## [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions")

AimFull StopIncrease Vehicle Speed

Just as in Character Combat, you can Aim with two [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions") before making a ranged attack. When you do so, you ignore all [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") bonuses to your target's [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") on your next attack. You still must have [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") to the target, however. You lose the benefits of Aiming if you lose [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") to your target or if you take any other [Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Action "Action") before making your attack. Aiming provides no benefit when making an [Area Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attack "Area Attack").

If a Vehicle Pilot hasn't already used a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action") or [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action") to move their [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") this turn, they can spend a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") to bring the [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") to a Full Stop. After that, the [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") is considered stationary. You cannot bring a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") to a Full Stop if you used the [All-Out Movement](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/All-Out_Movement "All-Out Movement") Action on your last turn.

If you are [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in the [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") skill and acting as a Vehicle Pilot, you may make a DC 20 [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") check as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") to push your [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") beyond its normal limits. (You can't [Take 10](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_10 "Take 10") on this check.) If the check fails, your Vehicle's [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") does not increase, and your [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") moves -1 step on the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"). If you succeed, your Vehicle's [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") increases by 1 square until the end of your next turn. For every 5 points by which you exceed the DC, your Vehicle's [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") increases by an additional 1 square.

You may attempt to Increase Vehicle Speed once per round as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action"), when using the [All-Out Movement](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/All-Out_Movement "All-Out Movement") Action.

## [Reactions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reactions "Reactions")

Avoid Collision

Any time your [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") is subject to a [Collision](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Collision "Collision"), the Vehicle Pilot can make a DC 15 [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") check as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"). If you succeed on the check, all targets involved in the [Collision](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Collision "Collision") take half damage. You can't attempt to Avoid Collision when you intentionally [Ram](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ram "Ram") a target, or when using [All-Out Movement](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/All-Out_Movement "All-Out Movement").

**Starship Scale:** [Collisions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Collisions "Collisions") are easier to avoid at [Starship Scale](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Scale "Starship Scale"). If you succeed on the [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") check to Avoid Collision in [Starship Scale](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Scale "Starship Scale"), no [Collision](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Collision "Collision") occurs.

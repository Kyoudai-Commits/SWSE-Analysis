# Unleashed Abilities

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unleashed_Abilities](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unleashed_Abilities) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:06:56Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

Unleashed Abilities are extremely powerful and devastating abilities that even the greatest heroes can use only once in a great while, if ever. (The use of these abilities is optional, and subject to Gamemaster approval.) In a cinematic sense, the times when these abilities come into play are incredibly powerful moments. The Gamemaster and players are encouraged to play up the drama and excitement of such a memorable scene.

Using an Unleashed Ability requires the expenditure of a [Destiny Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Point "Destiny Point"). To use one of these powers, your character must possess the [Unleashed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unleashed "Unleashed") Feat and have a [Destiny](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny "Destiny"). At the Gamemaster's discretion, a hero might be required to complete a special [Destiny](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny "Destiny") to gain access to this Feat (see the [Discovery](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Discovery "Discovery") Destiny). Unlike with [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") and other abilities, Unleashed Abilities are not selected ahead of time. Instead, you select the one you want to use based on the circumstances at the moment. However, you must meet any prerequisites mentioned in the descriptions. If you have the [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity") Feat, you are also able to select Force Unleashed Abilities (see below).

## Unleashed Abilities

The following Unleashed Abilities can be used by any character who possesses the [Unleashed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unleashed "Unleashed") Feat.

| UNLEASHED ABILITY | DESCRIPTION |
| --- | --- |
| Unleashed Bantha Rush | When you use the [Bantha Rush](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bantha_Rush "Bantha Rush") Feat, you can spend a [Destiny Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Point "Destiny Point") to automatically increase the distance you move your target by 5 squares and knock your target [Prone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prone "Prone"). |
| Unleashed Charge | By spending a [Destiny Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Point "Destiny Point"), you make an amazing [Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge "Charge") attack. As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you gain a +5 bonus on each attack roll and take a -2 penalty to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") until the start of your next turn. You can [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") up to twice your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") (minimum 2 squares) in a straight line through obstructed or unobstructed terrain, and then take a [Full Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full_Attack "Full Attack") Action as a part of this [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") instead of a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"). Allies do not hinder your ability to [Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge "Charge"). **Special:** If you have the [Improved Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improved_Charge "Improved Charge") Feat, you can alter your course around obstructions and enemies. If you have the [Powerful Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Powerful_Charge "Powerful Charge") Feat, you can apply the benefits of that Feat as well. |
| Unleashed Cleave | If you posses the [Cleave](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cleave "Cleave") Feat, when you deal enough damage to an opponent to reduce its [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") to 0, you can spend a [Destiny Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Point "Destiny Point") to get an immediate extra [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") against all opponents within your [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach"). This Unleashed Ability works in all other ways as the [Cleave](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cleave "Cleave") Feat. |
| Unleashed Devastating Attack | If you posses the **[Devastating Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Devastating_Attack "Devastating Attack")** Talent, when you make an attack (either as a single attack or part of a [Full Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full_Attack "Full Attack") Action), you can spend a [Destiny Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Point "Destiny Point") to treat every attack as though it exceeded the target's [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold"), regardless of whether or not the attack normally would, until the beginning of your next turn. |
| Unleashed Extreme Effort | When you use the **[Extreme Effort](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Extreme_Effort "Extreme Effort")** Talent, you can spend a [Destiny Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Point "Destiny Point") to increase the bonus provided by the Talent to +20. |
| Unleashed Firepower | By spending a [Destiny Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Point "Destiny Point"), as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") you can fire at up to five separate targets within [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range") of your ranged weapon. Make a single attack roll and compare the result to the [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") of each target. Roll a single damage roll and apply it to every target successfully hit. **Special:** Bonuses and penalties from other [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") and [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") stack with this ability. You can attack each target only once. You must have enough ammunition for each target; otherwise, you stop dealing damage when you run out. [Aiming](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aiming "Aiming") provides no benefit on this attack. |
| Unleashed Ignite Fervor | When you use the **[Ignite Fervor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ignite_Fervor "Ignite Fervor")** Talent, you can spend a [Destiny Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Point "Destiny Point") to provide the damage bonus to all allies within [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"). |
| Unleashed Keen Shot | If you posses the **[Keen Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Keen_Shot "Keen Shot")** Talent, when you make a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack"), you can spend a [Destiny Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Point "Destiny Point") to ignore all [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") on attacks until the beginning of your next turn. |
| Unleashed Mighty Swing | When you use the [Mighty Swing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mighty_Swing "Mighty Swing") Feat, you can spend a [Destiny Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Point "Destiny Point") to gain a +5 bonus on attack rolls and damage rolls, and you can make an automatic [Trip](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trip "Trip") attack (as with the [Trip](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trip "Trip") feat) against the target, also with a +5 bonus on the [Grapple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple "Grapple") check. |
| Unleashed Penetrating Attack | If you posses the **[Penetrating Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Penetrating_Attack "Penetrating Attack")** Talent, when you make an attack (either as a single attack or part of a [Full Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full_Attack "Full Attack") Action), you can spend a [Destiny Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Point "Destiny Point") to ignore all [Damage Reduction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Reduction "Damage Reduction") of your targets until the beginning of your next turn. |
| Unleashed Rage | By spending a [Destiny Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Point "Destiny Point") at the start of your [Rage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rage "Rage"), you gain an additional +10 bonus to your melee attack rolls and melee damage rolls, and a +5 bonus to your [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"). At the end of your [Rage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rage "Rage"), you move -2 [Persistent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persistent "Persistent") steps along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"). The penalties imposed by this [Persistent Condition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persistent_Condition "Persistent Condition") last until you take at least 20 minutes to recuperate, during which time you can't engage in any strenuous activity. **Special:** Bonuses and penalties from other [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") and [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") stack with this ability. |
| Unleashed Skirmisher | When you gain the benefit of the **[Skirmisher](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skirmisher "Skirmisher")** Talent on a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack"), you can spend a [Destiny Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Point "Destiny Point") to get an automatic [Critical Hit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Critical_Hit "Critical Hit") on your attack and gain a bonus to your base damage equal to the number of squares you moved. |
| Unleashed Sneak Attack | When you make an attack that deals extra damage due to the **[Sneak Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sneak_Attack "Sneak Attack")** Talent, you can spend a [Destiny Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Point "Destiny Point") to gain a +5 bonus on your melee or ranged attack roll, and deal an extra 5d6 points of damage with a successful hit. You must be within 6 squares of the target to make a **[Sneak Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sneak_Attack "Sneak Attack")** with a ranged weapon. This extra damage stacks with any bonus damage provided by the **[Sneak Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sneak_Attack "Sneak Attack")** Talent. |
| Unleashed Strafe | When you use the [Strafe](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strafe "Strafe") Feat, you can spend a [Destiny Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Point "Destiny Point") to target an area 4 squares long by 3 squares wide. |
| Unleashed Whirlwind Attack | When you use the [Whirlwind Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Whirlwind_Attack "Whirlwind Attack") Feat, you can spend a [Destiny Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Point "Destiny Point") to gain a +5 bonus on your attack roll, and knock [Prone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prone "Prone") any targets you damage. |

## Force Unleashed Abilities

Like the standard Unleashed Abilities above, Force Unleashed Abilities allow the user to do something incredible with [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). The following Unleashed Abilities are available only to characters who have the [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity") Feat, but otherwise function exactly as other Unleashed Abilities.

| UNLEASHED ABILITY | DESCRIPTION |
| --- | --- |
| Unleashed Battle Meditation | When you use the **[Battle Meditation](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battle_Meditation "Battle Meditation")** Talent, you can spend a [Destiny Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Point "Destiny Point") to have its bonuses apply to all allies within the same star system. |
| Unleashed Battle Strike | When you use the *[Battle Strike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battle_Strike "Battle Strike")* Force Power and you spend a [Destiny Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Point "Destiny Point"), your next [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack"), if successful, also moves the target an additional -2 steps along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"), regardless of whether the damage exceeds the target's [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold"). |
| Unleashed Corruption | When you successfully damage a target by using the *[Corruption](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Corruption "Corruption")* Force Power and you spend a [Destiny Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Point "Destiny Point"), the target takes the additional half damage each round for 5 rounds instead of just 1 round. |
| Unleashed Dark Rage | When you use the *[Dark Rage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Rage "Dark Rage")* Force Power, you can spend a [Destiny Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Point "Destiny Point") to gain a +10 Rage bonus on melee attack rolls and damage rolls for the remainder of the encounter. |
| Unleashed Force Blast | When you use the *[Force Blast](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Blast "Force Blast")* Force Power, you can spend a [Destiny Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Point "Destiny Point") to multiply the damage by 2. |
| Unleashed Force Grip | When you successfully use the *[Force Grip](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Grip "Force Grip")* Force Power on a target, you can spend a [Destiny Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Point "Destiny Point") to maintain the power with no concentration (requiring no action) for a number of rounds equal to half your [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") modifier (minimum 1 round). Taking damage still requires you to make [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") checks to maintain the *[Force Grip](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Grip "Force Grip")*. |
| Unleashed Force Lightning | When you use the *[Force Lightning](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Lightning "Force Lightning")* Force Power, you can spend a [Destiny Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Point "Destiny Point") to target all creatures in a 6-Square [Cone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cone "Cone") that originates from your square. |
| Unleashed Force Storm | When you use the *[Force Storm](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Storm "Force Storm")* Force Power and you spend a [Destiny Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Point "Destiny Point"), it deals 8d6 points of damage to adjacent targets for the duration of the Force Power. |
| Unleashed Force Thrust | When you use the *[Force Thrust](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Thrust "Force Thrust")* Force Power and you spend a [Destiny Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Point "Destiny Point"), opponents affected by the Force Power do not add their size modifier to their [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") checks to resist the Force Power's effect. A creature that would normally have a penalty on its [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") check due to size still retains that penalty. Additionally, the distance you move the target is multiplied by 2. If you push the target into a larger object, the target takes 1d6 points of damage per square moved by this Force Power. |
| Unleashed Move Object | When you hurl one object or character into another object or character with the *[Move Object](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Object "Move Object")* Force Power, and you spend a [Destiny Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Point "Destiny Point"), multiply the damage dealt by 2. |
| Unleashed Repulse | When you use the *[Repulse](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Repulse "Repulse")* Force Power and you spend a [Destiny Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Point "Destiny Point"), opponents affected by the Force Power do not add their size modifier to their [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") checks to resist the Force Power's effect. A creature that would normally have a penalty on its [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") check due to size still retains that penalty. Additionally, the distance you move the target is multiplied by 2. If you push the target into a larger object, the target takes 1d6 points of damage per square moved by this Force Power. |

# Category : Full-Round Actions

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Full-Round_Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Full-Round_Actions) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:08:09Z.

Full-Round Actions consume all of your effort during a given round, effectively replacing all other [Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Actions "Actions") on your turn. Some uses of [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills") require a Full-Round Action to complete. Examples include bypassing a lock (using the [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") skill), searching an area for clues (using the [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") skill), and entering a [Force Trance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Trance "Force Trance") (using the [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") skill).

A Full-Round Action can't span multiple rounds; for example, you cannot perform a Full-Round Action that replaces your [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action") and [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") in the first round and your [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") in the following round.

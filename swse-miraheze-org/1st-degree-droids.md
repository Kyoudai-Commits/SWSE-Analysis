# 1st-Degree Droids

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/1st-Degree_Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/1st-Degree_Droids) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:04:21Z.

*Reference Books: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook"), [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

Medical and Analytical 1st-Degree [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") have the following traits:

- **Ability Modifiers:** All 1st-Degree Droids receive +2 bonuses to both their [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") and [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom"), but suffer a -2 penalty to their [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength").
- **Behavioral Inhibitors:** 1st-Degree Droids firstly cannot knowingly harm a sentient creature, unless another creature would be harmed due to inaction. 1st-Degree Droids secondly obey orders from their designated owner (whether that be an actual owner or themselves).
- **Droid Traits:** All 1st-Degree Droids posses [Droid Traits](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Traits "Droid Traits").
- **Droid Talents:** All 1st-Degree Droids can select Droid Talents from the [1st-Degree Droid Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/1st-Degree_Droid_Talent_Tree "1st-Degree Droid Talent Tree").

# Category : The New Jedi Order Era

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:The_New_Jedi_Order_Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:The_New_Jedi_Order_Era) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:03:40Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

**25 to 37 Years after *A New Hope***

In this era [The New Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_New_Republic "The New Republic") is in place, although a small Imperial Remnant maintains control of a portion of the galaxy. [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"), on the rise again thanks to the efforts of Luke Skywalker, are at a crossroads. Some members of the Order, which numbers about one hundred individuals, want to take a more direct and deliberate role in galactic affairs. Skywalker, meanwhile, is struggling with whether or not he wants to reestablish the Jedi Council. Rash Jedi have led New Republic officials to be wary of the Knights, and suspicious of their true motives. Indeed, this leads to varying degrees of mistrust and even fear in the general populace, many of whom still remember the Emperor's anti-Jedi rhetoric from years before.

The first galaxy-wide threat in The New Jedi Order Era is the [Yuuzhan Vong](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong "Yuuzhan Vong") Invasion. It begins with Nom Anor, a spy who sows additional seeds of discord throughout the galaxy. His efforts give rise to antitechnology cults dedicated to the destruction of innocent [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids"). Masking his true identity, he recruits agents from native populations to carry out acts of sabotage and subversion. Nom Anor, however, is only the beginning of the [Yuuzhan Vong](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong "Yuuzhan Vong") threat.

The [Yuuzhan Vong](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong "Yuuzhan Vong") are humanoids that follow a religion of pain and nature. They hate machines of all kinds, using instead living technology that they have bioengineered to serve as ships, weapons, and tools. These fierce, holy warriors seek to conquer the galaxy and bring their faith to the infidels who regularly make use of unclean machines. Their invasion begins on far-flung worlds such as [Belkadan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Belkadan?action=edit&redlink=1 "Belkadan (page does not exist)") and [Helska](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Helska?action=edit&redlink=1 "Helska (page does not exist)"), but they quickly widen their hold on key sectors of [The Outer Rim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Outer_Rim?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Outer Rim (page does not exist)") and [The Mid Rim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mid_Rim?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Mid Rim (page does not exist)"), terraforming worlds to produce the yorik coral and other living materials that serve as their ships and weapons. [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") learn, to their horror, that this intractable new enemy cannot be sensed through [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"), former guardians of peace, become the hunted prey of the [Yuuzhan Vong](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong "Yuuzhan Vong"). Meanwhile, the extragalactic intruders continue their relentless advance toward [The Core](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Core?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Core (page does not exist)"), crushing any force that dares stand in their way. A few worlds surrender without a fight, while those that resist are rendered uninhabitable. In time [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant") itself falls, signalling the death of [The New Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_New_Republic "The New Republic").

Eventually the [Yuuzhan Vong](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong "Yuuzhan Vong") discover their history may not be as they thought, when the living planet [Zonama Sekot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Zonama_Sekot "Zonama Sekot") came to [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant") and fought defensively against the [Yuuzhan Vong](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong "Yuuzhan Vong"), "welcoming home its true inhabitants." The new Galactic Federation of Free Alliances ([The Galactic Alliance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Galactic_Alliance "The Galactic Alliance")) allows the [Yuuzhan Vong](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong "Yuuzhan Vong") to surrender and take [Zonama Sekot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Zonama_Sekot "Zonama Sekot") as their homeworld. While not all [Yuuzhan Vong](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong "Yuuzhan Vong") accept this, the primary threat of the [Yuuzhan Vong](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong "Yuuzhan Vong") ends as [Zonama Sekot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Zonama_Sekot "Zonama Sekot") flies into hyperspace to seek a new home for the [Yuuzhan Vong](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong "Yuuzhan Vong") in [The Unknown Regions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Unknown_Regions "The Unknown Regions").

Though the war is over, many threats still exist. Some [Yuuzhan Vong](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong "Yuuzhan Vong") and their cults still fight against technological civilization. [The Galactic Alliance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Galactic_Alliance "The Galactic Alliance") has to rebuild the galaxy. And the New Jedi Order seeks out threats to peace and justice.

## The Swarm War

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

**35 to 36 Years after *A New Hope***

For many years, [The Killik Hives](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Killik_Hives "The Killik Hives") keep their expansionist impulses in check, spreading only to a handful of planets in [The Unknown Regions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Unknown_Regions "The Unknown Regions") and occasionally exterminating rival hives in warfare. This status quo changes during the Yuuzhan Vong Invasion, when a [Starship](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship "Starship") carrying three Force-users- one Jedi and two darksiders- crashes in Killik Space. The Jedi, [Raynar Thul](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Raynar_Thul?action=edit&redlink=1 "Raynar Thul (page does not exist)"), becomes a Joiner in the powerful Unu Hive, and his Force abilities lead him to become the public face of a new, reunified Killik Hive.

The two remaining crash survivors become Joiners in [The Gorog Hive](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Gorog_Hive?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Gorog Hive (page does not exist)"). Their evil dispositions poison the Gorog, triggering an unconscious aggressive streak in the Killik's hive mind and a resultant expansion into [Chiss](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Chiss "Chiss") territory. [The Gorog Hive](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Gorog_Hive?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Gorog Hive (page does not exist)") becomes known as the Dark Nest, and one darksider becomes the hive's Night Herald while the other takes up the mantle of the Unseen Queen.

Once they become Joiners, the Force-strong personalities of the trio influence the collective will of the Hives- the [Killik](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Killik "Killik") government, such as it is- resulting in a territorial push in the direction of [The Chiss Ascendancy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Chiss_Ascendancy "The Chiss Ascendancy"). In 35 ABY, the standoff between [The Killik Hives](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Killik_Hives "The Killik Hives") and [The Chiss Ascendancy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Chiss_Ascendancy "The Chiss Ascendancy") turns into open warfare. Efforts by [The Galactic Alliance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Galactic_Alliance "The Galactic Alliance") to forge a peace in the so-called Dark Nest Crisis break down when several Jedi who fall under the influence of [Killik](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Killik "Killik") pheromones announce their new allegiance as Joiners. One year later, the situation escalates into a three-way shooting match known as The Swarm War. Ultimately, the Dark Nest meets defeat, and [The Killik Hives](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Killik_Hives "The Killik Hives") agree to accept relocation restrictions. [The Chiss Ascendancy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Chiss_Ascendancy "The Chiss Ascendancy"), however, stands ready to respond if the [Killiks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Killiks "Killiks") step out of line again.

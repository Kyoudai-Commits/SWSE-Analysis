# The Ember of Vahl

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Ember_of_Vahl](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Ember_of_Vahl) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:04:45Z.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

The Ember of Vahl is a cult that worships Vahl, a goddess of fiery destruction. The membership of the cult is primarily made up of the [Vahla](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vahla "Vahla"), a nomadic [Near-Human](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Near-Human "Near-Human") Species that plies the galaxy in an endless search for their homeworld. The ancestors of the cult were decadent and twisted beings that lived only to serve their goddess. Because of their destructive tendencies and devotion to [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"), they were decimated by the Jedi Knights several thousand years ago. In the millennia since, their descendants have eked out an existence as interstellar vagabonds.

Small-scale piracy and petty theft is used to fuel the limited economy of the [Vahla](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vahla "Vahla"). They are also known to abduct individuals for use as sacrifices to their goddess. Though individual [Vahla](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vahla "Vahla") often set out on their own, all members of the Species are expected to heed the demands of a central coven that consists of six priests who have been "chosen" by Vahl. The priests consult with Vahl via *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")*, interpret omens, and make policy for their people. Being true disciples of their goddess, the six Chosen of Vahl scar themselves in a display of faith and devotion.

Vahl is an embodiment of the destructive nature of [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). It is thought that the original Vahl was a powerful Force Adept who led her people to take control of their lost homeworld prior to being destroyed by [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"). She is often depicted as a mature female [Vahla](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vahla "Vahla") with smoldering black eyes and flowing ash-white hair.

Because of their Species' defeat in ages long past, the [Vahla](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vahla "Vahla") remain embittered and hateful of [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order"). During [The Dark Times](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Times "The Dark Times"), a number of [Vahla](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vahla "Vahla") helped the Empire hunt down Jedi Knights. In [The Legacy Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Legacy_Era "The Legacy Era"), this tradition continues, with [Vahla](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vahla "Vahla") plying the galaxy as independent bounty hunters.

**Membership:** Not surprisingly, most members of The Ember of Vahl are of the [Vahla](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vahla "Vahla") Species. On the rare occasion that they accept non-[Vahla](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vahla "Vahla") into their ranks, the individual must possess the [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity") Feat and must demonstrate their faith through a number of dangerous and fiery trials. Despite the fact that all [Vahla](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vahla "Vahla") are born Force-sensitive, not all of them become members of The Ember of Vahl. Members of The Ember of Vahl gain access to the [Ember of Vahl Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ember_of_Vahl_Talent_Tree "Ember of Vahl Talent Tree").

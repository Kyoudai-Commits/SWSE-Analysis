# The Luka Sene

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Luka_Sene](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Luka_Sene) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:04:15Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

Connection to [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") is far more common among [Miraluka](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Miraluka "Miraluka") than other [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species"). [Miraluka](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Miraluka "Miraluka") also rely on [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") more than others because they depend on [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to sense their surroundings in lieu of their lost eyesight. Thus, [Miraluka](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Miraluka "Miraluka") do not regard being with [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") as seers or mystics, and mystically based [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions") have few supporters in [Miraluka](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Miraluka "Miraluka") society.

However, some [Miraluka](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Miraluka "Miraluka") do present an increased aptitude with the sense-based aspects of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). Once identified, [Miraluka](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Miraluka "Miraluka") educators and leaders encourage such individual to join The Luka Sene, a formal organization, with meditation and education chambers in most cities on their homeworld. The Luka Sene concentrates first and foremost on developing the powers of their membership, particularly sense-related powers. Luka Sene is academic in atmosphere and and culture. Mysticism is still present, but used by most members as a basis for more practical application of [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers"). Offworlders regard The Luka Sene as a university for Force-users, a place where classes and education take place, but not a tradition that dominates an attendee's personal life to the degree seen in Jedi, Sith, or other [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions").

Members of The Luka Sene participate in all aspects of society and all fields. Most have only small degrees of increased sensitivity, which they use to improve their own lives, careers, and [Miraluka](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Miraluka "Miraluka") society. Those more gifted in [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") might become Administrators, Masters, or Mentors in the Luka Sene organization; a few eventually leave to join [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order"). It is uncommon to encounter a Luka Sene member off their homeworld, but some travel to meet [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order"), or at the uncommon request of an offworld family seeking advice on a talented child.

The Luka Sene provides guidance to protect [Miraluka](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Miraluka "Miraluka") from falling prey to [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"). Miraluka' inherent sensing abilities are not normally regarded as a threat, but they can still provide a path to [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"), especially in the talented individuals targeted for Luka Sene training. Some [Miraluka](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Miraluka "Miraluka") seek to develop powers contrary to The Luka Sene's emphasis on [The Light Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Light_Side "The Light Side") and practical sides of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). When a [Miraluka](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Miraluka "Miraluka") is suspected of falling to [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"), a Luka Sene team called the Sene Seekers might search him or her out to alter the individual's path. If redemption is hopeless, these teams are accompanied by security personnel to help capture or eliminate the darksider.

Members of The Luka Sene can select [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") from the [Luka Sene Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Luka_Sene_Talent_Tree "Luka Sene Talent Tree").

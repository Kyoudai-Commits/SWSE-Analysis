# Category : Move Actions

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Move_Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Move_Actions) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:06:48Z.

A Move Action represents [Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Actions "Actions") pertaining to physical movement. The most common Move Action is moving up to your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed"). Standing up from a [Prone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prone "Prone") position, opening a door, and drawing a weapon are also Move Actions. You can perform one Move Action on your turn, or two if you give up your [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"). With the exception of specific movement related [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills"), most Move Actions don't require [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks"). In some cases (such as shouldering a stuck door), [Ability Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Checks "Ability Checks") might be required.

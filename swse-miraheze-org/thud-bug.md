# Thud Bug

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Thud_Bug](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Thud_Bug) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:07:30Z.

Legacy Era Campaign GuideWeb Enhancements

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

Like [Razor Bugs](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Razor_Bugs "Razor Bugs"), Thud Bugs are insects engineered by the [Yuuzhan Vong](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong "Yuuzhan Vong") to serve as ranged weapons. A thrown Thud Bug is treated as a [Simple Weapon (Ranged)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Simple_Weapon_(Ranged) "Simple Weapon (Ranged)"), not a [Thrown Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Thrown_Weapon "Thrown Weapon"), for purposes of determining [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range"). Additionally, the wielder can choose to deal [Stun](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stun "Stun") damage instead of normal damage against a target at Point-Blank or Short [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range").

*Reference Book: [Web Enhancements](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Web_Enhancements "Web Enhancements") ([Star Wars Miniatures Previews](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Star_Wars_Miniatures_Previews "Star Wars Miniatures Previews"))*

Thud Bugs (or *nang hul* in Yuuzhan Vong) are nasty bioengineered weapons that fly toward an enemy at high speed when thrown. If the attack deals damage and the attack roll equals or exceeds a living target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"), the target is knocked [Prone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prone "Prone"). After hitting a target, the Thud Bug falls dormant ([Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") 15, 1 [Hit Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Point "Hit Point"), [Damage Reduction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Reduction "Damage Reduction") 10) until reset. Resetting a dormant Thud Bug so that it can be used again requires a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") for a [Yuuzhan Vong](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong "Yuuzhan Vong") or a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action") and a DC 25 [Knowledge (Life Sciences)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Life_Sciences) "Knowledge (Life Sciences)") check for any other [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species").

If the ranged attack roll is less than the target's [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), the Thud Bug returns to the attacker at the end of the turn. The attacker must use a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") to catch and reuse the Thud Bug; otherwise, the Thud Bug falls dormant in the attacker's square. If the target successfully uses the **[Deflect](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deflect "Deflect")** talent against an attack with a Thud Bug, the Thud Bug is destroyed.

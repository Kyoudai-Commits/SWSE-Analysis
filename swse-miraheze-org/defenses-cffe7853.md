# Defenses

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:06:51Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

Your Defenses represent your ability to avoid taking [Damage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage "Damage") and overcome attacks to the body and mind. You have three Defense Scores:

- **Reflex Defense:** How hard you are to hit in combat.
- **Fortitude Defense:** Your ability to resist debilitating effect and resist being incapacitated.
- **Will Defense:** Your willpower and your ability to resist effects that attack your mind.

Your [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species"), [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents"), [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"), and Actions may grant additional bonuses to one or more of these Defenses. For example, [Gamorreans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gamorreans "Gamorreans") gain a +2 [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") bonus to their Fortitude Defense, while a character with the [Improved Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improved_Defenses "Improved Defenses") feat gains a +1 bonus to all three Defenses.

## Reflex Defense

Your Reflex Defense (abbreviated Reflex) represents how hard you are to hit in combat, and most attacks target a creature's Reflex Defense. If an opponent's attack roll equals or exceeds your Reflex Defense, the attack hits.

Character CombatVehicle Combat

#### Reflex Defense = 10 + Heroic Level or Armor Bonus + [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") Modifier + Size Modifier + [Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class "Class") Bonus

- **Heroic Level:** Your Heroic Level is the sum of all levels you have in [Heroic Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Classes "Heroic Classes") ([Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi"), [Noble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble "Noble"), [Scoundrel](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrel "Scoundrel"), [Scout](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scout "Scout"), and [Soldier](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier "Soldier")) and [Prestige Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Classes "Prestige Classes") (such as [Ace Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ace_Pilot "Ace Pilot") or [Sith Lord](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Lord "Sith Lord")).
  - It does not include levels in the [Nonheroic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nonheroic "Nonheroic") or [Beast](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Beast "Beast") classes.
- **Armor Bonus:** Your Armor Bonus is determined by the [Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor "Armor") you wear. If you are wearing [Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor "Armor"), add your Armor Bonus to your Reflex Defense instead of your Heroic Level.
  - This represents the difference between using your innate skill to avoid injury and counting on your [Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor "Armor") to absorb the damage from incoming attacks.
- **[Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") Modifier:** Nimble targets are more difficult to hit than slow ones. Add your [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") Modifier to your Reflex Defense. If you are Flat-Footed or unaware of an attack, you lose your [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus (but not a penalty) to your Reflex Defense.
  - If you are Helpless (for example, knocked [Unconscious](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unconscious "Unconscious")), calculate your Reflex Defense as if you had a [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") score of 0 (-5 modifier).
- **Size Modifier:** Smaller creatures are harder to hit than bigger ones. Apply the appropriate Size Modifier to your Reflex Defense (and only your Reflex Defense). Size Modifiers are as follows:

| Huge | -2 | Tiny | +2 |
| Large | -1 | Diminutive | +5 |

#### Reflex Defense = 10 + Armor Bonus or Pilot's Heroic Level + Vehicle's [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") Modifier + Size Modifier

- **Armor Bonus/Heroic Level:** You can use the Vehicle's Armor Bonus instead of your own when calculating the Vehicle's Reflex Defense, or add your Heroic Level instead of an Armor Bonus.
- **[Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") Modifier:** A Vehicle's [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") Modifier represents how well it can move out of harm's way. If the [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot") is Flat-Footed, or if the Vehicle is out of control, or attacked by an undetected opponent, the Vehicle loses its [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") Bonus to its Reflex Defense.
  - If the Vehicle is at a [Full Stop](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full_Stop "Full Stop"), [Disabled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disabled "Disabled"), or powered down, it is treated as if it has a [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") score of 0 (-5 penalty to Reflex Defense).

### Flat-Footed

In any battle that begins with a [Surprise Round](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surprise_Round "Surprise Round"), you start the battle Flat-Footed. You remain Flat-Footed until your first regular turn in the [Initiative Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative_Order "Initiative Order"). You can't apply your [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus (if any) to your Reflex Defense while Flat-Footed.

### Helpless

A Helpless opponent- one who is bound, sleeping, unconscious, or otherwise at your mercy- is an easy target. You can sometimes approach a target that is unaware of your presence, get adjacent to it, and treat it as Helpless. If the target is in combat or some other tense situation, and therefore in a state of acute awareness and readiness, or if the target can use its [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus to improve its Reflex Defense, then that target can't be considered unaware. Furthermore, any reasonable precautions taken by the target- including stationing bodyguards, placing its back to the wall, or being able to make [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") checks- also precludes catching that target unaware and Helpless.

A [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") against a Helpless opponent gains a +5 bonus on the attack roll (equivalent to attacking a [Prone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prone "Prone") target). A [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") gets no special bonus. In addition, a Helpless opponent can't add its [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus (if any) to its Reflex Defense. In fact, its [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") score is treated as if it were 0, so its [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") modifier to Reflex Defense is -5.

## Fortitude Defense

Your Fortitude Defense (abbreviated Fortitude) represents your ability to resist the effects of [Poison](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Poison "Poison"), [Disease](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disease "Disease"), and [Radiation](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Radiation "Radiation"), as well as your ability to ignore effects that would incapacitate a normal being.

Character CombatVehicle Combat

#### Fortitude Defense = 10 + Heroic Level + [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") Modifier + [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") Bonus + [Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class "Class") Bonus

- *Heroic Level:* Your Heroic Level is the sum of all levels you have in [Heroic Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Classes "Heroic Classes") ([Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi"), [Noble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble "Noble"), [Scoundrel](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrel "Scoundrel"), [Scout](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scout "Scout"), and [Soldier](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier "Soldier")) and [Prestige Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Classes "Prestige Classes") (such as [Ace Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ace_Pilot "Ace Pilot") or [Sith Lord](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Lord "Sith Lord")).
  - It does not include levels in the [Nonheroic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nonheroic "Nonheroic") or [Beast](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Beast "Beast") classes.
- *[Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") Modifier:* Tougher, healthier targets are more difficult to hurt than weaker ones, so you add your [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") modifier to your Fortitude Defense.
  - A nonliving target (that is, any target without a [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") score, such as a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid")) instead adds its [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") Modifier to its Fortitude Defense.
- *[Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") Bonus:* Some kinds of [Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor "Armor") provide an [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") Bonus to your Fortitude Defense.

#### Fortitude Defense = 10 + Vehicle's [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") Modifier

- **[Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") Bonus:** A Vehicle's [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") modifier represents its overall toughness and durability.

## Will Defense

Your Will Defense (abbreviated Will) represents your willpower and your ability to resist certain [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") and other effects that attack your mind.

Character Combat

#### Will Defense = 10 + Heroic Level + [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") Modifier + [Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class "Class") Bonus

- *Heroic Level:* Your Heroic Level is the sum of all levels you have in [Heroic Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Classes "Heroic Classes") ([Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi"), [Noble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble "Noble"), [Scoundrel](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrel "Scoundrel"), [Scout](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scout "Scout"), and [Soldier](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier "Soldier")) and [Prestige Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Classes "Prestige Classes") (such as [Ace Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ace_Pilot "Ace Pilot") or [Sith Lord](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Lord "Sith Lord")).
  - It does not include levels in the [Nonheroic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nonheroic "Nonheroic") or [Beast](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Beast "Beast") classes.
- *[Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") Modifier:* Strong-willed characters are harder to influence than weak-willed ones. You add your [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") modifier to your Will Defense.
  - When you are unconscious, you have an effective [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") score of 0 (-5 modifier).

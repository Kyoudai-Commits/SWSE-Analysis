# Old Republic Era Campaigns

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Old_Republic_Era_Campaigns](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Old_Republic_Era_Campaigns) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:03:33Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

The galaxy is torn by war. [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") is fighting for its survival, and everywhere the enemies of civilization- [The Krath](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Krath "The Krath"), [The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians"), [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith")- threaten to shatter what tenuous hold the Republic has on the galaxy. [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers") clash on nearly every planet, and few worlds remain untouched by the violence of the times. This is the galaxy into which heroes in a Old Republic Era Campaign must venture forth, and it is a galaxy that teems with adventure possibilities.

An Old Republic Era Campaign gives Gamemasters opportunities that they do not have in other [Eras of Play](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Eras_of_Play "Eras of Play"), allowing the freedom to create unique stories that let the heroes influence galactic events. An Old Republic Era Campaign gives the Gamemaster *carte blanche* to create epic campaigns that put the heroes (and their [Destinies](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destinies "Destinies")) in the forefront of the setting, with only a few sources of existing continuity to deal with.

Old Republic Era Campaigns take place against a backdrop that captures everything that makes *Star Wars* unique. Even more so than in [The Clone Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Clone_Wars "The Clone Wars"), these are the days of [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") in their prime. They do battle with the evil Sith, and defend the galaxy against the Mandalorian hordes. Heroes rise to prominence and then fall into darkness, and villains who seem beyond saving find redemption and reject [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"). Soldiers leave the planets of their birth to head off to war on distant battlefields, only to be drawn to their homeworlds as the war comes quickly to a new front. Criminals thrive on the chaos of war, and nobles and corporations struggle to wrest the reins of power away from their adversaries, and perhaps even from the Republic. Yet, [The Old Republic Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic_Era "The Old Republic Era") has a feel all its own, and this section should help Gamemasters develop a sense for the qualities that make a campaign in this era unique, explaining how to capture that feeling and use it to create epic adventures.

## Abundance of Force-Users

A key element of an Old Republic Era Campaign is the many Force-using characters active throughout the galaxy at this time. Unlike campaigns set in the classic era, Jedi, Sith, and other Force-sensitive groups are spread throughout the galaxy. Moreover, [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order") has not been vigilant in scooping up up members of other Force-using groups. As a result, many worlds maintain alternate [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions") while still sending members to become Jedi. Though such [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions") are eventually absorbed into [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order") in later years, for now the Order's influence is less focused, allowing alternate [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions") to flourish even on worlds where a Jedi Watchman has been assigned to stand guard over the planet. Though Jedi Watchmen try to recruit members of alternate [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions") as Padawan learners, they do not have the manpower to force anyone to join the Order.

As a result, Force-using enemies are common. Almost any adventure might feature a Force-sensitive opponent or ally, whether that character comes from the Jedi or Sith organizations or from a smaller tradition. Additionally, Force-wielding heroes are not looked at askance by the general populace during this time; most worlds under Republic or Sith control are well aware of the capabilities of [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") and their darker counterparts, and encountering one is rarely cause for alarm. Meeting a Jedi might cause a stir in out-of-the-way communities, but most major cities have seen Jedi and Sith representatives in recent times and are unlikely to respond with fear or prejudice. Some corporations, governments, and criminal organizations are even known to employ Force-users to gain a small edge, and noble houses hide Force-sensitive sons and daughters from [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"), keeping them concealed to gain an advantage over other houses.

### [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order")

Between [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War") and [The Dark Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Wars "The Dark Wars"), The Jedi go from the height of their power to a mere remnant of their existence. Unlike the Jedi of later years, the Jedi in Old Republic Era Campaigns are likely to be fractious and unwilling to bow to their own central authority- the Jedi Council. Before [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War"), most Jedi are lone masters with a handful of pupils, or teachers at various academies scattered throughout the galaxy. As war spreads across the galaxy again and again, Jedi see the need for a strong central authority, and the Jedi Council gains more power. However, some Jedi are unwilling to accept what they see as the Jedi Council's yoke of command, and they defy the Council on a regular basis. Though this leads to in-fighting among The Jedi, it does have one positive side-effect: the bond between Jedi Masters and their apprentices becomes stronger. Padawans are more likely to follow in their Masters' footsteps in an Old Republic Era Campaign, and might side with their Masters against the Jedi Council if a dispute develops.

Moreover, the Jedi Enclaves and Academies spread throughout the galaxy, The Jedi Order is extremely decentralized. Academies can be found on [Arkania](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Arkania "Arkania"), [Dantooine](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dantooine "Dantooine"), [Dorin](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dorin "Dorin"), [Taris](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Taris "Taris"), [Telos](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Telos "Telos"), and [Teya IV](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Teya_IV?action=edit&redlink=1 "Teya IV (page does not exist)") to name only a few. Additionally, hundreds of Jedi are trained by lone Masters away from any academy or enclave. As a result, a Jedi can be accepted into the Order, raised and trained, and become a Jedi Knight without ever setting foot on [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant").

For Gamemasters, this mean that using The Jedi as antagonists or the motivators of plots is easier than in other eras. Conflicts between Jedi, although rarely violent, are not unheard of, and two factions of Jedi with opposing views might throw whole systems or even entire sectors of the galaxy into turmoil with their disagreements. For example, a Gamemaster wishing to use Jedi as antagonists for an adventure could create a scenario in which two Jedi Masters are at odds over how to deal with a trade dispute between planets. As The Jedi step in on behalf of each side of the dispute, both sides are emboldened and begin to act rashly. As Jedi flock to either cause, civil war breaks out in the system, with Jedi on either side trying to force the opposition to back down. Without a strong Jedi Council to step in and stop conflicts from escalating, the heroes might become caught between two Jedi ideologies with no hope of outside assistance, requiring them to step in and solve the dispute in whatever way they can.

### [The Krath](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Krath "The Krath")

Neither [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") nor [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") are the ones who initially tip the scales of destiny and plunge the galaxy into a century of war. The Krath, a small cult of Dark Side Force-users from [The Deep Core](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Deep_Core?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Deep Core (page does not exist)"), have the ignominious distinction of igniting [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War"). Though they are the first group of Dark Side cultists to rise in prominence in the galaxy, they are far from the last. The Krath, unlike [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith"), are largely unknown to [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") before the war, and as a result they are mysterious enemies that have the advantage of surprise over [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") and [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic").

The Krath are Dark Side cultists who base their powers on ancient Sith teachings. Founded by nobles from [The Deep Core](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Deep_Core?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Deep Core (page does not exist)"), The Krath quickly ascend to power when they seize control of the Empress Teta System. With one prominent and powerful star system under their control, The Krath expand outward, and soon they plunge the entire Core into open war, with attacks against both [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") and [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"). The Krath attract the attention of the newly revived Sith, and with a coalition between the Krath military, [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith"), and [The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians") (brought to The Krath by fallen Jedi [Ulic Qel-Droma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ulic_Qel-Droma "Ulic Qel-Droma")), the forces of darkness ravage [The Deep Core](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Deep_Core?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Deep Core (page does not exist)") and spread the war all the way to [The Outer Rim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Outer_Rim?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Outer Rim (page does not exist)"). Unfortunately for the galaxy, neither The Krath nor their Mandalorian allies have much interest in maintaining infrastructures or civil order, and as a result much of the galaxy succumbs to a tide of darkness.

The Krath present a unique opportunity for Gamemasters in that they are a source for nontraditional Dark Side Force-users. [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") have a great history with [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"), but The Krath are relative newcomers to the galactic scene and can fulfill nearly any role the Gamemaster needs. Krath enemies can be mysterious foes whose powers are unknown and whose techniques and politics are completely alien to the heroes. Likewise, they can be tempters who promote power, glory, and fame for the heroes, hoping to woo them to their side in [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War"). They might even be unlikely allies; not all The Krath are happy with their alliance with [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") or [The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians"), and corrupt Krath leaders might seize an opportunity to eliminate one of those factions if possible, agreeing to aid the heroes if needed.

### Return of [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith")

The Sith are thought to be extinct, but the Republic experiences a rude awakening when The Sith return to galactic prominence. These ancient foes of [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") can be the primary antagonists of many Old Republic Era Campaigns: they return from a thousand years of secrecy to rise to power in [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War"); they bide their time during [The Restoration Period](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Restoration_Period "The Restoration Period"); they watch from the shadows during [The Mandalorian Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorian_Wars "The Mandalorian Wars"); they clash directly with [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") during [The Jedi Civil War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Civil_War "The Jedi Civil War"); and they return to their vicious infighting during [The Dark Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Wars "The Dark Wars"). The Sith are omnipresent, though sometimes hidden, and they fit well into nearly any campaign. They are the classic foes of [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"), performing much the same function in this era as the Empire does during the Galactic Civil War.

**Chapter 10: The Sith** goes into more detail about The Sith, including their history and motivations, and provides numerous suggestions for how to integrate them into a campaign. Gamemasters should not hesitate to use The Sith in any Old Republic Era Campaign, as their presence is both iconic and exciting. The Sith provide excellent enemies for all kinds of characters- not just Jedi. Smugglers and criminals hate The Sith for their ruthless dealings with the fringe, and those loyal to [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") have little desire to see [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") gain any more power or seize any more Republic territory.

## Civilization Under Siege

Several successive wars throw [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") into turmoil, allowing the criminal elements of the galaxy to rise to prominence. Organized crime, in a variety of forms, seizes power, and lawlessness and violence become the rule on worlds where Republic presence is light.

### [The Exchange](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Exchange "The Exchange")

The Exchange is one of the largest criminal networks in the galaxy. Though their operations thrive on [The Outer Rim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Outer_Rim?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Outer Rim (page does not exist)")- outside Republic control and law enforcement- elements of their schemes and organization can be found throughout the galaxy. The organization is said to be run by an entity or individual known as [The Compeer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Compeer?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Compeer (page does not exist)"), but the person's location and identity remain mysterious, even to those high up in Exchange leadership.

On each planet where The Exchange has significant influence, a powerful crime lord oversees all Exchange operations. Each of these crime lords comes to power through the approval of and election by the other Exchange leaders. Though nominally allies, they compete for power, influence, and control. As masters of their respective planets, they were responsible for their operations' successes and failures. Notable leaders include [Davik Kang](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Davik_Kang?action=edit&redlink=1 "Davik Kang (page does not exist)") of [Taris](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Taris "Taris")- who is killed when [Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Revan "Revan") and others steal his ship, [The *Ebon Hawk*](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Ebon_Hawk "The Ebon Hawk") - and [Goto](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Goto "Goto"), alias of the Droid [G0-T0](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/G0-T0 "G0-T0"), who controls a major smuggling operation at [Nar Shaddaa](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nar_Shaddaa "Nar Shaddaa") years later.

The Exchange can be found on nearly all developed planets. Anyone with the right reputation, or determination, can eventually make contact with the organization. In fact, those who display particular aptitude in an area The Exchange wishes to exploit might attract overtures, threats, or both, depending on the response. Smugglers are always needed, but bounty hunters, mercenaries, slave traders, and infiltrators are also recruited.

The Exchange has a peculiar hierarchy, which few have managed to uncover. The Exchange maintains organized cells on worlds of interest, and each of those planetary cells is led by a single boss who rules over Exchange interests on that world. The bosses report to sector chiefs who have no cells of their own (though they might have a smaller support staff) and operate under secret identities, leading normal lives. Even the bosses do not know who the chiefs are in real life, as most communications are done over secret, secure communications channels. The chiefs report to the head of The Exchange, who is themselves a sector chief, so if a single sector chief's cover is compromised, the organization merely appears to be ruled by a council of sector chiefs.

### [The GenoHaradan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_GenoHaradan "The GenoHaradan")

The GenoHaradan are a secret bounty hunter's guild, controlled by several overseers out to influence galactic governments and other organizations through selective assassinations. Unlike other guilds, The GenoHaradan perform assassinations to influence galactic politics and manipulate the outcome of important events. The GenoHaradan overseers pull the strings of thousands of politicians and corporate executives, and though they might not have the overt power of [The Exchange](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Exchange "The Exchange"), they can achieve their goals when the need arises- and achieve them secretly.

The guild is so secretive that virtually no outsiders know of its existence, making the guild's impact on the galaxy difficult to assess- especially in times of war. [Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Revan "Revan") is rumored to have decimated the organization during their search for [The Star Forge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Star_Forge "The Star Forge"), but like so much of GenoHaradan history, this cannot be confirmed. The guild recruits primarily from the ranks of bounty hunters. However, the few members exposed to the outside world have come from a variety of criminal backgrounds.

The GenoHaradan are secretive, so prospective members must be approached by the organization to join; The GenoHaradan cannot be sought out. The organization has significant political interests, so brutes, loose cannons, and criminals who could endanger their goals are never accepted into The GenoHaradan. For Gamemasters, The GenoHaradan presents an opportunity to introduce an organization that manipulates galactic politics with secretive and brutal methods. Heroes might join The GenoHaradan to influence galactic politics, hoping to make a bigger mark on the galaxy than they would as lowly bounty hunters. Moreover, The GenoHaradan's goals are mysterious, and though their members might be brutal assassins, their work can be good for the galaxy.

A Gamemaster wishing to use The GenoHaradan in a campaign might give the organization goals similar to those shared by the heroes, forcing the players to decide whether they want to align themselves with the ruthless guild or oppose them, even if it makes the tasks ahead that much more difficult. As antagonists, The GenoHaradan can range from simple assassins and bounty hunters to villains of an entire campaign, requiring the heroes to oppose a secretive organization with malicious designs on the galaxy. Massive, climactic space battles or planet-spanning ground skirmishes rarely result from dealing with GenoHaradan overseers. They are more likely to be fought and defeated in up-close and personal ways.

### [Swoop Gangs](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swoop_Gangs "Swoop Gangs")

Swoop Gangs are the scourge of Outer Rim worlds and seedier sections of mega cities throughout the galaxy. In regions with wide open spaces, the gangs move about on their [Swoop Racers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swoop_Racers "Swoop Racers"), causing trouble wherever they go. In the megacities, they fly throughout the lower levels, competing in underground racing. At a minimum, the races are gambling opportunities, but far more often they are about honor or settling arguments- at least the nonviolent kind. The gangs participate in most forms of petty criminal activity and are particularly opportunistic, especially when it comes to kidnapping and theft.

In the low levels of the Undercity on the planet [Taris](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Taris "Taris"), two well-established and particularly competitive gangs are [The Black Vulkars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Black_Vulkars "The Black Vulkars") and [The Hidden Beks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Hidden_Beks "The Hidden Beks"). Both of these gangs have established their own base of operation, including their own garages where they store and tend to their customized [Daggerstar Swoop Racers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Daggerstar_Swoop_Racers "Daggerstar Swoop Racers"). Swoop Gang members are known to defect from one gang to another, especially if the leadership is more to their liking. The two gangs are never friendly, and open warfare often breaks over the smallest of disagreements. Of the two, [The Black Vulkars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Black_Vulkars "The Black Vulkars") are regarded as the more brutal, participating in slavery. [The Hidden Beks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Hidden_Beks "The Hidden Beks"), on the other hand, fight for the [Taris](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Taris "Taris") resistance during the Mandalorian occupation.

## Constant Warfare

From one end of [The Old Republic Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic_Era "The Old Republic Era") to the other, the galaxy is constantly torn by wars, and times of peace seem more rare than times of conflict. Most of the heroes in an Old Republic Era Campaign (except those set in [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War")) will have experienced nearly a lifetime of war, and have likely felt the rippling effect of massive battles and horrific devastation. As such, the face of the galaxy is much different than in the classic era; the advent of [The Clone Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Clone_Wars "The Clone Wars") was a major event in the galaxy, while the Republic has already weathered two major wars by the time [The Jedi Civil War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Civil_War "The Jedi Civil War") begins.

More than just affecting the heroes, the constant warfare plaguing the galaxy has changed the faces of planets and their populations irrevocably. Most planets have militias and defense forces that are significantly more powerful than they could have in peacetime. Conversely, some worlds have been so devastated by war that their populations are barely hanging on to their lives, and on many worlds the toll of war has simply exhausted their resources and lead to widespread famine, plague, and a collapse of infrastructure. Even worlds that have not seen the use of superweapons may look as though they have been devastated by one, with cities crumbling under a lack of maintenance and empty buildings looking like mere ghosts of a civilization with no populace.

Even liberation often brings devastation. When [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") liberate a planet from the clutches of [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") or [The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians"), the locals still suffer the side effects of that battle. Many native populations are as wary of their liberators as their oppressors, and sometimes they lash out at anyone they can blame for the pain they have endured.

### [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War")

The Great Sith War is the first major conflict of the era and one that will set the tone for the century of conflict to come. Unlike the later wars, the Great Sith War begins with a small group of tyrants in [The Deep Core](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Deep_Core?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Deep Core (page does not exist)") and then slowly expands to encompass the entire galaxy as disparate factions unite to battle against [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic"). During The Great Sith War, the galaxy is shocked out of complacency and suffers widespread devastation, reaching the point where the Republic itself is in danger of collapse. Were it not for [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") and their defeat of the Sith leader [Ulic Qel-Droma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ulic_Qel-Droma "Ulic Qel-Droma"), the galaxy might have been plunged into an inescapable chaos.

The dark forces of The Great Sith War are called from several different groups. As a result, adventures set during this war will be guaranteed to feature a wide variety of opponents. During this time, the enemy forces consist mainly of Krath warriors and their leaders, some Sith forces directly under the command of the new Sith Lords, and Mandalorian Crusaders (but not the Neo-Crusaders). Major antagonists will include Krath Death Witches, minor and major Sith Lords, Mandalorian commanders, and opportunistic mercenaries. More than in any other war, the opposition in a campaign set during The Great Sith War will be an amalgamation of several different factions.

During The Great Sith War, heroes might be agents of [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") or [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"), sent to deal with plots and mysteries related to [The Krath](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Krath "The Krath"), [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith"), or [The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians"). Adventures in The Great Sith War may deal with the ever-growing tide of barbarism and evil flowing from [The Deep Core](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Deep_Core?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Deep Core (page does not exist)"), and many times the heroes will find themselves outnumbered and surrounded by barbaric hordes of Krath warriors or the vicious forces of [The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians"). Plots and villains of the Great Sith War often tend to deal with arcane rituals of the ancient Sith Lords, and the tone of such a campaign will likely lean more toward space fantasy than traditional space opera.

### [The Mandalorian Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorian_Wars "The Mandalorian Wars")

During The Mandalorian Wars, the heroes face off against hordes of armored warriors from a variety of [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species")- or join them. During The Mandalorian Wars, [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") faces off against a powerful, ever growing enemy force without the assistance of [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"), leaving the skirmish to be an almost entirely military affair. Unlike the wars that precede and follow it, the struggles between [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") and other Force-using enemies are of minimal importance, and the battles of The Mandalorian Wars take place without the intervention of a mystical energy field or its manipulators.

[The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians") are the primary antagonists of The Mandalorian Wars. During this time, a few Crusaders who fought in The Great Sith War may still be engaged in the fight, but many of the Mandalorian forces are made up of Neo-Crusaders and their officers. A key factor in the Mandalorians' successes during this time is their ability to recruit new warriors from the populations of the planets they conquer; as a result, Gamemasters using Mandalorians in their campaigns can feel free to alter the statistics of the Neo-Crusaders, replacing the [Human](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Human "Human") Species Traits with those of other [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species"). Unlike [Sith Troopers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Troopers "Sith Troopers"), Mandalorians need not all be visually identical, and coming into a fight that features a hulking [Feeorin](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feeorin "Feeorin") Neo-Crusader alongside [Humans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Humans "Humans") and [Rodians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rodians "Rodians") in Mandalorian armor adds an interesting element to the fight.

The Mandalorian Wars represent a struggle between barbarism and civilization; though [The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians") are more organized under the Neo-Crusaders philosophies advocated by [Cassus Fett](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cassus_Fett "Cassus Fett"), they are still capable of unspeakable acts of violence. Adventures set during The Mandalorian Wars may feature massive battles where technology and skill of soldiers determines the outcome of the fight, not a duel between Jedi, and the time period lends itself well to gritty, brutal adventures where [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") teeters on the brink of being shattered under the strength of the Mandalorian assault.

### [The Jedi Civil War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Civil_War "The Jedi Civil War")

The Jedi Civil War is all about betrayal. Republic heroes who fought in The Mandalorian Wars now stand at the side of Sith Lords. Jedi who were once loyal servants of the Light now lie for the chance to be the next great Dark Lord of [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith"). During The Jedi Civil War, former allies find themselves on opposite sides of the conflict, with little choice but to fight their ex-comrades or be killed. During The Jedi Civil War, heroes are also presented with similar choices, and may need to choose sides or face off against old friends.

During The Jedi Civil War, the reborn Sith Empire is truly at the height of its power and influence. Unlike during The Great Sith War, [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") of The Jedi Civil War does not share the spotlight with any other factions. This Sith Empire employs armies full of [Sith Troopers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Troopers "Sith Troopers"), led by [Sith Officers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Officers "Sith Officers"), and ruled over by the Dark Side-wielding Sith Lords. This is [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") at its purest, at a point during which it most closely resembles Palpatine's Galactic Empire that will rise several thousand years in the future.

The Jedi Civil War provides many options for exciting adventures. Since the heroes never know whether their allies may secretly be planning on defecting to [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire"), it becomes difficult to trust anyone, and makes the eventual betrayal of allies all the more bitter. [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") are constantly trying to find ways to draw more worlds into their Empire, and [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") and [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") must foil their plots and clash with [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") head-on to prevent the galaxy from being swallowed by darkness. Adventures set in this war will likely take place against iconic *Star Wars* backdrops, featuring intense [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") duels on exotic worlds, space battles that determine the fate of entire star systems, and heroes outnumbered by legions of enemy troops.

### [The Dark Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Wars "The Dark Wars")

In many ways, The Dark Wars resemble the time of [The New Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_New_Republic "The New Republic") immediately following the Galactic Civil War. During The Dark Wars, the once-monolithic Sith Empire is reduced to many smaller territories, each ruled over by a warlord with ambitions of being the next great Sith Lord. Occasionally a leader rises that can unite those warlords (for example, when [Darth Sion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Sion "Darth Sion") and [Darth Nihilus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Nihilus "Darth Nihilus") seize control of the Sith remnants), but eventually even those temporary alliances crumble. [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") are still a threat, and [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") have been nearly eliminated, leaving [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic"), [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"), and [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") wearied and battered from the prior decades of conflict.

During The Dark Wars, Gamemasters have as many or more options for antagonists in their campaigns. [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") are still around and strong, if fractured, and serve as perfectly serviceable foes. However, [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") aren't the only interesting antagonists. For example, a rebellious Mandalorian leader might try to stage a coup on a Sith world, seizing that territory for themselves and plunging an entire sector into war. Mercenaries who fought in The Jedi Civil War might go on a rampage across the galaxy, or [The Exchange](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Exchange "The Exchange") could hatch a plot that could destabilize [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic").

A campaign set during The Dark Wars has the advantage of putting a lot of resources at the heroes' disposal. [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") is the larger and more stable force, and the various Sith warlords are too small and unwilling to cooperate to pose a significant threat. Though the Republic is cautious and does not want [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") to rise again, there is enough stability in the galaxy that commerce and politics can resume. The Republic military will still engage [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") from time to time, but the real threat is a Sith leader that can unite the warlords and lead a renewed assault against the Republic.

## Mysterious Enemies

Though the threat from enemies is ever-present, in an Old Republic Era Campaign those threats may be difficult to see at first. One's enemies may be hidden, remaining entirely unseen before striking out from the shadows, or they may be right in front of you, presenting a deceptive facade all the while planning to attack. An Old Republic Era Campaign is rife with antagonists and opponents whose motivations, history, plans, and appearances may be all but unknown to the heroes, and a major motivation in the campaign may simply be to find out the truth behind the mystery of an enemy. Threats can come from many angles in [The Old Republic Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic_Era "The Old Republic Era"), and the real danger is not seeing those threats until it is too late to resist them.

It is not just one's enemies that may be shrouded in mystery, but also a wide variety of events and plots as well. [Zayne Carrick](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Zayne_Carrick "Zayne Carrick") attempts to unravel the mystery behind the murder of his fellow Padawans against the backdrop of [The Mandalorian Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorian_Wars "The Mandalorian Wars"), while an amnesiac [Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Revan "Revan") retraces their steps to unlock the secrets of ancient Rakatan artifacts. There are things in the galaxy that no one knows about or understands, and the unearthing of ancient secrets can often lead to mysteries that require an entire campaign's worth of adventures to unravel. Campaigns themed around mysteries of this sort often involve lengthy investigations, surprise plot twists, false trails and red herrings, and a strong sense that in the unknown waits real and palpable danger.

The Gamemasters looking to add a sense of the mysterious and unknown danger to their campaigns, one of the best methods of doing so is to shroud one's villains in secrecy. A classic trope of the *Star Wars* saga, compelling and mysterious villains can spur on heroes who seek to find out more about who they are fighting and what malicious plots they have in store for the galaxy. Sith Lords like [Darth Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Revan "Darth Revan") and [Darth Nihilus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Nihilus "Darth Nihilus") are excellent examples of enemies whose secrets make them compelling characters. Players will want to know more about a mysterious villain naturally, but it is also the responsibility of the Gamemaster to provide them with ways of doing so. The path that [Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Revan "Revan") takes in rediscovering their own mysterious past is a prime example of an exciting adventure involving dangerous mysteries; the heroes of an Old Republic Era Campaign should undertake adventures that uncover secrets and delve into the unknown where danger and death could lurk behind every blast door.

### Betrayal

One key theme in an Old Republic Era Campaign is the concept of betrayal. Betrayal plays a major role in many of the stories set during this time period. [Ulic Qel-Droma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ulic_Qel-Droma "Ulic Qel-Droma") betrays [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order") and becomes a Dark Lord of [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith"); [The Jedi Covenant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Covenant "The Jedi Covenant") betrays their Padawans on [Taris](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Taris "Taris"); a large segment of [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") betrays its government to join [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") during [The Jedi Civil War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Civil_War "The Jedi Civil War"). Throughout [The Old Republic Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic_Era "The Old Republic Era") there is a constant danger that your friends and allies may only be waiting for the right time to betray you, or even may do so without any forethought. Those who are your allies today could be your enemies tomorrow, and it makes an uneasy life for those heroes who need to rely on the help of others to achieve their goals.

No one is immune to betrayal in an Old Republic Era Campaign. Jedi Masters, long servants of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), succumb to the draw of [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") and turn their backs on their comrades. Respected veteran military leaders may flee across battle lines to join the opposing forces, either out of a sense of preservation or even the belief that they have been fighting on the wrong side all along. Sith Lords squabble amongst themselves, waiting for the right moment to drive their [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") into the back of an ally who lets his guard down. For the heroes, this means that a mentor of today may become the enemy of tomorrow, or they may find themselves targeted by allies that would once shelter them. War does strange things to people, even peaceful people, and betrayal lurks in the heart of any being that feels fear, greed, paranoia, confusion, doubt, or loss.

Betrayal is not the sole province of nonplayer characters, either. Though many Gamemasters may wish to avoid conflict between heroes, the dynamics of this time period make it easier than ever for a hero to turn on their allies when the time is right. Sometimes this means that the hero falls to [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"), running off to join [The Krath](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Krath "The Krath") or [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") in their wars against [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic"). Other times this may mean drawing their comrades into a trap, luring them into the clutches of [The Exchange](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Exchange "The Exchange") to claim a handsome reward. Gamemasters should be careful when allowing this kinds of conflict to arise between heroes; though it can lead to compelling storytelling, it can also sometimes lead to friction or resentment between players. If a Gamemaster feels that the story could benefit a hero's betrayal, approach the player of that hero separately to discuss the motivations, timing, and consequences of that betrayal. Such a betrayal may be tied to that hero's [Destiny](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny "Destiny"), or it could simply be a natural occurrence in the storyline. In most cases, a hero that betrays their party should become an NPC, allowing the player of that character to create a new hero to join the party.

### Hidden Dangers

Along with the dangers of betrayal and enemies that the heroes must face, the galaxy itself is a treacherous place rife with secret weapons and artifacts. Like the era surrounding the Galactic Civil War, this galaxy is littered with superweapons and items of great power during this time period, each one of which awaits discovery. In fact, many adventures may revolve around the heroes in pursuit of a powerful weapon, racing against their enemies to claim it with failure resulting in galactic devastation. Below are some examples of dangerous mysteries that can be used in any Old Republic Era Campaign.

**The Dark Reaper:** Created by the ancient Sith Lords during [The Great Hyperspace War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Hyperspace_War?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Great Hyperspace War (page does not exist)"), The Dark Reaper is a Force-draining weapon rediscovered by [The Krath](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Krath "The Krath") and used in [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War"). At its heart is a device known as The Force Harvester, which drains the life essence from all living things around it. When activated, The Dark Reaper drains the life forces in the surrounding area, channeling that energy to boost the power of its [Turbolasers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Turbolasers "Turbolasers"). More than just a weapon of mass destruction, The Dark Reaper can defend itself using the very Force energy it steals from nearby living beings.

**The Mass Shadow Generator:** Utilized to deal a crushing defeat to [The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians") at [Malachor V](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Malachor_V "Malachor V"), The Mass Shadow Generator is a superweapon designed to draw upon the natural gravity shadows generated by a planet to unleash massive devastation to everything within the vicinity of the planet on which it is used. The Mass Shadow Generator is used on [Malachor V](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Malachor_V "Malachor V") due to the unique gravity anomalies in that system, though it is speculated that the device could function, with adjustments, in any star system. The Mass Shadow Generator not only devastates ships and [Space Stations](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Space_Stations "Space Stations") in orbit of the planet, it also destroys nearly everything on the surface of the planet itself, and two activations are enough to obliterate a planet entirely.

**Rakatan Mysteries:** The ancient [Rakata](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rakata "Rakata") left behind numerous technological mysteries that can serve as the focus of adventures or campaigns. The [Rakata](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rakata "Rakata") were masters of incredible technology, but when their Infinite Empire crumbled they left behind a legacy of artifacts of their advanced civilization that are just waiting to be found. Some of these ancient mysteries may involve powerful weapons, while others may be more benevolent, like advanced medical technology or methods of power generation unparalleled by existing technology. Secrets of [The Infinite Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Infinite_Empire "The Infinite Empire") can come in almost any shape, form, or function, giving a Gamemaster using Rakatan Mysteries a blank slate on which to design adventure hooks.

**The Star Forge:** The most influential piece of [Rakatan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rakatan "Rakatan") technology known to exist in [The Old Republic Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic_Era "The Old Republic Era"), The Star Forge is a massive [Space Station](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Space_Station "Space Station") that is capable of mass-producing [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles"), [Starships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships "Starships"), and weapons. The Star Forge draws energy from the Lehon System's sun to scan, analyze, and then infinitely reproduce any piece of technology imaginable. The rediscovery of The Star Forge is what allows [Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Revan "Revan") to transform their segment of the Republic Fleet into a massive invasion force, and The Star Forge continues to crank out new weapons of war as quickly as [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") can overrun new worlds.

## New Powers on the Rise

The repeated devastation of the galaxy leads to massive rebuilding efforts on behalf of [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic"). During this time, many corporations suddenly find themselves with vast amounts of political clout thanks to the Coruscant Financial Exchange Establishment Act. Similarly, as previously widespread organizations collapse as a result of assets lost during wartime, others step in to take their places, leading to a wealth of new powers in the galaxy coming onto the scene suddenly.

### [The Hyperspace Navigator's Guild](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Hyperspace_Navigator%27s_Guild "The Hyperspace Navigator's Guild")

Though far from a politically influential organization, The Hyperspace Navigator's Guild impacts the lives of sentient beings across the galaxy. During [The Old Republic Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic_Era "The Old Republic Era"), most ships throughout the galaxy begin to carry their own [Navicomputers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Navicomputers "Navicomputers"), and the [Hyperspace Beacons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hyperspace_Beacons "Hyperspace Beacons") maintained by the Navigator's Guild rapidly diminish in importance. This does not stop the guild from continuing to function as a primary repository for navigational data, especially in [The Unknown Regions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Unknown_Regions "The Unknown Regions") and [The Deep Core](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Deep_Core?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Deep Core (page does not exist)").

The Hyperspace Navigator's Guild is responsible for the discovery and establishment of new hyperspace routes throughout the galaxy. Scouts across the galaxy are constantly attempting to discover new hyperspace routes, and the Navigator's Guild pays handsomely for information regarding the creation of new hyperlanes. Once a scout reports the existence of a new route, the guild begins the lengthy process of verifying the safety and integrity of the route, investigating large amounts of resources and time to make sure that the route is safe and will remain in existence for the long-term.

Once a lane has been verified, The Hyperspace Navigator's Guild passes the data on the route on to the Space Ministry, from which point the coordinates are distributed throughout the galaxy. While a route is still under investigation, the guild is responsible for maintaining the [Hyperspace Beacons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hyperspace_Beacons "Hyperspace Beacons") marking the route and organizing scouts using the routes. During wartime, the Republic goes to great pains to ensure that knowledge of secret hyperspace corridors remains concealed, and the guildhouse on [Empress Teta](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Empress_Teta?action=edit&redlink=1 "Empress Teta (page does not exist)") becomes nearly as important as the Senate on [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant").

### The Ascent of Corporations

Major corporations form the backbone of the Republic economy and many political entities in the galaxy. The scope of a corporation ranges from very small, such as one based in a specific city, to incomprehensibly vast interstellar companies, such as [The Czerka Corporation](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Czerka_Corporation "The Czerka Corporation"). A successful corporation might operate for years, even centuries. Some remain family- or clan- owned operations, but others shift ownership and allegiances as they are bought, sold, or taken over by any number of methods.

Interstellar corporations are by far the most influential and powerful companies in the galaxy. Corporations control vast tracts of space, such as the Corporate Sector in the classic era. Those who live under corporate rule find that Republic law means little, especially in outlying areas. Most corporations look out for their bottom-line and the interests of their owners or leaders rather than looking after the welfare of those who live on company planets.

Officially known as the Adasca BioMechanical Corporation of Arkania, [Adascorp](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Adascorp "Adascorp") is one of the largest [Arkanian](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Arkanian "Arkanian") corporate dynasties. It is also the galaxy's largest bioengineering firm. It is owned by the House of Adasca and operated by current lord of the house, Lord [Arkoh Adasca](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Arkoh_Adasca "Arkoh Adasca"). Although the Lord of Adasca is not the ruler of [Arkania](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Arkania "Arkania"), outsiders are forgiven for assuming that is the case, given the extent of Adascorp's influence over the population.

Adascorp is famous for bioengineering and medical research advances. It is responsible for providing the galaxy with thousands of treatments and advancing medical knowledge at a tremendous pace. However, not all it's pursuits are as noble as some would hope. Some schemes are driven by pure economics, to enrich the corporation and expand its reach. Much of the galaxy would also be shocked to learn of Adascorp's persecution of [Arkanian Offshoots](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Arkanian_Offshoots "Arkanian Offshoots") and its promotion of pure-blooded [Arkanians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Arkanians "Arkanians") as a superior [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species").

For years, Adascorp headquarters is located on the massive Starship [The *Arkanian Legacy*](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Arkanian_Legacy "The Arkanian Legacy"). However, the great ship is lost along with Lord [Arkoh Adasca](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Arkoh_Adasca "Arkoh Adasca") in his ill-fated attempt to secure galactic power through Operation Dark Harvest. Arkoh's scheme is to militarize massive [Exogorths](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exogorths?action=edit&redlink=1 "Exogorths (page does not exist)") to enable them to decimate entire star systems. He tries to sell them in a bidding war between [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic"), [The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians"), and [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"). Unfortunately for Arkoh, the scheme falls apart when his lead researcher turns the [Exogorths](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exogorths?action=edit&redlink=1 "Exogorths (page does not exist)") against [The *Arkanian Legacy*](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Arkanian_Legacy "The Arkanian Legacy"). The loss of the ship and its extensive archives and research facilities sets Adascorp research back by decades. Worse, with no explicit heir to Arkoh's controlling interest in the corporation, ownership remains contested and is unlikely to be settled for years. The situation is complicated by the fact that the Republic continues to conceal the events around Operation Dark Harvest, seeking to protect its own interests with the [Arkanians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Arkanians "Arkanians").

[The Czerka Corporation](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Czerka_Corporation "The Czerka Corporation") is one of the most widespread and influential of the galaxy's vast interstellar corporations. They have their fingers in every market and they have offices on every major world and on minor or remote planets as well. The Czerka Corporation represents the worst in interstellar corporations: they are amoral, consumed with their own success, loyal only to themselves, and worried primarily only about their reputation. Despite their selfish ways, they provide a wide variety of products of decent- or at least acceptable- quality. Though customers are loath to deal with the company, Czerka products are economical, and the company's vast reach makes them widely available.

For years, President [Pollard Seario](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pollard_Seario?action=edit&redlink=1 "Pollard Seario (page does not exist)") has run The Czerka Corporation from the spaceport of Dreshdae, on the planet [Korriban](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Korriban "Korriban"). An unlikely headquarters for an interstellar corporation, the tiny outpost reveals much about the company and its leadership. First, the company is willing to maintain operations on any world, no matter which government or military force occupies the planet. Second, Seario is more than happy to ally himself and his company with [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") during [The Jedi Civil War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Civil_War "The Jedi Civil War"). Even though [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") is defeated, the alliance is not fatal to the corporation. After the obvious setback, the company works quickly to rebuild itself and strengthen its ties to [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic").

Czerka Corporation offices are utilitarian, and their staff is just customer friendly enough for their clientele to tolerate. They work hard to maintain a good reputation in the galaxy, even if they have to cover up failed ventures and illegal activities. Facilities include general stores on remote worlds such as [Tatooine](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tatooine "Tatooine"), slave trade on non-Republic worlds such as [Kashyyyk](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kashyyyk "Kashyyyk"), energy generators for entire cities, a highly successful arms division, and construction facilities for building freighters, bulk cruisers, and HK Assassin Droids. During [The Old Republic Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic_Era "The Old Republic Era"), The Czerka Corporation has at least one division devoted to any given pursuit. They are constantly on the search for new materials, markets, and worlds to exploit.

[Lhosan Industries](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lhosan_Industries "Lhosan Industries") is widely regarded as the inventor and first mass producer of the modern [Swoop Racer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Daggerstar_Swoop_Racer "Daggerstar Swoop Racer"). Because their first factory is located in the Lower City of [Taris](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Taris "Taris"), the planet becomes a center of swoop-racing popularity. Lhosan's plan to diversify to other worlds is disrupted by the Mandalorian occupation of [Taris](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Taris "Taris").

In a move that demonstrates the power and influence of the company, chairman [Jervo Thalien](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jervo_Thalien?action=edit&redlink=1 "Jervo Thalien (page does not exist)") works with [Haydel Goravvus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Haydel_Goravvus "Haydel Goravvus") to orchestrate the acceptance of [Taris](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Taris "Taris") into [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") to protect the planet and its supporting resource systems in the region. The plan succeeds. However, once it becomes apparent that the Mandalorian invasion is going to reach [Taris](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Taris "Taris"), Thalien wastes no time in pulling operations offworld. The resulting scandal threatens to devastate the company and [Jervo Thalien](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jervo_Thalien?action=edit&redlink=1 "Jervo Thalien (page does not exist)") personally, if it comes to light. Thalien works to make sure that never happens, but ultimately [Haydel Goravvus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Haydel_Goravvus "Haydel Goravvus") thwarts him, enabling the company to become a strong supporter of the war and [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic").

Lhosan Industries produces dozens of models of [Swoop Racers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swoop_Racers "Swoop Racers") of virtually any size and configuration a customer could request. Although purchasers customize the stock units, creating a vibrant aftermarket culture, Lhosan takes pride in providing a first-class product adored by fans throughout the galaxy. Their quick move from [Taris](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Taris "Taris") slows and hampers their production in the following months, but the Mandalorian onslaught itself reduces the immediate demand in any case.

## Timeline

The following timeline covers many of the major events in [The Old Republic Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic_Era "The Old Republic Era"). While it is by no means completely comprehensive, it covers the rise and defeat of major antagonists across the four wars in the setting, and should give Gamemasters a good idea of when to set their campaigns.

- **4000 BBY:** [Satal Keto](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Satal_Keto "Satal Keto") and [Aleema Keto](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aleema_Keto "Aleema Keto") discover ancient secrets of [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith"), including artifacts and tomes of knowledge, on their home planet of [Empress Teta](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Empress_Teta?action=edit&redlink=1 "Empress Teta (page does not exist)"). Satal and Aleema recruit hundreds of fellow aristocrats and form [The Krath](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Krath "The Krath"), a Dark Side cult based on Sith teachings.
- **3997 BBY:** Steeped in knowledge of [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), [The Krath](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Krath "The Krath") rally military forces throughout [The Deep Core](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Deep_Core?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Deep Core (page does not exist)") and stage a coup on their own homeworld of [Empress Teta](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Empress_Teta?action=edit&redlink=1 "Empress Teta (page does not exist)").
- **3997 BBY:** [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") are ambushed by Krath forces during a conclave on the planet [Deneba](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deneba?action=edit&redlink=1 "Deneba (page does not exist)"), causing massive Jedi casualties. After the battle, [Ulic Qel-Droma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ulic_Qel-Droma "Ulic Qel-Droma") departs and begins to infiltrate the upper echelons of [The Krath](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Krath "The Krath").
- **3997 BBY:** [Exar Kun](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exar_Kun "Exar Kun"), a Jedi apprentice, abandons his master to delve deeper into the secrets of [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"), traveling the galaxy in search of Sith lore. On [Korriban](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Korriban "Korriban"), Kun is educated in the ways of [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") and soon is elevated to the rank of Dark Lord of [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith").
- **3997 BBY:** After [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") attempt to extract him from the Krath-held world of [Empress Teta](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Empress_Teta?action=edit&redlink=1 "Empress Teta (page does not exist)"), [Ulic Qel-Droma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ulic_Qel-Droma "Ulic Qel-Droma") succumbs to the pull of [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"). When [Exar Kun](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exar_Kun "Exar Kun") arrives to stake his claim as Dark Lord of [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith"), he and Qel-Droma duel, but stop when ancient Sith spirits appear and declare them both Dark Lords of [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith"), master and apprentice. Many Jedi flock to the newly reborn Sith Order, forming a hierarchy of lesser Sith Lords.
- **3996 BBY:** The Krath forces, under the control of the Dark Lords of [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith"), begin expanding their newly formed empire, seizing worlds throughout [The Deep Core](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Deep_Core?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Deep Core (page does not exist)"). The Krath's ability to control the planets they have conquered is strained until [Ulic Qel-Droma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ulic_Qel-Droma "Ulic Qel-Droma") defeats [Mandalore the Indomitable](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mandalore_the_Indomitable "Mandalore the Indomitable") in a duel on the planet [Kuar](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kuar?action=edit&redlink=1 "Kuar (page does not exist)"). With [The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians") bound to the Sith Lords, their combined forces flow outward from [The Deep Core](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Deep_Core?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Deep Core (page does not exist)") and begin conquering planets across the galaxy.
- **3996 BBY:** After months of conquering planets from [The Deep Core](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Deep_Core?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Deep Core (page does not exist)") to [The Outer Rim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Outer_Rim?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Outer Rim (page does not exist)"), [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") launch their attack against the capital planet of [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic"), [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant"). During the battle, [Ulic Qel-Droma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ulic_Qel-Droma "Ulic Qel-Droma") is captured and subsequently put on trial for treason.
- **3996 BBY:** [Aleemo Keto](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aleemo_Keto?action=edit&redlink=1 "Aleemo Keto (page does not exist)"), leading the forces of [The Krath](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Krath "The Krath") in battle near the Cron Cluster, is killed after activating a supernova-inducing weapon aboard her warship. [The Krath](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Krath "The Krath") space fleet is devastated.
- **3996 BBY:** After an intense battle at [Onderon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Onderon "Onderon"), [Mandalore the Indomitable](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mandalore_the_Indomitable "Mandalore the Indomitable") is killed and [The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians") suffer a stunning defeat.
- **3996 BBY:** [Exar Kun](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exar_Kun "Exar Kun") and the remnants of [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") are defeated at [Yavin 4](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yavin_4 "Yavin 4"). [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War") comes to an end when Republic forces liberate [Empress Teta](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Empress_Teta?action=edit&redlink=1 "Empress Teta (page does not exist)").
- **3985 BBY:** The Coruscant Financial Exchange Establishment Act facilitates the restoration of the galaxy by allowing galactic corporations to invest in the Republic's infrastructure.
- **3976 BBY:** Under the leadership of [Mandalore the Ultimate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mandalore_the_Ultimate "Mandalore the Ultimate"), remnants of the Mandalorian clans begin testing the Republic's defenses by raiding Outer Rim worlds.
- **3973 BBY:** [Cassus Fett](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cassus_Fett "Cassus Fett") leads the Mandalorian massacre on [Cathar](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cathar,_Planet "Cathar, Planet"). Few [Cathar](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cathar "Cathar") survive the assault, but [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") suppresses news of the attack, fearing it might destabilize reconstruction efforts.
- **3965 BBY:** [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") engages [The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians") in small proxy skirmishes along [The Outer Rim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Outer_Rim?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Outer Rim (page does not exist)").
- **3964 BBY:** [The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians") stage a massive invasion of Republic Space, beginning [The Mandalorian Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorian_Wars "The Mandalorian Wars").
- **3963 BBY:** As [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") continues to battle [The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians") across all fronts, [Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Revan "Revan") attempts to persuade the Jedi Council to join the war. The council refuses, claiming that their losses during [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War") were too great.
- **3963 BBY:** [The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians") overrun numerous Republic worlds, including [Duro](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Duro "Duro"), [Iridonia](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Iridonia "Iridonia"), [Serroco](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Serroco?action=edit&redlink=1 "Serroco (page does not exist)"), and [Taris](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Taris "Taris"). Meanwhile, [Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Revan "Revan") discovers and reveals the truth about the massacre at [Cathar](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cathar,_Planet "Cathar, Planet"), winning dozens of converts from [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order"). These Jedi take a more active hand in [The Mandalorian Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorian_Wars "The Mandalorian Wars") and are branded [The Revanchists](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Revanchists "The Revanchists").
- **3961 BBY:** [Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Revan "Revan") discovers the Rakatan Star Map at [Kashyyyk](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kashyyyk "Kashyyyk"). [Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Revan "Revan") travels to [Malachor V](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Malachor_V "Malachor V") and discovers the Trayus Academy, delving deeply into the Sith secrets contained within.
- **3960 BBY:** Revan lures [The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians") to [Malachor V](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Malachor_V "Malachor V"), where a battle ensues between [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") and [The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians"). [Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Revan "Revan") defeats [Mandalore the Ultimate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mandalore_the_Ultimate "Mandalore the Ultimate"), activated the superweapon known as The Mass Shadow Generator, and shatters the Mandalorian fleet. [The Mandalorian Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorian_Wars "The Mandalorian Wars") officially end, and remnants of the opposition scatter throughout the galaxy.
- **3960 BBY:** [Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Revan "Revan") and their apprentice, [Malak](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Malak "Malak"), take a large segment of the Republic Fleet and vanish into [The Unknown Regions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Unknown_Regions "The Unknown Regions"). They rediscover The Star Forge, declare themselves Dark Lords of [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith"), and corrupt the Republic forces under their control.
- **3959 BBY:** [Darth Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Revan "Darth Revan") and [Darth Malak](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Malak "Darth Malak") return to [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic"), at the head of the newly constructed Sith Fleet. They begin their conquest of the galaxy, carving out a new Sith Empire and seizing control of [Korriban](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Korriban "Korriban"), where they establish a new academy.
- **3958 BBY:** Many among the Republic's military forces defect to [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire"), including [Saul Karath](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Saul_Karath "Saul Karath"). Jedi start to flock to Darth Revan's banner, and [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") begins to swallow up Republic worlds. The Sith carry out successful attacks at [Foerost](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Foerost?action=edit&redlink=1 "Foerost (page does not exist)") and [Telos IV](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Telos_IV "Telos IV"), among other worlds.
- **3957 BBY:** A Jedi task force captures [Darth Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Revan "Darth Revan") aboard their flagship after [Darth Malak](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Malak "Darth Malak") turns on his master and attempts to kill them. Revan's memory is erased, and the Jedi flee with them back to Republic Space.
- **3956 BBY:** [Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Revan "Revan") once again turns to [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order") and begins relearning the ways of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). Accompanied by [Bastila Shan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bastila_Shan "Bastila Shan"), Revan retraces their steps in the pursuit of The Star Forge, eventually discovering the truth behind their past.
- **3956 BBY:** Darth Malak's forces devastate the Jedi Enclave on [Dantooine](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dantooine "Dantooine").
- **3956 BBY:** In the Lehon system, the Republic clashes with the Sith at the Battle of [Rakata Prime](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rakata_Prime?action=edit&redlink=1 "Rakata Prime (page does not exist)"). [Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Revan "Revan") defeats [Darth Malak](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Malak "Darth Malak") in a duel, [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") defeats [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire"), and [The Jedi Civil War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Civil_War "The Jedi Civil War") comes to an end. [Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Revan "Revan") leaves once more for [The Unknown Regions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Unknown_Regions "The Unknown Regions").
- **3955 BBY:** Sith Lords, formerly loyal to [Darth Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Revan "Darth Revan") and [Darth Malak](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Malak "Darth Malak"), begin breaking [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") into smaller domains, declaring themselves sovereign warlords.
- **3954 BBY:** Sith assassins, under orders from [Darth Sion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Sion "Darth Sion"), begin a wide-spread assassination campaign against [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"). [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") are all but wiped out, and [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") is left without its Jedi defenders for the first time in centuries.
- **3954 BBY:** The remnants of [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") are largely unified by [Darth Sion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Sion "Darth Sion") and [Darth Nihilus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Nihilus "Darth Nihilus"). Though some Sith warlords continue to squabble over territory, Sion and Nihilus represent the most unified front for [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") during this time.
- **3951 BBY:** [Canderous Ordo](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Canderous_Ordo "Canderous Ordo"), now the Mandalore, reunites [The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians") and aids in the fight against the remnants of [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire").
- **3951 BBY:** Under the guidance of a disguised [Darth Traya](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Traya "Darth Traya"), [The Jedi Exile](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Exile "The Jedi Exile") defeats [Darth Sion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Sion "Darth Sion") and [Darth Nihilus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Nihilus "Darth Nihilus"). [Bao-Dur](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bao-Dur "Bao-Dur") activated The Mass Shadow Generator on [Malachor V](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Malachor_V "Malachor V") again, destroying what is left of the planet, and Traya is defeated.
- **3950 BBY:** The last remnants of [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") crumble, and [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") once again establishes control over worlds that have been under Sith rule since [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War").

# Category : Bio-Implants

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Bio-Implants](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Bio-Implants) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:07:23Z.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

Their invasion of the galaxy thwarted, the [Yuuzhan Vong](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong "Yuuzhan Vong") withdrew to [Zonama Sekot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Zonama_Sekot "Zonama Sekot"). A small few elected to remain behind, carving out lives for themselves on remote, undeveloped worlds or integrating into galactic society. A small number of Yuuzhan Vong [Shapers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shapers "Shapers") took this latter path and began using their advanced knowledge of bioengineering to make biological alterations to other sentient beings. These modifications were [Illegal](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Illegal "Illegal"), not to mention unpredictable and dangerous. Still, [Yuuzhan Vong](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong "Yuuzhan Vong") biological modification became an intriguing alternative to cybernetic enhancement, which had for years allowed criminals to gain a technological edge over their competitors.

Obtaining a Bio-Implant is risky business, for all of the Bio-Implants and modifications listed here are [Illegal](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Illegal "Illegal"). Most of the rogue [Shapers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shapers "Shapers") that offer biomodification do so from the darker corners of the galaxy, far from the probing eyes of [The Galactic Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Galactic_Empire "The Galactic Empire"). Moreover, there are rarely interested in Imperial credits, instead seeking payment in the form of favors, services, or bartered goods. Thus, the cost for a Bio-Implant represents the credit value of the modification, not particularly its actual price; Gamemasters are encouraged to require that any heroes seeking [Yuuzhan Vong](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong "Yuuzhan Vong") Bio-Implants perform some task for the [Shaper](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shaper "Shaper"), or provide [Rare](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rare "Rare") or [Illegal](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Illegal "Illegal") goods of the same value in trade. While the GM is free to allow the character to simply purchase one of these enhancements, they lose some of their uniqueness and mystery if the hero simply hands over a [Credit Chip](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Credit_Chip "Credit Chip") and receives an enhancement.

For example, one of the player characters might be looking to obtain the [Enhanced Vision Bio-Implant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Enhanced_Vision_Bio-Implant "Enhanced Vision Bio-Implant"), valued at 1,900 credits. Instead of just allowing the hero to pay for the enhancement, the Gamemaster decides to stage a short side adventure in which the heroes must deliver a message to a secret [Yuuzhan Vong](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong "Yuuzhan Vong") enclave on [Felucia](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Felucia "Felucia"). Since the credit reward for that adventure is the same as the cost of the Bio-Implant, the Gamemaster determines that the heroes receive no credit reward, but the hero in question obtains their Bio-Implant as reward for delivering the message. Of course, when the Empire hears that someone who has sought Illegal [Yuuzhan Vong](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong "Yuuzhan Vong") enhancements will be traveling to [Felucia](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Felucia "Felucia"), things could get dangerous...

Yuuzhan Vong Bio-Implants are like cybernetics in many respects, especially in regards to game mechanics. Implanting a Bio-Implant requires the [Biotech Surgery](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Biotech_Surgery "Biotech Surgery") feat. Once installed, the Biotech enhancement functions as described in their individual entry. Any character [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") can remove a Bio-Implant by performing [Surgery](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surgery "Surgery"); this requires a DC 20 [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") check at the end of the procedure, using the normal rules for performing [Surgery](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surgery "Surgery").

Sometimes the shaping of the new Bio-Implant has flaws, or the recipient's body rejects the Bio-Implant for some reason. When a Bio-Implant is first installed, make an attack roll (1d20+5) against the target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"), with a cumulative +2 bonus on the attack roll for each Bio-Implant the recipient already has. If the attack is successful, the recipient's body rejects the Bio-Implant and the recipient moves -1 [Persistent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persistent "Persistent") step down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"). The Bio-Implant is also destroyed. This [Persistent Condition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persistent_Condition "Persistent Condition") can only be removed with 8 hours of uninterrupted rest; furthermore, any future attempt to install the same kind of Bio-Implant gains a +10 bonus to its attack roll.

Because the [Yuuzhan Vong](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong "Yuuzhan Vong") have been cut off from [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), much of their [Biotechnology](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Biotechnology "Biotechnology") hinders a being's connection to [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). Creatures with Bio-Implants take a -1 penalty on [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") checks for each Bio-Implant (to a maximum penalty of -5).

Bio-Implants and the LawBio-Implants and the Law

Since [Yuuzhan Vong](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong "Yuuzhan Vong") Bio-Implants are [Illegal](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Illegal "Illegal"), anyone walking around with a Bio-Implant on display is likely to get into legal trouble, especially on Imperial planets where the laws are strictly upheld. A character can attempt to hide a Bio-Implant using the [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") skill (as though creating a [Deceptive Appearance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deceptive_Appearance "Deceptive Appearance")). [Poison Filters](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Poison_Filters?action=edit&redlink=1 "Poison Filters (page does not exist)") and [Replacement Body Parts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Replacement_Body_Parts?action=edit&redlink=1 "Replacement Body Parts (page does not exist)") for internal organs require no [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") check to hide, as they are not plainly visible. Hiding most [Cosmetic Enhancements](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cosmetic_Enhancements "Cosmetic Enhancements") and the [Enhanced Vision Bio-Implant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Enhanced_Vision_Bio-Implant "Enhanced Vision Bio-Implant") are considered a Simple Deception; [Replacement Body Parts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Replacement_Body_Parts?action=edit&redlink=1 "Replacement Body Parts (page does not exist)") for external replacements are usually Moderate Deceptions; [Body Spikes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Body_Spikes "Body Spikes"), [Natural Armor Bio-Implants](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Natural_Armor_Bio-Implants?action=edit&redlink=1 "Natural Armor Bio-Implants (page does not exist)"), and [Natural Weapon Bio-Implants](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Natural_Weapon_Bio-Implants?action=edit&redlink=1 "Natural Weapon Bio-Implants (page does not exist)") are usually Difficult Deceptions.

A character using sensors capable of detecting the physical health of a target (such as a [Medisensor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Medisensor "Medisensor")) can detect the presence of Bio-Implants, including internal Bio-Implants. A character with a [Sensor Pack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sensor_Pack "Sensor Pack") gains a +5 circumstance bonus on [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") checks to see through the [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") of hiding Bio-Implants.

The penalty for having an [Illegal](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Illegal "Illegal") Bio-Implant is severe. Under Imperial law, the offense warrants immediate arrest. The fine for having [Illegal](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Illegal "Illegal") Bio-Implants is usually 5,000 credits x the number of Bio-Implants possessed. Additionally, the offender must serve a minimum of 2 years in an Imperial corrections facility, where prison surgeons remove the Bio-Implants. Some individuals have special Imperial decrees that allow them to possess [Yuuzhan Vong](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong "Yuuzhan Vong") Bio-Implants legally. All Sith are covered under these decrees, and Imperial bureaucrats can usually obtain this exception as a part of their badge of office (though few do, since it is often seen as a defilement of the body). Individuals might be able to bribe Imperial officers to overlook Bio-Implants, and possibly to obtain an Imperial decree, though this latter option is usually expensive and requires some severe cajoling or the completion of some difficult task.

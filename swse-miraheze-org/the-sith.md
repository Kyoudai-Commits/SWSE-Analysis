# The Sith

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:04:04Z.

*-From the Sith Code*

An ancient order of Force-users devoted to [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") and determined to destroy [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"), The Sith have existed in many forms throughout the history of the galaxy. The original Sith were a [Near-Human](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Near-Human "Near-Human") population on [Korriban](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Korriban "Korriban"), subjected and ruled by outcast Dark Jedi several thousand years before the Battle of [Yavin](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yavin "Yavin"). The name "Sith" eventually came to refer to the Dark Side cult that ruled the Sith people (that is, the "Lords of the Sith"). When [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") discovered The Sith thousands of years later, it lead to [The Great Hyperspace War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Hyperspace_War?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Great Hyperspace War (page does not exist)"), the first of a long series of conflicts that ravaged the galaxy over the next few millennia. Eventually, The Sith (now known as the "Old Sith") was destroyed, and all it left behind were ancient [Holocrons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Holocrons "Holocrons") and the ruined tombs and temples on [Korriban](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Korriban "Korriban"), [Yavin 4](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yavin_4 "Yavin 4"), and a handful of other planets throughout the galaxy.

The current incarnation of The Sith (the "New Sith") is the result of another Dark Jedi who broke away from the order. Two thousand years before the Battle of [Yavin](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yavin "Yavin"), this Dark Jedi had come to the understanding that the true power of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") lay not through contemplation and passivity, but by tapping into one's emotions and [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"). Renaming himself [Darth Ruin](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Ruin?action=edit&redlink=1 "Darth Ruin (page does not exist)"), he stole a [Sith Holocron](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Holocron "Sith Holocron") from the archives of [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order") and began gathering followers to his banner. Awakening beliefs from the dark past, the New Sith cult began to grow, driven by the promise of new powers attainable by tapping into the hateful energies of [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"). It was only a matter of time before the order self-destructed. Internecine struggles by power-hungry Sith practitioners dwindled their numbers. Weakened by infighting, The Sith were easily wiped out by [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") at the Battle of [Ruusan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ruusan "Ruusan"), one thousand years after [Darth Ruin](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Ruin?action=edit&redlink=1 "Darth Ruin (page does not exist)") founded the Order.

However, one Sith had the cunning to survive. [Darth Bane](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Bane "Darth Bane") resurrected the cult so that there could only be two- no more, no less; a master and an apprentice. Bane adopted cunning, subterfuge, and stealth as the fundamental tenets of the new Sith Order. Bane took an apprentice. When that apprentice succeeded him, that new Sith Lord would take an apprentice. Thus, The Sith quietly continued for centuries, until the time of [Darth Sidious](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Sidious "Darth Sidious"). With great cunning and treachery, Sidious fomented a galactic civil war within [The Galactic Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Galactic_Republic "The Galactic Republic"), seized control of the Galactic Senate, wiped out [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order"), and replaced the Republic with [The Galactic Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Galactic_Empire "The Galactic Empire"). With Sidious as the Emperor and [Darth Vader](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Vader "Darth Vader") as his loyal apprentice, The Sith ruled the galaxy and plunged it into darkness. It remained so for decades until a new hope arose to bring [Darth Vader](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Vader "Darth Vader") back from [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") and extinguish the menace of The Sith.

The Sith Order rises once again in [The Legacy Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Legacy_Era "The Legacy Era"), some 140 years after the Battle of [Yavin](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yavin "Yavin"). The new Sith are led by the enigmatic [Darth Krayt](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Krayt "Darth Krayt") and serve as Dark Side enforcers to the reincarnated Empire. Though The Sith are not as common as [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") were during the days of the Republic, it is not uncommon to have a [Sith Apprentice](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Apprentice "Sith Apprentice") or [Sith Lord](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Lord "Sith Lord") attached to an important mission or garrison.

**Membership:** Any character with the [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity") feat can become a member of The Sith by being accepted as an apprentice by a [Sith Lord](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Lord "Sith Lord"). During [The Rise of the Empire Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Rise_of_the_Empire_Era "The Rise of the Empire Era"), there can only be one [Sith Lord](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Lord "Sith Lord") and one [Sith Apprentice](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Apprentice "Sith Apprentice"). Thus, the only way to join The Sith during this era is to wait (or arrange) for one of the two Sith to die.

*-The Code of The Sith*

The story of The Sith goes back one hundred thousand years, eons before even the rise of [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") or [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi").

The Sith were a people who called the world [Korriban](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Korriban "Korriban") home. But around the year 28,000 BBY, the archetypal model of ambition rises to power among them. This inflexible Sith ruler unites his world into a single kingdom, and claims the titles of both monarch and god of his people. History calls him King [Adas](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Adas?action=edit&redlink=1 "Adas (page does not exist)"), but according to legend, [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") bestows on him a different name: *Sith'ari*- the perfect being.

Three thousand years before the founding of the Republic, Adas' hegemony is invaded by [The Infinite Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Infinite_Empire "The Infinite Empire"), an immensely powerful interstellar nation. Here, later Sith would say, they prove their right to rule the galaxy. Outsmarting the technologically superior invaders, [Adas](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Adas?action=edit&redlink=1 "Adas (page does not exist)") conquers the conquerors, sacrificing his life in the process. With the aliens' technology, The Sith prosper, colonizing nearby worlds and even banishing a group of pureblooded Sith as far away as [Tund](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tund?action=edit&redlink=1 "Tund (page does not exist)"). But there is a greater gift [The Infinite Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Infinite_Empire "The Infinite Empire") bequeathed to The Sith- [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force").

Eventually, The Sith are again visited by aliens, this time by Dark Jedi exiled in [The Hundred-Year Darkness](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Hundred-Year_Darkness?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Hundred-Year Darkness (page does not exist)"). These exiles, initially called *Jen'jidai* in the Sith tongue, easily conquer the reigning Sith overlord, earning their leader [Ajunta Pall](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ajunta_Pall "Ajunta Pall") the title *Jen'ari* or "Dark Lord" of the Sith. Though a number of these Sith conquerors follow their leaders back to Republic Space for revenge against [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"), many stay to interbreed with the Sith by alchemical means. For the next two thousand years, Jedi blood mixes with Sith, breeding out feeble characteristics of each [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species")- such as superstition, sympathy, and loyalty- while sharpening desirable qualities such as ambition, cunning, and raw power in [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). At last, around the year 5,000 BBY, The Sith, led by the Dark Lord [Naga Sadow](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Naga_Sadow?action=edit&redlink=1 "Naga Sadow (page does not exist)"), is ready and invades Republic Space.

Because of treason, the Sith campaign fails, and special enforcers called [Jedi Shadows](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Shadows "Jedi Shadows") zealously eradicate every last vestige of The Sith they find. But in a twist of irony, a few short centuries later, the Jedi Knight [Freedon Nadd](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Freedon_Nadd "Freedon Nadd") finds two Sith relics the Shadows missed. Taking instruction from both King Adas' Holocron and Sadow's disembodied spirit, Nadd becomes a Dark Lord. In turn, he inculcates the Jedi [Exar Kun](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exar_Kun "Exar Kun") into The Sith creed, consequently bringing [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") to its knees as Kun's Brotherhood of the Sith instigates [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War").

Kun's Brotherhood is defeated, and in another blow, [The Mecrosa Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mecrosa_Order "The Mecrosa Order"), a long-time Sith bastion, overplays its hand in [The Tapani Sector](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Tapani_Sector?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Tapani Sector (page does not exist)") and is completely annihilated in [The Cleansing of the Nine Houses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Cleansing_of_the_Nine_Houses?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Cleansing of the Nine Houses (page does not exist)"). Still, The Sith need never fear extinction again. From the seed of Kun's conversion comes a host of Jedi followers, and after his death, his acolytes scatter throughout the galaxy with the teachings of The Sith ancients.

## [The Restoration Period](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Restoration_Period "The Restoration Period")/[The Mandalorian Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorian_Wars "The Mandalorian Wars")

**3,995-3,960 BBY**

Though the forces of [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") and [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") manage to throw back the combined forces of The Sith and [The Krath](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Krath "The Krath") during [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War"), many followers of The Sith remain loyal to their ways. As the Republic rebuilds and subsequently engages [The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians") in a bloody war of attrition, remnants of The Sith, some on worlds completely unknown to the Republic, continue to practice their dark arts in secret. Though they remain scattered and unable to mount a serious effort to regroup, Sith loyalists, fallen Jedi, and the descendants of powerful leaders in Exar Kun's Sith Empire await their chance to rebuild the mighty Sith Empire.

## [The Jedi Civil War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Civil_War "The Jedi Civil War")

**3,959-3,956 BBY**

They do not have to wait long, for soon [Darth Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Revan "Darth Revan") and [Darth Malak](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Malak "Darth Malak") appear, resurrecting [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") in a Jedi Civil War. Returning from [The Unknown Regions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Unknown_Regions "The Unknown Regions") following [The Mandalorian Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorian_Wars "The Mandalorian Wars"), the two Sith Lords engage in a lightning-fast campaign to reestablish [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") and reclaim conquered worlds. Among the first planets retaken by The Sith is [Korriban](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Korriban "Korriban"), which becomes one of the most important locations in [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") and houses the academy where new Sith leaders are trained. With [The Star Forge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Star_Forge "The Star Forge") producing huge amounts of [Starships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships "Starships"), [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles"), and weapons at all times, The Sith are able to resurrect their Empire in a matter of months.

At the height of The Jedi Civil War, [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") covers nearly one-third of the known galaxy, with many worlds hotly contested between [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") and [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire"). Some worlds join [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") willingly, while others are taken by force. Dozens if not hundreds of Sith apprentices learn the ways of [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), and a few even rise to the rank of Sith Master, training students of their own. With legions of armored troops spreading throughout the galaxy, all led by masters of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") seems unstoppable.

Betrayal spells the undoing of Revan's glorious, if short-lived, Sith Empire. At the moment [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") capture them, Darth Revan's own apprentice attempts to seize control, firing on Revan's flagship. Though [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") continues to grow for some months under the control of the traitorous [Darth Malak](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Malak "Darth Malak"), eventually the redeemed [Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Revan "Revan") slays their former apprentice and shatters the backbone of the reborn Sith Empire. The Jedi Civil War comes to an end as the once-unified Sith Empire fragments into dozens of smaller territories.

## [The Dark Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Wars "The Dark Wars")

**3,955-3,951 BBY**

[The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") crumbles, and within months former Sith Lords have declared themselves the masters of much smaller domains. As The Sith squabble among themselves, fighting over the scraps of [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire"), [Darth Sion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Sion "Darth Sion") and [Darth Nihilus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Nihilus "Darth Nihilus") engage in a campaign to wipe out [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"), with great success. Thanks to the foolish ambitions of the Jedi [Atris](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Atris "Atris"), Nihilus is able to wipe out almost all of the remaining Jedi, leaving only a small handful alive.

The skirmishes between the Sith warlords continue for nearly four years until [Darth Sion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Sion "Darth Sion") and [Darth Nihilus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Nihilus "Darth Nihilus") attempt to reunite The Sith under a single banner of strong leadership. Any hope for resurrecting [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") is undone by a lone surviving Jedi, and when [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order") is reborn The Sith fade back into the shadows, not to be seen again in force for many years.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

At the top of the Separatist hierarchy- and kept carefully hidden from both sides- are The Sith: [Darth Tyranus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Tyranus "Darth Tyranus") and his dark master, [Darth Sidious](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Sidious "Darth Sidious"). Under Darth Sidious's sinister tutelage, [Darth Tyranus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Tyranus "Darth Tyranus") ([Count Dooku](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Count_Dooku "Count Dooku")) spends ten years manipulating a minor political movement into a full-blown secessionist front while Sidious maneuvers the Republic loyalists into declaring all-out war.

[Darth Sidious](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Sidious "Darth Sidious") adheres to a tradition of secrecy imposed by The Sith a thousand years before, working his evil schemes through intermediaries and dupes while remaining hidden in the shadows. His organization includes politicians and anarchists, crime lords and corrupt law enforcement, schemers and dreamers, the wealthy and the impoverished- and, as the war draws to a close, the most powerful Jedi Knight alive: [Anakin Skywalker](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Anakin_Skywalker "Anakin Skywalker").

Sidious recruits the disillusioned [Count Dooku](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Count_Dooku "Count Dooku") shortly after the Jedi Master leaves [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order") over an ill-fated mission to the world of [Galidraan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galidraan?action=edit&redlink=1 "Galidraan (page does not exist)"). A suave and charismatic speaker, Dooku makes the ideal figurehead for the Separatists: a dynamic orator with a strong desire to see the Republic made to serve its most influential constituents, without all the inconvenient public safety rules and annoying trade regulations. Much of his own philosophy has formed the core of Separatist views, and it is little wonder that they flock to his side.

However, like all of Sidious's apprentices, Dooku is expendable. In fact, the Sith philosophy is for an apprentice to continually prove himself. If the apprentice does not survive, they can be replaced- often by whoever killed the apprentice. This is the case with [Darth Tyranus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Tyranus "Darth Tyranus")- slain by the Jedi [Anakin Skywalker](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Anakin_Skywalker "Anakin Skywalker"), who soon replaces Dooku as Sidious's new apprentice, [Darth Vader](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Vader "Darth Vader").

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

With the deaths of [Darth Vader](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Vader "Darth Vader") and [Emperor Palpatine](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Emperor_Palpatine "Emperor Palpatine"), it seemed by many as though The Sith had finally been extinguished from the galaxy. However, the forces of darkness are not so easily dispatched. A former Emperor's Hand, [Lumiya](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lumiya "Lumiya"), helped carry on the Sith tradition for a while, but the true resurrection of The Sith begins on the planet [Korriban](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Korriban "Korriban"), where a devotee named [A'Sharad Hett](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/A%27Sharad_Hett?action=edit&redlink=1 "A'Sharad Hett (page does not exist)") learns the ways of The Sith and discovers a group of Sith cultists planting the seeds of their eventual return. Hett proves to be the catalyst for this event, and his ascension to the rank of Sith Lord seals the galaxy's fate.

[A'Sharad Hett](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/A%27Sharad_Hett?action=edit&redlink=1 "A'Sharad Hett (page does not exist)") emerges from [Korriban](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Korriban "Korriban") as [Darth Krayt](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Krayt "Darth Krayt"), leader of the new Sith Order. He rescinds the Rule of Two put in place by [Darth Bane](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Bane "Darth Bane") thousands of years ago and, which limited The Sith to a single master and apprentice. He issues the Rule of One: the declaration that there will be one Sith Order composed of many Sith Lords and apprentices, all united under his rule. The return of The Sith in force spells doom for [The Galactic Alliance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Galactic_Alliance "The Galactic Alliance"). However, unlike previous iterations of The Sith Order, Darth Krayt succeeds in controlling the petty ambitions of the Sith Lords under his command and focuses their rage against [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order").

## Sith Acolytes

Though The Sith rule the galaxy from the Imperial throne on [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant"), not all of those who serve [Darth Krayt](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Krayt "Darth Krayt") are Sith Lords. The Sith maintain a cadre of students, sycophants, and aspirants who are collectively called Acolytes. These Acolytes mostly serve on [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant") where they act as aids and assistants to Sith Lords. [Darth Maladi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Maladi "Darth Maladi") has the largest collection of Acolytes at her disposal, which is not surprising given her penchant for using agents outside of The Sith Order (including her Sith spies). The Acolytes serve much the same purpose as Darth Sidious' Imperial advisors; they are semi-trusted confidants who serve alongside The Sith as they rule the galaxy, providing them with advice, service, and loyalty.

Most Sith Acolytes are hangers-on who walk the path of [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") but who, for whatever reason, do not have the capacity to master the mysteries that [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") presents to all students. They might know a few tricks, but rarely do their Sith masters allow them to attain anything approaching true power. They serve their chosen Sith Lord for as long as they are considered useful, after which point they are disposed of. Some Acolytes have managed to survive for many years in the service of their Sith masters, and many believe that, as a reward for their faithful service, they will be taken on as a true Sith Apprentice to be taught the ways of [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side").

Sith Acolytes are not considered part of Darth Krayt's Sith Order per se, though some of them pick up bits of lore culled from the teachings of The Sith. Although they seldom qualify for the [Sith Apprentice](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Apprentice "Sith Apprentice") Prestige Class, Gamemasters who wish to create Sith Acolytes for their own games can waive the requirement that the character must be a member of The Sith. However, Sith Acolytes should likely have no more than one or two [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") from the [Sith Apprentice](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Apprentice "Sith Apprentice") Prestige Class, and even that should represent the most elder Acolytes. Sith Acolytes make good mid-level opponents for heroes fighting against [The Galactic Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Galactic_Empire "The Galactic Empire"); they might even have a few [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") up their sleeves, but in the end they lack the power of a full-fledged [Sith Apprentice](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Apprentice "Sith Apprentice").

## The Sith Lords

The Sith fill a variety of roles in the new Galactic Empire. This begins at the top with the man who sits upon the Imperial throne on [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant"), [Darth Krayt](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Krayt "Darth Krayt"). Krayt is the supreme ruler of the Empire, and his word is law. The Emperor rules over the Council of Moffs as well as over other Sith Lords, making him the leader of the two greatest factions of [The Galactic Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Galactic_Empire "The Galactic Empire"). [Darth Krayt](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Krayt "Darth Krayt") also serves as the Sith Lord who has dominion over [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant"), and commands the Coruscant Defense Fleet and all of the military forces on the world. Both Sith and Imperial alike bow before [Darth Krayt](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Krayt "Darth Krayt").

Below [Darth Krayt](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Krayt "Darth Krayt") are the Sith Lords, who are taught in The Sith tradition. When a potential Sith candidate is found, whether young or seasoned, they are placed under the tutelage of an existing Sith Lord, serving as an apprentice to until such a time as his or her training is complete. At that time, the Sith Apprentice undergoes a right of passage that sometimes (though not always) requires the apprentice to slay his or her master. Being chosen as a Sith Master is a great honor, but not one without peril; it can be a new way bring powerful Sith into the galaxy, but it can also be a convenient way for [Darth Krayt](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Krayt "Darth Krayt") to dispose of a troublesome and ambitious Sith Lord. Additionally, [Darth Krayt](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Krayt "Darth Krayt") himself oversees many aspects of a Sith Apprentice's training, the final days of which usually take place on [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant").

The Sith Lords have several responsibilities within the Empire, which are handed down from [Darth Krayt](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Krayt "Darth Krayt") himself. One of the most important responsibilities is acting as an overseer of military forces. This can mean being assigned to an individual squad of soldiers, or being given command of an entire fleet of [Starships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships "Starships"). Not every fleet or sector has a Sith Lord in command, but most do; these Sith Lords are outside and above the military command structure, much as [Darth Vader](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Vader "Darth Vader") was over the forces of Palpatine's Empire. The Sith Lords command the military forces under their control, make field promotions, execute those officers who fail them, and direct the Empire's resources to stamp out dissent and sedition. When a Sith Lord is assigned to a military unit of any size, none under his or her command can oppose their wishes.

Other Sith Lords are given dominion over regions, sectors, or individual planets. Most of [The Core Worlds](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Core_Worlds?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Core Worlds (page does not exist)") are so heavily populated that they require their own Sith Lord to oversee affairs. These Sith are usually assigned alongside Moffs and other regional governors, though like their position in the military, the Sith Lords are outside and above the typical bureaucracy. Much as [Darth Krayt](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Krayt "Darth Krayt") rules over the Empire from his throne, many Sith Lords sit on thrones (both literal and figurative) of star systems and sectors. Sith Lords placed in rulership positions may be given domains of varying size, usually depending on the population of those areas; while some Sith Lords rule over individual [Core Worlds](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Worlds?action=edit&redlink=1 "Core Worlds (page does not exist)"), the same Sith Lord might rule over every planet in the Minos Cluster.

The final, and some might say most sinister, duty of the Sith Lords is to hunt down and capture (or kill) Jedi who remain active in the galaxy. The new Empire has no need of an Inquisitorius, as Palpatine did. The Sith Lords serve the same purpose as the Inquisitors, and captured Jedi are brought to [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant") to be tortured and interrogated by [Darth Maladi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Maladi "Darth Maladi"). Combined with the bounty on the heads of all Jedi, the Sith Lords have managed to capture and kill hundreds of Jedi who escaped the initial destruction of the [Ossus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ossus "Ossus") academy. Some Sith Lords are assigned to this duty exclusively, working alone to seek out hidden Jedi and expose them; others do so while holding a military outpost or rulership position.

### The Emperor's Hands

A select few Sith Lords are given the title of Emperor's Hand, a tradition that [Darth Krayt](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Krayt "Darth Krayt") has resurrected from the days of Palpatine. These Hands function as the Emperor's agents throughout the galaxy, performing secret tasks at Krayt's whim. The position of Emperor's Hand is one of great honor, and is fought over among the Sith Lords viciously. Many of The Sith struggle to prove their worthiness to [Darth Krayt](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Krayt "Darth Krayt") in hopes of becoming the next Hand, both to become one of the Emperor's most trusted agents but also to get close enough to him to be in position should the Emperor stumble and the chance to strike him down arise. Rumors exist that Krayt has at least one non-Sith Hand, though none of the Sith Lords have ever seen or heard from such an individual.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

One quality that has allowed The Sith to survive after so many millennia is their ability to adapt. This era in particular presents a gamut of Sith incarnations. The Sith operating at this time fall into the following general categories: Imperialists, Traditionalists, Secret Societies, and Remnants. Each offers unique gaming opportunities.

### [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire")

The reinvented Sith Empire of [Darth Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Revan "Darth Revan") and [Darth Malak](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Malak "Darth Malak") is a massive galaxy-threatening organization on par with [The Galactic Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Galactic_Empire "The Galactic Empire") of the original *Star Wars* trilogy. This version of The Sith has it all, from huge battleships and [Starfighters](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starfighters "Starfighters") to countless shocktroopers of both the "normal" and Force-using variety. They even have a Death Star-like superweapon, in the form of [The Star Forge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Star_Forge "The Star Forge"). Technology and military engagements naturally play a major role for these Sith, since a show of overwhelming force is a prized strategy. Nonetheless, this revised Sith Empire has not abandoned its supernatural roots, training an army of acolytes in an academy on the ancient Sith homeworld [Korriban](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Korriban "Korriban").

At the height of its power, The Sith Empire is much like [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") in that it is highly organized and has the ability to wage an effective war against its enemies. Despite the inherently traitorous nature of The Sith, The Sith Empire's hierarchy is surprisingly rigid, and has a rank structure that ensures that all war efforts do not suffer from infighting.

At the top of the command structure are the two most prominent Sith Lords, [Darth Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Revan "Darth Revan") and [Darth Malak](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Malak "Darth Malak") (though Malak assumes sole control of The Sith Empire after betraying his master). A number of [Sith Lords](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Lords "Sith Lords") serve under the two most prominent masters, including [Darth Bandon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Bandon "Darth Bandon"). Each being that achieves the full title of Dark Lord of the Sith is given control over some military or civilian assets; many [Sith Lords](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Lords "Sith Lords") are placed in charge of entire fleets or battalions, while others serve as the rulers of captured worlds. In many respects, the [Sith Lords](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Lords "Sith Lords") are above and beyond the normal rank structure, and they have the final authority over admirals, commanders, and entire sector governments. In many ways, the [Sith Lords](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Lords "Sith Lords") are comparable to the Moffs of [The Galactic Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Galactic_Empire "The Galactic Empire").

The Sith Navy consists of all of the military [Starships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships "Starships") under the control of The Sith Empire. The Sith Navy is further subdivided into fleets, some of which are large enough to patrol an entire sector effectively. Each fleet is under the control of an admiral, like [Saul Karath](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Saul_Karath "Saul Karath"), though these admirals in turn answer to a [Sith Lord](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Lord "Sith Lord"). Fleets are further subdivided into battle groups, which usually consist of five to ten [Capital Ships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Capital_Ships "Capital Ships") and various support craft. The descending order of ranks among the Sith Navy are admiral, vice admiral, commodore, captain, major, and lieutenant. All nonofficer personnel aboard [Starships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships "Starships") in the Sith Navy are [Sith Troopers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Troopers "Sith Troopers") or specially assigned crewmembers, such as students from the Sith Academy on [Korriban](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Korriban "Korriban") joining a crew for a special assignment.

Though there is no formal Sith Army, the ground forces of The Sith Empire are an entity all on their own. Used not only in ground warfare but also in the occupation, policing, and pacification of captured worlds, [Sith Troopers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Troopers "Sith Troopers") and their officers fall directly under the command of the [Sith Lords](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Lords "Sith Lords") they serve. While aboard ships of the Sith Navy they are under the command of the captain of the ship they serve on, but once planetside they report either to the [Sith Lord](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Lord "Sith Lord") ruling that planet or to the [Sith Lord](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Lord "Sith Lord") in command of the military efforts in the region. The highest-ranking Sith officers are commanders, who outrank colonels and lieutenants.

Among the [Sith Troopers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Troopers "Sith Troopers"), there are truly only two designations: troopers and commandos, though neither truly outranks the other. Lieutenants and colonels in command of squads handle the front-line efforts of [Sith Trooper](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Trooper "Sith Trooper") squads, reporting up the chain of command to colonels who apprise [Sith Lords](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Lords "Sith Lords") of ongoing situations and ensure the Sith Lords' commands are carried out.

### Traditionalists

The Brotherhood of the Sith of [Exar Kun](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exar_Kun "Exar Kun") and [Ulic Qel-Droma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ulic_Qel-Droma "Ulic Qel-Droma") and [The Sorcerers of Tund](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sorcerers_of_Tund "The Sorcerers of Tund") are both Sith traditions that rely significantly on the more esoteric and mystical practices of their ancient order. Adventures involving these groups carry a greater "fantasy" flavor, with arcane Dark Side powers and talismans, Sith swords, and alchemically created monsters at every turn. In contrast to the faceless empire of [Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Revan "Revan") and [Malak](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Malak "Malak"), the Brotherhood of the Sith is represented by a handful of tightly knit darksiders with an egalitarian methodology. The Brotherhood uses technological and military elements, but these are deemphasized in favor of [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") and [Sith Talismans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Talismans "Sith Talismans").

### Secret Societies

The Sith Triumvirate and [The Mecrosa Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mecrosa_Order "The Mecrosa Order") are two examples of Sith secret societies, similar to The Sith in the *Star Wars* prequels. The most prevalent tools of this class of Sith are secrecy, deception, and patience. Like a disease, they conquer from the inside and work from the small scale to the grand design. They reveal themselves only after calculated planning, in the meantime using their resources to quietly dismantle their enemies and sow chaos with precision strikes. The Sith Triumvirate carries out the genocide of [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order") through subterfuge and assassination, but [The Mecrosa Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mecrosa_Order "The Mecrosa Order") uses its Sith sparingly to intimidate, persuade, and murder key rivals until such time as they can establish their total domination.

### Remnants

The Sith fall into diasporas at the immediate conclusion of both [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War") and [The Jedi Civil War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Civil_War "The Jedi Civil War"). These Sith scatter to the cosmic winds, setting up their own petty kingdoms on distant worlds or fighting one another to claim the title of new Dark Lord. Still, even a small-time Sith can be big-time trouble for characters on a mission to eliminate the Sith remnants one Dark Side follower at a time. These Sith are violent, opportunistic, and protective of whatever power they have been able to consolidate. Some even have dreams of returning The Sith to their former glory, recruiting under the guise of disenchanted and charismatic Jedi Knights.

## Part 1: Portrayal

The Sith. The very name has haunted [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order") for millennia. This cult of Force practitioners has known both absolute power and unconscionable self-destruction. Indeed, they are something of an enigma- nightmares to all, but to themselves, a natural expression of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force").

It is no surprise that many players are drawn to the idea of exploring [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") and creating Sith characters. Although *[Threats of the Galaxy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threats_of_the_Galaxy "Threats of the Galaxy")* examines The Sith as enemies, this series of articles will explore the different aspects involved in developing, portraying, and using compelling and unique Sith characters in five eras of the *Star Wars* saga.

### The Sith Code

*Peace is a lie, there is only passion.*

*Through passion, I gain strength.*

*Through strength, I gain power.*

*Through power, I gain victory.*

*Through victory, my chains are broken.*

*The Force shall free me.*

*-From the Sith Code*

The Sith are creatures of passion and lust. [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") has its allure because it calls to the things we crave most in life, seducing those whose desires outstrip their sanity.

Selflessness is not a concept inherent in the Sith way of being. Those who choose to serve [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") out of devotion to a philosophical or religious ideal are rare. The exploration of passion inevitably leads to desire, and delving deeply into desire inevitably leads to greed. Power corrupts, and The Sith are no exception. Though one may begin on the path intending balance or even commanding [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") for altruistic purposes, all fall prey to greed and vanity. This is why groups of Sith, left to their own devices, eventually turn on each other. They are predators, and sooner or later, Sith regard their brethren as threats.

You might say the key difference between [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") and The Sith is that the Jedi contemplate and master inner space to serve others, whereas the Sith dominate and master outer space to serve themselves.

### Eras of the Sith

The Sith figure into five important eras of the *Star Wars* saga. The area you choose in which to play greatly determines the kind of Sith you'll be designing and how they fit in with the galaxy. It's important that you choose an era that works with the kind of Sith game you'd like to play.

#### [The Old Republic Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic_Era "The Old Republic Era")

Four thousand years before [The Galactic Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Galactic_Empire "The Galactic Empire"), [Darth Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Revan "Darth Revan") brought The Sith into the light after visiting Sith worlds and basking in the power of [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"). [Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Revan "Revan") and their apprentice, [Darth Malak](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Malak "Darth Malak"), brought about the bloody Jedi Civil War. They established the new Sith Empire and nearly wiped out [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi").

Since The Sith are prevalent and operate openly in this era, it is easy to play games involving Sith characters.

#### [The Rise of the Empire Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Rise_of_the_Empire_Era "The Rise of the Empire Era")

The Rule of Two characterized this era, as [Darth Sidious](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Sidious "Darth Sidious") completed his apprenticeship under the tutelage of [Darth Plagueis](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Plagueis?action=edit&redlink=1 "Darth Plagueis (page does not exist)") the Wise, and killed his master while he slept. Sidious first took [Darth Maul](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Maul "Darth Maul") as his apprentice, and after the vicious [Zabrak](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Zabrak "Zabrak") was slain by [Obi-Wan Kenobi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Obi-Wan_Kenobi "Obi-Wan Kenobi"), [Darth Tyranus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Tyranus "Darth Tyranus") slipped under his wing. Tyranus was, in turn, killed by [Anakin Skywalker](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Anakin_Skywalker "Anakin Skywalker"), who took Tyranus' place and became one of the most dreaded Lords of the Sith, [Darth Vader](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Vader "Darth Vader").

During this era, Darth Sidious' schemes required obfuscation and no lesser amount of treachery. His plans hinged on The Jedi's belief that The Sith were extinct. Although Sidious may have sheltered and dispatched Dark Jedi to satisfy his plans, he would not train them in the ways of The Sith.

Under canon, it is difficult to justify characters during this era who are true Sith. Dark Jedi characters, on the other hand, fit easily into the growing Sith menace. However, you could create a splinter sect of The Sith that has remained hidden in other parts of the galaxy, like that of [Darth Millennial](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Millennial?action=edit&redlink=1 "Darth Millennial (page does not exist)") and his Dark Force religion.

#### [The Rebellion Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Rebellion_Era "The Rebellion Era")

After [Darth Sidious](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Sidious "Darth Sidious") created [The Galactic Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Galactic_Empire "The Galactic Empire"), dissolved the Senate, and usurped control of the galaxy, he eased the Rule of Two a bit. He created the Secret Order of the Empire and brought Dark Side Force practitioners into the fold to be his prophets. He and Vader trained many [Dark Side Adepts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Adepts_(CL_6) "Dark Side Adepts (CL 6)"), using Dark Jedi as their most trusted and dangerous of servants, including the Emperor's Hand. However, the Rule of Two is kept intact in its strictest sense as neither Sidious or Vader passed on the true legacy and ways of The Sith.

As in [The Rise of the Empire Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Rise_of_the_Empire_Era "The Rise of the Empire Era"), true Sith are rare. Dark Jedi, however, are even more prevalent and can operate openly in the service of the Emperor. Again, other Sith with different views might exist in the shadows of the galaxy, but they would have to remain concealed. Most likely, [Darth Sidious](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Sidious "Darth Sidious") would view them as a threat to be extinguished (an interesting plot device in its own right). However, [Darth Vader](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Vader "Darth Vader") did have at least one secret apprentice...

#### [The New Jedi Order Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_New_Jedi_Order_Era "The New Jedi Order Era")

Although the Rule of Two prevented Vader from taking on a true apprentice, he did pass his teachings to pupils, notably the Dark Lady of the Sith, [Lumiya](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lumiya "Lumiya"). She trained several apprentices during this era, but they are not truly Sith because [Lumiya](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lumiya "Lumiya") herself does not become full-fledged Sith until her training by [Vergere](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vergere?action=edit&redlink=1 "Vergere (page does not exist)").

In [The New Jedi Order Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_New_Jedi_Order_Era "The New Jedi Order Era"), The Sith are all but extinct. It is only through the training of [Vergere](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vergere?action=edit&redlink=1 "Vergere (page does not exist)") that [Lumiya](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lumiya "Lumiya") becomes a true Dark Lady of the Sith. Dark Jedi characters would be more appropriate.

#### [The Legacy Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Legacy_Era "The Legacy Era")

During [The Confederation-Galactic Alliance War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Confederation-Galactic_Alliance_War?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Confederation-Galactic Alliance War (page does not exist)"), the Dark Lady [Lumiya](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lumiya "Lumiya") resurrected the Rule of Two and found herself the perfect apprentice- the grandson of [Darth Vader](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Vader "Darth Vader"). [Jacen Solo](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jacen_Solo?action=edit&redlink=1 "Jacen Solo (page does not exist)") followed in his ancestor's footsteps and became [Darth Caedus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Caedus?action=edit&redlink=1 "Darth Caedus (page does not exist)").

While Caedus was busy trying to gather power, he drew all the attention of [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") to him. Meanwhile, hidden on the ancient Sith planet [Korriban](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Korriban "Korriban"), [Darth Krayt](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Krayt "Darth Krayt") gathered other Dark Jedi to his cause and once again founded an Order of the Sith. This time, however, it was based on Krayt's Rule of One- there would be only one Order, under which there would be many Sith. Eventually, The Sith returned in numbers and brought the galaxy to its knees.

During the time of [Darth Caedus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Caedus?action=edit&redlink=1 "Darth Caedus (page does not exist)"), Sith are even more rare than they were in [The Rebellion Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Rebellion_Era "The Rebellion Era"). Again, Dark Jedi would be more appropriate as characters. However, after [Darth Krayt](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Krayt "Darth Krayt") emerges with his new Sith Order, The Sith once again become plentiful in the galaxy, more so than in any other era but those of the ancient past.

### Crafting the Sith

To create a compelling Sith character, you must consider several important factors, including their reason for falling to [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"), their personal interpretation of Sith beliefs, and their torturous apprenticeship.

#### Falling to the Dark Side

Though each Sith is unique, they share things in common. The most obvious is the presence of a person or event that pulled them to [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"). Many fall in pursuit of power; their desire for personal gain draws them to a philosophy of absolutes. Many others fall prey through their inability to resolve deep hurt, anger, fear, or hatred. These emotions consume their sanity and draw them into a spiral of suffering, transforming them into a potential Sith.

But these are not the only reasons. Some begin with a desire to discover balance in [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), believing that a true understanding of existence is possible only by examining the Light and Dark facets of all beings. Others begin with an altruistic desire to help others by rapidly increasing their power or gaining access to mythical abilities that only [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") can offer. Some are drawn through a misguided hunger for curiosity, a deep desire simply to know. Most often, it is a combination of these factors, but they all lead down the same path.

This crucial event is the foundation in Sith character design. It helps determine who they are on a core level, as well as what might redeem them (if the opportunity arises).

It might be helpful to sketch out a basic story of how your character first encountered the power of [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"). What made them curious enough to play with fire and ultimately become baptized by it, reborn as a creature of darkness?

While the most obvious class progression is from the [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") Heroic Class to the [Sith Apprentice](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Apprentice "Sith Apprentice") Prestige Class (and eventually to the [Sith Lord](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Lord "Sith Lord") Prestige Class), there are many other ways of arriving that might better fit your character's background. For example, [Darth Sidious](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Sidious "Darth Sidious") was never officially trained as a [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi"), instead spreading his web of intrigue as a [Noble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble "Noble"). The greatest restriction to entering the [Sith Apprentice](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Apprentice "Sith Apprentice") Prestige Class is learning [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") as a [Trained Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skill "Trained Skill"), but there are several methods to obtaining said [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill").

As long as you have [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity"), you can train in [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") by any of the traditional skill training methods, including the [Skill Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Training "Skill Training") Feat, increasing your [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") to the point where you get a new [Trained Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skill "Trained Skill"), or taking [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity") at 1st level and spending one of your starting [Trained Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skills "Trained Skills") on [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force").

Another method is, of course, taking levels in the [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") Heroic Class, the only [Heroic Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Class "Heroic Class") that has [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity") as a starting Feat, which can also be taken as a bonus Feat when [Multiclassing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Multiclassing "Multiclassing") into [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi"). Remember that levels in the [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") class do not necessarily mean training in [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order"). Instead, they could indicate training from a [Force Tradition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Tradition "Force Tradition") or simply the first steps of training by a Sith Master.

A character must have seven levels (in any combination of [Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Classes "Classes")) to be eligible for the [Sith Apprentice](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Apprentice "Sith Apprentice") Prestige Class.

#### Beliefs

The philosophy of The Sith is unique. It is a cult dedicated to the most dangerous of emotions, yet it rises to galaxy-shaking power again and again. One of the primary reasons for this is The Sith's absolute devotion to the law of the jungle- only the strong survive, and it is the right of the strong to prey on the weak. Through perpetual conflict, the strong are pushed down the path of perfection. This is the reason The Sith have pressed the galaxy into war and oppression time and again, some for reasons of personal strength and gain, and others through a philosophy of forcing change and evolution. Morality and peace are only obstructions to this ultimate goal.

The Sith also believe that the path to power is to attain a more holistic view of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") by embracing their passions. Whereas [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") seek to overcome "negative" emotions such as pain, anger, and hate, The Sith welcome them as natural- and as fuel in their quest for power.

These are the fundamental beliefs of The Sith, but each one internalizes and acts on them differently. Take a moment to determine which of these beliefs are most important to your character and how they work to enforce them on others and on the galaxy as a whole.

#### Apprenticeship

When developing a Sith character, consider their schooling in [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"). How did they slip under the wing of their master? What was their apprenticeship like? Every Sith Apprentice stirs in the shadows of their master for many years as they are instructed in [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"). The nature of this apprenticeship varies, but it relies on cruelty and manipulation. The lessons are designed to nurture the consuming passions of the apprentice while cultivating things that lead to aggression, such as pain, malice, and revenge.

In addition, Sith training works to eradicate fear and compassion- two emotions that cripple the strong. That which a Sith Lord fears or finds compassion for has power over them, something which they must not abide. Thus, they are forced to confront and conquer their fears, usually in traumatic ways, and the things they love are stolen from them, one by one, until they are no longer tethered to anything but their devotion to The Sith.

Apprenticeship is extreme, requiring tireless years of training. During the period when the Rule of Two prevailed, one Sith Apprentice must be more than a match for several Jedi since The Sith do not have the luxury of numbers. Like most great predators, a single Sith Lord, even an apprentice, is something to be feared.

Most apprenticeships conclude the day the student is willing and able to commit the ultimate act of "selflessness" and devotion to The Sith- killing or destroying someone that they love. This action demonstrates their embodiment of unattached strength, power, and mercilessness. For example, [Darth Vader](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Vader "Darth Vader") sacrificed [Mace Windu](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mace_Windu "Mace Windu") and, ultimately, [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order"). [Darth Caedus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Caedus?action=edit&redlink=1 "Darth Caedus (page does not exist)") murdered his aunt, [Mara Jade Skywalker](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mara_Jade_Skywalker "Mara Jade Skywalker"). Once this act is complete, the apprentice may take on the title of Sith Lord and their new name.

Nothing is more formative in a Sith's life than their apprenticeship. When they become a Sith Lord, it is the Sith Lord that their master crafted them to be.

If you haven't already done so, consider who your character's master was and how they trained your character. What were their harshest lessons? What did your character have to do to conquer their fears? Who was the beloved person in your character's life that they murdered to finally join the ranks of The Sith?

In addition, apprenticeship is the time when a Sith begins to hone and direct his abilities. What are your character's first [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents")? Do they come from the [Armor Specialist Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor_Specialist_Talent_Tree "Armor Specialist Talent Tree"), reflecting a Sith who appreciates a striking suit of armor (such as [Darth Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Revan "Darth Revan") or [Darth Krayt](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Krayt "Darth Krayt"))? Do they come from the [Duelist Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Duelist_Talent_Tree "Duelist Talent Tree") or [Lightsaber Combat Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Combat_Talent_Tree "Lightsaber Combat Talent Tree"), reflecting a Sith who appreciates the elegance of their [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") (such as [Darth Tyranus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Tyranus "Darth Tyranus") or [Darth Vader](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Vader "Darth Vader"))? Do they from the [Alter Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Alter_Talent_Tree "Alter Talent Tree"), [Control Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Control_Talent_Tree "Control Talent Tree"), or [Sense Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sense_Talent_Tree "Sense Talent Tree"), reflecting a Sith who deeply connects with the power of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") (again, such as [Darth Tyranus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Tyranus "Darth Tyranus") or [Darth Vader](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Vader "Darth Vader")). Do they come from the [Dark Side Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Talent_Tree "Dark Side Talent Tree") or [Sith Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Talent_Tree "Sith Talent Tree"), reflecting an apprentice who strongly embraces the power of [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") (such as [Darth Sidious](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Sidious "Darth Sidious"))?

## Part 2: The Becoming

To symbolize the giving up of their old life, a Sith Apprentice takes on a new name. In all eras after [Darth Bane](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Bane "Darth Bane"), The Sith take on the honorific Darth, a title that caused jealousy and infighting in ancient times. Darth is often thought to be a contraction of Dark Lord of the Sith, the first recorded use of which was in the ancient times of [Darth Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Revan "Darth Revan") and [Darth Malak](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Malak "Darth Malak"). Some believe it derives from the [Rakata](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rakata "Rakata"), an ancient race of corrupt creatures whose horrifying Infinite Empire was a tool of [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"), possibly meaning "emperor" or "immortal." The name given to a Sith Lord by their master is often something associated with the dark passions he or she revels. Bane, Krayt (as in the terrifying dragons of [Tatooine](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tatooine "Tatooine")), Maul, Plagueis (as in plague), Sidious (as in insidious), and Tyranus (as in tyranny) are examples of things that appeal to The Sith.

In the days of the Rule of Two, the relationship of Apprentice and Master can end only one way- with the death of the Master. Few apprentices are content to wait for their master to die of natural causes to take on the mantle of power, and, as in the Sith legend of [Darth Plagueis](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Plagueis?action=edit&redlink=1 "Darth Plagueis (page does not exist)") the Wise, many Masters are killed by their apprentices once they have taught everything they know. Sith Masters are hardly fools and know that death at the hands of their pupils is a very real possibility, but they view this as a necessary danger to keep the Sith lineage strong.

Now is the time to create your character's Sith name. Most likely you'll be using the honorific Darth, but what sort of dark, poetic name did your character's Master gift them with? The examples here should provide a good baseline from which to work.

It is also time to determine the current state of your character's relationship with their Master. Are they still in your character's life, or are they dead? If they're in your character's life, what is their relationship? How do they guide and use your character as their apprentice? If they're dead, did they die by incident, or did your character kill them to seize their power?

All Sith share a deep hatred of [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"). They view the Light Side Order as their ancient enemies, the one force that has stood in their way time and time again and the one thing capable of defeating them. The Sith look forward to a day when they can truly wipe [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") from the galaxy once and for all, something [Darth Sidious](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Sidious "Darth Sidious") and [Darth Vader](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Vader "Darth Vader") thought they had accomplished.

Contemplate your character's attitude toward [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"). Do they actively seek to destroy them, or do they follow other pursuits (while remaining happy to rain down suffering on any Jedi who cross their path)?

### General Appearance

Typically, The Sith have a distinctive appearance. Because of their obsessions and their views, they most often choose to stand out. Whether it be in their style of dress (such as the common choice of intimidating black robes or battle dress), in their personal affectation (such as wearing the bones of their victims or simply a wild style of hair or makeup), or just in the way they comport themselves, The Sith do not blend in.

Then there are the eyes. The flooding power of [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") often changes the eyes of The Sith to a sickly yellow, sometimes rimmed with red. The more Dark Side power that a Sith wields, the more likely it is that the change to their eyes is permanent. The eyes of a Sith are unnerving and can become difficult to conceal.

Many Sith Lords tattoo themselves in accordance with ancient custom, with black or alternating red and black markings covering their entire body. Some choose to decorate only part of their body, but since the tattoos are a symbol of their devotion to the order, the markings must be visible to all. Therefore, facial tattoos are common. Tattooing according to Sith tradition is, unsurprisingly, a very painful process.

Those forced to hide in plain sight are the exception, pretending to be as normal, upstanding, and even kindly as possible (such as [Darth Sidious](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Sidious "Darth Sidious") or [Darth Tyranus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Tyranus "Darth Tyranus")). Of course, their true nature stirs unseen, always plotting. A Dark Side practitioner may relish in the shadow play, building their lies and illusions, until it reaches just the right pitch to drop the pretense and reveal his allegiance. Ultimately, Sith do not prefer to hide what they are.

There are many facets to creating a unique look for your Sith character. The first question to answer: do they choose to fit in, or have they fully embraced the ways of The Sith? If they choose to fit in, they will look just like everyone else most of the time. But like most Sith, they will have another face that they wear when among their own kind.

#### Six Categories

The next question to consider: how does your character stand out? Though unique, most Sith fall into one of six categories:

1. Some Sith inspire fear from inside a suit of dreadful armor, such as the faceless [Darth Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Revan "Darth Revan") or the [Yuuzhan Vong](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong "Yuuzhan Vong") encasement of [Darth Krayt](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Krayt "Darth Krayt"). Though they are certainly awe-inspiring on their own, the armor of these Sith is a recognizable symbol of their might.
2. Some Sith (such as [Darth Maul](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Maul "Darth Maul") or [Darth Talon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Talon "Darth Talon")) undergo the painful process of extensive Sith tattooing, wearing the markings as a badge of pride to inspire terror. Although few who encounter the Sith may remember the details of their face or manner of dress, the tattoos will forever be burned in their minds.
3. Some choose to (or are forced to) embrace a deathlike visage, such as [Darth Sidious](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Sidious "Darth Sidious") or [Darth Nihl](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Nihl "Darth Nihl"). Nearly every culture has a taboo about death, and most maintain some fear of it, so these Sith are instantly associated with that fear.
4. Some Sith choose to (or are forced to) embrace the otherworldly in their appearance, such as the ghostly [Darth Nihilus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Nihilus "Darth Nihilus") or the undead [Darth Sion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Sion "Darth Sion"). Many people fear the unknown, and stories of ghosts and monsters are carried in cultures across the galaxy- a fact that these Sith prey upon.
5. Some (such as [Darth Caedus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Caedus?action=edit&redlink=1 "Darth Caedus (page does not exist)") or [Darth Vader](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Vader "Darth Vader") before his defeat on [Mustafar](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mustafar "Mustafar")) choose simply to let the power and bearing of The Sith ooze from their every action and are marked only by sickly yellow eyes. Their predatory movements and ways are typically enough to inspire awe and dread in those who encounter them.
6. The appearance of some Sith is a function of tragedy, such as the cybernetic [Darth Vader](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Vader "Darth Vader") or the undead [Darth Sion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Sion "Darth Sion"). Vader was nearly killed on [Mustafar](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mustafar "Mustafar") by [Obi-Wan Kenobi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Obi-Wan_Kenobi "Obi-Wan Kenobi") and is sustained by extensive cybernetic augmentation; his frightening appearance is a function of his life-support system. Sion's body is broken, and he should be dead, but his hatred and powerful connection to [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") keep his body intact. The Sith of this category wear their wounds with pride, boasting that they have cheated death.

These categories are an excellent place to start. Once you have determined the basic thrust of your character's Sith appearance, you can play with the particulars and even mix and match.

However, as they say, the devil is in the details. What kind of clothes do they wear? In what state are their eyes? Do they sometimes appear normal, or has the power of [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") taken them for all time? If they have chosen to undergo the painful process of tattooing, what do the markings look like, and how do they display them? Do they have any unusual eccentricities in manner or habit? These questions are merely a start, limited only by your imagination.

As inspiration, you might wish to take a look at the famous Sith found in *[Threats of the Galaxy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threats_of_the_Galaxy "Threats of the Galaxy")*: [Darth Bane](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Bane "Darth Bane"), [Darth Maul](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Maul "Darth Maul"), and the Dark Lady [Lumiya](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lumiya "Lumiya").

## Part 3: Design

Not all Sith are fallen Jedi. There are as many paths to darkness as there are paths in life. One of the most infamous and powerful Sith was [Darth Sidious](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Sidious "Darth Sidious"), a man named Palpatine who was a noble and a politician (growing out of the [Noble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble "Noble") Heroic Class). Some were warriors or survivors of one sort or another, whose prowess and/or [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity") drew the attention of a Sith Lord (growing out of the [Soldier](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier "Soldier") or [Scout](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scout "Scout") Heroic Classes). Those who live in the shadows of society may already be inclined to the obsessions of The Sith simply due to the nature of their lifestyle, especially if they are Force-sensitive (growing out of the [Scoundrel](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrel "Scoundrel") Heroic Class).

Others are [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") who fell or those whose control of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") was developed outside [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") tradition. [Darth Vader](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Vader "Darth Vader"), the scourge of [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"), was in fact a powerful and respected one himself until the lure of [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") claimed him. Perhaps the easiest explanation for a Force-using character to have strayed down the path of The Sith is that he lived in a world where falling to [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") was a regular concern.

### Meeting the Requirements

The first threshold on the path of The Sith is to gain access to the [Sith Apprentice](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Apprentice "Sith Apprentice") Prestige Class. To do so, your character must be at least 7th level, have [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") as a [Trained Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skill "Trained Skill"), and have the [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity") and [Weapon Proficiency (Lightsabers)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Lightsabers) "Weapon Proficiency (Lightsabers)") Feats. They must also have acquired a [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") equal to their [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") and be a member of The Sith, but these are both accomplished through roleplaying. The [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity") Feat (which provides access to the [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") Skill) and the [Weapon Proficiency (Lightsabers)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Lightsabers) "Weapon Proficiency (Lightsabers)") Feat are available only as starting [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") to the [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") Heroic Class.

An easy way to speed you along your path is to have your character acquire at least one level in the [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") class. Remember that this Jedi training does not have to be as a part of the Order; it can be from a variety of [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions") similar to [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order") or simply be the first step of training by a Sith Master.

Don't want your character to have been a [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") or to have had [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi")-like training? You can meet the requirements using your nonclass dependent [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"), which are gained at 1st level, 3rd level, 6th level, and every three levels thereafter. At 1st level, by purchasing the [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity") Feat, you are allowed to treat [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") as a [Class Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class_Skill "Class Skill"), which you can then choose as one of your starting [Trained Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skills "Trained Skills"). Later, choose [Weapon Proficiency (Lightsabers)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Lightsabers) "Weapon Proficiency (Lightsabers)"), and you now have a character eligible for the [Sith Apprentice](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Apprentice "Sith Apprentice") Prestige Class, regardless of their background.

If your character is [Human](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Human "Human"), it's even easier because they receive one bonus [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat") and one bonus [Trained Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skill "Trained Skill") at creation. For example, you can choose [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity") and [Weapon Proficiency (Lightsabers)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Lightsabers) "Weapon Proficiency (Lightsabers)") as your character's starting [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") and make [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") your bonus [Trained Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skill "Trained Skill") (which they have access to through the former Feat).

The simplest option is to simply declare your character's first class to be [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi"), in which case you gain everything you need to eventually become Sith.

To develop [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") during this time, your character needs to meet two of the same requirements- having the [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity") Feat and being [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force"). Once you meet those requirements, you can select the [Force Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Training "Force Training") Feat. This Feat is not offered as a bonus Feat in any Heroic Class, so the only way to get it is through the [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") your character acquires as a result of Character Level, not Class Level.

#### Choosing a Class

Once you figure out how to meet the requirements for the [Sith Apprentice](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Apprentice "Sith Apprentice") Prestige Class, you have room to play. You have at least six, if not seven, levels.

- If you wish your Sith to be manipulative and deceitful, the [Noble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble "Noble") class is the way to go. Starting in the [Noble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble "Noble") Heroic Class nets you more [Trained Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skills "Trained Skills") than any other Heroic Class, among which are [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception"), [Gather Information](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gather_Information "Gather Information"), and [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion").
- Starting in the [Scout](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scout "Scout") Heroic Class nets you the second largest number of [Trained Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skills "Trained Skills"), among which are those that make a fiercely independent Sith, such as [Climb](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Climb "Climb"), [Endurance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Endurance "Endurance"), [Jump](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jump "Jump"), [Survival](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Survival "Survival"), and [Swim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swim "Swim"), as well as the [Shake It Off](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shake_It_Off "Shake It Off") Feat.
- The [Scoundrel](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrel "Scoundrel") Heroic Class, as a starting class, nets you the manipulative ways of the [Noble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble "Noble") (though with fewer [Trained Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skills "Trained Skills")), adding the benefits of [Acrobatics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatics "Acrobatics") and [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") for those Sith wishing to be shadow warriors.
- For those on the path of a deadly predator, starting in the [Soldier](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier "Soldier") Heroic Class is the way to go. Though your character won't have many [Trained Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skills "Trained Skills"), they will be geared toward aspects of combat, and the sheer number of starting weapon proficiencies makes up for it.

These guidelines are appropriate for choosing the Heroic Class in which to begin your character's path towards The Sith. Once you begin to [Multiclass](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Multiclass "Multiclass"), it becomes more about the [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") and [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents"). We'll discuss those more in Possible Paths, below.

#### Choosing a Species

Some [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") are predisposed to The Sith or have natural advantages that lend themselves well to the darker path.

- [Bothans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bothans "Bothans") can make dangerous Sith, but only when the Rule of Two is in place. Their manipulative and crafty natures make them vicious in a world of stealth and guile. If they have [Gather Information](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gather_Information "Gather Information") as a [Trained Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skill "Trained Skill"), they gain [Skill Focus (Gather Information)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Focus_(Gather_Information) "Skill Focus (Gather Information)") as a bonus Feat, and they have access to [The Bothan SpyNet](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Bothan_SpyNet "The Bothan SpyNet").
- Some [Quarren](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Quarren "Quarren") who endured the devastation of their homeworld during the Galactic Civil War live with so much pain and hate that they might be drawn naturally to the ways of [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side").
- Natural hunters, Force-sensitive [Rodians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rodians "Rodians") can find their aggression and tenacity bringing them toward the world of a Sith Warrior. Their Heightened Awareness and Low-Light Vision can be of great benefit to the [Rodian](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rodian "Rodian") Sith Warrior, giving them the ability to notice danger before many others and to strike more effectively under cover of darkness. However, they are poor deceivers (thanks to their -2 [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma")) and are better suited for more violent pursuits.
- [Sullustans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sullustans "Sullustans") are a race whose curiosity can easily lead to recklessness, and it is those same traits that can bring about the downfall of the [Sullustan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sullustan "Sullustan") Jedi who finds themselves fascinated by the ways of [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"). Like [Rodians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rodians "Rodians"), their Darkvision and Heightened Awareness can be an advantage for the Sith Warrior, giving them the ability to notice danger in advance and even more utility for striking from the shadows.
- The violent and driven nature of the [Trandoshans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trandoshans "Trandoshans") lends itself well to the path of The Sith, as do their natural combative abilities. They make deadly Sith Warriors, with their Darkvision, Limb Regeneration, and Natural Armor abilities, plus the bonus [Toughness](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Toughness "Toughness") Feat. They can take more punishment than most.
- Though [Twi'leks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Twi%27leks "Twi'leks") often try to avoid open conflict, their tendency to live in the shadows can bring those who master [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to the stealth and guile of The Sith who live in the ages of the Rule of Two. Their +2 [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") and Deceptive ability make them masters of manipulation.
- The adventurous nature of the [Zabrak](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Zabrak "Zabrak") likewise can be drawn to explore the mysteries of [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"), and their adaptation to living in a tough environment serves them well as Sith Warriors (as evidenced by [Darth Maul](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Maul "Darth Maul")). Their Heightened Awareness and Superior Defenses abilities are of particular use.
- Still, [Humans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Humans "Humans") make up the bulk of The Sith. The natural chaos of their emotions is capable of producing the warping passion necessary to create a Sith Lord. Many of the most powerful, terrifying, and notorious Sith Lords through history have been [Human](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Human "Human") or [Near-Human](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Near-Human "Near-Human"), including [Exar Kun](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exar_Kun "Exar Kun"), [Darth Malak](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Malak "Darth Malak"), [Darth Bane](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Bane "Darth Bane"), [Darth Sidious](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Sidious "Darth Sidious"), [Darth Vader](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Vader "Darth Vader"), and [Darth Krayt](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Krayt "Darth Krayt"). Furthermore, their bonus [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat") and bonus [Trained Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skill "Trained Skill") give them more room to play when developing on their way to [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side").

### Possible Paths

Although there are many ways to craft a Sith, this section covers three main paths: the Sith Manipulator, the Sith Warrior, and the Fallen Jedi. Let's walk through the start of each kind of character through the final step of becoming a [Sith Apprentice](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Apprentice "Sith Apprentice") at 7th level in terms of [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills"), [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"), [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents"), and [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers").

#### The Sith Manipulator

At 1st level, this character begins in the [Noble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble "Noble") Heroic Class. They begin with [Linguist](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Linguist "Linguist"), [Weapon Proficiency (Pistols)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Pistols) "Weapon Proficiency (Pistols)"), and [Weapon Proficiency (Simple Weapons)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Simple_Weapons) "Weapon Proficiency (Simple Weapons)") as starting [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"), plus the [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity") Feat. For [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents"), they begin with **[Wealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wealth "Wealth")** from the [Lineage Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lineage_Talent_Tree "Lineage Talent Tree"). They also begin with [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception"), [Gather Information](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gather_Information "Gather Information"), [Knowledge (Bureaucracy)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Bureaucracy) "Knowledge (Bureaucracy)"), [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception"), [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion"), and [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") as [Trained Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skills "Trained Skills") (plus any additional based on their [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") bonus). The [Corruption](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Corruption "Corruption") Destiny is their path.

By the time they reach 3rd level, they have added the [Strong in the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strong_in_the_Force "Strong in the Force") and [Weapon Proficiency (Lightsabers)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Lightsabers) "Weapon Proficiency (Lightsabers)") Feats, as well as the **[Presence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Presence "Presence")** Talent from the [Influence Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Influence_Talent_Tree "Influence Talent Tree").

By 5th level, they have increased both their [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") and [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") attributes by one point each. They also add the [Force Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Training "Force Training") Feat and the **[Weaken Resolve](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weaken_Resolve "Weaken Resolve")** Talent from the [Influence Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Influence_Talent_Tree "Influence Talent Tree"). The [Force Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Training "Force Training") Feat confers one [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power"), *[Mind Trick](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mind_Trick "Mind Trick")* (plus, most likely, a few extra based on the character's [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") bonus).

At 7th level, they complete the [Noble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble "Noble") Heroic Class and enter the [Sith Apprentice](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Apprentice "Sith Apprentice") Prestige Class, finally reaching their goal. They either have behaved in such a way to garner the appropriate number of [Dark Side Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Points "Dark Side Points") or will automatically get them by falling to the corruption of The Sith and completing their [Destiny](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny "Destiny"). They have added the **[Force Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Deception "Force Deception")** Talent from the [Sith Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Talent_Tree "Sith Talent Tree") and the [Force Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Training "Force Training") Feat twice more, giving them *[Force Lightning](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Lightning "Force Lightning")* and *[Rebuke](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebuke "Rebuke")* (plus possible others based on their [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") bonus). They also choose a new [Destiny](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny "Destiny"), either [Destruction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destruction "Destruction") or [Discovery](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Discovery "Discovery").

#### The Sith Warrior

At 1st level, this character begins in the [Soldier](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier "Soldier") Heroic Class. They begin with [Armor Proficiency (Light)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor_Proficiency_(Light) "Armor Proficiency (Light)"), [Armor Proficiency (Medium)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor_Proficiency_(Medium) "Armor Proficiency (Medium)"), [Weapon Proficiency (Pistols)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Pistols) "Weapon Proficiency (Pistols)"), [Weapon Proficiency (Rifles)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Rifles) "Weapon Proficiency (Rifles)"), and [Weapon Proficiency (Simple Weapons)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Simple_Weapons) "Weapon Proficiency (Simple Weapons)") as starting [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"), plus the [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity") Feat. For [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents"), they begin with **[Armored Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armored_Defense "Armored Defense")** from the [Armor Specialist Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor_Specialist_Talent_Tree "Armor Specialist Talent Tree"). They also begin with [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative"), [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception"), and [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") as [Trained Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skills "Trained Skills") (plus any additional [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills") based on their [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") bonus). The [Corruption](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Corruption "Corruption") Destiny is their path.

By the time they reach 3rd level, they have added the [Weapon Focus (Lightsabers)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus_(Lightsabers) "Weapon Focus (Lightsabers)") and [Weapon Proficiency (Lightsabers)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Lightsabers) "Weapon Proficiency (Lightsabers)") Feats, as well as the **[Penetrating Attack (Lightsabers)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Penetrating_Attack_(Lightsabers) "Penetrating Attack (Lightsabers)")** Talent from the [Weapon Specialist Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Specialist_Talent_Tree "Weapon Specialist Talent Tree").

By 5th level, they have increased both their [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") and [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") attributes by one point each. They also add the [Force Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Training "Force Training") Feat and the **[Power of the Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Power_of_the_Dark_Side "Power of the Dark Side")** Talent from the [Dark Side Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Talent_Tree "Dark Side Talent Tree"). The [Force Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Training "Force Training") Feat confers one [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power"), *[Dark Rage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Rage "Dark Rage")* (plus possible others based on the character's [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") bonus).

At 7th level, they complete the [Soldier](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier "Soldier") Heroic Class and enters the [Sith Apprentice](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Apprentice "Sith Apprentice") Prestige Class, finally reaching their goal. Like the Sith Manipulator, they either have behaved in such a way to garner the appropriate number of [Dark Side Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Points "Dark Side Points") or will automatically get them by falling to the corruption of The Sith and completing their [Destiny](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny "Destiny"). They also add the **[Deflect](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deflect "Deflect")** Talent from the [Lightsaber Combat Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Combat_Talent_Tree "Lightsaber Combat Talent Tree"), either the [Dual Weapon Mastery I](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dual_Weapon_Mastery_I "Dual Weapon Mastery I") or [Power Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Power_Attack "Power Attack") Feat (depending on whether their [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") or [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") qualifies), and the [Force Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Training "Force Training") Feat, which gives them the *[Force Slam](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Slam "Force Slam")* Force Power. They also choose a new [Corruption](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Corruption "Corruption") or [Destruction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destruction "Destruction") Destiny.

#### The Fallen Jedi

At 1st level, this character begins in the [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") Heroic Class. They begin with [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity"), [Weapon Proficiency (Lightsabers)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Lightsabers) "Weapon Proficiency (Lightsabers)"), and [Weapon Proficiency (Simple Weapons)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Simple_Weapons) "Weapon Proficiency (Simple Weapons)") as starting [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"), plus the [Force Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Training "Force Training") Feat, which yields them the Force Power *[Surge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surge "Surge")* (plus possible others based on their [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") bonus). For [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents"), they begin with the **[Deflect](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deflect "Deflect")** Talent from the [Lightsaber Combat Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Combat_Talent_Tree "Lightsaber Combat Talent Tree"). They also begin with [Acrobatics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatics "Acrobatics") and [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") as [Trained Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skills "Trained Skills") (plus any additional [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills") based on their [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") bonus). The [Corruption](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Corruption "Corruption") Destiny is their path.

By the time they reach 3rd level, they have added the [Force Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Training "Force Training") Feat twice, which gives them the Force Powers *[Mind Trick](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mind_Trick "Mind Trick")* and *[Move Object](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Object "Move Object")* (plus possible others based on their [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") modifier) and the **[Force Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Perception "Force Perception")** Talent from the [Sense Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sense_Talent_Tree "Sense Talent Tree").

By 5th level, they have increased both their [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") and [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") attributes by one point each. They have also added the [Force Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Training "Force Training") Feat, giving them the Force Power *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")*, as well as the **[Visions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Visions "Visions")** Talent from the [Sense Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sense_Talent_Tree "Sense Talent Tree").

At 7th level, they complete the [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") Heroic Class and enters the [Sith Apprentice](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Apprentice "Sith Apprentice") Prestige Class, finally reaching their goal. Like the Sith Manipulator, they either have behaved in such a way to garner the appropriate number of [Dark Side Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Points "Dark Side Points") or will automatically get them by falling to the corruption of The Sith and completing their [Destiny](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny "Destiny"). They have also added the **[Dark Scourge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Scourge "Dark Scourge")** Talent from the [Sith Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Talent_Tree "Sith Talent Tree") as well as the [Force Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Training "Force Training") Feat twice, which gives them the Force Powers *[Force Grip](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Grip "Force Grip")* and *[Negate Energy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Negate_Energy "Negate Energy")* (plus any additional [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") based on their [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") bonus). They also choose a new [Corruption](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Corruption "Corruption") or [Destruction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destruction "Destruction") Destiny.

## Part 4: The Teeth in the Darkness

Previous articles looked at the essence of The Sith, choosing an appropriate name and appearance for your Sith character, and the game mechanics of designing that character. In this article, we delve into the technology used by Sith, the kinds of characters that best fit in the different eras, and ways to advance beyond the [Sith Apprentice](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Apprentice "Sith Apprentice") Prestige Class.

### Technology

The most notable piece of technology associated with a Sith is the [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber"). This ancient and elegant weapon is the tool of both [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") and The Sith.

To [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"), the crafting of a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") is a sacred task. Apprentices are taken to the ritual caves that hold the crystals necessary to create the focused core of the Jedi's weapon. The assembly is part technical, part manipulation of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). When it is done, the color of a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") depends on the type of crystal used in its creation. Yellow, blue, and green are common. Some instead hunt far and wide for their crystal, with results (such as Mace Windu's violet [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber")) that are the stuff of legends.

To The Sith, this is needlessly ritualistic. A [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") is a tool like any other, and all that matters is its wielder. For this reason, The Sith artificially grow the crystals for their [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers"). The outcome of such is consistent and distinct- all Sith [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers") are red.

The [Double-Bladed Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double-Bladed_Lightsaber "Double-Bladed Lightsaber") is the notorious weapon wielded by [Darth Maul](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Maul "Darth Maul") during [The Rise of the Empire Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Rise_of_the_Empire_Era "The Rise of the Empire Era"). These tricky weapons take time to master, but they can make one man fight as two. Some mistakenly believe that [Darth Maul](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Maul "Darth Maul") created the weapon, when in fact it was first unleashed under the fury of the Sith Lord [Exar Kun](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exar_Kun "Exar Kun") in ancient times. Those Sith Lords who lean toward the savagery of physical violence may find themselves drawn to the artistry and deadliness of the [Double-Bladed Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double-Bladed_Lightsaber "Double-Bladed Lightsaber").

Normally, attacking with both ends of a [Double-Bladed Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double-Bladed_Lightsaber "Double-Bladed Lightsaber") incurs a -10 penalty to both attacks. The Dual Weapon Mastery [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") will dramatically reduce that penalty. [Dual Weapon Mastery I](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dual_Weapon_Mastery_I "Dual Weapon Mastery I"), which reduces the penalty to -5, can be taken during character creation. [Dual Weapon Mastery II](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dual_Weapon_Mastery_II "Dual Weapon Mastery II") and [Dual Weapon Mastery III](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dual_Weapon_Mastery_III "Dual Weapon Mastery III") require higher [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") scores and advanced levels to acquire. Unless your character has a high [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") modifier or the [Weapon Focus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus "Weapon Focus") Feat, [Dual Weapon Mastery II](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dual_Weapon_Mastery_II "Dual Weapon Mastery II") is what's needed to be truly deadly. Since this Feat requires a high [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity"), the [Weapon Finesse](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Finesse "Weapon Finesse") Feat can offset these penalties nicely. The Force Power *[Dark Rage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Rage "Dark Rage")* also helps.

The [Sith Lanvarok](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Lanvarok "Sith Lanvarok") is a wrist-mounted projectile weapon from ancient times, exceedingly rare by the days of [The Clone Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Clone_Wars "The Clone Wars"). Inaccurate and with a long reload time, the [Sith Lanvarok](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Lanvarok "Sith Lanvarok") hurled a spray of metal discs to distract an enemy or as a weapon of first attack. However, Sith could use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to guide the discs, making them far more dangerous than the weapon would be in the hands of a normal wielder.

The Sith, like [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"), travel light. As long as they have robes, a cloak (probably [All-Temperature](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/All-Temperature_Cloak "All-Temperature Cloak")), and a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber"), they are content. Some also travel with currency and a [Utility Belt](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Utility_Belt "Utility Belt") that might contain three days' worth of food capsules, a [Medpac](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Medpac "Medpac"), a [Tool Kit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tool_Kit "Tool Kit"), a spare [Power Pack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Power_Pack "Power Pack"), a spare [Energy Cell](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Energy_Cell "Energy Cell"), a [Glow Rod](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Glow_Rod "Glow Rod"), a [Liquid Cable Dispenser](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Liquid_Cable_Dispenser "Liquid Cable Dispenser") with grappling hook, an [Aquata Breather](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aquata_Breather "Aquata Breather"), [Electrobinoculars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Electrobinoculars "Electrobinoculars"), or a few specialized [Tools](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tools "Tools") with which to do one's dark will. The Sith are happy to rely on their wits and their exceptional power.

Certain [Starfighters](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starfighters "Starfighters") might appeal to a Sith Lord. Those who were once Jedi may prefer a Jedi fighter to other craft. Those who served before the days of [The Clone Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Clone_Wars "The Clone Wars") would pilot the [Delta-7 *Aethersprite* Interceptor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Delta-7_Aethersprite_Interceptor "Delta-7 Aethersprite Interceptor"), whereas those who fought in [The Clone Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Clone_Wars "The Clone Wars") would prefer the [Eta-2 *Actis* Interceptor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Eta-2_Actis_Interceptor "Eta-2 Actis Interceptor"). Those in the favor of [Count Dooku](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Count_Dooku "Count Dooku") might also receive one of the rare and exotic [*Ginivex*-Class Starfighters](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ginivex-Class_Starfighters "Ginivex-Class Starfighters"). During [The Old Republic Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic_Era "The Old Republic Era"), The Sith had their own [Starfighters](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starfighters "Starfighters"), known as [Sith Interceptors](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Interceptors "Sith Interceptors"). Only one craft was designed for The Sith in [The Rise of the Empire Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Rise_of_the_Empire_Era "The Rise of the Empire Era"), [The Rebellion Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Rebellion_Era "The Rebellion Era"), and [The New Jedi Order Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_New_Jedi_Order_Era "The New Jedi Order Era")- a prototype that is owned by [Darth Sidious](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Sidious "Darth Sidious") and carefully guarded. The [Sith Infiltrator](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Infiltrator "Sith Infiltrator") is truly a craft worthy of the tradition of the Sith Lords.

Planet-side, Sith Lords prefer [Speeder Bikes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speeder_Bikes "Speeder Bikes") for their speed and maneuverability. The reflexes of a Force-user also make the bikes far less dangerous and more effective than they might be for the average person. For similar reasons, when bikes are impractical, Sith instead might choose racing or military [Speeders](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speeders "Speeders").

### Eras

The era in which you choose to play has great bearing on what kind of Sith your character can be.

[The Old Republic Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic_Era "The Old Republic Era") can call for any kind of Sith because they are prevalent in the universe. There will be more Sith Warriors than any other kind; the strongest of The Sith are those who rule. Characters with strong backgrounds in the [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi"), [Soldier](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier "Soldier"), and perhaps [Scout](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scout "Scout") Heroic Classes will be the most common, favoring [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills") and [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") designed to enhance combat ability and [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers").

[The Rise of the Empire Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Rise_of_the_Empire_Era "The Rise of the Empire Era"), [The Rebellion Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Rebellion_Era "The Rebellion Era"), and [The New Jedi Order Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_New_Jedi_Order_Era "The New Jedi Order Era") call for the Rule of Two, which requires the ways of stealth and guile. However, all Sith are trained as warriors as well, since one Sith must be a match for several Jedi. Characters with backgrounds in either the [Noble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble "Noble") or [Scoundrel](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrel "Scoundrel") Heroic Classes, balanced with [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") and either [Soldier](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier "Soldier") or [Scout](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scout "Scout"), are more the order of the day, favoring skills of manipulation and intimidation as well as those of combat.

[The Legacy Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Legacy_Era "The Legacy Era") begins with the Rule of Two and follows the same maxims as the previous three eras. However, once [Darth Krayt](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Krayt "Darth Krayt") takes his rightful place in the universe, The Sith flood out again. Sith Warriors again are common, as they were in [The Old Republic Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic_Era "The Old Republic Era").

### Advancement Paths

Once you have arrived at the [Sith Apprentice](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Apprentice "Sith Apprentice") Prestige Class, your character is now true Sith. Its been a bit of a journey so far, but where do you go from here?

Unless you have chosen a diverse array of [Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Classes "Classes") (either Prestige or Heroic) as your character's background, you have only a few directions in which to grow. The four [Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent_Trees "Talent Trees") from which a [Sith Apprentice](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Apprentice "Sith Apprentice") draws are the [Armor Specialist Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor_Specialist_Talent_Tree "Armor Specialist Talent Tree"), the [Duelist Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Duelist_Talent_Tree "Duelist Talent Tree"), the [Lightsaber Combat Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Combat_Talent_Tree "Lightsaber Combat Talent Tree"), and the [Sith Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Talent_Tree "Sith Talent Tree"), the only unique tree in this class. Your character's abilities will now aim toward vicious combat and growing in [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force").

If you have chosen a diversity of [Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Classes "Classes") as your character's background, you'll have more room to play. You can continue to develop your character in other [Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Classes "Classes") that can support their growth as a Sith, using the guidelines presented in Part 3: Design.

One unique way your character can develop is with the [Dreadful Countenance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dreadful_Countenance "Dreadful Countenance") Feat. It is a Feat solely devoted to the way Sith change in appearance and the effect it has on them. The requirements for the Feat are a [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") of 13 and membership in The Sith tradition, the latter accomplished by reaching the [Sith Apprentice](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Apprentice "Sith Apprentice") Prestige Class.

Upon becoming a member of the [Sith Apprentice](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Apprentice "Sith Apprentice") Prestige Class, your character will have access to [Force Techniques](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Techniques "Force Techniques"), which represent a deeper understanding of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). Starting at 2nd level, they gain a new [Force Technique](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Technique "Force Technique") every other level, improving and extending other mechanical uses of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force").

As your [Sith Apprentice](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Apprentice "Sith Apprentice") grows in levels, they gain access to the more advanced [Prestige Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Classes "Prestige Classes"). The most obvious place to go is the [Sith Lord](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Lord "Sith Lord") Prestige Class. There are only two requirements that differ from the [Sith Apprentice](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Apprentice "Sith Apprentice"): your character must be at least 12th level and must know at least one [Force Technique](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Technique "Force Technique"). At this point, your character is purged of fear and begins to learn the secrets of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), which allow them to start using [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") in new and powerful ways, but, unlike [Force Techniques](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Techniques "Force Techniques"), they cost [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") to use. At 2nd level and every level of [Sith Lord](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Lord "Sith Lord") thereafter, your character gains one [Force Secret](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Secret "Force Secret"). Levels in the [Sith Lord](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Lord "Sith Lord") Prestige Class do not offer access to new [Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent_Trees "Talent Trees"), instead continuing to develop in the [Sith Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Talent_Tree "Sith Talent Tree") and [Force Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Talent_Trees "Force Talent Trees"). However, your character becomes adept at using the dread tradition known as Dun Möch, which lets them tempt others to [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side").

The [Force Disciple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Disciple "Force Disciple") is another advanced Prestige Class that might be an option for a growing Sith. This class represents a Sith who wishes to delve more deeply into the mysteries of [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"), becoming a prophet. Your character becomes immune to [Mind-Affecting](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mind-Affecting "Mind-Affecting") effects, gains prophetic visions, and gains access to [Force Secrets](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Secrets "Force Secrets"). Their [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") come from those available to a [Force Adept](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Adept "Force Adept") and from [Force Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Talent_Trees "Force Talent Trees"). In addition to the requirements of the [Sith Apprentice](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Apprentice "Sith Apprentice") Prestige Class, the [Force Disciple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Disciple "Force Disciple") Prestige Class requires that your character is 12th level and has the Force Power *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")*. Also, it requires at least three levels of the [Force Adept](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Adept "Force Adept") Prestige Class, since there is no other way to acquire two [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") from the [Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent_Trees "Talent Trees") offered there.

Another interesting option, if you are willing to take the extra time, is to have your character work their way up through [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") ranks to reach the [Jedi Master](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Master "Jedi Master") Prestige Class. The only requirements beyond those needed to reach [Sith Apprentice](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Apprentice "Sith Apprentice") are that your character is 12th level, has one [Force Technique](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Technique "Force Technique") (easily gained through two levels of [Jedi Knight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Knight "Jedi Knight")), and is a member of [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"). They become fearless, gains meditative focus, and learn [Force Secrets](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Secrets "Force Secrets"). The [Jedi Master](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Master "Jedi Master") Prestige Class confers no new [Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent_Trees "Talent Trees"), instead focusing on the [Duelist Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Duelist_Talent_Tree "Duelist Talent Tree") and [Force Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Talent_Trees "Force Talent Trees"). In general, if your character follows this path to [Sith Lord](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Lord "Sith Lord"), it will take longer, but they will have access to a variety of Force-related powers and abilities to make up for it. Just imagine the power of a [Jedi Master](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Master "Jedi Master") who has advanced through the ranks of the [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") and [Jedi Knight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Knight "Jedi Knight") Classes- they will be very dangerous when they finally fall to [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side").

If your character has already fulfilled their [Destiny](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny "Destiny") and taken on the mantle of Sith Master, the [Education](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Education "Education") Destiny might be in order. Perhaps they will play an important part in training a dreadful Sith who has their own [Destiny](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny "Destiny") to wreak upon the galaxy. The bonus for fulfilling this [Destiny](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny "Destiny") is a +5 bonus to one [Class Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class_Skill "Class Skill") of your choice, which is particularly useful when applied to [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force").

## Part 5: Antiheroes

In the last several articles, we examined how to create a Sith character from the ground up using *Saga Edition* mechanics. But what if you're a Gamemaster and you want to include Sith characters in your playing group?

Your first consideration involves how many Sith you'll have in the group. Some groups might be composed of all Sith, and others might feature only one or a few Sith while the rest are characters who compliment them. There are advantages and challenges to both.

### Group Origins

The first quandary to solve is how the character group came to be. Why are they working together? Maybe they have common goals or common enemies. How did they come together? Perhaps they had masters who were comrades, or maybe they stumbled across one another during a mission.

#### All-Sith Groups

The advantage to creating All-Sith Groups is they are cut from the same cloth. They are adherents to [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") and share solidarity of philosophy and purpose. However, the danger with All-Sith Groups is obvious- in-fighting. The strongest Sith rules, and those under them will no doubt test them. Some Sith are satisfied with their place, while others constantly strive for authority.

You could agree with your group that one of them is in charge so that dissent doesn't percolate within the ranks, though such an approach is hardly realistic given Sith nature. Another option is to have the characters be equals who are under the thumb of a significantly stronger Sith. But what happens if the alpha dog is vanquished? Try to work out such concerns in advance unless you desire a group of duplicitous characters who are at each others' throats. Whether or not you like this dynamic, you should steer your players toward such behavior, even if they don't gravitate toward it themselves. It isn't optional; it's the nature of The Sith.

Perhaps the best way to deal with this problem is simply to strike an understanding among your players and build such competition into the plot. If your players intentionally create rivalries, with an agreement not to let them get in the way of broader gameplay, they can portray their Sith characters appropriately without obstructing the game or creating bad blood. In short, work it out and turn it into part of the story instead of leaving it to whims.

#### Groups With One Sith

On the other hand, the advantage to creating groups with only one or a few Sith characters is that the other characters won't typically care about whether the strongest is leading. These kind of groups can create a more harmonious dynamic, but remember, The Sith are selfish and serve themselves above all others. Because of their belief in strength and superiority, The Sith might begin to treat the other characters as lackeys and playthings. Of course, this can prove tremendously entertaining for the Gamemaster, and even the players, if the other characters would not normally choose to be in league with a Sith. How will they react once they unravel the identity of their new friend? How will they react once they come to understand that they've struck a deal with the devil in order to succeed in a mission?

Again, the best solution to these problems is to work things out ahead of time. Depending on the type of game, some characters might not mind being lackeys of The Sith- especially if the rewards for service are significant. The Sith can promise many things that appeal to many morally questionable individuals. If the rest of the characters are unaware that they've struck a bargain with a Sith, determine how that might play out before you get there. Otherwise, it is almost guaranteed to disrupt your group and the long-term goals of the game. Building this kind of plot twist into your story will create drama instead of chaos.

The type of group you create also depends upon the era in which you choose to play. In eras where The Sith are plentiful (like [The Old Republic Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic_Era "The Old Republic Era") or [The Legacy Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Legacy_Era "The Legacy Era")), All-Sith Groups are the order of the day and capable of operating openly. In eras dominated by the Rule of Two (like [The Rise of the Empire Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Rise_of_the_Empire_Era "The Rise of the Empire Era"), [The Rebellion Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Rebellion_Era "The Rebellion Era"), or [The New Jedi Order Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_New_Jedi_Order_Era "The New Jedi Order Era")), it makes sense to have one Sith in a group of characters who work within his or her plans. For example, in [The Rise of the Empire Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Rise_of_the_Empire_Era "The Rise of the Empire Era") or [The Rebellion Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Rebellion_Era "The Rebellion Era"), a group might consist of one Sith and several Dark Jedi or Dark Side [Force Adepts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Adepts "Force Adepts").

### Adventure Hooks

Once you have your group figured out, it's time to play. In games revolving around The Sith, even more so than in other games, it's important to give the characters challenges and goals. It might be tempting to give Sith space to mess around as the dark terrors that they are, but without serious challenges and plans, they will eventually turn on each other in boredom.

In eras where the Rule of Two is predominant, you will have to create adventures in which The Sith play it safe and have long-reaching goals. The other trick here is to create such adventures in a way that does not violate *Star Wars* canon. In eras where The Sith dominate, you can easily create adventures in such a way that the characters float from confrontation to confrontation without worry of stealth or canon.

On that note, here are a few adventure hooks to give you guidelines. The hooks are categorized by era, but some might be able to be modified to work in other eras.

#### [The Old Republic Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic_Era "The Old Republic Era")

In an era dominated by The Sith, one kind of adventure revolves around the quest for the legacy of The Sith. This involves hunting down hidden places of Sith power long abandoned by the rest of the galaxy, chasing down legends to uncover lost secrets, and experimenting with forgotten and perhaps even forbidden power and alchemy.

As a specific example, your Sith characters might stumble across an ancient [Holocron](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Holocron "Holocron") after taking a Jedi outpost or vessel. [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") did not open the [Holocron](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Holocron "Holocron") because doing so requires opening oneself up to [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"); instead, they hoped to bury it in their archives forever. However, your group opens the [Holocron](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Holocron "Holocron") and discovers the secrets within, which speak of an insignificant moon named [Oricon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Oricon?action=edit&redlink=1 "Oricon (page does not exist)") lost now in [The Outer Rim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Outer_Rim?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Outer Rim (page does not exist)"). An ancient Sith named [Darth Vitus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Vitus?action=edit&redlink=1 "Darth Vitus (page does not exist)") used the moon as a place to experiment with and expand his already significant knowledge of Sith Alchemy. The [Holocron](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Holocron "Holocron") provides hints on how to find [Oricon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Oricon?action=edit&redlink=1 "Oricon (page does not exist)"), and the hunt is an adventure on its own. Once the moon is found, the characters face the descendants of Darth Vitus's experiments- horrible monsters, many of which are strong in the power of [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"). The characters might eventually find Darth Vitus's compound, in which they could uncover powerful secrets to aid them in their war against [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi").

#### [The Rise of the Empire Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Rise_of_the_Empire_Era "The Rise of the Empire Era")

In an era exemplified by the Rule of Two, it will be challenging to create adventures that don't violate canon. As we know from the *Star Wars* films, [Darth Sidious](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Sidious "Darth Sidious") rules The Sith in this era, with only three apprentices that we know of- first [Darth Maul](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Maul "Darth Maul"), then [Darth Tyranus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Tyranus "Darth Tyranus"), then [Darth Vader](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Vader "Darth Vader"). However, we also know that [Darth Vader](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Vader "Darth Vader") had a secret apprentice, and it's possible for Sith groups in different parts of the galaxy to have reason to keep their existence a secret, even from other Sith.

As a specific example, your group could be composed of a secret Sith Apprentice trained by [Darth Vader](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Vader "Darth Vader") to hunt the last of the remaining Jedi (effectively using the basis for the *Star Wars: The Force Unleashed* video game). Underneath the secret Sith Apprentice could be a group of Dark Jedi, finally able to be out in the open under the Emperor's reign, or a group of [Clone Troopers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Troopers "Clone Troopers") trained to fight such a worthy prey. This hunt begins on the trail of [Sarn Vals](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sarn_Vals?action=edit&redlink=1 "Sarn Vals (page does not exist)"), a [Quarren](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Quarren "Quarren") Jedi Knight, whose presence was implicated after a mysterious disturbance in [The Corporate Sector](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Corporate_Sector?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Corporate Sector (page does not exist)").

#### [The Rebellion Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Rebellion_Era "The Rebellion Era")

Again, this era suffers the restrictions of canon and the Rule of Two. However, a situation similar to the one proposed in [The Dark Times](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Times "The Dark Times") would work just as well. [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") are all but gone, so a secret Sith Apprentice might lead special strike teams against [The Rebel Alliance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Rebel_Alliance "The Rebel Alliance"). Most likely, such teams will be composed of Dark Jedi or elite [Stormtroopers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stormtroopers "Stormtroopers"). You still have the option of creating groups in the fringes of the Empire that want nothing to do with the plans of [Darth Sidious](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Sidious "Darth Sidious").

As a specific example, your group might be one of Darth Vader's pet teams, led by his secret apprentice. Since [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") are no longer a threat, Vader has given the group the most sensitive and distasteful missions in the Empire. Through the capture and interrogation of a [Bothan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bothan "Bothan") spy, he has learned that the Rebellion is being aided on the distant planet of [Rentean](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rentean?action=edit&redlink=1 "Rentean (page does not exist)") in [The Outer Rim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Outer_Rim?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Outer Rim (page does not exist)"). The [Human](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Human "Human") inhabitants of the planet are secretly building [Starfighters](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starfighters "Starfighters") for the Alliance. The group is sent in to infiltrate the secret shipbuilding facility, destroy it, and teach the locals what it means to aid the enemies of the Empire- a lesson they won't soon forget.

#### [The New Jedi Order Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_New_Jedi_Order_Era "The New Jedi Order Era")

You need clever ideas to create workable Sith adventures for this era, since canon limits it greatly. Any Sith would have to have been hidden for some time or Dark Jedi who stumbled across [Sith Holocrons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Holocrons "Sith Holocrons") or something similar. Their activities must remain secret; canon shows that the Dark Lady [Lumiya](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lumiya "Lumiya") is the closest thing to Sith encountered in this era by [The New Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_New_Republic "The New Republic"). However, [Lumiya](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lumiya "Lumiya") herself encounters Sith on [Korriban](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Korriban "Korriban") during [The Legacy Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Legacy_Era "The Legacy Era"), and they most certainly would be active during the Yuuzhan Vong Invasion.

As a specific example, your group could consist of several Sith who never subscribed to the Rule of Two but were unable to operate openly due to the enormous power and long reach of [Darth Sidious](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Sidious "Darth Sidious"). Now that he's gone, these Sith grow in power on [The Outer Rim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Outer_Rim?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Outer Rim (page does not exist)"), recruiting new apprentices from among the Force-sensitive. Stealth and guile still prevail, but under the auspices of building an army that can go out into the galaxy to carve out a piece of [The New Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_New_Republic "The New Republic") for a new Sith Empire.

#### [The Legacy Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Legacy_Era "The Legacy Era")

Once again, The Sith emerge *en masse* under [Darth Krayt](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Krayt "Darth Krayt"). Groups can occupy any number of roles in the new Empire and can operate openly and with authority. There is resistance to be crushed and old secrets to be unearthed.

As a specific example, your group could repeat history and be Jedi Hunters during [The Sith-Imperial War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith-Imperial_War "The Sith-Imperial War"). This time, however, instead of being one Sith and a group of Dark Jedi (or troopers), your group consists only of Sith characters. A Jedi conclave has been striking out against the forces of Emperor [Roan Fel](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Roan_Fel "Roan Fel") and [Darth Krayt](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Krayt "Darth Krayt") from a secret hiding place, which your characters have been tasked with finding. Once they have been located, the Jedi are to be wiped out. Of course, things are rarely what they seem- the Jedi are setting a trap.

*Reference Book: [Web Enhancements](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Web_Enhancements "Web Enhancements")*

*By Sterling Hershey*

Amazingly dark and devastating powers are the purview of some of the greatest Sith Lords of [The Old Republic Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic_Era "The Old Republic Era"). They cheat death repeatedly. They devastate and consume entire worlds with a thought. They bend and twist [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to their needs and desires as [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") warps them into tools of its own. Average beings stand no chance of stopping these ultra-powerful monsters of [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"). Only the greatest heroes may ultimately defeat them.

In the *Star Wars Roleplaying Game Saga Edition*, using such powerful characters presents many challenges to the Gamemaster and game system itself. This is especially true when balancing story issues with game mechanics. As a GM, how do you handle characters with powers that are far beyond those that the heroes possess?

In the *[Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*, the approach taken to adjudicating these powers uses a combination of story devices, [Destiny Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Points "Destiny Points"), and existing [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents"), [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"), and [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers"). In this article, we discuss this method. We also provide alternative ideas for GM who wish to apply similar abilities to their own villains by creating specialized mechanics.

## Using Powers as Story Devices

The true function of some ultra-powerful abilities is as a story device. Each Sith Lord's power is specific to that character, created through his or her unique circumstances and developed to suit specific plot points. They are unique powers, with no expectation of duplication by other Sith Lords. They are also not powers likely (or able) to be shared or taught to an apprentice.

Using a power as a story device gives GMs maximum flexibility in creating and using their own powerful Sith Lords. The power simply operates as required to fit the plot. However, it is important to realize that while these dark abilities are devastatingly powerful, they are also limited in scope. They generally do not provide a great variety of powers; instead, they dramatically boost a single power or a limited number of related abilities.

Powers used as story devices should be more cinematic in nature and rarely used directly against the heroes. They are best used to shock, frighten, or horrify the heroes and drive the plot forward. If this type of power is used directly against the heroes, be prepared to allow them to make [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks"), make attacks, or use other abilities to escape or mitigate its effects. It should not be easy, requiring high [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill") DCs or even the use of a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") or [Destiny Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Point "Destiny Point").

### Example in Play

[Darth Nihilus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Nihilus "Darth Nihilus") is literally a destroyer of worlds, powered by an insatiable hunger that drives him to consume greater and greater populations. While one may reasonably expect someone fallen to [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") to employ *[Force Lightning](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Lightning "Force Lightning")* against a foe, no one expects a fleet of [Starships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships "Starships") commanded by Sith Lords consuming entire planets. Nor would Nihilus tolerate such a rival and competitor for his sustenance. Given the Sith Lord's unique background, it is not the type of ability that would be taught to an apprentice.

From a game mechanics point of view, there is little need to spend time developing rules and restrictions for such a unique power. It is a story device. From the GM's perspective, [Darth Nihilus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Nihilus "Darth Nihilus") consumes a world when the plot requires it. However, lesser aspects of his terrible hunger might be used directly against the heroes. These abilities are better emulated by the use of [Force Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Talents "Force Talents"), [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers"), [Force Secrets](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Secrets "Force Secrets"), and [Force Techniques](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Techniques "Force Techniques"). In this case, the GM should describe the use of such a Talent, like **[Drain Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Drain_Force "Drain Force")**, in a manner that suggests that it is powered by his unnatural hunger.

## Using Destiny Points

A second way to emulate an ultra-powerful ability is through the creative use of [Destiny Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Points "Destiny Points"). This encourages creative descriptions that relate to the ability's main use. Since there are a limited number of [Destiny Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Points "Destiny Points"), this automatically restricts the number of uses a given power has. This implementation works best with characters that fight against or otherwise interact with the heroes, where tighter integration with familiar mechanics may appeal to a player's sense of fair play.

Using [Destiny Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Points "Destiny Points") is a great way to account for powerful abilities in combat. Scoring an automatic [Critical Hit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Critical_Hit "Critical Hit") could be described as channeling a unique power instead of making a simple blaster shot or [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") strike. An automatic miss could be described as the absorption of a vast amount of power or deflecting an attack miraculously through [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), rather than the simple avoidance of an attack. The ability to act out of turn could emulate a character's amazing quickness on the battlefield or their ability to strike the heroes at an unexpected moment. Even using a [Destiny Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Point "Destiny Point") to immediately gain 3 [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") could be described in a manner that makes a character seem like they are rejuvenating themselves in a mystical fashion, even though they may not use the actual [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") until later in the encounter. The GM is under no obligation to specify the mechanics behind the effects during the game. However, they should consider doing so after the fact so that the players understand what is happening- even if their characters do not.

Gamemasters may find it useful to combine this option with the story device method. The Sith Lord might gain the regular [Destiny Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Point "Destiny Point") benefits or alternative benefits as dictated by the GM. While the story device provides the overall special ability and justifications for the existence of a power, using [Destiny Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Points "Destiny Points") allows for limitations on that power. This can be especially useful for new GM characters that the heroes must ultimately defeat. After all, just because an ability is extremely powerful, that doesn't mean its use is unlimited. Over a long adventure or campaign, the heroes might battle an enemy many times, whittling away at their supply of [Destiny Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Points "Destiny Points") until it is exhausted- and the enemy is finally defeated.

### Example in Play

[Darth Sion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Sion "Darth Sion") embodies the unbeatable enemy. He's a Sith so vile that he channels his own anger through [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to keep himself alive even though he should be dead. Thanks to his intense anger and pain, he literally holds his decaying body together through [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force").

Darth Sion's continued existence is best described as a story device, rather than as a specialized [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") that requires constant rolls to keep Sion whole. However, even though the character may believe that his ability to escape death is nearly infinite, it is actually limited by his [Destiny Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Points "Destiny Points") in an unusual way. When taking a final hit that would normally kill him, he spends a [Destiny Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Point "Destiny Point") to cause the attack to miss, and his story device background returns him to full [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") and the top of the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"). [Darth Sion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Sion "Darth Sion") cannot be defeated until his [Destiny Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Points "Destiny Points") are exhausted. Once they are gone, he feels the loss of power, loses the will to fight, and begins to question his existence. From this point, he is susceptible to The Jedi Exile's attempts to persuade him to give up his life as a much-desired escape from his painful existence.

## Alternative Mechanics

In their home games, some GMs might wish to use mechanics that emulate a story device or unusual power beyond what regular [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers"), [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"), [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents"), and class abilities already provide. A GM could create a new [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power"), but that implies that the ability could be learned by other Force-users, including the heroes. If that works for your campaign, and the limitations of using it in the [Force Power Suite](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power_Suite "Force Power Suite") works for your character concept, it is an acceptable option.

However, these ideas usually involve abilities that are beyond the scope of a single [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power"). In this case, the Special Quality comes into play. Special Qualities are normally reserved for special class features from [Prestige Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Classes "Prestige Classes") or for unusual abilities used by certain [Beasts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Beasts "Beasts") that are not connected to a [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat").

In this case, the GM could define the new powerful ability as a Special Quality and write a specific rule that dictates how the power operates every time. This is best used for a power that does not evolve or change over time. It is also expected that this is a narrowly defined mechanic. A power with multiple aspects might require several Special Quality descriptions.

For an example of a Special Quality that covers part of Darth Sion's abilities, here is one based on his similar ability in the *Star Wars* Miniatures Game:

**Eternal Hatred:** Whenever this character would be killed, make a DC 10 [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") check; if successful, this character is restored to full [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") and returned to the top of the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") instead of being defeated.

## Heroes and Powerful Abilities

Inevitably, some *Knights of the Old Republic* fans and players will want to turn their PCs into ultra-powerful Force-using characters. While the official products assume that the heroes act heroically, some GMs and players may wish to try a campaign or adventure based on [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") or to create a character that requires redemption.

Allowing a player character to gain such powerful abilities poses all of the same difficulties as that of a GM character- without the GM's direct adjudication of the power's use. Therefore, it is best to clearly define the extent of the power's use before play begins. Powers that function as story devices are probably the most difficult to craft and use. In that case, both the GM and the player must agree when the power could come into play. Powers based on [Destiny Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Points "Destiny Points") would be the most limiting in terms of the amount of use. They are most easily used to enhance [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers"), [Force Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Talents "Force Talents"), or similar abilities that already exist in the game. Special Qualities are easily the most adaptable option, allowing the GM and player to craft a specific power suited to the campaign.

However, there should be a cost to developing Dark Side abilities. It should be significant and impose a considerable penalty on the character's ability to accomplish other tasks. The severity of the cost and penalty should be proportional to the usefulness and extent of the creative power.

Disfigurement is the most obvious type of cost. Most powerful Sith Lords suffer some kind of major physical deformity or disfigurement. A character who takes on a strong power should suffer a similar fate. It should penalize one or more [Ability Scores](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Scores "Ability Scores"), including [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma"). For more on generating the look of a Sith character (whether a player character or a GM character), see Part 2: The Becoming in *Behind the Threat*.

Fortunately, handling ultra-powerful characters is a rare occurrence in *Saga Edition*. Though they are beyond the scope of the average game, they make excellent villains and plot devices. Defeating ultra-powerful characters is also a worthy goal for any long-term campaign. They make effective antagonists for heroes of virtually any level, though only the greatest heroes may ultimately overcome them.

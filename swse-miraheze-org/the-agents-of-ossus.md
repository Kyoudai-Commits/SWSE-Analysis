# The Agents of Ossus

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Agents_of_Ossus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Agents_of_Ossus) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:04:26Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

One of the few [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions") to actually grow during [The Dark Times](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Times "The Dark Times"), The Agents of Ossus were Force-sensitive citizens of the galaxy who grew up during the prominent years of the Empire. During Darth Vader's assault on the Jedi Temple, a young Jedi Padawan named [Jin-Lo Rayce](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jin-Lo_Rayce?action=edit&redlink=1 "Jin-Lo Rayce (page does not exist)") hid in the archives in the hope of avoiding detection by the newly named Sith Lord and his Clone Troopers. Rayce, who as a boy had been fascinated by the intricacies of Jedi history, had spent many long nights in the archives and was apprenticed to Jedi Knight [Jocasta Nu](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jocasta_Nu?action=edit&redlink=1 "Jocasta Nu (page does not exist)") as her most recent Padawan. As [Darth Vader](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Vader "Darth Vader") stormed through the Jedi Temple, [Jocasta Nu](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jocasta_Nu?action=edit&redlink=1 "Jocasta Nu (page does not exist)") went to face the Sith Lord to stop him from accessing the temple's signal beacon. Before she left, she told her Padawan to take a [Datapad](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Datapad "Datapad") filled with blueprints of the [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant") underbelly and flee the Jedi Temple through underground passages. As his Master was struck down, [Jin-Lo Rayce](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jin-Lo_Rayce?action=edit&redlink=1 "Jin-Lo Rayce (page does not exist)") escaped into the lower levels of [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant") and vanished from the sight of the Empire.

For some months Rayce lived in hiding in the depths of [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant"), avoiding Imperial patrols and subsisting on whatever he could find. Eventually he managed to stow away aboard a transport headed to [Chandrila](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Chandrila "Chandrila"), where he lived for a short time. Rayce bounced from one world in [The Core](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Core?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Core (page does not exist)") to another, sometimes escaping to worlds in [The Inner Rim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Inner_Rim?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Inner Rim (page does not exist)") and [The Outer Rim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Outer_Rim?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Outer Rim (page does not exist)"), always in search of Jedi survivors, and never finding any. During his travels, Rayce came into contact with young, Force-sensitive beings who would have been discovered by [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") had it not been for [The Clone Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Clone_Wars "The Clone Wars") and the rise of the Empire. After spending several months on the run, Rayce eventually came to terms with the fact that [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") were gone. Though only half-trained himself, Rayce decided to revisit the worlds he had traveled to and train those he encountered in the ways of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). Over time, he created several small cells of Force-sensitive agents who could tap into their powers in, at the very least, a rudimentary way.

[Jin-Lo Rayce](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jin-Lo_Rayce?action=edit&redlink=1 "Jin-Lo Rayce (page does not exist)") always made sure to keep his allegiances quiet. Even though he was training students in the ways of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), he didn't want even a whisper of the word "Jedi" to reach Imperial ears. Having lost his [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") in his escape from the Jedi Temple, Rayce never tried to teach his students the use of a traditional weapon (and, in truth, he had not yet mastered it himself). Instead, he taught the basics using [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to guide a blaster or a vibroblade, and he instructed his students in techniques that he developed living hidden on the streets of [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant") and a dozen other urban worlds. Eventually his students were learning techniques wholly different from the teachings that Rayce had learned among [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi").

Rayce decided to name his group of pupils after the planet [Ossus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ossus "Ossus"), a lost repository of Jedi knowledge. His students, The Agents of Ossus, would be repositories of knowledge as well- only they would not be lost, but rather in hiding. The Agents of Ossus grew within a few short years to have a presence on a number of civilized worlds. These successes, however, also led to tragic failures. [The Inquisitorius](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Inquisitorius "The Inquisitorius") hunted down and eradicated a number of cells, driving most other Agents of Ossus deeper into hiding. Those who were willing to continue their training were rare, and most were far more cautious than ever before in hiding their talents and powers.

Members of The Agents of Ossus focus on using [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to aid their survival under the heel of the Empire. Living almost exclusively in urban environments, The Agents of Ossus develop [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") that fit with their lifestyles. They use blasters and fly Starships, and they are as comfortable in the concrete jungle of a city as a [Wookiee](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wookiee "Wookiee") would be in the true jungles of [Kashyyyk](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kashyyyk "Kashyyyk"). They believe that they are the last torchbearers in a galaxy of darkness, and that their ability to use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force")- and their willingness to do so- is all that is keeping the galaxy from being plunged completely into the abyss of corruption fostered by the Empire. Though not necessarily engaged as dissidents against the Empire, by and large they choose to live outside the government's rules and regulations, and they strike out on their own path even when living among law-abiding citizens.

**Membership:** Any character who has the [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity") Feat can become a member of The Agents of Ossus by being accepted as an apprentice by a [Force Adept](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Adept "Force Adept") or [Force Disciple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Disciple "Force Disciple") who is already a member of this [Force Tradition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Tradition "Force Tradition"). Additionally, any character who has the [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity") Feat and is living on an urbanized world in [The Inner Rim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Inner_Rim?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Inner Rim (page does not exist)") or [The Core](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Core?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Core (page does not exist)") can be a member of The Agents of Ossus. Members of The Agents of Ossus gain access to the [Agent of Ossus Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Agent_of_Ossus_Talent_Tree "Agent of Ossus Talent Tree").

# Catch a Second Wind

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Catch_a_Second_Wind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Catch_a_Second_Wind) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:06:03Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

If you are reduced to one-half your maximum [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") or less, you can Catch a Second Wind as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"). You can only Catch a Second Wind once per day. Only characters with Heroic Levels can Catch a Second Wind; [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles"), [Beasts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Beasts "Beasts"), [Nonheroic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nonheroic "Nonheroic") characters, objects, and devices cannot. This Action heals one-quarter of your full [Hit Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Point "Hit Point") total (rounded down) or a number of points equal to your [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") score, whichever is greater. You can Catch a Second Wind only once per day.

Certain [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") or [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") may allow you to Catch a Second Wind more often, but never more than once in a single encounter. If you have the [Extra Second Wind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Extra_Second_Wind "Extra Second Wind") feat, you can Catch a Second Wind an additional time per day (but never more than once in a single encounter). A [Nonheroic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nonheroic "Nonheroic") character that takes the [Extra Second Wind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Extra_Second_Wind "Extra Second Wind") feat can Catch a Second Wind once per day.

## Multiple Second Winds

*Reference Book: [Web Enhancements](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Web_Enhancements "Web Enhancements") ([Jedi Counseling](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Counseling "Jedi Counseling"))*

Instead of having one Second Wind per day, every heroic character can Catch a Second Wind a number of times per day equal to 1 + (Class Bonus to [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense")) + ([Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") Modifier), with a minimum of 1. Any ability that would add an extra Second Wind (such as the [Extra Second Wind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Extra_Second_Wind "Extra Second Wind") feat or the **[Tough as Nails](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tough_as_Nails "Tough as Nails")** Talent) instead adds a number of Second Winds equal to this base number. As always, you can Catch a Second Wind no more than once in a single encounter.

## The Edge of Exhaustion

*Reference Book: [Web Enhancements](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Web_Enhancements "Web Enhancements") ([Jedi Counseling](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Counseling "Jedi Counseling"))*

If you have already used your Second Wind(s) for the day, you can catch another Second Wind by voluntarily moving -1 [Persistent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persistent "Persistent") step down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"). The [Persistent Condition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persistent_Condition "Persistent Condition") remains until you've had [Surgery](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surgery "Surgery") performed on you or until you get eight consecutive, uninterrupted hours of rest.

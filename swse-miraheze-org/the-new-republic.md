# The New Republic

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_New_Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_New_Republic) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:03:39Z.

*Reference Books: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook"), [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual"), [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

Mere days after the Battle of [Endor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Endor "Endor"), [The Rebel Alliance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Rebel_Alliance "The Rebel Alliance") becomes the Alliance of Free Planets and negotiates the creation of a new government as influential systems and sectors openly defect from the Empire. A month later, Mon Mothma creates the New Republic Provisional Council and publicly announces a "Declaration of a New Republic" signed by top Alliance leaders and officials from the most powerful breakaway systems. With [Mon Mothma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mon_Mothma "Mon Mothma") as Chief Councilor- and later, Chief of State- the fledgling New Republic government continues to evolve as its influence spreads through the galaxy. Rebel Alliance military units transition into similar roles in the expanding New Republic forces.

Within the Empire, rival factions vie for power. Some attempt to claim the Imperial throne, while others, especially Moffs and high-ranking military officials, quickly consolidate power within their own personal kingdoms. These warlords and political opportunists further weaken the Empire, allowing The New Republic time to consolidate its holdings. Additional systems decide to join the new government, often without military confrontations. Still, powerful Imperial forces hold out for years before The New Republic reclaims the galaxy's major worlds from Imperial rule.

Many threats align against The New Republic, the first involving an attempt by [Grand Admiral Thrawn](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grand_Admiral_Thrawn "Grand Admiral Thrawn") to sow discord among the newly reformed Galactic Senate. An even greater challenge comes in the form of the reawakened clone of [Emperor Palpatine](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Emperor_Palpatine "Emperor Palpatine"). This clone organizes an assault on The New Republic and pushes the former Rebel Alliance to the breaking point.

Rash Jedi have led New Republic officials to be wary of the Knights, and suspicious of their true motives. Indeed, this leads to varying degrees of mistrust and even fear in the general populace, many of whom still remember the Emperor's anti-Jedi rhetoric from years before. As Luke sets about rebuilding [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order"), a new threat appears from beyond the galaxy. The [Yuuzhan Vong](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong "Yuuzhan Vong") arrive, and their immunity to [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") makes them a great threat to [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi").

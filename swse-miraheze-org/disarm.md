# Disarm

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disarm](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disarm) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:06:09Z.

*Reference Books: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook"), [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you may attempt to Disarm an opponent, forcing them to drop one weapon (or object) that they are holding. Make a normal melee attack roll against your opponent, who gets a +10 bonus to their [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") during this Action. If your opponent is holding the weapon with more than one hand, you take a -5 penalty on your attack roll to Disarm them, per additional hand grasping the weapon.

If the attack succeeds, your opponent is Disarmed. If you successfully Disarm your opponent with an [Unarmed Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attack "Unarmed Attack"), you can take the Disarmed weapon (or object). Otherwise, it falls on the ground at your opponent's feet (inside their [Fighting Space](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fighting_Space "Fighting Space")). If your Disarm attack fails, your opponent can immediately make a free attack against you.

If you have the [Improved Disarm](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improved_Disarm "Improved Disarm") feat, you get a +5 bonus on your melee attack roll to Disarm an opponent, and your opponent doesn't get to make a free attack against you if your Disarm attempt fails. If you have the **[Ranged Disarm](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Disarm "Ranged Disarm")** Talent, you can attempt to Disarm your opponent with a ranged attack. If the attack fails, your opponent doesn't get to make a free attack against you.

If you are [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Acrobatics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatics "Acrobatics"), whenever you successfully Disarm your opponent you can make a DC 20 [Acrobatics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatics "Acrobatics") check as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action"). If the [Acrobatics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatics "Acrobatics") check succeeds, you snatch the object from the air. You must have at least one hand free to grab the item.

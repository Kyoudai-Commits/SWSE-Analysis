# Shockboxing

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shockboxing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shockboxing) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:05:22Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

The sport of Shockboxing is a violent and dangerous testing ground for hand-to-hand combat prowess. Two combatants don special [Shockboxing Gloves](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shockboxing_Gloves "Shockboxing Gloves") and then engage in melee combat with each other. The sport is a popular training method within military units, and the [Barabel](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Barabel "Barabel") Species is exceptionally well-suited to the competition. Shockboxers are tough and skilled at hand-to-hand combat, using their fists to deliver punishing blows and deflect incoming attacks.

Shockboxing in *Star Wars Saga Edition* can be as simple as a combat encounter between two Shockboxers. However, to prevent a Shockboxing match from being little more than a series of attack rolls, Gamemasters should encourage [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill") use as a part of the match. As an optional rule, during a Shockboxing match a character makes attack rolls and also [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks") during his or her turn. Each Shockboxer can make one [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action"), describing how he or she tries to gain an advantage for that exchange of attacks.

For example, you might choose to use [Endurance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Endurance "Endurance") to steel yourself against incoming attacks, [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") to try to [Feint](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feint "Feint"), [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") to look for signs of weakness, or [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") to try to [Intimidate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intimidate "Intimidate") your opponent. The Gamemaster should set the Skill DC (either against the opponent's [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses"), or with an appropriate DC for a character of that level). Success grants the combatant a temporary [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point"), which must be spent before the end of the next turn or it is lost. Typically, this [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") is used to grant a bonus to attack rolls, but during a Shockboxing match you can choose to add the results of a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") roll to your damage roll instead.

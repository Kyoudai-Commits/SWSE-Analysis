# The Aing-Tii Monks

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Aing-Tii_Monks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Aing-Tii_Monks) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:04:50Z.

DescriptionHistoryPhilosophyBuilding an Aing-Tii Monk

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

Patrolling the area of space near the Kathol Rift in large, organic ships and wielding advanced weaponry is a mysterious race known as the [Aing-Tii](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aing-Tii?action=edit&redlink=1 "Aing-Tii (page does not exist)"). Their homeworld is hidden somewhere within the Rift, and many people believe that the entire race is sensitive to [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), since the rest of the galaxy has only encountered Force-using members of the race. In truth, not all of the [Aing-Tii](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aing-Tii?action=edit&redlink=1 "Aing-Tii (page does not exist)") are Force-sensitive, but they do have a higher percentage of Force-users compared with other [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species").

The Aing-Tii Monks can be found almost exclusively within the nebulous clouds that form the Kathol Rift, near the edge of the galaxy. They actively patrol the area, looking for slaver ships or vessels in distress that they aggressively target and destroy. The location of their homeworld is unknown, despite many attempts over the years by several individuals to find it. However, it is believed to be located somewhere within the Rift. Very rarely are any [Aing-Tii](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aing-Tii?action=edit&redlink=1 "Aing-Tii (page does not exist)") found alive outside the Rift, and those that dare venture outside do not stray very far.

**Membership:** Only a few beings who are not members of the [Aing-Tii](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aing-Tii?action=edit&redlink=1 "Aing-Tii (page does not exist)") Species have been indoctrinated by The Aing-Tii Monks, but they are nevertheless part of this [Force Tradition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Tradition "Force Tradition"). Those who are not [Aing-Tii](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aing-Tii?action=edit&redlink=1 "Aing-Tii (page does not exist)") must demonstrate a willingness to learn the "true nature of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force")" and demonstrate that they have something to teach the Monks as well. Members of The Aing-Tii Monks can select [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") from the [Aing-Tii Monk Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aing-Tii_Monk_Talent_Tree "Aing-Tii Monk Talent Tree").

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

Very little is known about the [Aing-Tii](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aing-Tii?action=edit&redlink=1 "Aing-Tii (page does not exist)"). Their existence is a secret shared by relatively few individuals who have met Aing-Tii Monks. Even then, the meetings take place aboard their ships. In recent history, the [Aing-Tii](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aing-Tii?action=edit&redlink=1 "Aing-Tii (page does not exist)") are known to patrol the space known as the Kathol Rift and do not interact very much with the inhabitants of the region.

By the end of [The Clone Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Clone_Wars "The Clone Wars"), [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order") learns of The Aing-Tii Monks' existence and knows that the [Aing-Tii](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aing-Tii?action=edit&redlink=1 "Aing-Tii (page does not exist)") are decidedly uninterested in [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"). A few years before the Battle of [Yavin](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yavin "Yavin"), Master [Yoda](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yoda "Yoda") sends a former smuggler named [Jorj Car'das](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jorj_Car%27das "Jorj Car'das") to the [Aing-Tii](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aing-Tii?action=edit&redlink=1 "Aing-Tii (page does not exist)") to ask for their help in countering his failing health. The Monks agree to assist on the condition that Jorj stays with them to learn their ways. Several years pass before the Monks allow Jorj Car'das to leave their company and spend the rest of his life on [Exocron](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exocron?action=edit&redlink=1 "Exocron (page does not exist)"), a world in the Kathol Outback.

Four years after the Battle of [Endor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Endor "Endor"), the [Aing-Tii](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aing-Tii?action=edit&redlink=1 "Aing-Tii (page does not exist)") begin a search for what they call the Codex, an ancient piece of Darkstryder technology they believe to be sacred. Convinced that they cannot physically touch it, they abduct a [Human](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Human "Human") navigator named [Makezh](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Makezh?action=edit&redlink=1 "Makezh (page does not exist)"), erase his memories, and implant a subconscious command to seek out and obtain the Codex for them. The Monks' knowledge of [Human](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Human "Human") physiology is limited, however, and [Makezh](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Makezh?action=edit&redlink=1 "Makezh (page does not exist)") becomes mildly insane as a result of the operation. The search concludes on the planet [Demonsgate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Demonsgate?action=edit&redlink=1 "Demonsgate (page does not exist)"), a lone world beyond the Kathol Rift. The [Aing-Tii](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aing-Tii?action=edit&redlink=1 "Aing-Tii (page does not exist)") take the Codex, [Makezh](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Makezh?action=edit&redlink=1 "Makezh (page does not exist)"), and several Imperial [Stormtroopers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stormtroopers "Stormtroopers") for study.

After the conclusion of the Yuuzhan Vong War in 25 ABY, the Jedi Knight [Jacen Solo](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jacen_Solo?action=edit&redlink=1 "Jacen Solo (page does not exist)") travels to the Kathol Rift and studies with The Aing-Tii Monks. Although the Monks view Solo as an unsound student, they teach him about Flow-Walking and how to use the ability. Though Solo files a report of his time spent with The Aing-Tii Monks, much of the information Solo learned is deliberately withheld.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

The Aing-Tii Monks believe in the same [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") as [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") and [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith"), but their fundamental principle about it differs greatly in scope. Instead of merely two sides of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), [The Light Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Light_Side "The Light Side") and [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"), The Aing-Tii Monks believe that [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") has multiple aspects very much like a rainbow. Within these aspects, [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") moves and acts in ways that guide and help life in all of its forms, and no single aspect is more important than the others. In order for one to truly understand the nature of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), one must experience a little of each facet and gain a semblance of the entire spectrum.

Since The Aing-Tii Monks believe like [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") that life creates [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), they also believe that life can manipulate [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") as well, and even those who are not sensitive to the ebb and flow of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") have some measure of control over it. Despite this idea, The Aing-Tii Monks do not seek to take advantage of their special "gifts." They use their abilities for a more practical reason: moving themselves through space. Over the centuries, the [Aing-Tii](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aing-Tii?action=edit&redlink=1 "Aing-Tii (page does not exist)") have learned to use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to bend space and even time to transport themselves to various destinations. Beyond this application, they use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") for little else.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

Playing a hero that has been trained in the philosophies of The Aing-Tii Monks can be somewhat trickier than playing members of other [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions"). Since the [Aing-Tii](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aing-Tii?action=edit&redlink=1 "Aing-Tii (page does not exist)") Species does not lend itself easily to heroic adventuring, your hero is more likely to be a member of another [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") that came across The Aing-Tii Monks and convinced them to train you. Decide how you came into contact with the [Aing-Tii](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aing-Tii?action=edit&redlink=1 "Aing-Tii (page does not exist)"). Was your ship marooned on a world in [Aing-Tii](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aing-Tii?action=edit&redlink=1 "Aing-Tii (page does not exist)") space or saved from slavers by an [Aing-Tii](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aing-Tii?action=edit&redlink=1 "Aing-Tii (page does not exist)") patrol? Did you seek out The Aing-Tii Monks on purpose, making contact with the [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") and requesting their instruction, or did they single you out through some sort of mystic selection process? After completing your training with The Aing-Tii Monks, what did you do? For what [Destiny](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny "Destiny") did the [Aing-Tii](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aing-Tii?action=edit&redlink=1 "Aing-Tii (page does not exist)") give you their precious knowledge of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force")?

In game terms, the powers of The Aing-Tii Monks are best represented in those abilities that bend the rules of time and space. Your hero will want to make sure they have the *[Fold Space](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fold_Space "Fold Space")* Force Power, as well as some Talents from the [Aing-Tii Monk Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aing-Tii_Monk_Talent_Tree "Aing-Tii Monk Talent Tree"). If your character has the *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")* Force Power and the **[Visions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Visions "Visions")** Talent, you might interpret that when you use those abilities you are actually practicing the secret art of Flow-Walking (whereby you transport yourself back to witness images from the past, rather than just seeing them from the future). Your hero might describe using the *[Surge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surge "Surge")* Force Power as bending space to let you move more quickly, or your character might be a master of the *[Phase](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Phase "Phase")* Force Power, allowing you to pass through walls.

Other considerations for building your Aing-Tii Monk might include [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") and non-[Force Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Talent "Force Talent") selection. The [Aing-Tii](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aing-Tii?action=edit&redlink=1 "Aing-Tii (page does not exist)") are adamant in their war on slavers, and as such you might build your hero to do the same. You might have studied under an [Aing-Tii](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aing-Tii?action=edit&redlink=1 "Aing-Tii (page does not exist)") navigator aboard one of their [Living Starships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Living_Starships "Living Starships"), and as such you would be [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in the [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") skill. Perhaps you spent some time breaking up slaver convoys, and have the [Vehicular Combat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicular_Combat "Vehicular Combat") Feat or [Weapon Proficiency (Heavy Weapons)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Heavy_Weapons) "Weapon Proficiency (Heavy Weapons)"). Since the [Aing-Tii](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aing-Tii?action=edit&redlink=1 "Aing-Tii (page does not exist)") are careful to guard their secrets, you might take [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") and [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") that make you less susceptible to mental influence, boosting your [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") or discouraging others from making [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks") against your [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") at all.

# Heavy Armor

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Armor) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:05:44Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

Heavy Armor is the top of the line [Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor "Armor"), primarily used by military specialists, and high-spec mercenaries. All Heavy Armor shares the following characteristics unless otherwise specified:

- Heavy Armor inflicts a -10 [Armor Check Penalty](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor_Check_Penalty "Armor Check Penalty") (negated by [Armor Proficiency (Heavy)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor_Proficiency_(Heavy) "Armor Proficiency (Heavy)"))
- If a character has a base [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") of 6 Squares, Heavy Armor reduces it to 4 Squares
- If a character has a base [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") of 4 Squares, Heavy Armor reduces it to 3 Squares
- When [Running](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Running "Running") in Heavy Armor, you can only move up to three times your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") (instead of four times)

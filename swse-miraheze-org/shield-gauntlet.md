# Shield Gauntlet

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shield_Gauntlet](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shield_Gauntlet) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:05:13Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

While [Energy Shields](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Energy_Shields "Energy Shields") have fallen out of use in much of the galaxy, they can still be found among the nobility of [Kilia IV](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kilia_IV?action=edit&redlink=1 "Kilia IV (page does not exist)"). [The Kilian Rangers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Kilian_Rangers "The Kilian Rangers") use Shield Gauntlets to both provide defense and remind themselves and others of the other half of their oaths; to defend themselves and their families. A Shield Gauntlet can be worn even if you are wearing other [Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor "Armor"), although you cannot wear any items that cover your hands (such as [Combat Gloves](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Combat_Gloves "Combat Gloves"), a [Stunning Gauntlet](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stunning_Gauntlet "Stunning Gauntlet"), a [Cortosis Gauntlet](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cortosis_Gauntlet "Cortosis Gauntlet"), or [Shockboxing Gloves](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shockboxing_Gloves "Shockboxing Gloves")) with a Shield Gauntlet.

A Shield Gauntlet provides no benefit to the untrained, but those with certain Talents (see [Kilian Ranger Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kilian_Ranger_Talent_Tree "Kilian Ranger Talent Tree")) can use it to deflect [Ranged Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attacks "Ranged Attacks"). A character without the [Armor Proficiency (Light)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor_Proficiency_(Light) "Armor Proficiency (Light)") feat suffers [Armor Check Penalties](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor_Check_Penalties "Armor Check Penalties") as usual when wearing a Shield Gauntlet. A Shield Gauntlet requires an [Energy Cell](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Energy_Cell "Energy Cell") to operate.

**Type:** [Light Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Armor "Light Armor")

**Cost:** 1,500

**Weight:** 1 Kilogram

**Availability:** [Rare](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rare "Rare")

**Special:** The Shield Gauntlet can be worn in tandem with other clothing or [Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor "Armor").

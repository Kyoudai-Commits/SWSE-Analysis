# Simple Weapons (Melee)

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Simple_Weapons_(Melee)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Simple_Weapons_(Melee)) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:07:42Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

Simple Weapons include weapons that require no special training to use. [Unarmed Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attacks "Unarmed Attacks") and Natural Weapons are considered Light Simple Weapons in which all characters are proficient. [Weapon Proficiency (Simple Weapons)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Simple_Weapons) "Weapon Proficiency (Simple Weapons)") is a Starting Feat for all [Heroic Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Classes "Heroic Classes") (which is also applicable to [Grenades](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grenades "Grenades") and [Simple Weapons (Ranged)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Simple_Weapons_(Ranged) "Simple Weapons (Ranged)")).

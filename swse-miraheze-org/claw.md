# Claw

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Claw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Claw) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:08:17Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

**Cost:** 20 x [Cost Factor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cost_Factor "Cost Factor")

**Weight:** (5 x [Cost Factor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cost_Factor "Cost Factor")) Kilograms

Claws are an intermediate step between [Tool Mounts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tool_Mounts "Tool Mounts") and [Hands](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hands "Hands"). They are useful for grabbing onto objects to be moved, but aren't very good for tasks that require fine manipulation. For example, while a Droid could easily carry a blaster in its Claw, it would have difficulty firing it. A Droid using a Claw to perform a task that normally requires a true hand must make a DC 15 [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") check to succeed at the task. If the check fails, the Droid drops the object it is attempting to manipulate.

Claws deal damage when used for [Unarmed Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attacks "Unarmed Attacks") as determined by the Droid's size:

| DROID SIZE | FINE | DIMINUTIVE | TINY | SMALL | MEDIUM | LARGE | HUGE | GARGANTUAN | COLOSSAL |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| UNARMED DAMAGE | - | 1 | 1d2 | 1d3 | 1d4 | 1d6 | 1d8 | 2d6 | 2d8 |

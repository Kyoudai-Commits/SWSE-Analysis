# Category : Poison

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Poison](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Poison) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:06:41Z.

Poisons are [Hazards](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hazards "Hazards") that are harmful or fatal to creatures. They always attack the [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") of a target, ignoring [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") bonuses to [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"), [Damage Reduction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Reduction "Damage Reduction"), and [Shield Rating](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shield_Rating "Shield Rating"). If a Poison moves a target down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"), it likely imposes a [Persistent Condition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persistent_Condition "Persistent Condition") as well, which cannot be removed until the Poison is cured or until it fails an attack roll against the target.

# Category : The Rebellion Era

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:The_Rebellion_Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:The_Rebellion_Era) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:03:36Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

**0 to 5 Years after *A New Hope***

The Emperor rules with an iron will. His key supporters include Darth Vader, Moffs and Grand Moffs of varying degrees of power and influence, military commandeers, and a variety of secret police, spies, and assassins. The feared agents of the Imperial Security Bureau (the ISB) scour the galaxy for traitors and Rebels. The once-great Jedi Order has been eliminated, and only a handful of Force-sensitive individuals remain in hiding on out-of-the-way planets- other than those darksiders working for the Emperor's New Order.

The Senate has been disbanded. [The HoloNet](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_HoloNet "The HoloNet") has been restricted. [The Core Worlds](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Core_Worlds?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Core Worlds (page does not exist)") have been effectively cut off from the rest of the galaxy. [Alderaan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Alderaan "Alderaan"), a world of peace and influence, has been destroyed. The Emperor's Grand Admirals command the vast Imperial military machine, and hundreds of worlds have been tasked with keeping that machine going- no matter what the cost. New weapons of war appear regularly, from modified [Stormtroopers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stormtroopers "Stormtroopers") to [Walkers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Walkers "Walkers"), [TIE Fighters](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/TIE_Fighters "TIE Fighters") to [Star Destroyers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Star_Destroyers "Star Destroyers"). Interdictor cruisers capable of pulling ships out of hyperspace are seeing wider use. No Starship, private or otherwise, is safe from Imperial inspection, and boarding occurs on a regular basis.

Heroes of this era probably work for [The Rebel Alliance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Rebel_Alliance "The Rebel Alliance"). They can be part of a Rebel Cell, attached directly to Alliance High Command, or independents drawn to the Alliance's cause. In addition to [The Galactic Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Galactic_Empire "The Galactic Empire"), opponents of this era include crime lords, smugglers, bounty hunters, and traitors to The Alliance. It's all up to the heroes to help turn the tide of Imperial domination.

## The Rebellion Era

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

The *[Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")* focuses on roleplaying in the classic era of *Star Wars*. The Rebellion Era is depicted in the original *Star Wars* trilogy, which tells the tale of [Luke Skywalker](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Luke_Skywalker "Luke Skywalker") joining [The Rebel Alliance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Rebel_Alliance "The Rebel Alliance") and bringing down [The Galactic Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Galactic_Empire "The Galactic Empire"). Possibly the most iconic era in the saga's history, The Rebellion Era teems with themes and and individual aspects that even casual *Star Wars* fans recognize instantly. Setting a game in The Rebellion Era means playing in a time of great heroes such as [Luke Skywalker](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Luke_Skywalker "Luke Skywalker"), [Han Solo](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Han_Solo "Han Solo"), and [Leia Organa](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Leia_Organa "Leia Organa"), battling the same enemies that they fought, and struggling with the same dangers that they faced.

A [Rebellion Era Campaign](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign "Rebellion Era Campaign") differs from other campaigns in a variety of ways. Although it shares a common enemy with [The Dark Times](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Times "The Dark Times") (the Empire), The Rebellion Era does not pit the heroes against the entire galaxy without some help. Instead, it allows them to be a part of something greater than themselves. That said, they likely face greater dangers than do heroes of a [Dark Times Campaign](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Times_Campaign "Dark Times Campaign"). The Death Stars are but two of the Empire's superweapons, developed as part of the Tarkin Doctrine to strike fear into the hearts of its enemies. In addition, [Darth Vader](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Vader "Darth Vader") and [Emperor Palpatine](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Emperor_Palpatine "Emperor Palpatine") have an iron grip on the galaxy, and they have swayed (some would say brainwashed) much of its leadership into accepting and enforcing Palpatine's New Order.

The *Rebellion Era Campaign Guide* assumes that the heroes belong to [The Rebel Alliance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Rebel_Alliance "The Rebel Alliance"), either at the onset of the campaign or shortly after it begins. Of course, many options exist for play in The Rebellion Era, and other [Reference Books](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reference_Books "Reference Books") in the *Saga Edition* line cover other types of campaigns.This book describes life in the Alliance, hiding and fighting the Empire. The heroes of such a campaign perform as many great deeds as heroes such as [Han Solo](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Han_Solo "Han Solo") and [Princess Leia](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Princess_Leia "Princess Leia"), and make their own difference in the struggles of the Rebellion. Over the course of a campaign, the heroes fight the Empire, rise to prominence, and help free the galaxy from the clutches of tyranny.

Of course, not every hero begins his or her career with [The Rebel Alliance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Rebel_Alliance "The Rebel Alliance"). Like [Han Solo](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Han_Solo "Han Solo"), heroes in a [Rebellion Era Campaign](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign "Rebellion Era Campaign") might start out as everyday citizens, smugglers, or even Imperial soldiers. However, they are drawn inexorably closer to the Rebellion with each passing adventure. Even if the heroes never join the Alliance officially, it is highly likely that they will become entangled in the conflict with the Empire.

## A Time of Rebellion

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

The largest element that distinguishes The Rebellion Era from [The Dark Times](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Times "The Dark Times") is the presence of [The Rebel Alliance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Rebel_Alliance "The Rebel Alliance"). Also known as the Alliance to Restore the Republic, the organization is dedicated to ridding the galaxy of the tyranny of [The Galactic Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Galactic_Empire "The Galactic Empire"). However, since the Empire still has a merciless grip on untold worlds, the Rebellion must operate in secret. Most of [The Rebel Alliance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Rebel_Alliance "The Rebel Alliance") is organized into decentralized cells that receive instructions from Alliance leaders only sporadically.

Within these cells are individual heroes who have given up their normal lives in service of [The Rebel Alliance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Rebel_Alliance "The Rebel Alliance"). They live in the shadow of the Empire's power, lying low until the time comes to strike. Many cells operate openly on remote worlds, escaping the Empire's notice until they rise to action. The life of a Rebel is hard; it demands sacrifice and tough work. In the end, the soldiers of the Rebellion know that they have left their former lives for a purpose, and that one day all the hardships they have endured will result in the downfall of [The Galactic Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Galactic_Empire "The Galactic Empire").

The Rebellion Era lasts for only about eight and a half years, from the foundation of [The Rebel Alliance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Rebel_Alliance "The Rebel Alliance") until the capture of [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant") under [The New Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_New_Republic "The New Republic"). This period, also called the Galactic Civil War, pits the Alliance against the Empire in a struggle to free the galaxy. The following section highlights some of the major events in The Rebellion Era that serve as milestones in galactic history.

### The Corellian Treaty

The Rebellion Era officially begins with the signing of the Corellian Treaty, the document that founded [The Rebel Alliance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Rebel_Alliance "The Rebel Alliance"). The three primary founders are [Mon Mothma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mon_Mothma "Mon Mothma"), [Bail Organa](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bail_Organa "Bail Organa"), and [Garm Bel Iblis](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Garm_Bel_Iblis "Garm Bel Iblis"). Having been brought together under false pretenses by agents of [Darth Vader](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Vader "Darth Vader"), the governments of [Chandrila](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Chandrila "Chandrila"), [Alderaan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Alderaan "Alderaan"), and [Corellia](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Corellia "Corellia") agree to form an alliance, pooling the resources of their resistance groups to form the Rebellion. Shortly thereafter, [The Rebel Alliance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Rebel_Alliance "The Rebel Alliance") issues the Declaration to Restore the Republic, a political document that makes clear the purpose and mission of the new organization. With the publication of the Declaration, hundreds of resistance cells begin springing up on Imperial planets throughout the galaxy. With three major worlds willing to take a stand against the Empire, others find the resolve to resist as well.

### The Death Star Plans

The early years of the Rebellion see some success. One of the most notable accomplishments is the theft of the plans for the newly completed Death Star. The leaders of [The Rebel Alliance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Rebel_Alliance "The Rebel Alliance") have known about the existence of [The Death Star](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Death_Star "The Death Star") since before the organization's founding- in fact, several of them had been held hostage on the incomplete station before the signing of the Corellian Treaty. But on [Toprawa](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Toprawa?action=edit&redlink=1 "Toprawa (page does not exist)"), Rebel agents steal the plans and transmit them to the Alliance. The plans eventually make their way to the *Tantive IV*, the ship carrying [Leia Organa](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Leia_Organa "Leia Organa"). The capture of the plans is the first step toward bringing down the Empire, setting off a chain of events that eventually leads to the Emperor's death.

### The Battle of Yavin

The Death Star Plans fall into the hands of a young farmboy from [Tatooine](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tatooine "Tatooine") named [Luke Skywalker](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Luke_Skywalker "Luke Skywalker"). After [Alderaan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Alderaan "Alderaan") is destroyed in retribution for its rebellion, Luke rescues [Princess Leia](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Princess_Leia "Princess Leia") and arrives at [Yavin 4](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yavin_4 "Yavin 4"). Shortly thereafter, the Battle of [Yavin](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yavin "Yavin") takes place, resulting in the destruction of [The Death Star](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Death_Star "The Death Star"). Not only is this the first major blow that the Rebellion lands against the Empire, but it also brings Skywalker- and [Han Solo](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Han_Solo "Han Solo")- into the Alliance. The Battle of [Yavin](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yavin "Yavin") inspires more systems to join the Rebels and marks the start of a time when the Empire begins to take the Rebellion seriously.

### The Battle of Derra IV

[The Rebel Alliance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Rebel_Alliance "The Rebel Alliance") suffers its first crushing defeat above [Derra IV](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Derra_IV?action=edit&redlink=1 "Derra IV (page does not exist)"). Imperial forces ambush a Rebel supply convoy, destroying it. The convoy's destruction is a major blow to the Rebels' supply chain and leaves them shorthanded at their new base on [Hoth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hoth?action=edit&redlink=1 "Hoth (page does not exist)"). The Battle of [Derra IV](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Derra_IV?action=edit&redlink=1 "Derra IV (page does not exist)") ends the euphoria the Alliance had felt since its victory at [Yavin](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yavin "Yavin") and sets up a defeat that nearly unravels the Rebellion a short time later.

### The Battle of Hoth

The Rebel defeat at the Battle of [Hoth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hoth?action=edit&redlink=1 "Hoth (page does not exist)") is the first in a series of events that comes close to eliminating several of the Alliance's greatest heroes. With Echo Base understaffed as a result of the convoy ambush at [Derra IV](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Derra_IV?action=edit&redlink=1 "Derra IV (page does not exist)"), the Empire overruns the base and scatters its occupants to the far reaches of the galaxy. More important, the battle sets off a sequence of events that leads to the capture of Rebel leader [Han Solo](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Han_Solo "Han Solo") and nearly results in the loss of both [Luke Skywalker](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Luke_Skywalker "Luke Skywalker") and [Leia Organa](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Leia_Organa "Leia Organa"). Fortunately, the assistance of [Lando Calrissian](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lando_Calrissian "Lando Calrissian") on Cloud City foils Darth Vader's plans, leaving Luke and Leia free to fight another day.

### The Capture of The *Suprosa*

During the search for [Han Solo](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Han_Solo "Han Solo"), the Rebellion chances upon knowledge of a freighter, The *Suprosa*, which transports more than its manifest indicates. In truth, the ship carries information about the construction of the second Death Star, and the Alliance learns that the Emperor is overseeing the final stages of its construction. Combining this knowledge with technical readouts of the battle station, the Rebellion hatches a plan to strike a decisive blow against the Empire.

### The Battle of Endor

After Luke, Leia, and others rescue Han from [Jabba the Hutt](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jabba_the_Hutt "Jabba the Hutt"), the time for the attack comes at last. As the Alliance sabotages the Death Star's shield generator on [Endor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Endor "Endor"), the Rebel fleet assaults the incomplete space station. However, the ships fly into a trap, and only through the guile of Rebel leaders such as [Han Solo](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Han_Solo "Han Solo") and [Lando Calrissian](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lando_Calrissian "Lando Calrissian") does the Alliance fight through to victory. Shorty before the station's destruction, [Luke Skywalker](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Luke_Skywalker "Luke Skywalker") redeems [Darth Vader](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Vader "Darth Vader"), who in turn slays the Emperor. With the second Death Star destroyed and [Emperor Palpatine](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Emperor_Palpatine "Emperor Palpatine") dead, the Alliance finally claims the upper hand in the Galactic Civil War.

### The Birth of The New Republic

The Rebellion Era comes to a close with the establishment of [The New Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_New_Republic "The New Republic"). Following the victory at [Endor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Endor "Endor"), the Alliance still has a fight to win back the many worlds under Imperial control. Over two years after the Battle of [Endor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Endor "Endor"), members of [Rogue Squadron](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rogue_Squadron "Rogue Squadron") engineer the capture of [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant"), and [The New Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_New_Republic "The New Republic") seizes control of the seat of galactic power. With the new government legitimized, opposition from the remnants of the Empire fragments, allowing [The New Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_New_Republic "The New Republic") to establish freedom as the galactic standard once more.

## An Empire at its Peak

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

Another major difference between The Rebellion Era and [The Dark Times](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Times "The Dark Times") is that during The Rebellion Era, the Empire has reached its full power. Its grasp on the galaxy is like iron, and even the most distant reaches of known space have been converted to the New Order. As a result, there are very few safe places to hide from the Empire. Although some spaceports and out-of-the-way worlds might have only a small Imperial presence, there are still Imperial forces on nearby planets, as well as sector task forces jumping from system to system in search of Rebel activity.

The Empire hasn't staged a military occupation of the entire galaxy; instead, it has converted the cultures and philosophies of many worlds to the New Order. Young men and women grow up dreaming about attending the Imperial military academies, and noble families of the galaxy vie with one another for the Empire's favor. Local bureaucrats have become Imperial officials with the full might of the Empire at their backs. Humanocentrism (the view that [Humans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Humans "Humans") are the most important [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") in the galaxy) is at an all-time high, encouraged by [Emperor Palpatine](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Emperor_Palpatine "Emperor Palpatine") as a means of controlling the populace. Indeed, many of the galaxy's citizens are more than simply the subjects of Imperial tyranny- they are willing participants. Citizens' organizations such as COMPNOR indoctrinate people into believing that it is their civic duty to serve unquestioningly and to abandon personal freedom in order to strengthen the Empire.

The military, cultural, and political strength of [The Galactic Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Galactic_Empire "The Galactic Empire") makes the Rebellion's task especially important. Within two decades, the Empire has transformed worlds that once backed the Republic into supporters of [Emperor Palpatine](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Emperor_Palpatine "Emperor Palpatine"). [The Rebel Alliance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Rebel_Alliance "The Rebel Alliance") knows that with each passing day, the Empire grows stronger. In many ways, this is the galaxy's most desperate hour, as countless worlds teeter on the edge of forgetting what freedom was like.

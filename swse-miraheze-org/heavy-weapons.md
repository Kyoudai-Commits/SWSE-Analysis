# Heavy Weapons

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Weapons) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:05:46Z.

| DISTANCE | RANGE | PENALTY |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-50 Squares | Point-Blank | No penalty |
| 51-100 Squares | Short | -2 to attack rolls |
| 101-250 Squares | Medium | -5 to attack rolls |
| 251-500 Squares | Long | -10 penalty to attack rolls |

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

Large, powerful weapons that usually require a [Brace](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Brace "Brace") or tripod to operate fall under the category of Heavy Weapons, as do even larger weapons mounted to [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") and [Starships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships "Starships"). [Weapon Proficiency (Heavy Weapons)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Heavy_Weapons) "Weapon Proficiency (Heavy Weapons)") is a Class Bonus Feat for [Soldiers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldiers "Soldiers").

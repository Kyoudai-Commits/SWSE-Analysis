# Siang Lance

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Siang_Lance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Siang_Lance) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:05:12Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

The Siang Lance is one of the few types of technological devices found on [Kilia IV](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kilia_IV?action=edit&redlink=1 "Kilia IV (page does not exist)"), although only a small number of functional Siang Lances remain. By law, only members of a noble house may use a Siang Lance. In practice, a Siang Lance is given to active members of [The Kilian Rangers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Kilian_Rangers "The Kilian Rangers") as a badge of office and to remind themselves and others of one half of their oaths: to defend justice, chivalry, and the people of [Kilia IV](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kilia_IV?action=edit&redlink=1 "Kilia IV (page does not exist)"). A Siang Lance is an ancient model of a [Sporting Blaster Rifle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sporting_Blaster_Rifle "Sporting Blaster Rifle") with an affixed [Bayonet](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bayonet "Bayonet"). It is typically wielded with one hand, leaving the other hand free to use the Kilian Ranger's [Shield Gauntlet](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shield_Gauntlet "Shield Gauntlet").

A Siang Lance can be used to make [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity"). When you would make an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity") against a target, you may choose either to fire the Siang Lance as a ranged weapon or to use the weapon's [Bayonet](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bayonet "Bayonet") as a melee weapon. A Siang Lance requires a [Power Pack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Power_Pack "Power Pack") to operate. After 100 shots, the [Power Pack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Power_Pack "Power Pack") must be replaced.

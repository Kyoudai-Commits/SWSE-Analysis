# Medpac

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Medpac](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Medpac) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:08:51Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

**Cost:** 100

**Weight:** 1 Kilogram

Medpacs are compact packages designed to both equip a trained medic for work in the field and allow untrained individuals to apply [First Aid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/First_Aid "First Aid") in emergencies. A Medpac contains bandages, Bacta, synthetic flesh, coagulants, stimulants, and other medicines designed to help an injured patient recover quickly.

Once you use a Medpac, its contents are expended even if your [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") check is not successful. Any given creature can only benefit from the use of a Medpac once in a 24-hour period.

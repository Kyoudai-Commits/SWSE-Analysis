# Light Armor

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Armor) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:05:42Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

Light Armor is by far the most widespread [Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor "Armor") in the *Star Wars* galaxy, used by hunters, bounty hunters, bouncers, and anyone in between. All Light Armor shares the following characteristics unless otherwise specified:

- Light Armor inflicts a -2 [Armor Check Penalty](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor_Check_Penalty "Armor Check Penalty") (negated by [Armor Proficiency (Light)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor_Proficiency_(Light) "Armor Proficiency (Light)"))
- Light Armor does not affect a character's [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed")

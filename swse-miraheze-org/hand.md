# Hand

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hand](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hand) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:08:18Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

**Cost:** 50 x [Cost Factor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cost_Factor "Cost Factor")

**Weight:** (5 x [Cost Factor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cost_Factor "Cost Factor")) Kilograms

A Droid is considered to have a true Hand if its gripping Appendage includes at least three digits, one of which is opposable. Factory-model [3PO-Series Protocol Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/3PO-Series_Protocol_Droids "3PO-Series Protocol Droids") and [B1-Series Battle Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/B1-Series_Battle_Droids "B1-Series Battle Droids") come equipped with Hands.

Hands deal damage when used for [Unarmed Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attacks "Unarmed Attacks") as determined by the Droid's size:

| DROID SIZE | FINE | DIMINUTIVE | TINY | SMALL | MEDIUM | LARGE | HUGE | GARGANTUAN | COLOSSAL |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| UNARMED DAMAGE | - | - | 1 | 1d2 | 1d3 | 1d4 | 1d6 | 1d8 | 2d6 |

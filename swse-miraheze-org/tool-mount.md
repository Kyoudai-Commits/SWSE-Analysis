# Tool Mount

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tool_Mount](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tool_Mount) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:08:22Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

**Cost:** 10 x [Cost Factor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cost_Factor "Cost Factor")

**Weight:** (2 x [Cost Factor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cost_Factor "Cost Factor")) Kilograms

Tool Mounts are somewhat sturdier than [Instruments](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Instruments "Instruments"). A [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") must make a DC 15 [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") check to lift, carry, or drag objects for which its tools were not designed. The GM might rule that particularly delicate objects have a higher DC. If the check fails, the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") drops the object. Weapons mounted on a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") are considered Tool Mounts unless otherwise noted. A Tool Mount does not include the cost of the tool or weapon mounted on it.

Tool Mounts deal damage when used for [Unarmed Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attacks "Unarmed Attacks") as determined by the Droid's size:

| DROID SIZE | FINE | DIMINUTIVE | TINY | SMALL | MEDIUM | LARGE | HUGE | GARGANTUAN | COLOSSAL |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| UNARMED DAMAGE | - | - | 1 | 1d2 | 1d3 | 1d4 | 1d6 | 1d8 | 2d6 |

**Q:** A Droid's Tool Mounts- including weapon mounts- are one type of [Appendage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Appendage "Appendage"), and [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") can use their [Appendages](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Appendages "Appendages") to make [Unarmed Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attacks "Unarmed Attacks"). Since characters are automatically proficient with [Unarmed Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attacks "Unarmed Attacks"), would that mean that a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") is automatically proficient with any weapon on a Tool Mount?

**A:** No. While the weapon mount can be used for [Unarmed Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attacks "Unarmed Attacks") to bludgeon nearby foes, using the weapon itself for its intended purpose still requires proficiency to avoid the usual penalties.

For example, if an R2 unit had a [Blaster Pistol](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Blaster_Pistol "Blaster Pistol") installed on one of its Tool Mounts, it could make [Unarmed Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attacks "Unarmed Attacks") at no penalty, but it would take a -5 penalty on [Ranged Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attacks "Ranged Attacks") with the [Blaster Pistol](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Blaster_Pistol "Blaster Pistol"). (A [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") without a [Heuristic Processor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heuristic_Processor "Heuristic Processor") wouldn't be able to use the weapon without proficiency at all.)

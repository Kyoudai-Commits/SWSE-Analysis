# Grenades

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grenades](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grenades) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:07:58Z.

| DISTANCE | RANGE | PENALTY |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-6 Squares | Point-Blank | No penalty |
| 7-8 Squares | Short | -2 to attack rolls |
| 9-10 Squares | Medium | -5 to attack rolls |
| 11-12 Squares | Long | -10 to attack rolls |

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

Highly volatile explosives, Grenades are highly dangerous, used only by galactic armies, or professional mercenaries. Unless stated otherwise, Grenades are both [Area Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attack "Area Attack") and [Thrown Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Thrown_Weapons "Thrown Weapons") for the purpose of determining [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range"), with a [Burst](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Burst "Burst") radius determining their attack size. [Weapon Proficiency (Simple Weapons)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Simple_Weapons) "Weapon Proficiency (Simple Weapons)") is a Starting Feat for all [Heroic Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Classes "Heroic Classes") (which is also applicable to [Simple Weapons (Melee)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Simple_Weapons_(Melee) "Simple Weapons (Melee)") and [Simple Weapons (Ranged)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Simple_Weapons_(Ranged) "Simple Weapons (Ranged)")).

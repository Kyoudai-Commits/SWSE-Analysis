# Use Computer (Int)

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:08:45Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook"), [Starships of the Galaxy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships_of_the_Galaxy "Starships of the Galaxy")*

Use this skill to access secured files and defeat security systems.

**Special:** You can [Take 10](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_10 "Take 10") on Use Computer checks. You can [Take 20](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_20 "Take 20") on a Use Computer check except when attempting to Improve Access. When a computer is Friendly or Helpful to you, you gain an [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") bonus on all Use Computer checks made with that computer equal to its [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") bonus.

COMPUTER ATTITUDE STEPS

| ATTITUDE | CHECK MODIFIER | DESCRIPTION |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hostile | -10 | The computer treats you as a hostile intruder and attempts to trace your location and isolate your connection. |
| Unfriendly | -5 | The computer treats you as an unauthorized user and blocks your access to its programs and information. |
| Indifferent | -2 | The computer treats you as a guest or visitor and grants you access to non-secret programs and information (as long as this does not conflict with previous commands). |
| Friendly | +0 | The computer treats you as an authorized user and grants you access to any program and non-secret information (as long as this does not conflict with previous commands). You may add any [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") bonus provided by the computer to your Use Computer checks. |
| Helpful | - | The computer treats you as if you are its owner or administrator, granting access to all of its programs and information (even if doing so overrides previous commands). You may add any [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") bonus provided by the computer to your Use Computer checks. |

## Applications

The following are the primary applications of the Use Computer skill.

### Access Information

Getting information through a computer requires you to connect to the appropriate network (such as the HoloNet, or its equivalent in other eras) and locate the files you seek. Connecting to an Indifferent, Friendly, or Helpful network (a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action")) doesn't require a skill check if you are using a computer that's already linked to it; however, establishing a connection to a network using a remote computer requires a DC 10 Use Computer check. You can also get information without connecting to a network if you use a computer whose memory contains that information; the GM decides what information a computer's memory actually holds.

Finding information on a single topic requires a set amount of time (see below); at the end of this time, you must make a Use Computer check. The time required and the check DC are determined by the type of information sought.

For example, locating general information about a senator is easier than locating specific information (such as the senator's date of birth), which is easier than finding private information (such as the senator's private comm channel code), which is easier than uncovering secret information (such as the senator's cred stick code).

| INFORMATION | DC | TIME REQUIRED |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General | 15 | 1 Minute (10 Rounds) |
| Specific | 20 | 10 Minutes |
| Private | 25 | 1 Hour |
| Secret\* | 30 | 1 Day (8 Hours) |

*\*Secret information can only be accessed on a computer that is Helpful to you.*

### Astrogate

**Trained Only:** Moving from a given location to a desired destination through hyperspace requires a successful Use Computer check. Because every object in the galaxy is constantly in motion, the precise path between two locations changes from day to day. If the Astrogator uses current data (one day old at most), they can plot a safe course. Doing so usually requires 1 minute, at the end of which you must succeed on a DC 10 Use Computer check (without data with which to plot a jump through hyperspace, the base DC for the Use Computer check is 30, and requires 1 hour calculating coordinates and vectors before attempting the check). As a general rule, data for a particular route through hyperspace is available to anyone with access to [The HoloNet](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_HoloNet "The HoloNet")- although that data might be outdated if the route in question is not frequently traveled by other ships.

The difficulty of plotting a course through hyperspace is determined by how recently you have updated your Astrogation data, as shown on the table below.

| ASTROGATION DC | AGE OF ASTROGATION DATA |
| --- | --- |
| 10 | Less than 1 day |
| 15 | At least 1 day, but less than 1 standard week (5 days) |
| 20 | At least 1 week, but less than 1 standard month (35 days) |
| 25 | At least 1 month, but less than 1 standard year (368 days) |
| 30 | At least 1 year |

Additionally, you may choose to plot a hyperspace course more aggressively than normal, reducing your travel time by increasing the chance of something going wrong. For every 5 points by which you increase the Use Computer check DC, you may reduce your travel time by 1 day; if this would reduce the travel time to less than 1 day, you instead reduce the remaining travel time by one-half, to a minimum of 1 hour. You must make the decision to increase the Use Computer check DC before making the check. Alternatively, you may gain a +5 circumstance bonus on your Use Computer check if you voluntarily double the travel time.

Certain situations or circumstances can also modify the check, as shown in the table below. The lack of a [Navicomputer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Navicomputer "Navicomputer") (or, failing that, an Astromech Droid with stored coordinates) makes the task much more difficult. If time is of the essence, the Astrogator can perform the check as a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action") by taking a -10 penalty on their Use Computer check.

Astrogation DC Modifiers

| SITUATION | CHECK MODIFIER |
| --- | --- |
| Using [Navicomputer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Navicomputer "Navicomputer") | +5 |
| No [Navicomputer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Navicomputer "Navicomputer") Used\* | -10 |
| No HoloNet Access | -5 |
| Attempt to make the check in 1 round | -10 |

*\*Do not apply the penalty if the ship has current Astrogation data stored in an Astromech Droid or receives accurate transmitted data from another ship.*

If the Use Computer check is successful, the [Starship](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship "Starship") enters hyperspace without incident and arrives at its destination in a number of days equal to 1d6 x the Starship's [Hyperdrive](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hyperdrive "Hyperdrive") multiplier.

A failed Use Computer check indicates that the Astrogator has made a potentially dangerous error in their calculations. Make another Use Computer check using the same modifier against the same DC. If this second Use Computer check is successful, the error is caught before entering hyperspace, and the process of plotting a course must begin anew. If this second Use Computer check fails, the [Starship](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship "Starship") moves -1 [Persistent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persistent "Persistent") step on the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") and takes damage equal to 5% of its total [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") for every point by which the check fails. (The [Persistent Condition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persistent_Condition "Persistent Condition") and damage remain until the ship undergoes maintenance.) If the ship is not [Disabled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disabled "Disabled") or destroyed, it arrives at the intended destination in double the expected travel time. If the ship is [Disabled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disabled "Disabled"), it drops out of hyperspace in a random location somewhere between the point of origin and the destination (the exact location is determined by the GM).

A [Navicomputer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Navicomputer "Navicomputer") allows a character aboard a Starship to make a Use Computer check to Astrogate [Untrained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Untrained "Untrained").

### Dealing With Hazards

When the heroes have a space encounter, the effects should be dealt with in one of several ways. Under the rules for hyperspace hazards in the *[Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")* (see above), the ship takes damage and moves down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") with a [Persistent Condition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persistent_Condition "Persistent Condition"). Under normal rules, the [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") can be restored and the [Condition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition "Condition") can be removed with an hour's work and a [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") check. Some of the Space Hazards listed above also have their own [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks") associated with them.

Alternatively, the Gamemaster can require that the effects of the Space Hazards cannot be dealt with except by a more challenging [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") or a series of [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks"), and Gamemasters should feel free to use the sample skill DCs (see the [Job Generator](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Job_Generator "Job Generator")) to determine the difficulty of clearing the effects of the Hazard. As a general rule, Minor Hazards should use the Medium DCs, Moderate Hazards should use the Moderate DCs, and Major Hazards should use the Hard DCs.

For example, when a ship passes through an uncharted dust cloud, the GM can call for a Use Computer check to calibrate the ships sensors; alternatively, the GM could require a hero to physically leave the ship and clean the sensors manually, which could involve [Acrobatics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatics "Acrobatics") or [Climb](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Climb "Climb") checks to perform the task in [Zero-Gravity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Zero-Gravity "Zero-Gravity").

### Disable or Erase Program

**Trained Only:** If you are [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in Use Computer, you can disable or erase a program on a computer that is Helpful toward you. Disabling or erasing a program takes 10 minutes and requires a DC 15 Use Computer check.

### Improve Access

**Trained Only:** As a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"), you can make a Use Computer check to adjust the attitude of a computer in order to gain access to its programs and information. You must be able to communicate with the computer either through a direct interface (such as a keypad) or by connecting to it through an appropriate network (such as the HoloNet). Apply a modifier on the check based on the computer's current attitude toward you: Hostile -10, Unfriendly -5, Indifferent -2, Friendly +0.

If the check equals or exceeds the computer's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), the computer's Attitude shifts one step in your favor. If you fail, the computer's Attitude does not change. If you fail by 5 or more, the computer's Attitude becomes one step worse (for example, Indifferent to Unfriendly) and the computer notifies the computer's administrator of the access attempt.

A Hostile computer can be dangerous. If the computer becomes Hostile or you fail any Use Computer check made to Improve Access to a Hostile computer, it traces your exact location and notifies the nearest security personnel. In addition, if you fail by 5 or more when attempting to Improve Access to a Hostile computer, it isolates your connection and rejects any further attempts you make to access it for 24 hours.

### Issue Routine Command

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can Issue a Routine Command to a Friendly or Helpful computer. Examples include turning a computer on or off, viewing and editing documents or recordings in its memory, printing a hard copy of a document or image on a flimsiplast sheet, opening or closing doors that the computer controls, and the like.

Issuing Routine Commands doesn't normally require a Use Computer check. However, if another character issues a contradictory command, the computer follows the command of the character toward whom it has a better Attitude (for example, it follows a command from someone toward whom it is Helpful over someone toward whom it is Friendly).

If the computer has the same Attitude toward both characters, make an opposed Use Computer check against the competing character. If you succeed, your command takes effect. If you fail, the opposing character's command takes effect.

### Reprogram

**Trained Only:** You can Reprogram a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") to obey a new master, copy data stored in its memory banks, change its [Trained Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skills "Trained Skills") and other abilities, erase memories selectively or entirety (resetting the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") to its factory preset status). Reprogramming a Droid takes 10 minutes. Reprogramming requires that the Droid be shut down for the duration of the procedure (or see "Self-Programming" below).

Reprogramming [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") and [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") are more difficult, with a DC equal to the Droid's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") and 30 minutes of uninterrupted work, and attempts suffer a -5 penalty on your Use Computer check. Furthermore, [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") and [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") can only be Reprogrammed if they are neither a requirement for any of the Droid's [Prestige Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Classes "Prestige Classes") nor a prerequisite for any [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat") or [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") the Droid retains. As always, a Droid must meet all prerequisites for any replacement [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") or [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents").

- A [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") can only have [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") it is eligible for based on its [Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Classes "Classes"); thus, a Droid with 5 levels in the [Soldier](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier "Soldier") Class could only be Reprogrammed with 3 [Soldier Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier_Talents "Soldier Talents") (from [Soldier](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier "Soldier") levels 1, 3, and 5). A Droid with 5 levels of [Soldier](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier "Soldier") and 3 levels of [Scoundrel](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrel "Scoundrel") could be Reprogrammed with 3 [Soldier Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier_Talents "Soldier Talents") and 2 [Scoundrel Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrel_Talents "Scoundrel Talents").
- To Reprogram a [Trained Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skill "Trained Skill"), the programmer must be [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in that [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill") or purchase a skill package (100 credits). To Reprogram a [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat") or [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent"), the programmer must either have that [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat") or [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent"), or purchase a feat or talent package (1,000 credits).

If the Droid's owner is unable to do the Reprogramming themselves, they can hire a professional programmer to do the task for them. The standard cost of hiring a programmer is (Droid's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") squared) x 10 credits for a [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill"), or 10 times this amount for a [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat") or [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent"). This cost includes any necessary [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill"), [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat"), or [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") package.

#### Self-Programming

A Droid [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in Use Computer may attempt to reprogram itself. However, the Droid must have the appropriate [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill"), [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat"), or [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") package to do so, and it takes a -5 penalty on its skill check. A Droid attempting to Self-Reprogram does not have to be shut down, but it is [Helpless](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Helpless "Helpless") and unable to take any [Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Actions "Actions") until the attempt is completed.

#### Memory Wipes

Although intelligent [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") consider it frightful and ghoulish and a fate worse than death, the Memory Wipe is a fact of existence for most [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids"). Its primary purpose is to eradicate personality quirks that distinguish an [Independent Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Independent_Droid "Independent Droid"). Wiping a Droid's memory requires a successful Use Computer check against the Droid's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"). The [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") must be shut down to perform the Memory Wipe.

A Memory Wipe erases one Class Level per minute. A complete Memory Wipe reduces a Droid to a basic model with no personality quirks and no class abilities. For example, a [3PO-Series Protocol Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/3PO-Series_Protocol_Droid "3PO-Series Protocol Droid") that had been a 1st-level [Nonheroic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nonheroic "Nonheroic")/4th-level [Scoundrel](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrel "Scoundrel") becomes a 1st-level [Nonheroic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nonheroic "Nonheroic") basic model after a 4-minute Memory Wipe, losing its Heroic Levels and all corresponding benefits.

#### Restoring Memory

Unlike organic beings, Droids have a form of mechanical immortality: if a Droid's programming is saved to a computer system, and a new chassis and Droid Brain can be bought or found, others can attempt to load its memory into the new chassis and reactivate the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid"). A successful Use Computer check is required to transfer a Droid's programming into a new chassis. The DC for the Use Computer checks depends on the type of new chassis being used:

| CHASSIS TYPE | DC |
| --- | --- |
| Same Model | 20 |
| Different Model, Same Degree | 25 |
| Different Model, Different Degree | 35 |

Each time the transfer is attempted, the Droid must make a DC 15 [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") check. A failed check indicates that the Droid's memory suffers corruption: a permanent reduction of 1d6 [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence"). The corrupted programming can't be repaired; if the Droid's [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") modifier decreases because of corruption, the Droid's [Trained Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skills "Trained Skills") must be reduced accordingly. The Droid also loses access to [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") that have an [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") prerequisite higher than the Droid's new [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") score.

A Droid successfully transferred into another Droid of the same model resumes functioning as it was before the transfer, retaining all Class Levels, [Ability Scores](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Scores "Ability Scores"), [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills"), [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"), and [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents").

A Droid successfully transferred into a different model adopts the [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") and [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") of the new model but keeps its previous [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence"), [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom"), and [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") scores. The Droid retains its [Trained Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skills "Trained Skills"), although skill modifiers based on [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") and [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") might need adjusting. Finally, the Droid losses one Class Level (including all associated [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") and [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats")) as it has to reprogram and adapt its memory and sensory inputs for the new chassis.

## Additional Applications

The following applications of the Use Computer skill are compiled from the subsequent [Reference Books](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reference_Books "Reference Books") released for *Saga Edition*:

### Access and Reprogram Electronic Device

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

To access information contained with an electronic device, you must first connect to an Indifferent, Friendly, or Helpful computer (a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action")) via a wireless or wired system. Connecting wirelessly requires you to establish a connection as you would do with a network (see Access Information, above).

A wired connection may be as simple as obtaining the correct cable or making an [Improvised Connection](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improvised_Connection "Improvised Connection"). The same Attitudes and DCs used to Access Information (see above) from a computer are also used for devices, although most self-contained devices will not set off alarms or attempt to isolate your connection.

### Backtrail

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

**Trained Only:** A successful DC 25 Use Computer check reveals the identity of the last person to use the computer and the information they sought. The computer's Attitude modifies the DC as normal (see above).

### Copy or Reprogram Device

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

You can make a Use Computer check to reprogram a [Code Cylinder](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Code_Cylinder "Code Cylinder") or access card to accept new codes, or copy the existing codes to another [Code Cylinder](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Code_Cylinder "Code Cylinder") or access card. The device must be physically connected to an Indifferent, Friendly, or Helpful computer before it can be copied or reprogrammed. Copying or Reprogramming a [Code Cylinder](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Code_Cylinder "Code Cylinder") or access card takes 10 minutes. The DC for these actions depends on the level of security:

| SECURITY LEVEL | DC |
| --- | --- |
| Administrative (Civilian) | 20 |
| Encrypted Military | 30 |
| Encrypted Imperial Military | 35 |

### Cover Tracks

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

**Trained Only:** When you access a computer, you can conceal your presence and how you used the computer by increasing all Use Computer check DCs by 5. Any character who attempts to Backtrail the computer to determine your identity and what you did takes a -5 penalty on their Use Computer check.

### Detecting Hyperspace Travel

*Reference Book: [Starships of the Galaxy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships_of_the_Galaxy "Starships of the Galaxy")*

When a Starship enters or exits Hyperspace, it produces a short burst of cronau radiation. Any Starship, Space Station, or sensor array within the same star system can detect a cronau radiation burst with a DC 25 Use Computer check made as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"). Success indicates that the sensor operator detects the ship entering or exiting Hyperspace, and is aware of its point of arrival or departure. Tracking a ship through Hyperspace also relies on the presence of residual cronau radiation.

Taking a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action") to make a DC 25 Use Computer check measures the amount of Cronau radiation as well as the angle of departure of the vessel fleeing into Hyperspace, indicating the most likely destination. Cronau radiation lingers for several hours after a ship has fled into Hyperspace, though each hour since the ship made the jump to lightspeed increases the DC of the Use Computer check by 5.

### [Use Sensors](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Sensors "Use Sensors")

*Reference Book: [Starships of the Galaxy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships_of_the_Galaxy "Starships of the Galaxy")*

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), a Use Computer check lets a [System Operator](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/System_Operator "System Operator") hear or see other targets or detect someone or something sneaking up on them from behind. If the target is actively attempting to remain undetected, your Use Computer check is opposed by the target's [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") check. If the target is not making any special effort to avoid detection, the Use Computer check DC is determined by the target's size:

| TARGET SIZE | DC |
| --- | --- |
| Colossal | -15 |
| Gargantuan | -10 |
| Huge | -5 |
| Large | 0 |

On [Starship Scale](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Scale "Starship Scale") maps, your Use Computer check has a -5 penalty for every 10 squares between you and the target, and you take a -5 penalty on your check if attempting to detect a target that is powered down or otherwise not emitting an energy signature. Obstacles, [Cloaking Devices](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cloaking_Devices "Cloaking Devices"), [Jamming Suites](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jamming_Suites "Jamming Suites"), and sensor masks can also impose penalties.

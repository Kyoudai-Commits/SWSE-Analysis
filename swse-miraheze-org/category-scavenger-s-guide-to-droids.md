# Category : Scavenger's Guide to Droids

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Scavenger's_Guide_to_Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Scavenger's_Guide_to_Droids) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:05:23Z.

[![](//static.wikitide.net/swsewiki/thumb/4/41/Scavenger%27s_Guide_to_Droids.webp/300px-Scavenger%27s_Guide_to_Droids.webp.png)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/File:Scavenger%27s_Guide_to_Droids.webp)

From Astromechs to Battle Droids, the *Scavenger's Guide to Droids* features everything you need to bring the robotic inhabitants of the *Star Wars* saga to life in your own campaign. Featuring an in-depth look at Droids from the perspective of a variety of Droid experts, this book provides a wide array of new options for Droid heroes as well as allies and antagonists.

## Introduction

IntroductionUsing this BookA Scavenger’s Book

The relative peace is shattered by a small Astromech Droid waking from its sleep cycle, its lights penetrating the darkness. It looks around to get its bearings. Surrounding the Astromech are other [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") as far as its electronic eye can see. Realizing that it needs to escape, the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") kicks out its central leg and rolls through the room. The vibrations coming through the floor tell it that the structure it is in is moving, and it surmises that it is held in some giant [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle"). As the Astromech rolls along, it catches sight of other run-down [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") sitting amid the spare parts and junk scattered about the immense room: Protocol Droids, Repair Droids, Power Droids, other Astromech Droids, and some it has never seen before. As it begins to give up hope, the small [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") catches sight of a familiar Protocol Droid with a golden sheen. The Astromech excitedly rolls up, and the Protocol Droid recognizes its friend and exclaims with glee at finding a friendly chassis in their uncertain situation.

Does this scene sound familiar? The time spent by [R2-D2](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/R2-D2 "R2-D2") and [C-3PO](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/C-3PO "C-3PO") aboard the [Sandcrawler](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sandcrawler "Sandcrawler") on [Tatooine](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tatooine "Tatooine") reminds us that [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") are an integral part of the *Star Wars* universe. How much different would the saga be if [The Clone Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Clone_Wars "The Clone Wars") were fought between the Republic's Clone Army and bands of organic beings instead of against the Trade Federation's Battle Droids? Where would the story go if [R2-D2](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/R2-D2 "R2-D2") were an organic copilot in Luke Skywalker's [X-Wing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/X-Wing "X-Wing") as he made his attack run on [The Death Star](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Death_Star "The Death Star")?

The idea behind *Scavenger's Guide to Droids* is to provide players and Gamemasters the tools and information they need to generate [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") heroes, enemies, and noncombatants and to insert them into *Star Wars Roleplaying Game* campaigns. This book holds new options for [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") to become more than mere machines and to develop into full-fledged characters that work alongside or against organic heroes. [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") can be more than just equipment. They can be individuals.

The contents of this book are intended to help both players and Gamemasters create new and improved [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids"). Players will find expanded rules on creating Droid Heroes, while Gamemasters will find new information detailing the creation of Droid GM characters and Droid enemies to challenge the heroes wherever they might go in the galaxy.

Chapter One is devoted to [Droid Heroes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Heroes "Droid Heroes"). Players who want to create [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") characters to play will find new rules on creating a fully detailed [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") with which to adventure alongside organic characters. This chapter presents new [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") and [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") to help make your [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") character unique. Also, information about those companies that manufacture the various types of [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") can be found here, along with what a particular brand name brings to the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") that bears its name. Also included are several stock models that can be used as springboards to help players quickly put together [Droid Heroes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Heroes "Droid Heroes") and jump into the adventure.

Chapter Two looks at [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") from the other side of the gaming table. [Drold Allies](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Drold_Allies?action=edit&redlink=1 "Drold Allies (page does not exist)") are the subject of this chapter, in which the Gamemaster will find more information involving new game mechanics behind the portrayal of [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") in the galaxy, including an analysis of what role [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") play in the *Star Wars* universe as well as in the roleplaying game. Discussions about how [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") should work with and beside organic characters and how they should behave toward those around them can be found here as well as tips on how to make a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") more than just a piece of hardware.

Chapter Three delves into new [Droid Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Equipment "Droid Equipment") that can be used by [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") or those who work with them. New [Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapons "Weapons") and [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") that [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") can use or have installed into their chassis are found here, as well as new [Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapons "Weapons") and [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") that nondroids might opt to use on or against them. New [Starship](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship "Starship") and [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") equipment and systems relating to [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") can be found here as well.

Chapter Four is the [Droid Codex](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Codex "Droid Codex"), in which many new [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") are detailed. The contents of this codex, however, are different from other statistics block entries. Along with the standard statistics block and description, each entry includes a section that describes how the particular [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") can be used as equipment by a player character, a sidebar detailing ways to build a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") PC modeled after it, and suggestions about how the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") could be altered to perform functions not normally allowed by its original programming.

Throughout the [Droid Codex](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Codex "Droid Codex"), you will also find sidebars that present adventure hooks relating to the various droids. Each of these sidebars is presented from the point of view of a particular individual:

- **[Klikk](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Klikk?action=edit&redlink=1 "Klikk (page does not exist)")** is a [Jawa](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jawa "Jawa") trader: a scavenger and a junk trader from [Tatooine](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tatooine "Tatooine"). Most of his experiences with [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") involve their technical side, and his discussions are about equipment, making repairs, and rebuilding [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids").
- **[Raalo](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Raalo?action=edit&redlink=1 "Raalo (page does not exist)")** is a [Toydarian](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Toydarian "Toydarian") junk dealer who, despite being highly adept at making repairs and modifications to [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids"), is more interested in the business side of his operation and seeks to make as much profit as possible. His discussions focus on buying and selling [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids"), obtaining [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") (both legal and illegal) through [The Black Market](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Black_Market "The Black Market"), and relating details about [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") that he has peddled or purchased in the past.
- **[Mavven](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mavven?action=edit&redlink=1 "Mavven (page does not exist)")** is a [Besalisk](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Besalisk?action=edit&redlink=1 "Besalisk (page does not exist)") arms dealer who travels the galaxy. His business connections bring him information and firsthand experience with [4th-Degree Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/4th-Degree_Droids "4th-Degree Droids"), and many people consider him the leading expert in Combat Droids. His discussions revolve around [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") in combat, especially their involvement in battles and martial applications such as work in the military, in mercenary organizations, and in security.
- **[EV-6T6](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/EV-6T6?action=edit&redlink=1 "EV-6T6 (page does not exist)")** is an [EV-Series Supervisor Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/EV-Series_Supervisor_Droid "EV-Series Supervisor Droid"). Her position, in which she deals with [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") as an authority figure, is most often held by an organic being, and it gives her a unique perspective. Her discussions primarily focus on [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid")-to-[Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") interactions and on methods of dealing with different [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") personalities.

One question that might be crossing your mind as you read this is "Why is this a scavenger's book?" With all of the corporations, technicians, and other citizens of the galaxy that work with [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") on a daily basis, why are we focusing on scavengers? Although they might not be the most glamorous denizens of the *Star Wars* galaxy, scavengers of [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") and their parts and accessories are probably the most numerous of the organic creatures that deal with these mechanical beings. From the group of [Jawas](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jawas "Jawas") living in their [Sandcrawler](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sandcrawler "Sandcrawler") roaming the deserts of [Tatooine](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tatooine "Tatooine") to the highest-paid [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") technician employed by the wealthy residents of [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant"), those that repair, upgrade, or perform any kind of maintenance on [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") generally look to scavengers of one kind or another in order to find the best deals on the hardware they need to complete their tasks and to keep the [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") under their care operating in good condition.

As seen in Episode IV, the [Jawas](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jawas "Jawas") that abducted [R2-D2](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/R2-D2 "R2-D2") and [C-3PO](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/C-3PO "C-3PO") comb the deserts to locate any piece of technology they can grab, refurbish, and sell to the people living in the desert cities and other, more remote locations. This is their way of life and the way they earn a living. With the large number of [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") stored in their Sandcrawlers' cargo holds, the [Jawas](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jawas "Jawas") have a myriad of parts to use to repair any [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") they deem salvageable for resale. Fortunately for our [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") heroes, the [Jawas](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jawas "Jawas") saw how well maintained they were and did not use them for parts, figuring to make a lot of credits from selling two [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") in such excellent condition.

Captains of [Space Transports](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Space_Transports "Space Transports") know the value of maintaining [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids"). Those who struggle to make ends meet and scrape together every credit they can just to eat and put fuel in their ships know that any Droid they purchase is a significant investment. Keeping that investment in working condition is a minor expense for a long-term payout. With [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") to keep the ship operating smoothly, a [Starship](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship "Starship") captain has more time to devote to keeping the ship running and to finding the contracts that put food on the table and fuel in the tanks. Captains who are short on credits often find that scavengers are the best solution to their [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") maintenance needs.

The technician working for a wealthy benefactor, corporation, or government might have more resources available to obtain better parts, but will usually do everything possible to keep expenses under budget. Corporations and governments have budgets that their technicians are expected to follow, and rich sponsors do not like to see their invested funds squandered. Scavenging parts and accessories from other [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids"), or dealing with the scavengers that locate the parts, helps cut costs and keeps superiors happy.

The beings that deal in used [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") and that run the junk shops and [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") parts stores are the ones that scavenge the abandoned technology of any world, searching through the scraps for any usable parts. Junk dealers will have parts from hundreds, perhaps thousands, of different [Droid Models](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Models "Droid Models"). But the way in which they sell those parts can vary as much as any other business in the galaxy. Some dealers keep their wares all thrown together in huge plies behind their shops and allow patrons to rummage through the piles to find what they need. Other dealers might sort through the piles themselves to find what the customer needs (and to charge an extra finder's fee on top of the asking price for the part). The dealer might choose to sell a part as-is, letting the customer take his or her chances on the part's condition, or the dealer could offer to refurbish it for an additional fee.

In every segment of the *Star Wars* universe, [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") affect the lives of organic beings in some way. Whether [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") cook, clean, and serve organic lifeforms, help improve interactions between different [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species"), or help keep the [Starships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships "Starships") that allow interstellar travel and commerce active, their presence is extremely valuable to the galactic way of life. And the scavengers are the ones that keep the [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") running, silently helping maintain that way of life for everyone throughout the galaxy. Without the scavengers that provide their services to repair the [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids"), even the mightiest governments would grind to a halt and the galactic community as a whole would suffer. Without [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids"), the *Star Wars* universe that we know could not exist.

## Droid Heroes

[Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") are integral to the *Star Wars* universe. Indeed, the Droid Heroes [R2-D2](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/R2-D2 "R2-D2") and [C-3PO](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/C-3PO "C-3PO") are the first characters featured on-screen in the original movie *Star Wars: A New Hope*. They also represent two [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid")-hero archetypes: the hero and the reluctant hero. [R2-D2](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/R2-D2 "R2-D2") takes an aggressive role in pursuing his; and his master's goals, while [C-3PO](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/C-3PO "C-3PO") does what he must do when forced to rise to the occasion.

In the *Star Wars* universe, few [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") aspire to heroism. More often than not, they obey their orders and programming rather than act of their own accord. Battle Droids fight, but few go beyond their mission plans and orders. Astromechs accompany their pilots into battle, but most simply regard it as another job, albeit a dangerous one. Some bond with their pilots, but the bond does not always lead them to act beyond their programming.

No single event creates a Droid Hero. As a Droid's personality develops, it has experiences or gains programming that extends the Droid's sense of self-awareness. Personality often develops in units equipped with [Heuristic Processors](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heuristic_Processors "Heuristic Processors"), but units with [Basic Processors](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Basic_Processors "Basic Processors") occasionally exhibit such behavior. [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") who manage to alter their own programming, or have it altered for them, are more likely to act beyond their design parameters and become heroes.

Playing a Droid Hero brings its own challenges and benefits. [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") are considered property in most of the galaxy. Depending on the era, they might be feared, disliked, shunned, ignored, or not taken seriously. However, [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") are also able to fade into the background, enabling them to move without suspicion when another hero would be easily identified. As machines, they are not vulnerable to environmental threats in the same way that organic beings are. They are more quickly and easily repaired than organic characters.

Throughout all of the [Eras of Play](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Eras_of_Play "Eras of Play") of *Star Wars* lore, only a handful of major Droid Heroes exist. [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") that step beyond being merely mobile, intelligent computer systems become truly unique.

The *[Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")* presents two methods for creating a Droid Hero- playing a Custom Droid and playing a standard [Droid Model](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Model "Droid Model"). This chapter clarifies elements of the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") creation process and introduces a third option, using a stock [Droid Chassis](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Chassis "Droid Chassis"). Players can use any of the three methods to create their heroes. Each method provides options and trade-offs in levels of customization and character creation time.

## Option 1: [Playing a Custom Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Playing_a_Custom_Droid "Playing a Custom Droid")

This option, creating a new [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") model completely from scratch, gives a player the greatest freedom. This method also takes the longest, because each element of the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") must be created or selected, and its systems and accessories must be purchased individually.

## Option 2: [Playing a Standard Droid Model](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Playing_a_Standard_Droid_Model "Playing a Standard Droid Model")

This option allows a player to select a standard [Droid Model](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Model "Droid Model") based on statistics blocks already created for existing [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids"). Most of these models already have more than one level in a class, usually the [Nonheroic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nonheroic "Nonheroic") class. The character cannot enjoy the full benefit of a [Heroic Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Class "Heroic Class"), since [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") starting with a [Nonheroic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nonheroic "Nonheroic") class gain fewer [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"), [Trained Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skills "Trained Skills"), and [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") at first level. However, in exchange, players benefit from starting above first level, and this is a fast method of [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") creation. When a player uses this method of creating a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") character, the total cost of a standard [Droid Model](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Model "Droid Model") plus any player-added accessories and possessions cannot exceed 5,000 credits.

## Option 3: [Playing a Droid Chassis](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Playing_a_Droid_Chassis "Playing a Droid Chassis")

Players can select a stock Droid Chassis when creating a Droid Hero. This streamlined method of [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") creation treats each Droid Chassis as a [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species"). Each Droid Chassis represents the basis for creating a type of [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") rather than a specific model, so a player can quickly generate a new model of [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid"), or emulate an existing model while building a character entirely with Heroic Levels. Stock Droid Chassis can also be used to create specific models of [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids"), since the chassis share features of the specific [Droid Models](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Models "Droid Models") of that class of [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid").

However, the customization required to make the Droid Chassis match up with a specific [Droid Model](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Model "Droid Model") in question might make other methods more appealing. In some cases, an existing model might not be exactly replicated, but the differences can be attributed to variances in production or aftermarket customizations to the standard model. The Size and Speed of a Droid Chassis cannot be altered without Gamemaster approval. To create a Droid Hero using a stock Droid Chassis, follow these steps:

1. **Select the Chassis.** Apply [Ability](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability "Ability") modifiers, Size, Speed, Chassis Traits, [Languages](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Languages "Languages"), and other modifiers as you would when applying a [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") to a character.
2. **Review the Chassis' Bonus Equipment.** If you are creating a new or generic [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") without a specific model in mind, leave the [Droid Systems](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Systems "Droid Systems") as they are and proceed to the next step. If you are creating a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") of a specific model, compare this list to systems listed in the desired Droid's statistics block. If they are close enough for your purposes, proceed to the next step. An unwanted system can be swapped for another system within 10% of the unwanted system's cost without spending any credits. However, if upgraded or additional systems are required, they must be purchased with the Droid's starting credits (see below).
3. **Starting Credits:** Your Droid's [Heroic Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Class "Heroic Class") determines how many starting credits you have. You can spend your starting credits on Droid Systems as well as on normal [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment"). Bonus Equipment is considered part of the Droid's starting equipment; you are not required to spend credits on Bonus Equipment.

[Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") are temperamental machines, exposed to every kind of environmental and physical abuse. With limited programming and typically deficient maintenance schedules, many [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") develop odd mechanical glitches and personality quirks. Most are minor- more annoyances than anything dangerous. [Memory Wipes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Memory_Wipes "Memory Wipes") can eliminate personality quirks, but they do nothing for physical abnormalities.

Droid Quirks are optional in *Saga Edition*. They make interesting and easy roleplaying hooks, as well as give players inspiration for the development of personalities and backgrounds for their Droids. Some Droid Quirks might also have minor mechanical effects. If no game mechanic is indicated in the quirk description, a +2 or -2 circumstance bonus to [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks") or attack rolls might be appropriate whenever the quirk manifests. Players and Gamemasters are encouraged to select Droid Quirks that make characters memorable. Additionally, Droid Quirks can be assigned as the result of physical damage or sensory overload. If a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") is reduced to 0 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points"), roll 1d20+10. If the result exceeds the Droid's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"), the unit develops a Droid Quirk when it is repaired. The Gamemaster can assign an appropriate Droid Quirk or roll on Droid Quirks table.

Each Droid Quirk has an associated origin indicated in its description: Hardware (H) or Software (S). If both are indicated, one should be selected at the time the Droid Quirk is applied. Hardware Quirks result from faulty installations, physical damage, aging circuitry, or incompatible systems. Hardware Quirks can be removed by replacing or repairing the faulty system. Repairing Droid Quirks is notoriously difficult. If the repair check does not exceed the [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") DC by 10 or more, the new system works, but the Droid Quirk (or a similar Droid Quirk) returns 1d12 days later.

Software Quirks are tied to the Droid's internal subroutines. They can be caused by corrupted data, but they are more often a result of the Droid's learned responses from previous experience. Most Software Quirks are eliminated with a [Memory Wipe](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Memory_Wipe "Memory Wipe"). Corrupted data can be repaired with a successful DC 25 [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") check to [Reprogram](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reprogram "Reprogram") the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid"). Attempting to eliminate a Software Quirk without a [Memory Wipe](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Memory_Wipe "Memory Wipe") is difficult, often requiring help. A DC 30 [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") check succeeds in eliminating a Software Quirk, but the Droid's personality and responses might change in unforeseen ways because the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") must learn new responses. The results might not be exactly what was intended. Failure on the check results in the addition of another Software Quirk.

**Quirks as Persistent Conditions:** Another way to include Droid Quirks in the game is to apply one when a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") suffers a [Persistent Condition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persistent_Condition "Persistent Condition"). This allows the Gamemaster to use many Droid Quirks over the span of a campaign, and provides a rich source of plot hooks, humor, or dramatic situations. The Gamemaster should select a Droid Quirk that is related to the origin of the [Persistent Condition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persistent_Condition "Persistent Condition"). For instance, a [Persistent Condition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persistent_Condition "Persistent Condition") caused by physical damage should inflict a Hardware Quirk, whereas a hit from an [Ion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ion "Ion") weapon might cause either a Hardware or a Software Quirk. Droid Quirks applied in this manner should be automatically removed when the [Persistent Condition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persistent_Condition "Persistent Condition") is removed, instead of requiring the skill checks normally needed to eliminate a Droid Quirk.

### Scavenger's Secret: Quirky Droids

*A [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") without quirks? No such thing. Most quirks aren't severe enough to be a problem. I just tell customers that the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") has character. Personality. If they don't like it, I sell [Memory Wipes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Memory_Wipes "Memory Wipes") cheap. For the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid"), not the customer, but let me tell you, some customers could use one. [Memory Wipe](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Memory_Wipe "Memory Wipe") usually works if it's a software problem. Hardware problems are another deal. Replacing parts costs credits and time. No problem for me, I find the parts for a small fee. Install them for bigger fee, and then I sell the old parts to someone else. Make money all around.*

*Bad quirks are mostly annoying, but they can be expensive. I saw a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") with a bad [Memory Wipe](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Memory_Wipe "Memory Wipe") try to fix a [Landspeeder](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Landspeeder "Landspeeder") with old, incomplete [Walker](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Walker "Walker") specs. The owner was not so happy when she saw her new three-legged [Landspeeder](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Landspeeder "Landspeeder"), with her recently acquired vaporators welded to the body as the legs.*

*Dangerous quirks you need to take care of immediately. Most [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") won't try to kill you on purpose, but plenty of them can cause big damage inadvertently. Even the talkative types, they have a way of saying the wrong thing at the wrong time, get you in all kinds of trouble. Believe me, I know. Ever try to sell a unit that can't stop complaining about its own bad motivator? I've never seen customers leave so fast.*

For the most part, Gamemasters and players need not worry about the exact type of data ports and other connections between a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") and a computer, [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle"), or network. However, the Gamemaster might decide that this information should play a part in the story or would otherwise logically come into play. For instance, it might make sense for an Astromech Droid to be able to slice into a complex computer system, but a lowly Power Droid probably does not have the equipment for it. Also, rare or alien [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") or those from another era might have nonstandard ports. Certain [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") and [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") require a link in order to function. Below is a brief summary of the standard data ports found in the galaxy.

**Maintenance Data Link:** All [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") come standard with at least one Maintenance Data Link port for programming and maintenance. This wired link can be used with [Datapads](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Datapads "Datapads"), [Portable Computers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Portable_Computers "Portable Computers"), and systems designed for implementing [Droid Modifications](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Modifications "Droid Modifications"). This link is not normally suitable for the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") to use to attempt slicing or controlling a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") or automated systems. Any attempt to do so suffers a -10 penalty to any [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer"), [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics"), or other [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks"). This penalty does not apply to [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks") others make to the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid").

**Basic Data Link:** [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") are equipped with one or more Basic Data Links. This wired link allows a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") to transfer data efficiently and enables equipped [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids"), such as Pilot Droids, to operate computers and ship systems without physically touching the manual controls, although that capability usually depends on the system rather than the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid"). The port is suitable for data transfer, vehicular control, and slicing attempts.

**[Scomp Link](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scomp_Link "Scomp Link"):** This advanced [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid")-computer Data Link is found on [Starships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships "Starships") and on larger computers and networks. This port is suitable for all types of data transfer, vehicular control, and slicing attempts. All Astromech Droids are equipped with [Scomp Links](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scomp_Links "Scomp Links"), unless otherwise noted.

**Wireless Link:** Some [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") have the hardware to access Wireless Data Links. This type of link is only operable when a computer or network also has wireless capability. Accessing networks typically requires passwords or slicing. However, once connected, this type of link is suitable for all types of data transfer, vehicular control, and slicing attempts. An [Internal Comlink](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Internal_Comlink "Internal Comlink") does not allow a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") to access wireless data networks or make slicing attempts wirelessly.

### [Droid Manufacturers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Manufacturers "Droid Manufacturers")

Millions of manufacturing companies produce [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids"). Most are small companies that cater to local regions, manufacture specialized units, or focus on highly customized models. The major interstellar manufacturers dominate specific markets, although those markets shift as the demands of the galactic government and mega corporations change.

Throughout [The Clone Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Clone_Wars "The Clone Wars") and the Galactic Civil War, [Cybot Galactica](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cybot_Galactica "Cybot Galactica") and [Industrial Automaton](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Industrial_Automaton "Industrial Automaton") are the dominant manufacturers, with [Arakyd Industries](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Arakyd_Industries "Arakyd Industries"), [MerenData](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/MerenData "MerenData"), [Serv-O-Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Serv-O-Droid "Serv-O-Droid"), and [Veril Line Systems](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Veril_Line_Systems "Veril Line Systems") as major players in their respective markets. During [The Clone Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Clone_Wars "The Clone Wars"), most Separatist Battle Droids are manufactured by [Baktoid Combat Automata](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Baktoid_Combat_Automata "Baktoid Combat Automata") forces, with other units coming from [The Techno Union](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Techno_Union "The Techno Union"), [Colicoid Creation Nest](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Colicoid_Creation_Nest "Colicoid Creation Nest"), and [The Commerce Guild](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Commerce_Guild "The Commerce Guild"). However, [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") manufacturing has existed for thousands of years. In the time of [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic"), 4,000 years before [The Rebellion Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Rebellion_Era "The Rebellion Era"), [The Czerka Corporation](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Czerka_Corporation "The Czerka Corporation"), [Duwani Mechanical Products](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Duwani_Mechanical_Products "Duwani Mechanical Products"), and [Aratech Repulsor Company](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aratech_Repulsor_Company?action=edit&redlink=1 "Aratech Repulsor Company (page does not exist)") dominated the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") markets of their day; other well-known manufacturers, such as [MerenData](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/MerenData "MerenData") and [Serv-O-Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Serv-O-Droid "Serv-O-Droid"), were also becoming competitive.

Although manufacturing plants can be found on both major and lesser worlds, a significant percentage of [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") come from either [Mechis III](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechis_III "Mechis III") or [Telti](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Telti "Telti"). These two worlds specialize in [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") production to such an extent that their surfaces are essentially covered by automated precision-manufacturing plants. The major manufacturers use these fabrication facilities extensively. Most of the [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") built in these facilities are shipped by bulk freighter, but light freighter jockeys make a decent living running small orders to isolated regions of the galaxy.

### Adjudicating Droids

Adjudicating Droids can present unique challenges for the Gamemaster. Although they are essentially living machines, Droids cannot be treated as simply another [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") or character type. Below are common [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") issues and information on adjudicating them.

Damaged and Destroyed DroidsImprovised Droid TasksThe Droid that Gained Levels

Damaged and destroyed [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") are treated similarly to organic heroes when it comes to damage and death. However, [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") are also machines and can be reassembled or have their data accessed by other means, so they have additional options not available to organic characters. New approaches to damage and destruction are presented below. Information on [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") damage and repair from the *[Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")* is also included here for convenience.

- **Conditions:** A [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") is treated the same as an organic character when it comes to the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") and [Persistent Conditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persistent_Conditions "Persistent Conditions"). Unlike organic characters, however, a damaged [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") might gain a Droid Quirk.
- **Destroyed:** If a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") is reduced to 0 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") by an attack that deals damage equal to or greater than its [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold"), the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") is Destroyed. However, it can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to become Disabled instead of Destroyed.
- **Disabled:** When a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") is Disabled (the equivalent of being [Unconscious](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unconscious "Unconscious")), it moves -5 steps on the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"), falls [Prone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prone "Prone"), and is unable to take any [Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Actions "Actions"). It remains inert and inoperative until repaired. A [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") that is repaired immediately reactivates and can get up to fight again, but it starts [Prone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prone "Prone").
- **Repair Droid (Requires [Tool Kit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tool_Kit "Tool Kit")):** A character with [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") as a [Trained Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skill "Trained Skill") can spend 1 hour of work and make a DC 20 [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") check to repair a Damaged or Disabled [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid"), restoring [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") equal to the Droid's Character Level and removing any [Persistent Conditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persistent_Conditions "Persistent Conditions") currently affecting the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid"). A [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") can attempt to repair itself, but it takes a -5 penalty on its [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check").

## New Optional Rules

The following new optional rules can be included, in whole or in part, in your game to give Droid Heroes some of the versatility seen in the *Star Wars* films. As with all optional rules, inclusion of the following rules in the game is subject to Gamemaster approval.

- **Sacrificial Appendage:** When a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") would normally be reduced to 0 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") or moved to the bottom of the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"), the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") can choose instead to have one of its limbs severed. If the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") chooses this option, it is instead moved to the -4 step on the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"), or, in the case of being reduced to 0 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points"), it retains 1 [Hit Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Point "Hit Point"). The sacrificed [Appendage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Appendage "Appendage") should be a limb rather than a tool that the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") can easily do without. The [Appendage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Appendage "Appendage") can be successfully reattached with a repair check (see [Repair Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Repair_Droid "Repair Droid")). The [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") receives [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") from the repair, as usual.
- **Destroyed Droids:** Taking into account the method of destruction, the Gamemaster determines what parts of a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") remain after it is destroyed. Blaster fire or an explosion might scatter small and large pieces around the area. A [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") destroyed by a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") might have slices or holes burned through its chassis, or large sections of the Droid's appendages might be completely severed. The Gamemaster should also determine which of the Droid's systems, accessories, appendages, or tools are damaged or destroyed.
- **Reactivating a Destroyed Droid:** At the Gamemaster's discretion, a character can attempt to salvage and reactivate a destroyed [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid"). Reactivating a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") requires that its [Processor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Processor "Processor"), power source, and communication interface must be intact. The battery and communication interface can be replaced or replicated if needed. A destroyed [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") cannot reactivate itself. The character attempting the reactivation must make a DC 20 [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") check and work on the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") for 5 minutes.
  - A character can attempt to reactivate a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") as a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"), increasing the DC to 30. If the check is successful, the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") moves + 1 step on the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"). However, the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") is still considered to have 0 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") and cannot move, attack, or take any action other than [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks") for [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills") based on [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence"), [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom"), or [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma").
- **Salvaging a Destroyed Droid:** Salvaging a destroyed [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") requires a [Tool Kit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tool_Kit "Tool Kit"), time, spare parts, and additional [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") checks (see [Repair Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Repair_Droid "Repair Droid")). A reactivated [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") can assist in its own repairs by using the [Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another") Action, although it cannot attempt to repair itself directly. The time required to salvage a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") can be affected by the level of destruction sustained by the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid"). Repairs might take several hours, several days, or even several weeks, as determined by the Gamemaster. Destroyed [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") usually require spare parts costing 50% or more of the Droid's original cost.

Heroes might try to take maximum advantage of the unique resources a droid offers. A Droid's built-in equipment, traits, and unique social status in the *Star Wars* universe cause [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") and Droid Heroes to be called upon to carry out tasks well beyond their original programming.

When considering an unusual request for or by a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") character, consider the following:

- **Does the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") have a [Heuristic Processor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heuristic_Processor "Heuristic Processor")?** If not, then the request must be governed by the standards of the [Basic Processor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Basic_Processor "Basic Processor") or [Remote Processor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Remote_Processor "Remote Processor"), so the task must be laid out in specific terms. Unless the request is defined in ways the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") understands, the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") cannot act beyond its programmed [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills"), [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"), special abilities, and tasks. Even if the tasks are clearly delineated, the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") might perform them poorly or inefficiently or become confused if the task does not proceed as planned.
  - A [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") equipped with a [Heuristic Processor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heuristic_Processor "Heuristic Processor") is better equipped to handle unusual situations, so it can act on fewer, less specific instructions. Additionally, focusing on the advantages of the [Heuristic Processor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heuristic_Processor "Heuristic Processor") emphasizes the choice of processor in [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") creation, rewarding those players who select it.
- **Is the request already covered by an existing [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent"), [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat"), or [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") special quality?** Don't shortchange players who select certain Heroic Class, Droid, or Droid Prestige Class [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") and [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") by allowing other [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") to replicate those abilities too often. If it seems reasonable that a given [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") might be capable of an action similar to an existing ability, allow the action to proceed with a lesser chance of success and/or a lesser result than could be gained with the existing ability.
- **Is the request likely to be abused by the player in future sessions, or likely to become exploited regularly?** If not, allow the idea to be used at least in this instance. However, if the idea could become an unwelcome addition to future sessions, the Gamemaster can avoid denying its use outright by allowing the character to spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") or a [Destiny Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Point "Destiny Point") (if appropriate) for the action. Another option is for the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") to suffer a [Persistent Condition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persistent_Condition "Persistent Condition") and move -1 or more steps on the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") at the end of the task.
- **Should the task be covered by new [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent"), [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat"), or special quality?** Some tasks generated by players are more appropriately covered by creating a new Droid-degree [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent"), [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat"), or special quality. If the player creates a wholly customized or new [Droid Model](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Model "Droid Model"), a special quality can be added at the Gamemaster's discretion.
  - If the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") is an existing stock model, create a [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") or a [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat") instead. If the ability seems like something a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") ought to be able to learn, create a [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat"). If the ability seems focused on a trademark task of a particular model or degree of [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid"), create a [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") specific to that degree of [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid").
- **Adjudicating simple and complex tasks.** If the Gamemaster decides to proceed with the request, he or she should decide what actions, attacks, and [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks") are required for success. Simple tasks are typically covered by one [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check"), but unique circumstances might require more time and/or multiple checks. Complex tasks should take multiple rounds with multiple checks.
- **Plan for failure.** A [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") that performs a task outside its realm of expertise can make for potentially amusing or unexpected situations if it fails. The [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") might become confused, carry out the next operation without completing the first task, return unexpectedly, call on the owner for clarified instructions, or give up and report the failure. In the middle of a battle or a critical mission, this failure can cause confusion or threaten a mission's success, forcing the heroes to act quickly to resolve the situation. Gamemasters should take advantage of this potentially dramatic or amusing situation.

In adventuring parties featuring less than five players, adding a Gamemaster-controlled [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") to the party can be a great way to balance the party without having to adjust your encounter design. Many gaming groups can only muster three or four players and a Gamemaster, while the game is designed for a typical group of five players and a GM. Filling those empty player spots with [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") is a nice option, because a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") can easily fade into the background during noncombat encounters, whereas a living character that does so might seem shallow and one-dimensional. If you decide to include a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") ally as a means of filling out the party's numbers, you should probably think about the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") just like you would any other major Gamemaster character, and plan on it being with the party for the duration of the campaign.

You should choose a [Droid Model](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Model "Droid Model") that fills an appropriate niche in the party. Similarly, you should probably think about the kinds of adventures your heroes are going to be going on, and what roles need to be filled. If the party lacks a [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot"), a Pilot Droid seems like a good choice. However, there are other considerations. Some [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") don't do well on adventures due to their forms (try getting that [Wheeled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wheeled "Wheeled") Droid up a long set of stairs). A good option for creating [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") to round out a party of heroes is to use a stock [Droid Chassis](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Chassis "Droid Chassis") and then build the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") from the ground up. This not only lets you build exactly the kind of [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") you need to fill a gap in the party, it unshackles you from the usual physical forms of various [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids").

As the heroes gain levels, so should the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid"). The levels you choose for your [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") should likewise continue to help the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") fill in the gaps in the party. If the party is short on firepower, the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") should take levels with a high [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") and good combat [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents"). However, you also have another option to consider. If you want the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") to grow and to develop as an interesting Gamemaster character, consider having the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") take levels in the [Independent Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Independent_Droid "Independent Droid") Prestige Class. This helps represent the fact that the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") is becoming more and more unique thanks to its time with a party of heroes at the center of great, galaxy-shaking events.

## [Droid Allies](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Allies "Droid Allies")

[Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") occupy a unique place in *Star Wars*. They are machines that feel, automatons that think, robots that know. They fight wars, heal the sick, and track down Rebel scum. A [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") develops a personality, friendships, and rivalries, just as an organic being does over the course of a lifetime. [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") might squabble among themselves, but they will also offer any parts necessary to help repair a friend fallen in battle. Organic beings often treat their [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") less like property and more like companions. [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") are a remarkable part of life, even if they are not truly alive.

[Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") have a significant effect on history. Were it not for a pesky R2 unit, [The Rebel Alliance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Rebel_Alliance "The Rebel Alliance") would have never become [The New Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_New_Republic "The New Republic"). The legions of Battle Droids are worthy foes of [The Galactic Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Galactic_Republic "The Galactic Republic"). A Droid's recording of a Jedi meditation leads [Zayne Carrick](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Zayne_Carrick "Zayne Carrick") to fight his former masters. [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") are important pieces to the puzzle of what the galaxy has become. Some [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") designations are well known as heroes or villains: [C-3PO](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/C-3PO "C-3PO"), [HK-47](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/HK-47 "HK-47"), and [IG-88](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/IG-88 "IG-88").

Yet, some beings in the *Star Wars* universe consider [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") to be nothing more than tools- just hydrospanners with legs. [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") are sent into battle to be destroyed. They are unmourned if they do not return from unexplored regions of space. Indeed, some beings resent [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") for their efficiency and cause of the perception that they take jobs from organic beings who need feed their families. [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") are refused service in some businesses, and they are denied basic rights on some planets. In spite of, or perhaps because of, all their amazing achievements, [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") can bring out the worst in other beings. Galactic conflicts, such as [The Clone Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Clone_Wars "The Clone Wars"), in which [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") are programmed to kill and destroy are not forgotten by the beings whose homes and planets are overrun by mechanical menaces.

This chapter explores the role of [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") in the *Star Wars* universe, including their struggle for rights, their religious beliefs, and even a few old spacers' tales thrown in for good measure. It looks at [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") as equipment, as well as at some of the unique roleplaying opportunities available to [Droid Heroes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Heroes "Droid Heroes"). Players who would like to give extra spark to their [Droid Heroes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Heroes "Droid Heroes") can find plenty of interesting options. Gamemasters looking to add flavorful [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") characters to their stories can find useful hints, tips, hooks, and aids here.

## [Droid Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Equipment "Droid Equipment")

Unlike other heroes in the *Star Wars Roleplaying Game*, [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") characters are, literally, made from their equipment. Whether it is built-in or carried, factory installed or an aftermarket change, equipment defines what a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") can do, and, therefore, what it is. A [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") without its accessories is little more than a talking, thinking computer.

How a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") thinks of its equipment depends on the type of [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") it is, its intelligence, and its degree of independence. Carried equipment or possessions are less a part of a Droid's sense of identity than equipment that is fully installed and integrated into its processing system. Unintelligent [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") care nothing for their equipment, aside from the standard built-in self-preservation programming. [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") with minimal personalities might care more about keeping their systems functional, but not on an emotional level. A highly intelligent [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid"), or a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") with a highly developed personality, might care a great deal about its built-in systems and might grow attached to certain accessories.

Droid Equipment is also referred to as a system. Systems are divided into four categories: [Locomotion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Locomotion "Locomotion"), [Appendage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Appendage "Appendage"), [Processor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Processor "Processor"), or [Droid Accessory](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Accessory "Droid Accessory"). However, only those accessories that are integrated directly into the Droid's chassis are considered systems. An accessory that is attached to an appendage or carried by a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") is considered a possession.

Most Droid Equipment presented here requires installation on the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid"), using the [Modifying Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Modifying_Droids "Modifying Droids") rules. [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid")-specific equipment that does not require installation is noted as such. Equipment that other characters can use on [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") is also found in this chapter. The equipment costs listed are for new systems and typically include installation. However, Gamemasters can adjust costs depending on any number of factors, such as proximity to [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") service facilities, rarity of parts, or other local conditions.

In most of the galaxy, a Droid's master must request and pay for installation of a new system on a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid"). Few [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") shops are willing to make a change simply on a Droid's own request, even if it has the necessary credits. The [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") might be trying to circumvent its owner's wishes, so technicians are likely to refuse to risk the wrath of an owner or, in some cases, violate the law.

## [Droid Codex](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Codex "Droid Codex")

The number of [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") present in the *Star Wars* universe is enough to make one's head spin, for the sheer number of [Droid Models](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Models "Droid Models") alone could fill a Datapad's memory banks, and these [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") contribute to almost every facet of galactic society. With numerous manufacturing companies continually designing and building new lines of [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") as well as improving existing models, this industry has no end in sight.

This chapter, aptly named the Droid Codex, features a sampling of [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") from various periods of the *Star Wars* timeline that are newly written for the *Star Wars Roleplaying Game*. Each [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") presented has its own full entry that includes a statistics block, a description, a background, and an illustration. Although each entry is typically for the stock version of the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid"), any number of variants can exist and might reasonably be encountered, depending on the interests of the Gamemaster and the players. Each [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") also includes a statistics block for the [Protocol Format](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Protocol_Format "Protocol Format"). Gamemasters who are using the optional protocol rules can give the [Protocol Format](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Protocol_Format "Protocol Format") statistics block to the player who owns the Droid for quick reference.

Each [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") entry also includes a "Playing the Droid" sidebar, which includes a number of details about the construction and design of that [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid"). This area contains not only information about how to recreate the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") from scratch using the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") construction rules and new equipment from [Droid Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Equipment "Droid Equipment"), it also includes tips on how to properly portray the Droid's personality according to the data presented in [Droid Heroes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Heroes "Droid Heroes") and [Droid Allies](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Allies "Droid Allies") of this book.

The entries in this chapter indicate a number of modifications that Gamemasters can implement, letting the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") perform other functions not normally performed by stock models. These modifications might be mechanical upgrades or parts replacements that allow the Droid to undertake new tasks and provide some interesting twists and surprises during game play. These modifications can also serve as inspiration for developing your own variants of the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid").

In addition to the information listed above, each entry also contains a sidebar feature that provides additional background information about the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") and offers an adventure seed for an adventure revolving around that [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid"), to help the Gamemaster introduce the new [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") into the game.

# Mechanics (Int)

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:07:11Z.

*Reference Books: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook"), [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

Mechanics is a **Trained Only** skill.

You can bypass locks and traps, set and disarm explosives, fix malfunctioning devices, and modify and repair damaged [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids").

**Special:** You can [Take 10](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_10 "Take 10") or [Take 20](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_20 "Take 20") on a Mechanics check. When making a Mechanics check to [Jury-Rig](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jury-Rig "Jury-Rig"), you can't [Take 20](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_20 "Take 20"). Characters who are untrained in the Mechanics skill can still use the [Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another") action to assist on Mechanics checks.

**Retry:** You can usually retry a Mechanics check. In some specific cases, however, a failed Mechanics check has negative ramifications that prevent repeated check (see Disable Device below, for example).

## Applications

The following are the primary applications of the Mechanics skill.

### Disable Device

**Requires [Security Kit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Security_Kit "Security Kit"):** You can use this skill to disarm a security device, defeat a lock or trap, or rig a device to fail when it is used. The action takes a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"), and the intricacy or complexity of the item being disabled or sabotaged, as shown below:

| DEVICE | DC\* | EXAMPLES |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Simple | 15 | Sabotage a mechanical device, jam a blaster, bypass a basic mechanical lock |
| Tricky | 20 | Sabotage an electronic device, bypass a basic electronic lock |
| Complex | 25 | Disarm an electrical security system, bypass a complex mechanism or electronic lock |

*\*If you attempt to leave behind no trace of the tampering, increase the DC by 5.*

If the Mechanics check fails by 5 or more, something goes wrong. If it's a trap, you spring it. If it's some sort of sabotage, you think the device is disabled, but it still works normally.

**Requires [Tool Kit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tool_Kit "Tool Kit"):** Whenever you would make a Mechanics check to sabotage a piece of [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment"), you can install a booby trap to damage the next character who uses the item. For every 1d4 damage you would have the Booby Trap deal, you must increase the Mechanics DC by 5. So Booby Trapping a blaster to deal 3d4 damage to its next user requires a DC 30 Mechanics check (base DC 15 to jam the blaster, +15 for 3d4 damage).

Once the trap is installed, it attacks the next character to use the item, using your [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") against the target's [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"). If the attack hits, it deals the indicated damage.

### Handle Explosives

Setting a simple explosive to blow up at a certain spot doesn't require a check, but connecting and setting a detonator does. Also, placing an explosive for maximum effect against a structure calls for a check, as does disarming an explosive device. Setting a detonator, placing an explosive device, or disarming an explosive device is a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action").

Most explosives require a detonator to go off. Connecting a detonator to an explosive requires a DC 10 check. Failure means that the explosive fails to go off as planned. Failure by 10 or more means the explosive goes off as the detonator is being installed. You can make an explosive difficult to disarm. To do so, you choose the disarm DC before making your check to set the detonator (it must be higher than 10). Your DC to set the detonator is equal to the disarm DC - 5. For example, you might decide to make the disarm DC 20. The DC to set the detonator and disarm the explosives becomes 15 (instead of the normal 10).

Carefully placing an explosive against a stationary, unattended inanimate object increases the damage dealt by exploiting weaknesses in its construction. The GM makes the check (so that you don't know exactly how well you've done). On a result of 15 or higher, you ignore the [Damage Reduction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Reduction "Damage Reduction") of any object to which the explosives are attached. On a result of 25 or higher, the explosive deals double damage to the structure or vehicle against which it's placed. On a result of 35 or higher, it deals triple damage. In all cases, it deals normal damage to all other targets within its [Burst](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Burst "Burst") radius.

Using a [Security Kit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Security_Kit "Security Kit") to disarm an explosive that has been set to go off requires a check. The DC is usually 15, unless the one who set the detonator chose a higher disarm DC (see above). If you fail the check, you do not disarm the explosive. If you fail it by 5 or more, the explosive detonates while you are adjacent to it.

### Jury-Rig

You can make temporary repairs to any [Disabled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disabled "Disabled") mechanical or electronic device, from a simple tool to a complex component. Jury-rigging is a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action") and requires a successful DC 25 check. If you use a [Tool Kit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tool_Kit "Tool Kit"), you gain a +5 [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") bonus on the check. A jury-rigged device moves +2 steps on the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") and gains 1d8 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points"). At the end of the scene or encounter, the jury-rigged device moves -5 steps along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") and becomes [Disabled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disabled "Disabled") again.

### Repair

**Requires [Tool Kit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tool_Kit "Tool Kit"):** You can repair a damaged or [Disabled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disabled "Disabled") device (including [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") and [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles")). This requires at least 1 hour of work, at the end of which time you must make a Mechanics check. Only one character may Repair a given object at a time, but other characters may use the [Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another") Action to assist.

You can spend 1 hour and make a DC 20 Mechanics check to repair a damaged or [Disabled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disabled "Disabled") Droid, restoring [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") equal to the Droid's Character Level and removing any [Persistent Conditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persistent_Conditions "Persistent Conditions") currently affecting the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid"). A [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") can attempt to Repair itself, but it takes a -5 penalty on the Mechanics check.

You can spend 1 hour and make a DC 20 Mechanics check to repair a damaged or [Disabled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disabled "Disabled") object, restoring 1d8 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") and removing any [Persistent Conditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persistent_Conditions "Persistent Conditions") currently affecting the device or [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle"). If you are on board a damaged [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") while you attempt to Repair it, apply any penalties from the Vehicle's position on the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") on your Mechanics check. (Major [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") Repairs are best attempted in a garage, hangar, dry dock, or other specialized facility.)

## Additional Applications

The following applications of the Mechanics skill are compiled from the subsequent [Reference Books](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reference_Books "Reference Books") released for *Saga Edition*:

### Biotech Adaptation

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

**Requires [Tool Kit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tool_Kit "Tool Kit"):** You can use the Mechanics skill to create mechanical connections to a particular [Biotech Device](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Biotech_Device "Biotech Device"). When you use this application of the Mechanics skill, you perform one of the following actions:

- *Graft Technology:* You can graft sensors, communications equipment, and tiny computers onto a [Biotech Device](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Biotech_Device "Biotech Device"). You must spend 1 hour and make a DC 25 Mechanics check to do so. Once you have, you may treat the [Biotech Device](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Biotech_Device "Biotech Device") as a normal (Non-Biotech) device of the same type for the purposes of of the Mechanics and [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") skills.
  - For example, if you have a Villip (effectively a [Comlink](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Comlink "Comlink") with the [Biotech General Template](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Biotech_General_Template "Biotech General Template")), you may use this skill to graft technology onto the Villip. From that point on, you can apply any use of the Mechanics skill or the [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") skill on that device as if it were a normal [Comlink](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Comlink "Comlink").
  - You may not use the [Tech Specialist](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tech_Specialist "Tech Specialist") feat on a [Biotech Device](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Biotech_Device "Biotech Device"), even if it has been grafted with technology (you must use the [Biotech Specialist](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Biotech_Specialist "Biotech Specialist") feat instead).
- *Mechanical Enhancements:* You can mechanically enhance a [Biotech Device](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Biotech_Device "Biotech Device") or [Biotech Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Biotech_Vehicle "Biotech Vehicle"), effectively connecting another device to that piece of Biotech. You must spend 1 hour and make a DC 30 Mechanics check to do so. Once you have, you effectively treat the [Biotech Device](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Biotech_Device "Biotech Device") as though it also has the traits of the device you have enhanced it with.
  - You can only use this application of Biotech Adaptation on [Biotech Devices](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Biotech_Devices "Biotech Devices") upon which you have also used the Graft Technology application (see above). You can only enhance a [Biotech Device](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Biotech_Device "Biotech Device") with another device of its size or smaller.
  - A [Biotech Device](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Biotech_Device "Biotech Device") can only have one such mechanical enhancement, and only devices (not weapons or armor) can be attached to a [Biotech Device](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Biotech_Device "Biotech Device") in this manner.
  - For example, if you posses a [Razor Bug](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Razor_Bug "Razor Bug") (a Tiny object), you could attach an [Audio Recorder](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Audio_Recorder "Audio Recorder") to the [Razor Bug](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Razor_Bug "Razor Bug") using this application of Biotech Adaptation; at a later point, you could activate the [Recording Unit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Recording_Unit "Recording Unit") and hurl the [Razor Bug](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Razor_Bug "Razor Bug") onto a wall to clandestinely record conversations in the area.

Connecting Objects TogetherConnecting Objects Together

The Biotech Adaptation application allow you to connect [Biotech Devices](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Biotech_Devices "Biotech Devices") to non-Biotech Devices, effectively creating a hybrid device. For example, you could use the skill to connect [Recording Devices](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Recording_Devices "Recording Devices") to [Yuuzhan Vong](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong "Yuuzhan Vong") bugs like [Jariah Syn](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jariah_Syn "Jariah Syn") does before rescuing [Cade Skywalker](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cade_Skywalker "Cade Skywalker") from [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant"). However, Gamemasters should note that this application of the Skill probably shouldn't allow you to do something you couldn't do by connecting two non-Biotech Devices together.

Most of the time, you can just do things like connect a computer to a [Datapad](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Datapad "Datapad") without needing a [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check"). However, other combinations need to be more closely regulated; for example, connecting a recording rod to a [Thermal Detonator](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Thermal_Detonator "Thermal Detonator") to create an improvised, sound-based mine that might be exciting thing to do once, but if your players abuse that combination, it might be time to rethink how often you allow it. Feel free to ask for a Mechanics check, scaling the DC based on how common you want the modification to be.

Lastly, no equipment combination should be a better option than an existing piece of [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment"). Take a look at other gear and see if there is already a piece of [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") that does what your heroes want to do, and use that as a model for improvising the outcome of their creativity. Creative thinking is good, and you should encourage it among your players; just make sure it doesn't obviate existing options in the game.

### Build Object

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

**Requires [Tool Kit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tool_Kit "Tool Kit"):** You can make a Mechanics check to construct an object (other than a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid")) from scratch and/or with manufactured parts. Building an object requires a proper [Tool Kit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tool_Kit "Tool Kit"), raw materials, and possibly access to an electronics or machine shop. Some objects are beyond the ability of most characters. The raw materials for an object cost the same as the completed object, though heroes who have the [Scavenger](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scavenger "Scavenger") feat can often come up with raw materials scrounged from other [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment"). Use the costs given in in the [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") sections as a base guide for material costs. Remote locations, rare parts, or expensive materials might raise the costs to double, triple, or even higher.

When building an object, determine the object's final [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") based on the [Object Statistics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Object_Statistics "Object Statistics") table. For [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles"), use the actual [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") of the completed object. All objects being built in this manner start at 0 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points"). Each hour that you work on building an object, make a Mechanics check against the DC for the objects type (see Object Complexity table, below). Success indicates you build 1d8 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points")' worth of the object's total [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points"). When the item reaches full [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points"), it is completed and can be used as normal.

For example, a Large manufactured object has 10 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points"). However, since it is a Large item, it effectively has 50 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") for the purpose of determining how long it takes to build the object (how many [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") you must build before it is completed). ### Environmental Adaptation

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

**Requires [Tool Kit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tool_Kit "Tool Kit"):** Many [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") and objects do not work well in extremely harsh environments. The intense cold of [Hoth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hoth?action=edit&redlink=1 "Hoth (page does not exist)") or the severe heat of the day side of [Ryloth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ryloth "Ryloth") can cause [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") and objects to function poorly or not at all. You can spend 10 minutes adapting one object (or one hour adapting one [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle")) to the extreme environmental hazards, after which you make a Mechanics check.

If your Mechanics check result exceeds the object or Vehicle's current [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"), you can use the result of your Mechanics check in place of its [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") when it is attacked by [Extreme Temperatures](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Extreme_Temperatures "Extreme Temperatures") or [Atmosphere](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Atmosphere "Atmosphere") Hazards. However, when you do so, you effectively adapt the object or Vehicle's defenses for that particular environment. If you use that [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") or object on another planet that has a different environment, you must repeat this process for the new conditions.

You can [Take 20](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_20 "Take 20") on this check, but the adaptation takes 20 times as long.

### Hot Shot

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

**Requires [Tool Kit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tool_Kit "Tool Kit"):** You can overload an energy weapon to deliver a deadlier attack, but at the risk of a mishap or explosion. You must spend 1 hour and succeed on a Mechanics check. The DC is 20 plus 5 for every weapon size category above Tiny. If the check succeeds, the weapon deals an additional +3 points of damage. Any natural attack roll of 5 or lower, however, causes the weapon to become [Disabled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disabled "Disabled").

A natural attack roll of 1 causes the weapon to explode in the wielder's hands, dealing the modified weapon's damage to the wielder and half damage to all adjacent creatures.

### Improvised Connection

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

**Requires [Tool Kit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tool_Kit "Tool Kit"):** You may attempt to connect electronic devices or computers without proper cables, using improvised materials. This endeavor takes 1 hour and requires a DC 20 Mechanics check to succeed.

### Refit Antiquated Item

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

**Requires [Tool Kit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tool_Kit "Tool Kit"):** You can use the Mechanics skill to refit an [Antiquated Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Antiquated_Vehicle "Antiquated Vehicle") or [Antiquated Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Antiquated_Weapon "Antiquated Weapon"); using modern technology to replace outdated components. You must spend 20% of the base value of the [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") or weapon (before the application of the [Antiquated Vehicle Template](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Antiquated_Vehicle_Template "Antiquated Vehicle Template") or [Antiquated Weapon Template](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Antiquated_Weapon_Template "Antiquated Weapon Template")) on raw materials for the upgrade. The refitting process takes a number of days (each day representing 8 hours of continuous work) determined by the object's size: Medium or smaller, 1 day; Large, 2 days; Huge, 5 days; Gargantuan, 10 days; Colossal, 20 days; Colossal (Frigate), 60 days; Colossal (Cruiser), 180 days; Colossal (Station), 360 days.

If multiple characters [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in the Mechanics skill work on the refit simultaneously, divide the amount of time taken by the number of characters working on the refit (a maximum of 20 characters can reduce the refit time, minimum of 1 day). Once the work is complete, apply the [Refitted Vehicle Template](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Refitted_Vehicle_Template "Refitted Vehicle Template") or [Refitted Weapon Template](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Refitted_Weapon_Template "Refitted Weapon Template") to the base item instead of the [Antiquated Vehicle Template](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Antiquated_Vehicle_Template "Antiquated Vehicle Template") or [Antiquated Weapon Template](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Antiquated_Weapon_Template "Antiquated Weapon Template").

## Mechanics Untrained

*Reference Book: [Web Enhancements](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Web_Enhancements "Web Enhancements") ([Saga Edition FAQ](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Saga_Edition_FAQ "Saga Edition FAQ") (E20))*

To use Mechanics as described, you must be [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in the skill. You can make a Mechanics check [Untrained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Untrained "Untrained"), but you can't [Take 10](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_10 "Take 10") or [Take 20](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_20 "Take 20"). Furthermore, if you fail at an [Untrained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Untrained "Untrained") Mechanics check, the object in question moves -1 step on the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") (or, if the object was already disabled, it is instead destroyed).

### Task Training

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

Typically, a hero must be [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in the Mechanics skill to carry out any uses of that skill. However, heroes can be trained to do specific, simple tasks without the comprehensive knowledge normally required to use the complete skill. This specific training allows a hero to make an [Untrained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Untrained "Untrained") Mechanics check when attempting the task. At the Gamemaster's discretion, heroes already [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in the skill might receive a +2 circumstance bonus when attempting a task in which they received specific training. This bonus is best applied when the task at hand is unusual even for someone [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in Mechanics.

Task Training takes a minimum of 30 minutes for simple tasks and up to two hours for moderately complex tasks. Tasks requiring additional time or complexity are beyond the ability of heroes [Untrained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Untrained "Untrained") in the Mechanics skill. The trainer rolls a Mechanics check against the DC of the final task, and can benefit from [Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another") from one assistant also [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in the Mechanics skill.

If the check fails, the trainer fails to adequately demonstrate the task to his or her trainees and must start again. If the check succeeds, the trainee rolls a DC 25 [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") check and gain a +1 insight bonus for every 5 points the trainer rolled on his or her Mechanics check above the final task DC. If successful, the trainee learns the task.

Gamemasters can allow heroes to apply their Task Training knowledge to similar tasks, but this use of specific training must be limited so that it does not replace becoming [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in the Mechanics skill. Gamemasters might also rule that after a certain period of disuse, the hero must retrain to be reminded of the specific details of carrying out a particular task.

Although most useful for the Mechanics skill, Task Training can be applied to other [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills") (other than [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force")) at the Gamemaster's discretion.

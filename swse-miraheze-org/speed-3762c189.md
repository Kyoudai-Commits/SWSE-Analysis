# Speed

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:09:13Z.

*Reference Books: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook"), [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

Your Speed tells you how far you can [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") with a single [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action"). Your Speed depends mostly on your [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species"), although certain kinds of [Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor "Armor") can reduce your Speed. Some creatures, [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids"), and [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") have a natural Climb, Burrow, Fly, and/or Swim Speed in addition to their normal Walking Speed. Any effect that reduces Speed affects all of a creature's movement modes unless noted otherwise.

Moving Diagonally costs double. When [Moving](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Moving "Moving") or counting along a diagonal path, each diagonal step counts as 2 squares. If a character moves diagonally through [Low Objects](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Low_Objects "Low Objects") or Difficult Terrain, the cost of movement doubles twice (that is, each square counts as 4 squares).

Speed is measured in Squares. Each Square represents 1.5 meters (about 5 feet).

### Encumbrance

Wearing [Medium Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Medium_Armor "Medium Armor") or [Heavy Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Armor "Heavy Armor") or carrying a [Heavy Load](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Load "Heavy Load") reduces your character's Speed to three-quarters normal (4 squares if your Base Speed is 6 squares, or 3 squares if your Base Speed is 4 squares).

While wearing [Heavy Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Armor "Heavy Armor") or carrying a [Heavy Load](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Load "Heavy Load"), you can only [Run](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Run "Run") at triple your Speed (instead of quadruple).

### Flight

A creature with a Fly Speed is capable of flight, but not if carrying a [Heavy Load](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Load "Heavy Load").

Every [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") has a Speed, given in squares. The [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot") may move up to its listed Speed as a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action"), and up to four times its Speed with the [All-Out Movement](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/All-Out_Movement "All-Out Movement") Action. [Starships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships "Starships") and [Airspeeders](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Airspeeders "Airspeeders") have a separate listing for their Speed in [Starship Scale](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Scale "Starship Scale").

### Maximum Velocity

This is the maximum Speed a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") may move in [Character Scale](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Character_Scale "Character Scale"). It is seldom relevant in combat, because such speeds quickly move the vessel off the battle map, and out of range.

Every [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion") has a Speed, given in squares. A [Field Commander](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Field_Commander "Field Commander") can move a [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion") up to its listed Speed as a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action"), and up to four times its Speed with the [Hard March](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hard_March "Hard March") Action. All Speeds are listed in [Starship Scale](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Scale "Starship Scale"). For the most part, all [Battalions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalions "Battalions") based on creatures or characters have a ground Speed of 1. Battalions with the [Vehicle Battalion Template](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Battalion_Template?action=edit&redlink=1 "Vehicle Battalion Template (page does not exist)") or [Mounted Battalion Template](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mounted_Battalion_Template?action=edit&redlink=1 "Mounted Battalion Template (page does not exist)") might move faster.

### Vehicle Speed

Some [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles"), particularly [Ground Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ground_Vehicles "Ground Vehicles"), do not have a [Starship Scale](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Scale "Starship Scale") Speed. Use the table below to determine the [Starship Scale](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Scale "Starship Scale") Speed of any Battalions that do not have such a Speed listed.

| CHARACTER SCALE SPEED | STARSHIP SCALE SPEED |
| --- | --- |
| 0-11 Squares | 1 Square |
| 12-15 Squares | 2 Squares |
| 16+ Squares | 3 Squares |

## Diagonal Movement

[Moving](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Moving "Moving") diagonally costs double. When Moving or counting along a diagonal path, each diagonal step counts as 2 squares. If a character moves diagonally through [Low Objects](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Low_Objects "Low Objects") or Difficult Terrain, the cost of movement doubles twice (that is, each square counts as 4 squares). A character can't make Diagonal Movement past the corner or end of a wall that extends to a grid center.

## Difficult Terrain

Broken ground, buckled deck plating, and similar obstacles are collectively referred to as Difficult Terrain. It costs twice as much to move into a square containing Difficult Terrain. Creatures of Large size and bigger must pay the extra cost for moving across Difficult Terrain if any part of their Fighting Space moves into such a square. Difficult Terrain does not block [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") or provide [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover").

## Immobilized

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

When a creature is Immobilized it cannot move by its own volition. An Immobilized creature can take [Move Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Actions "Move Actions"), but it cannot [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") from its current space, and it cannot make [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks") associated with movement (such as [Acrobatics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatics "Acrobatics"), [Climb](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Climb "Climb"), [Jump](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jump "Jump"), or [Swim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swim "Swim")).

An Immobilized creature can still be moved by other effects, such as being the target of a [Bantha Rush](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bantha_Rush "Bantha Rush") or the *[Force Thrust](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Thrust "Force Thrust")* Force Power.

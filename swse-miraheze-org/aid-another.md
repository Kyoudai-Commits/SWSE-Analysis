# Aid Another

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:05:53Z.

*Reference Books: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook"), [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can aid an ally's next skill check or attack roll, or you can interfere with an enemy's attacks.

## Aiding a Skill Check

You can help another character achieve success on their [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") or [Ability Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Check "Ability Check") by making the same kind of [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") or [Ability Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Check "Ability Check") in a cooperative effort. If you roll a 10 or higher on your check, the character you are helping receives a +2 bonus on their own check. You can't [Take 10](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_10 "Take 10") on a [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") or [Ability Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Check "Ability Check") to Aid Another.

In [Vehicle Combat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Combat "Vehicle Combat"), only the [Copilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Copilot "Copilot") can assist on [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") checks, and only the [Commander](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Commander "Commander") can assist on [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") checks. Any [Crew Position](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Crew_Position "Crew Position") can aid any other [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") or [Ability Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Check "Ability Check").

## Aiding an Attack Roll

In combat, you can Aid Another character's attack by forcing an opponent to avoid your own attacks, making it more difficult for them to avoid your ally. Select an opponent and make an attack against a [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") of 10. If you succeed, you grant a +2 bonus on a single ally's next attack roll against that opponent.

In [Vehicle Combat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Combat "Vehicle Combat"), you can Aid Another character's attack by providing improved targeting data, or by coordinating that Attack with other Vehicle Actions.

- A [Gunner](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunner "Gunner") can grant a +2 bonus on another character's attack roll by making an attack against a [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") of 10.
- A [System Operator](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/System_Operator "System Operator") can grant a +2 bonus on another character's attack roll by making a DC 10 [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") check.
- A [Commander](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Commander "Commander") can grant a +2 bonus on another character's attack roll by making a DC 10 [Knowledge (Tactics)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Tactics) "Knowledge (Tactics)") check.

In [Mass Combat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mass_Combat "Mass Combat"), you can aid the attack roll of another [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion") or [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") by adding your Battalion's firepower to the attack.

- A [Field Commander](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Field_Commander "Field Commander") or [First Officer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/First_Officer "First Officer") can make a DC 10 [Knowledge (Tactics)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Tactics) "Knowledge (Tactics)") check to grant a single adjacent allied [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion") or [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") a +2 bonus on its next attack roll made before the beginning of the Field Commander's next turn.
- An [Attack Leader](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_Leader "Attack Leader") selects a target within the range of their Battalion's weapons, and the [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion") makes an attack roll against a [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") of 10 (applying all [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range") penalties as though the [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion") is attacking the designated target). If this attack is successful, the [Attack Leader](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_Leader "Attack Leader") grants a single adjacent allied [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion") or [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") a +2 bonus on its next attack roll made against that target before the beginning of the Attack Leader's next turn.

## Suppressing an Enemy

In combat, you can distract or interfere with an opponent, making their attacks more difficult. Select an opponent and make an attack against a [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") of 10. If you succeed, that opponent takes a -2 penalty on its next attack roll.

You can't use the Aid Another Action to Suppress a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle"), but you can Suppress an individual [Gunner](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunner "Gunner") (such as the lead [Gunner](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunner "Gunner") for a single [Weapon Battery](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Battery "Weapon Battery")).

In [Mass Combat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mass_Combat "Mass Combat"), you can suppress the attack of an enemy [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion") or [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") by bombarding the target with fire. An [Attack Leader](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_Leader "Attack Leader") can select a target within the range of their Battalion's weapons, and the [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion") makes an attack roll against a [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") of 10 (applying all [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range") penalties as though the [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion") is attacking the designated target). If this attack is successful, the [Attack Leader](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_Leader "Attack Leader") causes the target [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion") or [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") to take a -2 penalty on its next attack roll.

# The Kilian Rangers

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Kilian_Rangers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Kilian_Rangers) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:05:11Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

The Kilia System lies in an area of space marked "Unknown" on most astrogation charts. Over a thousand years ago, [Humans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Humans "Humans") colonized [Kilia IV](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kilia_IV?action=edit&redlink=1 "Kilia IV (page does not exist)"), the sole habitable planet in the system, bringing with them only the basic requirements to begin an agricultural colony. Since that time, the Kilians' technology has faded. They have only a handful of functioning technological devices, and their society has adapted to their situation. Kilians live in a basic feudal system, with a noble class ruling over the common working class of farmers and other merchants.

Though [Kilia IV](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kilia_IV?action=edit&redlink=1 "Kilia IV (page does not exist)") has no Jedi, it does have Force-sensitive inhabitants. Perhaps guided by what little the colonists knew of the old Jedi Order, the Kilians developed their own Force-using tradition, The Kilian Rangers. This small group defends the people of [Kilia IV](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kilia_IV?action=edit&redlink=1 "Kilia IV (page does not exist)") and enforces the law. The Kilian Rangers do not have [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers"), but they have a similar affinity for the [Siang Lance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Siang_Lance "Siang Lance") and the [Shield Gauntlet](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shield_Gauntlet "Shield Gauntlet"). Like a Jedi's [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber"), these devices serve as badges of office and as combat instruments. Kilian Rangers have three ranks- Kilian Squire, Kilian Ranger, and Kilian Lord- that are roughly analogous to the ranks of Padawan, Jedi Knight, and Jedi Master respectively.

Of course, where [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") exists, so too does [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"). Some Kilian Rangers fall into its embrace and follow their own desires, believing themselves too powerful to waste their time defending the peasants of [Kilia IV](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kilia_IV?action=edit&redlink=1 "Kilia IV (page does not exist)"). Those individuals take the title of Kilian Renegade.

**Membership:** A Kilian who has the [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity") feat can become a member of this [Force Tradition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Tradition "Force Tradition") by being accepted as a Squire to an existing Kilian Lord or Ranger. The candidate will be accepted only if there are fewer than five current Kilian Rangers (Squires, Rangers, and Lords combined). Members of The Kilian Rangers [Force Tradition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Tradition "Force Tradition") can select Talents from the [Kilian Ranger Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kilian_Ranger_Talent_Tree "Kilian Ranger Talent Tree").

# Stealth (Dex)

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:06:46Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

Stealth skill checks are affected by an **[Armor Check Penalty](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor_Check_Penalty "Armor Check Penalty")**.

Use this skill to slink past a sentry without being heard, catch your enemy off-guard, snipe from a concealed location, or perform sleight of hand.

**Special:** You can [Take 10](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_10 "Take 10") when making a Stealth check, but you can't [Take 20](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_20 "Take 20").

## Applications

The following are the primary applications of the Stealth skill.

### Sneak

Your Stealth check sets the DC for [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") checks made to notice you. If an opponent's [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") check equals or exceeds your Stealth check, your opponent notices you.

Any circumstance that hampers your ability to sneak imposes a -2 penalty on your check, while favorable circumstances grant a +2 bonus. For example, sneaking across a surface littered with debris imposes a -2 penalty on your Stealth check, while a room filled with abundant hiding places grants a +2 bonus on your check. If you move more than your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") in any given round, you take a -5 penalty on your Stealth check. If you move more than twice your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") in any given round, you take a -10 penalty on your Stealth check.

Your size provides a modifier to your Stealth checks:

| SIZE | MODIFIER |  | SIZE | MODIFIER |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fine | +20 | Large | -5 |
| Diminutive | +15 | Huge | -10 |
| Tiny | +10 | Gargantuan | -15 |
| Small | +5 | Colossal | -20 |
| Medium | +0 |  | |

### Conceal Item

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can attempt to conceal an item (such as a weapon) on your person. The concealed object must be at least one size smaller than you, and you get a modifier on your skill check based on the object's relative size:

| RELATIVE SIZE TO CHARACTER | MODIFIER |
| --- | --- |
| One size smaller | -5 |
| Two sizes smaller | +0 |
| Three sizes smaller | +5 |
| Four or more sizes smaller | +10 |

Other characters may notice a concealed object with a successful [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") check (opposed by your Stealth check result), but only if you do not have [Total Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Total_Concealment "Total Concealment"). A character gains a +10 circumstance bonus on their [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") check if they are physically touching you to search for concealed items; this requires a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action") that can only be performed if you are a willing, [Pinned](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pinned "Pinned"), or [Helpless](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Helpless "Helpless") target.

Drawing a Concealed Item is a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action").

### Create a Diversion to Hide

You can use the [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") skill to help you be stealthy. A successful [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") check gives you the momentary diversion you need to attempt a Stealth check even though people are aware of you. While the others turn their attention from you, you can make a Stealth check (as normal, with no penalty) if you can reach a hiding place of some kind as a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action").

### Pick Pocket

With a successful Stealth check as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can pilfer a small, hand-sized object from a target within reach. Your Stealth check is opposed by the target's [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") check, and the target gains a +5 bonus. If you fail by 4 or less, you are unable to take the item, but the target does not notice the effort. If you fail by 5 or more, you are unable to take the item and the target catches you in the act.

### Sleight of Hand

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can palm hand-sized objects, perform minor feats of legerdemain, or attempt to perform a minor action without being noticed (such as flipping a switch, pulling out a [Thermal Detonator](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Thermal_Detonator "Thermal Detonator"), or drawing a pistol under the cover of a table). All such efforts are opposed by observers' [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") check. Any observer that beats your Stealth check notices the action you attempted, and knows how you did it.

### Snipe

After making a ranged attack from hiding, you can try to hide again. You must be at least 2 squares from the target, and you must have already have successfully used Stealth to hide from the target. Make a new Stealth check (as normal, but with a -10 penalty) as a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action"). If you succeed, you remain hidden; otherwise, your location is revealed.

## Additional Applications

The following applications of the Stealth skill are compiled from the subsequent [Reference Books](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reference_Books "Reference Books") released for *Saga Edition*:

### Conceal Large Items

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

As a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"), you can attempt to conceal or hide a large item inside a room, behind a piece of furniture, inside a hidden compartment, or within a shipboard locker or other location. A large item is one that is one that is the same size category as you or larger.

Other characters can [Notice](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Notice "Notice") a concealed object or, if the item has [Total Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Total_Concealment "Total Concealment"), discover a telltale sign that something is amiss (such as unusual scratches, fibers, or smells) by making a successful [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") check (opposed by your Stealth check result). A character gains a +10 circumstance bonus to their [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") check if they are in the same square as the item or an adjacent square. Rooms or containers specifically constructed to conceal items can grant an [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") bonus of +5 to +20 (for features such as top-of-the-line [Smuggler's Compartments](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Smuggler%27s_Compartments "Smuggler's Compartments")) on your Stealth check.

You may not [Take 20](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_20 "Take 20") on this check, but you may [Take 10](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_10 "Take 10").

### Drop

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

You can use Stealth to deposit a small concealed item without attracting notice, such as slipping a drug into a drink or planting a bug on a person's clothes. Any observer that beats your Stealth check with a [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") check notices the Drop.

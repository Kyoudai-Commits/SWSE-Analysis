# Philosophy

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Philosophy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Philosophy) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:04:42Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

Without it, Palpatine would have no power to put his plans into motion as effectively as he did. Without it, the Sith-controlled Empire could not replace the Republic as the central government of the galaxy. Without it, neither [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") nor [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") would have any extraordinary abilities to help them accomplish their goals. Without [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), we would not have [Anakin Skywalker](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Anakin_Skywalker "Anakin Skywalker"), destined to fulfill an age-old prophecy.

Throughout the *Star Wars* saga, we have heard [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") referred to as an energy field created by all living things, the source of power for all Jedi, and that which unites all life in the galaxy. Although [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") is all that and more, we do not have very specific information about it. This chapter delves into the beliefs held by [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"), [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith"), and other [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions") in an attempt to answer pertinent questions about [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force").

## What is the Force?

[The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), by its nature, is one of the most enigmatic concepts of the *Star Wars* galaxy. It can both heal and harm. It can be a boon or a detriment. Depending on which philosophy of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") one follows, the interpretation of what [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") is and what it can do varies widely.

Of all the [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions") in the galaxy, [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order") probably has the best understanding of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). Even within the ranks of [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"), [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") takes on different roles and works in different ways. While they might seem similar in scope, the beliefs behind these interpretations can be vastly dissimilar.

### The Living Force

The Living Force, as accepted by Jedi throughout the ages, is believed to exist in most living creatures. Jedi who believe in the tenets of The Living Force rely on their instincts and become attuned to the living things around them. They are mindful of the future and what might result from their current actions, but they remain focused on the present. The Living Force has both a Light Side and a Dark Side, and Force-users must be mindful of their emotions lest they succumb to the temptation of [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"). Those who adhere to the doctrines of The Living Force believe that life creates [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") and that [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") is an omnipresent field of energy that surrounds and permeates living things, and that living things are connected by it. Through The Living Force, certain Jedi are able to retain their identities after death, manifesting as [Force Spirits](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Spirits "Force Spirits") while still becoming one with [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force").

Jedi who adhere to The Living Force view recognize the existence of midichlorians in all living beings. The higher the concentration of midichlorians in a life form, the stronger that life form's connection is to [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), and the greater its potential to use it- for good or evil.

### The Unifying Force

The Unifying Force, garnering less support among [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") than The Living Force, teaches that [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") is a singular power and has neither a Light Side nor a Dark Side. The Unifying Force has no sides and does not take sides, treating all living creatures equally. This view has been supported by the likes of [Vergere](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vergere?action=edit&redlink=1 "Vergere (page does not exist)") and [Emperor Palpatine](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Emperor_Palpatine "Emperor Palpatine"). (The only significant difference between the Jedi view and Palpatine's view is that [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") see [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") as a means to an end, while [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") see it as an end in itself.) Those who follow The Unifying Force tend to keep their eyes on the future and possibilities, leading them to act in ways to fulfill a [Destiny](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny "Destiny") instead of focusing on the here and now, like those who following The Living Force.

One particular view of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") that is often confused with The Unifying Force is known as The Potentium. While both philosophies believe that there is only one Force, The Potentium's take on the matter differs by stating that [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") is inherently good and what is perceived as [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") is actually a twisted perversion of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). By that standard, anyone following The Potentium way of thinking could perform any action and use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") in any way they see fit, as long as the intention behind it is good. [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order") does not support this belief at all, acknowledging its existence as nothing more than a means of misguiding people to [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") and subverting Jedi teachings.

### Other Interpretations

Other [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions") offer even more interpretations of the nature of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), providing further argument that [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") still remains a mystery for most of those who choose to study it.

[The Aing-Tii Monks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Aing-Tii_Monks "The Aing-Tii Monks"), based out of the Kathol Rift, view [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") in much the same way as [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"). However, they see it as as not having a Light Side or a Dark Side. They believe that [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") has varying levels, much like a rainbow, and can manifest itself in many different ways. People who use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") are not strictly good or purely evil when they use it. What they choose to do with it helps determine the color of The Force's spectrum in which they engross themselves.

[The Fallanassi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Fallanassi "The Fallanassi") believe in a power that works very similarly to [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), but they call it The White Current. The White Current flows like a river through the galaxy and all living beings. As such, a single person attempting to tap into it is like trying to divert a raging river with one's hand; it cannot be done. Instead, [The Fallanassi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Fallanassi "The Fallanassi") immerse themselves in the Current and let it flow through them like a conduit. They do not impose their will on it, but they merely shape the flow of the Current into whichever form is needed for the task at hand.

[The Sorcerers of Tund](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sorcerers_of_Tund "The Sorcerers of Tund") use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") in much the same manner as [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith"), but they portray the source of their power as magic. They have crafted a litany of arcane symbols and languages that permit them wield their [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") as spells. They use these spells to create illusions and alter their forms. Their belief in [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") as a magical power is so strong that even the most influential Jedi cannot hope to convince them otherwise.

Many beings beyond the Kathol Rift tap into a Force-like power called The Ta-Ree. The Ta-Ree is actually a twisted manipulation of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") in that region of space. It was created when a group of ancient [Kathol](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kathol?action=edit&redlink=1 "Kathol (page does not exist)") entered an immense crystalline structure to protect themselves from death; unfortunately for them, the structure's guardian decided to trap them inside. After a time, the survivors were absorbed by their crystal prison and twisted [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") and the fabric of space around it.

## Aspects of the Force

It's safe to say that throughout the galaxy, different [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions") interpret and view [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") differently. This section focuses on the philosophies accepted by [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order"), since [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") offer the most widely accepted and practiced viewpoints.

Throughout The Jedi Order's history, the endless struggle against [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") has revealed that [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") breaks down into two commonly opposed aspects: The Light Side and The Dark Side. While the majority of Jedi believe that The Light Side must prevail for order to be maintained, other acknowledge that The Dark Side's influence cannot be erased and will always be around, affecting those who call upon it.

### The Light Side

The part of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") known as The Light Side deals mainly with the ideals pertaining to the preservation and advancement of life: good, benevolence, and healing. Those who hold themselves to the tenets of The Light Side strive to establish harmony with their surroundings and the creatures that dwell in it. They act out of wisdom and logic, making decisions based on knowledge and peace instead of emotion and haste.

[The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"), as champions of The Light Side of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), take this view very seriously and have incorporated a variety of values and techniques to help promote this way of thinking and nurture the emotions that promote The Light Side. These values have accumulated into what is known as [The Jedi Code](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Code "The Jedi Code"), the modern version of which was documented and commented upon by Master [Odan-Urr](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Odan-Urr "Odan-Urr").

The Jedi CodeThe Jedi Code

While The Jedi Code dictates a number of issues relating to understanding and mastering [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), it is not entirely about that. A Jedi must feel [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to know it, and ancient texts and rules cannot substitute for that experience. At the same time, a Jedi's social behavior cannot be learned by applying [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to it. The Jedi Code also gives a set of expectations and guidelines by which a Jedi must conduct themselves. By following the Code, anyone can open up and allow The Light Side of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to flow freely through them and gain a better understanding of the universe and everything around them.

A closer examination of The Jedi Code helps to provide some insight not only into the way Jedi behave but also into the way following such strictures helps to foster a way of life conductive to averting the temptations of The Dark Side. The Code is categorized into three subjects: self-discipline, responsibility, and public service.

## Self-Discipline

Self-discipline is one of the key factors of Jedi behavior, and many lessons taught to [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") from a very young age focus on this basic doctrine. At first, the lessons are simple and no different than the lessons taught to other children throughout the galaxy. But as the young Jedi grows, the lessons increase in complexity to emphasize the importance of a disciplined life.

- **Conquer Arrogance:** A Jedi is taught that they are no better than another being who cannot touch [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). A Jedi is not a Jedi simply because of their strong connection to [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"); they are a Jedi because somebody taught them to be one.
- **Conquer Overconfidence:** As young Jedi pupils learn of their Force-using abilities, they start to take on the behavior that they can do anything with that power. Jedi instructors teach these students to realize their limits so that they can accurately measure how far they can go, as well as understand their own limitations.
- **Conquer Defeatism:** Possibly the greatest enemy a Jedi must face is the unshakable belief that nothing can be accomplished, regardless of the size of the task. A Jedi should plan for success before contemplating failure. Planning to fail increases the chance to fail, and expecting to lose encourages one to put forth insufficient effort.
- **Conquer Stubbornness:** A Jedi who focuses too much on a single goal or perspective loses sight of other possibilities, including ones that might yield better results. A Jedi must learn that staying on one path or clinging to one point of view can be costly. A Jedi must keep their mind open and not be afraid to change with the situation.
- **Conquer Recklessness:** Young Jedi always seem too anxious to master their [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers") and charge into battle without thinking ahead and developing some form of plan. These Jedi lack self-restraint. Acting impulsively has many times led to the loss of life or limb and placed easily obtained goals out of reach. A Jedi must learn to pace themselves at all times, be aware of unseen dangers and obstacles, and not tumble headlong into them.
- **Conquer Curiosity:** All people, no matter how public their lives are entitled to their privacy. A Jedi who pokes into somebody's business unnecessarily not only invades an individual's private life but also brings shame upon [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") for acting so rudely and creating distrust. Using [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to do so only complicates matters further. [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") does not exist to serve one's curiosity.
- **Conquer Aggression:** For those less experienced in the ways of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), knowing the difference between attack, defense, and aggression does not come easily. A Jedi can attack and even kill without aggression, especially if they are calm, at peace, and not filled with anger or hatred. However, killing one's opponent should not become a common act, and a Jedi must explore every alternative before employing lethal force.
- **Conquer External Loyalties:** A Jedi is free to have connections with others outside of the Order, but divided loyalties can compromise a Jedi's effectiveness. Other loyalties can distract them from the task at hand and cause undue hardship for others. For this reason, [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order") recruits students at a very young age and actively discourages relationships early in life that can create problems in the future. The Order also prohibits marriage without special permission from the High Council. A Jedi's loyalties should lie with [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order"), the Republic, and themselves, in that order.
- **Conquer Materialism:** Like external loyalties, possessions can also be a distraction. A Jedi doesn't need to lead a ascetic life, but they learn to travel light, carrying with them only the bare essentials. Very few Jedi own more than what they can carry.

## Responsibility

Once the ideals of self-discipline are learned, a Jedi becomes more open to The Light Side of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") and can more successfully listen to its will. In addition, a Jedi can begin taking responsibility for their actions. Any Jedi that does not take responsibility for their actions lacks the discipline expected of them as a member of [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order").

- **Practice Honesty:** Honesty is the first responsibility of [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"), and recognizing its importance is vital to becoming a valued member of the Order. Although certain situations might require a Jedi to stretch the truth or create falsehoods within a particular situation, a Jedi must remain honest with themselves, their Master, and the Council. Continually creating lies or subjecting others to delusions invites suspicion and incites anger from others upon discovery of the truth.
- **Honor Your Promises:** A Jedi that makes a promise should be prepared to follow through with that promise or make amends, if necessary. One who makes promises that they do not intend to keep creates dishonesty and is less likely to be trusted.
- **Honor Your Padawan:** Every Jedi Master that takes a Padawan under their wing has a responsibility toward helping that pupil complete their training. A Padawan should be treated with dignity and respect. A Master should not reprimand their Padawan in front of others, but thy should commend them in public when they do something worthy of praise. Doing this helps build their confidence and helps improve the relationship between the Master and their apprentice.
- **Honor Your Master:** Likewise, a Padawan should honor their Master at all times. Although disagreements might occur, taking it to the point of argument is not wise. When dealing with others apart from their Master, a Padawan should speak only when spoken to. At all other times, the Padawan should wait for their Master to address the issue. By doing so, the Master will not have to worry about the need to apologize for their apprentice's behavior.
- **Honor the Jedi Council:** Within [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order"), the High Council is the final authority in all matters. However, it cannot watch over every single Jedi in the galaxy. Therefore, a Jedi dispatched on a mission represents [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order") and the Council through their words and actions. At the same time, the Council must answer for what the Jedi says and does during their mission, and putting the Council in a difficult situation brings dishonor upon its members.
- **Honor the Jedi Order:** A Jedi's words and actions represent the Order as a whole. Positive words are deeds reflect positively, while negative ones damage the Order's reputation. Billions of people inhabit the galaxy, and only a small fraction of them have ever encountered a Jedi. The words and deeds of a single Jedi often create a first (And often lasting) impression of the Order as a whole.
- **Honor the Law:** [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order") was appointed by [The Galactic Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Galactic_Republic "The Galactic Republic") to be the protectors of peace and justice throughout the galaxy. As the Republic's enforcers of the law, [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") must also be bound by that same law. Any Jedi that is sent out to perform missions for the Republic must strive to avoid breaking the law. When venturing into areas of space outside the Republic's jurisdiction, a Jedi must exercise extreme care, for local governments might operate differently than the Republic, and an incident in which the Jedi breaks local laws might cause delays in resolving the situation through Republic channels.
- **Honor Life:** Since life is what strengthens The Light Side of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), honoring life in all forms is one of the highest priorities of [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order"). Care must be taken to protect life and avoid unnecessary death, for killing someone gives strength to [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). If a situation arises where a life must be taken after all other options have been contemplated, a Jedi should make sure that the reason is justified. Typically, this should be done in self-defense or the defense of others incapable of defending themselves. A Jedi should never assume that the taking of a life is no cause for concern. Once a Jedi does not care about committing murder, then they have begun walking down the path to [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side").

## Public Service

Even as [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order") strives to expand its knowledge of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), it also exists to serve the public's needs. If [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") did not have the ability to use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), they would still serve as protectors of peace and justice, for doing so is one of the main reasons why the Order was appointed by the Republic. The fact that Jedi can and do use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") merely accentuates the principle of serving the general public.

Many of the ideals set forth for public service meld easily with promotion of The Light Side of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), but one must carefully weigh their options when seeking to uphold these ideals to make sure that as few people as possible are adversely affected by doing so.

- **Duty to the Republic:** Despite misconceptions held by some of the populace, [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order") is not a part of [The Galactic Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Galactic_Republic "The Galactic Republic"). Nevertheless, [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") serve the Republic to uphold its laws and protect its citizens. When they are not needed, they are asked to stand aside, which [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") willingly do. This long-standing arrangement between the two organizations was created long ago, for reasons that have been long forgotten.
- **Render Aid:** In conjunction with promoting the ideals of The Light Side of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), all Jedi endeavor to provide aid to those in need whenever possible. However, a Jedi should not forego their other ideals to do so.
- **Defend the Weak:** A Jedi should defend those that are unable to defend themselves. At the same time, a Jedi must be aware that what might seem to be oppression in one culture might not necessarily be so in another. The morals and ethics of other cultures need to be carefully considered before taking action, lest a Jedi insinuate themselves into a situation where their help is not desired.
- **Provide Support:** Although a Jedi toils to help others in need, they must sometimes suspend their wishes and let others impart assistance instead, even if the Jedi could perform the act more quickly or easily. The Jedi should assist only as the situation requires, offer advice upon request, warn when necessary, and argue if proper reason fails.

Whispers of the Sith Spirit: Shades of DarknessWhispers of the Sith Spirit: Shades of Darkness

The temptation to tap into [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") is felt most strongly by those who have never sensed its allure. When a situation seems dire or when a character desperately wants to succeed at a given task, he or she can look to [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") to give them a boost.

If a character's [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") is less than or equal to half of his or her [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") score, he or she can choose to call upon [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") to roll 1d6 and add the result to any roll adjusted by the expenditure of a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point"). Doing so increases the character's [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") by 1. If the character commits an evil act with the bonus they receive from calling upon [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"), their [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") increases by an additional point.

### The Dark Side

Always seductive is The Dark Side of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). It tempts even the most pure souls to take the quick and easy path. It gives the impression that it is more powerful than The Light Side, creeping into the minds of those seeking shortcuts to gaining more power. However, The Dark Side is only easier, not more powerful. Instead of gaining strength from life like The Light Side, The Dark Side thrives on destruction and negative emotions such as fear, anger, and hate. Succumbing to these emotions leads one down The Dark Path to ruin.

The Dark Side promises a rapid rise in power. But as time goes on and one's reliance on The Dark Side increases, it in turn demands more from he or she who uses its power. Its corruption slowly overtakes the individual trapped within its grasp and wears away at his or her life force until they cannot possibly escape its grasp without some form of outside intervention.

The Order of [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") is the one [Force Tradition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Tradition "Force Tradition") most recognized as having embraced The Dark Side of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") hate the fact that [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order") has corrupted the galaxy with its false teachings, and its members struggle to destroy [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") and rule the galaxy forever. Although they do succeed for a time under [Darth Sidious](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Sidious "Darth Sidious") and his apprentice, [Darth Vader](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Vader "Darth Vader"), their rule of hatred and fear lasts only until [Luke Skywalker](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Luke_Skywalker "Luke Skywalker") redeems his father and helps him eradicate the last of [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") to finally bring balance to [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force").

Optional Rule: Resisting the Dark Side’s CallOptional Rule: Resisting the Dark Side’s Call

The lure of [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") is strong. It will tempt you in ways you never thought possible. Only the strongest of wills is able to resist succumbing to [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") after being tainted by its power for so long.

If a character's [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") is equal to more than half of his or her [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") score, he or she can attempt to resist taking a [Dark Side Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Point "Dark Side Point"). If they make a successful [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") check (DC = 5 + their [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score")), they avoid increasing their [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") in a situation where they normally would.

The Dark PathThe Dark Path

Those who walk the path toward The Dark Side of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") go through four different stages: Temptation, Imperilment, Submission, and Atonement or Redemption. Most users of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), even the purest of [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"), can go through at least one of these stages as they walk through life. Those who are ambitious, power-hungry, filled with hatred, overly passionate, or just outright evil will experience two or more of these stages during their descent into darkness. [Anakin Skywalker](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Anakin_Skywalker "Anakin Skywalker") went through all four stages during his life: Temptation as a Jedi Padawan, Imperilment while he served as a Jedi Knight during [The Clone Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Clone_Wars "The Clone Wars"), Submission when he served [Darth Sidious](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Sidious "Darth Sidious") as his apprentice [Darth Vader](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Vader "Darth Vader"), and Redemption when he hurled Sidious to his death and restored freedom and order to the galaxy.

Force-users can face these stages in a variety of different ways. Pupils and apprentices can grope with these stages as they learn about [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), their sense of morality, or their affinity with [The Jedi Code](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Code "The Jedi Code").

## Temptation

To those who can feel it, [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") offers great power. An individual sensitive to [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") views the galaxy in a much different way than those who cannot connect to [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). Through that insight, one can learn to use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") in many ways but also runs the risk of having a brush with The Dark Side.

As a Jedi begins to learn to wield [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") and master its techniques, they open themselves to the Temptations that come with it. Those who show impatience or eagerness to engage in combat are especially susceptible to The Dark Side's lure, as they quickly take an aggressive stance and often give in to fear or anger. If this course of action is what the Jedi prefers, then they should expect The Dark Side to tempt them in some way.

## Imperilment

A Force-user who has been tempted by The Dark Side enough to be on the verge of completely falling under its influence is considered Imperiled, and great strength of will is required to pull them back from the edge of the abyss on which they stand. While some show little concern about taking that last step to becoming a full darksider, others resist The Dark Side's call and try to use its power sparingly. They hear the voice inside their head warning them to not perform an act that will push them over the edge. Sometimes that voice is a whisper; other times it roars like thunder.

## Submission

Once a Jedi has completely submitted to The Dark Side (that is, once their [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") equals their [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") score), all bets are off. They no longer have any need, let alone desire, to monitor their actions, for they have become a full-fledged darksider whose agenda has totally changed from what it was at the beginning of their descent. Those who once worked with them now see what they have become and might choose to take appropriate action, including trying to kill them. More than likely, the darksider will leave the group to pursue their own goals and never see them again except as an adversary.

## Atonement or Redemption

Most of the time, one who is steeped in The Dark Side has no desire to return to the life they once led, wishing instead to continue along The Dark Path. However, exceptions exist. Those who are tainted might atone for their actions and are able to go through the effort to Redeem themselves. Those who have fully Submitted to The Dark Side cannot Atone and can only be Redeemed through an act of [Dramatic Heroism](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dramatic_Heroism "Dramatic Heroism").

#### Variations on the Dark Side

[The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") is usually presented as the driving force behind [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith"), the darkest evil in the galaxy. However, other [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions") have been known to walk the Dark Path or wield [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") from time to time and still avoid falling under its influence. Groups such as [The Jensaarai](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jensaarai "The Jensaarai") have members that occasionally tap into [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") and walk away seemingly untouched. According to [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"), these individuals become tainted and carry The Dark Side's influence with them, whether they realize it or not. Some people claim to have witnessed a slow metamorphosis of these persons' personalities over time as a result of their flirting with [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side").

Force-using Jedi who meddle with [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") without totally surrendering to it are sometimes referred to as Gray Jedi. A Gray Jedi taps into The Light Side and The Dark Side equally. Members of [The Jensaarai](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jensaarai "The Jensaarai") outwardly deny such claims that they are Gray, but [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") and [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") do not hesitate to say that [The Jensaarai](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jensaarai "The Jensaarai") refuse to admit the truth to themselves. There are those in the galaxy who are not affiliated with any [Force Tradition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Tradition "Force Tradition") that delve into both The Light Side and [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") that are true Gray Jedi. These people cause concern for [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"), since these Gray Jedi often portray themselves as holding full knowledge of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), sometimes claiming to know more than [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"), and often actively recruit other Force-sensitive people to join their cause.

Optional Rule: A Helping HandOptional Rule: A Helping Hand

Even though a darksider might not seek redemption on their own, some people connected to him or her might feel differently. These people might include friends, family, or some loved one who seeks to pull him or her back before they meet a terrible fate.

Any character with a strong connection to the darksider can urge him or her to turn away from [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"). This requires a [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check which can be made once per level for the darksider. If the [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") equals or exceeds the darksider's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") plus their [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score"), and they are actively listening to the plea, the darksider's [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") is reduced by 1. Others can attempt to assist the character making the [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") with the [Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another") Action.

## Padawans and Apprentices

Upon reaching knighthood, some Jedi seek to take on Apprentices, passing on their knowledge to future generations. Some Jedi have Apprentices thrust upon them, as [Ahsoka Tano](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ahsoka_Tano "Ahsoka Tano") was thrust upon [Anakin Skywalker](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Anakin_Skywalker "Anakin Skywalker"), and some Jedi choose to train Apprentices out of a sense of obligation, as [Obi-Wan Kenobi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Obi-Wan_Kenobi "Obi-Wan Kenobi") trained [Anakin Skywalker](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Anakin_Skywalker "Anakin Skywalker") and, later, [Luke Skywalker](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Luke_Skywalker "Luke Skywalker").

[Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") heroes in the *Star Wars Roleplaying Game* can also take on Apprentices, training them in the ways of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). There are two methods that can be used to adjudicate such an Apprenticeship. The first is to simply have the Gamemaster introduce the Padawan as an NPC, attached to a particular hero who is serving as the Padawan's Master. In this case, the Gamemaster simply crafts the statistics for the low-level Padawan and has the Padawan join the party, going on adventures as normal. The Padawan remains under the control of the Gamemaster, and no new game mechanics are needed.

The other method is to treat the training of an Apprentice as an important step for a Jedi Knight, both in terms of character development and also mechanically. This situation uses the new rules presented below to make an Apprentice more than just an NPC that tags along on adventures, and helps to circumvent the difficulties presented by adding NPCs to the party of higher-level character. Under these rules, the Padawan joins the Jedi on his or her adventures, all while helping his or her Master learn more about the nature of a Jedi Knight's responsibilities.

For the purposes of these rules, the phrases "Padawan" and "Jedi Knight" are interchangeable with the equivalent ranks in other [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions"), such as Sith Apprentices and Sith Lords, Jensaarai Apprentices and Jensaarai Defenders, and so forth.

### Obtaining a Padawan

You must be at least 8th level to obtain a Padawan. Provided an Apprentice is available (the Gamemaster is free to determine whether or not training a Padawan is appropriate for the campaign), the player need only declare that he or she wishes to take on a Padawan. At this point, the Gamemaster should craft an appropriate Padawan for the hero to train, using the following guidelines.

A Padawan obtained in this manner is a [Nonheroic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nonheroic "Nonheroic") character. The Padawan begins at 1st level, with the following starting [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"): [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity"), [Skill Training (Use the Force)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Training_(Use_the_Force) "Skill Training (Use the Force)"), [Weapon Proficiency (Lightsabers)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Lightsabers) "Weapon Proficiency (Lightsabers)"), and [Weapon Proficiency (Simple Weapons)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Simple_Weapons) "Weapon Proficiency (Simple Weapons)").

Padawans are likewise limited in their [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat") selection. Padawans cannot take the [Skill Focus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Focus "Skill Focus") Feat (representing the fact that the Apprentice has yet to master any particular [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill")), except for those that are granted as Conditional Bonus [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") by the Padawan's [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species").

The Gamemaster chooses the Padawan's [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species"), other [Trained Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skills "Trained Skills"), and [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"). Padawans are also given a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") and cannot build their own until 7th level. [Human](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Human "Human") Padawans should probably take [Force Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Training "Force Training") as their Species Bonus [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat"), and all Padawans should take [Force Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Training "Force Training") as their 3rd and 6th level [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") as well. The Gamemaster chooses [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") for the Padawan that are also known by the Padawan's Master.

### Padawans in Adventures

Since a Padawan is a [Nonheroic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nonheroic "Nonheroic") character that is likely significantly lower in level than the rest of the party, certain special benefits are conferred upon the Padawan while they undergo training. Whenever the Padawan is within 12 squares of their Master, they add their Master's Heroic Level to all of the Padawan's [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses"). Additionally, when the Padawan takes damage that would reduce them to 0 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") and that exceeds their [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold") (that is, damage that would kill them), they do not die but instead fall [Unconscious](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unconscious "Unconscious"). While [Unconscious](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unconscious "Unconscious"), the Padawan can still be killed by all of the normal means, including the [Coup de Grace](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coup_de_Grace "Coup de Grace") Action. Lastly, if the Padawan is adjacent to their Master, they can redirect any attack made against them to their Master as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), providing the Master is willing.

A Padawan is not directly under the control of the hero that trains them, though Gamemasters should have the Padawan obey their Master's commands. In combat, a Padawan can be used to set up [Flanks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flanks?action=edit&redlink=1 "Flanks (page does not exist)") or to [Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another") on attack rolls, or use [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") when appropriate to help out in battle. Outside of combat, the Padawan typically uses the [Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another") Action to help their Master on [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks"), or occasionally provide [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks") for [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills") in which no one else in the party is [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained").

Padawans do not take their own cut of the XP earned by a party; instead, a Padawan simply gains as much XP as their Master whenever XP is awarded. In this manner, Padawans gain levels much faster than their Masters, though this does allow a Jedi to train multiple Apprentices over the course of a single campaign.

### Benefits of Training a Padawan

Each time a Padawan gains a level, their Master gains the 24-hour bonus from the [Education](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Education "Education") Destiny, regardless of whether the Master has that [Destiny](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny "Destiny") or not. In addition to the traditional help that a Padawan can provide over the course of an adventure (such as aiding in combat, assisting on [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks"), and so forth), a Padawan can act as a method for delivering Gamemaster advice and suggestions to the Master that is training the Padawan.

### Achieving Knighthood

When a Padawan achieves 7th level, they continue to gain XP but cannot reach 8th level until certain circumstances are fulfilled. The Padawan must undergo their Jedi Trials- which typically involve undertaking some dangerous mission during which all of the lessons they have learned as a Jedi come into play- and then be granted the rank of Jedi Knight by their Master and the Jedi Council. Once this happens, the Apprentice is no longer considered to be a Padawan and ceases to be under the tutelage of the the hero. All of their [Nonheroic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nonheroic "Nonheroic") levels are traded for Heroic Levels, and the Gamemaster should rebuild the Padawan as a full-fledged Jedi Knight (with one level in the [Jedi Knight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Knight "Jedi Knight") Prestige Class). Upon achieving knighthood, the character is no longer a member of the party, but the Gamemaster should feel free to include that character as a recurring NPC.

Once a Padawan reaches knighthood, the hero who trained that Padawan becomes eligible to take the [Mentor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mentor "Mentor") Force Secret.

## Applications of the Force

Having knowledge of The Force provides a good foundation to being an outstanding Jedi. But, having that knowledge is only half of what is required to fulfilling your purpose. Being able to take that knowledge and apply it in everyday life completes the expectations of a Jedi.

Everything a Jedi can do with The Force can be broken down into three broad categories: Alter, Control, and Sense. Some abilities work in such a way that they span two or even all three categories. These abilities are not widely known, especially the ones that fall under all three groups, and the secrets to learning these powers have been locked away, to be revealed only to those who truly need them.

### Alter

Alter abilities are undeniably the most easily observable but least understood applications of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") known by [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order"). That is not to say that [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") know very little about this area, but a more appropriate phrase would be that the true nature of this use is still highly speculated. Using [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to affect anything outside the body or the senses is considered an Alter ability, since it is used to "alter" someone or something. Control abilities cannot be considered in the Alter category because they affect only the Force-user's body and nothing beyond that. Sense abilities do not make any effort to manipulate anything within the Force-user's environment, only detect it.

Alter abilities (be they [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers"), [Alter Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Alter_Talent_Tree "Alter Talent Tree"), or otherwise) have a variety of applications, from moving objects without physically touching them to creating a sound on the other side of a room to forming an illusion of something that is not really there. Several [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions"), including [The Sith Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Order "The Sith Order") and [The Zeison Sha](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Zeison_Sha "The Zeison Sha"), rely heavily on Alter applications of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to accomplish much of what they do.

The theories behind how Alter abilities work as as numerous as the theories of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). Science cannot explain them.

The most widely accepted theory is that [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") reacts to a Force-user's strength of will, allowing the individual to apply [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") with their mind to some other space or object.

Moving objects through [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") involves stretching out one's feelings through [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to mentally seize the object in question and exerting one's will on it so that it performs as they wish. Opponents of The Living Force question how this is possible on nonliving things, such as weapons and [Starships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships "Starships"). But, the official answer from [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") states that [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") is created by all living things and permeates the galaxy, even around nonliving things. An individual lifts a [Starship](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship "Starship") off the ground by mastering the space around it and not thinking of it as a lifeless object but as part of the greater universe.

At the same time, using powers where [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") is used as a telekinetic attack, such as when a Jedi pushes an opponent away through [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), calls upon the same basic technique of stretching out their feelings. But instead of seizing the opponent, they shove against the enemy and propel them backwards.

Another common application is known as the *[Mind Trick](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mind_Trick "Mind Trick")*, where a Jedi changes a person's perceptions or plants a suggestion into another's mind. Pushing the suggestion or desired effect through [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), the Force-user superimposes their will onto the target's mind. Accomplishing this task might be more difficult with some [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species"), such as the [Hutts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hutts "Hutts") or the [Toydarians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Toydarians "Toydarians"). Many Jedi scholars believe that this is due to the races' physiologies being drastically different than most other races in the galaxy. Others believe that this could be because members of those [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") have a lower midichlorian count then what other [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") typically have.

Healing other living creatures is one of the least common but most valued applications of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). Documented cases reveal that [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") can be used to close wounds, cure diseases, detoxify poisons, and heal other ailments. Despite the fact that Species' anatomies differ widely, [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") helps tend to the sick and wounded, leading many Jedi to support the notion that [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") values life above all else. This also explains why healing works on different [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") when the *[Mind Trick](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mind_Trick "Mind Trick")* does not, since the *[Mind Trick](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mind_Trick "Mind Trick")* does not perform any actions to help support life.

A rare but known technique is the ability to create illusions through [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). Though many people speculate on different methods to accomplish this task, including implanting the illusion into the minds of those the Force-user wants to see it, a number of Force-users that have mastered this practice state that they envision the image they want to create in their mind and push it out through [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to manifest at the desired location. Both theories are sound with the caveat that neither is grounded in science.

### Control

The abilities that fall under the Control category are those that involve tapping into abilities hidden within one's body and spirit to expand and enhance what can be already done or open new doors to talents that the Force-user did not originally have. [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") and [Force Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Talents "Force Talents") that improve a Force-user's speed, strength, or combat prowess fall under this category as well as healing one's own wounds.

Allowing [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to manipulate the body and its systems is probably one of the most easily understood applications of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") and one of the easiest to master. In fact, most other [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions") beside [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"), such as [The Matukai](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Matukai "The Matukai"), have mastered Control abilities to a better extent than [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") and are able to do far more with their bodies than some of the greatest Jedi Masters.

Using [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") in this capability requires more than just channeling it through the body. Knowing how the body works and its limitations is key to mastering Control abilities. Countless incidents have been documented where anxious Jedi younglings have tried to do too much at one time and injured themselves with broken limbs and strained muscles in the process. Like with any other lessons, starting small is the best approach. Also, knowing where to apply [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") is important to achieve the maximum result. A Jedi wishing to leap to a ledge several stories higher should know to focus [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") on certain muscles to reach the desired height.

### Sense

While some would argue that Sense abilities actually apply to improving the body and are more appropriately placed with Control, the truth is that Sense powers expand one's awareness and ability to perceive one's surroundings in more ways than just by sight and sound. Since Sense abilities deal entirely with perception and awareness, they deserve to be classified in their own category.

Force-using organizations such as [The Baran Do Sages](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Baran_Do_Sages "The Baran Do Sages") are known to master Sense abilities to levels beyond some of the more prominent [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions"), including [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order").

Depending on your outlook on [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), how Sense abilities work can be approached from two different angels: internally or externally. The internal theory states that the abilities exhibited by a Force-user are a result of applying [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to your own sensory organs to enhance the way they work. The external theory asserts that those same abilities result from the Force-sensitive individual picking up signals from other life forms through [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force").

Looking at the internal theory, Sense abilities increase the efficacy of one's sensory organs, passing new information on to the brain for interpretation. Sometimes, it involves opening the eyes to locate an object outside a person's normal sight range or to examine details too small for the naked eye to perceive. Other times, it allows the ear to detect sounds whose frequencies are too high or too low for normal hearing or hear sounds so soft that even electronic equipment can barely detect them. And at other times, [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") opens the mind to perceive signals in other spectrums not normally allowed by the mind, such as picking up other people's thoughts, transmitting one's own thoughts through [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to another person halfway across the galaxy, or perceiving disturbances in [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") caused by the sudden extermination of life on a massive scale. In these situations, applying [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to the specific sensory organ is all that is needed. Just a small application is enough to achieve the desired effect. While no documented cases exist of Force-induced blindness or deafness, pushing too much of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") on one single area is highly discouraged.

Taking a closer look at the external theory, Sense abilities do not truly enhance the individual's senses. However, the person stretches outward through [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to look for indications of other life forms around them and what they are doing. No power is applied to the Jedi's eyes, ears, or any of their other senses. Instead, the signals broadcasted by other living beings are delivered through [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to their brain to be interpreted as the mind would normally process sights and sounds received through normal means. As with the internal theory, not much of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") is required to use these abilities, but one should not overdo it.

### Influence of the Dark Side

Those allied with [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") find ways to use every aspect of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to their advantage. Darksiders twist [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to apply it in ways that are unnatural and self-serving. Even though [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") struggle to end these practices, some manage to avoid being caught and pass their knowledge along to students willing to learn their dark secrets and expand on what they have been taught.

The methods used by darksiders fall under the same three categories established by other Force-users in the galaxy: Alter, Control, and Sense. While [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") have not examined how these dark abilities manifest, the following theories and speculations about how they work are the most popular explanations within [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order").

Many of the abilities that are grouped under Control and Sense work much the same as those used by [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") and other organizations; the ability to improve what the body already does and perceives cannot vary too much. However, certain powers categorized under the Alter classification seem to be more easily recognized as originating from [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side").

By focusing their anger and hatred, many darksiders are able to fly into a berserker rage, increasing their battle prowess as they give themselves over to [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"). Using [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") in this way can sometimes push the body's capabilities beyond what it can normally handle, often leading to physical deformities or alterations (not unlike those witnessed in Darth Sidious). Observations indicate that these extreme boosts last for only a short time.

# The Krath

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Krath](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Krath) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:04:14Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

The Krath are a Sith-inspired organization created by the cousins [Satal Keto](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Satal_Keto "Satal Keto") and [Aleema Keto](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aleema_Keto "Aleema Keto"), heirs to the Empress Teta System (formally the Koros System of the Unification Wars). The two cousins and a number of other rich and spoiled aristocrats taught themselves a bit of Sith magic from artifacts recovered by Jedi Master [Odan-Urr](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Odan-Urr "Odan-Urr") during [The Great Hyperspace War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Hyperspace_War?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Great Hyperspace War (page does not exist)") a thousand years earlier. The group calls themselves The Krath, a name based on the fearsome creature of legend. After recovering a book of Sith secrets from a museum on [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant"), the cousins proceeded to the planet [Onderon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Onderon "Onderon"), which was in the midst of a revolt by supporters of the ancient Sith Lord [Freedon Nadd](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Freedon_Nadd "Freedon Nadd").

The spirit of [Freedon Nadd](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Freedon_Nadd "Freedon Nadd") had been working with Onderon's [King Ommin](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/King_Ommin?action=edit&redlink=1 "King Ommin (page does not exist)") to sustain [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith"). However, Nadd realizes that the King is on the brink of defeat by Jedi and Republic forces, so he transfers his support to Satal and Aleema, promising to guide them into a new Sith Golden Age. The cousins escape [Onderon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Onderon "Onderon") and return to the Empress Teta System, where they stage a coup to take control of the seven worlds. However, the populace revolts, resulting in a system-wide struggle in which The Krath unleash their new Sith powers, including impressive Sith sorcery and large-scale illusions. The Krath also skirmish with Jedi forces, using their [Krath War Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Krath_War_Droids "Krath War Droids") in a preemptive strike that results in the death of Master [Arca Jeth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Arca_Jeth "Arca Jeth").

The death of Master Arca has a profound impact on Jedi [Ulic Qel-Droma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ulic_Qel-Droma "Ulic Qel-Droma"). He pledges to bring down The Krath from the inside, by posing as a fallen Jedi. Ultimately, Ulic is seduced into joining [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") by Aleema, killing Satal in the process and taking his place as one of the rulers of The Krath. In his new position, he finds himself battling the Jedi forces who sought to save him from The Krath deceptions. During a climactic battle, [Exar Kun](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exar_Kun "Exar Kun") appears and engages Ulic in [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") combat. At that moment, the spirit of the Dark Lord of the Sith [Marka Ragnos](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Marka_Ragnos "Marka Ragnos") appears, drawn by the Sith amulets both combatants wear. The [Sith Spirit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Spirit "Sith Spirit") orders the two to join forces so that they can bring about the predicted return of [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith").

The two become allies. [Exar Kun](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exar_Kun "Exar Kun") follows his own path to create Sith converts from [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"). Ulic leads the military of The Krath, joining them with [The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians") after defeating [Mandalore the Indomitable](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mandalore_the_Indomitable "Mandalore the Indomitable") in single combat. The battles rage across the galaxy, including [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant"). Aleema falls as she triggers the destruction of [Ossus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ossus "Ossus"), betrayed by Ulic for her lack of loyalty. Eventually Ulic realizes his mistake in following [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"), but only after he kills his brother [Clay Qel-Droma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clay_Qel-Droma?action=edit&redlink=1 "Clay Qel-Droma (page does not exist)"), and [Nomi Sunrider](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nomi_Sunrider "Nomi Sunrider") blinds him to [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). Ulic leads [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") to [Yavin 4](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yavin_4 "Yavin 4"), in an attempt to stop [Exar Kun](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exar_Kun "Exar Kun"). They are successful, though unaware that Kun's spirit survives, trapped within one of his temples. The remaining Krath forces are destroyed or scattered. Some Sith forces retreat to [The Outer Rim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Outer_Rim?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Outer Rim (page does not exist)"), near [Korriban](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Korriban "Korriban").

The Krath are an unusual combination of reclaimed Sith learning and direct influence from [Sith Spirits](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Spirits "Sith Spirits"). None of the early Krath receive formal Sith training. Instead, they piece together techniques and Sith magic from artifacts and occasional input from powerful Sith spirits. Rather than avoiding [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"), The Krath have no such inhibitions and follow their investigations in any direction they deem worthy. They learn how to use ancient [Sith Artifacts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Artifacts "Sith Artifacts"), project massive and destructive Sith illusions, and wield Sith sorcery.

Those Krath that survive the end of [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War") are scattered in [The Outer Rim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Outer_Rim?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Outer Rim (page does not exist)"), mainly in regions around [Korriban](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Korriban "Korriban"). Some Krath are absorbed into older Sith settlements, but others continue on their own, with little outside influence. These Krath continue to delve the depths of [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"), but without the benefit of ancient Sith teachings or access to the technology left behind in the Empress Teta System. During the following decades, Jedi and Republic forces occasionally clash with these isolated pockets.

Members of The Krath can select [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") from the [Krath Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Krath_Talent_Tree "Krath Talent Tree").

# The Tyia

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Tyia](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Tyia) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:05:01Z.

DescriptionHistoryLocationPhilosophyBuilding a Tyia Adept

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

The practitioners of Tyia, a relatively peaceful sect of Force-users have only been widely known in the galaxy since the rise of Palpatine's Galactic Empire. One of the few [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions") to practice openly during the reign of the Empire, Tyia adepts spread their beliefs in the space lanes through peaceful discourse and meditation. Unlike many [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions"), practitioners of Tyia rarely come into conflict with the Empire, prolonging their freedom from the Emperor's Jedi hunters.

**Membership:** Although practitioners of Tyia are usually [Revwien](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Revwien?action=edit&redlink=1 "Revwien (page does not exist)"), Force-sensitive members of other [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") can embrace the philosophies of The Tyia and become practitioners of this [Force Tradition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Tradition "Force Tradition") as well. Members of The Tyia can select [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") from the [Tyia Adept Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tyia_Adept_Talent_Tree "Tyia Adept Talent Tree").

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

The [Revwien](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Revwien?action=edit&redlink=1 "Revwien (page does not exist)") are a sentient plant [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") hailing from a distant planet beyond [The Outer Rim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Outer_Rim?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Outer Rim (page does not exist)"). [Revyia](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Revyia?action=edit&redlink=1 "Revyia (page does not exist)") is a temperate world with long rolling plains and an equatorial jungle belt. The [Revwien](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Revwien?action=edit&redlink=1 "Revwien (page does not exist)") revere the jungle belt as a holy site. Those [Revwien](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Revwien?action=edit&redlink=1 "Revwien (page does not exist)") who display talent with [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") are sent to the jungle to develop their abilities and study The Tyia. For much of the galaxy's history, Revwien Force-users studied The Tyia and developed a kind of religion around it, spreading the word of their beliefs to any who would believe it.

The masters on [Revyia](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Revyia?action=edit&redlink=1 "Revyia (page does not exist)") sensed the deaths of [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") from the nefarious Order 66. The discord caused by the deaths of so many Force-sensitives beings sent the masters into a panic. They gathered their best and brightest in a temple grown from the largest trees on the planet. The mission was laid out plainly. The students were to leave their homeworld and seek out [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") to help them in the wake of the Great Jedi Purge, to help return the galaxy to a state of harmony.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

The practitioners of Tyia can be found anywhere, from [The Deep Core](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Deep_Core?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Deep Core (page does not exist)") to [The Outer Rim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Outer_Rim?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Outer Rim (page does not exist)"). They branched out from the [Revwien](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Revwien?action=edit&redlink=1 "Revwien (page does not exist)") homeworld in force during the days of [The Galactic Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Galactic_Empire "The Galactic Empire"), traveling the space lanes as passengers aboard tramp freighters, sharing their thoughts and wisdom with other beings that seemed interested in, or strongly connected to, [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). Practitioners of Tyia can be found anywhere in the galaxy that tramp freighters fly, but during the time of the Empire, they are more likely to be found in [Shadowports](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shadowports "Shadowports").

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

"Tyia" is the [Revwien](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Revwien?action=edit&redlink=1 "Revwien (page does not exist)") word for "breath." Tyia is the spark of life that all living creatures have. All creatures that breathe have some measure of power in The Tyia. As long as there is life in the galaxy, there will be Tyia. Students of The Tyia spend their time in the wilderness to experience how all life is connected. The prey eats the plant. The predator eats the prey. The predator dies and their remains feed the plant. All things reflect this cycle. Students of The Tyia learn to reach out through these connections and alter the world around them.

Things moving normally are referred to as "Harmony." Harmony is akin to [The Light Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Light_Side "The Light Side"), and most students of The Tyia strive for Harmony. They mediate disputes, heal those not ready to die, and do what they can to keep peace in the galaxy. When [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") intervenes, The Tyia fall into Discord. The Fall of the Republic was hastened by the Discord of Palpatine. [Anakin Skywalker](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Anakin_Skywalker "Anakin Skywalker") succumbed to his Discord because he could not be both a Jedi and a husband to [Padmé Amidala](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Padm%C3%A9_Amidala "Padmé Amidala").

Conflict is part of life. Though students of The Tyia do not carry [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers"), they know that predators hunt prey and war is a natural step in the evolution of the galaxy. While those who study The Tyia revere all life, they also battle those who would destroy it. They prefer to use Force abilities such as *[Force Thrust](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Thrust "Force Thrust")* and *[Force Slam](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Slam "Force Slam")* to knock down and immobilize opponents rather than pick up a blaster to do so. If there is no alternative, The Tyia would still rather [Stun](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stun "Stun") an enemy than kill it. Killing, even if defense, is a quick path to Discord.

Those students who fall from the path of The Tyia are given no name. They are believed to have lost the right to call themselves anything because of their Discord. [Revwien](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Revwien?action=edit&redlink=1 "Revwien (page does not exist)") who fall to [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") take on a shriveled cast. Their voices become whispers of fallen leaves. They long to break out of the endless cycle of The Tyia and often want to take as many others with them as possible. The fallen student of Tyia looks to destroy and disrupt. There are places on [Revyia](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Revyia?action=edit&redlink=1 "Revyia (page does not exist)") where the earth is black and nothing grows. These are the places where Harmony and Discord have battled. Discord never fully wins in the end, but it often extracts a great price when it loses.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

Since those who follow the way of Tyia tend toward pacifism, your Tyia Adept is more likely to come from a [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") where pacifism is at least occasionally acceptable; for example, you will find very few [Gamorrean](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gamorrean "Gamorrean") or [Trandoshan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trandoshan "Trandoshan") Tyia Adepts in the galaxy. That is not to say that they don't exist, but if you choose to create a Tyia Adept from a violent [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species"), be prepared to answer why your hero would reject cultural norms and become an advocate for peace and tranquility. That being the case, Tyia Adepts might be pacifists, but they are not always passive. A Tyia Adept might be reluctant to fire a blaster or lash out with the *[Force Slam](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Slam "Force Slam")* Force Power, but the adept will not stand by while their allies are harmed. Tyia Adepts tend to take steps to blunt anger (and attacks) and diffuse conflict, and as such you might look toward abilities that let you accomplish these objectives readily. Species that grant bonuses when healing (such as the [Selkath](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Selkath "Selkath")), those skilled at neutralizing conflict (such as the [Caamasi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Caamasi "Caamasi")), or those with [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") score bonuses are likely to make good Tyia Adepts.

A Tyia Adept's Force abilities focus on healing, the reduction of damage to allies, and breaking up violent conflict. Tyia Adepts prefer [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") such as *[Vital Transfer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vital_Transfer "Vital Transfer")* for healing the wounds created by Discord, as well as [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") such as *[Sever Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sever_Force "Sever Force")* that do not cause direct harm but, instead, inflict penalties when the target attempts to attack. Tyia Adepts are more likely to pursue talents that augment healing (such as **[Force Treatment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Treatment "Force Treatment")** Talent) or that grant boons to their allies, such as the **[Force Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Perception "Force Perception")** and **[Foresight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Foresight "Foresight")** Talents from the [Sense Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sense_Talent_Tree "Sense Talent Tree"). While you are less likely to choose damage-dealing [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") when building your Tyia Adept, you can certainly choose ones that intercede on you allies' behalf, such as the *[Inspire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Inspire "Inspire")* Force Power.

Since a Tyia Adept is trained to avoid violent entanglements and overcome enemies without resorting to brutality, the [Noble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble "Noble") and [Scoundrel](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrel "Scoundrel") Heroic Classes make good choices. For your Tyia Adept, you might consider picking a few [Noble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble "Noble") Talents that let you boost your allies (as opposed to harming your enemies), such as **[Bolster Ally](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bolster_Ally "Bolster Ally")** or **[Inspire Confidence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Inspire_Confidence "Inspire Confidence")**. Moreover, if you choose not to invest many of your [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") in [Force Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Talents "Force Talents"), you might go as far into the [Noble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble "Noble") Talents as to pick up **[Rally](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rally "Rally")**, which can help many of your allies get back on their feet. The [Scoundrel](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrel "Scoundrel") is also a good choice for your Tyia Adept, as [Scoundrels](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrels "Scoundrels") have many indirect ways of wreaking havoc with the enemy's actions, such as the **[Disruptive](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disruptive "Disruptive")** and **[Walk the Line](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Walk_the_Line "Walk the Line")** Talents. Such [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") allow you to participate in combat without necessarily having to make attack rolls.

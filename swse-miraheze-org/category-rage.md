# Category : Rage

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Rage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Rage) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:05:59Z.

Once per day, a [Species] can fly into a Rage as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"). While Raging, the [Species] temporarily gains a +2 Rage bonus on melee attack rolls and melee damage rolls, but cannot use [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills") that require patience and concentration, such as [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics"), [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth"), or [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force"). A fit of Rage lasts for a number of rounds equal to 5 + the [Species]'s [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") modifier.

- At the end of its Rage, the [Species] moves -1 [Persistent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persistent "Persistent") step along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"). The penalties imposed by this [Persistent Condition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persistent_Condition "Persistent Condition") persist until the [Species] takes at least 10 minutes to recuperate, during which time the [Species] can't engage in any strenuous activity.

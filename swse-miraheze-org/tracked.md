# Tracked

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tracked](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tracked) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:08:24Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

**Base Speed:** Small (4 squares); Medium (6 squares); Large or bigger (8 squares)

**Cost:** 20 x [Cost Factor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cost_Factor "Cost Factor") x (Base Speed Squared)

Tracked Droids are an improvement on the [Wheeled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wheeled "Wheeled") Droid, having rigid treads that give them more traction. Tracked Droids ignore the penalties of [Difficult Terrain](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Difficult_Terrain "Difficult Terrain"), but they take a -5 penalty on all [Climb](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Climb "Climb") checks.

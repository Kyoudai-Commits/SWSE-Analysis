# The Jedi

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:04:12Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

*There is no emotion; there is peace.*

*There is no ignorance; there is knowledge.*

*There is no passion; there is serenity.*

*There is no death; there is The Force.*

*-From [The Jedi Code](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Code "The Jedi Code")*

For centuries, the group of philosophers that would eventually become The Jedi contemplated the mysteries of the energy field known as [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). Eventually, some of the group's members mastered [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). After that, they dedicated themselves to using their newfound skills and powers for good, helping those in need. In the thousands of years that followed, the Jedi served as protectors of [The Galactic Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Galactic_Republic "The Galactic Republic"). Answering to their own Jedi Council and operating in concordance with the Judicial Department of the office of the Supreme Chancellor, the Jedi became the guardians of peace and justice in the galaxy.

The Jedi, in addition to battling interstellar criminals and settling galactic disputes, served as mediators, defenders, and teachers. Because of their sense of honor and the epic challenges they undertook, the Jedi became legends, serving as symbols of the best the Republic has to offer. With [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers") at their side and the power of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") flowing through them, the Jedi accomplished the tasks set before them with dedication and seeming invisibility. But that invincibility was only an illusion. Jedi often died in the defense of freedom and justice.

During [The Rise of the Empire Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Rise_of_the_Empire_Era "The Rise of the Empire Era") (Episodes I-III), ten thousand Jedi served the Republic and defended its vast territory. They identified potential recruits early in life, usually within the first six months of a child's existence. As infants, those attuned to [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") and accepted by the Jedi Council were taken to begin their training. Older children, if identified later in life as being Force-sensitive, were refused training in most cases; the Jedi believed that the fear and anger in older children made them too susceptible to [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"), and so it was unwise to train them.

All characters playing in this era that are Jedi Padawan Learners ([Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") Class Levels 1st through 6th) have a master ([Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") Class Levels 7th or higher). While there may be times when the Master and the Padawan travel together, most of their interactions happen between adventurers, while all characters are training and improving. The master is a GM character, offering training, advice, and aid when the GM feels such aid is appropriate and necessary. Unfortunately, by the end of this time period, [Anakin Skywalker](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Anakin_Skywalker "Anakin Skywalker") has turned to [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") and the Empire has slaughtered nearly all Jedi.

During [The Rebellion Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Rebellion_Era "The Rebellion Era"), the Jedi Purge has been completed. All but a handful of Force-users, let alone fully trained Jedi, were exterminated or corrupted to [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") and placed in the service of the Emperor. Force-sensitive individuals, such as Luke Skywalker and Leia Organa, were hidden from the Empire and cut off from their heritage. More powerful Force-users, such as Obi-Wan Kenobi and Yoda, purposely disappeared and worked to stay unnoticed.

[Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") characters in this era eventually attract a teacher, though the training these Force-users provide is haphazard at best. A would-be Jedi learns by experience, though occasional meetings with other Force-users, and even by studying with beings who follow other [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions"). It's not easy, which is why [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") in this period are few and far between, and few reach the level of power exhibited in earlier or later periods. A Gamemaster wishing to simulate this experience might require the potential character to have at least one non-Force-using [Heroic Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Class "Heroic Class") level before allowing them to take levels in the [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") class.

In [The New Jedi Order Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_New_Jedi_Order_Era "The New Jedi Order Era"), [Luke Skywalker](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Luke_Skywalker "Luke Skywalker") has trained about one hundred Jedi and identified perhaps a dozen others in need of training. Prominent Force-users such as [Mara Jade Skywalker](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mara_Jade_Skywalker "Mara Jade Skywalker") and [Corran Horn](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Corran_Horn "Corran Horn") help Skywalker train and coordinate the efforts of these Jedi, while promising students such as the Solo children and [Ganner Rhysode](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ganner_Rhysode?action=edit&redlink=1 "Ganner Rhysode (page does not exist)") show great promise for the future. [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") characters again receive a higher-level mentor who provides training and advice between adventures or when the Gamemaster deems such interaction to be warranted and important to a mission.

**Membership:** Any character with the [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity") feat can become a member of The Jedi Order by being accepted as an apprentice by a [Jedi Knight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Knight "Jedi Knight") or [Jedi Master](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Master "Jedi Master").

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

Eons ago, The Jedi Order was whole and in pursuit of a deeper understanding of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). However, some were not content with the Jedi path and turned to [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). They abandoned their pasts and were driven to the farthest reaches of the galaxy by the remaining Jedi. For thousands of years, The Jedi thought their long-lost traitorous comrades gone and forgotten. But when hyperspace explorers discover an empire ruled with an iron fist by the descendants of those fallen Jedi, the Order realizes that the exiles had found and enslaved a people called [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith"), taking the name as their own. When the whole of this Sith Empire turns its terrifying might against [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") in [The Great Hyperspace War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Hyperspace_War?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Great Hyperspace War (page does not exist)"), The Jedi fully grasp the consequences of that nearly forgotten decision to banish their fallen comrades.

Some Jedi denounce the Order's zeal to extinguish every trace of [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") in the war's aftermath, and many think [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") extinct. Then the mortal enemy of The Jedi returns in force. First the Jedi Knights [Exar Kun](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exar_Kun "Exar Kun") and [Ulic Qel-Droma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ulic_Qel-Droma "Ulic Qel-Droma") turn to [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") and ally themselves with [The Krath](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Krath "The Krath"), a Dark Side cult borne of ancient Sith tradition. As the newly revived Sith Empire thrusts the galaxy into the conflict that would be called [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War"), [Ulic Qel-Droma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ulic_Qel-Droma "Ulic Qel-Droma") cements his power by defeating the Mandalore and binding [The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians") to his cause. The Jedi face not only fanatical soldiers and skilled warriors but also their own comrades; Jedi flee the Order to join the Sith cause, and [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") begin to regain the numbers they once had during [The Great Hyperspace War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Hyperspace_War?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Great Hyperspace War (page does not exist)").

During this time, The Jedi Order is at the summit of its power and self-confidence. The Jedi High Council is a largely ceremonial body with no real authority over Jedi. Jedi Masters and Watchmen are entrusted to maintain peace and train the new generations of Jedi, allowing remarkable diversity and freedom of ideas. Though a dichotomy of "Light" and "Dark" remains firmly entrenched, schools of thought abound about how to best serve [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). Jedi Padawans are regularly accepted in their teens and twenties, and even though Jedi Knights rarely take more than two apprentices, a single Jedi Master might have dozens. Unfortunately, The Jedi suffer heavy losses during this war, and by the time The Jedi defeat Qel-Droma and the rest of [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") their numbers are drastically diminished.

## [The Restoration Period](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Restoration_Period "The Restoration Period")

**3,995-3,966 BBY**

Humbled by [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War"), The Jedi undertake major reform. The Jedi Council seeks to hold Jedi Masters and Watchmen to a higher standard of accountability and asserts more uniform practices. But as [The Jedi Code](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Code "The Jedi Code") indicates, Jedi do not rule over others, and the Council faces a long, uphill battle toward claiming unquestioned authority.

Tension is thick as differing points of view polarize the Order, and the Council seeks a reasonable median. [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") views the Jedi Council's newfound sternness as positive, but Jedi accustomed to a looser hierarchy see the Council as oppressive in its influence. Conversely, some Jedi are enthusiastic about implementing more stringent policies. [The Jedi Covenant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Covenant "The Jedi Covenant") is one example of a reactionary group that Jedi heroes might oppose.

Though few galaxy-spanning threats are apparent during this time, The Jedi must still deal with remnants of [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith"), though this is a lower priority given their need to rebuild the Order. The Jedi expunge [The Mecrosa Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mecrosa_Order "The Mecrosa Order") from the decadent Tapani Sector, and the Great Hunt turns Jedi into exterminators, slaying [Terentateks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Terentateks "Terentateks") and other Sithspawn left over from [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War"). However, the Order returns to some semblance of normal operation, with Jedi running down pirates, breaking up crime rings, adjudicating planetary disputes, and fighting pockets of Mandalorian marauders on distant worlds.

## [The Mandalorian Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorian_Wars "The Mandalorian Wars")

**3,965-3,960 BBY**

Once again the galaxy plunges into war as [The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians"), thought disorganized after their defeat in [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War"), begin their onslaught of Republic worlds at the command of [Mandalore the Ultimate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mandalore_the_Ultimate "Mandalore the Ultimate"). Even though Jedi numbers have recuperated, their confidence and solidarity are not. This first interstellar crisis since [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War") highlights the rift in Jedi ideology, with rogue Jedi pressing the Council to help [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") and the Jedi Council taking a stance of nonviolence. Once the charismatic Jedi Knight [Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Revan "Revan") defiantly joins The Mandalorian Wars, other Jedi must choose a side. [The Revanchists](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Revanchists "The Revanchists"), as those Jedi who choose to defy the Council and join the war are called, are heralded as heroes by the Republic and its citizens.

Jedi fighting [The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians") experience regular interaction with grateful Republic soldiers as they share the mess halls, battleships, and front lines. Reluctant Jedi are drawn into the war, but some of Revan's followers abandon the fight once they see how thoughtlessly their leader approaches [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"). Some of Revan's recruits are spies, reporting back to the Council, but few are prepared for Revan's betrayal. At [Malachor V](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Malachor_V "Malachor V"), Revan confronts [Mandalore the Ultimate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mandalore_the_Ultimate "Mandalore the Ultimate"), drawing him into a trap and activating a superweapon known as [The Mass Shadow Generator](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mass_Shadow_Generator "The Mass Shadow Generator"). [The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians") are destroyed, but celebrations are cut short as [Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Revan "Revan"), already steeped in [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") from their dealings with Sith remnants, flees to [The Unknown Regions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Unknown_Regions "The Unknown Regions"), taking a large piece of the Republic military with them.

## [The Jedi Civil War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Civil_War "The Jedi Civil War")

**3,959-3,956 BBY**

When [Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Revan "Revan") and [Malak](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Malak "Malak") return from [The Unknown Regions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Unknown_Regions "The Unknown Regions") at the head of a revitalized Sith Empire, The Jedi are inevitably drawn into the conflict as [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic") fights to defend itself against the might of [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire"). Though The Jedi Civil War is precipitated by a betrayal from within their own ranks, The Jedi have been preparing for battle since [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War"). Witnessing the Sith corruption of Revan's army, The Jedi are again filled with conviction, fighting with impunity beside loyal Republic soldiers. Popular opinion of The Jedi, however, has declined steeply. Failing to comprehend the convoluted causes of the invasion of [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire"), the galactic citizenry understands only that Jedi they once championed against [The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians") have now betrayed them; hence, they refer to the conflict as The Jedi Civil War.

The Jedi Civil War rages for several years until the Council decides to deal [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") a major blow. A Jedi strike team, led by legendary Jedi Padawan [Bastila Shan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bastila_Shan "Bastila Shan"), lands on Darth Revan's flagship, captures them, and brings them back to the Republic. The Jedi convert [Revan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Revan "Revan") back to [The Light Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Light_Side "The Light Side") of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), purging them of the corruption of [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith"). With Revan's help The Jedi locate [The Star Forge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Star_Forge "The Star Forge")- the source of the Sith military's resurgence- and defeat [Darth Malak](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Malak "Darth Malak"). Having defeated the Sith leaders, The Jedi continue to struggle against the remnants of [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire"), who retreat behind their borders to recuperate.

## [The Dark Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Wars "The Dark Wars")

**3,955-3,951 BBY**

With their leaders defeated, [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") crumbles, and The Jedi face not a single, united Sith front, but instead a dozen or more Sith warlords who fight against [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic"), The Jedi, and each other. Unfortunately for The Jedi, a small cadre of Sith warlords manage to band together long enough to deal a staggering blow to the Order, using assassins to decimate The Jedi, leaving but a handful of survivors. This is a dire time for The Jedi. Reeling from crippling losses sustained during The Jedi Civil War, the Order hobbles on the brink of extinction. The Jedi Council's authority has completely broken down and satellite academies are near-empty. The surviving Jedi are on a desperate mission to find the source of the killings.

After The Sith Triumvirate is discovered and eliminated by a surviving Jedi, the few remaining Jedi resurrect the Order from the ashes of its defeat.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

The fates of the Republic and The Jedi Order are entwined. For over a thousand generations, The Jedi serve as guardians of peace and justice for the Republic. These masters of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") grow and thrive alongside the Republic through the ages, dealing with crises that threaten the Republic and its citizens while maintaining peace and harmony in the galaxy.

The decline of the Republic heralds the decline of The Jedi Order as well. As more and more star systems react to the burden of heavy taxation and political corruption, The Jedi spread across the galaxy to keep order. However, they are unable to resolve every petty dispute or stem the threat of civil war.

The formation of [The Confederacy of Independent Systems](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Confederacy_of_Independent_Systems "The Confederacy of Independent Systems") and the Separatists' intention to declare war against [The Galactic Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Galactic_Republic "The Galactic Republic") force The Jedi to respond and attempt to quell the warmongering immediately. The Jedi Order's efforts to put down the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") forces of the Separatists fail and cost the lives of many of Jedi Knights, but the arrival of cloned troops with Master [Yoda](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yoda "Yoda") turns the tide of the battle and drives the Separatist leaders into retreat. [The Clone Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Clone_Wars "The Clone Wars") change the role of The Jedi dramatically. Once the arbiters of peace, they become leaders in war.

This change creates other shifts within The Jedi Order as well. While some view [The Clone Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Clone_Wars "The Clone Wars") as an opportunity to help save the Republic from destruction, others see their involvement in the war as a perversion of their basic beliefs. Some Jedi splinter away to form their own groups that maintain their ideals without compromise. Still others feel that The Jedi Order approaches the war from the wrong direction, so they split off to create their own factions that fight the war as they see fit. The Jedi Council does everything in its power to hold together the crumbling Republic, but their faith in the Republic and their authority is undermined by the Dark Lords of [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith").

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

The destruction of The Jedi Order and the execution of Order 66 dealt a huge blow to the forces of good in the galaxy. Though some Jedi survived the purge, their lives were irrevocably altered the moment [Anakin Skywalker](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Anakin_Skywalker "Anakin Skywalker") gave in to [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") and [Darth Vader](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Vader "Darth Vader") was born. No longer members of an ancient tradition of sages and guardians, in the blink of an eye surviving Jedi became the last vestiges of a broken tradition. Literally overnight, many Jedi found themselves without allies, without a home, and without any direction. The galaxy had gone from a war-torn landscape in which The Jedi acted as generals to a place of tyrannical order in which The Jedi were outlawed. Imagine waking up to find that, even though you had done nothing differently, today those who considered you an ally now consider you worthy of execution. All Jedi faced this jarring revelation in the days following Order 66, the repercussions of which shape what those few survivors would do during [The Dark Times](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Times "The Dark Times").

For some Jedi, the transition was more than they could bear. One day the galaxy is defended by The Jedi, and the next it is defenseless against the might of the Empire. Those surviving Jedi who were the most devastated by the deaths of their companions suffer greatly under the weight of the extermination of The Jedi. Some Jedi actually go insane, either partially or wholly, leaving them but shells of the great heroes they once were. In fact, an [Aleena](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aleena "Aleena") Jedi Knight named [Kazdan Paratus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kazdan_Paratus "Kazdan Paratus") was so deranged in the wake of the purge that he constructed an entire false world for himself on the junkyard planet of [Raxus Prime](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Raxus_Prime "Raxus Prime"). Though his was an extreme example, it simply illustrates how badly the extermination affects those surviving Jedi.

Some Jedi refuse to accept that the Order had been defeated, carrying on a personal crusade against the Empire. With the help of allies and other like-minded individuals, these Jedi fight small wars against the Empire, clinging to the belief that they can still bring down the tyrannical government and restore peace and justice to the galaxy. Others fight to lay the seeds of rebellion, hoping that their example will lead others to join their efforts. Jedi Master [Rahm Kota](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rahm_Kota "Rahm Kota"), a brilliant tactician and military commander from [The Clone Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Clone_Wars "The Clone Wars"), wages a private war against the Empire, striking out from hidden safe houses to sabotage Imperial facilities. Many heroic Jedi follow a similar path, since doing so allows them to remain true to their principles and beliefs.

Some Jedi simply abandon their past, instead choosing to start their lives over. Though for most of them The Jedi Order is the only life they have known, a select few put down their [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers") and abandon their meditative techniques, choosing to live a mundane life rather than spend their days on the run. These Jedi become law enforcement officials, bounty hunters, or mercenaries, or even take on jobs that don't make use of any of the combat training they have received. Though some consider these acts cowardly, most Jedi who abandon their previous lives see themselves as engaging in self-preservation. They believe that no one can take on the Empire alone, so they attempt to simply live the rest of their lives in peace. However, it does not seem as though [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") is willing to let these people go so easily, and some of them find that their [Destiny](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny "Destiny") brings them back into touch with their old habits.

Finally, a few Jedi choose to fade away into the backdrop of the galaxy, biding their time until they can act. They do not abandon their lives as Jedi, but they do not actively seek to draw the Empire's attention. Both [Yoda](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yoda "Yoda") and [Obi-Wan Kenobi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Obi-Wan_Kenobi "Obi-Wan Kenobi") choose this life, as does the [Togruta](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Togruta "Togruta") Jedi [Shaak Ti](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shaak_Ti "Shaak Ti"). By retreating into the shadows, these individuals ensure that the Empire's Jedi hunters do not find them, allowing them to prepare for a time in the future when they will once again step onto the galactic stage. These Jedi spend their days honing their skills in [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), meditating and practicing their [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") techniques. They believe that their [Destiny](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny "Destiny") has not yet been fulfilled, and that only the guidance of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), not their own desire to strike back at the Empire, is the true path to success.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

The Jedi Order lies in ruins. Not since [The Dark Times](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Times "The Dark Times") have The Jedi been so close to extinction. [The Sith-Imperial War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith-Imperial_War "The Sith-Imperial War") concludes with [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") ruling the galaxy and the fall of the Jedi Temple on [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant"). Imperial forces led by [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") attack the Jedi Academy on [Ossus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ossus "Ossus"), along with the [Yuuzhan Vong](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong "Yuuzhan Vong") who tend the planet's terraforming project. Among the dead are [Kol Skywalker](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kol_Skywalker "Kol Skywalker") and, many presume, his son Cade. A few Jedi flee [Ossus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ossus "Ossus"), scatter across the galaxy, and go into hiding. While the exact number of survivors is impossible to ascertain, some speculate that as much as half of The Jedi Order survives the massacre.

Prior to [The Sith-Imperial War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith-Imperial_War "The Sith-Imperial War"), The Jedi had championed the use of [Yuuzhan Vong Biotechnology](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong_Biotechnology "Yuuzhan Vong Biotechnology") to rehabilitate worlds ravaged during the Yuuzhan Vong Invasion. [Ossus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ossus "Ossus") served as the primary test case. Unfortunately, [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") secretly sabotage the effort in order to turn the galaxy against the [Yuuzhan Vong](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong "Yuuzhan Vong"), The Jedi, and [The Galactic Alliance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Galactic_Alliance "The Galactic Alliance"). A few [Yuuzhan Vong](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong "Yuuzhan Vong") avoid the attack on [Ossus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ossus "Ossus") and remain hidden there for many years afterward. They investigate the source of the Sith sabotage and protect Jedi artifacts from pirate sand thieves.

Seven years after the massacre on [Ossus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ossus "Ossus"), Jedi Master [Wolf Sazen](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wolf_Sazen "Wolf Sazen") and Jedi Knight [Shado Vao](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shado_Vao "Shado Vao") search for [Cade Skywalker](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cade_Skywalker "Cade Skywalker"), whom Wolf believes is still alive. They succeed in finding the troubled Skywalker and try to convince him to return to The Jedi Order. Thanks to a vision he receives, Sazen believes Skywalker could be a rallying point for all Jedi. Others are less optimistic about Skywalker's future. Ultimately, Cade asks to restart his training, and Wolf obliges. During this time, they discover the [Yuuzhan Vong](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong "Yuuzhan Vong") Shaper [Nei Rin](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nei_Rin "Nei Rin") and her protectors hidden below the Ossus Academy. Master [K'Kruhk](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/K%27Kruhk "K'Kruhk") is given custody of the surviving Jedi artifacts that the [Yuuzhan Vong](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong "Yuuzhan Vong") had protected for so many years.

Cade's training is incomplete when he rushes off to save [Hosk Trey'lis](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hosk_Trey%27lis "Hosk Trey'lis") from [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith"), attempting to break him out of the Sith Temple on [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant"). As Wolf and the others await Cade's return, Imperial forces return to investigate the ruined academy. Unable to locate the surviving Jedi, a suspicious Sith commander orders the complete destruction of the academy ruins by orbital bombardment.

As for those Jedi scattered throughout the galaxy, most remain hidden. Gathering in large groups draws the attention of [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith"). Jedi influence is weak. They know they must rebuild their Order before they can be of any use to the galaxy as a whole. [Wolf Sazen](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wolf_Sazen "Wolf Sazen") believes that [Cade Skywalker](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cade_Skywalker "Cade Skywalker") is the key to their revival and future success.

Skywalker is not the only hope, however. At the beginning of [The Sith-Imperial War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith-Imperial_War "The Sith-Imperial War"), [Kol Skywalker](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kol_Skywalker "Kol Skywalker") orders the creation of a secret Hidden Temple. Unfortunately, it is incomplete when the [Ossus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ossus "Ossus") massacre occurs. Very few Jedi know of its existence. However, a Jedi Council of three masters know and control the Hidden Temple: Master [Tili Qua](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tili_Qua?action=edit&redlink=1 "Tili Qua (page does not exist)") and two of the oldest surviving Masters, [K'Kruhk](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/K%27Kruhk "K'Kruhk") and [T'ra Saa](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/T%27ra_Saa?action=edit&redlink=1 "T'ra Saa (page does not exist)"). They secretly work to bring other Jedi to the temple and have located some Masters, a few Jedi Knights, and many younglings and Padawans.

## The Hidden Temple

Under the guidance of [Grand Master Luke Skywalker](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grand_Master_Luke_Skywalker "Grand Master Luke Skywalker"), The Jedi Order rebuilds itself and grows to incredible heights after the war with the [Yuuzhan Vong](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong "Yuuzhan Vong"). However, the growth and prosperity of The Jedi Order ends with the onset of [The Sith-Imperial War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith-Imperial_War "The Sith-Imperial War"). To keep the Jedi tradition alive in the face of extinction, Jedi Master [Nat Skywalker](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nat_Skywalker "Nat Skywalker") leads the construction of a secret temple and keeps its existence hidden even from other Jedi. Unfortunately, the defeat of [The Galactic Alliance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Galactic_Alliance "The Galactic Alliance") at [Caamas](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Caamas "Caamas") coupled with the massacre of The Jedi at [Ossus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ossus "Ossus") spells doom for The Jedi Order. Nat Skywalker's secret redoubt, dubbed The Hidden Temple by those Jedi who once knew of it, passes into obscurity as the few survivors of the Order are scattered to the far corners of the galaxy, hunted by [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") and [The Galactic Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Galactic_Empire "The Galactic Empire").

The Hidden Temple is located on the planet [Taivas](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Taivas?action=edit&redlink=1 "Taivas (page does not exist)") deep within a massive, subterranean cavern. It is actually constructed of several [Capital Ships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Capital_Ships "Capital Ships") connected to one another by various permanent edifices and enclosed walkways. Although The Jedi are careful not to leave behind records or cargo manifests that might lad enemies to [Taivas](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Taivas?action=edit&redlink=1 "Taivas (page does not exist)"), [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith"), in searching for just such a hidden redoubt, learn that Jedi had purchased several [Capital Ships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Capital_Ships "Capital Ships") for the purpose of creating a mobile, hidden fleet. Rumors still persist in many Imperial circles that such a fleet exists, and this is exactly the kind of misdirection that [Nat Skywalker](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nat_Skywalker "Nat Skywalker") planned when he choose the design for The Hidden Temple.

Since the final destruction of The Jedi Order at [Ossus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ossus "Ossus"), The Hidden Temple has seen little of its intended use. Over the years since the Order's dissolution at the hands of [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith"), a few Jedi Masters have found their way to the temple. However, most surviving Jedi have no idea that a secret Jedi base exists far from the eyes of [The Galactic Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Galactic_Empire "The Galactic Empire"). Following Cade Skywalker's escape from Imperial clutches, The Hidden Temple sees renewed activity, and several surviving Jedi Knights and Jedi Masters congregate there to meet with one another and plot a course for the surviving members of the Order.

The location of The Hidden Temple cannot be learned through [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks"); instead, finding the location of The Hidden Temple should be the reward for a short adventure, or something acquired during the course of the campaign. Once the heroes know that [Taivas](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Taivas?action=edit&redlink=1 "Taivas (page does not exist)") is their destination, reaching The Hidden Temple is no simple matter. [Vehicle Pilots](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilots "Vehicle Pilots"), [Copilots](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Copilots "Copilots"), or [System Operators](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/System_Operators "System Operators") on [Starships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships "Starships") approaching [Taivas](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Taivas?action=edit&redlink=1 "Taivas (page does not exist)") need to make a DC 20 [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") check to locate an entrance to the cavern housing The Hidden Temple, and then [Vehicle Pilots](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilots "Vehicle Pilots") must succeed on three consecutive DC 20 [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") checks to approach it safely. Failing the [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") check means that the character fails to find an entrance, and failing a [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") check results in a [Collision](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Collision "Collision") with a Colossal (Cruiser) sized rocky outcropping in the passage leading to the cavern.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

Like any other established organization, The Jedi Order has an internal structure that helps maintain stability and allows it to function properly. From the youngest infant to the Jedi Masters seated on the High Council, every member of The Jedi has a place and purpose.

All younglings undergo formal education within the temple on [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant") and learn what they need to be a Jedi. At the appropriate age, they are either selected by a Jedi Master to become a Padawan or join a branch of the Jedi Service Corps. While those that join the Service Corps usually live the rest of their lives in service there, Padawans continue their education under the direct tutelage of their Jedi Masters until they pass the trials to become a Jedi Knight. Jedi who expand their knowledge and understanding of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") might reach the level of Jedi Master, and those Jedi who serve The Jedi Order with distinction might be offered a seat on one of the councils that oversee the actions and activities of their fellow Jedi Knights and Jedi Masters.

## The Jedi Service Corps

If younglings pass the age to begin their training as a Padawan without being chosen by a Jedi Master, they are sent to the Jedi Service Corps. The Service Corps has four separate branches of service: the Agricultural Corps, the Educational Corps, the Exploration Corps, and the Medical Corps. Younglings are assigned to a branch that reflects their individual talents. There, they serve the rest of their time in The Jedi Order.

Each branch serves The Jedi Order and [The Galactic Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Galactic_Republic "The Galactic Republic") according to its designed purpose. The Agricultural Corps (or [AgriCorps](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/AgriCorps "AgriCorps")) serves the Republic by tending to the various farms established throughout [The Core Worlds](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Core_Worlds?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Core Worlds (page does not exist)") to help feed its citizens. The Educational Corps (or [EduCorps](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/EduCorps "EduCorps")) dispatches its members throughout the galaxy to provide for the education needs of many of the Republic's underprivileged children and, in turn, learn about the worlds to which they travel to help expand the knowledge base of The Jedi Order. The Exploration Corps (or [ExplorCorps](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/ExplorCorps "ExplorCorps")) primarily travels on missions to explore [The Unknown Regions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Unknown_Regions "The Unknown Regions") of the galaxy, charting new star systems and discovering new civilizations and races. The [ExplorCorps](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/ExplorCorps "ExplorCorps") also works in conjunction with like-minded organizations, such as [The Intergalactic Zoological Society](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Intergalactic_Zoological_Society?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Intergalactic Zoological Society (page does not exist)"). The Medical Corps (or [MedCorps](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/MedCorps "MedCorps")) tends to the medical needs of Republic citizens throughout the galaxy, assisting the medical staff in many of the galaxy's hospitals and medical facilities. In addition, they also are responsible for maintaining the infirmary located in the Jedi Temple on [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant").

## Specialized Jedi Knights

Jedi who achieve knighthood and have a natural talent in certain areas might choose to serve The Jedi Order in ways that allow them to use such talents to the best of their ability. Many areas are available from which one can choose, and the ones listed here are but a sample of the ways in which Jedi Knights might serve The Jedi Order.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

Throughout [The Old Republic Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic_Era "The Old Republic Era"), the formal organization of The Jedi Order adjusts and changes more frequently than in other eras. Some of these adjustments occur as the Jedi Council attempts to exert more control over the Order, but many are in response to external forces, such as the many wars of the era that directly impact all Jedi. Some organizations included here might be available or fully functional only at specific times.

## Academies and Enclaves

Throughout this era, Jedi training continues to be largely decentralized. For many, training takes place at academies and enclaves scattered throughout the galaxy. Even though these organizations recognize the direction of the Jedi High Council on [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant"), they operate independently and promote their own philosophical views on [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). The terms academy and enclave are sometimes used interchangeably. However, academies are educational facilities, whereas enclaves provide living quarters and support for fully trained Jedi. An enclave might house its own Jedi Council, and might have an attached academy. In either case, academies and enclaves are communal facilities, providing living quarters, meeting space, and safe haven to members.

**The Jedi Temple on Coruscant:** The most prominent Jedi Academy is located at the Jedi Temple on [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant"), under the auspices of the Jedi High Council. This academy is usually the largest in the Order, but its size and overall influence fluctuates with political and military events that impact [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant"). Training methods vary, but the Master and Apprentice model is most often used.

**The Jedi Enclave on Dantooine:** The Jedi Enclave on [Dantooine](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dantooine "Dantooine") is one of the oldest enclaves and academies in the Order. A satellite Council here manages Jedi affairs on nearby worlds in [The Outer Rim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Outer_Rim?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Outer Rim (page does not exist)"), and the chambers serve as a meeting place for the [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant") High Council when the Council is in the area. Many prominent Jedi students in the era study here. The enclave is completely destroyed by [Darth Malak](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Malak "Darth Malak") during [The Jedi Civil War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Civil_War "The Jedi Civil War"). The site is abandoned and in ruins for many years after the Sith attack, but it is eventually rebuilt.

**The Taris Academy:** The Taris Academy, housed in the Jedi Tower on the planet [Taris](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Taris "Taris"), is an example of a smaller training facility. Early classes vary in size, but in the years immediately before [The Mandalorian Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorian_Wars "The Mandalorian Wars"), it is home to five Masters with a single student each. In addition to teaching, the Masters there contribute to planetary security, with their Padawans keeping justice as part of their training. The Jedi Order and local officials do not know that the five Masters are also the First WatchCircle of Krynda's Covenant, sent there to investigate possible Sith threats to the galaxy. When these Masters perceive such a threat from their own students, they murder them and blame the lone surviving student, [Zayne Carrick](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Zayne_Carrick "Zayne Carrick"). The academy is closed, and the Jedi are recalled to [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant"). The building is later used as a headquarters for the Mandalorian invaders until its destruction by the planetary resistance.

### Using Academies and Enclaves

The decentralized and wide variations in the Jedi teaching techniques and organizations during this era provide Gamemasters with a lot of flexibility in their own games. The Gamemaster might place the campaign in a familiar setting, such as the Jedi Enclave on [Dantooine](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dantooine "Dantooine") or Jedi Temple on [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant"), and find plenty of material in this article to support it. The Gamemaster can also create a Jedi training facility populated with characters and backstories of their own devising, giving the Gamemaster the advantage of full control over the characters' fate and interaction with the player characters. Examples include:

- **The Solo Master:** A single Jedi Master provides training for one or more apprentices, often on an isolated planet. The Master might be a Jedi Watchman for the planet, or a freethinking Jedi, promoting their own view of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") without disruptions from the Jedi Council. Their views are not likely to be radically different from other Jedi, such as promoting [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"), but might be thought of as subversive and dangerous to the Council itself.
- **The Satellite Academy:** A small teaching facility run by several Jedi Masters and Knights with a small number of students, A satellite academy is attached to a larger or more prominent facility, such as the Jedi Temple on [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant") or the Jedi Enclave on [Dantooine](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dantooine "Dantooine"). Students might be training at this smaller facility for a limited time, or they might be permanent residents, making short trips to the main academy for additional training. These satellites might also provide other Jedi services. The Jedi Tower on [Taris](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Taris "Taris") is an example of a satellite academy.
- **The Regional Enclave:** A moderately sized Jedi enclave that serves as a regional center for Jedi personnel and major contact point for area governments and other organizations to work directly with The Jedi Order. The enclave might not have a Jedi Council of its own, but it will at least have one Jedi Master who is recognized as the leader. The enclave might promote its own view of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), or might have considerable influence in the area, ranging from a local presence to planetary or sectorwide influence.

## [The Jedi Covenant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Covenant "The Jedi Covenant")

An example of the limited reach and effectiveness of the Jedi High Council, The Jedi Covenant is a secret organization established within The Jedi Order. The Jedi High Council on [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant") is unaware of its existence. The Covenant is wholly dedicated to preventing the return of [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith"). Covenant members depend heavily on **[Visions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Visions "Visions")** and *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")* for guidance. Their fanatical dedication to this task leads them down paths that most Jedi would never find acceptable. However, they have been able to operate and grow without significant interference for twenty years before the Mandalorian onslaught.

[Krynda Draay](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Krynda_Draay?action=edit&redlink=1 "Krynda Draay (page does not exist)") establishes The Jedi Covenant after her perceived failure to recognize the threat [Exar Kun](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exar_Kun "Exar Kun") represented to The Jedi and the galaxy. Funded by the assets of her dead husband's fortune, she dedicates herself and her new organization to defeating [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith"). To achieve this goal, the Covenant recruits Jedi of many traits and talents. At its core, the Covenant depends on Jedi Consular vision techniques developed by Krynda. She trains other Consulars, organizing them into WatchCircles. They pass significant information on to Jedi Guardians and Jedi Shadows within the Covenant, enabling them to carry out any tasks necessary to support or prevent the Consular's visions from coming true.

At least, that is the theory. In reality, the situation is more complex and fluid. Those involved in the Covenant are on constant guard to keep the greater Jedi Order from discovering their activities. Many also have influential connections that they wish to protect or wield, in addition to their own ambitions within The Jedi Order. As such, the flow of information is not as direct as planned, and those acting upon the information are not always those intended in the structure of the Covenant. In some cases, a WatchCircle revealed to The Jedi Order or of the general public can be severed from the organization, framed as a single group of fanatics or wayward Jedi.

### Covenant Hierarchy

**[Krynda Draay](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Krynda_Draay?action=edit&redlink=1 "Krynda Draay (page does not exist)") and [Haazen](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Haazen?action=edit&redlink=1 "Haazen (page does not exist)"):** [Krynda Draay](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Krynda_Draay?action=edit&redlink=1 "Krynda Draay (page does not exist)") has the power, finances, and will to build the Covenant. She works with [Haazen](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Haazen?action=edit&redlink=1 "Haazen (page does not exist)") to establish the details and organization. In the years before [The Mandalorian Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorian_Wars "The Mandalorian Wars"), [Haazen](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Haazen?action=edit&redlink=1 "Haazen (page does not exist)") directs the day-to-day operations of the Covenant, leaving Krynda to concentrate on visions and WatchCircle training.

**WatchCircles:** WatchCircle cells are groups of three to eight Consulars called Seers of the Circle. They typically use *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")*, **[Visions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Visions "Visions")**, and Krynda's group techniques to foresee the future. Over time, WatchCircles are quietly installed across the galaxy, particularly near locations where Sith threats have been foreseen. Some are devoted to early warnings on various aspects of galactic life, such as the Economic WatchCircle. The WatchCircle of [Lucien Draay](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lucien_Draay "Lucien Draay") is known as the First WatchCircle. Other WatchCircles are named for fallen Jedi heroes of [The Great Sith War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Great_Sith_War "The Great Sith War"), such as WatchCircle Vodo for [Vodo-Siosk Baas](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vodo-Siosk_Baas "Vodo-Siosk Baas").

**Executors:** Each WatchCircle has a single Jedi Guardian who acts as an Executor, the facilitator who manages logistical details so the WatchCircles can do their job. The Executor is also responsible for reporting the WatchCircle's findings and activities back to [Haazen](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Haazen?action=edit&redlink=1 "Haazen (page does not exist)"). Executors also operate individually, providing general security for the Covenant. In all cases, these individuals are selected for their inability to use *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")* or other significant precognitive abilities, so that they do not taint or influence the findings of their WatchCircle.

**Covenant Shadows:** The Covenant Shadows operate at the direction of [Haazen](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Haazen?action=edit&redlink=1 "Haazen (page does not exist)"), at the request of an Executor, to carry out assignments and missions that a WatchCircle or Executor should not be associated with. When undertaking a mission, the Shadow operates under the Executor's authority, but the Shadow is detached from the Executor's group once the task is complete. Missions include investigating possible Sith-influenced individuals, as well as locating and acquiring Sith artifacts. They operate alone in most cases. The Shadows are removed from the rolls of The Jedi Order, either through bureaucratic subterfuge, or by being falsely reported dead. They give up their former lives in their dedication to the eradication of [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith"). The only full list of Shadows is kept at the Draay Estate and is maintained by [Haazen](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Haazen?action=edit&redlink=1 "Haazen (page does not exist)"), [Lucien Draay](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lucien_Draay "Lucien Draay"), and a small number of other Guardians involved in logistics.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

Jedi selected to lead The Jedi Order are grouped into four Councils: the Council of First Knowledge, the Council of Reassignment, the Council of Reconciliation, and the High Council. Each Council gathers in the Jedi Temple on [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant").

## The Council of First Knowledge

Assembling in the northwest tower of the Jedi Temple on [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant"), the Council of First Knowledge advises Jedi on matters that require access to and use of ancient Jedi lore. The Council also oversees the repository of [Holocrons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Holocrons "Holocrons") stored within their tower as well as certain sections of the Jedi Archives.

The Council of First Knowledge has five members: one permanent member known as the Caretaker of the Tower of First Knowledge, and four rotating members from the Jedi High Council. The Council's permanent member at the time of [The Clone Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Clone_Wars "The Clone Wars") is Jedi Master [Astaal Vilbum](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Astaal_Vilbum?action=edit&redlink=1 "Astaal Vilbum (page does not exist)").

## The Council of Reassignment

The Council of Reassignment, stationed in the northeast tower of the Jedi Temple on [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant"), organizes work for the younglings and other Jedi students not chosen to become Padawans. Members of the Council work with the Jedi Service Corps to ensure the fair distribution of members. In addition, the Council reassigns Jedi to other positions and responsibilities as warranted.

The Council of Reassignment is made up of five Jedi Masters: two permanent members, and three rotating members (usually members of the High Council) who serve five-year terms.

## The Council of Reconciliation

The purpose of the Council of Reconciliation, which is housed in the southwest tower of the Jedi Temple, is to seek out peaceful resolutions to various conflicts- usually political disputes- throughout the Republic and to assign Jedi on missions to implement those solutions.

The Council of Reconciliation has five rotating members who can be chosen from among any interested Jedi Masters.

## The High Council

The primary governing body of The Jedi Order, the High Council meets in the southeast tower of the Jedi Temple on [Coruscant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coruscant "Coruscant") and supervises all activities undertaken by The Jedi. In addition, the High Council also serves as an advising body for the Supreme Chancellor of the Republic. The High Council has twelve members: five permanent, four long-term, and three limited-term. Most members are Jedi Masters, but a few Jedi Knights have been selected to serve a limited term. The High Council selects all new members, with the exception of [Anakin Skywalker](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Anakin_Skywalker "Anakin Skywalker") (appointed by the Supreme Chancellor near the end of [The Clone Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Clone_Wars "The Clone Wars")).

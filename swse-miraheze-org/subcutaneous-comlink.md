# Subcutaneous Comlink

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Subcutaneous_Comlink](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Subcutaneous_Comlink) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:08:19Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

**Cost:** 4,000

**Installation Cost:** 3,000

**Rejection Attack Bonus:** +3

**Availability:** [Restricted](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Restricted "Restricted")

A Subcutaneous Comlink features special sensors and transmitters attached to the vocal and auditory organs of the host. This allows the creature with this [Advanced Cybernetic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Advanced_Cybernetic "Advanced Cybernetic") to hear [Comlink](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Comlink "Comlink") transmissions and to send his or her own by making only slight vocal sounds. When a being with a Subcutaneous Comlink wishes, he or she can communicate through this Subcutaneous Comlink without making a sound loud enough for others to hear.

# Jet Pack

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jet_Pack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jet_Pack) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:04:19Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

**Cost:** 300

**Weight:** 30 Kilograms

A Jet Pack is a propulsion system that a character can strap on, allowing flight over short distances. Arm and wrist controls are employed for maneuvering. Activating a Jet Pack is a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), and you gain a Fly Speed of 6 squares until the end of your turn. A Jet Pack has 10 charges and can be run continuously; no [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") is required to activate the Jet Pack on subsequent rounds of use.

Routine maneuvers do not require a [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") check, but you must make a DC 20 [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") check if you land after moving more than 12 squares during the same turn; on a failure you fall [Prone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prone "Prone"). A Jet Pack can lift up to 180 kg while flying. Replacement fuel cells cost 100 credits.

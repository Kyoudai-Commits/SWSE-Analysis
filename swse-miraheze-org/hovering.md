# Hovering

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hovering](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hovering) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:08:11Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

**Base Speed:** 6 squares

**Cost:** 100 x [Cost Factor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cost_Factor "Cost Factor") x (Base Speed Squared)

Hovering Droids use repulsorlift technology to float slowly above the ground (within 3 meters). They ignore the penalties of [Difficult Terrain](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Difficult_Terrain "Difficult Terrain").

# Blaster Carbine

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Blaster_Carbine](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Blaster_Carbine) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:04:43Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

This small Blaster Carbine has a short barrel and compact two-handed grip, making it look more like a long pistol than a rifle. Some Blaster Carbines, such as the E-5 used by [B1-Series Battle Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/B1-Series_Battle_Droids "B1-Series Battle Droids"), have a [Retractable Stock](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Retractable_Stock "Retractable Stock"). A Blaster Carbine without a [Retractable Stock](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Retractable_Stock "Retractable Stock") costs 850 credits (instead of 900 credits). Because of its compact design, a Blaster Carbine can always be used to make an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity"), even if its stock is not folded. A Blaster Carbine requires a [Power Pack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Power_Pack "Power Pack") to operate. After 50 shots, the [Power Pack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Power_Pack "Power Pack") must be replaced.

**Weapon Type:** [Rifles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rifles "Rifles")

**Size:** Medium

**Cost:** 900

**Damage:** 3d8

**[Stun](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stun "Stun") Setting:** YES

**Rate of Fire:** Single-Shot, [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire")

**Weight:** 2.2 Kilograms

**Type:** Energy

**Availability:** [Restricted](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Restricted "Restricted")

**Inaccurate:** This weapon cannot fire at targets at Long [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range").

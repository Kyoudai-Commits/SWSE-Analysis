# Category : Ranged Weapons

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Ranged_Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Ranged_Weapons) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:06:44Z.

At any given time, hundreds of manufacturers are creating and marketing a wide array of ranged weapons. These weapons run the gamut from small, concealable handheld weapons to tripod-mounted cannons that require a small crew to operate.

Below are Ranged Weapons by [Weapon Groups](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Groups "Weapon Groups"):

- [Exotic Weapons (Ranged)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapons_(Ranged) "Exotic Weapons (Ranged)")- Unusual, or unique Ranged Weapons that require specific training to properly utilize.
- [Heavy Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Weapons "Heavy Weapons")- Large Ranged Weapons that require both hands, and in some cases a tripod, to accurately use.
- [Pistols](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pistols "Pistols")- Handheld Ranged Weapons, favored for self-defense, and are easily concealed.
- [Rifles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rifles "Rifles")- Standard military weapons, used with both hands for precise and accurate shooting.
- [Simple Weapons (Ranged)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Simple_Weapons_(Ranged) "Simple Weapons (Ranged)")- Basic Ranged Weapons that are simple or primitive in nature and use.
  - [Grenades](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grenades "Grenades")- Highly volatile explosives, generally used against heavily armored, or immobile targets.

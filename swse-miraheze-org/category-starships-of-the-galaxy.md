# Category : Starships of the Galaxy

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Starships_of_the_Galaxy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Starships_of_the_Galaxy) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:04:08Z.

[![](//static.wikitide.net/swsewiki/thumb/a/a8/Starships_of_the_Galaxy.webp/250px-Starships_of_the_Galaxy.webp.png)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/File:Starships_of_the_Galaxy.webp)

Throughout the *Star Wars* saga, space battles have determined the fate of entire worlds and altered the course of galactic history. They set the scene for heroes to meet their destinies, putting victory and glory- or death and defeat- within the range of a turbolaser cannon.

## Introduction

The cold stillness of space is shattered by the chaos of dozens of turbolasers discharging at once. An Imperial [Star Destroyer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Star_Destroyer "Star Destroyer") fires broadsides at a [Nebulon-B Frigate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nebulon-B_Frigate "Nebulon-B Frigate"), the two passing one another in opposite directions like oceangoing ships passing through the night. In between exchanges of heavy weapons fire, a trio of [TIE Fighters](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/TIE_Fighters "TIE Fighters") twists between glowing laser bolts while two [X-Wing Fighters](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/X-Wing_Fighters "X-Wing Fighters") mimic their movements, swirling around in a deadly dance. A [Corellian Corvette](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Corellian_Corvette "Corellian Corvette"), leaking atmosphere and listing heavily, slams into the bridge of the [Star Destroyer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Star_Destroyer "Star Destroyer"), setting off a chain reaction that destroys both vessels.

Sounds exiting? It does. Want it in your *Star Wars* campaign? You should.

That is what *Starships of the Galaxy* is all about. This section is designed to give Gamemasters and players everything they need to inject space combat, on both small and large scales, into any *Star Wars Roleplaying Game* campaign. This section contains new options for heroes looking to make their mark on the galaxy from the cockpit of a [Starfighter](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starfighter "Starfighter") and features information for Gamemasters to help make this happen. Whether you are simply looking for some new ships to throw into the mix or want to make space combat a larger part of your campaign, this section provides everything you need to enhance your *Star Wars* [Starship](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship "Starship")-based experience.

### Lock S-Foils in Attack Position!

The contents of this book are intended to allow players and Gamemasters to inject [Starship Combat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Combat "Starship Combat") into an ongoing campaign. The *[Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")* presents basic rules for space combat that mimic the rules for [Character Combat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Character_Combat "Character Combat"), and provide a quick and easy way to integrate [Starship Combat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Combat "Starship Combat") into the game. Although those rules are perfectly are perfectly fine for most encounters, some players and GMs may wish to include more depth and diversity in their space combat. Likewise, Gamemasters who want to run a campaign that focuses on a fighter squadron will likely need more robust rules for space combat and more options for opponents, allowing them to create diverse encounters in [Starships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships "Starships") just as they would at character scale.

This section is designed as a campaign supplement that expands [Starship Combat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Combat "Starship Combat") to be more than just the occasional chase scene in which the heroes escape from [TIE Fighters](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/TIE_Fighters "TIE Fighters") without engaging in serious combat. By using the options in this section, a Gamemaster can shift the focus in a campaign to [Starship Combat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Combat "Starship Combat"), or simply expand existing Space Combat encounters to make them more exiting. Though adding options from this book will likely add complexity to your game, the new mechanics presented here will expand and enhance your spacefaring adventures.

Although many of the rules in this section can be used for [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") on the ground (or even in the air), most of this section focuses on [Starships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships "Starships") operating in space. [Starship](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship "Starship") statistics still contain atmospheric movement speeds (following the standard format established in [Vehicle Combat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Combat "Vehicle Combat")), but the new mechanics are mostly geared toward [Starship Combat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Combat "Starship Combat").

### Get Everyone Involved

In the past, one of the more common drawbacks to [Starship Combat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Combat "Starship Combat") was that it left many players sitting and waiting for the encounter to end due to their lack of [Starship](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship "Starship")-related [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills"). Even when participating, many characters found their options limited by their roles aboard the ship. One of the primary goals of *Starships of the Galaxy* is to make [Starship Combat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Combat "Starship Combat") more accessible to characters of all roles and archetypes. For example, the [Noble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble "Noble") will find information on using their [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") for [Inspiration](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Inspiration_Talent_Tree "Inspiration Talent Tree") and [Leadership](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Leadership_Talent_Tree "Leadership Talent Tree") in [Starship Combat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Combat "Starship Combat"), while the [Scoundrel](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrel "Scoundrel") will be able to disrupt and damage their opponents just like they do in [Character Combat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Character_Combat "Character Combat"). *Starships of the Galaxy* should engage all players- not just [Vehicle Pilots](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilots "Vehicle Pilots") and [Gunners](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunners "Gunners")- to get involved and use their special abilities in space combat scenes.

Moreover, this section presents a variety of new options for the core [Heroic Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Classes "Heroic Classes") and relevant [Prestige Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Classes "Prestige Classes") (such as the [Ace Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ace_Pilot "Ace Pilot") and the [Officer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Officer "Officer")). Members of each [Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class "Class") will find new [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") (and even new [Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent_Trees "Talent Trees")), some new uses for [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills"), and some new [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") that will round out their characters. The goal of these new abilities is to get each character more involved in space combat. The *Saga Edition* rules set goes a long way toward encouraging this behavior; even characters [Untrained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Untrained "Untrained") in the [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") skill have some capacity to pilot a ship, and their ability to make attacks with [Vehicle Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Weapons "Vehicle Weapons") is equally enhanced by many of the same [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") and [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") that affect normal [Ranged Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attacks "Ranged Attacks"). Essentially, any character's basic abilities make that character capable of participating in space combat in one way or another.

### Starship Specialists

One of the best uses for *Starships of the Galaxy*, as mentioned above, is enabling characters to take full advantage of the new [Starship Combat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Combat "Starship Combat") rules presented here. These new options range from the aforementioned [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") and [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") to an entirely new [Starship Maneuvers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Maneuvers "Starship Maneuvers") system. Similar in design to [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") system from the *[Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*, these [Starship Maneuvers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Maneuvers "Starship Maneuvers") turn [Starship Combat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Combat "Starship Combat") into more than just a matter of moving and shooting. With the expanded system provided in this section, [Starship Combat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Combat "Starship Combat") becomes a more tactical affair for some characters, letting them do incredible things when behind a flight stick.

Fortunately for heroes not piloting the ship, there are plenty of new options in this book as well. [Engineers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Engineers "Engineers") and other technically inclined characters will find a number of new rules in *Starships of the Galaxy* that should make it easier for them to jump in and participate in space combat. Similarly, many of the [Starship Maneuvers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Maneuvers "Starship Maneuvers") can be used by [Gunners](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunners "Gunners") as well as [Vehicle Pilots](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilots "Vehicle Pilots"), allowing weapons operators to take full advantage of special bonuses and abilities just as [Vehicle Pilots](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilots "Vehicle Pilots") might. With *Starships of the Galaxy* in hand, you won't have to be an [Ace Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ace_Pilot "Ace Pilot") just to be effective at [Starship Combat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Combat "Starship Combat"), encouraging all character types to take part in space combat encounters.

### Starship Codex

Though the new character options certainly make [Starship Combat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Combat "Starship Combat") more appealing, the bulk of this book is dedicated to presenting statistics and information for a variety of [Starships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships "Starships"), ranging from small [Starfighters](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starfighters "Starfighters") to massive [Capital Ships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Capital_Ships "Capital Ships") and [Space Stations](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Space_Stations "Space Stations"). Players might be tempted to view *Starships of the Galaxy* as a catalog of potential purchases, and as heroes rise in statue and wealth, many new options (including those available in this book) become open to them. The [Starship Codex](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Codex "Starship Codex") also serves Gamemasters as a collection of challenges just waiting to be thrown at the heroes. Whether you are looking for a new personal [Starfighter](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starfighter "Starfighter") or the perfect ship to add to an admiral's fleet, that chapter should have you covered.

The [Starship Codex](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Codex "Starship Codex") presents ships of all types and sizes, drawn from the full breadth of the *Star Wars* saga and Expanded Universe of comics, novels, and video games. The majority of the statistics in this section are for the stock, unmodified versions of these ships, making it easier for players to obtain these ships and make it their own. For example, rather than presenting statistics for [The *Ebon Hawk*](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Ebon_Hawk "The Ebon Hawk") (from the *Knights of the Old Republic* video game series), this chapter contains statistics for the stock model of this ship (the [*Dynamic*-Class Freighter](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dynamic-Class_Freighter "Dynamic-Class Freighter")), which players can then modify to their own tastes. In some cases, statistics are presented for "famous" versions of these ships, but each one also contains the stock statistics for the vessel without modifications.

### Make it Your Own

*Starships of the Galaxy* presents a complete guide to modifying your [Starship](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship "Starship") to make it the way you want it. Han Solo's famous line about having made a few "special modifications" to [The *Millennium Falcon*](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Millennium_Falcon "The Millennium Falcon") comes to life in the form of the [Starship Modification](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Modification "Starship Modification") rules. Want to put a new engine in your freighter? Want to upgrade the laser cannons on your [X-Wing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/X-Wing "X-Wing")? Want to give that old [Corellian Corvette](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Corellian_Corvette "Corellian Corvette") some new hull plating? Now you can, and it's not just a matter of paying someone else to do it. Techie character will now enjoy getting their hands dirty and making these modifications themselves, using new abilities to enhance the modifications.

The [Starship Modification](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Modification "Starship Modification") rules can be used to build a [Starship](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship "Starship") from scratch. Though doing this is far more time-consuming than heroes simply purchasing a new [Starship](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship "Starship") and enhancing it to fit their needs, these rules allow you to take a standard, base model of a ship and add each component individually to come up with a custom spacecraft. Though most good outlaw techs will prefer the tinkering and reworking of a [Starship](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship "Starship") that the [Starship Modification](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Modification "Starship Modification") rules provide, a [Noble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble "Noble") seeking their own custom [Space Transport](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Space_Transport "Space Transport") might use these rules to order a ship directly from the factory, giving them a ship that is unlike any other vessel in the galaxy.

Additionally, some of the ships in this section are presented with deckplans suited for use by adventuring heroes. These deckplans allow players and Gamemasters to create interesting encounters aboard the [Starship](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship "Starship") itself; after all, you never know when you're going to need to repel boarders or ambush [Stormtroopers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stormtroopers "Stormtroopers") after hiding in your own smuggling compartments.

### Enhanced Encounters

Gamemasters will find these sections exceptionally useful for designing [Starship Combat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Combat "Starship Combat") encounters. One of the primary goals of these sections is to help make space combat just as existing, dynamic, and engaging as [Character Combat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Character_Combat "Character Combat"). One of the means by which this is accomplished is in helping Gamemasters designing interesting encounters. An encounter's design is critical to holding the players' interest, and the advice and options in these sections should make it easy for Gamemasters to come up with encounters that are more interesting than simply shooting at enemy ships from a distance. Additionally, the information in these sections should make it easier for the Gamemaster to keep all members of the party- not just the [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot") and [Gunners](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunners "Gunners")- engaged and interested in space combat encounters.

Good space combat encounter design involves more than just populating the battlefield with interesting enemies. [Starship Combat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Combat "Starship Combat") provides new options for [Starship Hazards](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Hazards?action=edit&redlink=1 "Starship Hazards (page does not exist)") (such as debris fields and gaseous nebulae) and helps Gamemasters introduce complications and other new factors into combat to increase tension or provide a sense of urgency. Gamemasters who take full advantage of these sections should be able to create [Starship Combat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Combat "Starship Combat") encounters that not only challenge their players but also create memorable battles like those seen in the *Star Wars* films.

### Updated Statistics

This book features a number of updated and expanded statistics for ships appearing in the *[Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*. In some cases, the statistics in these sections differ from those featured in the *[Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*, having been changed due to new rules presented in this book or to improve game play. Although the statistics in the *[Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")* will still work for your game, those found in these sections should supercede the ones from the *[Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")* in most cases. However, since no two [Starships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships "Starships") of the same model need to be identical, Gamemasters should feel free to use statistics from both books to provide variety in game play.

## [Starship Adventures](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Adventures "Starship Adventures")

The very title of the *Star Wars* setting makes it clear- most adventures are going to take place at least partially in space. Whether they're running past an enemy blockade of a planet, escaping [Star Destroyers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Star_Destroyers "Star Destroyers") through an asteroid field, or fighting epic space battles to decide the fate of the galaxy, *Star Wars* heroes are going to end up in space. This chapter provides advice on adding [Starship Scale](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Scale "Starship Scale") encounters to a game and rules for heroes to go beyond being part-time pilots and fighter jockeys.

## [Starship Combat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Combat "Starship Combat")

Starship Combat in the *Star Wars* universe is fast-paced and frantic: [Starfighters](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starfighters "Starfighters") clash in a void littered with obstacles and debris, [Capital Ships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Capital_Ships "Capital Ships") fire broadsides at one another, and [Space Transports](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Space_Transports "Space Transports") run blockades while evading pursuit. All of these scenes are examples of the kind of excitement that Starship Combat generates.

The rules set for Starship Combat presented in [Vehicle Combat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Combat "Vehicle Combat") works for fast and cinematic battles, but some players and Gamemasters might wish to add additional depth to their [Starship](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship "Starship") encounters. This section presents new rules as well as guidelines for including additional mechanics and options for Starship Combat.

### [Starship Maneuvers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Maneuvers "Starship Maneuvers")

The Starship Maneuvers system provides new options for any character acting as a [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot") or [Gunner](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunner "Gunner") during a [Starship Scale](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Scale "Starship Scale") encounter. Similar to the system for using [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers"), the Starship Maneuvers system allows characters to perform incredible stunts or make use of advanced tactics in order to gain the upper hand in [Starship Scale](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Scale "Starship Scale") combat. Except for Starship Maneuvers used by [Gunners](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunners "Gunners"), only [Pilots](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilots "Pilots") of [Starfighters](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starfighters "Starfighters") and [Airspeeders](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Airspeeders "Airspeeders") may make use of the mechanics in this section- [Capital Ships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Capital_Ships "Capital Ships") and [Space Transports](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Space_Transports "Space Transports") are too large and cumbersome. (However, the [Combat Thrusters](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Combat_Thrusters "Combat Thrusters") Starship Modification may be used on [Space Transports](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Space_Transports "Space Transports") to circumvent this restriction).

A Starship Maneuver is a special action that allows a [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot") or [Gunner](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunner "Gunner") to do something beyond the bounds of the [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") skill and the abilities provided by the [Ace Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ace_Pilot "Ace Pilot") Prestige Class. Starship Maneuvers enhance attacks, strengthen defenses, reduce damage, or produce other unique effects. A character who takes the [Starship Tactics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Tactics "Starship Tactics") feat automatically learns a number of Starship Maneuvers equal to 1 + their [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") modifier (minimum of 1). A character can learn additional Starship Maneuvers by taking the [Starship Tactics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Tactics "Starship Tactics") feat again or by increasing their [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") modifier.

For example, Syal Antilles is a 5th-level [Soldier](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier "Soldier") with a [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") score of 13 (+1 modifier). When she advances to 6th level, she takes the [Starship Tactics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Tactics "Starship Tactics") feat and immediately gains two Starship Maneuvers. She chooses *[Attack Pattern Delta](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_Pattern_Delta "Attack Pattern Delta")* and *[Corellian Slip](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Corellian_Slip "Corellian Slip")*. At 8th level, Syal raises her [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") score from 13 to 14, thereby increasing her ability modifier from +1 to +2; at that time, she gains an additional Starship Maneuver (since she has taken the [Starship Tactics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Tactics "Starship Tactics") feat once); she chooses *[Target Lock](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Target_Lock "Target Lock")*. At 9th level, Syal selects the [Starship Tactics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Tactics "Starship Tactics") feat again, granting her three new Starship Maneuvers. She chooses *[Tallon Roll](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tallon_Roll "Tallon Roll")*, *[Angle Deflector Shields](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Angle_Deflector_Shields "Angle Deflector Shields")*, and a second use of *[Corellian Slip](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Corellian_Slip "Corellian Slip")*.

Starship Maneuvers in Your GameStarship Maneuvers in Your Game

Adding the Starship Maneuvers system to your game can greatly change it, so Gamemasters should feel free to allow or disallow this system as fits their campaign. Taking the [Starship Tactics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Tactics "Starship Tactics") Feat consumes a valuable [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat") slot, so players should likely do so only if the campaign features a good amount of space or [Airspeeder](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Airspeeder "Airspeeder") combat. A good rule of thumb is that if the player won't have a chance to use any of these Starship Maneuvers at least once per adventure, the Gamemaster should seriously consider whether to allow the Starship Maneuvers system in the game.

## [Starship Modifications](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Modifications "Starship Modifications")

As soon as heroes get their hands on a [Starship](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship "Starship"), they want to modify it. The desire for faster [Hyperdrives](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hyperdrives "Hyperdrives"), more weapons, better shields, and possibly even some space for smuggled goods generally comes even before the first payment to a [Hutt](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hutt "Hutt") loan shark is due. Modifying a [Starship](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship "Starship") gives players a sense of ownership that makes them more connected to a campaign, and it serves other useful purposes as well.

Heroes who want to improve a [Starship](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship "Starship") have a built-in reason to take risks to earn credits (or the help- and use of the repair bay- of a grateful military group). Although *Star Wars* focuses on heroic action-adventure rather than the act of cobbling together credits for tinkering with a ship, many characters are simply more motivated if they know there's a material reward on the line. Furthermore, a [Starship](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship "Starship") acts as a good base of operations, allowing heroes to have a place that seems like home even as they gallivant across the galaxy. Finally, as the heroes gain levels, a better-equipped ship prepares them for greater challenges.

A Gamemaster should find these rules also useful for enhancing [Starships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships "Starships") flown by friends and foes of the heroes as well as for creating new [Starships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships "Starships") to represent the thousands of different models of spacecraft found throughout the galaxy. Though designing a new model of [Starship](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship "Starship") is a lengthy process, it gives you a completely new class of Starship from which you can build yet more variants.

Using Starship ModificationsUsing Starship Modifications

At its core, this section is a catalog of new options to add to a [Starship](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship "Starship"). Though it includes rules for figuring out the cost of a new system, what resources the system needs, and how long it takes to install it, these are secondary concerns. Some players love carefully calculating how many quad lasers they can add to a freighter before they have to sacrifice some cargo space, and the rules are here for them to do that.

There's nothing wrong, however, with just choosing a new system and asking the Gamemaster what it will take to add it to your ship. Acquiring new [Starship](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship "Starship") components can be as easy as doing the right job for the right patron or paying a professional to bring your ship up to par with your needs. The GM might not make everything available when you want it, but eventually you should see improvements become available without having to count every credit and every day in the dock.

Similarly, if you're a Gamemaster, you don't need to worry about these details any more than you want to. If the heroes' ship seems underpowered compared to other [Starships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships "Starships") of an appropriate Challenge Level, you might add an extra system or two (perhaps contributed by a patron, friend, or even a bounty hunter who steals their ship and modifies it before the heroes get it back). This section should be a jumping-off point for more adventure and excitement, not a chore in number-crunching. The style of campaign you're running is going to impact the relative value of any [Starship](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship "Starship") so much that no hard-and-fast set of rules could tell you for sure what to allow for the heroes, anyway. Simply use your best judgment: if it turns out that a ship is overpowered, simply arrange for a new system to short out when the [Starship](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship "Starship") next moves down its [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"). Make sure you provide an explanation to your players, though- faulty parts, incompetent labor, and sabotage are all reasonable possibilities. Each of these reasons can spark new adventures as, for example, the heroes track down the crooked outlaw tech who sold them a shoddy engine motivator.

## [Starship Codex](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Codex "Starship Codex")

This chapter features new [Starships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships "Starships") from a variety of eras and locales. Each Starship has its own entry, with a complete stat block and image of the [Starship](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship "Starship"). Many entries describe variants or famous individual ships, to further expand the usefulness of entries. Additionally, some [Space Transports](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Space_Transports "Space Transports") have deckplans included in their entries. These ships are among the most suitable vessels for heroes to use as a base of operations and means of transport, and the deckplans are designed to let Gamemasters use the ships as an adventuring locale as well as a means of transport.

# Darth Krayt

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Krayt](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Krayt) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:03:45Z.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

*Affiliations: [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith"), [The Galactic Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Galactic_Empire "The Galactic Empire")*

Arguably the most powerful man in the galaxy, Darth Krayt is both the lead of [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") and the reigning Emperor. Born and raised among [Tusken Raiders](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tusken_Raiders "Tusken Raiders") on [Tatooine](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tatooine "Tatooine"), Darth Krayt's original name is A'Sharad Hett. Trained as a Jedi Knight, Hett manages to survive Order 66 by escaping his Clone Troopers and slaying them. As Palpatine's Empire rises, Hett returns to his home planet where he clashes with [Obi-Wan Kenobi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Obi-Wan_Kenobi "Obi-Wan Kenobi") who, unbeknownst to Hett, has been watching over the infant [Luke Skywalker](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Luke_Skywalker "Luke Skywalker"). After his duel with Kenobi, Hett is forced to flee [Tatooine](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tatooine "Tatooine") in disgrace. For a short while, Hett works as a bounty hunter, until one day he pursues a bounty to [Korriban](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Korriban "Korriban"). Once there, Hett stumbles upon an ancient [Sith Holocron](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Holocron "Sith Holocron") and begins to learn the secrets of [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith"). Oddly, time seems to pass more slowly for Hett than the galaxy at large, and when he leaves [Korriban](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Korriban "Korriban"), he discovers that Palpatine's Empire has fallen.

Hett explores [The Unknown Regions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Unknown_Regions "The Unknown Regions") and is captured by a [Yuuzhan Vong](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong "Yuuzhan Vong") scouting expedition. Trapped aboard a [Yuuzhan Vong](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong "Yuuzhan Vong") ship, Hett encounters another former Jedi, [Vergere](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vergere?action=edit&redlink=1 "Vergere (page does not exist)"). Subjected to torment in [Yuuzhan Vong](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong "Yuuzhan Vong") torture devices, Hett allows Vergere's words to seduce him into surrendering to his agony and to [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"). During his torment, Hett begins to have visions of a new Sith Order and a galaxy under his control. Eventually, Hett frees himself from the [Yuuzhan Vong](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong "Yuuzhan Vong") and makes his way back to [Korriban](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Korriban "Korriban"), whereupon he adopts a new Sith name: Darth Krayt. On [Korriban](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Korriban "Korriban"), Darth Krayt begins rebuilding [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith"). His influence expands, but when it becomes clear that he cannot live long enough to fulfill his vision, Darth Krayt begins placing himself in stasis, issuing his orders telepathically while artificially extending his lifespan with a combination of healing trances and [Bio-Implants](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bio-Implants "Bio-Implants"). In the meantime, his new Sith Order has forged an important alliance with [Emperor Roan Fel](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Emperor_Roan_Fel "Emperor Roan Fel"), lending their aid in the defeat of [The Galactic Alliance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Galactic_Alliance "The Galactic Alliance") and [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi").

By the end of [The Sith-Imperial War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith-Imperial_War "The Sith-Imperial War"), Darth Krayt declares himself Emperor. However, his body suffers the effects of having lived far beyond its natural lifespan. The Yuuzhan Vong [Bio-Implants](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bio-Implants "Bio-Implants") used to extend his life begin to consume him. Furthermore, healing trances and Force healing become less effective. Darth Krayt knows that his time is limited unless a new solution is found. At one point he captures [Cade Skywalker](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cade_Skywalker "Cade Skywalker"), an heir to the Skywalker bloodline gifted with incredible healing powers, in the hopes that one day he will be able to harness those same powers to prolong his life.

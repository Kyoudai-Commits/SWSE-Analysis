# The Believers

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Believers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Believers) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:04:38Z.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

Rising to prominence shortly after the Battle of [Naboo](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Naboo "Naboo"), The Believers, based in the Cularin System, are a cult of Force-sensitive beings. Members devote their energies to studying and embracing [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), with the intent of following the doctrines and customs of the ancient Sith that existed before [Darth Bane](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Bane "Darth Bane"). Rejecting Bane's Rule of Two, The Believers seek to expand their numbers to eventually challenge [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") directly and usher in a new age for [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith"). All Believers can be recognized by a distinctive tattoo, a shape that resembles the spire of the Sith fortress on [Almas](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Almas "Almas") with a broken [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") at its base.

At first, the existence of The Believers is thought to be nothing more than rumor, since their presence cannot be confirmed by even [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"). During [The Clone Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Clone_Wars "The Clone Wars"), however, Jedi stationed in the Cularin System slowly begin turning to [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") and joining The Believers, confirming the existence of [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") cult and increasing its size. As their numbers swell, The Believers create a plague virus to eradicate the [Caarite](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Caarite "Caarite") race and [The Metatheran Cartel](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Metatheran_Cartel?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Metatheran Cartel (page does not exist)"). Initially, the virus is successful, but it is stopped by a counteragent created from samples of the virus recovered from early victims of the plague. With their plans foiled, The Believers abandon the Sith Temple on [Almas](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Almas "Almas") and go underground for a short time.

The Believers return later, when [The Darkstaff](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Darkstaff?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Darkstaff (page does not exist)"), a [Sith Artifact](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Artifact?action=edit&redlink=1 "Sith Artifact (page does not exist)"), attempts to protect itself from a group of agents that discover its connection to the shadow lurkers and the [Oblee](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Oblee?action=edit&redlink=1 "Oblee (page does not exist)"). Found by [Len Markus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Len_Markus?action=edit&redlink=1 "Len Markus (page does not exist)") in the Cularin asteroid belt, the artifact possesses him and rises to become the new leader of The Believers. [The Darkstaff](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Darkstaff?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Darkstaff (page does not exist)") calls on The Believers to destroy the freelance agents that hunted it. However, the agents manage to thwart The Believers and free the [Oblee](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Oblee?action=edit&redlink=1 "Oblee (page does not exist)").

**Membership:** Anyone with the [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity") feat and a [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") of 1 or higher is welcome to join The Believers by being accepted as an apprentice by a [Force Adept](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Adept "Force Adept") or [Force Disciple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Disciple "Force Disciple") of the cult. Members of The Believers gain access to the [Believer Disciple Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Believer_Disciple_Talent_Tree "Believer Disciple Talent Tree").

# 4th-Degree Droids

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/4th-Degree_Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/4th-Degree_Droids) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:04:24Z.

*Reference Books: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook"), [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

Security and Battle 4th-Degree [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") have the following traits:

- **Ability Modifiers:** All 4th-Degree Droids receive a +2 bonus to their [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity"), but suffer -2 penalties to both their [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") and [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma").
- **Behavioral Inhibitors:** 4th-Degree Droids firstly obey orders from their designated owner (whether that be an actual owner or themselves).
- **Droid Traits:** All 4th-Degree Droids posses [Droid Traits](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Traits "Droid Traits").
- **Droid Talents:** All 4th-Degree Droids can select Droid Talents from the [4th-Degree Droid Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/4th-Degree_Droid_Talent_Tree "4th-Degree Droid Talent Tree").

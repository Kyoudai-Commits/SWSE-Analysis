# Power Generator

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Power_Generator](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Power_Generator) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:09:00Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

**Cost:** 750

**Weight:** 15 Kilograms

A Power Generator is a small fusion reactor that provides continuous power for [Heavy Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Weapons "Heavy Weapons"), [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles"), structures, and machinery. It can power anything up to a Gargantuan [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") or structure indefinitely, a Colossal [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") or structure for 1 day, and a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") or structure of Colossal (Frigate) size for 1 hour. After that, it is [Disabled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disabled "Disabled") and must be replaced.

If a Power Generator is destroyed while in use, its fusion reactor overloads and explodes, venting plasma into the surrounding area. When a Power Generator explodes, make an attack roll (1d20+10) against the [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") of every target within 4 squares. If the attack succeeds, the target takes 8d6 points of Energy damage. If the attack misses, the target takes half damage. A target with the **[Evasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Evasion "Evasion")** Talent takes half damage if the attack succeeds, and no damage if the attack misses. This is an [Area Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attack "Area Attack").

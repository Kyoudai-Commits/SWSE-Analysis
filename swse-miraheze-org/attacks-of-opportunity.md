# Attacks of Opportunity

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:05:52Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

[![](//static.wikitide.net/swsewiki/thumb/1/14/Attacks_of_Opportunity_I.webp/300px-Attacks_of_Opportunity_I.webp.png)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/File:Attacks_of_Opportunity_I.webp)

Luke Skywalker moves 1 square and Attacks Royal Guard A. He provokes an Attack of Opportunity from Royal Guard B.

If an opponent moves out of a square adjacent to you, or performs an action that forces them to let down their guard, you can make a single, immediate attack against that opponent (even if you've already acted during the round). This is called an Attack of Opportunity. You can only make Attacks of Opportunity with Melee Weapons, Natural Weapons, [Pistols](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pistols "Pistols"), and any weapon with a folded [Retractable Stock](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Retractable_Stock "Retractable Stock"). You may also make Attacks of Opportunity while [Unarmed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed "Unarmed") if you have the [Martial Arts I](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Martial_Arts_I "Martial Arts I") feat.

## Provoking an Attack of Opportunity

Two actions can Provoke Attacks of Opportunity:

- Moving out of a [Threatened Area](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threatened_Area "Threatened Area").
- Performing an action that distracts you from defending yourself, and lets your guard down while in a Threatened square.

Most characters Threaten the squares adjacent to them; larger creatures Threaten all squares within their [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach"). A creature only Threatens an area if it is armed with a Melee Weapon, a Natural Weapon, a [Pistol](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pistol "Pistol"), or any weapon with a folded [Retractable Stock](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Retractable_Stock "Retractable Stock").

### Moving out of a Threatened Square

When you move out of a Threatened Square, you generally provoke an Attack of Opportunity. You do not provoke an Attack of Opportunity if you use the [Withdraw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Withdraw "Withdraw") Action, or if you successfully [Tumble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tumble "Tumble"). Involuntary movement, such as being the target of a [Bantha Rush](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bantha_Rush "Bantha Rush") or the *[Move Object](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Object "Move Object")* Force Power, never provokes Attacks of Opportunity.

[![](//static.wikitide.net/swsewiki/thumb/7/7c/Attacks_of_Opportunity_II.webp/600px-Attacks_of_Opportunity_II.webp.png)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/File:Attacks_of_Opportunity_II.webp)

Mace Windu moves more than 1 square and Attacks the Rancor with his Lightsaber. Mace provokes Attacks of Opportunity from both Tusken Raider A and Tusken Raider B (who are armed with Gaffi Sticks) as he moves out of their [Threatened Area](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threatened_Area "Threatened Area"). He also Provokes an Attack of Opportunity from the Rancor as he moves through its [Threatened Area](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threatened_Area "Threatened Area") (the Rancor has a 2-square [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach")).

### Performing an Action that Distracts You

Some actions, when performed in a [Threatened Area](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threatened_Area "Threatened Area"), provoke Attacks of Opportunity because they make you divert your attention from the fight at hand. The following actions provoke Attacks of Opportunity:

- Making an [Unarmed Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attack "Unarmed Attack") without the [Martial Arts I](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Martial_Arts_I "Martial Arts I") feat
- [Aiming](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aiming "Aiming")
- Loading a weapon
- Picking up an item
- Retrieving a stored item
- Moving into an opponent's square
- Using any [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill") that distracts you or forces you to drop your guard (GM's determination)

## Making Attacks of Opportunity

An Attack of Opportunity is a single free attack, and you can only make one Attack of Opportunity per Round. You don't have to make an Attack of Opportunity if you don't want to. An Attack of Opportunity is always made at your full attack bonus, minus any situational penalties you suffer. You can't make an Attack of Opportunity if you're [Flat-Footed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flat-Footed "Flat-Footed").

If you have the [Combat Reflexes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Combat_Reflexes "Combat Reflexes") feat, you can make more than one Attack of Opportunity during a round. However, you may only make one Attack of Opportunity per provoking action. (Moving any number of squares is treated as a single provoking action.)

# Category : Scent

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Scent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Scent) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:07:41Z.

At close range (within 10 squares), [Species] ignore [Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Concealment "Concealment") and [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") for purposes of [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") checks, and take no penalty from poor visibility while [Tracking](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tracking "Tracking").

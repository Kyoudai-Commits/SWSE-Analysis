# Melee Attacks

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attacks) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:05:45Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook"), [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

With a melee weapon as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can strike any target in a square you can [Threaten](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threaten "Threaten"). Small and Medium characters threaten the squares adjacent to them. Bigger creatures may threaten a larger number of squares, as defined by their [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach").

**Melee Attack: 1d20 + [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") + [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") Modifier**

- *[Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus"):* Your [Heroic Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Class "Heroic Class") and level determine your [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus").
- *[Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") Modifier:* [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") helps you swing a weapon harder and faster, so your [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") modifier applies to melee attack rolls.

**Melee Damage: Weapon Dice + One-Half Heroic Level (Rounded Down) + [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") Modifier**

- *Weapon Dice:* A hit always deals at least 1 point of damage, even if penalties to damage bring the damage result below 1.
- *One-Half Heroic Level:* Weapons are simply more dangerous in the hands of powerful heroes (and villains).
  - Beasts add half their [Beast](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Beast "Beast") Level (rounded down) to damage rolls made with Natural Weapons.
- *[Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") Modifier:* When you hit with a melee weapon, you add your [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") modifier to Damage.
  - When you hit with a melee weapon that you are wielding two-handed, you add double your [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") bonus (if any) to the damage. This higher [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") modifier does not apply to two-handed Melee Attacks with [Light Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Weapons "Light Weapons").

Vehicle CombatMass Combat

With a melee weapon, you can strike any opponent within a square you [Threaten](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threaten "Threaten"). You can only [Threaten](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threaten "Threaten") squares within your [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach") from the Vehicle's space, and you don't [Threaten](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threaten "Threaten") spaces around the Vehicle at all if it grants [Total Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Total_Cover "Total Cover"). You can't make a melee attack in [Starship Scale](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Scale "Starship Scale"), except within the confines of the [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") you occupy.

For example, a Jedi on a Speeder Bike could attack targets adjacent to the bike with their [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber"), but the same Jedi couldn't do so while inside an [AT-AT](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/AT-AT "AT-AT").

All [Battalions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalions "Battalions") can make Melee Attacks and [Ranged Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attacks "Ranged Attacks") if they possess the appropriate weaponry. Apply the bonuses to the attack roll and compare the result to the [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") of the target, as normal. A Battalion's attack bonuses are listed in its statistics blocks.

A [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion") can make Melee Attacks against another [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion") occupying the same space (see [Fighting Space](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fighting_Space "Fighting Space")). Only ground [Battalions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalions "Battalions") can make Melee Attacks, and only against other ground [Battalions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalions "Battalions").

## Flanking

If you are making a melee attack against an opponent and you have an ally on the other side of an opponent so that the opponent is directly between the two of you, you are Flanking that opponent. You gain a +2 Flanking bonus on your melee attack roll ([Ranged Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attacks "Ranged Attacks") don't gain a Flanking bonus).

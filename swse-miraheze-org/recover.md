# Recover

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Recover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Recover) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:07:59Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You can spend three [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions") in the same round or across consecutive rounds to move +1 step on the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"). You cannot use the Recover Action while afflicted by a [Persistent Condition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persistent_Condition "Persistent Condition").

# Bowcaster

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bowcaster](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bowcaster) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:07:56Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

The Bowcaster is crafted exclusively by the [Wookiees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wookiees "Wookiees") of [Kashyyyk](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kashyyyk "Kashyyyk"). A fusion of modern and ancient technologies, the weapon hurls an explosive energy quarrel at incredible speed, much like an archaic rail gun. [Wookiees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wookiees "Wookiees") treat Bowcasters as [Rifles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rifles "Rifles"), not [Exotic Weapons (Ranged)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapons_(Ranged) "Exotic Weapons (Ranged)"). Bowcasters are treated as [Rifles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rifles "Rifles") for the purposes of determining weapon [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range"). A quiver of 10 quarrels costs 50 credits and weighs 1 kg.

**Weapon Type:** [Exotic Weapons (Ranged)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapons_(Ranged) "Exotic Weapons (Ranged)")

**Size:** Large

**Cost:** 1,500

**Damage:** 3d10

**[Stun](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stun "Stun") Setting:** NO

**Rate of Fire:** Single-Shot

**Weight:** 8 Kilograms

**Type:** Energy and Piercing

**Availability:** [Licensed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Licensed "Licensed"), [Rare](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rare "Rare")

**Accurate:** This weapon takes no penalty when firing at targets at Short [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range").

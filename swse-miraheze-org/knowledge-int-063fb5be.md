# Knowledge (Int)

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:08:41Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

Knowledge encompasses a number of unrelated skills. Knowledge represents a study of some body of lore, possibly an academic or even scientific discipline.

**Special:** You can [Take 10](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_10 "Take 10") when making a Knowledge check, but you can't [Take 20](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_20 "Take 20").

**Retry:** You can't reroll a failed Knowledge check. The roll represents what you know, and thinking about a topic a second time doesn't let you know something you never learned in the first place.

Each time you select Knowledge as a [Trained Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skill "Trained Skill"), you must choose a field of study from the list below:

| KNOWLEDGE FIELD | ENCOMPASSES |
| --- | --- |
| Bureaucracy | Business procedures, legal systems and regulations, and organizational structures |
| Galactic Lore | Planets, homeworlds, sectors of space, galactic history, and [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") |
| Life Sciences | Biology, botany, genetics, archaeology, xenobiology, medicine, and forensics |
| Physical Sciences | Astronomy, astrogation, chemistry, mathematics, physics, and engineering |
| Social Sciences | Sociology, psychology, philosophy, theology, and criminology |
| Tactics | Techniques and strategies for maneuvering in battle |
| Technology | Function and principle of technological devices; cutting edge theories and advancements |

## Applications

The following are the primary applications of the Knowledge skill.

### Common Knowledge

You can answer a basic question about a subject related to your field of study with a DC 10 check. For example, a DC 10 Knowledge (Life Sciences) check is enough to know that [Rodians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rodians "Rodians") are skilled hunters.

### Expert Knowledge

**Trained Only:** You can make a Knowledge check as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") to answer a question within your field of study that requires some level of expertise. The DC of the check ranges from 15 (for simple questions) to 25 (for tough questions). The GM may adjust the DC depending on the character's personal experience.

For example, a DC 20 Knowledge (Galactic Lore) check might reveal specific information about the inhabitants of the planet [Dathomir](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dathomir?action=edit&redlink=1 "Dathomir (page does not exist)"), but the DC may be lower if the character making the check has actually been there.

## Additional Applications

The following applications of the Knowledge skill are compiled from the subsequent [Reference Books](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reference_Books "Reference Books") released for *Saga Edition*:

### Anticipate Enemy Strategy

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

**Trained Only:** A good tactician can anticipate their enemy's movements and strategies, and then plan accordingly. As a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action"), a character [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in Knowledge (Tactics) can designate a target in their line of sight and make a Knowledge (Tactics) check to anticipate the enemy's next move. The DC for the check is equal to the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), or 10 + the target's CL if the target does not have a [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") (as is the case for [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") without unique [Pilots](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilots "Pilots")).

If successful, you gain special insight into the target's strategy and can anticipate its next move. You learn what actions the target is likely to take on its next turn based on the current circumstances; if circumstances change, the target's actions might change as well, though typically unless the situation changes drastically you should have a good idea of the target's likely next action. The Gamemaster is the final arbiter of just how much information you gain through the use of this application.

Typically this does not include information on the use of [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") or [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"), but it should provide information on whether the target is going to attack, who the target intends to attack, where the target might move to, or any other special strategies the target might employ.

### Battlefield Tactics

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

**Trained Only:** If you are the [Field Commander](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Field_Commander "Field Commander") in a [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion") during [Mass Combat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mass_Combat "Mass Combat"), you can use your tactical knowledge to grant extra [Standard Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Actions "Standard Actions") to other characters in your [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion"). The base check DC for this Knowledge (Tactics) skill check is 20, and it requires three [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions") made on consecutive rounds to activate.

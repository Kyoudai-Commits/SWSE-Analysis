# Category : Standard Actions

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Standard_Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Standard_Actions) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:06:35Z.

Standard Actions are usually the most important [Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Action "Action") you'll take in a round, and it often consists of some sort of attack- swinging a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber"), firing a blaster, throwing a punch, hurling a [Grenade](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grenade "Grenade"), and so on. You can perform one Standard Action on your turn.

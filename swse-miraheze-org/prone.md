# Prone

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prone) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:06:25Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

Various attacks, [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents"), [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"), and [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") can knock a character Prone. Characters can also purposefully fall Prone as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") (a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action") if they are [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Acrobatics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatics "Acrobatics") with a DC 15 skill check), and stand up from being Prone as a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action") (a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") if they are [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Acrobatics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatics "Acrobatics") with a DC 15 skill check).

A Prone character takes a -5 penalty on melee attack rolls. [Melee Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attacks "Melee Attacks") made against a Prone character gain a +5 bonus, while [Ranged Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attacks "Ranged Attacks") against a Prone character take a -5 penalty. Being Prone may also give a character [Total Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Total_Cover "Total Cover") instead of normal [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") (for example, being Prone behind a low wall), subject to the GM's discretion.

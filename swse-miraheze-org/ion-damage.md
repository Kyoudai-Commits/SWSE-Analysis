# Ion Damage

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ion_Damage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ion_Damage) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:07:12Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

[Ion Pistols](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ion_Pistols "Ion Pistols") and [Ion Rifles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ion_Rifles "Ion Rifles") emit powerful bursts of electrostatic energy that can disable [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids"), [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles"), and electrical devices in much the same way that [Stun](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stun "Stun") weapons can incapacitate living creatures. When you make a successful attack with a weapon that deals Ion Damage, first subtract half of the Ion Damage from the target's [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points"). Creatures that do not have a [Cybernetic Prosthesis](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cybernetic_Prosthesis "Cybernetic Prosthesis") are singed by the Ion energy, but suffer no other ill effects.

[Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids"), [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles"), electronic devices, and cybernetically enhanced creatures may suffer additional effects, as noted below:

- If the Ion Damage reduces the target's current [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") to 0, the target moves -5 steps on the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") and is either [Disabled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disabled "Disabled") or knocked [Unconscious](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unconscious "Unconscious").
- If the Ion Damage (before being halved) equals or exceeds the target's [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold"), the target moves -2 steps on the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track").

# Line of Sight

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:07:13Z.

[![](//static.wikitide.net/swsewiki/thumb/9/95/Line_of_Sight.webp/250px-Line_of_Sight.webp.png)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/File:Line_of_Sight.webp)

Two characters have Line of Sight to each other if there's at least one clear line between their spaces. A line that nicks a corner or runs along a wall does not provide Line of Sight. A character needs Line of Sight to an opponent to attack that opponent. Han Solo has Line of Sight to Stormtrooper A, but not to Stormtrooper B.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook"), [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

A character can target an opponent that they see, which is to say, any opponent within their Line of Sight. Draw an imaginary line from any point in the attacker's [Fighting Space](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fighting_Space "Fighting Space") to any point in the target's [Fighting Space](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fighting_Space "Fighting Space").

If the player who controls the attacking character can draw that line without touching a square that provides Total Cover (a wall, closed door, or similar barrier) or Total Concealment (thick smoke, total darkness, or anything else that prevents [Visibility](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Visibility "Visibility")), that character has a Line of Sight to the target. A line that nicks a corner or runs along a wall does not provide Line of Sight. Other characters and creatures, Low Objects, [Difficult Terrain](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Difficult_Terrain "Difficult Terrain"), and pits do not block Line of Sight.

Many [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") require that the target be both within Line of Sight and within a certain range. The [Sense Surroundings](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sense_Surroundings "Sense Surroundings") application of the [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") Skill can be used to perceive targets despite the presence of [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") such as walls and doors or [Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Concealment "Concealment") such as fog and smoke. By the strictest interpretation of the rules, using [Sense Surroundings](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sense_Surroundings "Sense Surroundings") to ignore penalties to [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") checks does not grant Line of Sight; it merely allows one to perceive the target (just as hearing someone coming around the corner doesn't grant Line of Sight.)

However, in some situations a Gamemaster may wish to allow a character to use [Sense Surroundings](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sense_Surroundings "Sense Surroundings") to gain Line of Sight. If a character has the [Improved Sense Surroundings](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improved_Sense_Surroundings "Improved Sense Surroundings") Force Technique, the Gamemaster can allow a character that has perceived a target to also have Line of Sight to that target, provided that the character used the [Sense Surroundings](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sense_Surroundings "Sense Surroundings") application of the [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") Skill earlier in the character's turn.

## Line of Effect

Line of Effect works just like Line of Sight, but it ignores squares that provide Total Concealment. For example, a character who is [Blind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Blind "Blind") or in total darkness doesn't have Line of Sight to any target, but that character has Line of Effect to any target that doesn't have Total Cover.

## Cover

Creatures and terrain features can provide Cover against attacks. A creature with Cover gains a +5 Cover bonus to their [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), no matter how many creatures and terrain features are between it and the attacker. Terrain features that provide Cover include trees, walls, and cargo crates.

A target with Cover or Improved Cover takes no damage from [Area Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attacks "Area Attacks") if the attack roll is less than the target's [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"). For a [Burst](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Burst "Burst") or [Splash](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Splash "Splash") weapon, determine Cover relative to the center of the weapon's area of effect. For an [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire") weapon, determine Cover relative to the attacker.

To determine whether an opponent has Cover, choose one corner of the attacker's square. If any line from this corner to any corner of the target's square passes through a barrier or any square occupied by a creature, the target has Cover. The target does not have Cover if the line runs along or touches the edge of a wall or other square that would otherwise provide Cover. An adjacent opponent never has Cover.

[![](//static.wikitide.net/swsewiki/thumb/2/22/Cover.webp/450px-Cover.webp.png)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/File:Cover.webp)

A line that runs along a wall or that that nicks a corner of a wall does not provide Cover. Stormtrooper A does not have Cover from Han Solo. Stormtrooper B does not have Cover from Princess Leia. Han and Leia do not have Line of Sight to one another.

You can't make an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity") against an opponent with Cover relative to you. You can use Cover to make a [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") check. Without Cover, you usually need Concealment to make a [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") check. Any creature with a [Fighting Space](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fighting_Space "Fighting Space") larger than 1 square determines Cover against [Melee Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attacks "Melee Attacks") slightly differently than smaller creatures. Such a creature can choose any square that it occupies to determine whether an opponent has Cover against [Melee Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attacks "Melee Attacks"). Similarly, when making a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") against such a creature, you can pick any of the squares it occupies to determine whether it has Cover against you.

[Battalions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalions "Battalions") can gain Cover and Concealment just as characters do. Determine Cover or Concealment as normal for an attacker targeting a [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion") in a particular square.

### Low Objects

Low Objects provide Cover to creatures in those squares. However, the attacker ignores Low Objects in its own [Fighting Space](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fighting_Space "Fighting Space") and adjacent squares. Low Objects in the attacker's space and in adjacent squares don't provide Cover to opponents; essentially, the attacker shoots over them.

### Improved Cover

In some cases, Cover may provide a greater bonus to [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"). For instance, a character peering around a corner, or through a narrow aperture has even better Cover than a character standing behind a low wall, or a Landspeeder. In such situations, double the normal Cover bonus to [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") (+10 instead of +5). Furthermore, Improved Cover provides a +5 bonus on [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") checks.

The GM may impose other penalties or restrictions to attacks depending on the details of the Cover. For example, to strike effectively through a gun port, you need to use a long thrusting weapon, such as a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber"). A [Vibro-Axe](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vibro-Axe "Vibro-Axe") just isn't going to get through a narrow slit.

### Total Cover

If you don't have a Line of Effect to your target (for instance, if they are completely behind a high wall), they are considered to have Total Cover from you. You can't make an attack against a target that has Total Cover.

### Crew Cover

Most Vehicles provide at least some Cover to their passengers. Passengers gain a Cover bonus to their [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") against any attacks that target them instead of the [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle"). A [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") can provide no Cover, normal Cover]] (+5), Improved Cover (+10), or Total Cover. You cannot attack a target that has Total Cover. The Cover a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") provides to its passengers is included in its statistics.

## Concealment

Concealment encompasses all circumstances where nothing physically blocks a blow or shot, but where something interferes with an attacker's accuracy. An attack that would normally hit, might actually miss because the target has Concealment. A target might gain Concealment from fog, smoke, poor lighting, tall grass, foliage, or other effects that make it difficult to pinpoint the target's location.

To determine whether your target has Concealment from your [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack"), choose a corner of your square. If any line from this corner to any corner of the target's square passes through a square or border that provides Concealment, the target has Concealment. When making a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") against an adjacent target, your target has Concealment if their space is entirely within an effect that grants Concealment (such as a cloud of smoke).

If you attack a target with Concealment, you take a -2 penalty on your attack roll. Multiple sources of Concealment (such as a defender in a fog at night, with no illumination) do not apply additional penalties. If you attempt to [Notice Targets](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Notice_Targets "Notice Targets") with Concealment, you take a -5 penalty on your [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") check.

[Battalions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalions "Battalions") can gain Cover and Concealment just as characters do. Determine Cover or Concealment as normal for an attacker targeting a [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion") in a particular square.

### Ignoring Concealment

Concealment isn't always effective. For instance, a character with Low-Light Vision ignores Concealment from darkness (but not Total Concealment; see below). Likewise, a character with Darkvision ignores all Concealment from darkness (even Total Concealment). Fog, smoke, foliage, and other obstructions work normally against characters with [Darkvision](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darkvision "Darkvision") or [Low-Light Vision](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Low-Light_Vision "Low-Light Vision").

### Total Concealment

If you have Line of Effect to a target, but not Line of Sight (for instance, if they are in total darkness or if you are [Blinded](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Blinded "Blinded")), they are considered to have Total Concealment from you. You can't attack an opponent that has Total Concealment, though you can attack into a square that you think they occupy.

If you attack a target with Total Concealment, you take a -5 penalty on your attack roll. You can't make [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity") against an opponent with Total Concealment, even if you know what square or squares the opponent occupies. If you attempt to [Notice Targets](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Notice_Targets "Notice Targets") with Total Concealment, you take a -10 penalty on your [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") check.

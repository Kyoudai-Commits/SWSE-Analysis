# Category : Swift Actions

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Swift_Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Swift_Actions) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:06:11Z.

Swift Actions represent things that require very little time or effort can be accomplished. Some [Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Actions "Actions"), [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"), and [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") require one or more Swift Actions to perform. You normally get one Swift Action per round, but you can take a second Swift Action instead of a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") or [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action"), and you can take three Swift Actions in a round if you give up both your [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") and [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action").

Multiple Swift Actions usually have to occur on the same round, or consecutive rounds, and some Actions require that the multiple Swift Actions be consecutive (that is, no other Action interrupts them). This is noted in the Action's description.

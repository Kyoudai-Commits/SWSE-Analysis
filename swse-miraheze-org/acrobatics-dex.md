# Acrobatics (Dex)

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatics) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:05:39Z.

*Reference Books: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook"), [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide"), [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

Acrobatics skill checks are affected by an **[Armor Check Penalty](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor_Check_Penalty "Armor Check Penalty")**.

You can move at normal speed across [Difficult Terrain](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Difficult_Terrain "Difficult Terrain"), keep your balance while walking on a narrow surface, take less damage from a fall, slip free of restraints or [Grappling](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grappling "Grappling") a foe, and get up from [Prone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prone "Prone") safely. In addition to the specific options listed below, you can use Acrobatics to perform typical tumbling, flipping, or gymnastic maneuvers.

**Special:** You can't [Take 10](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_10 "Take 10") or [Take 20](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_20 "Take 20") on an Acrobatics check. If you are [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in Acrobatics, you gain a +5 dodge bonus to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") when [Fighting Defensively](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fighting_Defensively "Fighting Defensively").

## Applications

The following are the primary applications of the Acrobatics skill.

### Balance

A successful Acrobatics check allows you to move at half speed along a narrow surface such as a ledge or wire. The DC of the Acrobatics check varies with the width of the surface (see below). If the surface is slippery or unstable, increase the DC by 5. A failed check means you fall [Prone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prone "Prone") and must make a DC 15 Acrobatics check to catch the ledge or wire. You are considered [Flat-Footed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flat-Footed "Flat-Footed") while balancing, and thus you lose your [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") (if any).

If you are [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in Acrobatics, you aren't considered [Flat-Footed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flat-Footed "Flat-Footed") while Balancing. If you take damage while balancing, you must immediately make another Acrobatics check against the same DC to keep from falling.

| NARROW SURFACE | ACROBATICS DC |
| --- | --- |
| 8-15 cm wide | 10 |
| 4-7 cm wide | 15 |
| Less than 4 cm wide | 20 |

### Cross Difficult Terrain

**Trained Only:** With a successful DC 15 Acrobatics check, you can move through [Difficult Terrain](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Difficult_Terrain "Difficult Terrain") at your normal [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed").

### Escape Bonds

With a successful Acrobatics check, you can slip free of restraints (DC varies, see below), wiggle through a tight space (DC 20), or escape from a [Grapple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple "Grapple") (DC = the grappler's [Grapple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple "Grapple") check). The DC to slip free of a restraint depends on the type of restraint (see below). It takes a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") to escape a [Grapple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple "Grapple"). It takes a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action") to escape a [Net](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Net "Net") or to move 1 square through a tight space. It takes 1 minute to escape from [Binder Cuffs](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Binder_Cuffs "Binder Cuffs"), ropes, or manacles.

If you are [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in Acrobatics, you can increase the DC by 10 to reduce the time required to Escape Bonds. Escaping a [Grapple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple "Grapple") takes a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action"); escaping a [Net](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Net "Net") or moving 1 square through a tight space requires a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"); and only 5 rounds are required to escape from [Binder Cuffs](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Binder_Cuffs "Binder Cuffs"), ropes, or manacles.

| RESTRAINT | ACROBATICS DC |
| --- | --- |
| Ropes | Opponent's [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") check + 10 |
| [Net](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Net "Net") | 15 |
| [Binder Cuffs](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Binder_Cuffs "Binder Cuffs") | 25 |

### Fall Prone

**Trained Only:** If you are [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in Acrobatics and succeed at a DC 15 check, you can drop to a [Prone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prone "Prone") position as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action") (instead of a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action")). Additionally, if you are [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in Acrobatics and succeed a DC 15 check, you can stand up from a [Prone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prone "Prone") position as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") (instead of a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action")).

### Reduce Falling Damage

**Trained Only:** With a successful DC 15 Acrobatics check, you can treat a fall as if it was 3 meters (2 squares) shorter when determining [Falling Damage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Falling_Damage "Falling Damage"). For every 10 points by which you beat this DC, you can subtract an additional 3 meters from the fall for determining damage. If you make this check and take no damage from the fall, you land on your feet.

If you are struck by a falling object, you can reduce the damage you take by half with a successful DC 15 Acrobatics check (see [Falling Objects](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Falling_Objects "Falling Objects")).

When falling great distances, you can attempt to use your Acrobatics skill to guide your decent. Make a DC 20 Acrobatics check to land on a target near a square you would normally land in. You can adjust your target by 1 square for every 60 meters (40 squares) fallen. Gamemasters can give favorable or unfavorable circumstance adjustments to the check based on mitigating circumstances- for example, the amount of wind or the favorable use of [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment"), such as baggy clothing.

### Tumble

**Trained Only:** If you succeed a DC 15 Acrobatics check, you can Tumble through the [Threatened Area](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threatened_Area "Threatened Area") or [Fighting Space](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fighting_Space "Fighting Space") of an enemy as part of your [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action") without provoking an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity"). Each threatened or occupied square that you Tumble through counts as 2 squares of movement.

## Additional Applications

The following applications of the Acrobatics skill are compiled from the subsequent [Reference Books](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reference_Books "Reference Books") released for *Saga Edition*:

### Catch Item

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

**Trained Only:** Whenever you successfully [Disarm](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disarm "Disarm") your opponent, you can make a DC 20 Acrobatics check as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action"). If the Acrobatics check succeeds, you snatch the object from the air. You must have at least one hand free to grab the item.

### Low or High Gravity Environments

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

**Trained Only:** You can make a DC 20 Acrobatics check to negate the penalties to attack rolls in [Low Gravity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Low_Gravity "Low Gravity") and [High Gravity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/High_Gravity "High Gravity") environments.

### Nimble Charge

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

**Trained Only:** If you succeed on a DC 25 Acrobatics check, you can [Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge "Charge") through [Low Objects](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Low_Objects "Low Objects") and [Difficult Terrain](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Difficult_Terrain "Difficult Terrain"). If you fail this check, you cannot attack at the end of your movement. You can combine this with the Cross Difficult Terrain action (see above); however, the DC increases to 35.

### Zero-Gravity Environments

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

**Trained Only:** You can use the Acrobatics skill to help maneuver in [Zero-Gravity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Zero-Gravity "Zero-Gravity") environments. When crossing wide spaces or traversing congested areas, you can make an Acrobatics check (at no penalty) to arrive on target. As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can make a DC 20 Acrobatics check to lessen your attack roll and skill check penalty to -2 (instead of the normal -5).

# Grapple

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:06:58Z.

*Reference Books: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook"), [Web Enhancements](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Web_Enhancements "Web Enhancements") ([Saga Edition FAQ](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Saga_Edition_FAQ "Saga Edition FAQ") (Q154))*

A Grapple attack is an improved version of the [Grab](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grab "Grab"). You can only make a Grapple attack (a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")) if you have a [Grappling Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grappling_Feat "Grappling Feat") (such as [Pin](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pin "Pin") or [Trip](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trip "Trip")). You can only Grapple an opponent up to one size category larger than you, and only one opponent at a time. A Grappling attack is treated as an [Unarmed Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attack "Unarmed Attack") (thus provoking [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity") without the [Martial Arts I](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Martial_Arts_I "Martial Arts I") feat), except that it deals no damage. If the Grappling attack hits, you and the target immediately make opposed Grapple checks. A Grapple check is:

**1d20 + [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") + [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") or [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") modifier (whichever is higher) + Size Modifier (see below)**

If your check result equals or exceeds the target's check result, the target is Grappled.

The effects of a Grapple depend on the specific Feat or combination of Feats you are using (for example; [Pin](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pin "Pin"), [Pin](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pin "Pin") and [Crush](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Crush "Crush"), [Trip](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trip "Trip"), or [Trip](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trip "Trip") and [Throw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Throw "Throw")). Alternatively, if you are armed with a [Light Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Weapon "Light Weapon"), you may deal damage with that weapon if you win the opposed Grapple check; no attack roll is necessary.

## Size Modifiers

Size modifiers for Grapple checks are as follows:

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Diminutive | -15 | Gargantuan | +15 |
| Tiny | -10 | Colossal | +20 |
| Small | -5 | Colossal (Frigate) | +25 |
| Medium | +0 | Colossal (Cruiser) | +30 |
| Large | +5 | Colossal (Station) | +35 |

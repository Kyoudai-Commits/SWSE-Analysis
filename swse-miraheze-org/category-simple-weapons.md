# Category : Simple Weapons

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Simple_Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Simple_Weapons) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:08:48Z.

Simple Weapons include weapons that require no special training to use (including [Grenades](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grenades "Grenades"), [Simple Weapons (Ranged)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Simple_Weapons_(Ranged) "Simple Weapons (Ranged)"), and [Simple Weapons (Melee)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Simple_Weapons_(Melee) "Simple Weapons (Melee)")). [Unarmed Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attacks "Unarmed Attacks") and Natural Weapons are considered Light Simple Weapons in which all characters are proficient. [Weapon Proficiency (Simple Weapons)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Simple_Weapons) "Weapon Proficiency (Simple Weapons)") is a Starting Feat for all [Heroic Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Classes "Heroic Classes").

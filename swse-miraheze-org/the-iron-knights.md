# The Iron Knights

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Iron_Knights](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Iron_Knights) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:04:54Z.

DescriptionHistoryLocationPhilosophyBuilding an Iron Knight

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

The Iron Knights is a highly unusual [Force Tradition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Tradition "Force Tradition") comprised solely of a race of silicon-based creatures called [Shards](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shards "Shards"). The [Shards](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shards "Shards") are both sentient and Force-sensitive. The Iron Knights are [Shards](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shards "Shards") that have placed themselves inside of [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") bodies, allowing them to interact with their surroundings much like other sentients would. The Iron Knights use the combination of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") and their [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") bodies to pursue their agendas, which are very much in line with [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"). Despite persecution and the occasional misunderstanding, The Iron Knights prove to be stalwart and loyal defenders of peace and justice.

**Membership:** Only [Shards](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shards "Shards") can become Iron Knights. Iron Knights are considered part of [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") Force Tradition for the purposes of qualifying for the [Jedi Knight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Knight "Jedi Knight") and [Jedi Master](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Master "Jedi Master") Prestige Classes. Members of The Iron Knights can select [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") from the [Iron Knight Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Iron_Knight_Talent_Tree "Iron Knight Talent Tree").

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

In order to talk about The Iron Knights, one must discuss the [Shards](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shards "Shards"). Native to the planet of [Orax](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Orax?action=edit&redlink=1 "Orax (page does not exist)"), [Shards](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shards "Shards") appear as small, immobile clusters of multifaceted crystals. [Shards](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shards "Shards") pulse with faint internal light, giving hints of their sentience. These individual [Shard](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shard "Shard") clusters bunch together into large colonies, which communicate through a band of electromagnetic energy. A [Shard](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shard "Shard") can live for thousands of years in this environment. [Humans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Humans "Humans") colonizing the planet eventually realize that the [Shards](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shards "Shards") are sentient and communicate with them using devices that translate their electromagnetic pulses into speech. Some [Shards](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shards "Shards") volunteer to be implanted inside [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids"), enabling them to leave their colonies and explore the rest of the galaxy. The [Shards](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shards "Shards") use their newfound mobility to meet with representatives of [The Galactic Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Galactic_Republic "The Galactic Republic"), sending their [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid")-bodied ambassadors to establish relations with other cultures and [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species").

For unknown reasons, some [Shards](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shards "Shards") are Force-sensitive, a fact discovered by Jedi Master [Aqinos](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aqinos?action=edit&redlink=1 "Aqinos (page does not exist)"). During [The Arkanian Revolution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Arkanian_Revolution?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Arkanian Revolution (page does not exist)"), Master [Aqinos](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aqinos?action=edit&redlink=1 "Aqinos (page does not exist)") takes many [Shards](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shards "Shards") as Padawans, teaching them about [The Living Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Living_Force "The Living Force"). Calling themselves The Iron Knights, several of their number join in that conflict and help win the day. The Jedi Council excommunicates [Aqinos](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aqinos?action=edit&redlink=1 "Aqinos (page does not exist)") for sharing knowledge of [The Living Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Living_Force "The Living Force") with the [Shards](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shards "Shards"), and he goes into exile, taking several of the [Shard](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shard "Shard") Jedi along. This decision saves The Iron Knights from extinction. When the Great Jedi Purge happens, most of the [Shards](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shards "Shards") on [Orax](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Orax?action=edit&redlink=1 "Orax (page does not exist)") are destroyed and the planet is heavily mined. [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") are banned from the surface of [Orax](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Orax?action=edit&redlink=1 "Orax (page does not exist)"), and few [Shards](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shards "Shards") manage to escape. Now refugees and fugitives, the majority of The Iron Knights go into hiding on the planet [Dweem](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dweem?action=edit&redlink=1 "Dweem (page does not exist)").

The Iron Knights lay low for decades, until they are rediscovered by a band of students from the New Jedi Order. Realizing their potential, the Order accepts The Iron Knights into its ranks. During the Yuuzhan Vong Invasion, The Iron Knights prove an instrumental part of the New Jedi Order's defenses, although the Yuuzhan Vong's hatred of technology makes The Iron Knights favorite targets for destruction. Numerous Iron Knights serve with honor and distinction during the conflict.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

Iron Knights are most commonly encountered in two different eras. The first is around the time of [The Arkanian Revolution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Arkanian_Revolution?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Arkanian Revolution (page does not exist)"), roughly 50 years before the Battle of [Yavin](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yavin "Yavin"). At this time, The Iron Knights are new to the galaxy at large and just beginning to learn the ways of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). What they lacked in experience, they made up for in enthusiasm.

The Iron Knights rise to prominence again during the time of the New Jedi Order, after surviving the Great Jedi Purge by hiding in their [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") bodies and using their mechanical shells to conceal their covert anti-Empire activities. By the time of the Yuuzhan Vong Invasion, The Iron Knights are entrenched elements within the New Jedi Order.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

[Shards](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shards "Shards") are deeply contemplative and curious creatures. Their unusual method of communication is ponderously slow, meaning that discussions can take days to play out and decisions up to years. The Iron Knights are different- their connection to their [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") hosts allow them to interact with the galaxy in much the same way as other sentients. They are fascinated with both the speed and short lives of other creatures. They find the galaxy a dynamic, vibrant place and go to great lengths to protect others from harm or to stop injustice when encountered.

Despite their zeal, The Iron Knights are as likely to succumb to [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") as any Force-user. Iron Knights take such transgressions very seriously and spend much of their downtime in deep contemplation of the very nature of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to recognize and ward off The Dark Side's temptations.

The Iron Knights are Jedi and train with [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers"). However, they are quite capable of defeating enemies using the weapons built into their [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") bodies. The Iron Knights prefer [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") bodies that are built for battle, such as the [Juggernaut War Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Juggernaut_War_Droid "Juggernaut War Droid") or the [FLTCH-Series Battle Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/FLTCH-Series_Battle_Droid "FLTCH-Series Battle Droid").

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

Building an Iron Knight is much like building a Jedi. Sine only [Shards](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shards "Shards") can become Iron Knights, all you really need to worry about are which [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents"), [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"), and [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") to take. Since Iron Knights are likely to be warding into the thick of combat, one of your best choices is the **[Force Repair](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Repair "Force Repair")** Talent. Similarly, your hero should consider [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") and [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") that bolster your [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points"), ability to catch a [Second Wind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Second_Wind "Second Wind"), and so forth. Likewise, defensive [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") such as **[Block](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Block "Block")** and **[Deflect](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deflect "Deflect")** can keep you from relying as much on healing. Otherwise, build your Iron Knight just as you would any other Jedi- after all, The Iron Knights consider themselves to be Jedi, and so should your hero.

# Kilian Ranger Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kilian_Ranger_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kilian_Ranger_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:05:10Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

As a respected member of [The Kilian Rangers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Kilian_Rangers "The Kilian Rangers"), you have learned their methods of using [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). You are a master of the [Siang Lance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Siang_Lance "Siang Lance") and the [Shield Gauntlet](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shield_Gauntlet "Shield Gauntlet"), and you use them to defend chivalry and justice. Only Force-users that are part of [The Kilian Rangers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Kilian_Rangers "The Kilian Rangers") may select [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") from this tree.

## Core Talents

### Empower Siang Lance

*Prerequisites:* [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +7, **Siang Lance Mastery**

You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to empower a [Siang Lance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Siang_Lance "Siang Lance"), which takes a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"). After the [Siang Lance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Siang_Lance "Siang Lance") in empowered, it deals an additional die of damage when you wield it. Others who wield the weapon do not gain the bonus damage die.

### Shield Gauntlet Defense

Once per turn as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), you can gain a +2 deflection bonus to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") against any one [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack"). To use this Talent, you must be wearing an active [Shield Gauntlet](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shield_Gauntlet "Shield Gauntlet"), you must be aware of the attack, and you must not be [Flat-Footed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flat-Footed "Flat-Footed").

### Shield Gauntlet Deflect

*Prerequisite:* **Shield Gauntlet Defense**

Once per round as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), you can negate a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") by making a successful [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check. The DC of the skill check is equal to the result of the attack roll you wish to negate. To use this this Talent, you must be wearing an active [Shield Gauntlet](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shield_Gauntlet "Shield Gauntlet"), you must be aware of the attack, and you must not be [Flat-Footed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flat-Footed "Flat-Footed"). You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to use this Talent to negate a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") against an adjacent character.

You can use **Shield Gauntlet Deflect** to deflect some of the barrage of shots fired from a ranged weapon set on [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire"). If your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check succeeds, you take half damage if the attack hits an no damage if the attack misses.

### Shield Gauntlet Redirect

*Prerequisites:* [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +5, **Shield Gauntlet Defense**, **Shield Gauntlet Deflect**

This Talent allows you to redirect a deflected blaster bolt along a specific trajectory so that it damages another creature or object in its path. When you successfully perform a **Shield Gauntlet Deflect**, you can make an immediate [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") against another target within 6 squares of you which you have [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"). If the attack succeeds, it deals normal weapon damage to the target.

Only single blaster bolts can be redirected in this manner. You cannot use this Talent to redirect barrages from [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire") weapons and other types of projectiles. To use this Talent, you must be wearing an active [Shield Gauntlet](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shield_Gauntlet "Shield Gauntlet"), you must be aware of the attack, and you must not be [Flat-Footed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flat-Footed "Flat-Footed").

### Siang Lance Mastery

You treat a [Siang Lance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Siang_Lance "Siang Lance") as a [Rifle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rifle "Rifle") instead of as an [Exotic Weapon (Ranged)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon_(Ranged) "Exotic Weapon (Ranged)"). Additionally, you gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls with a [Siang Lance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Siang_Lance "Siang Lance"). This Talent counts as the [Weapon Focus (Siang Lance)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus_(Siang_Lance) "Weapon Focus (Siang Lance)") feat for the purpose of satisfying prerequisites. If you also have the [Weapon Focus (Rifles)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus_(Rifles) "Weapon Focus (Rifles)") feat, the attack bonus provided by this Talent does not stack with the attack bonus provided by [Weapon Focus (Rifles)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus_(Rifles) "Weapon Focus (Rifles)").

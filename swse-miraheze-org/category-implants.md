# Category : Implants

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Implants](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Implants) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:06:37Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

Seeking to gain an edge against their enemies, military forces experiment with mechanical Implants designed to augment a character's efficiency. These Implants, which are inserted directly into the brain and integrated into a character's neural pathways, alter bioelectricity and chemical impulses controlling everything from reaction times to the release of adrenaline.

Due to the complex nature of this technology, living creatures can have only a single Implant at any given time, though they can be switched out as needed. If an Implant is removed, the character immediately loses all benefits and penalties associated with the Implant. Installing or removing an Implant follows the same rules as installing a [Cybernetic Prosthesis](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cybernetic_Prosthesis "Cybernetic Prosthesis"), requiring a surgeon with the [Cybernetic Surgery](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cybernetic_Surgery "Cybernetic Surgery") feat, although unlike some cybernetic parts an Implant cannot be installed by the recipient. A character with an Implant is considered to have a cybernetic part, making them vulnerable to [Ion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ion "Ion") damage and imposing the same penalties on [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") checks as other cybernetic body parts (-1 cumulative penalty).

Additionally, characters without the [Implant Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Implant_Training "Implant Training") feat take a -2 penalty to their [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") due to the Implant's interference with normal brain functions. Implants can disrupt the body's ability to deal with system shocks, and a character with an Implant moves 1 additional step down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") when another effect would normally move them down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"), regardless of the source of the effect.

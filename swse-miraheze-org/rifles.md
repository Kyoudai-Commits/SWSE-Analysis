# Rifles

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rifles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rifles) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T08:05:21Z.

| DISTANCE | RANGE | PENALTY |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-30 Squares | Point-Blank | No penalty |
| 31-60 Squares | Short | -2 to attack rolls |
| 61-150 Squares | Medium | -5 to attack rolls |
| 151-300 Squares | Long | -10 to attack rolls |

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

Standard military weapons, used with both hands for precise and accurate shooting, Rifles deal more damage and have a greater [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range") than [Pistols](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pistols "Pistols"). You take a -5 penalty on attack rolls if you wield a Rifle with one hand (regardless of the weapon's size relative to you). [Weapon Proficiency (Rifles)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Rifles) "Weapon Proficiency (Rifles)") is a Starting Feat for [Scouts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scouts "Scouts") and [Soldiers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldiers "Soldiers"), and is a Class Bonus Feat for [Nobles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nobles "Nobles").
